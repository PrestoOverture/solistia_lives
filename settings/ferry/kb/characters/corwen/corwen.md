# Corwen Ash

Boatwright of [Wren's Ford](../../locations/sallow/towns/wrens_ford.md), 52, and [Bram Holt](../others/bram_holt.md)'s oldest friend. He built the ferry and most of the boats on this stretch of the river, including the one Aled Reed drowned in during the flood. Since then he has looked out for Aled's son, [Tobin Reed](../others/tobin_reed.md), and got him his place as Bram's apprentice. Bram listens to him more than to anyone.
