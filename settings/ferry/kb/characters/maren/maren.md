# Maren Leith

An agent of the Calloway trading house of Lowmouth, 29, staying at the Heron Inn in [Wren's Ford](../../locations/sallow/towns/wrens_ford.md) for a week. She says openly that the house wants to buy the crossing rights and has offered a large sum to whoever holds them after the spring tide. What the house means to do with them she has not said.
