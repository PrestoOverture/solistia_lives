# Hester Crane

Keeper of the Heron Inn in [Wren's Ford](../../locations/sallow/towns/wrens_ford.md), 60, a widow, [Ilse Holt](../ilse/ilse.md)'s employer and the village's surest source of news. Whatever is said at her tables, she knows by morning, and so does everyone else by noon. She makes good money from [Maren Leith](../maren/maren.md)'s stay and would welcome anything that brought more wagons through, and she likes Ilse.
