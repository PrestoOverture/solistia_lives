# Tobin Reed

Bram Holt's apprentice, 17, son of Aled Reed, who drowned in the flood fifteen years ago. He lives in the ferry house with [Bram](bram_holt.md). He is strong, sure on the water and badly wants the ferry, though he rarely says so. [Corwen Ash](../corwen/corwen.md) has looked out for him since the flood. He is wary of [Ilse Holt](../ilse/ilse.md), who treats him like a hired hand.
