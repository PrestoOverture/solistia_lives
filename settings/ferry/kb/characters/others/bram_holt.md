# Bram Holt

The ferryman of [Wren's Ford](../../locations/sallow/towns/wrens_ford.md), 71, uncle of [Ilse Holt](../ilse/ilse.md). He has run the ferry for forty years. His hands shake now and the cable is getting the better of him. He has said he will name who takes the ferry on the evening of the spring tide, at the landing, as his father named him. He is torn between his niece, who is family but afraid of the water, and his apprentice [Tobin Reed](tobin_reed.md), who is good on the water but young. He trusts [Corwen Ash](../corwen/corwen.md)'s judgment above anyone's. He has heard the Calloway offer and dislikes it, but he is not a rich man.
