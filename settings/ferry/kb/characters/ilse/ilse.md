# Ilse Holt

Bram Holt's niece, 34, cook at the Heron Inn in [Wren's Ford](../../locations/sallow/towns/wrens_ford.md). She grew up on the ferry and worked it with her uncle until the flood fifteen years ago, when she was thrown in and pulled out by [Corwen Ash](../corwen/corwen.md). Since then she has kept to the kitchen and has not crossed at night. The village assumes she will be named to the ferry. Her uncle has never said so.
