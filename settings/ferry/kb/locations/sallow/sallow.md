# The River Sallow

A wide, slow, brown river that runs south through farm country to the port city of Lowmouth. It floods in spring when the snow melts upriver; the highest water comes with the spring tide, when the river backs up from the sea. There is no bridge between the hill towns and Lowmouth. Wagons cross on ferries, and the biggest crossing is [Wren's Ford](towns/wrens_ford.md).
