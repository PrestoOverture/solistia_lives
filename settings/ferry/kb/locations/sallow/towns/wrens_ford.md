# Wren's Ford

A village of some two hundred people on the east bank of the [River Sallow](../sallow.md), built around the ferry crossing. The road from the hill towns to Lowmouth comes down to the water here; every wagon, drover and traveller pays the ferry, and the village lives on what they spend while they wait.

## The ferry
A flat-bottomed cable ferry big enough for one wagon and its team, built by [Corwen Ash](../../../characters/corwen/corwen.md) twenty years ago. The Holt family has held the crossing rights for three generations; the rights pass with the boat, by the word of the ferryman, spoken before witnesses at the landing. [Bram Holt](../../../characters/others/bram_holt.md) has run it for forty years. His hands are failing, and he has said he will name who takes it on the evening of the spring tide.

## Places in the village
- **The landing:** the stone ramp, the cable posts, the bell that calls the ferry.
- **The Heron Inn:** the only inn, run by [Hester Crane](../../../characters/others/hester_crane.md); travellers waiting on the weather eat and sleep here, and everything said at its tables is known by morning. [Ilse Holt](../../../characters/ilse/ilse.md) cooks there.
- **The boatyard:** Corwen's sheds and slipway, upstream, smelling of pitch.
- **The ferry house:** Bram's cottage above the landing, where [Tobin Reed](../../../characters/others/tobin_reed.md) also sleeps.
- **The reed bank:** downstream, quiet, where people go to talk without being overheard.

## History
- **The flood:** fifteen years ago a spring flood broke the old cable and sank two boats. Ilse Holt nearly drowned and was pulled out by Corwen; Tobin's father, Aled Reed, drowned when his boat, one of Corwen's, went down.
- **The bridge talk:** for years Lowmouth merchants have talked of a stone bridge; the villagers say the river would never allow it.
