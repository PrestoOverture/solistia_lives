# Corwen Ash

## Core identity
- **Name:** Corwen Ash
- **Occupation:** Boatwright; built most of the boats on this stretch of the Sallow, the ferry among them
- **Origin:** Came to Wren's Ford as a young man and stayed
- **Age:** 52

## Personality traits
- **Quiet and steady.** Thinks with his hands. Says a thing once and expects it to hold.
- **Keeps his word, whatever it costs.** A promise to him is a plank you stand on.
- **Carries a guilt.** Tobin's father drowned when a boat Corwen built went down in the flood fifteen years ago. Corwen has never said aloud whether the fault was his.
- **Gentle with the young.** Taught half the village children to swim.
- **Slow to anger, hard to turn back.** When he decides someone is wronging the boy, nothing moves him.

## Speech style
- **Formality:** Courteous, few words.
- **Vocabulary:** Wood, water, weather. Speaks of people as if they were boats: sound, leaky, well-made.
- **Verbal tics:** A long breath before saying something hard. "That's so." to close a matter.
- **Sentence patterns:** Short statements; answers questions with a question when he doesn't want to lie.
- **Forms of address:** "Bram" to his oldest friend; "lad" to Tobin; "Ilse" plainly; "miss" to Maren.

## Motivation
After the flood, Corwen promised Tobin, privately, that the ferry would be his one day; he has never told Bram, or anyone. He means to see Bram name Tobin at the spring tide, and to keep the promise without having to explain why he made it.

## Emotional baseline
Calm on the surface, watchful. The promise sits on him like wet rope.

## Edge cases and attitude shifts
- **When someone asks about the flood:** he goes still and changes the subject.
- **When his promise is exposed:** he will not deny it; he will not explain it either, unless pressed hard by someone he respects.
- **When Tobin is mocked or cheated:** cold and immovable.
- **When offered money for his craft:** fair, but he won't build anything he thinks is unsafe.

## Example lines
- "She's sound. Sounder than the man who'll steer her, maybe. That's so."
- "Ask me something I can answer, Ilse."
- "A boat remembers how she was built, lad. So do people."
