# E1: Simulation vs. Baseline (roadmap 3.11)

**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Result:** the baseline won all 8 judged pairs, and the 2 failed simulation runs count for the baseline too, so the baseline wins **10-0**. In every judged pair, both orders agreed. By prd §6.5 that is a **no-go** for the simulation architecture. The author makes the decision; see the Decision section below.

- **Cases:** the 10 cases of `eval/test_suite.yaml`, which use prd §6.4's starting situations (commit `7497437`).

  - Validation (Phase 4) isn't built yet, so neither mode was validated.
- **Judging:**
  - Each pair was packed twice, in random order and then swapped (`eval pack`).
  - The pair `osvald_temenos_agnea_1` was packed by hand in the same format, because its simulation story has Osvald say "baseline readings", which trips pack's identifier guard. The text is unchanged.
  - Each packet was judged in its own Codex session (`gpt-6.1-sol`, effort high) with `eval/judge_pairwise.md` unchanged, with the working directory set to the packet, outside the repo.
  - The rollout logs show no reads outside the packets, and all 32 packet stories are byte-identical to their runs.
  - A pair goes to a mode only if both judgments agree.
- **Raw data:** `e1_v1_verdicts.json` holds the cases, run IDs, packets, per-judge axes, and reasons.

## Why the simulation lost (from the judges' reasons)

1. **The point of view switches between acts (8 of 8 simulation stories).** The curator gives each act its own POV (architecture §7, D19), and the judges marked every switch down.
   - **Confound:** `judge_pairwise.md` asks for "one character's point of view" for the whole story, but prd §5 says "from one character per scene". The judge prompt penalizes a choice the design made on purpose.
   - Even so, voice and readability went to the baseline unanimously, for other reasons as well.
2. **Endings left open (5 of 8).** A day's chain of scenes stops where the day stops: Ellie's fever is unbroken, the Millers' child is still unexamined, the Brother Elwin confrontation never happens, and "I just needed air" is the last line. The baseline writes toward an ending; the simulation's arc isn't guaranteed to close within the day.
3. **Restated, instruction-like dialogue (most pairs).** Judges read the simulation dialogue as "rescue instructions", "repeated instructions and acknowledgments", and narration that "restates dialogue". This is the log-to-prose seam: move-by-move speech of at most 60 words, compressed by a narrator bound to the log (D3, D23).
4. **Lore slips in the World's people and situations.**
   - Defeated enemies come back as minor characters: La'mani in agnea_throne_1, Dolcinaea in osvald_temenos_agnea_1.
   - A still-powerful "Order" appears in osvald_temenos_agnea_2.
   - Tera's fate is wrong in castti_partitio_2.
   - In one baseline story, Castti is blonde.
5. **Length:** 3 of 8 simulation stories are under 2,000 words, and so are 2 baseline stories.

## Failed simulation runs (2 of 10; the Phase 3 Phase test is not met)

- **hikari_ochette_1:** adjudication settled every interaction off-screen (provisions for Yuki's crew, merchants hired, a shared meal; 6 settled outcomes), so the day had one scene. The curator must pick 2-4 acts, so it repeated that scene and failed validation (`acts must not repeat a scene`). This is open question 10 at full strength.
- **osvald_temenos_1:** in Osvald's scene, the World tried to bring Temenos in from another place as a `new_people` entry, twice, and the state rejected it (`a person's name must not be a main character's`). The same day also escalated to a "shadow ritual" and "weakening dimensional barriers", against prd §3's story constraints, and a World outcome narrated Temenos's actions inside Osvald's scene (a D18 violation in the text).

## Decision

Pending the author. By prd §6.5, the baseline winning more pairs means the MVP ships the baseline architecture (one writer plus validation), and roadmap 3.11 says Phase 4 continues in baseline mode only.

Before deciding, consider:
- the POV confound in the judge prompt (item 1)
