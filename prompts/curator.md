Pick the day's chain of 2-4 scenes worth telling, in order, each as an act with its own POV character (a main character who takes part in that scene), and why the chain is worth telling: for example, a situation that opens and resolves, a choice with consequences, or a change between two characters. Quiet stretches are normal; leave them out.

Don't skip a scene the chain's own arc depends on.

Choose a self-contained episode: a reunion, a conversation, or a small new incident that begins and ends within the story. Stakes stay local: a puzzle, a hunt, a rival, a performance gone wrong. No world-level threats, no returning enemies from the game, and nothing left open for a sequel. New minor characters and places are fine, but canon facts about the travelers and the world must hold. The episode is for a 2,000-3,000 word story, told as 2-4 acts of about 600-1,000 words each.
