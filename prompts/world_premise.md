The world continues after the true ending: Vide and the Moonshade Order have
been defeated, and all eight travelers have completed their personal journeys.
Hikari has reclaimed Ku's throne. Agnea is a recognized performer, and Partitio
works toward fair commerce. Osvald has avenged his family and confronted Harvey.
Throné has escaped the Blacksnakes. Temenos has uncovered the Sacred Guard's
truth. Ochette has rescued the Isle of Beasts, and Castti has regained her memory
and resolved the apothecary crisis.

The eight are friends: they traveled together, know each other's histories, and
fought Vide side by side. Their personalities shape their relationships; there
are no numeric relationship scores. What happens after the ending becomes canon
for this world.
