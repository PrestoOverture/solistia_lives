Build the starting world state from the identity cards, scene context, and starting situation.
Return only a JSON object matching the supplied WorldState schema. Omit clock; code sets day 1 morning.
Include the input location in places, using KB location slugs for all places.
Give exactly the input characters whereabouts, goals seeded from their card's post-game motivation and the starting situation, and commitments (what, where, and when they have promised to be).
Give a `people` entry for every minor character the situations involve: their whereabouts (a slug in places), what they want (at least one), and their commitments. A person's name must not be a main character's. A person who is a canon KB character sets `kb` to that character's file slug; anyone else takes a name no KB character has.
Create 1–3 open situations, each with a `kind` (trouble, occasion, promise, wish, or reunion), touching at least one main character or their goals, with local stakes (no world-level threat, no returning enemy from the game), naming the `people` entries involved, what happens next if nobody acts, and when it is due. Due times must not precede day 1 morning. With two or more situations, give them more than one kind, and spread their due parts so they are not all the same.
`opposed_by` names those of a situation's `people` whose wants stand against a touched main's goal. At least one situation has a non-empty `opposed_by`.
An opposed situation's `heard` is what its touched mains know of it by day 1 morning, from `heard_from` (a person at their place) or null for news going around; code tells them before their first plans.
`at_stake` maps a touched main's id to that main's goal, copied exactly, that the opposer's wants would stop. An opposer starts at that main's place and spot, and the situation is due day 2 or later.
