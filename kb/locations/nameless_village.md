# Nameless Village

> *"Welcome to Nameless Village! You bring stories? Tell news from outside!"*
> — Beastling at the village entrance

**Nameless Village** is an isolated, tradition-bound settlement nestled within the dense rainforests of [Toto'haha](totohaha.md). Inhabited primarily by a small, reclusive population of [Beastlings](../world/beastling.md), the village serves as the setting for Chapter 4 of [Temenos Mistral](../characters/temenos.md)'s investigation.

## Geography & Sacred Duty

Nameless Village connects to the [Southern Nameless Village Traverse](southern_nameless_village_traverse.md) to the south and opens into the mystical [Wandering Wood](wandering_wood.md) to the north. 

For generations, the villagers have acted as the solemn custodians of the surrounding wild, watching over the entrance to the Wandering Wood and the ancient sacred grounds of the [Rifted Rock](rifted_rock.md) that lie beyond. The villagers provide counsel and ritual guidance to those worthy outsiders who arrive seeking to undertake the arduous "trial" of journeying through the woods to the sacred altar.

## Governance & Customs

- **The Mayor & The Sacred Vessel**: The settlement is governed by an elder mayor, but its social and spiritual hierarchy is tied to an ancient custom: a chosen child is held in strict seclusion from birth to be groomed as the village heir. The current heir is a seven-year-old Beastling girl designated as the "vessel," completely forbidden from stepping foot outside her ancestral home. The governing elder, [Shirlutto](../characters/shirlutto.md), bears deep scars across her back sustained during her own sequestered youth as a vessel—scars whose true origin remains closely guarded.
- **Village Culture & Inhabitants**:
  - **Indigenous Brew**: The village produces a potent traditional alcohol, heavily rumored to be concocted from local insects and venomous serpents.
  - **Trauma of the Outside**: The village's wariness toward outsiders is underscored by the past experiences of its residents; one local Beastling was previously captured by human traffickers and exhibited as a spectacle in civilized settlements before returning home.
  - **Human Integration**: Despite the community's insular Beastling culture, a lone human resides peacefully within the village, serving as the local tavern-keeper.
