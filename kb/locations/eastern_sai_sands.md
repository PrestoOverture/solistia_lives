# Eastern Sai Sands

> "*...Allow me to tell you a legend passed down in this dojo. In this world, there are eight altars, each dedicated to one of the eight [gods](../world/gods.md). The altar of [Brand](../world/brand.md) the Thunderblade is hidden deep within the Eastern Sai Sands. If a chosen warrior were to find the altar, they would obtain unparalleled skill. None have succeeded to date. But perhaps it has been waiting for someone like you to find it...*"  
> — Warriors Guild Master

The **Eastern Sai Sands** is a desert region in [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md), located east of the settlement of [Sai](sai.md).

## Geography and Routes

The desert expanse serves as a crossing between Hinoeuma and the [Harborlands](harborlands.md):
- To the west, the sands lead into the [Southern Sai Sands](southern_sai_sands.md).
- To the east, the trail exits Hinoeuma toward the [Western Conning Creek Coast](western_conning_creek_coast.md).
- To the south lies the secluded [Altar of the Thunderblade](altar_of_the_thunderblade.md).
- To the north stands the entrance to the [Decaying Temple](decaying_temple.md).

## Notable Sites and Lore

- **Altar of the Thunderblade**: Hidden deep within the sands, this sacred altar is dedicated to [Brand](../world/brand.md) the Thunderblade, one of the eight gods of Solistia. According to legends preserved by the Warriors Guild, any chosen warrior who reaches the altar is blessed with peerless skill.
- **Decaying Temple**: An ancient ruin situated at the northern periphery of the sands.

## Wildlife

The Eastern Sai Sands harbor a wide variety of desert beasts and undead threats:
- **Fauna**: Desert Apes, towering Desert Apekings, Assassin Bugs, Giant Scorpions, Sandworms, massive Desert Worms, Spiked Skinks, and Dark Rollers.
- **Flora and Entities**: Withered Wanderweeds, Dire Gold Jellyfish, Insidious Ivory Ammonite, Whiskered Catfish, and remnants of fallen warriors known as Undead Soldiers.
