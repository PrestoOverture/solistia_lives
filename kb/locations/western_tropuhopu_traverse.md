# Western Tropu'hopu Traverse

The **Western Tropu'hopu Traverse** is an ancient coastal and jungle route in the [Toto'haha](totohaha.md) region, located directly west of the city of [Tropu'hopu](tropuhopu.md).

## Geography

Western Tropu'hopu Traverse serves as an essential thoroughfare across eastern Toto'haha, linking several key points:
- East: [Tropu'hopu](tropuhopu.md)
- West: [Beasting Bay: Anchorage](beasting_bay_anchorage.md)
- North: [Southern Nameless Village Traverse](southern_nameless_village_traverse.md)
- South: [Altar of the Huntress](altar_of_the_huntress.md)

The traverse features water channels navigable by boat, dense tropical brush, and ancient ruins scattered along the path.

## Landmarks

### Hunters' Guild
The guild headquarters for Hunters is established along the traverse. The guild master is an elderly [Beastling](../world/beastling.md) who claims to be 120 years old.

### Altar of the Huntress
Tucked away in the southern reaches of the traverse lies the [Altar of the Huntress](altar_of_the_huntress.md), dedicated to [Draefendi](../world/draefendi.md), the Huntress. According to local legend recounted by the Hunters' Guild Master, when a chosen hunter visits the sacred altar, they are granted special power by the goddess.

## Notable Inhabitants

- **Elderly Apothecary**: An older herbalist who can be found by ruins along the path during the day, searching for ingredients to brew *kampo*, a traditional medicine originating from [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md).
