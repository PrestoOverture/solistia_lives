# Harborlands

The **Harborlands** is one of the eight major regions of the continent of Solistia, encompassing the long, winding coastal lands of the south. Characterized by bustling ports, maritime commerce, oceanic cliffs, and emerging industrial hubs, the region serves as the primary maritime gateway between the western continent and lands beyond.

## Geography and Culture

Southern Solistia is defined by extensive tracts of coastline, maritime inlets, and ocean trade networks. 
- During the Age of Exploration roughly a century ago, ships departing from the piers of [Canalbrine](canalbrine.md) charted the vital sea routes connecting to the eastern continent.
- The region is famously marked by the colossal lighthouse of Canalbrine, constructed twenty years prior by engineers who fled the destruction of the kingdom of [U](../world/u.md). Built using specialized ore polished to a mirror sheen to serve as a reflector plate, its beacon is said to be visible across the water as far as [New Delsta](new_delsta.md).
- The Harborlands is also an important bridgehead for the Order of the Sacred Flame; the grand church of Canalbrine serves as a launching point for clerics and evangelists traveling from the eastern continent to spread the teachings of the [Sacred Flame](../world/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md).

## Major Settlements

- **[Canalbrine](canalbrine.md)**: A prosperous port town built directly over maritime waterways, where citizens navigate via canals, bridges, and small boats. It is central to the travels of the apothecary [Castti Florenz](../characters/castti.md) (Chapter 1) and the cleric [Temenos Mistral](../characters/temenos.md) (Chapter 2).
- **[Conning Creek](conning_creek.md)**: A serene coastal town nestled near steep seaside cliffs. It is known as the roosting site of the legendary wardenbeast [Cateracta](../world/cateracta.md). The cliffs outside town are also the sorrowful site where [Osvald V. Vanstein](../characters/osvald.md)'s wife and daughter supposedly died five years prior. The town features prominently in Osvald's tale (Chapter 3), [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md)'s quest (Chapter 2), and the crossed journey of Temenos and [Throné Anguis](../characters/throne.md).
- **[Roque Island](roque_island.md)**: An offshore private island and industrial compound owned by [Roque Brilliante](../characters/roque_brilliante.md), housing the sprawling headquarters and manufacturing facilities of the [Roque Company](../world/roque_company.md). It is accessible only by private vessel and serves as the climactic setting for [Partitio Yellowil](../characters/partitio.md)'s Chapter 4.

## Landmarks and Dungeons

### Altars
- **[Altar of the Charitable](altar_of_the_charitable.md)**: A hidden divine shrine honoring [Bifelgan](../world/bifelgan.md) the Trader.

### Field Routes
- **[Western Canalbrine Coast](western_canalbrine_coast.md)**: Coastal approaches to the western gates of Canalbrine.
- **[Canalbrine Bridge](canalbrine_bridge.md)**: The stone bridge spanning the harbor waterways.
- **[Northern Conning Creek Coast](northern_conning_creek_coast.md)** and **[Western Conning Creek Coast](western_conning_creek_coast.md)**: Rocky roads bordering the shores around Conning Creek.
- **[Roque Island: Anchorage](roque_island_anchorage.md)**: The fortified harbor handling cargo and passenger ships arriving at Roque Island.

### Caverns and Dungeons
- **[Canalbrine: Water Source](canalbrine_water_source.md)**: The subterranean spring that supplies fresh drinking water to Canalbrine, targeted by poisoners [Veron and Doron](../characters/veron_and_doron.md).
- **[Sacred Guard Ship](sacred_guard_ship.md)**: A naval galleon deployed by the [Sacred Guard](../world/sacred_guard.md), commandeered during Temenos's investigation into the crimes of [Vados the Architect](../characters/vados.md).
- **[Cavern of the Sea God](cavern_of_the_sea_god.md)**: An ocean cavern that served as the sacred den of [Cateracta](../world/cateracta.md), closely guarded by [Alpione](../characters/alpione.md).
- **[Cavern of the Moon and Sun](cavern_of_the_moon_and_sun.md)**: A hidden cavern shrouded in deep shadows whose passages open according to the changing light of the celestial bodies.
- **[Guard Outpost](guard_outpost.md)**: The coastal garrison in Conning Creek commanded by the corrupt [Captain Stenvar](../characters/stenvar.md).
- **[The Roque Company: West Tower](the_roque_company_west_tower.md) & [East Tower](the_roque_company_east_tower.md)**: Massive factory towers on Roque Island where heavy steam machinery, including the [Steam Tank Obsidian](../world/steam_tank_obsidian.md), are engineered and built.
- **[Lair of the Usurper](lair_of_the_usurper.md)**: A secluded grotto containing hidden plunder.
