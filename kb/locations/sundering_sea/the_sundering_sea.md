# The Sundering Sea

**The Sundering Sea** is the expansive ocean that divides the western and eastern continents of Solistia.

## Overview

The Sundering Sea serves as the vast oceanic expanse separating Solistia's continents and surrounding archipelagoes. While regular travel between the continents initially relies on established ferry anchorages between coastal ports, private voyages across its open waters become possible aboard the seafaring vessel *Grand Terry*, acquired during [Partitio Yellowil](../../characters/travelers/partitio.md)'s commercial endeavors in [Tropu'hopu](../../stories/side_stories/totohaha/the_scent_of_commerce_tropuhopu.md).

The open sea is populated by dangerous aquatic beasts and legendary sea monsters, including the [Battle-Worn Shark](../../world/beasts/battle_worn_shark.md) and the colossal [Scourge of the Sea](../../world/beasts/scourge_of_the_sea.md). Uniquely, during the cataclysmic eclipse of [The Journey for the Dawn](../../stories/main/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md), the open waters of the Sundering Sea are the only region where regular wildlife persists rather than succumbing entirely to shadowy fiends.

## Notable Isles and Landmarks

The Sundering Sea encompasses numerous isolated islands, ruins, and maritime landmarks:

- **[On the Water](wilds/on_the_water.md)**: The open expanse of ocean connecting the continents and surrounding archipelagos.
- **[Lighthouse Island](wilds/lighthouse_island.md)**: A lonely, frozen island inhabited solely by a lighthouse keeper and his wife.
- **[Nameless Isle](dungeons/nameless_isle.md)**: A remote tropical island inhabited by ferocious beasts such as the [Gigantes](../../world/beasts/gigantes.md), as well as the eccentric archaeologist [Georges Lazuli](../../characters/side_stories/georges_lazuli.md).
- **[The Lost Isle](wilds/the_lost_isle.md)**: An ancient island veiled in mist, housing a perplexing labyrinth of invisible pathways and the altar of the Arcanist.
- **[Shipwreck of the Empress](dungeons/shipwreck_of_the_empress.md)**: A sprawling, decaying vessel lost at sea and adrift on the waves.
- **[Curious Nest](dungeons/curious_nest.md)**: A gloomy sea grotto littered with the bones of fallen sailors and adventurers, guarded by the [Tyrannodrake](../../world/beasts/tyrannodrake.md).
- **[Vidania](dungeons/vidania.md)**: A legendary, sacred island upon which the [gods](../../world/gods/gods.md) originally sealed away the wicked deity [Vide](../../world/gods/vide.md).
- **[The Gate of Finis](dungeons/the_gate_of_finis_octopath_traveler_ii.md)**: A mysterious, uncharted island (designated in nautical charts simply as `???`) harboring interdimensional ruins and the sealed gateway to the fallen god [Galdera](../../world/gods/galdera.md).
