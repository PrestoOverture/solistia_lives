# Shipwreck of the Empress

The **Shipwreck of the Empress** is an eerie maritime dungeon located drifting in the northwestern waters of [The Sundering Sea](../the_sundering_sea.md).

## Overview

The ruined husk of the imperial vessel is infested with undead buccaneers and dangerous sea creatures. Deep inside the wreckage, a mysterious skeleton sits undisturbed behind a desk within the captain's cabin.

## Notable Relics

The wreckage hides several rare artifacts and armaments:
- Rusty Dagger: An ancient dagger that can be reforged into a Divine Weapon by the Master Blacksmith.
- Master Thief's Sapphire Stone
- Lost Tribe's Spear
- Cursed Shield
