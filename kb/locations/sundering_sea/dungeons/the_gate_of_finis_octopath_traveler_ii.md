# The Gate of Finis

The **Gate of Finis** (initially designated on nautical charts simply as **???**) is an ominous gateway located on an uncharted island in [The Sundering Sea](../the_sundering_sea.md). It serves as a portal connecting Solistia to the Netherworld plane where the dark god [Galdera](../../../world/gods/galdera.md) was sealed away.

> *"On an island lost in sea split twain,*  
> *lies a portal to an alien plane.*  
> *Beware! Be warned! Ye fools who'd approach,*  
> *for none should draw near, this gate encroach."*  
> — Kaie the poet

## Overview and Lore

To common sailors and passing voyagers, the secluded island appears to feature nothing more than a smooth, featureless cliff face and a barren expanse of rock. In truth, this stone facade conceals the dormant Gate of Finis, inscribed with ancient script.

Through research into ancient texts across Solistia—specifically chronicled across the quests [The Traveler's Bag](../../../stories/side_stories/the_travelers_bag.md), [Procuring Peculiar Tomes](../../../stories/side_stories/crestlands/procuring_peculiar_tomes.md), and [From the Far Reaches of Hell](../../../stories/side_stories/crestlands/from_the_far_reaches_of_hell.md)—scholars and travelers uncover the rituals required to reveal the entrance in the quest [A Gate Between Worlds](../../../stories/side_stories/sundering_sea/a_gate_between_worlds.md).

When activated, the blank wall transforms into the colossal gateway, granting passage into the dark abyssal realm guarded by the Omniscient Eye and Galdera himself. Upon the fallen god's defeat, the portal is permanently sealed once more, its mystical archway reverting into an ordinary, impassable wall of stone.
