# Nameless Isle

**Nameless Isle** is an uncharted island and optional dungeon situated out upon the waters of [The Sundering Sea](../the_sundering_sea.md). Far removed from the established governance of Solistia's major nations, the isolated isle is characterized by untamed wilderness and ancient ruins.

The island became the site of a hazardous archaeological expedition undertaken by [Georges Lazuli](../../../characters/side_stories/georges_lazuli.md), an eccentric scholar and researcher hailing from [Montwise](../../crestlands/towns/montwise.md). Georges traveled to the island to investigate its ancient history and forgotten relics, having to contend with the savage wildlife inhabiting the shoreline and subterranean passages. Deep within the island's interior dwells the ancient stone monstrosity [Gigantes](../../../world/beasts/gigantes.md), an archaic guardian watching over the isle's secluded treasures.
