# Vidania

**Vidania**, known in myth as the **Isle of Illusions** and **World's End**, is a legendary lost island located in [The Sundering Sea](../the_sundering_sea.md). It serves as the ancient prison of the wicked god [Vide](../../../world/gods/vide.md).

> *"That's... The battlefield of the [gods](../../../world/gods/gods.md)... Vidania. The isle of illusions - a legend passed down by the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../../../world/factions/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md). To think it actually exists..."*
> — [Temenos Mistral](../../../characters/temenos/temenos.md)

## History

According to scripture preserved by the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../../../world/factions/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md), Vidania was the primordial battlefield where the eight [gods](../../../world/gods/gods.md) of Solistia fought Vide. Harnessing the combined divine strength of his brethren, [Aelfric](../../../world/gods/aelfric.md) struck down the wicked god. Using the divine embers of the [Sacred Flames](../../../world/lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md), the gods sealed Vide within the earth and submerged the entire island beneath the ocean floor to prevent his awakening.

The summit of the island houses the ancient ruins known as **Castle Vidania**, bearing heraldic emblems shaped like Vide's all-seeing eye.

## The Journey for the Dawn

Millennia after its sinking, the conspirators of the [Moonshade Order](../../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md) extinguished the four Sacred Flames across Solistia, shattering the ancient seal and causing Vidania to rise once more from the depths of the Sundering Sea.

[Oboro](../../../characters/hikari/oboro.md) journeyed to Castle Vidania to execute the final rite of resurrection. Pursued by the eight travelers—who had rekindled the Sacred Flames—Oboro challenged their pursuit of hope before casting himself backward into the dark flames, sacrificing his body and soul to manifest Vide into the world.

Upon the desolate peaks of Vidania, the eight travelers joined together in a final struggle against Vide, vanquishing the wicked god and driving away the [Shadow](../../../world/lore/shadow.md). With Vide's destruction, the eternal night over Solistia was dispelled, and the travelers stood together to watch the sun rise over the dawn of a new era. Following Vide's defeat, the island once more became lost to the sea.
