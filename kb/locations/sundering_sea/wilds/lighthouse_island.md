# Lighthouse Island

**Lighthouse Island** is a field location situated on the northern fringes of the [Sundering Sea](../the_sundering_sea.md), west of [Frigit Isle](../../winterlands/towns/frigit_isle.md).

The island is home to a lighthouse keeper and his wife. The keeper possesses a piece of aelmorite needed to repair the lighthouse in [Canalbrine](../../harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md).
