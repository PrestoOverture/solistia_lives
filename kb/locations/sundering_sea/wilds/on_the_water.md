# On the Water

**On the Water** is a field location representing navigation across the open waters of the [Sundering Sea](../the_sundering_sea.md).

## Overview

The open waters of the [Sundering Sea](../the_sundering_sea.md) become freely traversable after purchasing an ocean-faring vessel during [Partitio](../../../characters/travelers/partitio.md)'s quest, [The Scent of Commerce: Tropu'hopu](../../../stories/side_stories/totohaha/the_scent_of_commerce_tropuhopu.md). 

Navigating the waters allows travelers to reach isolated islands, investigate shipwrecks, and confront formidable sea terrors dwelling in the deep, including the [Battle-Worn Shark](../../../world/beasts/battle_worn_shark.md) and the [Scourge of the Sea](../../../world/beasts/scourge_of_the_sea.md).
