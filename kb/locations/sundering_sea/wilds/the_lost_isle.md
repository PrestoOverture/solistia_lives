# The Lost Isle

**The Lost Isle** is a mysterious, secluded island situated in the northeastern reaches of [The Sundering Sea](../the_sundering_sea.md).

> *"Find the true path."*
> — Signpost at the island entrance

## The Labyrinth and the Secret Path

The island is dominated by a sprawling stone maze populated only by eerie specters known as Blood Remnants and Blood Revenants. At the end of the labyrinth, a second signpost offers cryptic guidance:

> *"The true path... Free from wickedness and distraction, Thy mind's eye shall be thy guide."*

Rather than following the maze's deceptive dead ends, a traveler who observes closely will find an invisible pathway leading westward across the open air, faintly glimmering with blue light.

## The Arcanist Descendant

Across the invisible bridge sits a secluded sanctuary where the last **Arcanist Descendant** awaits. Speaking in the archaic tongue of the ancient gods (*"Unto thee... I impart the lost secrets of the heavens"*), the mystic bestows the forgotten wisdom of the Arcanist job upon worthy seekers. Having fulfilled this sacred obligation, the descendant promptly disappears.

## Relics

The isle harbors several ancient relics, including the Lost Tribe's Dagger, the Great Sage's Staff, and multiple Ancient Cursed Talismans.
