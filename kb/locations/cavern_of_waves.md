# Cavern of Waves

The **Cavern of Waves** is a remote sea grotto situated along the coastline of [Toto'haha](totohaha.md), accessible via [Beasting Bay: Anchorage](beasting_bay_anchorage.md).

## Overview

Carved into the coastal bluffs of Toto'haha by the ceaseless assault of ocean currents, the Cavern of Waves is filled with surging seawater and resonant subterranean echoes. The deep chambers are hazardous and seldom braved by common beastlings or sailors.

## Wildlife & Inhabitants

The dark flooded channels and rocky caverns house dangerous tropical and marine wildlife, including massive Giant Gators, Demon Jellyfish, Thorny Eels, Ducaducas, swarms of Horse Fly and their Lord of the Flies, alongside supernatural apparitions such as the Light Revenant and Wind Remnant Mk.II.
