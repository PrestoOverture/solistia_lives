# Canalbrine Bridge

**Canalbrine Bridge** is a major coastal crossing and field route situated in the [Harborlands](harborlands.md).

## Geography

The bridge spans coastal shallows and serves as a vital crossroad connecting multiple territories:
- To the east lies the [Western Canalbrine Coast](western_canalbrine_coast.md).
- To the west, the road crosses into Hinoeuma via the [Northern Ryu Sands](northern_ryu_sands.md).
- To the south, trails lead along the [Northern Conning Creek Coast](northern_conning_creek_coast.md).
- Looming to the north is the [Lair of the Usurper](lair_of_the_usurper.md).

## History and Architecture

Both Canalbrine Bridge and the neighboring coastal lighthouse near the [Lair of the Usurper](lair_of_the_usurper.md) bear heavy scars of warfare and decay, leaving sections of the grand structure broken and in disrepair. Local records indicate past military conflicts devastated the span. 

Despite its ruined state, the site attracts ambitious craftsmen determined to see it restored:

> "*I want to build a bridge that will last forever.*"
