# Flamechurch: Cathedral

**Flamechurch: Cathedral**, also known as **Aelfric Cathedral**, stands at the northern terminus of [Flamechurch](flamechurch.md) in the [Crestlands](crestlands.md). Serving as the seat and holy sanctuary of the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../world/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md), it draws vast multitudes of pilgrims from across Solistia. The grand structure connects south to the [Flamechurch: Cathedral Entrance](flamechurch_cathedral_entrance.md) and features passages down to the [Cathedral Cellars](cathedral_cellars.md).

## History & Architecture

Two years prior to the events of the journey, the cathedral underwent comprehensive structural remodeling overseen by [Vados](../characters/vados.md) the Architect.

- **The Stained Glass Window**: Dominating the sanctum is a magnificent central window crafted using large elemental soulstones of fire, ice, thunder, and wind. Beyond its artistic grandeur, the window's embedded elemental energies serve as a consecrated ward against malevolence and evil forces.
- **The Pontiff's Chambers**: Located in the far right wing of the cathedral, these private chambers housed the supreme leadership of the Order. The room conceals a hidden passageway giving access to secret upper chambers.
- **Ecclesiastical Archives**: The cathedral houses sacred scriptures and intimate historical writings, including the private journals of past pontiffs, preserving insights and esoteric lore not found even in the grand [library](montwise_library.md) of [Montwise](montwise.md).
- **Sanctuary Duties**: Clerics maintain constant vigil within the halls, receiving confessions from pilgrims by day and admitting urgent petitions by night.

## Story Events

- **The Murder of Pontiff Jörg**: During [Temenos](../characters/temenos.md)'s investigation in Chapter 1, [Pontiff Jörg](../characters/jorg.md) summons the inquisitor to discuss troubling whispers of emergent heresy. Before their discussion can take place, Temenos and the Sanctum Knight [Crick](../characters/crick_wellsley.md) arrive to find Jörg brutally slain. The beast [Felvarg](../characters/felvarg.md) had breached the high window, lured into the inner sanctum by an incense deliberately prepared to draw the creature.
- **The Mystery of "Alpates"**: During their joint investigation, Temenos and [Throné](../characters/throne.md) search the cathedral after hearing rumors of a divine treasure named "Alpates," reputed to be a relic of the gods. Their search uncovers the secret door within the pontiff's chambers leading to the hidden upper floor, where they encounter a woman named [Alpates](../characters/alpates.md). She entrusts them with a fragment of an ancient clouded mirror before slipping away to reunite with her clan.
- **Window Restoration**: Following the beast attack that shattered the great window, a cleric undertakes the side story [Cathedral Window Repair](../stories/cathedral_window_repair.md) to gather the elemental soulstones necessary to reforge its warding glass.
