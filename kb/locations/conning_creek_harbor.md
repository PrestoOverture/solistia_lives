# Conning Creek: Harbor

**Conning Creek: Harbor** is the port sub-area of the town of [Conning Creek](conning_creek.md), located within the [Harborlands](harborlands.md).

## Overview

The harbor connects south directly to [Conning Creek](conning_creek.md) and north toward the [Cavern of the Sea God](cavern_of_the_sea_god.md). Beyond its daily trade in freshly hauled catches, the harbor is known for its dried fish.

## Story

During her Chapter 2 ([Cateracta](../world/cateracta.md) Route), [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md) journeys to the harbor following the mournful cry echoing across the sea. After confronting [Alpione](../characters/alpione.md) at the pier, Ochette looks out to an adjacent islet to discover the massive skeletal remains of Cateracta, who was slain while weakened by the [Dark Hunter](../characters/petrichor.md). The wind whistling through the hollow bones produces a sorrowful sound that echoes across the waters.
