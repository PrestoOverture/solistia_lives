# Northern Ryu Sands

**Northern Ryu Sands** is an arid desert passage in the region of [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md).

## Geography and Connections

The desert trail links central Hinoeuma with the neighboring Harborlands and Wildlands:
- **[Ryu](ryu.md)** to the south, the desert settlement where [Hikari Ku](../characters/hikari.md) begins his exiled journey
- **[Canalbrine Bridge](canalbrine_bridge.md)** to the east, spanning the border toward the Harborlands
- **[Southern Oresrush Wilds](southern_oresrush_wilds.md)** to the north, leading toward the mining frontiers of the Wildlands

## Ecology and Inhabitants

The unforgiving dunes are inhabited by harsh desert wildlife and nocturnal undead, including Desert Apes, swarms of Army Ants and Dire Army Ants, Cactus Rollers, Skull Rollers, Marionette Skeletons, and burrowing Sandworms. Local water features within the wastes support specialized aquatic life, such as Ivory Ammonites and Gold Jellyfish.

## Story Events

- **The Traveler's Bag**: If Hikari is selected as the starting protagonist, the side quest *The Traveler's Bag* occurs on these sands as he departs Ryu, assisting a robbed wayfarer in recovering stolen possessions.
- **Weapons of Ku**: Following the events of Hikari's Chapter 3, the cache of contraband weapons confiscated from the [Secret Forest](secret_forest.md) is transported here and guarded by a Ku loyalist on the northwestern reaches of the trail.
