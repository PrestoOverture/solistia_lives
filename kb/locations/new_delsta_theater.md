# New Delsta: Theater

> "*Welcome to the Delsta Theater, where our guests want for nothing!*"
> — [La'mani](../characters/lamani.md)

The **New Delsta: Theater** (also known as the Delsta Theater) is a lavish entertainment venue situated in the northern sector of [New Delsta](new_delsta.md) in the [Brightlands](brightlands.md). It serves as the artistic centerpiece of the metropolis, attracting wealthy spectators and renowned performers from across Solistia.

## Architecture and History

The theater connects southward to the main streets of [New Delsta](new_delsta.md) and westward to the **[Theater: Backstage](theater_backstage.md)** area. 

- **Construction**: A carpenter named Lind hailing from [Healeaks](abandoned_village.md) originally assisted in the building's construction.
- **Performances and Stars**: The interior is adorned with posters celebrating the superstar dancer [Dolcinaea Luciel](../characters/dolcinaea_luciel.md). Productions hosted in the auditorium range from grand musicals to eclectic plays, including a work titled *Cat Egg for Sale*.
- **Cuani's Legacy**: Decades prior, Agnea's late mother, the legendary dancer [Cuani Bristarni](../characters/cuani_bristarni.md), performed upon this stage.

## Story Events

- **Agnea's Chapter 2**: [Agnea Bristarni](../characters/agnea.md) comes to New Delsta with dreams of performing in the grand theater, following in the footsteps of her mother. However, access is strictly controlled by ticket and monitored by the haughty manager [La'mani](../characters/lamani.md), leading Agnea to perform first in the Backstreets before challenging the establishment.
- **The Epilogue**: In the culmination of the travelers' journey, Agnea fulfills her lifelong dream by taking center stage at the New Delsta Theater for a triumphant performance. The living companions, allies, and friends she made across Solistia gather together in the auditorium to watch the spectacle.
