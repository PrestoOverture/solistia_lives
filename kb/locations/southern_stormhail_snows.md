# Southern Stormhail Snows

The **Southern Stormhail Snows** is a snowy mountain passage in the [Winterlands](winterlands.md), connecting [Northern Montwise Pass](northern_montwise_pass.md) to the fortress city of [Stormhail](stormhail.md).

## Overview

Stretching across the frozen highlands beneath the shadow of the mountains, the trail serves as the primary route linking the Crestlands around Montwise with the Winterlands. Along the route branches the path leading [Beneath the Wall](beneath_the_wall.md). 

The imposing [Great Wall](../world/great_wall.md) in this region draws scholars and Sacred Guard knights who brave the bitter mountain chill to investigate ancient architecture and uncover the mythic [Infernal Castle](infernal_castle.md). As one observing [Sanctum Knight](../world/sacred_guard.md) notes:

> *"These scholars are tough. They push on with their research in this cold..."*
