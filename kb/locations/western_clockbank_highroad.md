# Western Clockbank Highroad

**Western Clockbank Highroad** is a major crossroads and field route in the [Brightlands](brightlands.md).

## Geography and Connections

The Western Clockbank Highroad serves as an essential overland thoroughfare linking several key regions and settlements across eastern Solistia:

- **East**: Connects to the [Southern Clockbank Highroad](southern_clockbank_highroad.md), continuing onward toward the factory city of [Clockbank](clockbank.md).
- **West**: Links with the [Eastern New Delsta Highroad](eastern_new_delsta_highroad.md), leading travelers to the metropolis of [New Delsta](new_delsta.md).
- **North**: Ascends into the [Eastern Flamechurch Pass](eastern_flamechurch_pass.md), serving as a gateway into the mountains of the [Crestlands](crestlands.md).
- **South**: Leads to the entrance of the [Sunken Maw](sunken_maw.md), a treacherous subterranean cavern.
