# Castle Ku

**Castle Ku** is the grand citadel and royal seat of the kingdom of [Ku](../world/ku.md), situated above [Ku: Castle Town](ku_castle_town.md) in [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md).

## Overview

As the political and military stronghold of the realm, Castle Ku stood impregnable for centuries amidst the endless warfare of the desert sands. Built with grand timber and stone, its architecture features black lacquer adorned with vivid vermilion and scarlet hues, projecting an air of stern dignity and martial authority.

Inside a lower chamber on the castle's eastern wing, the strategist [Oboro](../characters/oboro.md) maintained his private writings ([Oboro's Journal](../stories/oboros_journal_script.md)), recording the shadow machinations behind the realm's downfall.

## Story Significance

- **The Coup**: At the conclusion of Chapter 1 of [Hikari](../characters/hikari.md)'s tale, Prince Mugen executes a bloody coup, slaying King Jigo and seizing the fortress. Forced into exile, Hikari departs the citadel to gather loyal allies across Solistia.
- **The Liberation of Ku**: In the climax of Chapter 5, Hikari and his comrades storm the fortress, overcoming elite Ku defenders and guardians to confront [King Mugen](../characters/mugen.md) in the royal throne room. After defeating his brother, Hikari ascends the throne as king.

## Post-War Era

Under King Hikari's rule, Castle Ku undergoes profound reforms:
- Breaking centuries of rigid isolation, the citadel gates are opened, allowing common folk and citizens to walk the castle grounds freely.
- Although extensive post-war reconstruction severely strained the royal treasury and thinned administrative staff, the people of Ku embraced the dawn of a peaceful era:

> "*The wounds left by the civil war are deep, but nevertheless, our people still look to the future.*"
