# Montwise: Library

> *"Welcome to the Montwise library. What sort of tome are you looking for?"*
> — Librarian

The **Montwise Library** (known in full as the Montwise Grand Library) is a prominent institution and sub-area within the academic town of [Montwise](montwise.md), located in the [Crestlands](crestlands.md). Housing generations of Solistian history, scholarship, and literature, it stands as the continent's greatest repository of knowledge.

## Layout & Architecture

The library connects directly to the streets of [Montwise](montwise.md), as well as a hidden passage on its second subterranean level leading into the [Underground Laboratory](underground_laboratory.md):
- **Section Layout**: Books on dance are housed in the eastern wing, while geology texts are located on the second basement floor, situated directly before the hidden entryway to the secret laboratory.
- **Architectural Details**: The library's stained-glass windows share identical ornate patterns with those seen in [Lostseed Castle](lostseed_castle.md), the [Abandoned Church](abandoned_church.md), and [Flamechurch Cathedral](flamechurch_cathedral.md). Among its interior furnishings and decor is a preserved traditional weaving pattern hailing from [Toto'haha](totohaha.md).

## Notable Lore & Inhabitants

- **The Head Librarian**: The head librarian possesses a legendary memory, capable of recalling the precise shelf and location of every tome within the vast archives without consulting a catalog.
- **Unusual Tomes**: The library features a specialized "Unusual Tomes" department. Through the side quest *[Procuring Peculiar Tomes](../stories/procuring_peculiar_tomes.md)*, the collection acquires three exceptionally rare texts: *Dispatches from [Beastling](../world/beastling.md) Island*, *The Curious Legend of the [Great Wall](../world/great_wall.md)*, and *From the Far Reaches of Hell*.
- **The Beastling Scholar**: By night, a young Beastling secretly slips into the archives to devour any volume she can reach, having developed a keen fascination with clerics and the doctrines of the [Sacred Flame](../world/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md).
- **Scholarly Visitors**: The archives attract professionals from across the realm, including a presiding judge from [Timberain](timberain.md) who spends his days studying ancient legal precedents.
- **Temenos's Visit**: In the past, the cleric [Temenos](../characters/temenos.md) traveled to the library on an errand dispatched by [Pontiff Jörg](../characters/jorg.md). Temenos later recalled that the librarian serving at that time was an exceptionally peculiar individual, though he offered no further details.
