# Sacred Guard Ship

> *"There, [Crick](../characters/crick_wellsley.md)! That's the [Sacred Guard](../world/sacred_guard.md)'s ship! We must be swift!"*
> — [Temenos](../characters/temenos.md), pursuing [Vados](../characters/vados.md)

The **Sacred Guard Ship** is an official vessel of the [Sacred Guard](../world/sacred_guard.md) docked at the harbor of [Canalbrine](canalbrine.md) in the [Harborlands](harborlands.md).

## Story

During Chapter 2 of [Temenos](../characters/temenos.md)'s story, Temenos and the Sacred Guard knight [Crick](../characters/crick_wellsley.md) board the ship to corner [Vados the Architect](../characters/vados.md). Temenos seeks to interrogate Vados and hold him accountable for the murders of [Pontiff Jörg](../characters/jorg.md), [Lucian](../characters/lucian.md), and Canalbrine's local apothecary. 

The vessel remains moored at Canalbrine's harbor until Temenos finishes his subsequent investigations in Chapter 3, after which the ship departs the port.
