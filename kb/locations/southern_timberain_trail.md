# Southern Timberain Trail

The **Southern Timberain Trail** is a forested road located in the [Leaflands](leaflands.md), serving as the southern approach to the kingdom's seat at [Timberain](timberain.md).

## Geography and Layout

The trail connects several key destinations across the region:
- **[Timberain](timberain.md)** to the north
- **[Northern Wellgrove Trail](northern_wellgrove_trail.md)** to the east
- **[House Wellows Manor](house_wellows_manor.md)** to the northwest

## Lore and Hauntings

The path borders the accursed ruins of [House Wellows Manor](house_wellows_manor.md) and is touched by supernatural occurrences linked to the side quest [A Mysterious Box](../stories/a_mysterious_box.md).

- **The Ghost on the Pergola**: At night, travelers resting along the trail near Timberain may spot the apparition of a young girl standing on a wooden pergola. If approached, the phantom instantly vanishes into thin air.
- **The Worn Journal**: Once the tragic secret of the manor is resolved during [A Mysterious Box](../stories/a_mysterious_box.md), the ghost disappears forever, leaving behind the [Worn Journal](../stories/worn_journal_script.md) upon the pergola to reveal her fate.
- **The Spectral Voice**: Should anyone carry the Mysterious Box along the trail toward the manor, an eerie disembodied voice from the west echoes across the trees, demanding: *"Give it back."*
