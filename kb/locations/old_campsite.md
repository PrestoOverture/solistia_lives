# Old Campsite

The **Old Campsite** is a field location in [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md), connecting [Sandflow Pass](sandflow_pass.md) to the [Sand Lion's Den](sand_lions_den.md).

## Overview

The site was once an encampment in the desert of [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md) that was abandoned after the ground gave way in a collapse. Upon arriving at the ruined encampment during her journey, [Castti](../characters/castti.md) observes:

> *"There's no one here. I can only pray they weren't caught when the ground gave way..."*
