# Sai: East District

> *"I love this town. Lots of people drift through, after all."*
> — Local Townsperson

**Sai: East District** is an eastern district of the desert town of [Sai](sai.md) in [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md), bordering the mountainous path toward [Dragonridge](dragonridge.md).

## Overview & History

The structures in the East District were originally erected as the fortifications of Fort Orochi under the ancient kingdom of [U](../world/u.md). Following its fall, the stone ruins and old buildings became a impoverished quarter that houses drifters, orphans, and refugees seeking shelter in the desert.

## Landmarks & Inhabitants

- **Mother's Statue**: A memorial statue erected in tribute to [Cuani](../characters/cuani_bristarni.md), standing as an emblem of hope for the downtrodden. Two decades prior, Cuani danced throughout this district and mentored the young orphan [Dolcinaea](../characters/dolcinaea_luciel.md). During [Agnea Bristarni](../characters/agnea.md)'s fourth chapter, the statue is destroyed in the conflict over the town's ownership.
- **The Orphanage**: A shelter for impoverished children maintained by [Platt](../characters/platt.md) and his wife.
- **Dolcinaea's Old Dwelling**: A secluded warehouse in the heart of the district where Dolcinaea lived in extreme poverty prior to her stardom. The quarters contain simple worn attire, a poster of Cuani, and the [Dancer's Journal](../stories/dancers_journal_script.md).
- **Drifters and Outcasts**: The district's night markets and dusty alleys attract transient wanderers, including an exiled swordswoman fugitive and an itinerant thief marked by chains on his wrists.
