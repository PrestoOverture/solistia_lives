# Prison: Underground Passage

The **Prison: Underground Passage** is a subterranean tunnel complex situated beneath the penal colony on [Frigit Isle](frigit_isle.md) in the [Winterlands](winterlands.md), linking the [Frigit Isle: Yard](frigit_isle_yard.md) to [Frigit Isle: Entrance](frigit_isle_entrance.md).

## Geography and Architecture

Constructed beneath the frozen fortress yard, the passage consists of damp, ice-encrusted stone corridors and drainage channels running adjacent to the guards' quarters. Deep within its shadowed vaults stands a forgotten, bloodstained guillotine, evidencing that clandestine executions were once carried out away from the eyes of the general prison population. The freezing tunnels harbor cold-adapted vermin, including frost moles, white bats, and ice wisps.

## Story

The underground passage is central to [Osvald V. Vanstein](../characters/osvald.md)'s carefully calculated escape during Chapter 1. Having analyzed the prison's structural blueprints and guard shift schedules, Osvald identifies an entry grate beside the guards' station. Accompanied by his fellow inmate [Emerald](../characters/emerald.md), Osvald traverses the subterranean passages toward the outer cliffs, ultimately confronting the cruel [Warden Davids](../characters/davids.md) before making their daring leap into the freezing ocean.
