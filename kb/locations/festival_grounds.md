# Festival Grounds

> "*[Agnea](../characters/agnea.md)! Today, this stage'll be all yours!*"  
> — Cropdale Villager

The **Festival Grounds** is an outdoor community venue located in the [Leaflands](leaflands.md), situated just north of the rural village of [Cropdale](cropdale.md) and connected via the [Forest Path](forest_path.md).

## Description and Atmosphere

The grounds serve as the focal point for Cropdale's annual village celebrations. Set amidst open woodland clearings, the site is arranged with festive food stalls, banners, and a central raised wooden performance stage. The festivities traditionally run uninterrupted from sundown to sunrise. Kept clear and protected by the villagers, the grounds are free from wandering wild beasts.

## Story Events

During Chapter 1 of [Agnea Bristarni](../characters/agnea.md)'s tale, the village prepares to host its long-awaited festival. The celebration faces disaster when a voracious boar-like beast, the [Duorduor](../characters/duorduor.md), is lured toward the grounds by the aroma of cooking food. After Agnea and her tavern employer [Gus](../characters/gus.md) venture into the adjacent [Veil of Trees](veil_of_trees.md) to rescue her sister [Pala Bristarni](../characters/pala_bristarni.md) and vanquish the monster, the festival proceeds.

That evening, Agnea takes center stage before her assembled neighbors and family, performing a moving dance in honor of her late mother, [Cuani Bristarni](../characters/cuani_bristarni.md). The performance marks her final night in Cropdale before setting off on her journey across Solistia to become a star.
