# Crestlands

The **Crestlands** is one of the eight major regions of Solistia, defined by its towering mountain peaks, rolling hills, and deep religious devotion.

## Overview

The region is dominated by the faith of the [Sacred Flame](../world/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md). Perched high atop a stony summit stands [Aelfric Cathedral](flamechurch_cathedral.md), the grand seat of the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../world/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md). Pilgrims have journeyed here since time immemorial to worship before the Sacred Flame housed within. Even through the bitter winter a century past, the holy flame continued to burn, sustaining the spirits of the faithful. In spiritual, moral, and political authority, the church serves as the sovereign influence across the province.

## Settlements

- **[Flamechurch](flamechurch.md)**: A tranquil, autumnal village nestled at the base of the cathedral. While modest in size, its citizenry thrives by hosting and supplying the steady stream of pilgrims making their way up the mountain path.
- **[Montwise](montwise.md)**: A bustling mountain metropolis famous for two monumental institutions: its peerless grand [library](montwise_library.md)—a repository of boundless history and scholarship—and its violent [underground arena](montwise_underground_arena.md).
- **[Merry Hills](merry_hills.md)**: A vibrant, sprawling city celebrated as the cultural center of music and theater in Solistia. Every year, it hosts the [Grand Gala](../world/grand_gala.md), an international festival attracting performers, artists, and audiences from across the realm.

## Sacred Sites & Notable Locations

- **[Altar of the Flamebringer](altar_of_the_flamebringer.md)**: An ancient shrine dedicated to the god of light, [Aelfric](../world/aelfric.md).
- **[Cathedral Cellars](cathedral_cellars.md)**: Subterranean vaults and catacombs beneath [Flamechurch: Cathedral](flamechurch_cathedral.md).
- **[Seat of the Water Sprite](seat_of_the_water_sprite.md)**: Secluded ruins concealed behind a thundering mountain waterfall.
- **[Forsaken Graveyard](forsaken_graveyard.md)**: A somber, neglected cemetery below Montwise, dimly lit with vigil candles and haunted by the horrific Devourer of Dreams.
- **[Abandoned Church](abandoned_church.md)**: A crumbling house of worship standing in the wilderness east of Montwise.
- **[Underground Laboratory](underground_laboratory.md)**: A secret research compound beneath Montwise established by [Harvey](../characters/harvey.md) to conduct monstrous alchemical and biological experiments.
- **[Shrine of Ul'sterra](shrine_of_ulsterra.md) & [Stage of the Moon and Sun](stage_of_the_moon_and_sun.md)**: A grand amphitheater and sacred shrine dedicated to [Sealticge](../world/sealticge.md), the Lady of Grace, serving as the setting for the Festival of Grace.
