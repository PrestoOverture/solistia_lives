# Beasting Bay: Anchorage

**Beasting Bay: Anchorage** is a harbor and field area on the island of [Toto'haha](totohaha.md).

> *"Whenever we crew members find something on the ferry that a passenger accidentally left behind, we take good care of it."*  
> — Ferry Staff

## Geography and Routes

The anchorage acts as Toto'haha's primary maritime connection to the western and eastern continents, operating ferry services to [Canalbrine](canalbrine.md) in the Harborlands and [New Delsta Harbor: Anchorage](new_delsta_harbor_anchorage.md) in the Brightlands. 

By overland and coastal routes, the anchorage connects to:
- [North Beasting Traverse](north_beasting_traverse.md) leading inland toward [Beasting Village](beasting_village.md)
- [Western Tropu'hopu Traverse](western_tropuhopu_traverse.md)
- [Cavern of Waves](cavern_of_waves.md)

## Life and Culture

The harbor bustles with islanders, travelers, and ferry hands. The ferry crew diligently manages passenger affairs, keeping custody of forgotten personal effects and sharing unclaimed provisions among the crew. 

Each morning, a young beastling arrives from the [North Beasting Traverse](north_beasting_traverse.md) to open a food stall west of the dock landing, selling basic meats and provisions to departing voyagers.

Following the conclusion of her hunts across Solistia and the resolution of [Alpione's Next Chapter](../stories/alpiones_next_chapter.md), the hunter [Alpione](../characters/alpione.md) can be encountered residing around the anchorage.
