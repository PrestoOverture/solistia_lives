# Frigit Isle: Mining Site

> *"I understand this island was once dotted with mines. Entire mountains were reduced to rubble for ore, and the prison was built atop the wreckage. To this day there remains phosphoal buried in the frigid earth. It's immensely flammable, and sells for a fair bit of coin. So the prisoners are forced to mine it, and the guards line their pockets with the proceeds."* — [Osvald](../characters/osvald.md)

**Frigit Isle: Mining Site** is a forced-labor quarry located on [Frigit Isle](frigit_isle.md) in the [Winterlands](winterlands.md), situated immediately east of [Frigit Isle: Yard](frigit_isle_yard.md).

## Overview

Frigit Isle was originally home to extensive mining operations that reduced whole mountains to rubble in pursuit of ore before the penal colony was built over the abandoned works. Buried deep within the frozen ground is phosphoal, an extremely volatile and flammable substance highly valued across Solistia.

Prisoners held on Frigit Isle were subjected to punishing labor shifts at the mining site, forced to extract phosphoal under the watchful surveillance of Warden [Davids](../characters/davids.md) and the prison guards, who lined their pockets by selling the mined minerals. Due to the extreme arctic temperatures and physical exhaustion, many convicts collapsed while working the site; those who could no longer work were frequently finished off by the guards.
