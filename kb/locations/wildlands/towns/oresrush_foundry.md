# Oresrush: Foundry

The **Foundry** is an industrial sub-area within the mining town of [Oresrush](oresrush.md) in the [Wildlands](../wildlands.md).

## Overview

Located in the upper section of [Oresrush](oresrush.md), the foundry was originally built to smelt and process silver ore extracted from the local mines. 

Beneath its industrial surface, the abandoned foundry building secretly served as a horrific base of operations for [The Slaver](../../../characters/throne/the_slaver.md), an operative connected to [Mother](../../../characters/throne/mother.md) and the Blacksnakes. During [Throné Anguis](../../../characters/throne/throne.md)'s journey ([Mother](../../../characters/throne/mother.md)'s Route), she infiltrates the locked facility to find the Slaver presiding over human trafficking and forcing captives into lethal games of chance at a macabre fixture known as "Death's Table."

Following Throné's confrontation with the Slaver, the foundry falls silent, remaining sealed until her personal quest reaches its conclusion, after which the desolate structure stands empty.
