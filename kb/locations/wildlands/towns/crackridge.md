# Crackridge

**Crackridge** is a cliffside mining town situated in the arid [Wildlands](../wildlands.md), perched precipitously above a yawning chasm. It serves as a major setting for [Ochette](../../../characters/ochette/ochette.md)'s Chapter 2 ([Tera](../../../world/beasts/tera.md) Route) and [Temenos](../../../characters/temenos/temenos.md)'s Chapter 3 (Crackridge Route).

## Overview

Crackridge connects west to the [Western Crackridge Wilds](../wilds/western_crackridge_wilds.md), south along the [Path to the Bed of the Titan](../dungeons/path_to_the_bed_of_the_titan.md), and east toward the desolate [Abandoned Road](../wilds/abandoned_road.md) leading to the [Fellsun Ruins](../dungeons/fellsun_ruins.md). 

The settlement was originally established by three prospectors who tapped into the surrounding mineral-rich crags. Multiple quarries on the outskirts yield a distinctive red stone that commands high prices across the realm for grinding into fine pigments. Local scholars observe that the ravine creates unusual wind shears, frequently whipping the dusty streets into sudden whirlwinds.

## History & The Guardian Tera

The chasm below Crackridge is the resting place of [Tera](../../../world/beasts/tera.md), a fiery [Creature of Legend](../../../world/lore/creatures_of_legend.md). In ancient times, Tera saved the surrounding territories from a catastrophic volcanic eruption by collapsing the mountain inward, transforming the fiery peak into a sunken valley. Exhausted by the feat, the guardian settled into a deep, centuries-long slumber beneath the ravine. Whenever Tera's sleep is disturbed, seismic tremors shake Crackridge to its foundations.

## The Moonshade Order

Underneath its daytime veneer, Crackridge harbors a clandestine network of [Moonshade Order](../../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md) cultists. Its adherents identify one another by crescent moon necklaces and gather under cover of darkness. The cultists are intensely hostile to visiting clerics of the Sacred Flame and vehemently deny the existence of the nearby [Fellsun Ruins](../dungeons/fellsun_ruins.md), despite closely guarding the path leading to them.

- Adherents maintain a vigilant watch over the [Abandoned Road](../wilds/abandoned_road.md): an elderly woman keeps watch by the inn by day, a young man armed with a Forbidden Staff treks eastward daily without speaking to outsiders, and a mysterious child vanishes down the abandoned trail when approached at night.
- Cult members refuse to part with their crescent insignias under any circumstances.
- Despite their secrecy, townsfolk are aware of the Sacred Flame within the Fellsun Ruins; after [Ori](../../../characters/partitio/ori.md)'s ritual sacrifice, townspeople venture into the ruins to recover her unconscious form. Ori later recuperates at Crackridge's inn following the events of the [Journey for the Dawn](../../../stories/main/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md).

## Culture & Notable Residents

- **The Crackridge Inn**: The inn has gained renown well beyond the Wildlands thanks to the attentive and impeccable daytime service provided by its staff.
- **The Talkative Armorer's Patron**: An eminent veteran soldier frequents the town armory, eager to expound upon weaponcraft and tactics at such exhausting length that customers routinely abandon the shop mid-conversation.
- **The Guardian of the Dark Tome**: A reclusive scholar resides in the town guarding the dark grimoire *From the Far Reaches of Hell*, chanting a repetitive mantra of "Death. Pain. Destruction."
