# Crackridge Harbor: Anchorage

**Crackridge Harbor: Anchorage** is a coastal port in the [Wildlands](../wildlands.md), serving as a primary maritime gateway connecting the western territories to the eastern continent.

## Overview

The harbor connects overland to the [Western Crackridge Wilds](western_crackridge_wilds.md) to the north and the [Eastern Cropdale Trail](../../leaflands/wilds/eastern_cropdale_trail.md) to the west. Its ferry lines provide passage across the sea to [New Delsta Harbor: Anchorage](../../brightlands/wilds/new_delsta_harbor_anchorage.md) in the Brightlands.

## Lore & Notable Figures

- **The Smuggling Guild Master**: The local Guild Master oversees the ferry service while operating a discreet smuggling enterprise on the side, ferrying clients across the sea for a price if they can pass a private interview whose criteria he guards as a trade secret.
- **Bale's Arrival**: Following [Osvald](../../../characters/osvald/osvald.md)'s escape from [Frigit Isle](../../winterlands/towns/frigit_isle.md), his former fellow inmate [Bale](../../../characters/osvald/bale_octopath_traveler_ii.md) surfaces at the anchorage, having somehow managed his own escape from the penal colony.
- **The Rogue Smuggler**: An unaffiliated bandit frequents the docks, maintaining an unregistered, clandestine vessel that does not appear on official port manifests, rumored to possess ties reaching beyond Solistia.
- **The Fleeing Lovers**: Among the clandestine travelers at night is a runaway noblewoman hailing from [Timberain](../../leaflands/towns/timberain.md) who eloped with a commoner lover; oblivious to his scheming, her partner plans to make off with her fortunes as soon as they set sail.
