# Path to the Duskruin Shrine

The **Path to the Duskruin Shrine** is a high-altitude wilderness road in the eastern [Wildlands](../wildlands.md), connecting the frontier town of [Gravell](../towns/gravell.md) to the ancient [Duskruin Shrine](../dungeons/duskruin_shrine.md).

## Geography and Environment

Leading east from Gravell through barren, rocky badlands, the desolate path is inhabited by vicious wildlife including bloody bulls, raging rhinos, dreadwings, and wild molekings. While the ruins lying beyond are of ancient antiquity, the area has fallen under the sinister influence of the [Moonshade Order](../../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md).

## Story

The road is the scene of a critical sequence in [Osvald V. Vanstein](../../../characters/osvald/osvald.md)'s final confrontation with [Harvey](../../../characters/osvald/harvey.md) during Chapter 5. As an unnatural dark mist engulfs the trail leading toward the shrine, several Gravell villagers fall under mind control through dark purple crystals distributed by the cult. Osvald must confront and subdue each afflicted townsman along the road to shatter their crystals and restore their sanity before continuing toward the shrine to rescue his daughter [Elena](../../../characters/osvald/elena_vanstein.md).
