# Southern Oresrush Wilds

The **Southern Oresrush Wilds** is a rugged field route located just south of the mining town of [Oresrush](../towns/oresrush.md) in the [Wildlands](../wildlands.md).

## Geography

The arid wildland route forms an overland crossroads between several regions:
- To the north, the path leads directly into the mining settlement of [Oresrush](../towns/oresrush.md).
- To the south, travelers cross the desert frontier into the [Northern Ryu Sands](../../hinoeuma/wilds/northern_ryu_sands.md) of [Hinoeuma](../../hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md).
- To the west, the trail heads into the lush foliage of the [Southern Cropdale Trail](../../leaflands/wilds/southern_cropdale_trail.md) within the [Leaflands](../../leaflands/leaflands.md).

## Notable Events

For travelers setting out from Oresrush—specifically [Partitio Yellowil](../../../characters/travelers/partitio.md) when chosen as the initial protagonist—the road south serves as the setting for the side story [The Traveler's Bag](../../../stories/side_stories/the_travelers_bag.md), where a wandering traveler is ambushed by bandits upon entering the wilds.
