# Abandoned Road

The **Abandoned Road** is a field route situated in the [Wildlands](../wildlands.md), connecting the town of [Crackridge](../towns/crackridge.md) to the [Fellsun Ruins](../dungeons/fellsun_ruins.md).

## Overview

The road traverses the arid outskirts of Crackridge toward ancient ruins. [Reiza](../../../characters/temenos/reiza.md) cautions travelers regarding the dangers along the path, noting that there is "no shortage of bandits out that way" when seeking an escort toward the outskirts.
