# Southern Crackridge Wilds

> *"Our guild's been looking for the [Altar of the Trader](altar_of_the_trader.md) for a long time now. Supposedly a chosen merchant will be granted a special power if they find it. We know it's in the Southern Crackridge Wilds somewhere, but I haven't had any luck locating the altar so far. Maybe only the chosen one can find it..."*
> — Merchants Guild Master

The **Southern Crackridge Wilds** is a rugged wilderness trail located in the canyon country of the [Wildlands](../wildlands.md). It links the [Western Crackridge Wilds](western_crackridge_wilds.md) southward toward [Crackridge Harbor: Anchorage](crackridge_harbor_anchorage.md) and connects to the perilous excavation site at the [Unfinished Tunnel](../dungeons/unfinished_tunnel.md).

## Landmarks

- **[Altar of the Trader](altar_of_the_trader.md)**: A sacred altar dedicated to the goddess [Bifelgan](../../../world/gods/bifelgan.md), Trader of the divine pantheon. Concealed inside a hidden passage near the entrance to an old mining tunnel along the route, the altar was long sought after by the Merchants Guild to bestow divine blessings and advanced commerce talents upon worthy merchants.
