# Silver Mine

The **Silver Mine** is a mining excavation located directly east of [Oresrush](../towns/oresrush.md) in the [Wildlands](../wildlands.md).

> *"Partitio! We've got trouble! That gang's stirrin' up mischief at the mine again!"*
> — [Joe](../../../characters/partitio/joe.md)

## History and Background

During the early boomtown days of Oresrush, the Silver Mine was the lifeblood of the community founded by [Papp Yellowil](../../../characters/partitio/papp_yellowil.md) and [Roque](../../../characters/partitio/roque_brilliante.md). During this period—chronicled during the prologue of [Partitio Yellowil](../../../characters/partitio/partitio.md)'s tale—local rowdies and thugs from [Giff](../../../characters/partitio/giff.md)'s gang repeatedly stirred up trouble around the mine's entrances and workings. Partitio stepped forward to defend the miners and drive the criminals out.

In later years, once the main silver vein was tapped dry and the town fell on harder economic times under Giff's exploitation, the original tunnels fell into disuse. In the modern era, the site became known as the [Abandoned Silver Mine](abandoned_silver_mine.md), its deeper recesses having caved in and shifted over the intervening sixteen years.
