# Ivory Ravine

The **Ivory Ravine** is a treacherous subterranean chasm situated beneath the [Western Gravell Wilds](../wilds/western_gravell_wilds.md) in the [Wildlands](../wildlands.md).

## Overview

Carved deep into the rocky expanse of the Wildlands, the ravine is an eerie underground abyss shrouded in perpetual gloom. The cavern is widely feared as the domain of the monstrous Deep One, a colossal beast guarding the Eye of Calamity.

Deep within its recesses lie buried relics of bygone eras, including weapons associated with early peoples such as the Lost Tribe's Axe.

## Wildlife and Inhabitants

The cavern depths are populated by shadows and ferocious creatures, including Dark Remnants, Dark Revenants, Wild Moleking III, and aggressive avian clans consisting of Ancient Birdian V, Ancient Birdian VI, Ancient Birdking I, Ancient Birdking II, and Ancient Birdking III.
