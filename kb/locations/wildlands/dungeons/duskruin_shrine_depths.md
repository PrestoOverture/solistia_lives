# Duskruin Shrine: Depths

> "*I was worshipping near the [Duskruin Shrine](duskruin_shrine.md)... Without warning, a strange black fog rose from the ruins.*"
> —Adherent

**Duskruin Shrine: Depths** is the innermost sanctuary of the [Duskruin Shrine](duskruin_shrine.md), nestled deep within the crags of the [Wildlands](../wildlands.md). It serves as the climatic battleground for [Osvald V. Vanstein](../../../characters/travelers/osvald.md)'s final confrontation.

## Architecture & Lore

The depths house the resting altar and tomb of the legendary [Archmage D'arqest](../../../characters/others/darqest.md). The chamber walls feature distinctive circular carved patterns and arcane iconography identical to the monumental designs found upon the [Gate of Finis](../../sundering_sea/dungeons/the_gate_of_finis_octopath_traveler_ii.md).

## The Final Confrontation

Within this shadowed altar room, Osvald confronts his bitter rival, [Professor Harvey](../../../characters/osvald/harvey.md), in a catastrophic duel of supreme sorcery. Both sorcerers clash using opposing embodiments of the [One True Magic](../../../world/lore/one_true_magic.md)—Harvey unleashing corrupted abyssal energy fueled by malice and [Shadow](../../../world/lore/shadow.md), while Osvald unlocks the formula's genuine essence derived from love and sacrifice.

Following the titanic clash, a dense black fog temporarily billows outward from the depths into the surrounding area, terrifying worshippers of the [Moonshade Order](../../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md). Long after the battle's conclusion, D'arqest's ancient stone altar remains visibly cracked and scarred from the explosive arcane collision.
