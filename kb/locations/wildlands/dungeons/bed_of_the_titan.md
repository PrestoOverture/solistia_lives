# Bed of the Titan

The **Bed of the Titan** is a subterranean expanse and cavern in the [Wildlands](../wildlands.md), accessible via the [Path to the Bed of the Titan](path_to_the_bed_of_the_titan.md).

> *"I've heard the stories ever since I was a child. They say Tera sleeps at the bottom, way down there."*  
> — [Pom](../../../characters/ochette/pom.md)

## Geography and Lore

Lying deep beneath the arid wastes of the Wildlands, the Bed of the Titan is an immense subterranean hollow known in local folklore as the sleeping place of the primordial Creature of Legend, [Tera](../../../world/beasts/tera.md). The depths remain dead silent, completely uninhabited by common fauna.

## History and Events

During [Ochette](../../../characters/ochette/ochette.md)'s search across Solistia to awaken the three ancient beasts before the Night of the Scarlet Moon, she journeys to the Wildlands alongside the merchant beastling [Pom](../../../characters/ochette/pom.md). Navigating deep into the canyon to the Bed of the Titan, Ochette confronts and calms the enraged volcanic titan Tera, successfully earning the creature's trust and cooperation.
