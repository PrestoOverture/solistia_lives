# Duskruin Shrine

> "*...I'd stay away from the ruins to the east. Dangerous monsters have made their den there.*"
> —Villager in [Gravell](../towns/gravell.md)

The **Duskruin Shrine** is a sprawling ancient dungeon situated east of [Gravell](../towns/gravell.md) in the rugged [Wildlands](../wildlands.md). It serves as the opening section of the final dungeon during [Osvald V. Vanstein](../../../characters/osvald/osvald.md)'s quest for vengeance.

## History & Significance

The origins of the shrine extend deep into antiquity, long predating contemporary cults or organizations. Built as the resting place for the mortal remains of the legendary ancient [Archmage D'arqest](../../../characters/others/darqest.md), the shrine has long been revered by scholars of magic across Solistia due to D'arqest's profound contributions to the arcane arts.

In recent times, however, the ancient sanctuary was claimed by the [Moonshade Order](../../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md). The cult converted the immemorial stone halls into a temple for their shadowed rites, attracting devoted adherents from the impoverished settlement of [Gravell](../towns/gravell.md).

## Environment & Story

Reached via the [Path to the Duskruin Shrine](../wilds/path_to_the_duskruin_shrine.md), the foreboding corridors of the outer shrine are haunted by dangerous creatures—including ravenous bats, molekings, and elemental remnants—many manipulated by [Harvey](../../../characters/osvald/harvey.md) during his research into forbidden sorcery. 

Osvald journeys through the Duskruin Shrine alongside his companions to track down Harvey, pushing through the ancient complex toward the innermost chamber, the [Duskruin Shrine: Depths](duskruin_shrine_depths.md).
