# Path to the Bed of the Titan

The **Path to the Bed of the Titan** is a rugged subterranean chasm and pathway located in the [Wildlands](../wildlands.md), connecting the clifftop settlement of [Crackridge](../towns/crackridge.md) down to the [Bed of the Titan](bed_of_the_titan.md).

## Geography and Environment

Descending deep below Crackridge via wooden ladders and stone ledges, the trail traverses arid canyons and cavernous ravines. The underground passage is inhabited by subterranean and desert wildlife, including wild moles, shaggy spiders, vampire bats, and king condors roosting in the deep fissures.

## Story

The path serves as the route taken during [Ochette](../../../characters/travelers/ochette.md)'s Chapter 2 (Tera Route). Guided by the local Crackridge boy [Pom](../../../characters/ochette/pom.md), Ochette descends this precarious valley trail to reach the sacred sanctuary where [Tera](../../../world/beasts/tera.md), the slumbering earth wardenbeast, resides.
