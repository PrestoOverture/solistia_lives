# Fellsun Ruins

> "*Bones... Human bones. Broken and disfigured by repeated blows. The darker stains, then, must be blood. What a horrific end... The bones grow in number the further in I go... I must follow their trail.*"  
> — [Temenos](../../../characters/travelers/temenos.md), investigating the ruins

The **Fellsun Ruins** is an ancient underground sanctuary and ruin located in the [Wildlands](../wildlands.md), accessed via the [Abandoned Road](../wilds/abandoned_road.md) near [Crackridge](../towns/crackridge.md). Once the sacred haven of the [Kal](../../../world/lore/kal.md) people, it hides dark tragedies and one of Solistia's greatest divine secrets.

## History

### The Hero Kal and Archmage D'arqest
According to ancient inscriptions preserved within its inner chambers, the sanctuary was established in antiquity by the Keepers of the Flame, followers of the legendary hero Kal:

> "*Long ago... on this land... there came an uninvited guest. The Archmage [D'arqest](../../../characters/others/darqest.md). He called forth the night. The night brought beasts. Beasts that devoured the people. In our hour of need, only one rose to challenge the Archmage. One by the name of Kal. Pure of heart, Kal conjured the azure flame and ushered in a new dawn upon the land. The power of night now rests in the [Nameless Village](../../totohaha/towns/nameless_village.md). It awaits the dark rite that shall awaken it once more. None must go there. The azure flame must be protected. We follow in the footsteps of our hero. We are the Keepers of the Flame. The People of Kal.*"

### The Massacre of the Kal
Thirty years prior to the journey of the eight travelers, the [Moonshade Order](../../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md) invaded the sanctuary and slaughtered the entire Kal tribe to suppress their knowledge of the flames and the coming night. The caverns became a charnel house littered with the broken bones of the Kal. 

The sole survivor of the slaughter was a young girl named [Dena](../../../characters/temenos/kaldena.md). Traumatized by the butchery and furious at the silence of the divine, she renounced faith in the [gods](../../../world/gods/gods.md) and swore revenge against both the Moonshade Order and the heavens, eventually taking the name Kaldena and ascending through the ranks of the Sacred Guard.

## Story Events

During [Temenos](../../../characters/travelers/temenos.md)'s investigation into the murder of Pontiff Jörg (Chapter 3: Crackridge Route), his search leads him along the Abandoned Road and deep into the Fellsun Ruins. Tracing the gruesome path of massacred remains, Temenos discovers the ancient murals chronicling the hero Kal, D'arqest, and the hidden truth behind Kaldena's origins.

Behind the central mural at the heart of the ruins burns one of Solistia's four [Sacred Flames](../../../world/lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) in absolute secrecy. When the final eclipse of [The Journey for the Dawn](../../../stories/main/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md) commences, this mural is shattered, exposing the azure Sacred Flame to the world.

## Wildlife and Denizens

The haunted ruins are infested with subterranean beasts, predatory scavengers, and animated remains:
- **Beasts**: Wild Mole IV, Wild Mole V, King Condor, and Vampire Bats.
- **Spectral and Undead Entities**: Light Remnants and animate skeletal constructs known as Marionette Bones.
