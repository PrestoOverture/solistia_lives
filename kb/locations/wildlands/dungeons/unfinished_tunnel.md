# Unfinished Tunnel

The **Unfinished Tunnel** is an active excavation mine and cavern situated off the [Southern Crackridge Wilds](../wilds/southern_crackridge_wilds.md) in the [Wildlands](../wildlands.md).

> *"You can never do too many safety checks! After all, even the smallest oversight can cost someone their life."*
> — Overseer, upon completing *[The Cave Monster](../../../stories/side_stories/wildlands/the_cave_monster.md)*

## Overview & Side Story

Originally excavated as a subterranean mining and transit tunnel, work within the cavern ground to an abrupt halt when the monstrous beast Manymaws took up residence within the depths. The vicious creature drove out the mining crew and paralyzed several laborers who failed to flee in time.

Following the intrusion, a cautious foreman was stationed at the entrance to prevent civilians and workers from entering harm's way. During the side story "[The Cave Monster](../../../stories/side_stories/wildlands/the_cave_monster.md)", travelers confront and slay Manymaws, neutralizing the threat, rescuing the paralyzed workers, and allowing the mining crew to resume operations safely.
