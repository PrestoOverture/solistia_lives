# Wildlands

The **Wildlands** is one of eight regions in the continent of Solistia, located in northwest Solistia. The region borders the [Harborlands](../harborlands/harborlands.md) to the south, the [Leaflands](../leaflands/leaflands.md) to the west, and [Hinoeuma](../hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md) to the southwest.

## Geography and Environment

Northwest Solistia is a barren wasteland—a blasted expanse of volcanic rock that resists even the loneliest blade of grass. The few souls who eke out their existence here have long suffered the sulfuric whims of Mount Fiatsuro and its unstable neighbors.

Among the rock and stone dwell the golt, an unusually large breed of goat who subsist on a meager fare of local weed. The cheese produced from their milk is especially scrumptious and has become a local specialty. 

The volcanic soil conceals a wealth of valuable minerals, prompting the establishment of numerous small mining settlements across the territory.

## Settlements

- **[Oresrush](towns/oresrush.md)**: A southern silver mining town founded by silver merchants [Papp Yellowil](../../characters/partitio/papp_yellowil.md) and [Roque Brilliante](../../characters/partitio/roque_brilliante.md). Rumors persist that a slave-trading chapter of the [Blacksnakes](../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md) also makes its base here.
- **[Crackridge](towns/crackridge.md)**: A small settlement perched above a volcano in the north. Its population primarily comprises members of the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md). Beneath the town lies the [Bed of the Titan](dungeons/bed_of_the_titan.md), dwelling place of the legendary titan [Tera](../../world/beasts/tera.md).
- **[Gravell](towns/gravell.md)**: A northern settlement home to a descendant of a long line of legendary blacksmiths, located near the resting place of the notorious archmage [D'arqest](../../characters/others/darqest.md).

## History and Lore

- Silver from Oresrush has long supplied fine crafts throughout Solistia; eight years prior to the present era, [Partitio](../../characters/travelers/partitio.md) referred to a "King Dudley" whose crown was fashioned from Oresrush silver, though the king's broader history remains obscure.
