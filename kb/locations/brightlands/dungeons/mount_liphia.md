# Mount Liphia

> *"Santanejo blossoms? Those flowers that grow on the summit of Mount Liphia?"*
> — [Elma](../../../characters/castti/elma.md)

**Mount Liphia** is a rugged mountain dungeon located in the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md), accessible via the [Path to Mount Liphia](../wilds/path_to_mount_liphia.md). Known for its hazardous crags and ferocious wild beasts, the mountain's high elevation creates a unique climate where rare flora thrives.

Mount Liphia serves as the dungeon setting for Chapter 3 of [Castti Florenz](../../../characters/travelers/castti.md)'s storyline. The apothecary ascends the treacherous peaks in search of santanejo blossoms, an elusive medicinal flower that blooms exclusively along the mountain's windswept summit, vital for compounding life-saving remedies.
