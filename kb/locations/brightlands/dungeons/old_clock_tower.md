# Old Clock Tower

The **Old Clock Tower** is a dungeon location in the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md), accessible from the town of [Clockbank](../towns/clockbank.md).

## Overview

Standing within [Clockbank](../towns/clockbank.md), the Old Clock Tower fell into disrepair after its uppermost gears were displaced by the nesting of a powerful avian monster known as the [Heavenwing](../../../world/beasts/heavenwing.md). 

During the side story [For Whom the Clock Tower Tolls](../../../stories/side_stories/brightlands/for_whom_the_clock_tower_tolls.md), travelers enter the tower to defeat the beast and clear the way for its repair. After the Clock Tower Technician successfully restores the mechanism, the tower's great bell resumes chiming across the town. As the technician wryly remarks:

> *"I went through all the trouble of fixing the clock tower, and some people still complain. They say they can't slack off at work with it ringing when it should. Can you believe that?"*
