# Diamante's Estate

> "*[Scaracci](../../../characters/throne/scaracci.md) is on his way to the Diamante estate as we speak. Sneak in there and kill them both. However, [Diamante](../../../characters/throne/diamante.md) is wary after our last break-in. Security will be tighter. There is a way in, but...I don't like it. Diamante often craves the pleasures of the flesh and often invites women to his estate to sate those desires... You could disguise yourself as a brother girl to get close to him.*"
> —[Father](../../../characters/throne/father.md) to [Throné](../../../characters/throne/throne.md)

**Diamante's Estate** is a private mansion located in the city of [New Delsta](../towns/new_delsta.md) within the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md). It serves as the setting for the climax of Chapter 1 of [Throné Anguis](../../../characters/throne/throne.md)'s story.

## Atmosphere & Layout

The mansion is an opulent residence heavily fortified with private guards and watchdogs. Stormy weather surrounds the manor, with frequent flashes of lightning illuminating the windows throughout its corridors and halls.

## Story

Following a previous break-in, [Diamante](../../../characters/throne/diamante.md) tightened security around his home. However, aware that Diamante regularly invited women into his quarters to satisfy his carnal desires, [Father](../../../characters/throne/father.md) instructs [Throné](../../../characters/throne/throne.md) to disguise herself as a brothel girl in order to gain entry and assassinate both Diamante and [Scaracci](../../../characters/throne/scaracci.md).

Upon infiltrating the manor, Throné discovers that fellow Blacksnake member [Pirro](../../../characters/throne/pirro.md) has already breached the estate ahead of her, carving a bloody path through the guards and leaving a trail of corpses throughout the halls. Throné follows this trail of bodies up to the mansion's upper balcony, where she is ultimately forced into a fatal confrontation with Pirro.

## Aftermath

Following the conclusion of Throné's first chapter, the entrance to the estate is sealed shut. It remains inaccessible until the completion of Throné's journey, after which the mansion can be entered once more; the bodies left in Pirro's wake have been cleared away, though the path through the broken window leading out onto the balcony remains impassable.
