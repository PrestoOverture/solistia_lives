# Lostseed Castle: Upper Level

> *"Welcome to my humble castle."*  
> — [Claude](../../../characters/throne/claude_blacksnakes.md), in [Ori's Journal Fragments](../../../stories/documents/oris_journal_fragments.md)

**Lostseed Castle: Upper Level** is a dungeon sub-area in the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md), located directly above [Lostseed Castle](lostseed_castle.md).

It serves as the climactic section of [Throné](../../../characters/travelers/throne.md)'s journey, where she confronts [Claude](../../../characters/throne/claude_blacksnakes.md), the progenitor and leader of the [Blacksnakes](../../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md), in order to win the key to her collar and achieve her freedom.
