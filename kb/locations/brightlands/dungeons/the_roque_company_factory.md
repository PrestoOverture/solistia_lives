# The Roque Company: Factory

The **Roque Company: Factory** is a massive industrial manufacturing plant situated in the Industrial District of [Clockbank](../towns/clockbank_industrial_district.md) in the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md).

> *"So this is a steam engine! Steamy, huh? Guess that makes sense. Mister Roque... You really are changin' the world, just like you said."*
> — [Partitio Yellowil](../../../characters/partitio/partitio.md)

## Overview and Lore

The factory is the primary production facility established by the [Roque Company](../../../world/factions/roque_company.md) to mass-produce steam engines engineered by [Floyd](../../../characters/partitio/floyd.md). Under the direction of [Roque](../../../characters/partitio/roque_brilliante.md), the facility represents the cutting edge of industrial progress in Solistia, powered by heavy machinery and defended by corporate guards and automated mechanical sentinels.

## Story Events

During Chapter 2 of Partitio's journey, Partitio arrives in Clockbank hoping to reunite with Roque and witness the miraculous steam engine in person. Upon inspecting the factory, however, he uncovers the brutal exploitation and harsh labor conditions imposed upon the factory workers. To protect the workers and rescue Floyd, Partitio storms the factory depths and defeats [Garnet](../../../characters/partitio/garnet.md), Roque's ferocious watchdog overseeing the production floor.
