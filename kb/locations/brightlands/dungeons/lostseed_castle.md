# Lostseed Castle

**Lostseed Castle** is an ancient fortress situated at the rear of the ruined city of [Lostseed](../towns/lostseed.md) in the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md). It serves as the stronghold of [Claude](../../../characters/throne/claude_blacksnakes.md), progenitor and founder of the [Blacksnakes](../../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md). The lower floors connect the city of Lostseed to [Lostseed Castle: Upper Level](lostseed_castle_upper_level.md).

During Chapter 4 of [Throné Anguis](../../../characters/travelers/throne.md)'s journey, she infiltrates the castle to confront Claude and obtain the final key to unlock the collar around her neck.

## Architecture and Atmosphere

The interior of Lostseed Castle is decaying and foreboding:
- **Barricaded Gates**: Heavy wooden barricades stand alongside the entrance, indicating that the castle was intentionally blocked off or quarantined in the past.
- **Architectural Parallels**: The castle floor features an ornate four-leafed tile motif identical to the masonry found in [New Delsta](../towns/new_delsta.md)—specifically in front of the [New Delsta: Theater](new_delsta_theater.md) and outside the [Game Parlor](../towns/new_delsta_game_parlor.md) in the backstreets. Furthermore, its elongated windows and diamond-patterned stone trim match the ancient stonework of the [Montwise: Library](../../crestlands/towns/montwise_library.md).
- **Inhabitants and Fauna**: Beyond animated remnants and revenants wandering its halls, the castle is infested with Ettin Snakes—giant two-headed serpents that echo the symbol and iconography of the Blacksnakes.

Near the castle threshold, an emaciated young woman clutches a newborn child, pleading in agony to any who pass:
> *"Ah... Ahhhh... P-please... My baby... Kill...my baby...Please..."*
