# New Delsta: Game Parlor

> "*I don't know much about what goes on behind the scenes. It's far better not to...*"
> — Manager

**New Delsta: Game Parlor** is an illicit gambling establishment located in the [New Delsta: Backstreets](new_delsta_backstreets.md) district of [New Delsta](new_delsta.md), in the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md). Beneath its facade of gaming and entertainment, the parlor functions as a den and front for the [Blacksnakes](../../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md) syndicate.

## Structure and Operations

The game parlor is connected directly to the alleyways of the backstreets. While offering games of chance to locals and visitors alike, the parlor's operations are thoroughly rigged:

- **Coerced Management**: The front manager running the daily operations has been coerced into servitude by the Blacksnakes, who bound him with golden bracers engineered to deliver lethal poison should he disobey or attempt escape.
- **Predatory Gambling**: The establishment's dealers and pit bosses operate as skilled cheats, completely controlling game outcomes. They systematically ensnare new gamblers by orchestrating significant initial wins, deliberately cultivating addictive streaks before plunging their victims into debt and financial devastation.
