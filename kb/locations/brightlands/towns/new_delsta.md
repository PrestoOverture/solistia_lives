# New Delsta

> "*Dignity, glamour, chaos, and tradition... That is the scent of this city!*"
> — Traveler

**New Delsta** is a bustling metropolis located in the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md). Known as the beating heart of the region, it is the city where the thief [Throné Anguis](../../../characters/travelers/throne.md) resides and serves as the setting for Chapter 1 of her tale, Chapter 2 of [Agnea Bristarni](../../../characters/travelers/agnea.md)'s journey, the first Crossed Path of [Osvald V. Vanstein](../../../characters/travelers/osvald.md) and [Partitio Yellowil](../../../characters/travelers/partitio.md), and the final gathering of the eight travelers in the Epilogue.

## Geography and Layout

New Delsta connects to several key districts and surrounding territories:
- **[New Delsta: Backstreets](new_delsta_backstreets.md)** to the west
- **[New Delsta: Theater](../dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md)** to the north
- **[Diamante's Estate](../dungeons/diamantes_estate.md)** to the northeast
- **[Eastern New Delsta Highroad](../wilds/eastern_new_delsta_highroad.md)** to the east
- **[Underground Waterway](../dungeons/underground_waterway.md)** accessible to the south

Notable establishments and sub-areas within the city include:
- **Hotel Moon Delsta**, the local inn
- The city tavern, run by a cheerful tavernkeeper
- A long-running armory and a provisioner shop
- **[New Delsta: Game Parlor](new_delsta_game_parlor.md)**

Throughout the city, a griffin motif reminiscent of Atlasdam can be observed on various facades, including on the exterior of [Diamante's Estate](../dungeons/diamantes_estate.md) and over [Gil](../../../characters/agnea/gil.md)'s tavern in the Backstreets.

## History and Culture

According to the [Mercantile Manuscript](../../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md), New Delsta was established approximately fifty years ago by emigrants from the fallen kingdom of [Delstasch](../../../world/nations/delstasch.md), who named their settlement to honor their former home. As the Brightlands industrialized and developed, theaters and game parlors were erected to entertain swelling crowds, making the city a center of modern civilization where wealth and entertainment flourish.

The famed merchant [Masoud](../../../characters/partitio/masoud.md) praised New Delsta's merchandise, noting that its high-quality goods never fail to amaze. The culinary scene is similarly celebrated: the city's leading crepetissier crafts a fruit-stuffed deluxe crepe renowned across the realm, attracting sightseers from afar, while many fashionable bistros draw culinary inspiration from the meat-cooking traditions of the [beastlings](../../../world/species/beastling.md).

Despite its rapid rise, an [Elderly Guard](../../../characters/throne/elderly_guard.md) from the orphanage at [Mother's Garden](../../leaflands/dungeons/mothers_garden.md) observes that the core character of New Delsta has altered little over the past half-century.

## Society and Urban Life

Beneath New Delsta's dazzling glamour lies stark social inequality and friction:
- **High Society & Politics**: Posters featuring the superstar dancer [Dolcinaea Luciel](../../../characters/agnea/dolcinaea_luciel.md) adorn walls across the city, promoting shows at the grand theater. Meanwhile, one of New Delsta's influential aristocrats and congress members worries that unchecked population growth endangers public order, privately advocating for the expulsion of the city's poor.
- **Local Figures**:
  - The proprietor of the city's armory belongs to a family business spanning generations; his eldest son studies economics and business at the university in [Montwise](../../crestlands/towns/montwise.md).
  - An elderly woman frequenting the armory is quietly suspected of being behind a string of recent unsolved murders.
  - A renowned master leatherworker, whose craftsmanship was once sought after across the world, completely shuttered his workshop and lost all will to work following the death of his beloved wife.
  - The tavern's friendly host is known to generously treat patrons, though he is prone to sudden bouts of fury during which he has wrecked his own tavern.
  - An elegant woman working in the brothel and seen in the tavern after dark is whispered to be a displaced princess from a fallen kingdom.
- **Past Visitors**: In travel banter with Agnea, the cleric [Temenos Mistral](../../../characters/travelers/temenos.md) recalls once visiting New Delsta in the company of [Pontiff Jörg](../../../characters/temenos/jorg.md).

## Story Events

- **Throné's Recruitment**: When travelers encounter New Delsta without Throné already in their company, they come across two suspicious thugs tormenting a stray puppy on the city streets. Intervening to protect the animal causes the scoundrels to flee into the shadows, where they are ambushed and dealt with by Throné. Impressed by the travelers' kindness, Throné joins their quest.
- **The Blacksnakes**: Throné begins her quest to win her freedom from the Blacksnakes syndicate from New Delsta, targeting [Diamante](../../../characters/throne/diamante.md) and planning her infiltration through the city's underbelly.
- **Agnea's Audition**: Agnea arrives in New Delsta seeking her big break at the grand theater, crossing paths with the superstar [Dolcinaea Luciel](../../../characters/agnea/dolcinaea_luciel.md) and her manager [Veronica](../../../characters/agnea/veronica.md).
- **Osvald & Partitio's Crossed Path**: Osvald and Partitio collaborate in New Delsta on an enterprise involving scientific and commercial ambition.
