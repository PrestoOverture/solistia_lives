# Lostseed

**Lostseed** is a secluded, ruined town located in the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md), situated deep in the valley directly beneath the bustling metropolis of [New Delsta](new_delsta.md). It is accessed via the gondola across the [Deserted Highroad](../wilds/deserted_highroad.md) and leads directly to [Lostseed Castle](../dungeons/lostseed_castle.md). It is uncovered during Chapter 4 of [Throné Anguis](../../../characters/travelers/throne.md)'s journey as the ancestral seat and breeding ground of [Claude](../../../characters/throne/claude_blacksnakes.md).

## Geography and Atmosphere

Despite being considerably larger than settlements such as [Ryu](../../hinoeuma/towns/ryu.md) or [Cape Cold](../../winterlands/towns/cape_cold.md), Lostseed is entirely absent from modern maps. According to the guide operating the Deserted Highroad gondola, the settlement is so forgotten that it has gone unnoticed for centuries despite its proximity below New Delsta.

Lostseed is enveloped in perpetual, chilling stillness. Day or night, a massive full moon hangs frozen in the sky—a trait shared with the Deserted Highroad and [Mother's Garden](../../leaflands/dungeons/mothers_garden.md). Chains attached to manacles are embedded directly into the city walls, echoing Claude's motifs of enslavement and control. The town's shopkeepers and innkeeper stand unnervingly facing backward into the walls, while groups of candles are positioned throughout the town in clusters totaling eight, possibly corresponding to the eight deities of Solistia.

## History

The city was originally founded centuries ago by the elopement of a runaway prince and princess. Its name originally referenced the royal pair's lost lineage—the "lost seed" of their noble line. Over generations, however, Claude repurposed the hidden, isolated city into a breeding pen for prospective vessels. His children were raised and culled here to produce an ideal human vessel worthy of being possessed by [Vide](../../../world/gods/vide.md), turning the "lost seed" into the harbinger of Solistia's destruction.

A legendary master thief carrying the dagger Mooneater once journeyed to this lost city following rumors heard by the Thief's Guild, never to return.

## Inhabitants

The residents of Lostseed are Claude's discarded or lingering "vessels," individuals stripped of identity, sanity, and purpose:

- **Pure Vessel**: A young boy who knows nothing of the outside world, murmuring faint greetings (*"... Hello."*).
- **Ruined Vessel**: A broken figure whose frantic mutterings interweave the professions of the eight [gods](../../../world/gods/gods.md) (*"The dancer whose former cleric boyfriend was cut down by a swoRdsman who claimed to be a thiEf was so beautiful that yeSterday because there was no apotheCary the hUnter betrayed the mErchants and stabbed the scholar as instructed and the hunter now Means to kill you and mE both but I know I know I knowwwwwwwww."*). The irregular capitalization within his ramblings secretly spells out "RESCUE ME." He stands before a ring of eight candles.
- **Broken Vessel**: An aged woman trapped in the city for generations, unable to recall the cherished thing she once sought or whether it mattered at all.
- **Hollow Vessel**: A despairing girl who has closed her eyes to the darkness that knows no dawn, lingering beside the abandoned buildings.
- **Old Vessel**: A vessel consumed by darkness, repeating the words "Death Corruption Destruction" incessantly.
- **Empty Vessel**: A silent, unresponsive figure who stares blankly into the void.
