# New Delsta: Backstreets

> "*It's sad to say, but lives aren't worth much in this town...*"
> — Elderly Man

**New Delsta: Backstreets** is the impoverished sub-district of [New Delsta](new_delsta.md) in the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md). Standing in sharp contrast to the glamour and wealth of the main thoroughfares, the backstreets are marked by poverty, vice, and decay, serving as the primary operational territory for the [Blacksnakes](../../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md) criminal syndicate.

## Geography and Landmarks

The backstreets connect eastward and southward to the main districts of [New Delsta](new_delsta.md), and lead northward to the [New Delsta: Game Parlor](new_delsta_game_parlor.md). Notable establishments and locations within the district include:

- **Montraine's Tavern**: A modest dive open only at night, run by the tavernkeeper and pianist [Gil](../../../characters/agnea/gil.md). The tavern was originally owned by the renowned retired pianist Montraine, whose music once drifted through the slums and inspired a young Gil, whom Montraine mentored. While Gil admits the drinks are cheap and advises patrons not to expect fine food, the establishment remains a gathering place for locals.
- **Rival Brothels (Fengari and Amant)**: Two competing brothels operate out of the backstreets. Amant's courtesans have enraptured various city residents—including the town provisioner, his son, and the elderly armorer—and frequent the upscale tavern in New Delsta by night. One of the district's brothels is secretly patronized by prominent politicians, celebrated actors, and wealthy figures; the last guard stationed there met a mysterious and tragic fate.
- **The Backstreets Cemetery**: Situated on the western end of the district, this graveyard serves as the burial ground for members of the Blacksnakes. While rumor attributes the cemetery's creation to [Mother](../../../characters/throne/mother.md), [Throné Anguis](../../../characters/travelers/throne.md) expresses skepticism. An elderly resident living adjacent to the cemetery takes it upon himself out of compassion to collect and bury the unclaimed bodies of those who die unmourned in the alleys.

## Story Events

- **Agnea's Chapter 2**: The dancer [Agnea Bristarni](../../../characters/travelers/agnea.md) meets [Gil](../../../characters/agnea/gil.md), who invites her to dance at Montraine's Tavern. Her joyful performance draws patrons from across New Delsta into the slums. This success arouses the spite of the theater manager [La'mani](../../../characters/agnea/lamani.md), who orders thugs to trash the tavern to keep the lower classes in their place. After Agnea confronts and defeats La'mani, the community works together to repair the tavern, inspiring Gil to compose the "Song of Hope."
- **Throné's Quest**: Operating from the heart of the Blacksnakes' domain, Throné frequently navigates the backstreets. Following the death of her syndicate brother [Pirro](../../../characters/throne/pirro.md), his grave is placed in the backstreets cemetery. Later in her journey, Throné discovers that [Claude](../../../characters/throne/claude_blacksnakes.md) has carved a cryptic directive into Pirro's gravestone: *"I await where it all began."*
