# Abandoned Village

The **Abandoned Village**, formerly known as **Healeaks**, is an uninhabited ruin situated in the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md). It is connected to the [New Delsta Flats](../wilds/new_delsta_flats.md) and the [Path to Mount Liphia](../wilds/path_to_mount_liphia.md).

## History

### Eir's Apothecaries

Two years prior, Healeaks was a peaceful, thriving community and the home base of [Eir's Apothecaries](../../../world/factions/eirs_apothecaries.md), a dedicated traveling medical band composed of [Castti Florenz](../../../characters/castti/castti.md), [Malaya](../../../characters/castti/malaya.md), brothers [Andy](../../../characters/castti/andy.md) and [Randy](../../../characters/castti/randy.md), [Elma](../../../characters/castti/elma.md), and [Trousseau](../../../characters/castti/trousseau.md). Their tireless healing earned them the deep affection and trust of the villagers.

During their stay, a young girl named [Sally](../../../characters/castti/sally.md) fell gravely ill despite the efforts of Trousseau and her protective brother [Temm](../../../characters/castti/temm.md). Determining that only a santanejo blossom growing atop the monster-infested summit of [Mount Liphia](../dungeons/mount_liphia.md) could cure her, Castti and Trousseau climbed the mountain together to gather the petals, successfully curing Sally and earning the admiration of the town.

### The Purple Rain

Some time later, Castti and her fellow apothecaries returned to Healeaks, only to find the community plunged into disaster. Sally and Temm had been found dead near the mountain slopes, and Trousseau had disappeared. A toxic purple rain began falling upon the settlement, rapidly sickening and killing the villagers despite the desperate efforts of the apothecaries to treat them.

Noticing plume of noxious smoke rising from the peak of Mount Liphia, Castti, Malaya, Andy, and Randy ascended the mountain while Elma remained behind. At the summit, they discovered Trousseau in a robe and mask. Having been swayed by an enigmatic man into believing that death was the only true salvation from life's endless suffering, Trousseau had murdered the siblings to lure the villagers into the open rain. As the fumes overpowered Castti, Trousseau departed to prepare a wider massacre in [Timberain](../../leaflands/towns/timberain.md).

Andy and Randy sacrificed their lives to smother the toxic flame, stopping the poisonous clouds. Malaya carried the fading Castti back down the mountain, keeping her alive with snowdrop blossoms. Upon returning to Healeaks, they found that Elma had lost her life defending the troupe's horse, [Jeyah](../../../characters/castti/jeyah.md). Malaya loaded Castti onto Jeyah, rode to the shore, and set Castti adrift in a skiff onto the sea, placing the fate of finding a cure in her hands.

### Desolation

In the aftermath of the massacre, a traveling elder was the first outsider to discover the devastated, empty settlement. Taking it upon himself to bury every dead villager and pray for their souls—a grim endeavor that required more than ten days—he lamented: "*There's nothing more I can do...*"

The former carpenter of Healeaks, Lind, later relocated and helped build the [New Delsta: Theater](../dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md). Despite the village standing completely abandoned, faint lights can still be seen flickering within its windmills at night when viewed from the [Path to Mount Liphia](../wilds/path_to_mount_liphia.md) and the heights of Mount Liphia.
