# Clockbank

> *"Hoho, welcome to Clockbank. Lovely weather we're having today, isn't it?"*
> — Clockbank Townsperson

**Clockbank** is a bustling industrial river town located in the eastern [Brightlands](../brightlands.md). Built along the riverbanks, it serves as the administrative headquarters of the [Roque Company](../../../world/factions/roque_company.md) and the focal setting for [Partitio Yellowil](../../../characters/travelers/partitio.md)'s Chapter 2.

## History & Industry

According to the [Mercantile Manuscript](../../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md), Clockbank grew into prosperity following the Age of Discovery roughly a century ago. True to its name, the city is defined by precision horology and machinery—"things that tick in any form." Its craftsmen are renowned continent-wide for their mechanical pocket watches, celebrated as the most accurate timepieces in Solistia.

The town operates under the tight economic grip of the Roque Company, with every resident—minors included—subject to company taxation.

## Districts & Landmarks

- **Town Center**: Defined by cobblestone lanes, riverside quays, and a central lion-headed stone fountain. It connects south to the [Southern Clockbank Highroad](../wilds/southern_clockbank_highroad.md), north to [Clockbank: Industrial District](clockbank_industrial_district.md), and northeast toward the [Old Clock Tower](../dungeons/old_clock_tower.md).
- **[Old Clock Tower](../dungeons/old_clock_tower.md)**: The town's historic timepiece landmark. Once repaired, its great bell chimes at every shift between day and night. Its technician secretly works as an anonymous street graffiti artist.
- **[Clockbank: Industrial District](clockbank_industrial_district.md)**: The factory core of the town, housing the steam-powered Roque Company production plants and workshops.

## Society & Underworld

Beneath its orderly exterior of ticking gears and polite greetings, Clockbank harbors a dense criminal underbelly. A significant number of residents lead double lives, influenced by the clandestine presence of the Thieves' Guild operating after dark in the industrial quarter. 

Notable figures in the town's shadows include:
- The legendary figure declared by newspapers to be the "World's Greatest Phantom Thief"—now an elderly woman living quietly with her granddaughter after a lifetime of stealing legendary treasures.
- A covert betting syndicate in the local underworld wagered on who can successfully poison a citizen born naturally immune to all known toxins.
- A secretive pair of foreign travelers frequenting the tavern's upper floor who belong to an ancient lineage of assassins whose killing arts are transmitted to only a single heir in each generation.
