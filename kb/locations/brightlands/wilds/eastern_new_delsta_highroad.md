# Eastern New Delsta Highroad

The **Eastern New Delsta Highroad** is a traveled thoroughfare located in the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md), situated just east of the bustling metropolis of [New Delsta](../towns/new_delsta.md).

## Geography and Routes

The highroad acts as a vital overland route in the Brightlands:
- To the west, the road enters the city of [New Delsta](../towns/new_delsta.md).
- To the north, the pathway heads into the [New Delsta Flats](new_delsta_flats.md).
- To the east, the trail extends toward the [Western Clockbank Highroad](western_clockbank_highroad.md).

## Notable Inhabitants and Landmarks

- **Arkar's Workshop**: To the northeast of the highroad stands the workshop of [Arkar](../../../characters/side_stories/arkar.md), an eccentric inventor who develops various ingenious mechanical devices and passes on the skills of the Inventor job to worthy travelers.

## Story and Quests

- **The Traveler's Bag**: If the thief [Throné Anguis](../../../characters/throne/throne.md) is chosen as the starting traveler, she encounters the side story [The Traveler's Bag](../../../stories/side_stories/the_travelers_bag.md) along this road shortly after departing New Delsta.
- **A Genius Inventor**: Travelers who discover Arkar's dwelling can undertake the side story [A Genius Inventor](../../../stories/side_stories/brightlands/a_genius_inventor.md), helping him craft new contraptions.

## Wildlife

The open plains and waterways around the highroad are populated by numerous beasts and creatures:
- **Terrestrial Wildlife**: City Ratkins, Mad Ratkins, docile and fluffy Meeps, larger Shaggy Meeps, Night Ravens, and roaming Giant Sheep.
- **Aquatic Life**: Waterways crossing the highroad contain Red Betta fish and elemental Thunder Wisps.
