# Path to Mount Liphia

The **Path to Mount Liphia** is a mountainous field route in the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md), connecting the desolate [Abandoned Village](../towns/abandoned_village.md) to the towering slopes of [Mount Liphia](../dungeons/mount_liphia.md).

## Geography and Environment

Winding upward from the ruined settlement, the path ascends rugged highlands populated by mountain wildlife, including mammoth sheep, blue bulls, wicked wasps, and scavenging ratkins.

## Story and History

The trail features prominently in [Castti Florenz](../../../characters/castti/castti.md)'s search for her lost memories during Chapter 3. In the past, Castti and the members of [Eir's Apothecaries](../../../world/factions/eirs_apothecaries.md) climbed this route toward Mount Liphia to uncover the cause of the toxic purple rain that had poisoned and annihilated the villagers below. When Castti later retraces her footsteps along this quiet ascent, fragments of her suppressed memories begin returning to her as she approaches the mountain where the truth behind the tragedy took place.
