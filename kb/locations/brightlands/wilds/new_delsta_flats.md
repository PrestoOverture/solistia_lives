# New Delsta Flats

> "*Did you come here hoping to find something good, too?*"
> — Soldier at the black market

**New Delsta Flats** is a sprawling open field in the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md), situated outside the city of [New Delsta](../towns/new_delsta.md).

## Geography and Connections

The flats serve as an important transit hub connecting several adjacent regions and landmarks:
- **[Southern Cape Cold Snows](../../winterlands/wilds/southern_cape_cold_snows.md)** to the north, leading toward the frigid Winterlands
- **[New Delsta Harbor: Anchorage](new_delsta_harbor_anchorage.md)** to the west, providing maritime access to the sea
- **[Abandoned Village](../towns/abandoned_village.md)** to the east
- **[Abandoned Waterway](../dungeons/abandoned_waterway.md)** to the northwest
- **[Eastern New Delsta Highroad](eastern_new_delsta_highroad.md)** to the south

## The Black Market

The northern sector of the New Delsta Flats is best known for its clandestine Black Market, which convenes strictly under cover of night. Between one and three rotating merchant stalls appear under the dark, peddling goods that are exceedingly difficult to acquire through legitimate commerce elsewhere in Solistia. These offerings include illicit bottled powders, hazardous and medicinal concoction herbs, rare soulstones, shady fruit, specialty armor, exotic accessories, and weapons.
