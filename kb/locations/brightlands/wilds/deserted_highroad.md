# Deserted Highroad

> "*This ropeway is something of an antique from long ago. Would you like to go for a ride, [young lady](../../../characters/throne/throne.md)?*"
> —[Guide](../../../characters/throne/guide_thrones_story.md)

The **Deserted Highroad** is a field location in the [Brightlands](../brightlands.md). It connects from the [Underground Waterway](../dungeons/underground_waterway.md) and leads across to the forgotten city of [Lostseed](../towns/lostseed.md).

## Overview

The highroad features an antique ropeway and gondola operated by a mysterious [Guide](../../../characters/throne/guide_thrones_story.md), which serves as the passage into the hidden ruins of [Lostseed](../towns/lostseed.md). Regardless of whether it is day or night, a full white moon hangs constantly in the sky above the desolate road.

## Connections & Lore

- **Connections**: Connects to the [Underground Waterway](../dungeons/underground_waterway.md) and [Lostseed](../towns/lostseed.md).
- **Travel Banter**: Arriving at the Deserted Highroad triggers a faint memory in [Castti](../../../characters/castti/castti.md). She recalls having been here before while taking a walk along the highroad with someone, though she cannot remember anything further.
