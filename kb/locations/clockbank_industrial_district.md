# Clockbank: Industrial District

> *"Greetings! Welcome to the Roque Company's factory."*
> — Roque Company Guard

**Clockbank: Industrial District** is the manufacturing and factory quarter of [Clockbank](clockbank.md), located in the eastern [Brightlands](brightlands.md). It connects south to the main town square and north to [The Roque Company: Factory](the_roque_company_factory.md).

## Overview & Industry

Dominated by the sprawling facilities of the [Roque Company](../world/roque_company.md), the district is populated largely by factory laborers, clerks, and machinists driving the city's booming mechanical and horological industries. Outside the entrance to the main factory complex stands an imposing statue of the company's founder and proprietor, [Roque Brilliante](../characters/roque_brilliante.md), whose portrait also hangs prominently inside the corporate headquarters. Within the administrative offices, overworked scribes often work late into the night, slumping over their desks in exhaustion.

## Underworld Activity

Behind the facade of industrial productivity, the district conceals illicit nighttime operations. The Thieves' Guild convenes under cover of darkness within a locked building situated at the western end of the quarter, accessed only through a secret subterranean tunnel.

## Notable Inhabitants

Wandering the district is a friendly white cat named Catharine, recognized by the locals as a fixture of the industrial quarter.
