# Altar of the Thunderblade

The **Altar of the Thunderblade** is a hidden sanctuary in [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md) dedicated to [Brand](../world/brand.md), the Thunderblade.

> *"Chosen warrior...  
> Unto thee, I grant new power."*  
> — [Brand](../world/brand.md)

## Geography and Access

The entrance to the altar is located in the [Eastern Sai Sands](eastern_sai_sands.md), reached along a secluded path south of the main trail and behind a ruined building.

Within the altar stands a statue depicting Brand. When approached by the warrior prince [Hikari](../characters/hikari.md), the god bestows the martial secret of the Ultimate Stance.

## Lore

According to the Guild Master of the Warriors Guild in [Sai](sai.md), no warrior had ever succeeded in locating the Altar of the Thunderblade, making Hikari the first known warrior in living memory to receive Brand's divine blessing.
