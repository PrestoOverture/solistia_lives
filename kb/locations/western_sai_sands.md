# Western Sai Sands

The **Western Sai Sands** is a desert field route in the [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md) region.

## Geography

Western Sai Sands serves as a crossroads connecting multiple regions and desert pathways:
- North: [Eastern Wellgrove Trail](eastern_wellgrove_trail.md) (leading into the [Leaflands](leaflands.md))
- East: [Southern Sai Sands](southern_sai_sands.md) (leading toward the town of [Sai](sai.md))
- Northwest: [Quicksand Gaol](quicksand_gaol.md)
- South: [Eastern Ku Sands](eastern_ku_sands.md) (leading toward the realm of [Ku](../world/ku.md))

An oasis is located within the western sands, providing water amidst the arid desert terrain.

## Notable Inhabitants

- **Portly Merchant**: A thirty-one-year-old merchant with an indefatigable smile who sets up shop beside the oasis. Disregarding the advice of everyone nearby, he purchased a dilapidated former street stall at full asking price without any forward planning. His reckless business behavior intensified following the death of his cautious brother. He laughs about running low on merchandise during the day and chuckles about how difficult merchant life is at night. He is connected to the Thin Merchant in the neighboring [Southern Sai Sands](southern_sai_sands.md); when the Thin Merchant departs, the Portly Merchant also disappears from the oasis, leaving behind no wares or stall equipment.
