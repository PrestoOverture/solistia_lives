# Giff's Manse

> *"I've been holdin' back ever since they took our deed away from us. We've all suffered in silence. Now, well...I've finally understood. That dastard landowner, well, only thing on their mind is how to grow rich and fat off our toils. They don't care a white 'bout what happens to us or our town. That's why we gotta take it back - and our lives with it. Now that that's settled, let's hustle on over to that snake [Giff](../characters/giff.md)'s place!"*  
> —*[Partitio](../characters/partitio.md), rousing his posse*

**Giff's Manse** is an opulent estate located in the [Wildlands](wildlands.md), connected via the [Abandoned Silver Mine](abandoned_silver_mine.md). It serves as the stronghold of the corrupt landlord [Giff](../characters/giff.md) and is the climactic location of [Partitio Yellowil](../characters/partitio.md)'s opening journey.

## Overview & Architecture

The manse stands in stark contrast to the destitute conditions of nearby [Oresrush](oresrush.md), reflecting Giff's accumulated wealth extracted from the town's laborers. 

- **Entrance**: A prominent portrait of Giff hangs conspicuously within the entrance hall.
- **Courtyard & Fountains**: The manse features an ornamental fountain at its center, alongside a sculpted lion fountain matching those found in [Clockbank](clockbank.md) and [Timberain Castle: Town Square](timberain_castle_town_square.md).
- **Chambers**: The private reception room houses a large dining table that is violently split in two during the confrontation with Partitio.

The estate is heavily guarded by hired thugs and patrol dogs tasked with keeping local townsfolk and miners from reclaiming their town's deed.

## Story

After enduring eight years of poverty and exploitation under Giff's stranglehold, Partitio realizes that the landlord has no intention of helping the people of Oresrush. Rallying the town's silver miners and laborers into a determined posse, Partitio marches on Giff's Manse, defeats Giff and his enforcers, and reclaims the deed to restore freedom to Oresrush.
