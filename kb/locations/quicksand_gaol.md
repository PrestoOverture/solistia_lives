# Quicksand Gaol

**Quicksand Gaol** is a subterranean dungeon located beneath the dunes of [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md), accessible via the [Western Sai Sands](western_sai_sands.md).

## Geography and Environment

A cavernous pit formed by treacherous collapsing sands and shifting dunes, the gaol is a hazardous subterranean sinkhole. The cavern is populated by hostile desert creatures adapted to the sands, including vagrant lizardmen, desert worms, assassin bugs, savage scorpions, and spiked skinks.

## Lore and Significance

Deep within the subterranean hollow dwells the Lord of the Sands, a colossal burrowing desert monster that preys on unsuspecting travelers swallowed by the dunes. Defeating this behemoth yields the rare Quicksand Monster's Liver. 

Guarded behind the beast's lair rests a chest containing the Rusty Axe, one of the forgotten ancient relics sought by weapon masters to be reforged into the legendary Lionheart's Axe for the Armsmaster.
