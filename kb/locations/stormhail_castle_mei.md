# Stormhail: Castle Mei

> *"This castle belongs to [House Mei](../world/clan_mei.md). There shall be no violence here."*
> — [Kunzo](../characters/kunzo.md)

**Stormhail: Castle Mei** is a fortified stronghold and sub-area of the city of [Stormhail](stormhail.md), located high in the snowy mountains of the [Winterlands](winterlands.md). It connects west across the [Stormhail: Bridge](stormhail_bridge.md) and east toward [Castle Mei: East Tower](castle_mei_east_tower.md).

## History and Strategic Role

The fortress serves as the ancestral seat of [Clan Mei](../world/clan_mei.md). It was originally established by [Clan Ku](../world/ku.md) as a northern military outpost intended to facilitate their campaign to conquer the eastern continent. According to local residents, Castle Mei was constructed upon the ancient ruins where a castle from a great empire once stood.

Following the events of [Hikari](../characters/hikari.md)'s journey in Stormhail (Chapter 4), the stronghold becomes accessible as the domain of Clan Mei once more.
