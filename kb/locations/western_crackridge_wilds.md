# Western Crackridge Wilds

**Western Crackridge Wilds** is an arid wilderness route located in the [Wildlands](wildlands.md).

## Geography and Connections

Carved across arid canyons and rugged plateaus, the Western Crackridge Wilds serves as a crucial crossroads in the northern Wildlands:

- **East**: Leads into the cliffside town of [Crackridge](crackridge.md).
- **South**: Connects to the [Southern Crackridge Wilds](southern_crackridge_wilds.md), which leads down toward the Abandoned Silver Mine and Bed of the Titan.
- **North**: Extends into the remote [Western Gravell Wilds](western_gravell_wilds.md).

## Points of Interest

### Merchants' Guild
The western reach of the wilds houses the headquarters of the **Merchants' Guild**. Aspiring tradespeople seek out the guild master here to acquire secondary merchant licenses. 

The encampment attracts notable peddlers:
- **Fortune's Favorite**: A buoyant merchant who does business by the guild master during daylight hours, dealing exclusively in purchases.
- **Conjurer of Commerce**: A silver-tongued merchant who replaces him at nightfall, relying on persuasion and negotiation rather than pure fortune.
