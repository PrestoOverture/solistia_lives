# Beneath the Wall

**Beneath the Wall** is a cavern located beneath the [Great Wall](../world/great_wall.md) in the [Southern Stormhail Snows](southern_stormhail_snows.md), within the [Winterlands](winterlands.md).

## Overview

Entered from the snowfields south of Stormhail, this subterranean cavern initially appears to contain little of interest beyond an old wooden bridge spanning a vast subterranean abyss. A Senior Assistant stationed nearby nervously warns travelers about the stability of the passage:

> "*Hey... Try not to bring too many people around here, will you?*"

## Secret Descent

Though seemingly unremarkable, the fragile bridge cannot support substantial weight. If too many people cross the span at once, the wooden planks collapse beneath them, plunging the travelers into the darkness below.

This fall reveals the hidden entrance to the [Infernal Castle](infernal_castle.md), a forgotten fortress concealed deep beneath the foundation of the Great Wall.
