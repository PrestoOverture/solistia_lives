# Ryu

> *"Welcome to Ryu, traveler. I hope you have a nice stay."*
> — Local Elder

**Ryu** is a small desert outpost town located in the arid region of [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md), connected directly to the [Northern Ryu Sands](northern_ryu_sands.md). It is the smallest settlement in Solistia.

## Overview & Culture

According to the [Mercantile Manuscript](../stories/masoud_script.md), Ryu serves as a notable crossroads and rest stop for wayfarers, where news and rumors from across the world eventually gather and settle.

The settlement was single-handedly established and maintained by an elderly man who financed and built the outpost out of the desert sands using his own funds. Passing travelers hold him in high regard for his fairness and honesty. While an ambitious local peddler attempts to assert control over the outpost and makes personal investments to boost his own influence, the elder graciously tolerates these ambitions, seeing them as beneficial to Ryu's ongoing growth.

Among the outpost's inhabitants is an animal lender who leases horses, camels, and mounts to desert wanderers. Other residents include a merchant couple who uprooted their family and migrated across the desert from [Sai](sai.md) to do business here.

## Story

Ryu serves as the destination and concluding setting of [Hikari](../characters/hikari.md)'s Chapter 1 journey across the sands. If Hikari is not chosen as the initial traveler, the party encounters him here standing against a group of ruffian soldiers harassing the local villagers, after which he joins the party.
