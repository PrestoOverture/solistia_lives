# Moonview Cliff

> *"No. It's not over. Something's coming... And it smells...familiar."*
> — [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md), scenting the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../world/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md)

**Moonview Cliff** is a coastal cliff and dungeon sub-area on the island of [Toto'haha](totohaha.md). Connected to the [Stormy Cape](stormy_cape.md), this windswept precipice overlooking the sea serves as the dramatic culmination of [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md)'s quest.

## Story

Moonview Cliff forms the fourth and final phase of Ochette's Chapter 3 dungeon. Having gathered the island's [Creatures of Legend](../world/creatures_of_legend.md)—[Tera](../world/tera.md), [Glacis](../world/glacis.md), and the offspring of [Cateracta](../world/cateracta.md)—Ochette ascends the cliff to face the catastrophic advent of the Night of the Scarlet Moon.

Upon the precipice, Ochette and the legendary beasts confront the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../world/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md)—the corrupted form of her lost companion whom she did not select at the outset of her journey (either [Akalā](../characters/akala.md) or [Mahina](../characters/mahina.md)). During the violent confrontation, Ochette is hurled off the precipice into the raging sea below, eventually washing ashore onto an adjacent, secluded stretch of beach beneath the cliffs before rallying for the final battle.
