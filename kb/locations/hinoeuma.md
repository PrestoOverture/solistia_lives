# Hinoeuma

**Hinoeuma** is an arid region in southwestern Solistia, renowned for its vast desert expanses, ancient martial traditions, and history of perpetual warfare.

## Geography and Environment

Hinoeuma is primarily dominated by endless rolling sand dunes, harsh desert winds, and rocky canyons. Amidst the barren wastes lies an oasis corridor—a strip of semi-arid riverlands extending north to south. In these fertile river bottoms, crops of barley and corn thrive in abundance. 

Because of the scarcity of water and arable territory, countless bloody conflicts have been fought over the oasis and surrounding wells, giving rise to numerous kingdoms that rose and fell over the centuries.

Hinoeuma borders the [Harborlands](harborlands.md) to the east, the [Wildlands](wildlands.md) to the northeast, and the [Leaflands](leaflands.md) to the north.

## History, Culture, and Legends

- **The Conjurer Goddess**: The region takes its name from [Priestess Hinoekagura](../characters/priestess_hinoekagura.md), the legendary goddess of conjuration whose myths and trials have been preserved here since ancient times.
- **Kingdoms of Ku and U**: Two decades prior to the journey of the eight travelers, the aggressive warrior nation of [Ku](../world/ku.md) conquered the fortress city of [Sai](sai.md) (then known as the Orochi Bastion) from the neighboring kingdom of [U](../world/u.md), bringing about the total downfall of U.
- **Brevity of Names**: A cultural oddity of Hinoeuma is the prevalence of extremely short names for nations, clans, and settlements, such as Ku, U, [Sai](sai.md), and [Ryu](ryu.md). A local desert proverb humorously claims that people keep their words short because "the longer the mouth flaps, the more sand finds its way in."

## Major Settlements

- **[Ku: Castle Town](ku_castle_town.md)**: A militaristic capital located at the southwestern tip of Solistia. Dominated by martial barracks and weapon smiths, it is ruled by the cursed, war-hungry Clan Ku and serves as the center of [Hikari Ku](../characters/hikari.md)'s storyline.
- **[Sai](sai.md)**: A fortified desert city built around ancient stone bastions. Formerly an important stronghold of the fallen realm of U, it is fraught with social tension between its native townsfolk and impoverished refugees huddled in its Eastern District.
- **[Ryu](ryu.md)**: A quiet desert waystation and trading post situated in the northwest sands. Serving as a crucial rest stop for caravans crossing the dunes, it is renowned as a hub where gossip and intelligence from across Solistia converge.

## Landmarks and Dungeons

### Altars
- **[Altar of the Thunderblade](altar_of_the_thunderblade.md)**: A sacred shrine hidden in the eastern sands dedicated to the war god [Brand](../world/brand.md).

### Prominent Sites and Dungeons
- **[Castle Ku](castle_ku.md)**: The towering, imposing fortress palace that serves as the seat of the monarchs of Ku.
- **[Five-Tiered Tower](five_tiered_tower.md)**: A sacred pagoda located near the entrance of Castle Ku. It houses a series of spiritual trials left behind by [Priestess Hinoekagura](../characters/priestess_hinoekagura.md), testing warriors before imparting the secret arts of the Conjurer.
- **[Decaying Temple](decaying_temple.md)**: An ancient ruin deep in the sands accessible only to lone wanderers, used as a lair by the cursed swordsman [Karma](../characters/karma.md).
- **[Tranquil Grotto](tranquil_grotto.md)**: A cavernous ruin buried in the sands east of Ku, hiding secrets tied to [The Journey for the Dawn](../stories/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md).
- **[Dragonridge](dragonridge.md)**: Sheer desert bluffs overlooking Sai.
- **[Sand Lion's Den](sand_lions_den.md)**: Subterranean burrow systems excavated by a ferocious [Sand Lion](../world/sand_lion.md).
- **[Quicksand Gaol](quicksand_gaol.md)**: Dangerous shifting-sand pits infested with giant sandworms and the predatory Lord of the Sands.
