# Southern Clockbank Highroad

> *"There is an altar in the [Brightlands](brightlands.md) said to grant special skills to thieves. Or rather, a 'chosen' thief who finds the altar. I heard it was hidden somewhere a \*tiny\* bit obscure along Southern Clockbank Highroad... But thieves are sharp. I'm certain a member of our guild would find it with ease. Hoho."*
> — Thieves Guild Master

The **Southern Clockbank Highroad** is a traveled thoroughfare in the [Brightlands](brightlands.md), connecting the industrial city of [Clockbank](clockbank.md) westward to the [Western Clockbank Highroad](western_clockbank_highroad.md).

## Landmarks

- **[Altar of the Prince of Thieves](altar_of_the_prince_of_thieves.md)**: Hidden within a secluded riverside cavern accessible by boat along the highroad, this sacred shrine is dedicated to [Aeber](../world/aeber.md), the Prince of Thieves. According to the master of the Thieves Guild, chosen thieves who seek out and reach this obscure sanctuary receive sacred blessings and secret arts.
