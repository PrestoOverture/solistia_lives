# Winterlands

The **Winterlands** is one of the eight regions comprising the continent of Solistia, characterized as a harsh, frozen expanse of snow and ice.

## History and Governance

The regional capital is [Stormhail](towns/stormhail.md), a heavily fortified mountainous city that serves as the headquarters of the [Sacred Guard](../../world/factions/sacred_guard.md) and the seat of [Clan Mei](../../world/factions/clan_mei.md). 

The Sacred Guard was originally founded roughly 380 years ago as the brainchild of Archbishop Kinchler. Following a catastrophic cold snap that ravaged the eastern continent in the previous century, the holy order established its headquarters in Stormhail to oversee continental peace and uphold law and order across Solistia.

## Geography and Economy

Beyond the mountain fortress of Stormhail, the region includes:
- **[Cape Cold](towns/cape_cold.md)**: A coastal fishing hamlet situated along the frozen sea. Local fishermen bring in daily hauls of salmon, trout, mackerel, squid, and other cold-water catches. Residents also work in textiles and agriculture, recently bolstered by the cultivation of a hardy strain of cold-resistant barley.
- **[Winterbloom](towns/winterbloom.md)**: A quiet, snowswept town formerly governed by Lady [Rosa](../../characters/castti/rosa.md) and later co-ruled by [Melia](../../characters/castti/melia.md) and [Greg](../../characters/castti/greg.md), known for its fine wheat and apple cider, as well as its adjoining criminal district.
- **[Frigit Isle](towns/frigit_isle.md)**: A bleak, isolated prison island situated in the icy waters of the [Sundering Sea](../sundering_sea/the_sundering_sea.md), used to incarcerate Solistia's most dangerous convicts under strict guard.

### Dungeons and Strongholds

- **[Ruffians' Hideout](dungeons/ruffians_hideout.md)**: An icy forest tract occupied by bandits.
