# Frigit Isle

> *"Frigit Isle. A solitary island in the far northern reaches of the sea. Some call it the edge of the world. Blanketed in snow all through the year. The nearest continent is due east, four days' journey by sea - even on the largest vessel. Even then, the fierce blizzards at sea can make a wreck of the mightiest ships. Trips to and from this accursed place are heavily regulated."* — [Osvald](../../../characters/osvald/osvald.md)

**Frigit Isle** is a solitary prison island in the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md), located in the far northern reaches of the [Sundering Sea](../../sundering_sea/the_sundering_sea.md). Blanketed in snow and ice throughout the year, it is often referred to as "the edge of the world."

## History

Frigit Isle was constructed atop the wreckage of abandoned ore mines lining the island. The prison is situated in the bitter far northern seas, where fierce blizzards can wreck even the sturdiest vessels. The nearest continent lies four days' journey to the east by sea, and all travel to and from the island is heavily regulated.

For eighty years, criminals convicted of the most heinous crimes across Solistia were sent to Frigit Isle, holding over 300 inmates at its peak. Manned by a single warden and 34 guards, the facility held 292 prisoners at the time of [Osvald](../../../characters/osvald/osvald.md)'s incarceration. Conditions inside were deplorable, and sentences frequently outlived the convicts. Prisoners were subjected to forced labor mining phosphoal under the watchful eyes of the warden and guards, with many succumbing to the extreme cold.

Escape was considered impossible due to the freezing waters and harsh elements. Over its eighty-year history, not a single prisoner was known to have successfully escaped alive, though bodies of convicts who attempted to flee occasionally washed ashore at [Cape Cold](cape_cold.md).

Common prisoners wore open-fronted shirts without buttons, scraps of cloth as scarves, and cloth-wrapped leather footwear, using lengths of cord as belts to secure cloth scraps or bags. Iron neck shackles and muzzles were reserved for convicts deemed particularly dangerous or capable of magic.

## Story

The prison's routine was disrupted when Osvald, a scholar convicted on a life sentence for the murder of his wife and daughter, was incarcerated on the island. Warden [Davids](../../../characters/osvald/davids.md) fitted Osvald with a heavy metal muzzle to suppress his spellcasting abilities.

Five years into his sentence, Osvald attacked a fellow inmate who had stolen his research notebook. Davids and the guards intervened, beating Osvald. The next day during phosphoal mining labor, the same inmate collapsed from hypothermia, whereupon Warden Davids ruthlessly killed him.

Over several days, Osvald gathered intelligence on the prison's underground passage, acquired thermal gear, and formed an alliance with [Emerald](../../../characters/osvald/emerald.md), a fellow inmate. During a periodic inspection of the prison, Osvald and Emerald launched an escape attempt through the underground passages. Warden Davids and his guards intercepted them, but Osvald and Emerald defeated and killed Davids.

Upon reaching the docks, they found that Davids had already alerted the inspectors' ship, placing the vessel on high alert. Despite Osvald's warning, Emerald attempted to commandeer the vessel, only to be surrounded by guards. To allow Osvald time to escape on an improvised raft, Emerald ignited phosphoal to set the ship ablaze, perishing in the fire and drawing the guards away.

Following Osvald's escape, the inspectors uncovered the warden's extreme corruption and the horrific conditions of the facility. The prison was officially shut down and the remaining inmates relocated. Despite the closure, an enigmatic inmate designated "Prisoner No. 186666"—sentenced to 25 years of imprisonment for killing his son two decades earlier—remained behind on the prison bridge at night.
