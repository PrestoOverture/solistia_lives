# Stormhail: Sacred Guard Headquarters

> *"Welcome to the [Sacred Guard](../../../world/factions/sacred_guard.md)'s headquarters. How may I assist you?"*
> — Registrar

The **Stormhail: Sacred Guard Headquarters** is a town sub-area within the mountain city of [Stormhail](stormhail.md) in the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md). Serving as the central base of operations and seat of command for the [Sacred Guard](../../../world/factions/sacred_guard.md), the facility houses administrative offices, barracks, and an extensive library.

According to [Ochette](../../../characters/ochette/ochette.md), the headquarters distinctly smells like "evil people."

## The Secret Archive

Within the headquarters' library, a hidden mechanism is concealed behind a volume titled *The Creation of Heaven and Earth*, where the cryptic clue "Break the earth's shackles, and look to the heavens" is carved into the woodwork. Interacting with the rafters activates a hidden passage located at the foot of a large stained-glass window. 

During [Temenos Mistral](../../../characters/temenos/temenos.md)'s investigation into church corruption (Chapter 3), this secret doorway is unlocked, revealing access to the [Forbidden Shrine](../dungeons/forbidden_shrine.md) where restricted, heretical tomes and suppressed records are stored away from the public eye.
