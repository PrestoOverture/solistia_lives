# Stormhail: Bridge

**Stormhail: Bridge** (ストームヘイル-大橋-) is a major district and transit thoroughfare within the mountain city of [Stormhail](stormhail.md) in the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md).

> "*This snow never stops. [Glacis](../../../world/beasts/glacis.md) sends it down from the peak. Has done for years now. Ever since... A band of hunters went after Glacis. We set up this barrier after that, for Glacis's protection.*"
> — Sanctum Knight

## Geography

The district spans the monumental stone bridge connecting the lower city of Stormhail to two key destinations:
- **[Sacred Peak Altahe](../dungeons/sacred_peak_altahe.md)** to the north: The mountain ascent has long been blocked off by a fortified barrier manned by the [Sacred Guard](../../../world/factions/sacred_guard.md), erected to shield the divine guardian [Glacis](../../../world/beasts/glacis.md) from poachers after hunters previously provoked the beast's wrath.
- **[Stormhail: Castle Mei](stormhail_castle_mei.md)** to the east: The ancestral seat of Clan Mei.

## Story Events

- **[Hikari Ku](../../../characters/travelers/hikari.md)**: During Hikari's arrival in Stormhail to treat with Clan Mei, a fierce storm sees a thunderbolt strike and shatter the bridge leading to the fortress. The chasm is subsequently bridged with sturdy wooden planking.
- **[Ochette](../../../characters/travelers/ochette.md)**: Seeks passage past the Sacred Guard's barricade to ascend Sacred Peak Altahe and quell Glacis.
