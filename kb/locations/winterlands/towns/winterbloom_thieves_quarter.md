# Winterbloom: Thieves' Quarter

> *"Careful, now. This is no place for common folk like yourself."*
> — Local Thief

The **Thieves' Quarter** is a squalid sub-district within the snowbound town of [Winterbloom](winterbloom.md) in the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md). It connects south to the main quarters of [Winterbloom](winterbloom.md) and north to the [Snowhares' Den](../dungeons/snowhares_den.md).

## Overview and History

Thieves and vagabonds first began congregating in this quarter over thirty years ago. Over time, it developed into a notorious haven for pickpockets, thugs, and underground syndicates. The [Blacksnakes](../../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md) maintain a covert stronghold within the quarter, with several local thieves sharing familial ties and half-sibling relations tied back to the organization.

The quarter also attracts outcasts from other regions; outside the tavern at night stands a bandit hailing from [Hinoeuma](../../hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md), a former royal warrior who detests cowardice.

## Story Events

During Chapter 2 of [Throné Anguis](../../../characters/travelers/throne.md)'s journey ([Father](../../../characters/throne/father.md)'s Route), Throné arrives in Winterbloom's Thieves' Quarter to meet with Father. Upon arriving at the local tavern, she is assaulted by three thugs whom she quickly dispatches, only to find Father had arranged the encounter to test her edge.

Father assigns her to help him raid the nearby [Snowhares' Den](../dungeons/snowhares_den.md) to execute [Bergomi](../../../characters/throne/bergomi.md), leader of the rival Snowhares gang. Sensing an opening to break her collar, Throné resolves to strike Father down during the chaos. Although they successfully neutralize Bergomi, Father anticipates Throné's intent and turns on her first, wounding her and leaving her behind in the den before she can regroup for another attempt on his life.
