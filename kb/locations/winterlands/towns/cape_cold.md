# Cape Cold

**Cape Cold** is a small, snowbound coastal settlement located on a frigid promontory in the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md).

## Geography and Atmosphere

Perched on the northern coast facing an icy, tempestuous sea, Cape Cold endures relentless winter cold and perpetual snowfall. A single route, the [Southern Cape Cold Snows](../wilds/southern_cape_cold_snows.md), connects the secluded hamlet with the interior of the Winterlands:

> "*Ngh. This village has but one season: frigid.*"

Because of the unforgiving waters separating the mainland from the penal colony on [Frigit Isle](frigit_isle.md), the frozen bodies of escaping convicts occasionally wash ashore along Cape Cold's desolate coastlines.

## Economy and Culture

Despite the severe climate, the village maintains a steady livelihood:
- **Fisheries**: According to the [Mercantile Manuscript](../../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md), fishermen bring in daily hauls of cold-water catch, including salmon, trout, mackerel, and squid.
- **Agriculture**: Villagers cultivate a specialized, hardy breed of cold-resistant barley alongside basic textile production.
- **Village Life**: The close-knit community includes hard-bitten fishermen, retired scholars, and newcomers such as a former dancer from [New Delsta](../../brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md) adjusting to the quiet, frozen life. At night, an aristocratic patriarch accompanied by an attendant watches over one of the cottages until dark, covertly funding the local fishing guild through anonymous benefactions.

## Story Significance

Cape Cold serves as the mainland landfall for the scholar [Osvald V. Vanstein](../../../characters/osvald/osvald.md) after his harrowing breakout from Frigit Isle and treacherous ocean crossing, marking the close of Chapter 2 of his quest for vengeance.
