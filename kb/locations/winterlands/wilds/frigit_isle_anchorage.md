# Frigit Isle: Anchorage

> *"The inspectors' vessel should be docked at the pier, and yet... The warden likely told them about our escape."* — [Osvald](../../../characters/osvald/osvald.md)

**Frigit Isle: Anchorage** is the coastal dock of [Frigit Isle](../towns/frigit_isle.md) in the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md). It connects uphill to [Frigit Isle: Entrance](frigit_isle_entrance.md) and serves as the island's maritime link across the [Sundering Sea](../../sundering_sea/the_sundering_sea.md) to [Cape Cold](../towns/cape_cold.md).

## Overview

The anchorage serves as the sole official landing point on Frigit Isle, where scheduled supply and inspection vessels dock. Due to the extreme blizzards and freezing waters of the northern seas, sea passage from this dock is treacherous and heavily regulated.

During [Osvald](../../../characters/osvald/osvald.md)'s prison break, he and [Emerald](../../../characters/osvald/emerald.md) reached the anchorage intending to seize the inspectors' ship. Finding the harbor placed on high alert by Warden [Davids](../../../characters/osvald/davids.md) and swarming with prison guards, Emerald sacrificed himself by setting the ship on fire using phosphoal. This distraction allowed Osvald to escape into the frigid waters aboard a makeshift raft fashioned from ice and frozen straw.

## Inhabitants and Wildlife

Aside from prison guards patrolling the docks, the icy terrain and shoreline are inhabited by arctic wildlife and monsters, including Frost Mole I, Frost Mole II, Ash Raven, Ice Wisp, and Wolf.
