# Altar of the Scholarking

The **Altar of the Scholarking** is a sacred site in the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md) dedicated to [Alephan](../../../world/gods/alephan.md), the Scholarking.

> *"Chosen scholar...  
> Unto thee, I grant new power."*  
> — [Alephan](../../../world/gods/alephan.md)

## Geography and Access

The entrance to the altar is located in the [Western Winterbloom Snows](western_winterbloom_snows.md), concealed beneath a stone bridge. Travelers reach it by following the paths toward the frozen lake from either the northeast or northwest side and moving underneath the bridge structure. The portal to the altar is set into the north wall beneath the bridge.

Within the shrine stands a statue of Alephan. When the scholar [Osvald](../../../characters/travelers/osvald.md) communes with the deity's likeness, he is granted the power of Teach.
