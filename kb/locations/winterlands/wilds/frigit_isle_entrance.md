# Frigit Isle: Entrance

> *"We've done it! We've escaped that godforsaken prison!"* — [Emerald](../../../characters/osvald/emerald.md)

**Frigit Isle: Entrance** is an exterior pathway on [Frigit Isle](../towns/frigit_isle.md) in the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md), connecting the prison compound above with the coastal docks below.

## Geography and Connections

Frigit Isle: Entrance sits on the snowy slopes between [Frigit Isle: Yard](../towns/frigit_isle_yard.md) to the north and [Frigit Isle: Anchorage](frigit_isle_anchorage.md) to the south. It also connects directly to the concealed outer breach of the [Prison: Underground Passage](../dungeons/prison_underground_passage.md).

During normal prison operation, the massive gates leading into the Yard were strictly barred. Following the closure of the penal facility, the exterior gates connecting the entrance to the yard remain open.

## History and Events

During their prison break, [Osvald](../../../characters/travelers/osvald.md) and [Emerald](../../../characters/osvald/emerald.md) navigated through the secret underground tunnels and emerged at the entrance area, momentarily believing they had successfully evaded the guards before discovering the harbor was on alert.

## Wildlife

The snow-covered grounds outside the prison walls are inhabited by dangerous arctic beasts, including Frost Mole I, Frost Mole II, Ash Raven, Ice Wisp, and Wolf.
