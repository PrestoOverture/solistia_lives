# Western Winterbloom Snows

The **Western Winterbloom Snows** is a snowy overland route situated within the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md), connecting the road from [Eastern Cape Cold Snows](eastern_cape_cold_snows.md) to the town of [Winterbloom](../towns/winterbloom.md).

> *"Have you heard of the altar said to grant a chosen scholar new power? If you wish to find that altar... I implore you to ask yourself why our guild is stationed here. The rest should fall into place if you do what a scholar does best and observe."*
> — Scholars Guild Master

## Landmarks & Overview

The wind-chilled route cuts through the frigid mountainous terrain and is home to notable scholastic and religious landmarks:

- **Scholars' Guild**: A branch of the scholars' guild is established along these snowy paths, where the guildmaster instructs and evaluates scholars traveling through the Winterlands.
- **Altar of the Scholarking**: Tucked away in the snows near the guild outpost lies the [Altar of the Scholarking](altar_of_the_scholarking.md), an ancient sanctuary consecrated to [Alephan](../../../world/gods/alephan.md), the Scholarking. As hinted by the guildmaster, scholars who observe the land and uncover the altar can receive divine arcane power.
