# Ruffians' Hideout

**Ruffians' Hideout** is a dungeon located in the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md), connected to the [Eastern Cape Cold Snows](../wilds/eastern_cape_cold_snows.md). It serves as the base for a gang of ruffians who terrorize travelers and locals in the surrounding region.

## Access

The hideout can be entered from the [Eastern Cape Cold Snows](../wilds/eastern_cape_cold_snows.md) through one of two methods:
- Directly subduing or knocking out the lookout guard posted at the main land entrance.
- Sailing into the interior by boat via the connecting river.
