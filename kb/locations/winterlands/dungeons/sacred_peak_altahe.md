# Sacred Peak Altahe

> *"It is said that [Stormhail](../towns/stormhail.md) owes its existence to its guardian, [Glacis](../../../world/beasts/glacis.md). Residing on the Sacred Peak Altahe, it is thought that Glacis put an end to the cold snap that nearly spelled doom for all who lived here."*
> — [Mercantile Manuscript](../../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md)

**Sacred Peak Altahe** is a sacred, towering mountain located in the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md), connected directly to [Stormhail: Bridge](../towns/stormhail_bridge.md). It is marked on maps as a mountain encircled by clouds.

## Lore & History

Sacred Peak Altahe is the domain and sanctuary of [Glacis](../../../world/beasts/glacis.md), the frost-winged guardian and one of Solistia's three ancient [Creatures of Legend](../../../world/lore/creatures_of_legend.md). Local lore and mercantile histories credit Glacis with halting a catastrophic cold snap that once brought the region to the verge of extinction, allowing the city of [Stormhail](../towns/stormhail.md) to endure.

Access to the mountain is strictly barred and guarded by the [Sacred Guard](../../../world/factions/sacred_guard.md) to protect the sanctum and prevent unauthorized travelers from ascending the treacherous peak.

## Story

During [Ochette](../../../characters/ochette/ochette.md)'s journey (Chapter 2: Glacis's Route), Ochette seeks an audience with Glacis to secure her aid against the coming Night of the Sorrowful Moon. Upon gaining entry, Ochette lets out a powerful roar that inadvertently triggers a massive avalanche, collapsing and sealing off the mountain's primary path.
