# Infernal Castle

> "*The legend of this wall continues thus: the [Great Wall](../../../world/lore/great_wall.md) is merely one small segment of some greater fortress, and the central citadel itself lies deep underground. Many archaeologists have spent years attempting to uncover this hidden redoubt to no avail.*"
> —*[Mercantile Manuscript](../../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md)*

The **Infernal Castle** is a secret subterranean fortress in the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md), located directly [Beneath the Wall](beneath_the_wall.md).

## Lore and History

According to the *[Mercantile Manuscript](../../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md)*, Solistian legend holds that the [Great Wall](../../../world/lore/great_wall.md) is merely a single visible segment of a vast ancient fortress, with its central citadel buried deep underground. For centuries, archaeologists searched in vain to uncover this hidden redoubt.

The castle's architecture bears a striking resemblance to ancient ruins like the [Seat of the Water Sprite](../../crestlands/dungeons/seat_of_the_water_sprite.md) and the [Forbidden Shrine](forbidden_shrine.md), suggesting they may have been constructed by the same ancient civilization.

Before the former high priest of the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../../../world/factions/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) vanished without a trace, he uttered the cryptic words, "To the Infernal Abyss." His relic, the *High Priest's Book of Scripture*, was left behind deep within the Infernal Castle, indicating that he met his demise in these depths.

## Access and Inhabitants

Access to the sunken castle requires crossing a fragile bridge in the cavern [Beneath the Wall](beneath_the_wall.md). The bridge collapses when subjected to the weight of seven people, plunging them directly into the chasm of the citadel below.

Deep within the ruins lurks the monstrous Dreadwolf.
