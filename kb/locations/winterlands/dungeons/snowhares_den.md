# Snowhares' Den

> *"There's one sentry at the entrance. Our job is to infiltrate the hideout and eliminate the leader of the [Snowhares](../../../world/factions/snowhares.md). You will take the lead, [Throné](../../../characters/travelers/throne.md). I will provide support."*
> — [Father](../../../characters/throne/father.md)

The **Snowhares' Den** is an underground criminal hideout situated in the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md), accessed directly from [Winterbloom: Thieves' Quarter](../towns/winterbloom_thieves_quarter.md).

## Story

The den serves as the headquarters of the [Snowhares](../../../world/factions/snowhares.md), a rival bandit syndicate operating out of Winterbloom under the leadership of [Bergomi](../../../characters/throne/bergomi.md). 

During Chapter 2 of [Throné Anguis](../../../characters/travelers/throne.md)'s journey (Father's Route), [Father](../../../characters/throne/father.md) brings Throné here on a mission for the [Blacksnakes](../../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md). After bypassing the sentry guarding the entrance, Throné infiltrates the subterranean stronghold to corner and eliminate Bergomi as part of Father's assignment.
