# Castle Mei: East Tower

> *"They tell me a castle from a great empire once sat on this site, as if that matters anymore."*
> — Clan Mei Soldier

**Castle Mei: East Tower** is a fortified stronghold within [Stormhail: Castle Mei](../towns/stormhail_castle_mei.md), located in the frigid mountains of the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md). Serving as the ancestral seat and military bastion of [Clan Mei](../../../world/factions/clan_mei.md), it connects the lower castle grounds to the [Castle Mei: Gallows](castle_mei_gallows.md).

## Overview & History

According to the garrisoned guards, Castle Mei was constructed over the foundations of an ancient fortress dating back to a lost empire. Under the leadership of [Rai Mei](../../../characters/hikari/rai_mei.md) and her clan, the East Tower served as a critical defensive bastion and staging ground for Clan Mei's forces.

During [Hikari Ku](../../../characters/hikari/hikari.md)'s journey to win Clan Mei to his cause, he traverses the East Tower to confront Rai Mei and halt the impending executions orchestrated under the shadow of Ku's tyranny. The tower is heavily defended by Clan Mei spearmen, ferocious war dogs, and thunder-powered sentinel automatons, alongside alpine creatures such as albino bats and frostwing serpents that nest within the high, drafty stone ramparts.
