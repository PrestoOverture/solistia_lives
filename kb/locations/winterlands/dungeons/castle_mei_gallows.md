# Castle Mei: Gallows

> *"Are you truly putting Prince Hikari to the sword?"*
> — Kunzo to Rai Mei

**Castle Mei: Gallows** is an execution platform and high tower within [Stormhail: Castle Mei](../towns/stormhail_castle_mei.md) in the [Winterlands](../winterlands.md). Reached by ascending through [Castle Mei: East Tower](castle_mei_east_tower.md), it serves as the climactic setting for [Hikari Ku](../../../characters/hikari/hikari.md)'s confrontation with [Rai Mei](../../../characters/hikari/rai_mei.md).

## Overview & Story

Perched high above the frozen fortress, the Gallows tower was designated by Clan Mei as a place of judgment and execution. Under the pressure of King [Mugen](../../../characters/hikari/mugen.md)'s ruthless subjugation of Solistia, Clan Mei was coerced into enforcing Ku's harsh demands, bringing Rai Mei to the brink of executing political prisoners and turning her blade against her former friend.

A confrontation takes place atop the gallows, where [Kunzo](../../../characters/hikari/kunzo.md) questions whether Rai Mei has truly forsaken honor to put Prince Hikari to the sword. Hikari reaches the platform to challenge Rai Mei, engaging in a decisive duel to break Mugen's hold over her conscience and unite Clan Mei with his struggle to restore Ku.
