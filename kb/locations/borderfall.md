# Borderfall

**Borderfall** is a scenic mountain and waterfall pass located within the [Crestlands](crestlands.md).

## Geography

Borderfall forms a natural frontier connecting western and eastern Crestlands routes:
- To the west lies the [Eastern Flamechurch Pass](eastern_flamechurch_pass.md).
- To the east lies the [Western Montwise Pass](western_montwise_pass.md).
- High above the waters sits the entrance to the cavernous [Seat of the Water Sprite](seat_of_the_water_sprite.md).
- Tucked within the pass is the sacred [Altar of the Flamebringer](altar_of_the_flamebringer.md).

## Landmarks

- **Clerics' Guild**: The pass serves as the headquarters for the Clerics' Guild, where priests and acolytes gather to study divine teachings.
- **Church of the Flamebringer**: Built directly into the cliffs to safeguard the [Altar of the Flamebringer](altar_of_the_flamebringer.md), this holy sanctuary allows chosen clerics to commune with divine power:

> "*Did you know that there is an altar that bestows a chosen cleric with special powers? This church was built to protect that very altar. You can reach it by going through that door just there. I urge you to take a look if you're interested.*"
