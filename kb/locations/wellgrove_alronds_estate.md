# Wellgrove: Alrond's Estate

> "*Welcome, honored guest! Please, take your ease!*"

## Overview

**Wellgrove: Alrond's Estate** is a town sub-area of [Wellgrove](wellgrove.md) in the [Leaflands](leaflands.md). It is the private residence of the famously wealthy aristocrat Lord [Alrond](../characters/alrond_rondwell.md).
