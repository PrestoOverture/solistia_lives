# Abandoned Silver Mine

The **Abandoned Silver Mine** is a field location situated in the [Wildlands](wildlands.md), connecting the town of [Oresrush](oresrush.md) to [Giff's Manse](giffs_manse.md).

## Overview

The area represents the present-day state of the former [Silver Mine](silver_mine.md). It features a railroad bridge and blocked-off mining tunnels that remain after mining operations ceased.
