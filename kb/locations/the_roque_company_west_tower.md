# The Roque Company: West Tower

**The Roque Company: West Tower** is a corporate facility situated on [Roque Island](roque_island.md) within the [Harborlands](harborlands.md), connected to [Roque Island: Headquarters](roque_island_headquarters.md).

## Overview

The West Tower forms part of the sprawling island headquarters of the [Roque Company](../world/roque_company.md). The facility is heavily secured by company security forces and mechanical sentries, as well as captured or tamed creatures including Ocean Lizardkings.

## Story

During [Partitio Yellowil](../characters/partitio.md)'s business journey to secure the rights to the steam engine, the investigative reporter [Ori](../characters/ori.md) guides Partitio through the corridors of the West Tower to bypass security and sneak into the corporate auditorium where [Roque](../characters/roque_brilliante.md) is holding a major presentation event.
