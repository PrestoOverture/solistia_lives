# Theater: Backstage

**Theater: Backstage** is the backstage area of the grand [theater](new_delsta_theater.md) in [New Delsta](new_delsta.md), located within the [Brightlands](brightlands.md).

## Overview

Connecting to both the main auditorium and the upper [passageways](theater_passageway.md), the backstage labyrinth supports the grand productions mounted at New Delsta's theater. The area is filled with stage rigging, props, backdrops, and costume racks—including a coatrack featuring a brimmed hat identical to the one worn by [Partitio Yellowil](../characters/partitio.md). Promotional posters celebrating the famous dancer [Dolcinaea Luciel](../characters/dolcinaea_luciel.md) line the hallways and dressing areas.

## Story

During [Agnea Bristarni](../characters/agnea.md)'s journey in New Delsta, she pursues the theater's ruthless manager, [La'mani](../characters/lamani.md), into the backstage corridors. Agnea seeks to confront him over his exploitation and cruelty toward [Gil](../characters/gil.md) and the tavern workers of the [New Delsta: Backstreets](new_delsta_backstreets.md). 

After pursuing La'mani through the backstage area, their confrontation spills directly out onto the main stage. Following Agnea's victory over La'mani and his troupe, the stage curtain rises to reveal the superstar Dolcinaea, who intervenes and forces La'mani to treat Agnea and her friends with decency under threat of leaving the production.
