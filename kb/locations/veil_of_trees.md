# Veil of Trees

The **Veil of Trees** is a dense woodland located off the [Forest Path](forest_path.md) near [Cropdale](cropdale.md) in the [Leaflands](leaflands.md).

> *"Have you seen Pala? She went raspberry pickin' in the woods, but she ain't come back yet. And that's not even the worst of it... In the forest...I found a duorduor's hoofprints."*
> — [Gus](../characters/gus.md)

## Overview & Story Role

The forest is a familiar foraging ground for Cropdale's residents, though dangerous beasts occasionally wander beneath the thick boughs.

During Chapter 1 of [Agnea](../characters/agnea.md)'s journey, her younger sister [Pala](../characters/pala_bristarni.md) slips into the woods to gather wild raspberries for Agnea's send-off festival at the tavern. After Pala fails to return and local tavern keeper [Gus](../characters/gus.md) spots fresh tracks belonging to a dangerous beast, Agnea and Gus venture into the woods together. In the depths of the grove, they encounter and slay a ravenous, aggressive boar-like monster called the [Duorduor](../characters/duorduor.md), successfully rescuing Pala and preserving the town's celebration.
