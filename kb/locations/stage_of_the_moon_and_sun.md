# Stage of the Moon and Sun

The **Stage of the Moon and Sun** is a location in the [Crestlands](crestlands.md), connected to the [Shrine of Ul'sterra](shrine_of_ulsterra.md). It serves as the grand venue where [Agnea Bristarni](../characters/agnea.md) confronts the superstar dancer [Dolcinaea Luciel](../characters/dolcinaea_luciel.md) during the [Grand Gala](../world/grand_gala.md)'s Festival of Grace.

> "*[Sealticge](../world/sealticge.md), Lady of Grace, is no more. In her place is another worthy of the title. She stands before you tonight to entertain you. Her name is... [Dolcinaea Luciel](../characters/dolcinaea_luciel.md)! Ahahaha!*"
> — Dolcinaea at the Festival of Grace

## Lore

The stage is situated within the sacred precincts connected to the Shrine of Ul'sterra. Surrounded by seating for vast spectator crowds, it hosts the climax of the Grand Gala, where Dolcinaea attempts to claim the mantle of the Lady of Grace before being challenged in dance by Agnea.
