# Southern Cape Cold Snows

The **Southern Cape Cold Snows** is a snowy field route situated in the [Winterlands](winterlands.md). It serves as the primary thoroughfare linking the coastal village of [Cape Cold](cape_cold.md) to the [Eastern Cape Cold Snows](eastern_cape_cold_snows.md) and southward into the [Brightlands](brightlands.md) via the [New Delsta Flats](new_delsta_flats.md).

## Story & Events

For travelers arriving from the frozen north, this passage represents the southern boundary of the Winterlands where the snowfields meet the warmer climes of the Brightlands. If [Osvald](../characters/osvald.md) is chosen as the starting traveler upon escaping [Frigit Isle](frigit_isle.md), the side story *[The Traveler's Bag](../stories/the_travelers_bag.md)* takes place along this road when an itinerant traveler is ambushed by bandits.
