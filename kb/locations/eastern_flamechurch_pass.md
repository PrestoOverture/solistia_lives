# Eastern Flamechurch Pass

**Eastern Flamechurch Pass** is a mountain trail located in the [Crestlands](crestlands.md), situated just outside the town of [Flamechurch](flamechurch.md).

## Geography and Routes

The pass serves as a central crossroads linking several distinct regions:
- To the west lies the settlement of [Flamechurch](flamechurch.md).
- To the north, the path climbs into the [Eastern Cape Cold Snows](eastern_cape_cold_snows.md).
- To the south, the road leads toward the [Western Clockbank Highroad](western_clockbank_highroad.md).
- To the east, the trail connects to [Borderfall](borderfall.md).

## Story and Events

When the cleric [Temenos](../characters/temenos.md) embarks on his journey from Flamechurch as a starting traveler, he travels through the Eastern Flamechurch Pass and becomes involved in the side story [The Traveler's Bag](../stories/the_travelers_bag.md).

## Wildlife

The rugged mountain pass is inhabited by various fauna and creatures, including Mountain Apes, Aggressive Ants, Giant Falcons, Dread Falcons, Rockadillos, Highland Goats, and nocturnal Shadow Wisps.
