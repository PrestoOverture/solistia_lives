# Mother's Garden: Orphanage

**Mother's Garden: Orphanage** is a field settlement located in the [Leaflands](leaflands.md), situated along the [Road to Mother's Garden](road_to_mothers_garden.md) before the gated entrance to [Mother's Garden](mothers_garden.md).

## Overview & History

Concealed behind a facade of charitable piety, the orphanage was established by [Mother](../characters/mother.md) as a clandestine breeding and training ground for future [Blacksnakes](../world/blacksnakes.md) operatives, referred to within the syndicate as "hatchlings." To secure the patronage and funding of the nearby monastery in [Wellgrove](wellgrove.md), the guild's caretakers and handlers masqueraded as gentle clerics and nuns, while the criminal [Morozov](../characters/morozov.md) acted as the facility's day-to-day manager.

Within the oppressive walls of the institution, children were subjected to rigorous training, espionage grooming, and severe abuse under the threat of summons to [Blackbell Tower](mothers_garden.md). Cryptic notations recorded in the original profiles of certain orphans—such as "Moonfang blood," "white wolf language," and "Shadowsage Eye" (corresponding to the Moonfang dagger, White Wolf Scarf, and Shadowsage Necklace)—hinted at rare lineages, innate talents, or night-aligned affinities among the charges.

## Liberation & Reformation

Following the death of [Mother](../characters/mother.md) and the conclusion of [Throné Anguis](../characters/throne.md)'s journey, the criminal ring was dismantled. Genuine nuns from the monastery took over the care of the children, transforming the compound into a legitimate, joyful refuge.

The reform was further solidified following the resolution of [Mira](../characters/mira.md)'s quest, when the [Elderly Guard](../characters/elderly_guard.md)—who had long maintained a conflicted watch over the compound under guild orders—dedicated his remaining years to acting as a loving, protective surrogate father to all the orphans. The children flourished under this newfound safety; among them, an enterprising young orphan began hand-carving ornate wooden accessories of such high quality that traveling merchants arrived in droves to purchase them, donating every coin of his earnings directly to the orphanage's maintenance.
