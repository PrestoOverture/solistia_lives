# Oresrush

**Oresrush** is a frontier mining town located in the [Wildlands](wildlands.md). It is the hometown of the merchant [Partitio](../characters/partitio.md) and serves as the setting for the beginning of his journey, as well as the setting for [Throné](../characters/throne.md)'s pursuit of [Mother](../characters/mother.md).

> *"We'll make this town twice as prosperous as it once was!"*
> — Townsperson

## Geography and Layout

Oresrush is nestled in mineral-rich territory surrounded by volcanic soil. It connects to the [Southern Oresrush Wilds](southern_oresrush_wilds.md) to the south, the [Oresrush: Foundry](oresrush_foundry.md) to the north, and the [Silver Mine](silver_mine.md) (later the [Abandoned Silver Mine](abandoned_silver_mine.md)) to the east. Path exits from the settlement are marked with wooden guideposts inscribed in [Solistian script](../world/alphabet.md).

Key establishments and landmarks include:
- **Papp's House**: The residence of [Papp](../characters/papp_yellowil.md) and Partitio, where a hat identical to Partitio's hangs on a rack in the study.
- **The Tavern**: A central gathering spot for miners and laborers, featuring a portrait of eight travelers hanging upon its eastern wall.
- **The Ranch**: Operated by a former knight who once rode dozens of warhorses into battle viewing them merely as instruments of war. After a mount saved his life on the battlefield, he experienced a change of heart and founded the ranch to atone and care for horses.
- **The Saddlery**: A leatherworking shop that secretly acts as an underworld contact point for [The Slaver](../characters/the_slaver.md).
- **The Foundry**: The industrial smelting center in the upper tier of town where mined ores are processed.

## History

According to the [Mercantile Manuscript](../stories/masoud_script.md), the volcanic soil of the surrounding Wildlands conceals immense mineral wealth, prompting the rise of numerous boomtowns. 

### Foundation and Prosperity
Sixteen years before the present day, Oresrush did not exist; the site was merely a desolate, barren plot of land. The silver merchants [Papp](../characters/papp_yellowil.md) and [Roque](../characters/roque_brilliante.md) purchased the property and founded the settlement as a silver-mining venture. Eight years later, the town reached the height of its economic boom, becoming one of Solistia's richest sources of silver, where a single bag of ore could command as much as 1,800,000 leaves.

### The Lean Years
Shortly following its peak, the original landowner bought back the settlement and levied ruinous taxes on the silver extraction. Deprived of their livelihood, the townspeople fell into bitter poverty, struggling without work and surviving on stale bread crusts while the buildings decayed.

### Revitalization
Years later, [Partitio](../characters/partitio.md) rallied his lifelong friends—including [Ned](../characters/ned_octopath_traveler_ii.md), [Joe](../characters/joe.md), [Harry](../characters/harry_octopath_traveler_ii.md), and [Nikki](../characters/nikki.md)—to take a stand against the corrupt landowner's ruthless enforcer, [Giff](../characters/giff.md). By overcoming Giff and reclaiming the stolen silver, Partitio broke the oppressive monopoly, restored economic vitality to Oresrush, and inspired the townspeople to rebuild their home.

## Culture and Lore

- **The Corvushooter**: According to Partitio, Oresrush was once plagued by swarms of intrusive crows. To drive them away, Papp invented a peculiar device called the "Corvushooter"—a wooden stick designed to fling large metal pellets at the birds. The metal ammunition quickly became a runaway commercial success in town.
- **Local Affairs**: Oresrush serves as the locale for community troubles such as [Stolen Goods](../stories/stolen_goods.md) and the search for levity in [Wanted: A Good Joke](../stories/wanted_a_good_joke.md).
