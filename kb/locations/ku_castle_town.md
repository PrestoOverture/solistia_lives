# Ku: Castle Town

**Ku: Castle Town** is a settlement in [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md) that serves as the heart of the kingdom of [Ku](../world/ku.md). Connected south to the [Southern Ku Sands](southern_ku_sands.md) and north to [Castle Ku: Entrance](castle_ku_entrance.md), the town has historically housed both lowborn commoners and soldiers under a regime marked by oppression and relentless warfare, in which the lower classes were treated as expendable. The castle town has stood impregnable for centuries; its front gate has not been breached once in its long history, forcing enemy armies to assault its western gate instead. Unlike most major settlements across Solistia, Ku: Castle Town has no inn.

Stringing across the town are lanterns crafted to resemble the human soul, designed to send off the spirits of the deceased. Near the settlement entrance, a horse lender raises a herd of horses renowned among traveling merchants for their stamina, docility, and obedience. In the aftermath of the civil war, a cleric from the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../world/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) also begins visiting the town by night to proselytize and extend the faith's reach within Ku's borders.

## History

### The Usurpation and Liberation
For generations, Ku engaged in continuous warfare against neighboring territories until King [Jigo Ku](../characters/jigo_ku.md) brought an end to foreign campaigns. When Jigo selected his younger son [Hikari Ku](../characters/hikari.md) as his successor, [General Mugen](../characters/mugen.md) launched a violent coup, assassinating his father and seizing the throne. Mugen subsequently razed Ku: Castle Town to crush potential rebellion, forcing Hikari to flee into exile.

The town remained firmly under Mugen's martial grip until Hikari returned at the head of an allied army. Storming through the castle town, Hikari confronted and vanquished Mugen. Upon taking the throne, King Hikari issued a royal proclamation declaring that Ku would emancipate all people it had subjugated and pursue a future of peace, diplomacy, and mutual fellowship.

Following the war, much of the town lay in ruins, with collapsed buildings and debris lining the thoroughfares, prompting local carpenters and residents to labor extensively to rebuild the settlement to its former state.

### Ritual of Remembrance
Following Hikari's coronation, [Benkei](../characters/benkei.md) suggested holding a celebration in his honor. Working alongside [Agnea](../characters/agnea.md), Hikari chose to dedicate the event as the "Ritual of Remembrance," honoring all who perished during the conflict. Together, Hikari and Agnea gathered sacred tree wood, ceremonial wine, a dancer's mask, and enlisted the lutist [Yomi](../characters/yomi.md) to perform an elaborate memorial ceremony for the fallen.
