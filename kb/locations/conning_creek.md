# Conning Creek

**Conning Creek** is a coastal town located in the [Harborlands](harborlands.md). It serves as the setting for [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md)'s Chapter 2 ([Cateracta](../world/cateracta.md) route), [Osvald](../characters/osvald.md)'s Chapter 3, and [Temenos](../characters/temenos.md) and [Throné](../characters/throne.md)'s Crossed Path (Part 2).

## Overview

Conning Creek connects to [Conning Creek: Outskirts](conning_creek_outskirts.md) to the east, [Conning Creek: Harbor](conning_creek_harbor.md) to the northwest, a [Guard Outpost](guard_outpost.md) to the north, and the [Western Conning Creek Coast](western_conning_creek_coast.md) to the west. The town maintains a bustling daytime marketplace and is known for its strong seafood culture, with townsfolk having an especial affinity for octopus. The Apothecaries' Guild is established in the southern district of the settlement.

## History

In the past, Conning Creek was protected by the sea guardian [Cateracta](../world/cateracta.md), who preserved the town by braving a colossal tidal wave. The ordeal left the sacred beast severely weakened, allowing the [Dark Hunter](../characters/petrichor.md) to strike her down. With the passage of time, Cateracta's sacrifice was largely forgotten by the citizenry, leaving only her bleached bones.

## Culture & Notable Lore

- **Seafood Cuisine**: The head chef at the local tavern is celebrated as the "God of Seafood Cuisine," renowned for whole roasted octopus, his lunch special "Chef's Choice on a Stick," and the dinner special "Rare Fish Supreme." His apprentices have traveled widely, disseminating his recipes and culinary seasonings throughout Solistia. His inventive daughter created the famous local specialty "octoball stew."
- **Palatial Prodigy**: A young local girl is known among residents as the "Palatial Prodigy" due to her refined and sophisticated tastes.
- **The Heirloomed Vest**: For generations, a prominent local lineage kept an heirloom vest believed to bestow fortune upon its wearer. Its current owner, a bedridden young girl living on the [Conning Creek: Outskirts](conning_creek_outskirts.md), suffers from a severe illness (intertwined with the quest [A Young Girl's Wish](../stories/a_young_girls_wish.md)).
- **The Watch at the Ruins**: A solitary guard has spent five years continuously surveilling the burned remnants of [Osvald](../characters/osvald.md)'s former estate on the [outskirts](conning_creek_outskirts.md), stationed there under the direction of [Harvey](../characters/harvey.md).
- **Sunken Cargo**: According to the leader of the Apothecaries' Guild, the legendary Double Tomahawk was recovered from an uncharted island but lost beneath the waves of the [Sundering Sea](the_sundering_sea.md) when the transport vessel ran aground southwest of Conning Creek.
