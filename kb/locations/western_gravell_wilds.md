# Western Gravell Wilds

**Western Gravell Wilds** is a desolate, high-danger wilderness region in the northern reaches of the [Wildlands](wildlands.md).

## Geography and Connections

A rugged expanse of jagged earth and canyon approaches, the Western Gravell Wilds links the frontier settlement of Gravell with neighboring regions:

- **East**: Leads into the secluded border town of [Gravell](gravell.md).
- **Southwest**: Connects back toward the [Western Crackridge Wilds](western_crackridge_wilds.md).
- **South**: Descends into the perilous chasm of the [Ivory Ravine](ivory_ravine.md).

## The Myth of the Sunshadow Staff

A local Wildlands myth, remembered by elders and the head of the Clerics' Guild from her childhood in Gravell, speaks of a wicked entity that takes root within the darkness of children's hearts. At the break of dawn outside the entrance to Gravell, an eight-year-old child possessed by this malevolent force wanders the wasteland.

When confronted and coerced, the entity manifests as a monstrous shadow of pure dread and hatred, professing that fear bears countless names—ranging from pestilence, curse, and slaughter to humanity itself. Once the malevolence is driven from the child's heart, the entity is exorcised and sealed away, crystallizing into the legendary **Sunshadow Staff**.
