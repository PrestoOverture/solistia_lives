# Eastern Wellgrove Trail

The **Eastern Wellgrove Trail** is a forested road located in the [Leaflands](leaflands.md), leading east from the town of [Wellgrove](wellgrove.md).

## Geography and Routes

The trail acts as a transition point between the lush foliage of the Leaflands and the neighboring desert lands:
- To the west, the path leads into the town of [Wellgrove](wellgrove.md).
- To the north, the road connects with the [Northern Wellgrove Trail](northern_wellgrove_trail.md).
- To the south, the forest path transitions across regional borders into the [Western Sai Sands](western_sai_sands.md) of Hinoeuma.

## Story and Quests

- **The Runaways**: Prior to the resolution of the side story [The Runaways](../stories/the_runaways.md), an elusive horse thief hides along the southern reaches of the trail.

## Wildlife

The dense woods bordering the trail host numerous forest beasts and birdfolk:
- **Fauna**: Forest Foxes, Red Foxes, quilled Porcupines, Demon Deer, Horned Howlers, Giant Boars, and formidable Gargantuan Boars.
- **Insects and Avian Tribes**: Silky Cocoons and mature Great Silk Moths, alongside territorial bands of Woodland Birdians led by their Woodland Birdking.
