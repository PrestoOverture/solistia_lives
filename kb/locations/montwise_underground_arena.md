# Montwise: Underground Arena

> *"So, this is the fabled arena? I hope it's worthy of me..."*
> — Soldier

The **Montwise Underground Arena** is a clandestine fighting colosseum and sub-area hidden beneath the academic town of [Montwise](montwise.md) in the [Crestlands](crestlands.md). Concealed far beneath the scholarly avenues of the city, gladiators fight to the death within its sands to satisfy the wagers of wealthy spectators and gamblers. Rather than formal music, the arena is characterized by the roaring ambiance of cheering spectators echoing through the stone amphitheater at night.

## Story

During Chapter 2 of [Hikari](../characters/hikari.md)'s journey, the exiled prince journeys to Montwise to recruit the famed tactician [Kazan](../characters/oboro.md). Upon finding him, Kazan forces Hikari into the arena pit to fight a consecutive gauntlet of hardened combatants. Kazan places massive wagers on the young prince—hailed as the "Sword of the West"—using the escalating payouts to settle astronomical debts totaling billions of leaves.

After Hikari vanquishes the reigning champion, [Bandelam the Reaper](../characters/bandelam.md), Kazan orchestrates the downfall of the arena's corrupt owner, [Borneau](../characters/borneau.md). By liberating the oppressed gladiators from Borneau's cruel exploitation, Kazan and Hikari earn their unyielding loyalty, and the fighters pledge themselves to Hikari's campaign to reclaim Ku.

## Notable Gladiators & Lore

- **Investigator Turned Patron**: The Sacred Guard once dispatched a cleric to Montwise to investigate and shut down the illicit arena. However, the investigator was completely corrupted by the venue, ultimately becoming its guest of honor and squandering his entire church stipend on high-stakes gambling.
- **The Fan of Elegance**: An Enthusiastic Spectator frequents the arena stands. Originally an ardent admirer captivated by the graceful swordplay of [Yurinas](../characters/yurinas.md), he became enamored with the prowess of the traveler who defeated her and watches the ring in hopes of another glimpse.
- **Notable Competitors**:
  - **Chrono, Hunter of Sins**: A former Sanctum Knight who has taken as many lives in the ring as he has claimed tournament victories.
  - **Schneider, the Deadly Stinger**: A cunning, undefeated combatant widely rumored to poison his blades.
  - **Vlad the Bloodstained**: A brutal brawler whose nose bleeds whenever he grows stimulated by violence, driven by an intoxicating thirst for blood.
- **Extra Battles**: The underground arena also serves as the proving ground for Solistia's Extra Battles against formidable adversaries from beyond.
