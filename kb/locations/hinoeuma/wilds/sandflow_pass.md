# Sandflow Pass

> *"Hello! Is anyone there? Please, shout if you can hear me!"*
> — [Castti Florenz](../../../characters/castti/castti.md), searching for wounded

**Sandflow Pass** is a desert pathway in [Hinoeuma](../hinoeuma.md), connecting the town of [Sai](../towns/sai.md) to the [Old Campsite](old_campsite.md).

## Overview & Story

The pass is an arid corridor subject to shifting sands and dangerous desert predators. During Chapter 2 of [Castti Florenz](../../../characters/castti/castti.md)'s travels (Sai Route), Castti traverses Sandflow Pass in search of wounded soldiers caught in the devastating quicksand caused by the subterranean stirring of the [Sand Lion](../../../world/beasts/sand_lion.md).

Along the pass sits an outpost guardhouse, notably containing an old map of Orsterra hung upon its wall.
