# Battlefield

The **Battlefield** is a war-torn expanse located in [Hinoeuma](../hinoeuma.md).

## History and Events

Three years prior to the journey to reclaim [Ku](../../../world/nations/ku.md), the battlefield served as the site of a bloody military clash depicted in [Hikari](../../../characters/hikari/hikari.md)'s past. During the conflict, Prince Hikari led Clan Ku's forces into combat against enemy troops from the south, culminating in a clash against the Southern General.
