# Southern Ku Sands

The **Southern Ku Sands**, also known as the **Valley of Sandstorms**, is a desert field route in [Hinoeuma](../hinoeuma.md) lying just south of [Ku: Castle Town](../towns/ku_castle_town.md).

> *"The Valley of Sandstorms," they call it. Wind from this region is funneled into this pass.*
> — [Kazan](../../../characters/hikari/oboro.md)

## Geography

The Southern Ku Sands connects to [Ku: Castle Town](../towns/ku_castle_town.md) to the north and leads toward the [Eastern Ku Sands](eastern_ku_sands.md) to the east.

The pass earned its moniker due to the fierce desert winds naturally funneled through the rocky valley corridor. When frigid air currents from the eastern sea meet the southern desert winds and sweep through the valley, they can unleash destructive gale-force winds capable of smashing open fortified castle gates.

A clifftop grave memorial overlooking the sands is known as the **Crest of Heroes**.

## History & Story

During [Hikari](../../../characters/hikari/hikari.md)'s youth, Southern Ku Sands became a critical battleground. Following the coup by [Mugen](../../../characters/hikari/mugen.md) and the fall of Castle Ku, Hikari fought his way through the sands, where he was confronted and betrayed by his former comrade [Ritsu Mishuyo](../../../characters/hikari/ritsu_mishuyo.md). Following their battle, Hikari went into exile, and the upper approaches of the pass were subsequently fortified and blocked by Mugen's garrison.

Years later, during Hikari's return to liberate Ku, his tactician [Kazan](../../../characters/hikari/oboro.md) exploited the valley's distinctive atmospheric conditions. By calculating the timing of the eastern sea winds, Kazan harnessed the sandstorms to breach Ku's defenses and lead the allied army into the city.

## Notable Inhabitants

Outside of the city conflicts, an arena-like stage to the west of the sands is frequented by an aspiring swordsman undergoing a profound martial journey:
- Beginning as a humble **Refined Warrior** seeking tutelage from wandering masters, his growing prowess leads him to become a **Confident Warrior**, constantly hunting for worthy opponents.
- Upon attaining peak martial skill, he falls into hubris as an **Arrogant Warrior** clad in black armor, believing he has conquered the sword's ultimate peak and viewing his former master as nothing more than a stepping stone left behind.
- When brought low in combat, he is humbled and becomes the **Proud Warrior**, acknowledging that arrogance stems from a weak heart. Clad once more in red armor, he commits himself to refining both spirit and blade to one day become a true warrior worthy of standing alongside the king of Ku.
