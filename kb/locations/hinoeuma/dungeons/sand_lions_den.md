# Sand Lion's Den

> *"I'm going to have a word with that [sand lion](../../../world/beasts/sand_lion.md). There's no time. If it thrashes about again, every last person here will call these sands their grave."*
> — [Castti](../../../characters/castti/castti.md)

**Sand Lion's Den** is a cavernous underground lair in [Hinoeuma](../hinoeuma.md), connected to the [Old Campsite](../wilds/old_campsite.md) near [Sai](../towns/sai.md).

## Overview & Story

The subterranean cavern is the nesting place of the giant, predatory [Sand Lion](../../../world/beasts/sand_lion.md). When disturbed, the creature thrashes violently beneath the desert, churning the earth and opening immense pits of quicksand across the surface above.

During Chapter 2 of [Castti Florenz](../../../characters/castti/castti.md)'s journey (Sai Route), the battle between Sai's immigrant garrison and invading Timberain soldiers is abruptly halted when an enraged Sand Lion destabilizes the ground, trapping combatants from both sides in lethal quicksand. To halt further destruction and rescue the dying soldiers, Castti and the local garrison commander [Edmund](../../../characters/castti/edmund.md) descend deep into the den to confront and subdue the ferocious beast.
