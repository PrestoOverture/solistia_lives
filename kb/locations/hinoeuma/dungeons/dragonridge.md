# Dragonridge

> "*I will be watching the show from atop the hill...*"
> —[Dolcinaea Luciel](../../../characters/agnea/dolcinaea_luciel.md), preparing to watch [Sai: East District](../towns/sai_east_district.md)'s destruction

**Dragonridge** is a rugged highland dungeon located in the arid region of [Hinoeuma](../hinoeuma.md). Branching directly off [Sai: East District](../towns/sai_east_district.md), the rocky bluff overlooks the impoverished eastern half of the settlement.

## Overview & Geography

Connected to the dusty outskirts of [Sai: East District](../towns/sai_east_district.md), Dragonridge features twisting rocky paths and cavernous inclines inhabited by desert vermin such as scorpions, beetles, and desert worms. Its highest outlook commands a panoramic view over Sai and the surrounding desert sands.

## Story

Dragonridge serves as the setting for the climax of [Agnea Bristarni](../../../characters/agnea/agnea.md)'s fourth chapter. The famed superstar [Dolcinaea Luciel](../../../characters/agnea/dolcinaea_luciel.md) conspires to demolish the East District of [Sai](../towns/sai.md) against the will of its impoverished townspeople in order to erect a magnificent new performance stage. Dolcinaea retreats to the heights of Dragonridge to watch the planned demolition as entertainment, stationing her loyal and stalwart bodyguard [Veronica](../../../characters/agnea/veronica.md) along the trail to thwart any interference.

Determined to preserve the townspeople's homes and livelihood, Agnea ascends the treacherous ridge and defeats Veronica in battle. Reaching the peak, Agnea confronts Dolcinaea directly, challenging her superficial, self-aggrandizing vision of dance and reaffirming her commitment to bringing hope and joy to everyday people through her art.
