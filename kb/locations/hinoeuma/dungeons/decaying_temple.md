# Decaying Temple

The **Decaying Temple** is an ancient, ruined temple located in the arid deserts of [Hinoeuma](../hinoeuma.md).

## Overview

The temple lies north of the [Eastern Sai Sands](../wilds/eastern_sai_sands.md). Engraved directly upon its heavy entrance gates is the austere warning: *"Come alone if you wish to pass through the gate."* In keeping with this trial, the entrance remains sealed unless a warrior approaches completely unassisted—barring entry to anyone accompanied by traveling companions or summoned allies.

Deep within the ruined sanctuary awaits the solitary blade master [Karma](../../../characters/side_stories/karma.md).

## Ties to Ku & Relics

- **Dragon Banners of Ku**: Hanging throughout the dusty stone corridors are red-and-gold dragon banners identical to those adorning [Castle Ku](../towns/castle_ku.md), strongly linking the temple's forgotten origins to the martial history and royal lineage of [Ku](../../../world/nations/ku.md).
- **The Fire Dragon's Glaive**: Hidden behind a crimson banner within a secret inner chamber marked by the dragon motif lies the Fire Dragon's Glaive. According to the Merchants' Guild master in the [Western Crackridge Wilds](../../wildlands/wilds/western_crackridge_wilds.md), this legendary polearm was famously used in antiquity to strike down a fire-breathing monster in the [Wildlands](../../wildlands/wildlands.md), before being stolen away and hidden from the world.
