# Five-Tiered Tower

The **Five-Tiered Tower** is an ancient tower and trial dungeon located at [Castle Ku: Entrance](../towns/castle_ku_entrance.md) in the region of [Hinoeuma](../hinoeuma.md), within the city of [Ku](../../../world/nations/ku.md). It can only be entered once [Hikari](../../../characters/hikari/hikari.md)'s journey is concluded.

## Overview

Unlike conventional dungeons, the Five-Tiered Tower features no random wandering beasts. Instead, it consists of five successive floors, each guarded by a formidable adversary:

- **First Floor**: Gken
- **Second Floor**: Ichchadhari the Snake Charmer
- **Third Floor**: Tyran the Seeker
- **Fourth Floor**: Auðnvarg
- **Fifth Floor**: [Priestess Hinoekagura](../../../characters/side_stories/priestess_hinoekagura.md)

The fourth floor is distinctive for containing a skeleton lying in a puddle of blood with a sword embedded in it, whereas the other trial floors remain bare.

Ascending to the uppermost tier and vanquishing [Priestess Hinoekagura](../../../characters/side_stories/priestess_hinoekagura.md) concludes the trial in the side story [A Tower of Trials](../../../stories/side_stories/hinoeuma/a_tower_of_trials.md), rewarding the travelers with the Proof of the Conjurer and unlocking the secrets of the Conjurer discipline.
