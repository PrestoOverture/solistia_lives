# Tranquil Grotto

**Tranquil Grotto** is a cave located in the [Hinoeuma](../hinoeuma.md) region of Solistia, accessible from the [Eastern Ku Sands](../wilds/eastern_ku_sands.md). Deep within the grotto's depths lies one of Solistia's [Sacred Flames](../../../world/lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md).

## History and Lore

Near the grotto's entrance stands an ancient mural which, according to [Hikari](../../../characters/travelers/hikari.md), depicts the beginning of [Ku](../../../world/nations/ku.md). Additionally, circular stone patterns identical to those found on the wall of the [Gate of Finis](../../sundering_sea/dungeons/the_gate_of_finis_octopath_traveler_ii.md) can be observed carved into the grotto.

During their childhood, the dancer [Yomi](../../../characters/others/yomi.md) and her sister [Tsuki](../../../characters/hikari/tsuki.md) often took refuge in the Tranquil Grotto, where the two would play the lute together in peaceful seclusion.

## Story Events

The Tranquil Grotto is the setting for the second part of [Hikari Ku](../../../characters/travelers/hikari.md) and [Agnea Bristarni](../../../characters/travelers/agnea.md)'s Crossed Paths story. Drawn by the distant melody of Yomi's lute, Hikari and Agnea venture deep into the cavern to find her.
