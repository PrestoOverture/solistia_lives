# Castle Ku: Entrance

> *"We must always remain wary of danger, especially in times of peace. That's why I became a soldier."*
> — Ku Soldier

**Castle Ku: Entrance** is a prominent district within [Ku: Castle Town](ku_castle_town.md) in the desert realm of [Hinoeuma](../hinoeuma.md). Standing at the threshold of the royal seat of power, it represents the martial heart of the [Ku](../../../world/nations/ku.md) nation and connects directly to [Castle Ku](castle_ku.md) and the [Five-Tiered Tower](../dungeons/five_tiered_tower.md).

## Overview & History

[Castle Ku](castle_ku.md) has stood impregnable for centuries, its great gate having never been breached in all of the nation's blood-soaked history. Outside the gates hung the Bell of Dawn, a massive bell whose ominous tolling summoned the realm's warriors and signaled the outbreak of war. Under the reign of warlords such as King [Mugen](../../../characters/hikari/mugen.md), the castle grounds were strictly off-limits to commoners, heavily guarded by elite soldiers and sentinel automatons.

During the civil war to reclaim Ku, [Hikari Ku](../../../characters/travelers/hikari.md) gathered his allies at the castle gates. Here, [Rai Mei](../../../characters/hikari/rai_mei.md), [Ageha](../../../characters/hikari/ageha.md), [Kunzo](../../../characters/hikari/kunzo.md), [Zeto](../../../characters/hikari/zeto.md), and [Benkei](../../../characters/hikari/benkei.md) stood watch as Hikari advanced to face Mugen.

Following Hikari's ascension as king, he ordered the Bell of Dawn taken down from its perch, permanently retiring the herald of bloodshed to symbolize Ku's commitment to lasting peace. The entrance courtyard was opened freely to common citizens. In the peacetime following the civil war, the district became a civic meeting ground; notably, the wife of a local blacksmith regularly visits the entrance to passionately voice petitions and humanistic proposals, which Hikari's new ministers collect directly from the people to shape imperial policy.
