# Sai

> *"Welcome to our boring backwater. You're one odd duck to want to come here."*
> — Townsperson at the city entrance

**Sai** is a desert town situated in the eye of the desert in [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md). It connects to [Sai: East District](sai_east_district.md), the [Southern Sai Sands](southern_sai_sands.md), and [Sandflow Pass](sandflow_pass.md). It is the hometown of the renowned dancer [Dolcinaea Luciel](../characters/dolcinaea_luciel.md) and houses the northern headquarters of the Warriors' Guild.

## History & Society

According to the [Mercantile Manuscript](../stories/masoud_script.md), Sai was originally a fortress bastion known as the Orochi Bastion, held by the ancient kingdom of [U](../world/u.md). Two decades prior, the fortress fell to the conquering forces of [Ku](../world/ku.md). Today, the crumbling stone remains of Fort Orochi have been repurposed as tenement housing for the poor in Sai's eastern district.

Four years prior, Sai and its population of western immigrants became entangled in a bitter war against [Timberain](timberain.md). Due to relentless skirmishes, sandstorms, and treacherous desert conditions, the town suffers a chronic shortage of medical aid, making medicine an exceptionally prized commodity and keeping the local hospital under heavy demand.

## Notable Inhabitants

- **[Masoud](../characters/masoud.md)**: A legendary master merchant and author of the celebrated *[Mercantile Manuscript](../stories/masoud_script.md)*, who resides in Sai alongside his daughter.
- **[Mao](../characters/mao.md)**: A dedicated apothecary stationed at the Sai hospital.
- **[Edmund](../characters/edmund.md)**: A commander stationed in Sai defending the western immigrant settlement against Timberain forces.
- **[Laila](../characters/laila.md)**: A troubled local girl taken under the wing of [Agnea Bristarni](../characters/agnea.md).

## Story

### Scent of Commerce (Partitio)
Upon arriving in Sai, [Partitio Yellowil](../characters/partitio.md) recognizes the merchant crest of [Masoud](../characters/masoud.md). Wishing to read the famed *[Mercantile Manuscript](../stories/masoud_script.md)*, Partitio approaches Masoud, who tests his understanding of commerce by challenging him to present a merchant's greatest treasure. With guidance from Masoud's daughter, Partitio answers that a merchant's true treasure is neither gold nor commodities, but oneself—one's accumulated experience and the trusted connections built over a lifetime. Convinced, Masoud entrusts him with the key to his archives.

### Castti's Route (Chapter 2)
[Castti Florenz](../characters/castti.md) arrives in Sai pursuing fragments of her past. She meets [Mao](../characters/mao.md) and commander [Edmund](../characters/edmund.md) amidst an escalating military confrontation between local immigrant fighters and a Timberain legion led by commander [Griff](../characters/griff.md). When both armies are trapped in treacherous sinkholes created by an enraged [Sand Lion](../world/sand_lion.md), Castti risks the battlefield to treat the wounded of both sides. After vanquishing the beast in the [Sand Lion's Den](sand_lions_den.md), Edmund and Griff negotiate a ceasefire at the town clinic, and Castti regains vital memories of her former leadership in [Eir's Apothecaries](../world/eirs_apothecaries.md).

### Agnea's Path (Chapter 4)
Sai is also tied intimately to the legacy of Agnea's mother, [Cuani Bristarni](../characters/cuani_bristarni.md), who previously stayed here and taught dance to local children—including an impoverished orphan girl who later rose to stardom as [Dolcinaea Luciel](../characters/dolcinaea_luciel.md). Dolcinaea, desperate to erase all traces of her humble background, buys up the property deeds to Sai and sends her personal bodyguard [Veronica](../characters/veronica.md) to evict the townspeople and raze the town. Agnea rallies the townspeople, teaches young [Laila](../characters/laila.md) how to dance in the town square, and confronts Veronica. Following Veronica's defeat, Dolcinaea departs, daring Agnea to challenge her on the grand stage of the Festival of Grace in [Merry Hills](merry_hills.md).
