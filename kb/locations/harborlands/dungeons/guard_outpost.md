# Guard Outpost

> *"As I thought, the guards are my best lead. It was [Stenvar](../../../characters/osvald/stenvar.md), their captain, who ordered me investigated. It is my understanding he had a clandestine meeting with [Harvey](../../../characters/osvald/harvey.md) before the incident. He ought to still be at the guards' main outpost. I shall seek him out."*  
> —*[Osvald](../../../characters/travelers/osvald.md)*

The **Guard Outpost** is a fortified garrison in *Octopath Traveler II*, situated within the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md) and connected directly to the coastal town of [Conning Creek](../towns/conning_creek.md). It serves as the local headquarters for the town guard under the command of [Captain Stenvar](../../../characters/osvald/stenvar.md).

## Story

Following his escape from the penal colony on [Frigit Isle](../../winterlands/towns/frigit_isle.md), [Osvald V. Vanstein](../../../characters/travelers/osvald.md) returns to Conning Creek to retrace the conspiracy that framed him for the murder of his wife and daughter. Deducing that Captain Stenvar had conspired with [Harvey](../../../characters/osvald/harvey.md) in exchange for bribes, Osvald infiltrates the Guard Outpost to confront the captain directly. 

After cutting through the garrison's enforcers, Osvald corners Stenvar in his private office, exposes his greed, and extracts information regarding Harvey's current whereabouts. Rejecting Stenvar's corrupt offers of money, Osvald incinerates the captain's hoarded bribe gold.

## Interior & Features

- **Garrison Rooms**: The outpost contains barracks and tactical planning rooms, including one room that displays a framed map of Orsterra upon the wall.
- **The Captain's Quarters**: The upper sanctum where Stenvar oversaw operations and stored his bribery riches. Following Osvald's visit, the chamber floor remains permanently marked with soot stains and blackened coins from the scholar's blaze.
