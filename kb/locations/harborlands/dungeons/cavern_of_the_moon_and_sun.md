# Cavern of the Moon and Sun

The **Cavern of the Moon and Sun** is an ancient subterranean grotto situated along the [Western Conning Creek Coast](../wilds/western_conning_creek_coast.md) in the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md).

## Overview & Architecture

The cavern is characterized by ancient stonework and archaic architecture strongly reminiscent of ruins found across Solistia, such as the [Infernal Castle](../../winterlands/dungeons/infernal_castle.md), the [Seat of the Water Sprite](../../crestlands/dungeons/seat_of_the_water_sprite.md), and the [Forbidden Shrine](../../winterlands/dungeons/forbidden_shrine.md). At the heart of the cavern stands a weathered, solitary headstone marking the grave of an unknown individual.

The cavern displays an unusual supernatural quality tied to time and light: differing pathways and chambers appear shrouded or revealed depending on whether it is day or night.

## Story Significance

During their journey, [Temenos Mistral](../../../characters/travelers/temenos.md) and [Throné Anguis](../../../characters/travelers/throne.md) venture into the depths of the Cavern of the Moon and Sun during their crossed path travels. Seeking the truth behind the enigmatic [Alpates](../../../characters/others/alpates.md), the cleric and the thief navigate the shifting illumination of the cave to uncover the clues she left behind.

## Inhabitants & Threats

The grotto's cold, mist-laden waters and stone halls are inhabited by perilous nocturnal and aquatic entities, including Ice Remnant Mk.IIs, towering Ice Revenants, Scythe Crabs, Sea Anemones, and hostile clans of Vagrant Froggen V, Vagrant Froggen VI, and their Vagrant Frogking I.
