# Lair of the Usurper

The **Lair of the Usurper** is a subterranean dungeon in the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md). It is located within the caverns directly beneath the ruined lighthouse at [Canalbrine Bridge](../wilds/canalbrine_bridge.md).
