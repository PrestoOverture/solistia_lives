# Sacred Guard Ship

> *"There, [Crick](../../../characters/temenos/crick_wellsley.md)! That's the [Sacred Guard](../../../world/factions/sacred_guard.md)'s ship! We must be swift!"*
> — [Temenos](../../../characters/temenos/temenos.md), pursuing [Vados](../../../characters/temenos/vados.md)

The **Sacred Guard Ship** is an official vessel of the [Sacred Guard](../../../world/factions/sacred_guard.md) docked at the harbor of [Canalbrine](../towns/canalbrine.md) in the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md).

## Story

During Chapter 2 of [Temenos](../../../characters/temenos/temenos.md)'s story, Temenos and the Sacred Guard knight [Crick](../../../characters/temenos/crick_wellsley.md) board the ship to corner [Vados the Architect](../../../characters/temenos/vados.md). Temenos seeks to interrogate Vados and hold him accountable for the murders of [Pontiff Jörg](../../../characters/temenos/jorg.md), [Lucian](../../../characters/temenos/lucian.md), and Canalbrine's local apothecary. 

The vessel remains moored at Canalbrine's harbor until Temenos finishes his subsequent investigations in Chapter 3, after which the ship departs the port.
