# Canalbrine: Water Source

**Canalbrine: Water Source** is a subterranean cavern and freshwater spring located south of [Canalbrine](../towns/canalbrine.md) in the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md).

## Overview

The cavern houses the pure natural spring from which the inhabitants of Canalbrine draw their municipal drinking water. Accessed via [Canalbrine: Path to the Water Source](../wilds/canalbrine_path_to_the_water_source.md), its pristine subterranean pools are vital to the life and health of the port city.

## Story Significance

During Chapter 1 of [Castti Florenz](../../../characters/castti/castti.md)'s journey, two foul, venomous beasts known as [Veron and Doron](../../../characters/castti/veron_and_doron.md) take up residence inside the cavern:

> "*...What is this foul smell? It is true what they say: filth is the root of all illness. Now then... What is it that's made all this mess? Foul vapors abound... I need to be careful not to inhale any effluvia.*"

The creatures' toxic secretions taint the aquifer, poisoning the populace downstream. Castti tracks the source of the outbreak into the caverns, defeats the twin monsters, and cleanses the spring using purebalm, restoring clean water to Canalbrine.
