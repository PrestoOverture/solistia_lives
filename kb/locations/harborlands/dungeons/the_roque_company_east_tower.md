# The Roque Company: East Tower

The **Roque Company: East Tower** is one of the main administrative and corporate wings of the [Roque Company](../../../world/factions/roque_company.md) headquarters, situated on [Roque Island](../towns/roque_island.md) in the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md).

> *"This is Mister Roque's base of operations, huh..."*
> — [Partitio Yellowil](../../../characters/travelers/partitio.md)

## Overview and Story

During Chapter 4 of Partitio's journey, Partitio arrives at Roque Island to negotiate the purchase of the steam engine patent from [Roque](../../../characters/partitio/roque_brilliante.md). To reach the executive quarters and factory complex, Partitio navigates the East Tower, contending with corporate guards, automated sentinels, and trained watch beasts.

## Interior and Details

The East Tower reflects both Roque's immense industrial wealth and his ego:
- **Portraits and Statues**: Three large portraits of Roque adorn the walls, and a prominent statue of the industrialist stands beside the tower exit.
- **Orsterran Map**: An office within the tower displays a large framed map depicting the distant continent of Orsterra.
- **Sentimental Keepsake**: In an upper room, an old hat identical to Partitio's sits quietly upon a cabinet—a lingering relic of Roque's past partnership with [Papp Yellowil](../../../characters/partitio/papp_yellowil.md).
