# Roque Island

> *"Everything on this island is the personal property of President Roque. Including the people who work here..."*
> — Tavern Assistant

**Roque Island** is an industrial island settlement located in the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md), entirely owned by the corporate magnate [Roque Brilliante](../../../characters/partitio/roque_brilliante.md) and the [Roque Company](../../../world/factions/roque_company.md). Because the island is strictly private property, no commercial ferries sail to its shores, making it accessible only by private vessel, such as [Terry](../../../characters/partitio/terry.md)'s ship. It connects directly to [Roque Island: Anchorage](../wilds/roque_island_anchorage.md) and [Roque Island: Headquarters](roque_island_headquarters.md).

A large bronze statue of President Roque stands in the center of the city. Due to the heavy industrial output and dense concentration of factories, the island suffers from poor air quality, and nightlife among the workers is virtually nonexistent.

## Society and Labor

The island functions under a rigid corporate regime where employees are identified by registration numbers, job descriptions, and salaries rather than individual biographies. All workers are required to live on-site, though company-mandated housing rent is steep. Furthermore, every commercial shop on the island operates as a subsidiary of the Roque Company, forming an insular closed economy deliberately structured to recoup every leaf paid out in wages.

The engineer who engineered the island's foundational infrastructure utilized groundbreaking architectural techniques decades ahead of their time. However, due to his extensive knowledge of proprietary company secrets, he is kept under constant, heavy surveillance.

## Industry and Operations

The sprawling factories on Roque Island mass-produce inexpensive, high-quality military hardware that attracts widespread demand from foreign buyers, several of whom resort to bribery to secure shipments. To supply its cutting-edge research, the factory covertly deals with underworld networks; criminal smugglers supply specialized materials needed for new development in exchange for factory-manufactured weapons, which are subsequently trafficked on the black market.

The island also harbors a heavily guarded, top-secret zone behind reinforced gates where President Roque constructed and concealed the [Steam Tank Obsidian](../../../world/beasts/steam_tank_obsidian.md). During [Partitio](../../../characters/travelers/partitio.md)'s confrontation with Roque, a hidden passage within the local tavern is uncovered, opening an alternate route directly into [Roque Island: Headquarters](roque_island_headquarters.md).
