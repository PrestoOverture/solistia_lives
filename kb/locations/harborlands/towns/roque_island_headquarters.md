# Roque Island: Headquarters

> *"No unauthorized personnel beyond this point."*
> — Guard

**Roque Island: Headquarters** is the corporate administrative and industrial core of [Roque Island](roque_island.md), situated within the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md). It connects south to the main town settlement and bridges access to [The Roque Company: West Tower](../dungeons/the_roque_company_west_tower.md) and [The Roque Company: East Tower](../dungeons/the_roque_company_east_tower.md).

A statue of [Roque](../../../characters/partitio/roque_brilliante.md) stands in front of the company facility to the east. The headquarters operates under intense security and continuous activity; according to local guards, the corporation's research and development division never sleeps.

## Story Events

During [Partitio](../../../characters/travelers/partitio.md)'s Chapter 4, Roque delivers an address announcing his industrial ambitions to a gathered assembly of reporters, audible across the bridge connecting the factory towers. Within the fortified compound behind the headquarters' gates, Roque harbors and deploys the [Steam Tank Obsidian](../../../world/beasts/steam_tank_obsidian.md) in an effort to crush Partitio and defend his monopoly over steam technology.
