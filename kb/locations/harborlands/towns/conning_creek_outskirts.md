# Conning Creek: Outskirts

**Conning Creek: Outskirts** is a district situated just east of the main town of [Conning Creek](conning_creek.md) in the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md).

## Overview

The outskirts connect westward directly back into [Conning Creek](conning_creek.md). The area is defined by the scorched, tragic ruins of [Osvald](../../../characters/travelers/osvald.md)'s former estate, which was torched five years prior by [Harvey](../../../characters/osvald/harvey.md) in a frame-up that blamed Osvald for the murder of his wife and daughter. A quiet forest lies near the grounds, where Osvald historically took solitary walks to think and develop his magical theories.

## Story

The area appears in a flashback at the start of Osvald's Chapter 2, depicting life before the arson. In Chapter 3, Osvald returns to the ruined estate to confront his past and search for clues regarding Harvey, crossing paths with [Lady Clarissa](../../../characters/osvald/clarissa.md).
