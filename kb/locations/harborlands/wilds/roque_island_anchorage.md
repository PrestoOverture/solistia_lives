# Roque Island: Anchorage

> *"This industrial island will bring change to the world."*
> — Guard

**Roque Island: Anchorage** is a field and harbor location situated within the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md). Located along the coast just outside the industrial settlement of [Roque Island](../towns/roque_island.md), it serves as the primary port facility and entry point for vessels arriving at the island.

Within the harbor's guardhouse, a map of Orsterra hangs prominently displayed on the wall.
