# Western Canalbrine Coast

**Western Canalbrine Coast** is a coastal route located in the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md).

## Geography

The Western Canalbrine Coast stretches along the shoreline just outside the port city of [Canalbrine](../towns/canalbrine.md), serving as the primary overland link between Canalbrine to the east and the [Canalbrine Bridge](canalbrine_bridge.md) to the west, which leads inward toward the rest of the continent. The area features sandy banks, rocky outcrops, and shallow coastal waters navigable by canoe.

## Notable Events

- **[The Traveler's Bag](../../../stories/side_stories/the_travelers_bag.md)**: If [Castti Florenz](../../../characters/travelers/castti.md) begins her journey from Canalbrine, she encounters an embattled traveler along this coast whose belongings have been stolen by opportunistic thieves, prompting an intervention to recover the bag.
