# Northern Conning Creek Coast

> "*'Walk atop the ocean waves and you shall find the altar of Dohter the Charitable.' Those are the words of a prophecy passed down in our guild. When the chosen apothecary reaches the altar, they will be granted a great power... Or so the prophecy says. I've never heard of anyone actually succeeding. Not to mention it's impossible to walk on water. I suppose you could probably walk along the shallows of Northern Conning Creek Coast, but would that suffice...?*"
> — Apothecaries Guild Master

**Northern Conning Creek Coast** is a coastal wilderness route located in the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md).

## Geography and Points of Interest

The coast borders the ocean and links several key paths and landmarks:
- **[Western Conning Creek Coast](western_conning_creek_coast.md)** to the south
- **[Altar of the Charitable](altar_of_the_charitable.md)** to the east, reached by navigating the coastal waters and shallows

### The Altar of Dohter
The coastline is central to an ancient prophecy cherished by the Apothecaries Guild. According to the guild's lore, an apothecary who walks across the water to reach the hidden [Altar of the Charitable](altar_of_the_charitable.md) is granted extraordinary power by the god [Dohter](../../../world/gods/dohter.md) the Charitable.

## Notable Inhabitants and Wildlife

- **The Waiting Woman**: Standing sentinel along the bluffs is an elderly woman, 124 years of age, who has spent over fifty years looking out across the sea, waiting faithfully for the return of her lost lover.
- **Fauna**: The coast and adjoining waters are inhabited by diverse maritime wildlife, including Ocean Lizardmen tribes, Kingfishers, Greater Kingfishers, Rock Tortoises, Mossy Tortoises, Azure Urchins, Scissor Crabs, Sea Scorpions, Sea Slugs, Portly Penguins, and predatory marine life such as Sapphire Bettas and Maneaters.
