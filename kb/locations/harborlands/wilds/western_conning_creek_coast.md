# Western Conning Creek Coast

**Western Conning Creek Coast** is a coastal field region situated in the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md), located immediately west of [Conning Creek](../towns/conning_creek.md).

## Geography and Connections

The coastline features a mix of rocky paths, beaches, and elevated bluffs connecting several surrounding regions:

- **East**: Leads directly into the coastal settlement of [Conning Creek](../towns/conning_creek.md).
- **North**: Connects to the [Northern Conning Creek Coast](northern_conning_creek_coast.md).
- **Northwest**: Leads to the entrance of the [Cavern of the Moon and Sun](../dungeons/cavern_of_the_moon_and_sun.md).
- **Southwest**: Crosses into the desert borders of the [Eastern Sai Sands](../../hinoeuma/wilds/eastern_sai_sands.md) within the Hinoeuma region.

## Points of Interest

- **The Vineyard**: The coast is home to a local grape farm and vineyard. In the side story [Goading the Grapes](../../../stories/side_stories/harborlands/goading_the_grapes.md), travelers assist the struggling grape farmer in improving his crop cultivation, resulting in a thriving harvest of grapes used for medicines and provisions.
