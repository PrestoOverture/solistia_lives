# Canalbrine: Path to the Water Source

**Canalbrine: Path to the Water Source** is a coastal and cavernous pathway situated just south of [Canalbrine](../towns/canalbrine.md), within the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md).

## Overview

The path connects the port town of Canalbrine with its vital freshwater supply at [Canalbrine: Water Source](../dungeons/canalbrine_water_source.md). The town relies entirely on the clear spring water flowing from this cavernous route:

> "*We draw our water from a spring that flows out of a cavern south of town.*"

## Story Significance

During [Castti](../../../characters/castti/castti.md)'s arrival in Canalbrine, an outbreak of sudden sickness grips the townspeople. Suspecting contamination of the municipal reservoir, Castti traverses this path to reach the cavern springs and uncover the source of the poison.
