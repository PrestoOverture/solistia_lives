# Altar of the Charitable

The **Altar of the Charitable** is a sacred shrine situated in the [Harborlands](../harborlands.md), accessible via the [Northern Conning Creek Coast](northern_conning_creek_coast.md).

## Overview

The shrine is dedicated to [Dohter](../../../world/gods/dohter.md) the Charitable, the divine patron of apothecaries. Hidden along the coastal cliffs of the Harborlands, it houses an ancient stone effigy of the deity. When an apothecary of pure intent, such as [Castti Florenz](../../../characters/castti/castti.md), communites with the statue, Dohter's divine voice speaks from beyond:

> "*Chosen apothecary...
> Unto thee, I grant new power.*"

According to the head of the Apothecaries Guild, a sacred prophecy handed down through generations of healers foretells the path to the shrine: *"Walk atop the ocean waves and you shall find the altar of Dohter the Charitable."*
