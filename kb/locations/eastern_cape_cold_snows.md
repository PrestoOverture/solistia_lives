# Eastern Cape Cold Snows

> "*Ah, it's so nice to sell my wares without looking over my shoulder!*"
> —Peddler, after the bandits' defeat

The **Eastern Cape Cold Snows** is an expanse of frigid, snow-covered trails in the [Winterlands](winterlands.md). Acting as an important regional junction, it links the remote coastal settlement of [Cape Cold](cape_cold.md) and the city of [Winterbloom](winterbloom.md) to the neighboring [Crestlands](crestlands.md).

## Geography & Connections

The snows encompass rolling drifts, frozen hillocks, and an icy river running eastward. 
- **Connections**:
  - West to [Southern Cape Cold Snows](southern_cape_cold_snows.md)
  - North to [Western Winterbloom Snows](western_winterbloom_snows.md)
  - South to [Eastern Flamechurch Pass](eastern_flamechurch_pass.md)
  - East into the secluded [Ruffians' Hideout](ruffians_hideout.md)

Native fauna inhabiting the snowfields includes snow marmots, snow foxes, wolves, frost moles, and lumbering frost bears, with fossilized fish swimming beneath the icy waterways.

## Story & Events

The highroad features in the side story [Ruffians' Redoubt](../stories/ruffians_redoubt.md). For a time, traveling peddlers and merchants along this trade passage were threatened and extorted by a gang of bandits holed up in the [Ruffians' Hideout](ruffians_hideout.md). The criminal encampment could be approached either by overpowering the sentry guarding the overland route or by rowing a small boat eastward down the river. Once travelers defeated the bandits and cleared the lair, the trade route was secured, allowing merchants to conduct business without fear.
