# Western Montwise Pass

**Western Montwise Pass** is a central mountain crossroads and pass located in the [Crestlands](crestlands.md).

## Geography and Connections

Western Montwise Pass serves as an important alpine junction linking key settlements and frontier passes across the Crestlands and beyond:

- **East**: Leads directly to the academic city of [Montwise](montwise.md).
- **West**: Descends toward the roaring cataracts of [Borderfall](borderfall.md), where the Clerics' Guild resides.
- **North**: Ascends into the [Northern Montwise Pass](northern_montwise_pass.md), leading travelers through the crags toward [Stormhail](stormhail.md) in the Winterlands. A stern checkpoint guard initially stations himself at this northern choke point to restrict passage.
- **South**: Traverses the southern ridges toward the [Western Merry Hills Pass](western_merry_hills_pass.md).
