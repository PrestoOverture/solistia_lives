# Toto'haha

**Toto'haha** (トト・ハハ島) is a lush, tropical island region located in southeastern Solistia. It is the largest island in an expansive archipelago dotted across the ocean, characterized by colorful fauna, ancient ruins, and dense jungle wilderness.

## Geography and Name

Toto'haha was first encountered by continental pioneers twenty years before the main story. Geographically, it is split into distinct sectors:
- **The Western Jungles**: Populated primarily by the indigenous [beastlings](../../world/species/beastling.md), a petite folk possessing animal features.
- **The Eastern Coast**: Populated primarily by humans who arrived with the continental expansion.
- **The Far North**: A secluded realm accessible only by watercraft across the rivers and seas.

### Origin of Name
In ancient beastling tradition, the northern island of the landmass was referred to as **Haha**, while the southern island was known as **Toto**. Following the inauguration of a communal festival honoring family bonds and racial prosperity, the names of the two halves were united into **Toto'haha**.

## Settlements

- **[Beasting Village](towns/beasting_village.md)**: A settlement deep in the jungle where beastlings and humans dwell together under the guidance and protection of the village warden, [Juvah](../../characters/ochette/juvah.md).
- **[Tropu'hopu](towns/tropuhopu.md)**: A sun-drenched coastal resort built over the waters of the eastern shore. Frequented by affluent vacationers from [New Delsta](../brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md), it is famous for its floating theater, high-grade coffee beans, and vital role on maritime trading routes between the eastern and western continents.
- **[Nameless Village](towns/nameless_village.md)**: A remote beastling village located in the far northern reaches, isolated from human expansion and guarding ancient traditions.

## Notable Landmarks and Dungeons

- **[Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](dungeons/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md)**: Ancient sacred ruins housing prehistoric murals of the [Creatures of Legend](../../world/lore/creatures_of_legend.md) and enshrining one of the realm's [Sacred Flames](../../world/lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md).
- **[Wandering Wood](dungeons/wandering_wood.md) & [Rifted Rock](dungeons/rifted_rock.md)**: A labyrinthine primeval forest and cavern near the Nameless Village where the ancient sorcerer [D'arqest](../../characters/others/darqest.md) first summoned the [Shadow](../../world/lore/shadow.md).
- **[Cavern of Waves](dungeons/cavern_of_waves.md)**: A flooded subterranean sea cave on an outlying isle.
- **[Sinking Ruins](dungeons/sinking_ruins.md)**: Submerged ancient ruins haunted by great primordial creatures including the [Behemoth](../../world/beasts/behemoth.md).
- **Coastal Bluffs**: A series of seaside precipices including the [Summit of Strife](dungeons/summit_of_strife.md), [Verdant Wood](dungeons/verdant_wood.md), [Stormy Cape](dungeons/stormy_cape.md), and [Moonview Cliff](dungeons/moonview_cliff.md).
- **[Altar of the Huntress](wilds/altar_of_the_huntress.md)**: A sacred outdoor altar dedicated to [Draefendi](../../world/gods/draefendi.md).

## Story Significance

- **[Ochette](../../characters/ochette/ochette.md)**: Toto'haha is Ochette's ancestral homeland. From Beasting Village, she departs on a journey across Solistia to locate the Creatures of Legend to protect her home from the prophesied [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../../world/lore/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md).
- **[Agnea Bristarni](../../characters/agnea/agnea.md)**: Performs at the floating theater in Tropu'hopu during Chapter 3 of her journey.
- **[Partitio Yellowil](../../characters/partitio/partitio.md)**: Purchases maritime transport and follows the Scent of Commerce in Tropu'hopu.
- **[Temenos Mistral](../../characters/temenos/temenos.md)**: Investigates the Nameless Village and breaches the Wandering Wood to discover the truth behind the Shadow and Kaldena's conspiratorial order.
