# Tropu'hopu

**Tropu'hopu** is a tropical coastal resort town situated in eastern [Toto'haha](../totohaha.md). Connected to the [Western Tropu'hopu Traverse](../wilds/western_tropuhopu_traverse.md) to the west, it also provides access to the [Tropu'hopu: Floating Theater](tropuhopu_floating_theater.md) to the north and the [Tropu'hopu: Shipyard](tropuhopu_shipyard.md) to the east.

## Overview and History

According to the [Mercantile Manuscript](../../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md), Tropu'hopu was originally established by the first continental pioneers to settle on Toto'haha. Due to its maritime proximity across the bay to [New Delsta](../../brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md), the settlement evolved into a favored vacation resort for wealthy mainlanders. Tropu'hopu also serves as a crucial stop along trade routes between Solistia's eastern and western continents, exporting various luxury commodities including rich coffee beans.

Both Tropu'hopu and the city of [Merry Hills](../../crestlands/towns/merry_hills.md) display distinctive white banners bearing emblems of the sun and moon, though each city uses a unique stylistic design. Local sailors are known to wear handmade shell accessories crafted by a resident islander as protective amulets at sea.

## Story Connections

Tropu'hopu is a prominent setting in the journeys of several travelers:
- [Agnea Bristarni](../../../characters/agnea/agnea.md) visits Tropu'hopu during Chapter 3 of her journey, seeking out the town's renowned floating theater.
- [Partitio Yellowil](../../../characters/partitio/partitio.md) travels here during his "Scent of Commerce" endeavors, visiting the town's shipyard in search of a vessel.

## Notable Inhabitants

Tropu'hopu is home to a diverse mixture of islanders and travelers from across Solistia:
- **The Cat Connoisseur**: A woman living on the west side of town who possesses an inexplicable bond with felines. Her earliest memories are of being surrounded by cats, and local rumors suggest she can comprehend their language. Her home shelters eight cats, while another lingers near the town provisioner.
- **The Weather-Wise Boy**: An eight-year-old boy with sea-colored eyes gifted with an extraordinary intuition for shifting weather and tides. His accurate forecasts regularly surpass those of veteran sailors, earning him great esteem throughout the port.
- **The Former Mercenary**: A young man residing on the western edge of town who formerly fought under the moniker "Fiend in Black Steel," having reportedly slain over a hundred soldiers in combat.
- **The Deep Diver**: A retired free diver living in town who holds the record for Solistia's deepest ocean dive.
- **The Shipbuilder's Uncle**: An artisan living on the east side whose niece works at the local shipyard, fiercely guarding her craft from colleagues (likely [Terry](../../../characters/partitio/terry.md)).
- **Foreign Settlers**: Visitors and migrants include an inquisitive scholar from [Montwise](../../crestlands/towns/montwise.md) studying Toto'haha's ancient ruins, as well as an elderly man hailing from the [Crestlands](../../crestlands/crestlands.md) who relocated seeking a tranquil change of pace.
- **The Rescued Islander**: A local resident who was once rescued from drowning by a friendly [Beastling](../../../world/species/beastling.md); the two can be seen drinking together peacefully on the beach under the stars.
