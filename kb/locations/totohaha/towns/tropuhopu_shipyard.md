# Tropu'hopu: Shipyard

The **Shipyard** is a town sub-area of [Tropu'hopu](tropuhopu.md), situated on the island of [Toto'haha](../totohaha.md).

## Overview

Located along the waterfront adjacent to the main settlement, the shipyard serves as a hub for maritime construction and vessel maintenance. It plays a pivotal role in [Partitio](../../../characters/travelers/partitio.md)'s journey: during his "The Scent of Commerce" questline in Tropu'hopu, Partitio travels here to purchase an ocean-faring ship for 100,000 leaves, granting the party the ability to sail Solistia's open seas.
