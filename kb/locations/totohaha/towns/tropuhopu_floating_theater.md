# Tropu'hopu: Floating Theater

The **Floating Theater** is a prominent sub-area within the island settlement of [Tropu'hopu](tropuhopu.md), located in the region of [Toto'haha](../totohaha.md). Constructed directly over the water, the open-air performance venue is considered a centerpiece of local culture and a point of immense pride for the town's mayor and citizenry.

## Overview

The floating stage hosts theatrical and artistic performances set against Toto'haha's coastal backdrop. Near the entrance to the theater, a local street vendor operates a popular food stall that has become an acclaimed destination for traveling gourmands, celebrated for its inventive use of island spices—a reputation recognized even by hunters and trappers from [Beasting Village](beasting_village.md).

The venue also serves as a hub of theatrical craft and architectural inspiration:
- **Costuming**: The local artisan who designs and crafts wardrobe for the stage performers draws heavy inspiration from the famed costume designer of [New Delsta's theater](../../brightlands/dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md) (who frequents [Merry Hills: Shrine Entrance](../../crestlands/towns/merry_hills_shrine_entrance.md)).
- **Architecture**: The floating playhouse attracts visiting craftspeople from across Solistia; at night, an architect from [Wellgrove](../../leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md) can be seen studying its unique over-water engineering in hopes of eventually constructing a grand playhouse of her own.
