# Beasting Village

**Beasting Village** is a jungle settlement on the island of [Toto'haha](../totohaha.md). It is the home of the hunter [Ochette](../../../characters/travelers/ochette.md) and the setting for Chapters 1 and 3 of her journey.

> *"At last, I've found it! This is the island with the beastling village. My ancestor's writings were not a work of fiction after all!"*  
> — Foreign Traveler

## Geography and Layout

The village is situated deep in the jungles of Toto'haha, connected to the [North Beasting Traverse](../wilds/north_beasting_traverse.md) to the north and the [Path to the Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../wilds/path_to_the_tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md) to the south. 

The settlement is divided into two distinct sections:
- **The Beastling Quarter**: Home to the native [beastling](../../../world/species/beastling.md) population. Every structure was crafted by a single master carpenter whose durable woodwork has withstood decades. The beastlings trade exclusively in jerky rather than monetary coins.
- **The Human Quarter**: A settlement of human migrants, trappers, and travelers situated alongside the beastling community, complete with a tavern and human-run shops.

## Society and Culture

Relations between the beastlings and humans have historically been marked by mistrust and division. A beastling sentinel wounded in an unprovoked human attack keeps vigil until dawn to prevent trespassers from infiltrating their grounds. The beastlings live by ancient hunting principles, including the adage: *"Kind hunters are hungry hunters."*

Despite lingering tensions, gradual reconciliation develops between the two communities:
- A young human girl, rescued from peril in the wilds by Ochette, develops a deep fondness for the beastlings and subsists almost entirely on jerky while trying to bridge the gap between their peoples.
- The music of the traveling dancer [Agnea](../../../characters/travelers/agnea.md) also helps bridge the divide, bringing the two communities together in shared harmony.
- A local human trapper supplies fresh catches to food merchants across the island in [Tropu'hopu](tropuhopu.md).
- Other residents include a former knight whose homeland fell to the military conquerors of [Ku](../../../world/nations/ku.md), and a foreign scholar who traveled across the seas after verifying ancestral records about the beastling realm.

## History and Events

During [Ochette](../../../characters/travelers/ochette.md)'s youth, she trained as a hunter in the village under the village elder [Juvah](../../../characters/ochette/juvah.md). After choosing her lifelong beast companion—either the malamaowl [Mahina](../../../characters/ochette/mahina.md) or the lajackal [Akalā](../../../characters/ochette/akala.md)—she rescued a human girl lost in the wilderness, an act that planted early seeds of goodwill between humans and beastlings.

Later, as the prophesied Night of the Scarlet Moon loomed over Solistia, the ancient evil of the [Dark Entity](../../../characters/ochette/dark_entity.md) assaulted the village and the sacred [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../dungeons/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md), forcing Ochette and her allies to defend the village and preserve the island's balance.
