# Sinking Ruins

The **Sinking Ruins** are submerged, ancient ruins located along the western side of the [Southern Nameless Village Traverse](../wilds/southern_nameless_village_traverse.md) on the island of [Toto'haha](../totohaha.md).

## Overview

The partially flooded halls and overgrown stone passages are inhabited by dangerous aquatic predators, ancient Froggen, and the fearsome legendary monster known as the [Behemoth](../../../world/beasts/behemoth.md).

## Landmarks and Relics

- **Ancient Gravestone**: Deep within the ruins stands an immense gravestone set beneath the roots of an ancient tree, whose occupant and origin remain lost to history.
- **Relics**: The site contains the Rusty Bow—an ancient armament that can be restored into a Divine Weapon by the Master Blacksmith—as well as the Sea God's Spear.
