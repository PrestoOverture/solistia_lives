# Verdant Wood

The **Verdant Wood** is a dense forest area and dungeon section located on the island of [Toto'haha](../totohaha.md).

## Geography

The forest forms part of the path leading to the heights of the island:
- To the west lies the [Stormy Cape](stormy_cape.md).
- To the east, the trail advances into the [Summit of Strife](summit_of_strife.md).

## History & Story

During [Ochette](../../../characters/ochette/ochette.md)'s quest to protect the island from the encroaching Night of the Calamity, the Verdant Wood became overrun by a horde of dark monsters. Enraged by the desecration of her home, the Wardenbeast [Tera](../../../world/beasts/tera.md) intervened:

> *"You... How dare you? How dare you disturb my island!? Feel the force of my rage! Hraaaaaa!!!"*

With a furious blow, Tera smashed open a vast chasm in the earth, obliterating the fiends and clearing the way forward for Ochette.
