# Summit of Strife

> *"As you can see, I have prepared as best I could. Here we are surrounded by sheer rock walls. There is no place to run. If we can lure the enemy in, the advantage will be ours. All that remains...is to wait."*
> — [Juvah](../../../characters/ochette/juvah.md)

The **Summit of Strife** is an elevated natural redoubt and defensive chokepoint situated on the island of [Toto'haha](../totohaha.md). Hemmed in by sheer rock cliffs on all sides, it connects the [Verdant Wood](verdant_wood.md) to the west with the [Path to the Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../wilds/path_to_the_tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md) to the east.

## The Defense of Toto'haha

Due to its restrictive topography that leaves no avenue for escape, Village Chief [Juvah](../../../characters/ochette/juvah.md) chose the summit as the fortified ground to stage the [Beastlings](../../../world/species/beastling.md)' defense against the calamity of the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../../../world/lore/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md). During [Ochette](../../../characters/travelers/ochette.md)'s Chapter 3, Juvah and the Beastling defenders hold the line here against encroaching hordes of dark monsters, buying time for Ochette to pursue the corruption to its source.
