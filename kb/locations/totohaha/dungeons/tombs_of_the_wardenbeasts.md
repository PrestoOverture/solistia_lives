# Tombs of the Wardenbeasts

The **Tombs of the Wardenbeasts** is an ancient sacred ruin located on the island of [Toto'haha](../totohaha.md), connected to the [Path to the Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../wilds/path_to_the_tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md).

> "*A young girl from our village wandered into the Tombs of the Wardenbeasts. Danger stalks those ruins. Humans who enter are never seen again. But this is not the case for you who were here on this island...before. Will you enter the ruins and find our lost girl?*"
> — Cohazeh

## Lore and Significance

Regarded with reverence and dread by the island's inhabitants, the ancient subterranean ruins contain the colossal skeletal remains of primordial Wardenbeasts. According to [Cohazeh](../../../characters/ochette/cohazeh.md), the human villagers of Toto'haha strictly avoid the tombs, believing that any mortal who trespasses within never returns.

Deep within the cavernous halls stands an ancient painted mural illustrating the [Creatures of Legend](../../../world/lore/creatures_of_legend.md)—the great beasts of land, sea, and sky—and foretelling the coming catastrophe known as the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../../../world/lore/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md). Furthermore, the sanctum enshrines an ancient hearth housing one of Solistia's four [Sacred Flames](../../../world/lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md).

## Story Events

- **[Ochette](../../../characters/travelers/ochette.md)'s Tale**: In Chapter 1, Ochette ventures into the depths of the tombs after a human girl wanders inside. In the deepest sanctum beside the sacred mural, she finds two orphaned beast cubs—the malamaowl [Mahina](../../../characters/ochette/mahina.md) and the lajackal [Akalā](../../../characters/ochette/akala.md)—and chooses one to become her lifelong companion and partner.
- **The Journey for the Dawn**: During the climax of [The Journey for the Dawn](../../../stories/main/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md), a shadow-spawned [Grotesque Monster](../../../world/beasts/grotesque_monster.md) guards the tomb's sacred brazier, requiring the travelers to vanquish it to rekindle the island's Sacred Flame against Vide's perpetual night.
