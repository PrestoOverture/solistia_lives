# Wandering Wood

The **Wandering Wood** is an enchanted, labyrinthine forest situated on northern [Toto'haha](../totohaha.md).

## Geography

Located north of the [Nameless Village](../towns/nameless_village.md), the deep forest conceals the approach to the secluded bluffs of the [Rifted Rock](rifted_rock.md).

The wood is naturally shrouded in an optical and spatial illusion known among the locals as an "island riddle." Travelers attempting to navigate without guidance find that pathways to the north, east, and west endlessly loop back into the same central clearing, barring uninvited outsiders from reaching the sacred interior of the island.

## History & Story

During his pursuit of the secrets behind the mysterious [Moonshade Order](../../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md) and the origin of the Kal tribe, [Temenos Mistral](../../../characters/travelers/temenos.md) arrived at the woods in his Chapter 4. Finding the forest impossible to penetrate alone, Temenos recruited the aid of the young beastling [Shirlutto](../../../characters/temenos/shirlutto.md):

> *"Doing good. Island riddle. You solving. Very good."*
> — [Shirlutto](../../../characters/temenos/shirlutto.md)

Guided by Shirlutto's knowledge of the island's riddles, the illusion dissipated, transforming the looping pathways into a clear passage that allowed Temenos to reach the Rifted Rock.
