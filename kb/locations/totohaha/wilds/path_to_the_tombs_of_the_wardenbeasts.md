# Path to the Tombs of the Wardenbeasts

The **Path to the Tombs of the Wardenbeasts** is a tropical wilderness route on the southern reaches of the island of [Toto'haha](../totohaha.md), connecting [Beasting Village](../towns/beasting_village.md) to the sacred [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../dungeons/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md).

## Geography and Environment

Winding south from the beastling settlement through tidal lagoons and jungle brush, the route features both land pathways and shallow waterways. Local wildlife includes island froggens, lloris, motley slugs, and blue jellyfish adrift in the coastal channels.

## Story

The path serves as the initial hunting ground and training ground for the young hunter [Ochette](../../../characters/travelers/ochette.md) in Chapter 1:
- Instructed by village elder [Juvah](../../../characters/ochette/juvah.md) to hunt beasts threatening the local ecosystem, Ochette tracks down and defeats an overgrown [King Iguana](../../../world/beasts/king_iguana.md) along this road, butchering it for meat to feed the village.
- Soon after, ominous disturbances stir across the island. The local islebirds cry out in terror, warning of an approaching catastrophe: *"Flee this place. Get far away. Calamity...is coming."*
- Investigating the cries, Ochette encounters a shadowy [Dark Entity](../../../characters/ochette/dark_entity.md) looming near the sacred tombs, marking the earliest portent of the Night of the Scarlet Moon.
