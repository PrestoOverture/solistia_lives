# Altar of the Huntress

The **Altar of the Huntress** is an ancient shrine situated on the island of [Toto'haha](../totohaha.md), accessible via the [Western Tropu'hopu Traverse](western_tropuhopu_traverse.md).

## Overview

The shrine is dedicated to [Draefendi](../../../world/gods/draefendi.md) the Huntress, the patron deity of hunters. Tucked away along the secluded wilderness of the western traverse near Tropu'hopu, it houses a stone effigy of the goddess. When a worthy hunter, such as [Ochette](../../../characters/travelers/ochette.md), approaches and communes with the statue, Draefendi's divine presence speaks to them:

> "*Chosen hunter...
> Unto thee, I grant new power.*"
