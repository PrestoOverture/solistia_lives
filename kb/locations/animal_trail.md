# Animal Trail

The **Animal Trail** is a secluded wooded path and dungeon located in the [Leaflands](leaflands.md), connected to the [Forest Path](forest_path.md).

> *"Something's...not right. No animals. No bugs chirping, no birds singing. It's like the forest...disappeared."*  
> — [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md)

## Geography and Ecology

The trail winds through dense woods and underbrush characteristic of the Leaflands. In its central clearing grow bushes bearing shishkaba fruit—a plant variety also native to the southern isle of [Toto'haha](totohaha.md).

## History and Events

The trail serves as the setting for the joint journey undertaken by the apothecary [Castti](../characters/castti.md) and the hunter [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md).

During their first venture down the trail, Castti and Ochette investigate disturbances among the local wildlife and confront an agitated [Dire Duorduor](../characters/dire_duorduor.md). Later, an unnatural darkness overtakes the forest during the onset of the Night. The woods fall eerily silent as birds, beasts, and insects vanish entirely, replaced by malevolent entities known as the [Wriggling Shadow](../world/wriggling_shadow.md) and the [Creeping Shadow](../world/creeping_shadow.md).
