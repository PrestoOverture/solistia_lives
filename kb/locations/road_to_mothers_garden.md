# Road to Mother's Garden

The **Road to Mother's Garden** is a field location situated within the [Leaflands](leaflands.md). It connects the town of [Wellgrove](wellgrove.md) to the northeast with [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](mothers_garden_orphanage.md) to the northwest.
