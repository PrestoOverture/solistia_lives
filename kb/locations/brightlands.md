# Brightlands

The **Brightlands** is an urbanized region located on the eastern continent of Solistia, renowned for hosting some of the continent's largest metropolises and centers of culture and commerce.

## Overview

Blessed by warm sunshine and a temperate climate, the region owes its name to the bright, fertile landscape. Early agricultural abundance sparked rapid growth, drawing immigrants, commerce, and wealth. Development followed swiftly, giving rise to grand theaters, bustling game parlors, and thriving industrial sectors. In modern Solistia, the glow of city gaslights and civilization shines as brilliantly as the sun overhead.

## Geography and Settlements

### Major Cities and Towns

- **[New Delsta](new_delsta.md)**: Solistia's cultural beating heart and largest city. New Delsta is a glittering metropolis famous for its grand theater, lively entertainment districts, and high society, shadowed beneath by the criminal underworld of the [Blacksnakes](../world/blacksnakes.md) guild.
- **[Clockbank](clockbank.md)**: An industrial hub in the eastern reaches that has flourished since the Age of Discovery. Dominated by its landmark clocktower, the city is a forge of technological innovation and steam power, where much of the populace is employed by the [Roque Company](../world/roque_company.md).
- **[Abandoned Village](abandoned_village.md)**: A ruined, empty settlement nestled in the hills, once serving as a base of operations for [Eir's Apothecaries](../world/eirs_apothecaries.md).
- **[Lostseed](lostseed.md)**: A decaying, isolated settlement hidden behind foggy cliffs and reachable only by gondola, inhabited by broken souls known as "vessels."

## History

In centuries past, the northern expanse of the Brightlands was ruled by the Nyx royal dynasty. The realm collapsed after a bitter dynastic struggle in which a younger prince assassinated his elder brother, bringing ruin upon the kingdom. The surviving prince fled into obscurity, and his impoverished descendants still live unaware of their true royal legacy in Clockbank.
