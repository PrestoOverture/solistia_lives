# Cavern of the Sea God

> *"In years past, Cateracta was the guardian deity of this sea. Even if it has been so long that nobody in town remembers it. I want to show you something. It's deep inside these caverns. I'll wait for you there."*
> — Alpione

The **Cavern of the Sea God** is a coastal grotto situated off [Conning Creek: Harbor](conning_creek_harbor.md) in the [Harborlands](harborlands.md).

## Overview & Story

The cavern served as the final sanctuary of the legendary beast [Cateracta](../world/cateracta.md), the Sea God who protected the Harborlands and Solistia four centuries ago. After sacrificing her strength to halt a devastating tidal wave and subsequently falling to the Dark Hunter [Petrichor](../characters/petrichor.md), Cateracta left behind a single egg within the secluded recesses of the cave.

The hunter [Alpione](../characters/alpione.md), the lone survivor of the band that defended Cateracta, maintained a lonely vigil over the egg for years. During [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md)'s quest to enlist the Creatures of Legend, Alpione guides the young hunter deep into the cavern. There, the egg hatches into the baby sea dragon [Acta](../characters/acta.md), who inherits its mother's duty to protect [Toto'haha](totohaha.md) and joins Ochette's travels.

## Inhabitants & Wildlife

The tidal pools and rock formations of the grotto are home to diverse marine creatures, including Azure Urchins, Rock Tortoises, Kingfishers, venomous Sea Scorpions, Sea Snakes, and Shadow Bats.
