# North Beasting Traverse

**North Beasting Traverse** is an ancient coastal trail situated on the island of [Toto'haha](totohaha.md).

## Geography and Connections

The trail forms the primary overland route connecting the northern coast to the beastling settlements:
- **[Beasting Bay: Anchorage](beasting_bay_anchorage.md)** to the north, providing sea passage to other regions of Solistia
- **[Beasting Village](beasting_village.md)** to the south, home of the beastlings and the starting point of [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md)'s journey

## Wildlife and Inhabitants

The dense, tropical foliage along the traverse is populated by indigenous island fauna, including Island Froggens, various breeds of Lloris and Long-Eared Lloris, Motley Slugs, Evil Jellyfish near the tideline, Fierce Flies, and large Salamanders.

## Story Events

- **The Traveler's Bag**: If Ochette is chosen as the starting protagonist, the side story *The Traveler's Bag* takes place along this path shortly after she sets out from Beasting Village, where she encounters an itinerant traveler whose satchel has been stolen by bandits.
