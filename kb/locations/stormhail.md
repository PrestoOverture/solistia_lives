# Stormhail

**Stormhail** is a heavily fortified mountain city located in the far northern reaches of the [Winterlands](winterlands.md). It is characterized by severe cold, towering stone fortifications, and persistent factional tension.

> "*Mmhm, this cold feels like Stormhail. You'd best get inside as soon as you can.*"
> — Townsperson at the city entrance

## Geography

Stormhail is situated high in the northern peaks of the Winterlands. The city connects south to the [Southern Stormhail Snows](southern_stormhail_snows.md), northwest across the [Stormhail: Bridge](stormhail_bridge.md) toward Castle Mei, and north to the [Stormhail: Sacred Guard Headquarters](stormhail_sacred_guard_headquarters.md). To the south lies the perilous Pit of [D'arqest](../characters/darqest.md), an ancient site that has claimed the lives of numerous exploring scholars.

The city is buffeted by a ceaseless blizzard blowing down from [Sacred Peak Altahe](sacred_peak_altahe.md), where the divine beast [Glacis](../world/glacis.md) resides. Long ago, Glacis saved Stormhail from a monstrous blizzard that threatened to bury the settlement; afterward, the mountain path was walled off to protect the guardian beast.

## History and Factional Struggles

Stormhail has a long history of military strife:
- **War of the Great Wall**: The ancient conflict between the kingdoms of [Fj'all](../world/fjall.md) and [Delstasch](../world/delstasch.md) began as a skirmish along the walls of Stormhail.
- **Rival Powers**: In modern times, the city is caught between two competing authorities:
  - **The [Sacred Guard](../world/sacred_guard.md)**: Established first in the city, the Sacred Guard maintains control through harsh measures—interrogating, imprisoning, torturing, and executing townspeople accused of heresy.
  - **Clan Mei**: Led by [Rai Mei](../characters/rai_mei.md), Clan Mei governs from Castle Mei across the bridge. To minimize friction and avoid open skirmishes with the Sacred Guard, Clan Mei's soldiers make their patrols through the city only at night.

## Story Events

Stormhail serves as the backdrop for multiple travelers' journeys:
- **[Ochette](../characters/ochette.md)**: Journeys here during her search for Glacis, scaling Sacred Peak Altahe to soothe the raging guardian, which finally halts the perpetual blizzards assailing the town.
- **[Temenos Mistral](../characters/temenos.md)**: Investigates the Sacred Guard Headquarters to uncover the corruption within the order and confront Captain Kaldena's designs.
- **[Hikari Ku](../characters/hikari.md)**: Comes to Stormhail seeking an alliance with Clan Mei and Rai Mei against Mugen's regime.

## Notable People
