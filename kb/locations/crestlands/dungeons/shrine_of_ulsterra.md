# Shrine of Ul'sterra

The **Shrine of Ul'sterra** is a sacred temple located just outside [Merry Hills](../towns/merry_hills_shrine_entrance.md) in the [Crestlands](../crestlands.md), connecting directly to the [Stage of the Moon and Sun](stage_of_the_moon_and_sun.md).

> *"Ah, the famous Shrine of Ul'sterra! How I've longed to lay eyes on it! I'm so moved I could weep!"*
> — Traveler in Merry Hills

## Overview and Lore

The shrine contains a sanctum dedicated to [Sealticge](../../../world/gods/sealticge.md), the Lady of Grace. Under normal circumstances, the holy site remains strictly off-limits to the general public. However, once a year during the [Grand Gala](../../../world/lore/grand_gala.md), its gates are opened to host the prestigious Festival of Grace.

Architecturally, the interior of the shrine features distinctive four-pronged floor tile motifs, patterns that also appear in ancient structures in [New Delsta](../../brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md) and [Lostseed Castle](../../brightlands/dungeons/lostseed_castle.md).

## Story Events

During Chapter 5 of [Agnea Bristarni](../../../characters/agnea/agnea.md)'s journey, Agnea arrives in Merry Hills to participate in the Grand Gala. The manager [La'mani](../../../characters/agnea/lamani.md) deploys hired guards and thugs throughout the corridors of the shrine to control the competition and safeguard his interests. Agnea and her companions navigate through the shrine to reach the main stage for her final dance competition against [Dolcinaea](../../../characters/agnea/dolcinaea_luciel.md).
