# Underground Laboratory

The **Underground Laboratory** is a hidden subterranean research facility located beneath the [Montwise: Library](../towns/montwise_library.md) in the [Crestlands](../crestlands.md). Accessible only via a concealed passageway, it served as the covert workshop where [Harvey](../../../characters/osvald/harvey.md) conducted forbidden alchemical research in his obsessive quest to attain the [One True Magic](../../../world/lore/one_true_magic.md).

> *"So this is Harvey's laboratory. Are these...chimeras...? He's combining creatures to create new varieties. This kind of study is forbidden. He mixes the blood of monsters in attempts to create even stronger magical abominations. He presumes this will lead him to the One True Magic, and so he worked on it in secret. That said...I've never seen chimeras like these before. Did he employ my formulae to arrive at these combinations?"*
> — [Osvald](../../../characters/travelers/osvald.md)

## Atmosphere & Layout

The laboratory is a gruesome site dedicated to unnatural biological synthesis. Within its damp stone chambers, Harvey mixed the blood of disparate beasts to manufacture powerful hybrid abominations, utilizing magical formulae stolen from Osvald's own research:

- **Cultivation Vats**: Large containment tubes line the halls of the laboratory. During Osvald's confrontation in Chapter 4, three vats ruptured, releasing the [Cobbled Creature](../../../world/beasts/cobbled_creature.md), [Deformed Creature](../../../world/beasts/deformed_creature.md), and [Synthesized Creature](../../../world/beasts/synthesized_creature.md) to defend the lab.
- **The Sealed Cylinder**: After the chimeras are defeated, a single preservation vat remains unbroken, holding a shadowy, human-like silhouette. Because Harvey harvested the blood of Osvald's wife, [Rita Lumina](../../../characters/osvald/rita_lumina.md), to forge the [Grieving Golem](../../../characters/osvald/grieving_golem.md), this sinister figure represents the chilling extent of Harvey's human experimentation.
- **Inner Sanctorum**: The deepest recesses of the laboratory are littered with human skulls, dark bloodstains, and the colossal skeletal bones of an enormous beast, bearing testament to the deadly toll of Harvey's pursuit of dark magic.
