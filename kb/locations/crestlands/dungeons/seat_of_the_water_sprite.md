# Seat of the Water Sprite

The **Seat of the Water Sprite** is an ancient dungeon located at the base of the [Borderfall](../wilds/borderfall.md) waterfall in the [Crestlands](../crestlands.md).

## Lore and Architecture

The ruins share a distinct architectural style with the [Forbidden Shrine](../../winterlands/dungeons/forbidden_shrine.md) and the [Infernal Castle](../../winterlands/dungeons/infernal_castle.md), suggesting that all three structures may have been constructed by the same ancient builders.

Deep within the ruins rests the Rusty Staff, an ancient armament that can be reforged into one of the legendary Divine Weapons by the Master Blacksmith.
