# Forsaken Graveyard

The **Forsaken Graveyard** is an eerie, abandoned cemetery situated in the craggy hills of the [Crestlands](../crestlands.md), south of the scholarly city of [Montwise](../towns/montwise.md).

> *"Have you heard tell of a certain notorious double-edged sword? It is a tremendously powerful weapon, but its might comes at a cost. Some even consider it cursed. It is called the Forbidden Blade. It changed hands countless times before finally being laid to rest in a cemetery in the [Crestlands](../crestlands.md). Will you find the blade and learn to wield it? Or will you leave the cursed blade where it lies? The choice is yours, my friend. This, too, is part of your training."*  
> — Warriors Guild Master

## Overview

Shunned by travelers and locals alike, this somber graveyard serves as the secluded resting place for dark relics. Chief among them is the Forbidden Blade, a formidable double-edged weapon whose immense power extracts a steep toll. Having passed through the blood-stained hands of countless warriors across the eras, the cursed sword was ultimately interred within these desolate grounds.

The graveyard is haunted by animated remains such as Puppet Bones, scavenging packs of Vagrant Ratkin V and Vagrant Ratking I, and various prowling beasts. Deep within the cemetery lurks the monstrous Devourer of Dreams, who stands vigil over the cursed burial site.
