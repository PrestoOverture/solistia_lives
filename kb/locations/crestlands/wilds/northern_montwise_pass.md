# Northern Montwise Pass

**Northern Montwise Pass** is a rugged mountain trail in the [Crestlands](../crestlands.md).

## Geography and Access

The pass serves as a high mountain artery linking the Crestlands with the Winterlands:
- **[Western Montwise Pass](western_montwise_pass.md)** to the south
- **[Southern Stormhail Snows](../../winterlands/wilds/southern_stormhail_snows.md)** to the north

Travelers heading north must get past a guard stationed at the northern boundary of Western Montwise Pass to access this high mountain route.

## History and Environment

Given the presence of residual gold dust and silver ore deposits in the area, Northern Montwise Pass is believed to have originally functioned as an active mining area before being abandoned. The rugged terrain is characterized by an absence of ambient music, echoing only with the calls of native beasts such as Mountain Apes, Mountain Ape-kings, Dark Spiny Shrews, Horned Flies, Mountain Goats, Wild Weasels, and Winged Serpents.
