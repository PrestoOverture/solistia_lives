# Western Merry Hills Pass

**Western Merry Hills Pass** is a high-altitude mountain path located in the [Crestlands](../crestlands.md).

## Geography and Connections

Traversing rugged mountainous terrain, the pass serves as the western approach to the vibrant festival town of Merry Hills:

- **East**: Leads directly into the gates of [Merry Hills](../towns/merry_hills.md).
- **North**: Connects into the rugged trails of [Western Montwise Pass](western_montwise_pass.md).

## Notable Inhabitants

- **Foreign Assassins**: Under the cover of night, a group of foreign assassins lurks within the northeastern cliffs of the pass. Combatants and merchants who prove their mettle against these blade-wielding mercenaries can negotiate contracts to hire their lethal services.
