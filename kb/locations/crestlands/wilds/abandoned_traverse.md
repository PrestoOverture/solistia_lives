# Abandoned Traverse

The **Abandoned Traverse** is a mountain trail and field location situated in the [Crestlands](../crestlands.md), connecting the city of [Montwise](../towns/montwise.md) to the [Abandoned Church](../dungeons/abandoned_church.md).

## Overview

The secluded mountain traverse leads toward the ruins of the Abandoned Church. During her pursuit of [Father](../../../characters/throne/father.md), [Throné Anguis](../../../characters/throne/throne.md) tracks him across the pass, challenging him before their final confrontation with the words: "*Is this where you want to breathe your last?*"

Outside the entrance to the Abandoned Church along the traverse sits an unmarked grave, believed to be the final resting place of [Marietta](../../../characters/throne/marietta.md). The church held deep significance for Father, as it was where he and Marietta had been wed years before. Following her fatal duel with Father, Throné is seen lingering before this quiet gravesite.
