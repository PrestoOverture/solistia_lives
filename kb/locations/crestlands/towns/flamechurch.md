# Flamechurch

**Flamechurch** is a tranquil town nestled in the [Crestlands](../crestlands.md), situated directly south of the holy [Flamechurch: Cathedral](flamechurch_cathedral.md). Connected to the cathedral via the [Flamechurch Pilgrims' Way](../wilds/flamechurch_pilgrims_way.md) and bordered to the east by the [Eastern Flamechurch Pass](../wilds/eastern_flamechurch_pass.md), it serves as both the starting point of [Temenos](../../../characters/temenos/temenos.md)'s journey and an essential waystation for pilgrims traveling to revere the Sacred Flame.

## Overview & Culture

Though modest in population, the town thrives primarily on religious pilgrimage. According to the [Mercantile Manuscript](../../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md), the townsfolk make a comfortable living catering to the constant influx of devout travelers journeying toward the cathedral. The town features a tavern, an inn, a general store, and its own local church.

Life in Flamechurch revolves around the faith:
- **Sacred Symbolism**: The torches illuminating the streets and pathways are fashioned after the ceremonial braziers that house the Sacred Flame.
- **Strict Ordinances**: Secular indulgences are strictly regulated; by church decree, the only alcoholic beverage merchants are permitted to supply or sell within Flamechurch is a consecrated wine reserved for ceremonial rites, the vending rights to which command a steep price.
- **The Local Church**: Inside the town church hangs an official portrait of [Pontiff Jörg](../../../characters/temenos/jorg.md). The local piano-playing cleric frequently holds musical recitals, which reliably draw large, appreciative crowds.
- **Town Life**: Pastoral and quiet, Flamechurch is home to sheep, chickens, and dragonflies flitting about during the day. Among its devout congregation, Sister [Mindt](../../../characters/temenos/arcanette.md) is held in high esteem—inspiring a young cleric to emulate her serene demeanor, earning the admiration of a local girl who aspires to take the cloth, and secretly capturing the heart of the courier who runs messages between the town and the Order.

## Story Events

- **Temenos's Induction**: If Temenos is encountered as a prospective companion rather than chosen as the journey's first traveler, he is found in Flamechurch confronted by a hostile Insurgent, requiring travelers to intervene and dispatch the agitator before Temenos joins the party.
- **The Journey for the Dawn**: During the perpetual darkness cast during [The Journey for the Dawn](../../../stories/main/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md), Flamechurch is gripped by terror. Townsfolk barricade themselves inside their homes, altering their dialogue until the high priestess [Arcanette](../../../characters/temenos/arcanette.md) is confronted and overthrown.
- **Pilgrim Safety**: In the side story [Pilgrim Protection](../../../stories/side_stories/crestlands/pilgrim_protection.md), travelers aid in securing safe transit along the trail leading from the town to the cathedral.
