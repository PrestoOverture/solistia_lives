# Merry Hills: Shrine Entrance

**Merry Hills: Shrine Entrance** is a town sub-area located immediately north of [Merry Hills](merry_hills.md) in the [Crestlands](../crestlands.md), connecting the main town below to the sacred [Shrine of Ul'sterra](../dungeons/shrine_of_ulsterra.md) above.

## Gathering of Performers

At night, the courtyard before the shrine fills with dozens of entertainers and artists. The lively assembly includes singing sisters, an author, a composer, a perpetually laughing jester, a living statue performer, a pyrotechnician, and a comedic duo staging a routine titled "Beauty and the [Beastling](../../../world/species/beastling.md)."

## Notable Figures

- **The Composer**: A 31-year-old musician who wrote "All for You," the celebrated opera melody performed by [Dolcinaea Luciel](../../../characters/agnea/dolcinaea_luciel.md). He composed the song on the day his daughter was born, writing its lyrics to express a parent's wish to devote their life to their child.
- **The Fireworks Performer**: A pyrotechnician hailing from [Hinoeuma](../../hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md) who crafts fireworks using crushed soulstones formed into spheres, producing brilliant aerial bursts while closely guarding the formula.
- **The Costume Designer**: An innovative designer responsible for stage costumes seen in productions at the [New Delsta: Theater](../../brightlands/dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md), widely watched by the fashion world for her forward-thinking aesthetics.
- **The Troupe Impresario**: A manager found near the entrance during the daytime. Though in charge of an ensemble of eight dancers, the dancers deemed his interference harmful to their synchronized routines and voted to bar him from rehearsals.
