# Merry Hills

> *"Merry Hills is a town dedicated to the arts! Artists and entertainers from the world over settled here."*  
> — Tavern Assistant

**Merry Hills** is a bustling city in the [Crestlands](../crestlands.md), connected to the [Western Merry Hills Pass](../wilds/western_merry_hills_pass.md) to the west and the [Merry Hills: Shrine Entrance](merry_hills_shrine_entrance.md) to the north. Renowned as the cultural haven and home of the arts, it is populated by artists, musicians, dancers, actors, and performers from across the world. The final chapter of [Agnea Bristarni](../../../characters/agnea/agnea.md)'s journey takes place here.

## Culture and Festivities

Merry Hills hosts the annual [Grand Gala](../../../world/lore/grand_gala.md), an expansive event where entertainers flock from all lands to perform, culminating in the Festival of Grace. During celebrations, the city displays white banners bearing the emblems of the sun and moon.

Despite its celebratory spirit, the freethinking artistic enclave of Merry Hills is closely monitored by the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../../../world/factions/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md), which views the uninhibited creative community as a potential breeding ground for heresy.

## Notable Inhabitants and Street Life

- **The Local Baker**: A town baker whose small breads are a favorite snack for visitors. He also prepares acclaimed sandwiches and sweet breads, selling them on the streets and filling special orders.
- **The Elderly Actor**: A veteran performer who conducts an emotive one-man show in the corner of the local tavern at night.
- **The Anonymous Poet**: The creator of the literary work *The Arrival of Antiquity*, who spends his days standing quietly and anonymously beneath the city bridge.
- **The Knife Peddler**: A merchant offering special-order knives whose storytelling draws devoted admirers, once allowing him to sell three hundred knives in a single day.
