# Montwise

> *"My goodness, just look at all these tomes! This place is a veritable mountain of academic treasure."*
> — Scholar at the local book market

**Montwise** is an academic mountain town located in the eastern [Crestlands](../crestlands.md). Recognized across Solistia as a preeminent seat of scholarship, the town is home to an expansive library, a renowned book trade, and a clandestine underground arena hidden beneath its studious exterior.

Montwise serves as the setting for [Hikari](../../../characters/travelers/hikari.md)'s Chapter 2, [Throné](../../../characters/travelers/throne.md)'s Chapter 3 ([Father](../../../characters/throne/father.md)'s route), [Osvald](../../../characters/travelers/osvald.md)'s Chapter 4, and the final episode of Osvald and [Partitio](../../../characters/travelers/partitio.md)'s Crossed Path. It is also the location of the side quests *[Tourney Champion](../../../stories/side_stories/crestlands/tourney_champion.md)* and *[Will Research for Money](../../../stories/side_stories/crestlands/will_research_for_money.md)*.

- [Montwise: Underground Arena](../dungeons/montwise_underground_arena.md) via a subterranean entrance
- [Western Montwise Pass](../wilds/western_montwise_pass.md) to the west

## Culture & Notable Lore

- **The Old Book Market**: Montwise boasts what is widely considered the finest old book market across both the eastern and western continents. The market attracts scholars and collectors alike, and is strictly regulated to ensure only the highest quality tomes are bought and sold, although it is also known to harbor multiple heretical texts.
- **Library Architecture**: The walls of the town's renowned library are adorned with carved reliefs of griffins and regal kings' heads, evoking a stately and solemn atmosphere.
- **The Underground Arena**: Beneath the city operates an illegal subterranean colosseum where gladiators battle for wealthy gamblers. Competitors and veterans frequent the local tavern, including a gladiator known as "The Brazen Brute" (standing 4 cubits tall and weighing 13 stone, boasting a record of 48 wins and 6 losses—reputed as a fighter luckier than he is strong, and a testament to fortune favoring the bold).
- **Academic Exploitation**: The pursuit of knowledge in Montwise also attracts predation; the struggling astronomer [Regulus](../../../characters/others/regulus.md) fell into financial distress after being swindled by unscrupulous local moneylenders while seeking funding for his research.
