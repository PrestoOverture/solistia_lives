# Flamechurch: Cathedral Entrance

**Flamechurch: Cathedral Entrance** is the ceremonial courtyard and sub-area linking the [Flamechurch Pilgrims' Way](../wilds/flamechurch_pilgrims_way.md) to the holy [Flamechurch: Cathedral](flamechurch_cathedral.md) within the [Crestlands](../crestlands.md). It provides access toward the [Cathedral Cellars](../dungeons/cathedral_cellars.md) and houses the foremost of the four [Sacred Flames](../../../world/lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) burning across Solistia.

## The Sacred Flame & Environs

The Sacred Flame burns brightly at the center of the terrace, framed by symbolic stonework:
- **The Eight Pedestals**: Arranged in a circle surrounding the flame stand eight stone plinths. Only two currently support statues—those honoring [Aelfric](../../../world/gods/aelfric.md), the Flamebringer, and [Aeber](../../../world/gods/aeber.md), the Prince of Thieves. The remaining six pedestals remain conspicuously vacant, their intended likenesses omitted for reasons lost to church history.
- **Nighttime Vigilance**: While daytime pilgrims move freely through the courtyard, nocturnal visits are strictly monitored. Sentries of the [Sanctum Knights](../../../world/factions/sacred_guard.md) enforce security inspections, searching all belongings of visitors entering after dark.
- **Courtyard Life**: The plaza serves as a common congregating point. A genial elderly local frequents the flame during daylight, routinely offering candies to weary travelers. In contrast, nighttime brings shadowy figures, including a remorseful youth bound by the inescapable collar of the [Blacksnakes](../../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md) guild, seeking divine redemption in vain.

## Story Events

- **The Vigil of Ort**: In the wake of Kaldena's fall and following the conclusion of Temenos's investigation, the former Sanctum Knight [Ort](../../../characters/temenos/ort.md) contends with his shattered convictions in this plaza during the side story [Ort's Next Chapter](../../../stories/next_chapters/orts_next_chapter.md).
- **The Long Night**: During [The Journey for the Dawn](../../../stories/main/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md), when Solistia is blanketed in unnatural darkness, the clergy and visitors in the courtyard respond with mounting terror, voicing prayers of salvation until dawn is restored. [Throné](../../../characters/throne/throne.md) notes here that the star dancer [Dolcinaea](../../../characters/agnea/dolcinaea_luciel.md) had scheduled a public performance on the grounds.
