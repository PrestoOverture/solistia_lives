# Altar of the Prince of Thieves

The **Altar of the Prince of Thieves** is a hidden shrine in the [Brightlands](brightlands.md) dedicated to [Aeber](../world/aeber.md), the Prince of Thieves.

> *"Chosen thief...  
> Unto thee, I grant new power."*  
> — [Aeber](../world/aeber.md)

## Geography and Access

The altar is situated along the [Southern Clockbank Highroad](southern_clockbank_highroad.md), concealed within a hidden cave on the southeastern end of the river.

Inside the cavern stands a sacred statue of Aeber. When approached by the thief [Throné](../characters/throne.md), the deity grants her the power of Veil of Darkness.
