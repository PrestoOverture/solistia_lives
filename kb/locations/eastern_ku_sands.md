# Eastern Ku Sands

The **Eastern Ku Sands** is a desert field route located in the arid region of [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md).

## Geography and Routes

The desert trail connects several landmarks across Hinoeuma:
- To the west, the route extends into the [Southern Ku Sands](southern_ku_sands.md).
- To the north, the sands transition toward the [Western Sai Sands](western_sai_sands.md).
- To the east, the trail provides access to the cavern known as the [Tranquil Grotto](tranquil_grotto.md).

## Wildlife

The harsh desert environment is home to various formidable beasts and monsters, including Desert Apekings, Carmine Eagles, Dire Skull Rollers, Woolly Spiders, Venomous Scorpions, and spectral Headless Horsemans.
