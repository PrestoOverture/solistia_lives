# Gravell

> *"This village has been beset by calamity and plague. Most of our youngest and brightest are no longer with us."*  
> —*Gravell Villager*

**Gravell** is a remote, dwindling village tucked into the ravines of the [Wildlands](wildlands.md) in *Octopath Traveler II*. It lies connected between the [Western Gravell Wilds](western_gravell_wilds.md) and the [Path to the Duskruin Shrine](path_to_the_duskruin_shrine.md), serving as the destination for [Osvald V. Vanstein](../characters/osvald.md)'s final confrontation.

## History & Calamities

Situated in close proximity to the ancient [Duskruin Shrine](duskruin_shrine.md), Gravell has long been afflicted by a string of devastating misfortunes, plagues, and fatal accidents. The recent wave of tragedies was unleashed after a team of researchers excavated and disturbed the adjacent ruins. The resulting afflictions claimed the lives of much of the town's youth, leaving behind a weary, insular populace where visits from outside travelers are treated as an uncommon novelty.

## Culture & Notable Figures

Despite its desolate atmosphere, Gravell retains several unique cultural traits and residents:

- **Local Goods**: The villagers are known for crafting a distinct cheese made from goat milk. The village provisioner is operated cooperatively by local widows.
- **Porta the Blacksmith**: Residing in the village center, [Porta](../characters/porta.md) is the descendant of a revered lineage of ancient smiths. He possesses the ancestral knowledge needed to reforge the rusty weapons of legendary Armsmasters.
- **Protectors & Scholars**: A retired mercenary woman patrols the village boundaries by day to keep prowling beasts at bay. Meanwhile, an inquisitive scholar resides in the town researching her manuscript, *"On the Spate of Misfortunes in the Land where the [Archmage](../characters/darqest.md)'s Corpse rests."*
- **Ritual Candles**: Fourteen candles are placed along the eastern boundary of the village—arranged in a cluster of three and a ring of eight, with three additional candles placed further down the eastern road.

## The Myth of the Sunshadow Staff

A longstanding Wildlands myth rooted in Gravell tells of a malevolent dark entity that preys upon the hidden fears in children's hearts, possessing them and causing them to wander the village outskirts at dawn. According to local lore, if the possessing shadow is confronted and banished, the malice is neutralized and sealed away into the legendary Sunshadow Staff.
