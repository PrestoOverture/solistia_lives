# Eastern Cropdale Trail

The **Eastern Cropdale Trail** is a forest path in the [Leaflands](leaflands.md) connecting the farming village of [Cropdale](cropdale.md) to the wider region.

## Geography & Routes

Winding through verdant woodlands and riverbanks east of Cropdale, the trail serves as a major overland and waterways conduit:
- **West**: Leads directly into the rural village of [Cropdale](cropdale.md).
- **South**: Connects toward the [Southern Cropdale Trail](southern_cropdale_trail.md).
- **North**: Heads toward [Crackridge Harbor: Anchorage](crackridge_harbor_anchorage.md), providing maritime transit between the Leaflands and the Wildlands.

## Story & Significance

Travelers embarking on journeys from Cropdale frequently pass through this trail. If [Agnea Bristarni](../characters/agnea.md) begins her travels from her hometown, she encounters a traveler in distress along this route during the side story [The Traveler's Bag](../stories/the_travelers_bag.md).

## Wildlife & Inhabitants

The lush woodlands, meadows, and river waters along the trail are inhabited by native beasts and forest humanoids, including clans of Woodland Birdian I, Woodland Birdian II, and Woodland Birdian III, alongside Marmots, Majestic Marmots, Mossy Meeps, Howlers, formidable Giant Boars, and river-dwelling Ancient Fish.
