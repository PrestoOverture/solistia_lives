# Theater: Passageway

**Theater: Passageway** is an interior corridor within the grand theater of [New Delsta](new_delsta.md), situated in the [Brightlands](brightlands.md).

## Overview

The passageway serves as an upper connective corridor linking different sections of [Theater: Backstage](theater_backstage.md). During [Agnea Bristarni](../characters/agnea.md)'s pursuit of the elusive theater manager [La'mani](../characters/lamani.md), she races through this elevated corridor to cut off his escape before their climactic showdown on the theater stage.
