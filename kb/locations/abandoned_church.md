# Abandoned Church

The **Abandoned Church** is a ruined church and dungeon situated in the [Crestlands](crestlands.md), accessed via the [Abandoned Traverse](abandoned_traverse.md).

## Lore

The sanctuary is desolate and forgotten. In the past, [Marietta](../characters/marietta.md) remarked on its melancholy state: "*What a sad little church... Blessed by no one, just like us.*"

During her journey, [Throné Anguis](../characters/throne.md) travels through the Abandoned Traverse to the church to track down and confront [Father](../characters/father.md) in the conclusion of her Chapter 3 route.
