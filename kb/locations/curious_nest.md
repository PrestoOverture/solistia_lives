# Curious Nest

**Curious Nest** is an island cavern located in the [Sundering Sea](the_sundering_sea.md).

## Overview

The cavern serves as a bone-strewn nesting ground and predatory lair situated out amidst the open ocean waters. Deep within its winding, perilous interior dwells the monstrous ancient apex beast known as the [Tyrannodrake](../world/tyrannodrake.md).
