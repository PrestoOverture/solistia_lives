# Frigit Isle: Yard

> *"Forced labor is followed by a vanishingly small window of free time. It is in this free time that I review the remaining pieces of my plan."* — [Osvald](../characters/osvald.md)

**Frigit Isle: Yard** is the central courtyard and communal hub of the penal colony on [Frigit Isle](frigit_isle.md) in the [Winterlands](winterlands.md).

## Layout and Connections

The yard connects directly north to [Frigit Isle: Prison](frigit_isle_prison.md), east to the forced labor pits of [Frigit Isle: Mining Site](frigit_isle_mining_site.md), and contains a concealed hatch to the west that leads into the [Prison: Underground Passage](prison_underground_passage.md).

During normal operation, convicts were granted a brief window of free time following grueling labor shifts. During this period, inmates congregated in the open yard, visited the mess cafeteria, or accessed the prison library before guards locked them into the cellblocks for the night.

## The Library

Despite the prison's brutal regime, the compound houses a surprisingly large library. Although regulations forbade prisoners from removing books from the premises, convicts routinely took volumes with impunity due to the timidity of the guard librarian. The librarian notably took the time to teach at least one previously illiterate inmate how to read, suggesting the facility may have originally been founded with rehabilitative aims before devolving into a corrupt and punitive labor camp.

## Notable Inhabitants

The courtyard served as the meeting ground where [Osvald](../characters/osvald.md) coordinated his escape with [Emerald](../characters/emerald.md) and gathered necessary supplies and intelligence. Other individuals present in the yard included:
- [Bale](../characters/bale_octopath_traveler_ii.md), a susceptible guard whose leverage Osvald exploited during his escape preparations.
- Inmates convicted of major mainland crimes, including mass poisoners, illegal potion brewers, black-market smugglers, forgers, human traffickers, and serial arsonists.
