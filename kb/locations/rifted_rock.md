# Rifted Rock

> *"Long time. This cave. We protecting."*  
> — [Shirlutto](../characters/shirlutto.md)

The **Rifted Rock**, also called the "Twilight Shrine" by [Ori](../characters/ori.md), is an ancient cavern dungeon located on the island of [Toto'haha](totohaha.md), connected to the [Wandering Wood](wandering_wood.md). At the end of the cavern lies a sanctum adorned with a crescent moon-and-circle motif reminiscent of the [Stage of the Moon and Sun](stage_of_the_moon_and_sun.md).

## History

Centuries ago, the archmage [D'arqest](../characters/darqest.md) summoned the [Shadow](../world/shadow.md) deep within the Rifted Rock. Through this rite, he purged a group of humans of their worldly greed, transforming them into the progenitors of the [Beastlings](../world/beastling.md). Ever since, the beastling people have dutifully guarded and protected the cave across generations.

## The Climax of Temenos's Journey

The cavern serves as the stage for the climax of [Temenos Mistral](../characters/temenos.md)'s story. In its depths, Temenos corners [Captain Kaldena](../characters/kaldena.md), uncovering the sinister conspiracy surrounding the church and her ambitions tied to the ancient rite.
