# Underground Waterway

The **Underground Waterway** is a subterranean drainage network and sewer tunnel running beneath the city of [New Delsta](new_delsta.md) in the [Brightlands](brightlands.md).

> *"I just don't get it... Why is this door locked? ...This job is a nightmare."*
> — [Pirro](../characters/pirro.md)

## Overview & Story Role

The dim, damp passages of the waterway serve as a clandestine conduit beneath the metropolis, frequently traversed by rogues and operatives of the [Blacksnakes](../world/blacksnakes.md).

### Diamante's Estate Infiltration
In Chapter 1 of [Throné](../characters/throne.md)'s story, Throné and fellow thief [Pirro](../characters/pirro.md) navigate the flooded channels to evade city watch patrols and infiltrate [Diamante's Estate](diamantes_estate.md), fighting off rival pursuers who track them into the tunnels.

### The Locked Doorway
Deep in the subterranean waterway stands a massive sealed door leading beyond the city to the [Deserted Highroad](deserted_highroad.md). This barrier cannot be bypassed until Chapter 4, when Throné obtains the keys carried by both leaders of the Blacksnakes—[Father](../characters/father.md) and [Mother](../characters/mother.md). Combining their keys unlocks the threshold, allowing Throné to venture forward to the ruined settlement of [Lostseed](lostseed.md).
