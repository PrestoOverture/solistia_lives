# Starfall Spring

**Starfall Spring** is a grotto dungeon in the [Leaflands](leaflands.md). 

## Geography

Starfall Spring is accessed by entering the river along the [Eastern Cropdale Trail](eastern_cropdale_trail.md) and navigating downstream south through the [Southern Cropdale Trail](southern_cropdale_trail.md).

## Lore

The cavern is inhabited by wild fauna and Ancient Ratkins, with the deeper reaches claimed by the giant winged creature known as the Monarch. Deep in the spring behind the boss lair sits a skeleton resting upon a chair, accompanied by a High Priest's Amulet.
