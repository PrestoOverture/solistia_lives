# Abandoned Waterway

The **Abandoned Waterway** is a subterranean drainage dungeon situated in the [Brightlands](brightlands.md), located beneath the [New Delsta Flats](new_delsta_flats.md) west of the [Abandoned Village](abandoned_village.md).

## Overview

A dark, waterlogged canal network running under the flats, the waterway is infested with river fauna and lurking monsters, including the formidable Carnivorous Plant. Given its geographical proximity to the ruins of the Abandoned Village (formerly Healeaks), the canal system is believed to have originally served as infrastructure connecting to the former settlement.
