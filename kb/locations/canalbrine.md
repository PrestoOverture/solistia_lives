# Canalbrine

**Canalbrine** is a major harbor town located in the [Harborlands](harborlands.md) on the western continent of Solistia. Known for its intricate network of waterways and maritime traditions, it serves as a central hub of trade, exploration, and faith.

## Geography

Built along a sheltered inlet, Canalbrine is defined by navigable canals that wind between stone paved walkways, bridges, and seaside quays.
- To the west lies the [Western Canalbrine Coast](western_canalbrine_coast.md).
- The town's municipal water pipeline leads to the [Canalbrine: Path to the Water Source](canalbrine_path_to_the_water_source.md).
- Its bustling docks connect via ferry to [Beasting Bay: Anchorage](beasting_bay_anchorage.md) on Toto'haha, as well as the [Sacred Guard Ship](sacred_guard_ship.md).

## History and Heritage

Canalbrine boasts a storied maritime history. According to the [Mercantile Manuscript](../stories/masoud_script.md), roughly a century ago during the Age of Exploration, maritime voyages originating from Canalbrine's harbor successfully charted the first sea routes to the eastern continent.

Following the downfall of the ancient kingdom of [U](../world/u.md), waves of refugees migrated eastward into the Harborlands, establishing a significant presence in Canalbrine. The architectural and engineering genius of U remains embedded throughout the town's urban design, most notably in its sophisticated canal network and grand lighthouse.

## Landmarks and Culture

- **Canalbrine Lighthouse**: Constructed twenty years ago by master engineers hailing from U, the soaring stone lighthouse casts a brilliant beacon across the [Sundering Sea](the_sundering_sea.md). Its illumination is said to be visible from as far away as [New Delsta](new_delsta.md).
- **Church of the Sacred Flame**: An influential sanctuary that serves as the primary outpost and launch point for the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../world/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) on the western continent.
- **Canalways and Boat Access**: The waterways are woven so closely into the town's infrastructure that certain shops, including the armory, can be accessed directly by watercraft.
- **Tavern and Entertainment**: Canalbrine's inn is widely celebrated for its innkeeper's cuisine. By night, the upper floor of the tavern hosts dance performances by the famed [Hermes](../characters/hermes.md). Near the town entrance, a celebrated traveling violinist plays on the street corner after dark, eschewing traditional theater halls for the open night air.

## Story Involvements

- **[Castti Florenz](../characters/castti.md)**: After being pulled half-drowned from a drifting skiff by a compassionate vessel captain, the amnesiac apothecary is brought ashore in Canalbrine. When a mysterious illness breaks out traced to tainted water at the [Canalbrine: Water Source](canalbrine_water_source.md), Castti uncovers the poison, concocts a cure, and earns the deep gratitude of the town.
- **[Temenos Mistral](../characters/temenos.md)**: The clerical inquisitor journeys to Canalbrine seeking the theologian [Lucian](../characters/lucian.md) for insights regarding the assassination of [Pontiff Jörg](../characters/jorg.md). The inquiry leads Temenos to investigate a string of sinister murders orchestrated by [Vados the Architect](../characters/vados.md).
