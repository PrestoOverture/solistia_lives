# Sunken Maw

The **Sunken Maw** is a subterranean aquatic cavern and dungeon located in the [Brightlands](brightlands.md). Situated off the [Western Clockbank Highroad](western_clockbank_highroad.md), the cavern is flooded with water and can only be navigated by boat.

## Ecology and the Delsta Devil

The depths of the cave are home to the Delsta Devil, a monstrous aquatic beast prized by local gourmands for its exceptionally delicious culinary meat. Within the cavern stands an elevated fisherman's shack accessible by ladder; luring the beast out from a dark trench in the cavern's waters requires the fisherman's specialized bait.
