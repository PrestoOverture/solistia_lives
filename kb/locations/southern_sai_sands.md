# Southern Sai Sands

The **Southern Sai Sands** is a desert field route located south of the oasis town of [Sai](sai.md) in the [Hinoeuma](hinoeuma.md) region.

## Geography

The desert trail links central and southern Hinoeuma:
- To the north, the path leads directly into [Sai](sai.md).
- To the west, the trail connects to the [Western Sai Sands](western_sai_sands.md).
- To the east, the trail extends into the [Eastern Sai Sands](eastern_sai_sands.md).

## Notable Inhabitants

Along a trail branching to the right just outside the entrance to Sai, travelers may encounter a nervous, gaunt figure known as the **Thin Merchant**:
- **Description & Demeanor**: An emaciated 42-year-old man who draws ragged, anxious breaths while calling out to passersby. He perpetually sighs under heavy stress, questioning when his endless restocking cycle will finally end: *"I wonder how much more I'll have to stock until it's all over..."* The true source of his merchandise remains unknown.
- **Connection**: He shares an enigmatic connection with the Portly Merchant stationed in the neighboring [Western Sai Sands](western_sai_sands.md). Once the Thin Merchant exhausts his stock and departs the desert permanently, the Portly Merchant likewise vanishes from the sands along with the paraphernalia surrounding his nearby home.
