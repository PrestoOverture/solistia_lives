# Northern Wellgrove Trail

> "*The Shrine of Ul'sterra is famous for honoring the goddess Sealticge... But I've heard there is another altar to the Lady of Grace hidden somewhere in the Leaflands. Rumor has it that Sealticge will grant the dancer who finds that shrine a special power. I searched high and low for it when I was young... I heard it could be found on the Northern Wellgrove Trail, but alas... My search was fruitless. Supposedly only the chosen dancer will be able to locate it. I may not have received Sealticge's blessing, but perhaps your luck will be different.*"
> — Dancers Guild Master

**Northern Wellgrove Trail** is a heavily wooded, rain-swept forest path located in the [Leaflands](../leaflands.md).

## Geography and Connections

The trail serves as an overland transit corridor linking major settlements throughout the region:
- **[Southern Cropdale Trail](southern_cropdale_trail.md)** to the east
- **[Eastern Wellgrove Trail](eastern_wellgrove_trail.md)** to the south, leading to the scholarly trading town of Wellgrove
- **[Southern Timberain Trail](southern_timberain_trail.md)** to the west, leading toward the royal seat of Timberain
- **[Altar of the Lady of Grace](altar_of_the_lady_of_grace.md)** to the north

## The Altar of the Lady of Grace

Deep within the trail, concealed inside a secluded cavern overhung by thick curtains of forest vines, lies the **[Altar of the Lady of Grace](altar_of_the_lady_of_grace.md)**. Dedicated to the goddess [Sealticge](../../../world/gods/sealticge.md), the altar is the subject of legend within the Dancers Guild. The guild master tells of an elusive blessing granted only to the chosen dancer who successfully discovers the hidden sanctuary.

## Environment and Inhabitants

- **Perpetual Rain**: The trail is notable for being enveloped in near-constant rainfall. The weeping canopy foreshadows the tragic downpour that surrounds [Timberain](../towns/timberain.md) during the climax of [Castti Florenz](../../../characters/castti/castti.md)'s quest against [Trousseau](../../../characters/castti/trousseau.md).
- **Travelers**: Itinerant Sacred Guard clerics periodically pass through the woods, including a knight and squire who seek shelter beneath rock overhangs at night, and a devout traveler who meditates before the path's great central tree.
- **Wildlife**: Indigenous fauna includes tribes of Woodland Birdians and Birdkings, Demon Deer, Forest Foxes, Red Foxes, Giant Boars, Gargantuan Boars, Porcupines, Silky Cocoons, and Great Silk Moths.
