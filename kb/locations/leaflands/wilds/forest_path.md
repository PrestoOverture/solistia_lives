# Forest Path

The **Forest Path** is a wooded trail situated in the verdant [Leaflands](../leaflands.md), serving as a central overland junction outside the rural settlement of [Cropdale](../towns/cropdale.md).

## Geography & Routes

Winding through shaded glades, leafy thickets, and waterways, the path links several neighboring territories:
- **South**: Leads directly into the village of [Cropdale](../towns/cropdale.md).
- **North**: Ascends toward the [Festival Grounds](festival_grounds.md).
- **West**: Connects to the [Veil of Trees](../dungeons/veil_of_trees.md).
- **East**: Leads into the dense woods of the [Animal Trail](../dungeons/animal_trail.md).

## Wildlife

The woods along the path are home to forest fauna such as Marmots and Mossy Meeps, as well as clans of Woodland Birdian I and Woodland Birdian II. The traversing waters support aquatic creatures including the Ancient Fish.
