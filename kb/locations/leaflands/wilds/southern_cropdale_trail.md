# Southern Cropdale Trail

The **Southern Cropdale Trail** is a field route located outside [Cropdale](../towns/cropdale.md) in the [Leaflands](../leaflands.md).

## Geography

The trail connects several key routes across western and southern Solistia:
- To the north lies the [Eastern Cropdale Trail](eastern_cropdale_trail.md).
- To the east, the trail leads toward the [Southern Oresrush Wilds](../../wildlands/wilds/southern_oresrush_wilds.md).
- To the west, the path winds toward the [Northern Wellgrove Trail](northern_wellgrove_trail.md).
- To the south, travelers can access [Starfall Spring](../dungeons/starfall_spring.md).

A campsite situated at the southernmost point of the trail serves as the gathering place where [The Journey for the Dawn](../../../stories/main/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md) and the travelers' final epilogue begin.
