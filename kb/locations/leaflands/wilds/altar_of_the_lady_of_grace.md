# Altar of the Lady of Grace

The **Altar of the Lady of Grace** is a sacred site in the [Leaflands](../leaflands.md) dedicated to [Sealticge](../../../world/gods/sealticge.md), the Lady of Grace.

> *"Chosen dancer...  
> Unto thee, I grant new power."*  
> — [Sealticge](../../../world/gods/sealticge.md)

## Geography and Access

The entrance to the altar is located along the [Northern Wellgrove Trail](northern_wellgrove_trail.md). It lies partially obscured by a log below a cliff near the path leading toward the [Southern Cropdale Trail](southern_cropdale_trail.md).

Within the sanctuary stands a divine statue of Sealticge. When approached by the dancer [Agnea](../../../characters/travelers/agnea.md), the goddess bestows the power of Windy Refrain.

## Lore

Although the altar bearing Sealticge's name is situated off the Northern Wellgrove Trail, the [Shrine of Ul'sterra](../../crestlands/dungeons/shrine_of_ulsterra.md) is also said to house the Altar of the Lady of Grace.
