# Leaflands

The **Leaflands** is a region enveloped by verdant forests in northwestern Solistia, renowned for its rich timber reserves, thriving agriculture, and longstanding traditions of dance and harvest celebrations.

## Geography and Environment

The Leaflands is defined by deep woodland canopies, lush rolling river valleys, and productive clearings. 

Making use of the area's abundant natural resources, industries such as forestry, lumber, agriculture, and paper manufacturing flourish throughout the territory. The region's rich soil yields some of the finest produce on the continent, most notably the raspberries and peaches harvested around the village of [Cropdale](towns/cropdale.md).

The Leaflands borders [Hinoeuma](../hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md) to the south and the [Wildlands](../wildlands/wildlands.md) to the east. While the southern town of [Wellgrove](towns/wellgrove.md) prospers from trade with the arid desert lands across the southern border, wide disparities between wealth and poverty continually spark social strife, and border disputes with neighboring desert realms persist without end.

## Major Settlements

- **[Cropdale](towns/cropdale.md)**: A tranquil woodland village famed for its orchards of raspberries and peaches, as well as its celebrated raspberry pies and annual Harvest Festival. It is the starting point of the dancer [Agnea Bristarni](../../characters/agnea/agnea.md)'s journey and the setting for [Castti Florenz](../../characters/castti/castti.md) and [Ochette](../../characters/ochette/ochette.md)'s Crossed Paths.
- **[Wellgrove](towns/wellgrove.md)**: A prosperous commercial town situated in the southern woods, home to the wealthy merchant-aristocrat [Alrond Rondwell](../../characters/partitio/alrond_rondwell.md) and his butler [Misha](../../characters/partitio/misha.md). Beneath its surface prosperity, economic recession and steep inequality foster discontent, and the notorious [Blacksnakes](../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md) criminal syndicate secretly maintains an orphanage on its outskirts. The town features in the journeys of [Partitio Yellowil](../../characters/partitio/partitio.md) (Chapter 3), [Hikari Ku](../../characters/hikari/hikari.md) (Chapter 3), and [Throné Anguis](../../characters/throne/throne.md) (Chapter 3, Mother's Route).
- **[Timberain](towns/timberain.md)**: The grand capital of the kingdom of Timberain, built along the shores of an inland lake and encircled by dense woodlands. A center of government, lumber milling, and paper production, its royal military remains frequently embroiled in conflict with southern desert polities. It serves as the dramatic climax of Castti's confrontation against [Trousseau](../../characters/castti/trousseau.md) (Chapter 4).

## Landmarks and Dungeons

### Altars
- **[Altar of the Lady of Grace](wilds/altar_of_the_lady_of_grace.md)**: A sacred woodland shrine dedicated to [Sealticge](../../world/gods/sealticge.md), the Lady of Grace and patron goddess of dancers.

### Field Routes
- **[Forest Path](wilds/forest_path.md)**: A tranquil woodland track approaching Cropdale.
- **[Festival Grounds](wilds/festival_grounds.md)**: The communal gathering meadow near Cropdale where the village hosts its seasonal Harvest Festival.
- **[Eastern Cropdale Trail](wilds/eastern_cropdale_trail.md)** and **[Southern Cropdale Trail](wilds/southern_cropdale_trail.md)**: Verdant wilderness pathways winding through the rural outskirts of Cropdale.
- **[Northern Wellgrove Trail](wilds/northern_wellgrove_trail.md)** and **[Eastern Wellgrove Trail](wilds/eastern_wellgrove_trail.md)**: Forest highways leading travelers into Wellgrove.
- **[Road to Mother's Garden](wilds/road_to_mothers_garden.md)**: A path leading to the secluded estate of the Blacksnake syndicate's orphanage.
- **[Southern Timberain Trail](wilds/southern_timberain_trail.md)**: The primary highway leading north into the kingdom's capital.

### Caverns, Mansions, and Fortresses
- **[Veil of Trees](dungeons/veil_of_trees.md)**: A wild, berry-rich forest grove near Cropdale inhabited by the great wild boar [Duorduor](../../characters/agnea/duorduor.md).
- **[Starfall Spring](dungeons/starfall_spring.md)**: A secluded inland spring and grotto accessible only by boat along forest waterways, sanctuary of the great winged Monarch.
- **[Animal Trail](dungeons/animal_trail.md)**: A woodland path frequented by wild duorduor and game animals, later corrupted by encroaching shadow entities.
- **[Secret Forest](dungeons/secret_forest.md)**: A concealed forest thicket west of Wellgrove used for illicit black-market weapons trading, overseen by [General Rou](../../characters/hikari/rou.md).
- **[Mother's Garden](dungeons/mothers_garden.md)**: A manicured estate housing the orphanage operated by [Mother](../../characters/throne/mother.md) of the Blacksnakes, infamous for the bell tower used to toll signals of corporal punishment.
- **[House Wellows Manor](dungeons/house_wellows_manor.md)**: An abandoned, dilapidated mansion shrouded in dark sorrow and haunted by wandering spirits.
- **[Timberain Castle](dungeons/timberain_castle.md)** and **[Timberain Castle: Roof](dungeons/timberain_castle_roof.md)**: The fortified stone citadel serving as the seat of the kingdom of Timberain, where Trousseau attempted to disperse his lethal purple toxin into the rainclouds above.
