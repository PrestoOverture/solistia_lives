# House Wellows Manor

**House Wellows Manor** is an abandoned, haunted estate situated in the [Leaflands](../leaflands.md), connected to the [Southern Timberain Trail](../wilds/southern_timberain_trail.md) near [Timberain](../towns/timberain.md).

## The Tragedy of House Wellows

The manor was once the stately home of the aristocratic Wellows family. Years prior, the entire household was brutally butchered inside the mansion during a birthday celebration. The only known survivor of that night was an attendant who later lived in seclusion in Timberain, rumored to have accepted a bribe to aid the murderer. 

An eerie first-hand account of the massacre survived in the [Worn Journal](../../../stories/documents/worn_journal_script.md), which was later discovered left behind at a pergola on the Southern Timberain Trail.

## Supernatural Phenomena

Following the massacre, the estate fell into decay and became cursed by restless spirits and dark forces:
- **Apparitions**: In the dead of night, phantoms of the slaughtered family appear around the grand staircase—a spectral mother and child standing to the left, and the father standing to the right. Both disappear into thin air whenever approached.
- **Poltergeist Activity**: Visitors to the manor experience chilling manifestations, including frantic pounding on the locked front doors, phantom children giggling, and the rustle of invisible papers shuffling through empty rooms.
- **The Slashed Portrait**: An upstairs chamber contains a mutilated portrait of the family, before which thirteen candles are arrayed in two ominous rings of five and eight.
- **An Unnatural Stillness**: A suffocating silence permeates the estate; no background music or fanfares echo within its walls, even during encounters with the dark creatures stalking the halls.

## Story Events

In the side story [A Mysterious Box](../../../stories/side_stories/crestlands/a_mysterious_box.md), travelers recover a sealed, eerie reliquary from the [Merry Hills: Shrine Entrance](../../crestlands/towns/merry_hills_shrine_entrance.md) and deliver it to the upstairs chamber with the slashed portrait. At night, the spectral lady of House Wellows manifests to receive the box. With its delivery, the curse binding the household is broken, the hauntings cease, and the tortured spirits find peace.

## Denizens and Manifestations

The dark spiritual residue of the manor attracts shadowy entities and aberrant wildlife:
- **Entities**: Shadow Revenants, Shadow Remnant Mk.II, and arcane Dark Sentinel Mk.II constructs.
- **Beasts**: Abnormally resilient and aggressive Devil Deer.
