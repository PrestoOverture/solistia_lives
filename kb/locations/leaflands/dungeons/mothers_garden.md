# Mother's Garden

> *"I will tell you where Mother went...as promised... She hides in Wellgrove... In a place called Mother's Garden... It's officially an orphanage...and has the support of the local monastery... But it's where she raises her hatchlings...for the nest... You could probably...sneak in...as a nun..."*
> — [The Slaver](../../../characters/throne/the_slaver.md)

**Mother's Garden** is a secluded estate and dungeon hidden within the forests near [Wellgrove](../towns/wellgrove.md) in the [Leaflands](../leaflands.md). Ostensibly an extension of a charitable orphanage supported by the local monastery, it functions as the private domain and training grounds of [Mother](../../../characters/throne/mother.md), a high-ranking leader of the [Blacksnakes](../../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md) guild.

The estate serves as the climactic setting for [Throné Anguis](../../../characters/travelers/throne.md)'s Chapter 3 ([Mother](../../../characters/throne/mother.md)'s route).

## Overview & Atmosphere

Mother's Garden connects directly to [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](../wilds/mothers_garden_orphanage.md). Regardless of the time of day across the rest of Solistia, Mother's Garden is trapped in perpetual, eerie twilight, shrouded under a dark night sky where a full moon continuously hangs above its grounds.

Within the estate gardens grow sinister black roses. The thorns and petals of these flowers are harvested by Mother to brew the deadly, incurable poison loaded into the locked iron collars that the Blacksnakes force upon their operatives to ensure absolute subservience.

## Blackbell Tower

At the deepest point of Mother's Garden stands **Blackbell Tower**, an ominous belltower illuminated by the permanent full moon. The tolling of Blackbell Tower's bell serves as a terrifying summons for the children raised in the adjoining orphanage: whenever the bell rings, an orphan is summoned up the tower to endure brutal physical punishment or death at Mother's hands. It is atop Blackbell Tower that Throné finally confronts and executes Mother, claiming the key to her own collar.
