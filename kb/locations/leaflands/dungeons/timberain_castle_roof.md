# Timberain Castle: Roof

**Timberain Castle: Roof** is the high rooftop area of [Timberain Castle](timberain_castle.md) in the [Leaflands](../leaflands.md). 

> "*The rain... It's starting to change color. [Trousseau](../../../characters/castti/trousseau.md)... I knew I'd find you here.*"
> — Castti Florenz

## Lore and Story Events

Perched atop the royal fortress with an unobstructed view overlooking the whole of [Timberain](../towns/timberain.md), the castle roof becomes the staging ground for [Trousseau](../../../characters/castti/trousseau.md)'s scheme during Chapter 4 of [Castti Florenz](../../../characters/castti/castti.md)'s tale. 

Seeking to bring painless release to all living souls, Trousseau uses the elevation to disperse toxic chemical concoctions into the skies, causing rainclouds over the city to turn into a lethal purple deluge during the royal coronation. Castti ascends to the rooftop to confront her fallen former comrade, defeating him in battle before administering the panacea rain that rescues the poisoned citizens below.
