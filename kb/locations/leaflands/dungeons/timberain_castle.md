# Timberain Castle

**Timberain Castle** is the royal palace and fortress of the kingdom of [Timberain](../towns/timberain.md), situated within the [Leaflands](../leaflands.md).

> "*Halt! What business do you have in Timberain Castle?*"
> — Gatekeeper at night

## Architecture and Layout

Timberain Castle rises above the town, connected below to the [Timberain Castle: Town Square](../towns/timberain_castle_town_square.md) and above to the [Timberain Castle: Roof](timberain_castle_roof.md). Within its fortified halls lies a grand royal audience chamber with twin thrones. Positioned behind the thrones is a prominent statue of justice depicting a judge bearing a sword and scales beneath a proclamation inscribed in [Solistian script](../../../world/lore/alphabet.md), mirroring the motif seen in Timberain's high courthouse.

## Story Events

During Chapter 4 of [Castti Florenz](../../../characters/castti/castti.md)'s journey, the fallen apothecary [Trousseau](../../../characters/castti/trousseau.md) infiltrates the royal stronghold to unleash a lethal airborne deluge upon the city during the prince's coronation celebrations. Trousseau leaves a trail of unconscious castle guards behind him, having rendered them into deep slumber with medicinal sedatives to reach the castle heights unobstructed. Castti ascends the silent halls of the castle to confront him on the roof.
