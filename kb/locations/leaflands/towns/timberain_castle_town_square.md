# Timberain Castle: Town Square

**Timberain Castle: Town Square** is the grand plaza and courtyard connecting the town of [Timberain](timberain.md) to the gates of [Timberain Castle](../dungeons/timberain_castle.md) in the [Leaflands](../leaflands.md).

> "*[Timberain](timberain.md) is a beautiful and verdant nation, and I won't let this garden be an exception.*"
> — Royal Gardener

## Overview and Layout

The square is situated immediately before the castle ramparts, characterized by verdant royal flower gardens, waterside pavilions, and stone arcades. 

Key locations and landmarks within the district include:
- **The Castle Gates**: Heavily fortified gates leading up to [Timberain Castle](../dungeons/timberain_castle.md). Previously sealed by a portcullis, the entrance was later reinforced by sentries demanding strict security reviews following the "purple rain" crisis during the prince's coronation.
- **The Guardhouse**: A municipal military outpost where a vintage map of the distant land of Orsterra hangs framed upon the wall.
- **Waterside Pavilion**: A quiet pavilion at the edge of the lake where scholars and townsfolk gather.
- **Hidden Passages**: A secluded path guarded by an elderly royal veteran who safeguards the Exorcising Bow, an heirloom of the Timberain crown.

## Notable Inhabitants

- **The Eighth Princess**: One of the king's royal daughters, found taking the air in the square.
- **Royal Gardener**: Dedicated to maintaining the kingdom's reputation for lush floral beauty.
- **Mysterious Reader**: An elderly man found reading by the water pavilion who claims to be an acquaintance of an old friend of the king's grandfather.
- **The Gatekeeper**: A vigilant sentry stationed at the castle entrance.
