# Wellgrove: Empty House

**Wellgrove: Empty House** is a town sub-area in [Wellgrove](wellgrove.md), located within the [Leaflands](../leaflands.md).

## Overview

The Empty House is an unoccupied commercial building in the center of Wellgrove. During [Partitio Yellowil](../../../characters/partitio/partitio.md)'s business venture in town, he secures the premises and renovates the vacant property into a bustling commercial venue, establishing the [Wellgrove: Department Store](wellgrove_department_store.md).
