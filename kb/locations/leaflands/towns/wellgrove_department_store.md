# Wellgrove: Department Store

> "*Welcome to [Alrond](../../../characters/partitio/alrond_rondwell.md)'s Department Store!*"
> — Guard near the entrance

## Overview

**Wellgrove: Department Store**, officially named **Alrond's Department Store**, is a bustling commercial sub-area of [Wellgrove](wellgrove.md) in the [Leaflands](../leaflands.md). Prior to its revitalization, the structure was known simply as [Wellgrove: Empty House](wellgrove_empty_house.md).

## History

During his travels, the merchant [Partitio Yellowil](../../../characters/partitio/partitio.md) renovated the vacant building into Solistia's first full-scale department store with the backing of Lord [Alrond](../../../characters/partitio/alrond_rondwell.md). The project created a centralized emporium gathering wares, craftsmen, and merchants from across Solistia.

## Commerce and Community

Alrond's Department Store became an immediate economic hub, offering generous wages and working conditions that drew prospective employees via "Help Wanted" postings in town. The store features a variety of specialty shops, including weapon and armor smiths, a provisioner, an apothecary shop stocking rare Concoct ingredients, and a music record merchant.

The establishment attracted patrons and merchants from across the realm:
- **Culinary and Craft Innovation**: A local merchant began crafting novel varieties of jerky and sausage using spices and tools purchased at the store. A visiting [Sanctum Knight](../../../world/factions/sacred_guard.md) documented that a master weapon sharpener had set up shop within the building.
- **Fair Trade with Hinoeuma**: Impressed by Alrond's equitable agreements—unlike other traders who offered unfair prices and looked down upon people from the desert—a merchant from [Hinoeuma](../../hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md) agreed to supply rare goods from his homeland for wholesale.
- **Notable Visitors**: The royal head chef of [Timberain](timberain.md) frequents the store to buy exotic spices in search of the ideal flavor ratio for royal curry. At night, a librarian from [Montwise](../../crestlands/towns/montwise.md) searches the aisles for specialized materials needed to repair ancient volumes.
- **Wider Influence**: The department store's striking success caught the attention of a wealthy visitor from [New Delsta](../../brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md), who resolved to construct a similar department store in his own city.
