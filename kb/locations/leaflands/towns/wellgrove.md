# Wellgrove

**Wellgrove** is a prominent mercantile and agricultural town nestled in the [Leaflands](../leaflands.md).

## Geography

Wellgrove sits at a major crossroads connecting the central forests to neighboring southern and western regions:
- To the east runs the [Eastern Wellgrove Trail](../wilds/eastern_wellgrove_trail.md).
- To the west lies the concealed entrance to the [Secret Forest](../dungeons/secret_forest.md).
- To the northwest stands [Wellgrove: Alrond's Estate](wellgrove_alronds_estate.md).
- To the north is the municipal plaza housing [Wellgrove: Department Store](wellgrove_department_store.md) and the Dancers' Guild.
- To the south winds the [Road to Mother's Garden](../wilds/road_to_mothers_garden.md).

## History & Culture

According to historical records preserved in the [Mercantile Manuscript](../../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md), Wellgrove originally flourished as a booming trade hub by exchanging timber, crops, and textiles with the resource-starved desert lands of [Hinoeuma](../../hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md) to the south. This severe disparity in wealth eventually ignited persistent border skirmishes, embroiling Wellgrove and the kingdom of [Timberain](timberain.md) in armed conflict against migrating refugees and raiders from [Sai](../../hinoeuma/towns/sai.md).

Over time, trade stagnation plunged the town into a prolonged economic depression. Wellgrove's fortunes were dramatically reversed during [Partitio Yellowil](../../../characters/travelers/partitio.md)'s travels: by organizing the fractured local peddlers and securing the backing of the wealthy aristocrat Lord [Alrond](../../../characters/partitio/alrond_rondwell.md), Partitio unified the town's merchants to establish Solistia's very first Department Store.

Wellgrove is also noted for:
- **Flora & Industry**: The **well iris**, a native flower celebrated for its gorgeous blossoms but notorious for fruits that emit a pungent stench. By adopting traditional boiling techniques from [Cropdale](cropdale.md) using wild grass, craftsmen produce an exquisite textile dye that doubles as an effective natural insect repellent.
- **Craft & Drink**: The region is celebrated across Solistia for its fine beverages and brewing.
- **The Dancers' Guild**: Headquartered in a northern manor, this guild serves as a renowned center of performing arts where famous stars such as [Hermes](../../../characters/temenos/hermes.md) once trained.

## Notable Inhabitants & Story Connections

- **Lord [Alrond](../../../characters/partitio/alrond_rondwell.md)**: An eccentric multi-millionaire whose vast manor occupies the town's highest hill. Alrond finances the town's private defense force and plays a crucial role in Partitio's commercial endeavors as well as assisting [Hikari Ku](../../../characters/travelers/hikari.md)'s struggle against Mugen.
- **[Throné Anguis](../../../characters/travelers/throne.md)**: In her pursuit of Mother, Throné follows the Blacksnakes' bloody trail southward through the town gates toward [Mother's Garden](../dungeons/mothers_garden.md).
- **The Mysterious Merchant**: An enigmatic figure who appears by night near the department store, rumored to smuggle contraband goods in and out of the adjacent Secret Forest.
- **Town Diviner**: A local fortune-teller who lurks outside the tavern after dusk, known for issuing dire or grand prophecies that prove wildly inaccurate.
