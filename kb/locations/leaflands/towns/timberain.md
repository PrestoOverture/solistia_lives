# Timberain

**Timberain** is a lakeside royal city and kingdom nestled deep within the forests of the [Leaflands](../leaflands.md). Governed by an antiquated monarchy, it serves as the political and judicial center of the region.

> "*Hmm... This town is as beautiful as the first day I laid eyes on it.*"
> — Local soldier

## Geography and Economy

Timberain sits bordered by vast sweeps of primeval woodlands and scenic waterways. Rich woodland loam provides fertile soil for agriculture, while abundant forestry sustains thriving lumber, carpentry, and paper manufacturing industries. Timberain is also famed throughout the realm for its silkworm cultivation and fine silk textiles.

The city connects north to the [Timberain Castle: Town Square](timberain_castle_town_square.md) and south to the [Southern Timberain Trail](../wilds/southern_timberain_trail.md).

## Government and Society

Timberain retains a traditional feudal aristocratic order distinct from the industrialized or mercantile nations of Solistia:
- **The Monarchy**: The realm is ruled by a royal house whose crest features a golden lion head set upon green banners. The ruling monarch is known for amassing precious gems from across Solistia, though many citizens and guards quietly harbor resentment toward the court's vanity, high-handed privilege, and pervasive surveillance network. The royal lineage includes at least eight princesses and an heir whose coronation feast draws visitors from across the world.
- **The Military**: The crown's forces are split between favored inner royal guards and disgruntled outer sentries. Knights recognized for supreme martial skill bear the title "Arms of the Kingdom." The royal line holds sacred relics, most notably the legendary Exorcising Bow, gifted only to soldiers acknowledged by the monarch.
- **The High Courthouse**: Presided over by [Judge Astell](../../../characters/side_stories/astell.md), Timberain's grand court conducts major criminal proceedings spanning the entire western continent—including the framing and wrongful conviction of [Osvald V. Vanstein](../../../characters/osvald/osvald.md).
- **Foreign Relations**: Four years before the main story, Timberain engaged in an armed conflict with [Sai](../../hinoeuma/towns/sai.md) to block immigrant laborers from entering the country. The conflict subsided largely due to the humanitarian efforts of [Castti Florenz](../../../characters/castti/castti.md) and Eir's Apothecaries, though laborers from Sai continue to seek employment in secret.

## Story Events

### Castti's Tale
In Chapter 4 of Castti's journey, the fallen apothecary [Trousseau](../../../characters/castti/trousseau.md) plans to exterminate Timberain by releasing toxic rain from atop [Timberain Castle](../dungeons/timberain_castle.md) during the prince's coronation. Castti warns the realm, assisted by [Commander Griff](../../../characters/castti/griff.md) and [Edmund](../../../characters/castti/edmund.md), who order citizens indoors. Castti ascends the palace heights, defeats Trousseau, and synthesizes a curing rain that cleanses the toxin, preserving the kingdom.
