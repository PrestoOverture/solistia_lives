# Roi

> "*[Temenos](temenos.md), I... I can no longer trust the church! You are the only person I *can* trust, apart from the [pontiff](jorg.md). As someone who was lifted up by the pontiff himself, I wish to confide in you.*"
> — Roi

## Overview

**Roi** was an inquisitor within the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../world/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md), the adoptive brother and closest companion of [Temenos Mistral](temenos.md), and an adoptive son of [Pontiff Jörg](jorg.md). His mysterious murder five years before the main story serves as the catalyst for Temenos's pursuit of truth behind the corruption festering within the church.

## Profile

### Appearance
Roi had short brown hair and wore the traditional white and blue vestments of an inquisitor of the Sacred Flame, similar in design to those worn by Temenos.

### Personality
Temenos describes Roi as an exceptionally honest and upright soul who never doubted anyone or anything. To counterbalance Roi's trusting, naive nature and shield him from harm, Temenos developed his own habit of relentless skepticism and questioning. Beneath Roi's gentleness lay deep moral fortitude; when confronted with betrayal inside the church, he chose to risk his life to investigate rather than remain silent.

## History

### Early Life and Brotherhood
Roi and Temenos were orphans taken in as foundlings and raised together by Pontiff Jörg. During their childhood, Roi contracted a life-threatening illness, but survived after Temenos prayed continuously for his recovery. The two grew up as brothers, each entering the priesthood and rising to the rank of inquisitor under the pontiff's mentorship.

Years before his death, Roi encountered a young [Crick Wellsley](crick_wellsley.md), whose impoverished noble family had resorted to theft and deceit. Sensing the boy's inner honor, Roi assured Crick that a "noble flame" burned within him, inspiring Crick to turn his life around and dedicate himself to becoming a knight in the Sanctum Guard.

### The Darkblood Bow and Death
Five years prior to the events of *Octopath Traveler II*, Roi discovered the dark relic known as the Darkblood Bow hidden covertly within the church. Realizing that high-ranking members of the clergy were compromised, Roi paid a clandestine late-night visit to Temenos, confiding that he no longer trusted the church and that Temenos and Pontiff Jörg were the only people he believed in. 

Roi set out alone to neutralize or dispose of the Darkblood Bow. However, he was intercepted and murdered by operatives of the [Moonshade Order](../world/moonshade_order.md), who seized the bow and placed it in the hands of the cultist [Petrichor](petrichor.md).

### Legacy
Roi's memory profoundly shaped Temenos, reinforcing his conviction that doubt is an essential tool in finding the divine truth. Furthermore, dialogue and visual details strongly imply that the [Dark Entity](dark_entity.md) fought by [Ochette](ochette.md) in Toto'haha was a twisted incarnation created from Roi's remains or essence: upon its defeat, the creature's dying words echo what Roi said to Temenos before departing, and shredded remnants of a cleric's robe cling to its tail.
