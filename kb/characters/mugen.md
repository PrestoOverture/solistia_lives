# Mugen

**General Mugen**, later crowned **King Mugen**, is a prince of [Ku](../world/ku.md), the older half-brother of [Hikari Ku](hikari.md), and the primary antagonist of Hikari's story. Known feared throughout the realm as the "Scarlet Demon of Ku," Mugen is a ruthless and formidable warrior who subscribes wholly to the doctrine that might makes right. Tracing his lineage back to the archmage [D'arqest](darqest.md), Mugen is driven by the dark, cursed blood of Clan Ku and seeks to plunge the world into constant war.

## Profile

### Appearance
Mugen is clad from head to toe in imposing red and black samurai armor. A severe metal faceplate conceals his visage. Beneath the mask, his face is haggard, aged, and corpselike—the physical toll exacted by years of succumbing to the dark influence of the [Shadow](../world/shadow.md) and his own relentless bloodlust.

### Personality
Brutal, merciless, and wildly ambitious, Mugen believes that only the ruthless deserve to rule Ku and the wider world. Although plagued and urged on by a dark inner voice driving him toward slaughter, Mugen embraces this malice willingly and without hesitation or remorse. He rejects compassion as weakness, declaring that Clan Ku's greatness was built solely on violence and domination. He exhibits no regard for human life; after conquering the kingdom of [Sa](../world/sa.md), he ordered the total extermination of its populace simply to eradicate any lingering grudges that could serve as "seeds of rebellion."

## History

### Background
Mugen was born the eldest son of [King Jigo](jigo_ku.md), carrying the cursed bloodline of Clan Ku inherited from [D'arqest](darqest.md). Eight years prior to the events of the story, Mugen learned that Hikari's mother, [Kura](kura.md), belonged to the [Lumina Clan](../world/lumina_clan.md)—an ancient bloodline capable of opposing the Shadow. Seeking to purge this threat, Mugen coerced [Jin Mei](jin_mei.md) into ambushing Kura and Hikari. When Kura was murdered, Hikari was consumed by his inherited darkness, forcing Jin to knock the boy unconscious. Mugen promptly executed Jin for failing to eliminate Hikari, though he spared Clan Mei to exploit them in future conflicts.

Five years later, Mugen spearheaded a military campaign across southern [Hinoeuma](../locations/hinoeuma.md). When Hikari showed mercy to a defeated enemy commander, Mugen executed the captive on the spot and berated his half-brother for his soft-heartedness. Following the campaign, Mugen had a falling out with his master strategist [Kazan](oboro.md) and began scheming to violently depose his father, whom he believed had grown too weak to fulfill Ku's destiny.

### Usurpation of Ku
Mugen set his coup into motion by using a castle merchant to draw Hikari away from the capital. Mugen's faction then set fire to the castle town while Mugen confronted King Jigo directly in the throne room. Enraged that Jigo intended to name the compassionate Hikari as crown prince, Mugen struck his father down. Hikari arrived only in time to hear Jigo's dying words before being forced to flee for his life. Mugen purged Hikari's remaining allies in the capital, launched a propaganda campaign painting Hikari as a patricidal traitor, and placed a massive bounty on his head.

Taking the crown as King Mugen, he embarked on aggressive expansion, conquering the kingdom of [Sa](../world/sa.md), slaying the [King of Sa](king_of_sa.md), and ordering his aide Ageha to annihilate the populace. When [Ritsu Mishuyo](ritsu_mishuyo.md) presented the severed head of [General Rou](rou.md) alongside a fabricated tale of treason, Mugen saw straight through the deception. Recognizing Ritsu's cold ambition and tactical value, Mugen promoted him to general. When Ageha expressed anxiety over the rebel force Hikari was gathering, Mugen revealed the Darkblood Blade, confident that Hikari's divided heritage would lead to his ruin.

### Fall of the Enshrouded King
After Hikari's forces breached the castle and defeated Ritsu, Hikari confronted Mugen in the palace. As they clashed blades, Mugen revealed his responsibility for Kura's murder and his goal of wiping out the Lumina lineage. During the duel, Hikari's inner darkness briefly manifested, but to Mugen's fury, Hikari managed to conquer and integrate his shadow-self—a feat never accomplished by any member of Clan Ku.

Driven to madness by Hikari's triumph over the blood curse, Mugen surrendered himself entirely to the darkness within him. Invoking the dark sorcery of the Darkblood Blade, he transformed into the monstrous **Enshrouded King**, a six-armed manifestation of carnage wielding the Darkblood Blade alongside Ku's ancient weaponry: a Spear of Strength, Battle-Tested Dagger, Battle-Tested Axe, Bow of Carnage, and Staff of Carnage. Despite this terrifying transformation, Hikari defeated him, freeing Ku from his tyranny and exposing Mugen's withered face as he died.
