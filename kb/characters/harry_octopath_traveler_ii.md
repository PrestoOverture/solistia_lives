# Harry (Octopath Traveler II)

> *"Let's get 'em good, Boss!"*
> — Harry, to [Partitio](partitio.md)

**Harry** is a 38-year-old miner living in [Oresrush](../locations/oresrush.md). He is a member of [Partitio Yellowil](partitio.md)'s posse alongside [Nikki](nikki.md), [Ned](ned_octopath_traveler_ii.md), and [Joe](joe.md). A hardworking family man, Harry labors tirelessly to provide for his beloved wife and six children. Although considerably older than Partitio, he chose to throw his lot in with the young merchant due to Partitio's chivalrous spirit and leadership.

## Profile

### Appearance
Harry has short black hair. He dresses in typical miner's garb, wearing a brown hat, brown trousers, a white work shirt, and red work gloves.

### Personality
Harry is the most outspoken member of Partitio's posse. Prone to self-deprecating humor, he is occasionally skeptical of Partitio's generosity toward strangers, though his loyalty to the young merchant never wavers. He holds a fierce hatred for Oresrush's exploiters, branding them "lily-livered lowlives" and showing eagerness to stand up to them. Desperation and starvation can test his moral resolve—at the height of Oresrush's poverty, he was driven to steal food, justifying his actions with the pressing need to feed his family—yet he readily heeds Partitio's guidance and makes amends.

## Story

As a longstanding ally in Partitio's circle, Harry helps Partitio defend Oresrush against [Giff](giff.md) and his band of ruffians who attempt to seize control of the town's silver mining revenue. When the silver market crashes and the town falls into grinding poverty under the landowner's ruthless grip, Harry and his fellow miners endure extreme hardship, struggling to find work and scraping by on whatever scraps of food they can find.

The misery eventually causes tempers to flare within the posse, leading a starving Harry to steal a bread crust from Nikki. When Partitio intervenes, Harry remorsefully returns the food, and Partitio splits his own ration among the entire group. Shortly afterward, Giff arrives to mock the starving townsfolk. Pushed to their limits, Partitio rallies Harry and the rest of the gang to confront Giff directly.

Together, the posse overthrows Giff's regime and uncovers evidence proving that the landowner falsified contracts to swindle the miners out of their rightful earnings. With the town's wealth restored, Oresrush begins to flourish once more. Later, Harry and his companions spend their leisure time teasing and supporting Joe, helping him build up the courage to woo the woman of his dreams.
