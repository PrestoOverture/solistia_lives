# Juvah

> *"I have ruled as warden of the island for many years now, but I will die one day, just like the creature that gave us this meat. When that time comes, [Ochette](ochette.md), the island will become your burden to bear."*
> — Master Juvah, to Ochette

**Master Juvah** is the ancient Warden of the Isle, leader of the [Beastlings](../world/beastling.md) of [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md), and the adoptive father and mentor of [Ochette](ochette.md). At over two centuries old, he serves as the primary protector of the island and guides Ochette to become his successor.

## Profile

### Identity

### Appearance
Juvah is a massive, majestic white [lion](../world/felines.md) adorned with distinct blue markings across his back. His forelegs and tail are ornamented with elegant golden braids and metal bracelets, symbolizing his revered station.

### Personality
Revered as the wisest of the Beastlings, Juvah possesses deep spiritual wisdom and a profound reverence for the natural order. He instructs Ochette to trust her instincts and heart rather than relying solely on eyes or ears. As the island's guardian, he takes his responsibilities gravely, teaching Ochette to preserve ecological harmony by hunting and culling creatures that grow too aggressive or numerous. Though his physical vitality has waned with his advanced age, he remains fiercely protective of [Beasting Village](../locations/beasting_village.md), willingly throwing himself into battle against dangerous beasts.

Initially, Juvah harbors deep suspicion and resentment toward human settlers, viewing them as greedy and disruptive to the natural balance of Toto'haha. However, through shared trials and Ochette's example, he eventually lets go of his animosity and embraces peaceful coexistence.

## History

### Backstory
As a wardenbeast, Juvah's lifespan far exceeds that of ordinary beastlings, allowing him to witness generations of hunters come and go. Approximately twenty years prior to the story, he befriended a human, through whom he learned to speak human tongues.

When Ochette was an abandoned infant, Juvah looked upon her and felt a prophetic voice stir in his heart, foretelling that she was destined to save Toto'haha from total annihilation. He took the child in, raising her as his daughter and apprentice. Ochette learned to speak the human tongue fluently by imitating him, and as a cub, she often fell asleep curled in his warm mane. During her early training, she accidentally sheared off a portion of his mane with an arrow, an incident that prompted him to emphasize clearer verbal communication and discipline.

When Ochette was young, Juvah brought her to ancient ruins and presented her with two rare beasts—the *lājackal* [Akalā](akala.md) and the *malamaowl* [Mahina](mahina.md)—directing her to choose one as her lifelong hunting partner. After Ochette selected her companion, the rejected animal was instantly overtaken by a malignant dark aura, attacking the party before fleeing into the wilderness.

### The Night of the Scarlet Moon

#### Chapter 1
A decade later, Juvah confronted human settlers from the adjacent village who encroached upon beastling territory. The skirmish subsided when Ochette returned and repelled their fighter. Later, while sharing a meal on the western bluffs, Juvah informed Ochette that she must prepare to succeed him as guardian of the island, noting ominous disturbances within the forest.

Their discussion was interrupted by [Cohazeh](cohazeh.md), the human village chief, who apologized for the prior incident and requested assistance in rescuing a lost human child stranded within the [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../locations/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md). Though Juvah remained distrustful, Ochette volunteered to enter the tombs. While she was away, a monstrous [Dark Entity](dark_entity.md) assaulted Beasting Village. Juvah held the beast at bay to allow the villagers to evacuate, suffering severe wounds in the process. Ochette arrived in time to slay the entity.

Grateful for their aid, Cohazeh declared a truce in their territorial dispute. Juvah then brought Ochette into the sacred ruins to reveal the ancient prophecy of the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../world/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md)—a cataclysmic return of the Shadow occurring every four centuries. Before resting to recover from his injuries, Juvah commanded Ochette to journey across Solistia to bring the three legendary creatures of Toto'haha back to the island.

#### Chapter 3
Upon Ochette's return with the legendary beasts [Tera](../world/tera.md) and [Glacis](../world/glacis.md), Juvah learned of the death of the third, [Cateracta](../world/cateracta.md), and commended Ochette for adopting Cateracta's orphaned offspring. He led the beastlings to the Summit of Strife to fortify their defenses. When Cohazeh arrived to press land claims, Juvah rebuffed her, warning her of the imminent disaster.

When the moon turned blood-red, hordes of shadow beasts overwhelmed the island, attacking both the beastling settlement and the human village. Putting aside their feud, the two peoples joined forces. Guided through the Verdant Wood and across the Stormy Cape by Tera and Glacis, Juvah and Ochette reached Moonview Cliff to confront the source of the invasion: the companion animal Ochette had left behind a decade prior, now mutated into the colossal [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../world/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md).

After Ochette was thrown from the cliffs during the onslaught, Juvah rushed to the shore, discovering that she had survived and absorbed the empowering radiance of the First Flame. Juvah witnessed Ochette vanquish the beast and offer it compassionate solace in its dying breath, bringing the cataclysm to an end. Moved by the unity forged during the crisis, Juvah formally established lasting peace between the beastlings and the humans, wholeheartedly endorsing Ochette's role as the island's true protector.
