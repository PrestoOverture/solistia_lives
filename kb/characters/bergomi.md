# Bergomi

> *"I accept your challenge. I'm through with you and your kin. I've set my sights on stronger allies... The death of the [Blacksnakes](../world/blacksnakes.md) is nigh!"*
> — Bergomi, before battle

## Overview

- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Location**: [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md)
- **Occupation**: Leader of the [Snowhares](../world/snowhares.md)

**Bergomi** was the leader of the Snowhares, a vicious criminal syndicate operating out of [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md) involved in extortion, narcotics, thievery, and human trafficking.

## Appearance

Bergomi has long dark hair and wears a distinctive green overcoat. In combat, he fights wielding lethal spiked needles fastened to his fists.

## Personality

Bergomi is a self-serving, capricious opportunist who seeks to align himself exclusively with whichever power he perceives as dominant. Seeing the rival [Blacksnakes](../world/blacksnakes.md) as vulnerable, he quickly turned against them. Despite his aggressive posturing, his nerve collapses in defeat; after being beaten in battle, he immediately begs for his life and offers to switch allegiances.

## Story

As leader of the Snowhares, Bergomi controlled the underworld of Winterbloom from the gang's [hideout](../locations/snowhares_den.md). When he attempted to break away from the Blacksnakes and seek alliances with rival factions, [Father](father.md) dispatched [Throné](throne.md) to assassinate him.

Throné infiltrated the den and confronted Bergomi. Despite ingesting combat drugs to enhance his strength, Bergomi was defeated and brought to his knees. When Father subsequently arrived at the den and struck Throné, Bergomi attempted to exploit the chaos to flee. Before he could escape, Father threw a dagger, killing him instantly.
