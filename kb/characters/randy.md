# Randy

> "*You were a trooper too, [Jeyah](jeyah.md).*"
> — Randy, upon returning to Healeaks

**Randy** was a skilled apothecary and veterinary specialist who served as a core member of [Eir's Apothecaries](../world/eirs_apothecaries.md). He was the younger and distinctly more reliable brother of [Andy](andy.md).

## Profile

### Personality and Vocation

Randy was sensible, diligent, and pragmatic, serving as a much-needed counterbalance to his impetuous older brother Andy. While he often quarreled with Andy and openly wished his brother possessed more brains than brawn, the two brothers shared an unbreakable bond. 

Initially, Randy had been reluctant to join Eir's Apothecaries, only enlisting at the persistent urging of Andy and Chief [Castti Florenz](castti.md). Once integrated into the order, however, his dedication deepened until he became even more active and committed to the troupe's humanitarian missions than Andy himself. Alongside fellow apothecary [Elma](elma.md), Randy was a veterinarian who looked after animals, including the order's trusty travel horse, [Jeyah](jeyah.md).

## Biography

### The Tragedy at Healeaks

Randy traveled extensively with Eir's Apothecaries across Solistia to deliver medical care. Upon returning to their headquarters in the mountain settlement of [Healeaks](../locations/abandoned_village.md), the troupe found the village devastated by the machinations of their former comrade [Trousseau](trousseau.md), who was burning a toxic pyre atop the mountain that tainted the atmosphere and produced lethal purple rain.

Confronting the catastrophe, Randy and his brother Andy fought their way into the noxious blaze. In a heroic act of devotion to their comrades and the villagers, both brothers sacrificed their lives attempting to extinguish the accursed flames and halt the spread of the fatal pestilence.
