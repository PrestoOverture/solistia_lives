# Emerald

> "*They made me mine phosphoal 'til I spit blood. So I might as well put it to good use! Heh... Farewell, Professor.*"
> — Emerald, prior to setting the inspection ship ablaze

**Emerald** was an inmate and underworld informant imprisoned on [Frigit Isle](../locations/frigit_isle.md) alongside [Osvald V. Vanstein](osvald.md). A sharp and observant convict, he partnered with Osvald to orchestrate an escape from the frozen penal colony, ultimately laying down his life so that Osvald could regain his freedom.

## Profile

### Appearance

Emerald was a twenty-seven-year-old man with blond hair. As an inmate on Frigit Isle, he wore a simple white shirt and ragged brown prisoner trousers, and carried a concealed dagger.

### Personality

Emerald was cool, calculating, and exceptionally observant. As an informant, he made a habit of watching those around him and piecing together secrets, which allowed him to deduce Osvald's intention to break out of prison. Though hardened by criminal life, Emerald carried deep sorrow and guilt over the loss of his family. When their escape bid deteriorated, he chose self-sacrifice over self-preservation, demonstrating a profound desire for atonement and empathy toward Osvald, whose tragic loss mirrored his own.

## Biography

### Early Life and Imprisonment

Earlier in his life, Emerald was married and had a young daughter. He became entangled with an underworld syndicate, from which he embezzled funds. Although he managed to kill the syndicate enforcers sent after him, the organization retaliated by murdering his wife and child. In the aftermath of the violence, Emerald was arrested and sentenced to hard labor in the freezing phosphoal mines of [Frigit Isle](../locations/frigit_isle.md).

### The Escape from Frigit Isle

In the prison cafeteria following a shift of forced labor, Emerald approached Osvald, having deduced that the scholar was searching for an accomplice. Agreeing to work together, Emerald used his network inside the facility to obtain two vital assets: a schedule ledger for the inspector ship and the key to the iron muzzle forcibly locked onto Osvald's face.

While working in the mines, Emerald quietly confirmed to Osvald that he had secured the items. However, Warden [Davids](davids.md) noticed the exchange and brutally beat both men, warning them against further communication. Emerald later slipped Osvald the ledger, confirming that an inspection vessel was slated to arrive the very next day.

As the escape commenced, Emerald unlocked Osvald's muzzle outside the entrance to an underground tunnel, restoring Osvald's ability to speak and cast magic. Navigating the subterranean passage beneath the prison, they discovered the escape ladder had been dismantled. Davids and a squad of guards ambushed them, having monitored Emerald's activities. After a fierce battle, Osvald and Emerald defeated Davids and his guards. When reinforcements closed in, Osvald blasted a hole through the wall and raised a wall of magical flames to hold back the guards, enabling their escape to the exterior bluffs.

Outside, with the island garrison on high alert, the two fought through sentries and sheltered in a coastal shack. Using his knowledge of ice magic, Osvald crafted ice dinghies that carried them across the freezing water to the opposite cove. There, Emerald asked Osvald about the murder of Osvald's wife and daughter, though Osvald remained silent. Despite Osvald's warnings, Emerald moved to seize the anchored inspection ship to escape by sea.

When the vessel was immediately surrounded and overrun by prison forces, Emerald realized a clean getaway was impossible. Resolving to give Osvald the diversion required to slip away into the sea unnoticed, Emerald ignited the ship's cargo of mined phosphoal. The explosion engulfed the vessel in flames, claiming Emerald's life while allowing Osvald to swim to safety.

### The Washed-Up Letter

Prior to his escape attempt, Emerald penned a letter apologizing for his criminal past and outlining his bid for freedom, sealing it inside a glass bottle and casting it into the sea. The bottle eventually drifted across the ocean and was recovered by a lighthouse keeper at [Lighthouse Island](../locations/lighthouse_island.md). The letter was subsequently delivered to Emerald's mother, [Ruby](ruby.md), in [Timberain](../locations/timberain.md), who upon reading her son's final words found the strength to forgive him.
