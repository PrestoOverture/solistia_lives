# Guide (Throné's Story)

> *"Home. Everyone has a place to return to. Though no one has the freedom to decide where that is. If you want, I can take you there. No ticket necessary."*
> — Guide

The **Guide** is an enigmatic old man who operates the ancient gondola connecting the [Deserted Highroad](../../locations/brightlands/wilds/deserted_highroad.md) to [Lostseed](../../locations/brightlands/towns/lostseed.md). He is one of [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md)'s countless children and the brother of the [Elderly Guard](elderly_guard.md) at [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](../../locations/leaflands/wilds/mothers_garden_orphanage.md).

## Profile

### Appearance
The Guide is an elderly man with a white beard, dressed in a hooded brown robe tied with a pale sash.

### Personality
Operating the solitary gondola to Lostseed, the Guide possesses an eerie and slow, steady demeanor. He offers passage across the chasm while recounting tales of the past and hinting at Lostseed's origins. Accepting his fate without struggle, he believes that every person has a predetermined role to fulfill. He carries out his duty running the gondola without ever resisting his station in life or questioning the horrors transpiring within Lostseed.

## Story

Years before [Throné Anguis](../travelers/throne.md) arrived, the Guide ferried her mother, [Marietta](marietta.md), across the chasm to Lostseed. To soothe her nervous anticipation, he shared the tale he recites to all travelers: the founding legend of Lostseed, in which a prince and princess from warring kingdoms fell in love and eloped with their loyal retainers, building Lostseed as a hidden sanctuary while their homelands tore each other apart in war.

The Guide continued operating the gondola across the years. When Throné journeyed along the Deserted Highroad in pursuit of Claude, she encountered the Guide and his gondola. He ferried her toward Lostseed while recounting the city's ancient founding. Throughout the journey, he offered cryptic remarks about how the turning gears of the gondola seemed to turn back time, observing that all riders are children of the same progenitor if one looks back far enough. While Throné sensed a strange familiarity in him, she could not initially place it. Upon disembarking at the gates of Lostseed, she realized his resemblance to the [Elderly Guard](elderly_guard.md) of Mother's orphanage.

Later, during the side story [Mira and the Elderly Guard's Next Chapter](../../stories/next_chapters/mira_and_the_elderly_guards_next_chapter.md), the Guide meets his brother, the Elderly Guard, and carries him across to Lostseed so he can reunite with [Mira](mira.md).
