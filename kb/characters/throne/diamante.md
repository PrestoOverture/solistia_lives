# Diamante

**Diamante** was a wealthy plutocrat merchant residing in [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md). The master of [Diamante's Estate](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/diamantes_estate.md), he climbed to power through shrewd and ruthless business practices, ultimately becoming the target of an assassination and theft orchestrated by the [Blacksnakes](../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Occupation**: Plutocrat Merchant
- **Location**: [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md)
- **Status**: Deceased

### Appearance
Diamante dressed in a refined gray nobleman's attire topped with a hat bearing a white feather.

### Personality
Diamante built his fortune through an aggressive managerial philosophy, recruiting capable individuals regardless of origin while showing utter ruthlessness toward anyone he deemed incompetent. Despite his high standing in New Delsta society, [Father](father.md) noted that he harbored unsavory appetites, frequently summoning women from the city's pleasure houses to his private estate.

## Story

Diamante amassed immense wealth and influence in the Brightlands, constructing a heavily guarded manse on the outskirts of New Delsta. At some point, he acquired the Darkblood Staff, a relic tied to the dark schemes of the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md).

His possession of the artifact drew the attention of [Arcanette](../temenos/arcanette.md), who contracted the Blacksnakes thieves' guild to steal the staff. When a crew of Blacksnake thieves infiltrated his estate, Diamante's sentries detected the intruders, giving chase through the estate grounds and city waterways. Diamante was subsequently cornered in his study by the rogue thief [Scaracci](scaracci.md). Before Scaracci could act, both Diamante and Scaracci were executed by [Pirro](pirro.md), serving as unwitting victims in a deadly elimination game staged by Father to cull the thieves' syndicate.
