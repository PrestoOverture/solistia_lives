# Masked Boy

The **Masked Boy** is a mysterious child encountered in [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md) during [Throné Anguis](../travelers/throne.md)'s pursuit of [Mother](mother.md). Unbeknownst to both himself and Throné at the time, he is one of the numerous offspring fathered by [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md), making him Throné and [Pirro](pirro.md)'s half-brother.

## Profile

### Appearance
The boy has shoulder-length pale blond hair and green eyes, concealed behind a plain, circular white mask. He dresses in a simple white shirt and black trousers. When unmasked, his facial features bear an uncanny resemblance to Pirro during his childhood.

### Personality
Skittish yet instinctively curious, the boy follows Throné through the streets of Oresrush. Though easily frightened into fleeing when cornered or unmasked, he feels an unexplained, intuitive pull toward Throné that compels him to seek her out.

## Story

While investigating the whereabouts of Mother and [the Slaver](the_slaver.md) in Oresrush, Throné notices a masked child trailing her footsteps. She sneaks behind him and snatches the mask from his face. Startled by the boy's appearance, Throné hesitates, allowing the frightened child to break away and flee.

Following her encounter with the Slaver, Throné crosses paths with the boy once more. Looking closely at his face, she is struck by how closely he resembles Pirro as a young boy and remarks that she feels as though they have met before. The child admits that he followed her because he instinctively sensed she was someone important to him. When Throné asks the boy about his father, he reveals that he has never known who his father is—a circumstance Throné acknowledges they have in common.
