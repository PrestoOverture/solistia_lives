# Scaracci

> *"Take that, ya lousy vermin!"*
> — Scaracci, after defeating enemy thugs

**Scaracci** was a member of the [Blacksnakes](../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md) thieving syndicate in [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md). Unbeknownst to him in life, he was one of the many children sired by [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md), making him a half-brother to [Throné Anguis](throne.md), [Pirro](pirro.md), [Donnie](donnie.md), [Father](father.md), and [Mother](mother.md). Though he affected a belligerent and reckless demeanor, he harbored a deep fear of the guild's discipline.

## Profile

### Appearance
Scaracci had brown hair and wore a black vest over brown trousers, with black bracelets adorning his wrists.

### Personality
Scaracci was a selfish, crude pessimist with a habit of spitting on the ground. His crass humor and unwelcome innuendos constantly alienated his associates, and his nonsensical remarks—such as likening guards to rats and proposing to bait them with cheese—drew irritation from his peers. In combat, his self-serving and craven nature was paramount: he was known to use his own allies as human shields and taunt those around him indiscriminately.

## History

### Life in the Blacksnakes
Born as one of Claude's countless offspring, Scaracci was taken into the Blacksnakes as a child and trained as an assassin and thief. He operated alongside his generational peers Pirro, Donnie, and Throné. During a gathering among the four, they discussed the rumor of an elusive treasure known as "[Alpates](../others/alpates.md)" and placed bets on its existence, with Scaracci wagered against it.

### The Diamante Estate Heist
Scaracci was sent with Pirro, Throné, and Donnie into the waterways beneath New Delsta on an assignment to steal "the mark" from [Diamante's Estate](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/diamantes_estate.md). The heist collapsed into an ambush when city guards surprised them, killing Donnie before he could pick the lock to their escape route. Falling back into the sewers, the trio fought through pursuing guards, concluding that an insider had betrayed the guild and tipped off the city watch. After slaying the guard captain and securing an alternate escape route, Scaracci made a crude remark toward Throné, earning cold contempt from both her and Pirro.

Upon returning above ground, Scaracci sought to deflect scrutiny from himself by reporting to Mother that Throné had bungled the operation, subsequently avoiding the guild's formal gathering.

### Death
The next day, Father dispatched Scaracci to the Diamante estate with orders to execute Diamante and Pirro, who had been accused of being the syndicate's traitor. In truth, Father had engineered the confrontation to pit the assassins against one another to eliminate the weak. Within the estate, Scaracci and Diamante were both slain by Pirro.
