# Donnie

**Donnie** was a thief and skilled lockpicker belonging to the [Blacksnakes](../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md) guild in [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md). Unbeknownst to him, he was one of [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md)'s many children, making him a half-brother to his close companions [Throné Anguis](../travelers/throne.md), [Pirro](pirro.md), and [Scaracci](scaracci.md).

### Appearance
Donnie had short black hair and wore a simple white shirt and thief's attire.

### Personality
Donnie possessed considerable talent as a lockpick, with Scaracci often relying on him to "work his magic" on complex locks. He shared an easygoing camaraderie with his cellmates, debating and betting with them over rumors regarding the legendary treasure "[Alpates](../others/alpates.md)" (voting that the treasure was a myth). Despite the brutal ethos of the Blacksnakes, Donnie felt genuine loyalty and affection for his crew, apologizing in his dying breaths for slowing them down as they fled.

## Story

Raised in the shadow of the Blacksnakes, Donnie worked alongside Throné, Pirro, and Scaracci, unaware that they were all children of the guild's shadowy founder, Claude. 

During an operation to infiltrate [Diamante's Estate](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/diamantes_estate.md) in New Delsta, the crew was discovered by the mansion's sentries. While escaping into the underground sewers, Donnie was gravely wounded. Throné carried and supported him as they fled toward an exit, but guards cornered them in the waterways. Although Throné, Pirro, and Scaracci fought off their pursuers, Donnie's injuries proved fatal, and he succumbed to blood loss in the sewers.
