# Marietta

**Marietta** was a master thief, the former leader of the [Blacksnakes](../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md) guild, and the biological mother of [Throné Anguis](throne.md). Her tragic romantic entanglements with both [Sebastian](father.md) and the syndicate's founder, [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md), set into motion the cycle of betrayal that defined the Blacksnakes.

## Profile

### Appearance
Marietta closely resembles her daughter, possessing similar dark hair, facial features, and earrings. She wore a simple yet elegant black dress.

### Personality
Marietta was a highly skilled and capable assassin and thief. Despite her leadership over the syndicate, she harbored a low sense of self-worth, considering herself an irredeemable outcast from society. She spoke wistfully of intangible treasures, leaving behind a riddle about an inner virtue that no thief could ever steal.

## Story

### Rise and Romance with Sebastian
After joining the Blacksnakes, Marietta rose swiftly through the guild's hierarchy. During a burglary of a wealthy manse, she met [Sebastian](father.md) and took a liking to him. As their partnership deepened into love, Marietta ascended to lead the Blacksnakes. 

One day, the lovers met at an [Abandoned Church](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/abandoned_church.md) in the [Abandoned Traverse](../../locations/crestlands/wilds/abandoned_traverse.md) outside [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md). Marietta confessed her love, revealed she was carrying Sebastian's unborn child, and spoke of abandoning their life of crime to live together in peace. She presented him with a riddle: whether he knew of something within a person that no thief, no matter how great, could ever steal.

### Betrayal and Death
Their dreams of escaping the underworld collapsed when Marietta became involved with [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md), the immortal progenitor and secret master of the Blacksnakes. Terminating her romance with Sebastian, she aborted their unborn child and conceived a daughter with Claude. Marietta named the infant girl Throné and posed the same riddle regarding unstealable inner light to Claude.

Upon Marietta's return to [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md), she was confronted by a heartbroken and vengeful Sebastian, who slit her throat in an alleyway. As she lay bleeding, [Mother](mother.md) arrived to deliver the final blows. With her dying breaths, Marietta prayed that her infant daughter would one day find true happiness.

Following her death, Sebastian preserved her memory through a cherished dagger named Marietta. Throné later visited an unmarked grave at the Abandoned Traverse near the church where Marietta and Sebastian had once exchanged their vows.
