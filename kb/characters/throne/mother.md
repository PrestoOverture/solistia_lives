# Mother

**Mother** is a co-leader of the [Blacksnakes](../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md) thieves' guild and one of the primary antagonists in [Throné Anguis](../travelers/throne.md)'s story alongside [Father](father.md). Valuing strict discipline and obedience above all else, Mother commands the guild's initiates through terror and torment, overseeing the recruitment and indoctrination of young thieves across Solistia.

## Profile

### Appearance
Mother is a middle-aged woman with short blonde hair, blue eye shadow, and an ever-present sadistic smirk. She dresses in an elegant pink gown and carries a whip, which she wields with practiced brutality both as an instrument of discipline and as a lethal weapon in battle.

### Personality
Cruel, tyrannical, and sadistic, Mother believes that the only reliable way to command total submission is through fear and agony. She derives palpable delight from the suffering of her subordinates, regularly whipping the guild's "children" within an inch of their lives for the slightest infraction or sign of defiance. Even obedience brings no true safety under her rule, as she secures compliance by locking heavy iron collars around her charges' necks.

## History

### Rise in the Blacksnakes
Originally a low-ranking member of the Blacksnakes, Mother schemed to seize control of the guild. She manipulated [Sebastian](father.md) by informing him that his lover, the guild's previous leader [Marietta](marietta.md), had aborted their child and consorted with the Blacksnakes' enigmatic master, [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md). After Sebastian slit Marietta's throat in a fit of despair, Mother loomed over her dying rival and mockingly promised to care for Marietta's newborn child, Throné. However, Sebastian took the mantle of "Father" and warned Mother that he would slaughter her if she ever attempted to kill the infant, compelling her under threat of death never to reveal Marietta's true identity to Throné.

### Reign of Terror and the Orphanage
Assuming joint leadership of the Blacksnakes, Mother subjected Throné to persistent torment throughout her upbringing, punishing even the slightest defiance with the lash. To expand the syndicate's ranks, Mother established an front at [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](../../locations/leaflands/wilds/mothers_garden_orphanage.md) near [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md). She recruited vulnerable youths and regularly traveled to [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md) to purchase children from [the Slaver](the_slaver.md). She orchestrated the guild's ruthless succession trials, forcing foster siblings to butcher one another until only the most hardened killers survived.

### Encounter in New Delsta
Following the compromised heist at Lord [Diamante](diamante.md)'s estate, [Scaracci](scaracci.md) accused Throné of causing the contract's failure. Mother viciously whipped Throné until [Pirro](pirro.md) stepped in to shield her, taking the brunt of Mother's blows. Ending the gathering, Mother departed [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md) to acquire more children while Father prepared the fatal contest for their successor.

### Downfall at the Blackbell Tower
When Throné tracked Mother to the orphanage in Wellgrove, Mother pierced her disguise and used a young inductee named [Mira](mira.md) as bait to lure Throné into an ambush at the Blackbell Tower. Despite fighting with savage fury, Mother was mortally wounded. Unrepentant to the end, Mother's only expressed regret was that she had not killed Throné in infancy. When Mother spat on Throné's demand for the key to unlock her collar, Throné ended her life, taking the key and unwittingly sparking a cycle of vengeance in Mira.
