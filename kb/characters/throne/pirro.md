# Pirro

> "*We do what we can to survive in this dump. Living with a collar around our necks from our "parents". I want to rise in the ranks and get my hands on some *real* money… Buy good liquor, good smokes, fall asleep in a big bed. I want to enjoy my life before I bite the dust.*"
> — Pirro

**Pirro** was a prominent member of the [Blacksnakes](../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md) thieves' guild operating in [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md). Though resigned to a grim existence under the thumb of the guild's masters, he harbored dreams of rising through the ranks to secure true wealth and freedom before his death. Unknown to him for most of his life, he was one of the many children sired across Solistia by [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md), making him a half-brother to [Throné Anguis](throne.md), [Scaracci](scaracci.md), [Donnie](donnie.md), [Father](father.md), and [Mother](mother.md).

## Profile

### Appearance

Pirro was a young man with shoulder-length blond hair kept back by a dark leather headband, and sharp, piercing blue-green eyes.

### Personality

Pirro was a calm, capable thief and assassin, fully accustomed to bloodshed and theft as conditions of survival in the slums. Beneath his cynical exterior, however, he was weary of the cheapness of human life within the guild and resented living under the leash of Mother and Father. He longed for the simple pleasures of comfort—fine liquor, good tobacco, and a warm bed—wishing to experience genuine luxury before his violent line of work claimed him.

Among the Blacksnakes, Pirro shared an exceptionally close, protective bond with Throné. He confided his secret ambitions to her and stepped forward to take a brutal whipping in her place when a mission went awry. Even when forced into a lethal duel against her for the succession of the guild, Pirro hesitated to strike the first blow. With his dying breath, he offered no resentment, affirming his respect for her skill and urging her to break free from the guild's collar.

## Biography

### The Failed Heist

During a botched nighttime heist that resulted in the loss of their client, Pirro fled through the [Underground Waterway](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/underground_waterway.md) beneath New Delsta alongside Throné, Scaracci, and a severely injured Donnie. Finding the primary gate locked, they fought off pursuing cutthroats, but Donnie died of his injuries before he could pick the mechanism. Pirro, Throné, and Scaracci fought through a second contingent of pursuers at the alternate grate and scattered into the night to throw off pursuit.

Upon their return to the guild, Scaracci placed the entire blame for the failure on Throné. Mother subjected Throné and Pirro to punishment, during which Pirro gallantly stepped forward to absorb the lashings on Throné's behalf.

### The Succession Trap

The following day, Father approached Pirro with a covert assignment: infiltrate [Diamante's Estate](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/diamantes_estate.md) and eliminate both Count Diamante and Throné. Unbeknownst to them, the guild leaders had arranged the contract as a death match to cull their subordinates and test their prospective successors.

Reaching the manor ahead of Throné, Pirro slaughtered both Scaracci and Count Diamante. When Throné arrived, Pirro explained the reality of the trial they had been trapped in: only one of them could walk away to claim the Blacksnakes' leadership. Though reluctant to kill the woman he cared for, Pirro engaged her in a fierce duel. Throné defeated and mortally wounded him. As he lay dying, Pirro praised her as the finest killer in the nest and encouraged her to escape the Blacksnakes and seize her own freedom.

### Legacy and Phantom

Following his death, Pirro was buried in the graveyard of the [New Delsta: Backstreets](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta_backstreets.md). Later, [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md) etched a chilling summons into Pirro's tombstone for Throné to find: *"I await where it all began."* 

During Throné's final confrontation with Claude in [Lostseed](../../locations/brightlands/towns/lostseed.md), Claude conjured a phantom apparition of Pirro alongside the ghosts of Father and Mother to torment Throné in battle.
