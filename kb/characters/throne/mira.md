# Mira

**Mira** is a young thief raised in [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](../../locations/leaflands/wilds/mothers_garden_orphanage.md) who belongs to the [Blacksnakes](../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md) guild and encounters [Throné Anguis](throne.md).

## Overview

- **Age**: 13
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](../../locations/leaflands/wilds/mothers_garden_orphanage.md)
- **Affiliation**: [Blacksnakes](../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md) (formerly); caretaker of Mother's Garden: Orphanage

> "*... I'll take care of everyone here. And I promise never to raise a hand against them.*"
> — Mira at the orphanage

Mira was taken in as an orphan on a rainy night. Though the memories of her early childhood faded, she never forgot the warmth of the hand that reached out to her—the first time in her life she had ever felt maternal affection. Unknown to her until years later, she was born in [Lostseed](../../locations/brightlands/towns/lostseed.md) as one of the many offspring of [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md).

### Appearance

Mira is a young teenage girl with short brown hair who wears a plain white shirt and a black skirt.

### Personality

Mira is an agile and capable pickpocket whose driving motivation was to earn the approval and love of [Mother](mother.md). Regarding herself as a slow learner who regularly incurred Mother's savage discipline, Mira developed a deep sense of unworthiness. Even while enduring this abusive upbringing, she retained an innate compassion, tending to the whip wounds of fellow orphans. However, her desperate longing for maternal love made her fiercely protective of Mother, blinding her to the woman's monstrous nature.

## History

### Life in Mother's Garden

Orphaned along with her brother, Mira was brought to [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](../../locations/leaflands/wilds/mothers_garden_orphanage.md) to be drilled in thievery and murder for the [Blacksnakes](../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md). 

While pickpocketing in [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md), Mira stole a valuable Wolf Diamond and presented it as tribute to Mother. Pleased by the offering, Mother formally initiated Mira into the syndicate, locking a choker around her neck and leading her deep into the secluded grounds. Mother bound Mira to a bell as bait for Throné, who had come seeking Mother's collar key to win her own freedom.

Throné defeated Mother in combat and severed Mira's ropes. Desperate to save her parental figure, Mira begged Throné to show mercy. When Throné executed Mother regardless, Mira was left heartbroken and consumed by hatred, screaming that she would never forgive Throné.

### The Truth in Lostseed

Driven by vengeance, Mira tracked Throné to the decaying city of [Lostseed](../../locations/brightlands/towns/lostseed.md). By the time she arrived, Throné had already departed after slaying Claude. Exploring the ruins, Mira learned the truth of her lineage: like Throné, she had been sired by Claude before being sent away.

The orphanage's [Elderly Guard](elderly_guard.md), having pursued Mira out of concern for her safety, caught up with her in the forsaken town. Following a tearful discussion about their shared past and trauma, Mira let go of her desire for retribution. Together, they returned to [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](../../locations/leaflands/wilds/mothers_garden_orphanage.md) to raise and protect the remaining children, with Mira swearing never to raise a hand in anger against them.
