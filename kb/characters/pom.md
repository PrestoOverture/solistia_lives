# Pom

> "*Sheesh... I'm starving.*"
> — Pom, lying on the streets of Crackridge

**Pom** is an eighteen-year-old youth from the Wildlands settlement of [Crackridge](../locations/crackridge.md) known far and wide as the town's most voracious gourmand. He lives purely to eat, having sampled nearly every plant, beast, and edible-looking mineral in the region.

## Profile

### Appearance

Pom is a portly young man with short brown hair. He dresses in a simple white collared shirt with blue trousers held up by suspenders.

### Personality

Pom is defined by an insatiable appetite and habitual laziness. He frequently collapses on the road complaining of starvation rather than finding food himself. While he possesses notable cowardly instincts—fleeing at the first hint of combat—he is honest enough to uphold a bargain once his stomach is full.

## Biography

### Saved by Legend

When Pom was just a year old, his nose led him astray: following an enticing aroma, he crawled directly off the edge of a cliff. He was miraculously caught and spared from death by [Tera](../world/tera.md), the Creature of Legend, though Pom grew up with no recollection of the event.

### Guiding Ochette

Years later, [Ochette](ochette.md) arrived in Crackridge seeking the whereabouts of Tera. She discovered Pom sprawled on the ground in the throes of hunger. In exchange for guiding her to the titan's resting place, Pom demanded savory jerky prepared from buttermeep roaming the outskirts.

After feasting on the meat, Pom declared an ambition to become a master hunter and guided Ochette into the [Bed of the Titan](../locations/bed_of_the_titan.md). However, the moment Ochette engaged the massive beast in battle, Pom fled in terror. Following Ochette's victory and Tera's pacification, Pom promptly reconsidered his hunting career, deciding instead that raising livestock was a far safer method to ensure a steady supply of meat.

### Later Life

Pom eventually relocated to the coastal town of [Conning Creek](../locations/conning_creek.md), where he spends his days leisurely deliberating between afternoon tea and supper while savoring preserved meats. During the celebrations following the end of the journey, he is seen happily sharing jerky alongside the beastling elder [Shirlutto](shirlutto.md).
