# "Hikari"

> *"How long do you plan on playing the part of the noble warrior? Submit to your nature, [Hikari](hikari.md). Hehehe... I know you better than anyone, Hikari. You have a thirst that can never be sated. Show them, Hikari. Show them your face stained crimson."*
> — "Hikari"

**"Hikari"** is the malevolent alter ego and dark manifestation of Prince [Hikari Ku](hikari.md). Born of and nourished by the accursed bloodline of Clan [Ku](../world/ku.md) and its link to the [Shadow](../world/shadow.md), this entity lurks within Hikari's subconscious, constantly whispering to him during battle and tempting him to succumb to sadistic frenzy and slaughter his foes.

## Profile

### Appearance
"Hikari" appears physically identical to Hikari Ku, but is distinguished by glowing crimson eyes, an unnatural echoing timbre to his voice, and an aura of dark flames constantly swirling about his form.

### Personality
Sadistic, unrelenting, and bloodthirsty, "Hikari" embodies the violent heritage of Clan Ku. He despises Hikari's noble ideals, compassion, and pursuit of peace, mocking them as foolish delusions. He revels in cruelty and urges Hikari to butcher defeated opponents without mercy. His ultimate goal is to crush Hikari's spirit, eradicate his moral restraint, and claim permanent dominion over his body.

## History

### Awakening
"Hikari" first awakened during Hikari's childhood. When assassins ambushed young Hikari and his mother, [Kura](kura.md), she sustained fatal wounds protecting her son. In the agony of watching his mother die, Hikari's grief triggered the dormant curse of his lineage. Succumbing to the dark blood, "Hikari" seized control and slaughtered the assassins in a fit of demented laughter. He then turned his blade upon Hikari's friend [Jin Mei](jin_mei.md), who narrowly survived the assault. When Hikari regained his senses, he had no memory of the massacre, leaving him terrified of the dark power coursing through his veins.

### Story

Throughout Hikari's journey to reclaim Ku, "Hikari" repeatedly surfaces whenever blood is shed:

- **Chapter 1:** After Hikari defeats his treacherous former friend [Ritsu Mishuyo](ritsu_mishuyo.md) at the [Crest of Heroes](../locations/southern_ku_sands.md), "Hikari" whispers in his mind, demanding Ritsu's death. Hikari steels his resolve, banishes the dark urge, and spares his defeated foe.
- **Chapter 2:** Following the defeat of [Zeto the Butcher](zeto.md) in [Montwise](../locations/montwise.md), "Hikari" manifests visibly before Hikari, commanding him to execute Zeto. Hikari once again rejects the bloodlust, forcing his dark self to recede.
- **Chapter 3:** During a duel with [General Rou](rou.md) in [Wellgrove](../locations/wellgrove.md), "Hikari" asserts stronger control, temporarily possessing Hikari's body and unleashing shadowy power against his opponent. Though Hikari manages to stay his blade before dealing a fatal blow, the internal struggle leaves him incapacitated, allowing Rou and Ritsu to escape.
- **Chapter 5:** Following the defeat of King [Mugen](mugen.md) within [Castle Ku](../locations/castle_ku.md), "Hikari" manifests fully, plunging the throne room into shadow and trapping Hikari in a duel for ultimate control of his soul. "Hikari" attempts to completely subsume Hikari's consciousness. However, the memories and bonds of the companions Hikari made along his journey empower him to break free. Drawing strength from his comrades, Hikari strikes down his shadow self once and for all.

Upon his defeat, "Hikari" dissolves into black smoke, breaking the hold of Clan Ku's curse over Hikari and transforming his inner power from dark shadowy thrall into radiant light.

## Lore & Banter

- **Scent:** In travel banter, [Ochette](ochette.md) detects two distinct scents radiating from Hikari: one warm and gentle, and a second pungent, unpleasant odor emanating from deep within him, which she identifies with the entity lurking inside his soul.
