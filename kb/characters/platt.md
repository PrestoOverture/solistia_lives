# Platt

> "*This town is all we have left.*"
> — Platt

**Platt** is an elderly resident of [Sai: East District](../locations/sai_east_district.md) who, alongside his wife, operates a shelter and makeshift orphanage in the town's impoverished old quarter. He provides care, lodging, and love to abandoned youth, children whose parents are away working, and orphans with nowhere else to turn.

## Profile

### Appearance

Platt is a sixty-five-year-old man with a bald head and a full white beard.

### Personality

Kind-hearted, gentle, and patient, Platt loves every child under his roof as if they were his own offspring. Despite the financial strain of managing an ever-growing household on meager resources, he remains steadfastly committed to protecting his charges and preserving the tight-knit community of Sai's old quarter. He feels immense pride and joy watching [Laila](laila.md) overcome her anger and develop into a spirited dancer and source of hope for the district.

## Biography

### The Orphanage in Sai

In the impoverished East District of [Sai](../locations/sai.md), Platt and his wife established a home for neglected, orphaned, and displaced children. With the child population steadily increasing, running the home grew increasingly expensive, but the household managed to endure through anonymous donations and the generosity of local townspeople. A neighborhood fruit merchant regularly supplies Platt and the orphans with extra produce free of charge, and the local traveling apothecary provides them medicine at discounted rates.

### Defending the East District

During [Agnea Bristarni](agnea.md)'s visit to Sai, Platt and his wife were chasing down the rebellious young orphan [Laila](laila.md), who had defaced the town square's statue. Recognizing a familiar grace in Agnea, the couple invited the traveling dancer to their home, where they warmly discovered that Agnea was the daughter of [Cuani Bristarni](cuani_bristarni.md), a dancer they fondly remembered.

Their gathering was abruptly interrupted by [Veronica](veronica.md), who stormed the orphanage demanding that Platt and the orphans vacate the premises. Platt held his ground, weary of the repeated harassment and threats of eviction. After Laila boldly scolded the intruder, Veronica withdrew, warning that it would be their final chance to leave willingly.

When the superstar dancer [Dolcinaea Luciel](dolcinaea_luciel.md) arrived to evict the residents by force to demolish the slums and construct a vanity estate in her own honor, Agnea stood in defense of the district. Inspired by Agnea, Laila and the townspeople united, driving Dolcinaea and Veronica away. Platt, his wife, and Laila saw Agnea off with deep gratitude, confident that the community had grown stronger and that the orphanage would endure.
