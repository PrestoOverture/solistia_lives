# King of Sa

The **King of Sa** was the ruler of the desert nation of [Sa](../world/sa.md) in [Hinoeuma](../locations/hinoeuma.md). He was overthrown and executed by [Mugen](mugen.md) following the invasion and conquest of his homeland by the realm of [Ku](../world/ku.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Title**: King
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Realm**: [Sa](../world/sa.md)

### Appearance
The King of Sa dressed in regal desert attire, wearing a long dark robe paired with an ornate, tall ceremonial headdress befitting his sovereign status.

### Personality
Little is known of the king's personal temperament. Having governed his realm under the protection of a longstanding alliance, he failed to anticipate the ruthless treachery and militaristic ambitions of Ku's new regime under Mugen.

## Story

During the reign of King [Jigo Ku](jigo_ku.md), the realm of Ku maintained a peace treaty with Sa, guaranteeing the smaller nation military defense in exchange for a portion of its material resources.

Following Jigo's death, [Mugen](mugen.md) seized the throne of Ku and promptly violated the non-aggression pact, launching a full-scale military invasion of Sa. Although his advisor [Ageha](ageha.md) suggested enslaving and conscripting Sa's population into Ku's army, Mugen rejected the proposal, ordering the systematic slaughter of the conquered populace to eradicate any possibility of future rebellion.

The King of Sa was captured alive by Ku's soldiers and dragged in chains to [Castle Ku](../locations/castle_ku.md), where Mugen summarily executed him within the imperial throne room.
