# Lucian

**Lucian** was a respected theologian and scholar based in [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md) in *Octopath Traveler II*. His discoveries concerning forbidden rites play a critical role in [Temenos Mistral](temenos.md)'s investigation into the shadow threatening Solistia.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Occupation**: Theologian and Scholar
- **Location**: [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md)

## Biography

Throughout his travels and scholarly research, Lucian authored numerous treatises on religious and occult history, culminating in a significant volume entitled *The Final Night*. During his field research, he uncovered records from the people of [Crackridge](../locations/crackridge.md) containing the specific instructions for the ancient dark ritual intended to resurrect the malevolent entity [Vide](../world/vide.md).

Recognizing the immense danger of this forbidden knowledge, Lucian traveled to the cathedral city of [Flamechurch](../locations/flamechurch.md) to personally warn [Pontiff Jörg](jorg.md) of what he had found—a meeting witnessed by the church inquisitor [Temenos Mistral](temenos.md). Following this audience, Lucian returned to his laboratory in Canalbrine to continue his investigations.

Shortly after Pontiff Jörg was assassinated, Lucian was found dead in his home. Temenos investigated the crime scene and deduced that Lucian had been murdered by [Vados](vados.md) as part of a series of ritualistic sacrifices designed to bring about the Night of the Sorrow and instigate a dark curse. Examining Lucian's body and recovered field notes allowed Temenos and the Sacred Guard knight [Crick Wellsley](crick_wellsley.md) to anticipate the perpetrators' next victim: the dancer [Hermes](hermes.md).
