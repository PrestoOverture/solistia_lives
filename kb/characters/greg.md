# Greg

**Greg** is the nephew of Lady [Rosa](rosa.md) Glenville of [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md) and the cousin of [Melia](melia.md). He acts as an antagonist in [Castti Florenz](castti.md)'s Winterbloom storyline before attempting to reform.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 20
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md)
- **Occupation**: Noble of House Glenville
- **Relatives**:
  - [Rosa](rosa.md) Glenville (aunt, deceased)
  - [Melia](melia.md) (cousin)

### Appearance
Greg wears a red coat trimmed with gold and sports a pompous hairstyle that [Throné Anguis](throne.md) derided as "the most pretentious hairstyle."

### Personality
Greg is selfish, presumptuous, and cowardly, yet insists his ambitions are for the good of Winterbloom. As the eldest son of an impoverished noble line with a long heritage, he grew frustrated with his aunt Rosa's traditional methods, dreaming of modernizing Winterbloom into a bustling commercial hub akin to [Clockbank](../locations/clockbank.md). Lacking both patience and moral scruples, he went so far as to kidnap his young cousin and hire thieves to force Castti out of town.

When his hired thugs turn on him, Greg breaks down into tears, exposing his fragile resolve. Following Castti's stern reprimand, Greg reflects on his actions, confesses his crime to Rosa, and apologizes to Melia. Despite his reformation, his persistent laziness and lack of confidence cause him to shirk hard administrative work, leaving Melia to carry most of the governing burden. Deep down, he harbors an earnest desire to make his own mother proud.

## Story

Believing himself next in line to inherit the Glenville estate until Melia comes of age, Greg visits his bedridden aunt Rosa to urge her to step down. When Melia and Castti arrive, Melia scolds him and escorts him outside. Seizing the opportunity, Greg knocks Melia unconscious, abducts her, and leaves a ransom note demanding Castti meet him alone at the tavern in [Winterbloom: Thieves' Quarter](../locations/winterbloom_thieves_quarter.md).

At the tavern, Greg appears flanked by three hired bandits from the [Snowhares](../world/snowhares.md), ordering Castti to cease administering medicine to Rosa and leave Winterbloom immediately. However, bandit leader [Plukk](plukk.md) shoves Greg aside and attacks Castti. After Castti subdues the thugs, a distraught Greg breaks down. Castti rebukes his cowardice and orders him to confess to his aunt.

Greg returns to the manse, confesses his wrongdoing to Rosa, and asks Melia for forgiveness. On Melia's twelfth birthday, Rosa grants her title and lands jointly to Melia and Greg before passing away.

Following Rosa's death, Melia governs House Glenville diligently, while Greg groans over stacks of paperwork. When Plukk's gang returns to the manse, Greg suggests feigning ignorance and arresting them, but is dismissed by [Lily](lily_octopath_traveler_ii.md) and Melia, who graciously hires the reformed thieves as estate gardeners. During the epilogue, Greg is seen applying for employment with [Thurston](thurston.md), seeking to build an honest career.
