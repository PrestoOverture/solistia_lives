# Sesque

> *"You stay on land long enough, the waves start to call..."*
> — Sesque, in [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md)

> *"I'd love to make Master Navigator one of these days..."*
> — Sesque, in [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md)

**Sesque** is a 22-year-old sailor residing in [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md) and the protective older brother of [Senah](senah.md). He works aboard an ocean liner voyaging to the eastern continent. Good-natured but somewhat short-tempered, he shares a tight bond with his younger brother and dreams of one day making Master Navigator, sailing the seas with Senah as his ship's cook.

## Profile

### Appearance
Sesque has light brown hair and wears a sailor's cap.

### Personality
Sesque is an honest, hardworking sailor whose fierce devotion to his brother occasionally sparks his hot temper. He reacts aggressively when he believes Senah is in danger, yet he possesses the humility to admit his mistakes, apologize sincerely, and express heartfelt gratitude when shown he was in the wrong.

## History

### The Water Contamination in Canalbrine
When Canalbrine's water supply becomes poisoned, Sesque's younger brother Senah collapses in the town streets. [Castti Florenz](castti.md) and [Malaya](malaya.md) bring the unconscious boy home, where Castti correctly identifies the illness as poisoning rather than sun fever and administers a dose of lycanthe antipyretic.

Just as the medicine begins to take effect, Sesque returns home. Seeing an unknown apothecary leaning over his stricken brother, Sesque panics, accuses Castti of poisoning Senah, and drives her from the house in a fury.

Once Castti cleanses the town's reservoir and Senah regains full health, Sesque realizes that Castti's swift intervention saved his brother's life. Deeply remorseful, he apologizes to Castti for his wrongful accusations and thanks her, after which the two brothers resume their peaceful life together in Canalbrine.
