# Karma

**Karma**, initially encountered as the **Sword Hunter**, is a cutthroat swordsman and thief who serves as the adversary in the side story "[Sword Hunter in the Decaying Temple](../stories/sword_hunter_in_the_decaying_temple.md)".

## Profile

### Identity
- **Gender**: Male
- **Occupation**: Thief, rogue swordsman
- **Location**: [Decaying Temple](../locations/decaying_temple.md)

### Personality
Karma is a ruthless and bloodthirsty cutthroat who steals precious blades from the defenseless. When challenged, he openly revels in violence, declaring that his sword thirsts for human blood.

## Story

Prior to being tracked down, Karma ambushed a young boy and stole his cherished Keepsake Sword. To evade retribution, the thief retreated into the remote depths of the [Decaying Temple](../locations/decaying_temple.md), a sacred ruin whose ancient rules restrict entry to only a single challenger at a time.

When a traveler ventures alone into the temple to recover the heirloom, Karma brandishes his stolen blade, boasting that the weapon demands blood before attacking. Following a fierce duel, Karma is vanquished, allowing the stolen Keepsake Sword to be reclaimed and returned to its rightful owner.
