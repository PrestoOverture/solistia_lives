# Temenos Mistral

> *"That, you must find out for yourself, though I must warn you: there are few things worthy of our faith."*
> — Temenos, to [Crick](crick_wellsley.md)

## Overview

- **Age**: 30
- **Height**: 178 cm (5'10")
- **Origin / Location**: [Flamechurch](../locations/flamechurch.md), [Crestlands](../locations/crestlands.md)
- **Occupation**: Inquisitor, Cleric of the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../world/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md)
- **Adoptive Family**: [Pontiff Jörg](jorg.md) (adoptive father, deceased), [Roi](roi.md) (adoptive brother, deceased)

**Temenos Mistral** is a cleric and inquisitor in the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../world/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) based in [Flamechurch](../locations/flamechurch.md). A quiet, sharp-witted investigator who questions even the [gods](../world/gods.md), he sets out to uncover the truth behind the murder of Pontiff Jörg and the sinister conspiracies threatening Solistia.

## Appearance

Temenos is a thirty-year-old man with a baby face, pale complexion, blue eyes, and silver-white hair. He stands at 178 cm (5'10"). He wears a long dark turquoise robe accented with gold embroidery resembling fire along the hem, paired with matching trousers and black shoes. Over his robes, he wears a white buttoned-up cape fastened with wooden buttons. He is usually seen carrying the golden Staff of Judgment. While his bearing is typically gentle and easygoing, his gaze sharpens when pursuing a lead or confronting a foe. [Ochette](ochette.md) has remarked that he has "big, adorable eyes."

## Personality

Temenos is calm, composed, and outwardly indolent, yet possessed of razor-sharp intelligence and an unyielding commitment to uncovering the truth. He maintains a deep distrust of authority figures, with the sole exception of [Pontiff Jörg](jorg.md), which fuels frequent tension with the [Sanctum Knights](../world/sacred_guard.md). The untimely death of his adoptive brother and predecessor, [Roi](roi.md), shook his faith in the [Sacred Flame](../world/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md), leaving him willing to openly criticize the gods—frequently alarming the devout young knight [Crick](crick_wellsley.md). Guided by his personal motto, "doubt is what I do," Temenos follows evidence wherever it leads, regardless of taboo or church doctrine.

He is known for a dry, snarky wit, frequently making sly comments, puncturing others' naivety, and teasing his companions (especially Crick) mercilessly. His approach to ethics is flexible: he does not hesitate to coerce answers from suspects or tell white lies if it aids an investigation. While his sharp tongue can occasionally seem mean-spirited, Temenos sees his verbal jabs as a practical way to help others recognize their blind spots.

Temenos rarely loses his composure, maintaining an aloof, rational perspective during even the most gruesome investigations. His cool exterior falters only in moments of profound personal loss, such as following Crick's murder. Even in grief, he channels his anger into purposeful deduction, determined to honor the dying wishes of Roi and Crick by unmasking the rot within the church. Outside of investigations, he admits that physical labor has never agreed with him and prefers resting whenever possible. According to [Osvald](osvald.md), Temenos also has an "elegant way of eating," leaving no scraps behind.

## Relationships

- **[Pontiff Jörg](jorg.md)**: Temenos's adoptive father and mentor. Jörg took in both Temenos and Roi as foundlings and appointed them as inquisitors, granting Temenos the authority to investigate church affairs. Temenos viewed Jörg as a true friend and one of the few individuals who had unconditional faith in him.
- **[Roi](roi.md)**: Temenos's adoptive brother and predecessor as Inquisitor. When Roi fell gravely ill in their youth, Temenos prayed for his recovery. Five years before the story, Roi uncovered church secrets and the Darkblood Bow before being silenced, leaving Temenos a warning that sparked his enduring skepticism.
- **[Crick Wellsley](crick_wellsley.md)**: An earnest young Sanctum Knight (age 22, 182 cm) whom Temenos affectionately dubs his "little lamb." Crick acts as a steadfast companion and moral foil throughout Temenos's investigation into the Pontiff's murder. Though Temenos mercilessly teases Crick's earnest devotion, the two forge a deep philosophical bond, and Crick's eventual death deeply shakes Temenos.
- **Sister [Mindt](arcanette.md)**: A sister of the Order of the Sacred Flame at the church in Flamechurch, where Temenos tells stories of the Sacred Flame to local children. She frequently chastises him for his theatrics and easygoing attitude. She is later revealed to be Arcanette, an operative of the Moonshade Order.
- **[Throné Anguis](throne.md)**: A fellow traveler whose cynicism, razor-sharp instincts, and curiosity Temenos greatly appreciates. He notes that had she followed his faith he would have been forced to judge her crimes; instead, they become investigative partners, with Temenos referring to her as his "assistant" while she calls him "detective."

## History

### Prior to Story

Temenos and [Roi](roi.md) were foundling orphans adopted together by [Pontiff Jörg](jorg.md). During their youth, Roi fell severely ill, recovering after Temenos prayed over him. Both young men pursued careers within the clergy and were appointed Inquisitors by the Pontiff. When Temenos first assumed the role of Inquisitor, scriveners frequently approached him seeking scandalous gossip, earning his deep disdain.

Five years prior to the events of the story, Roi visited Temenos's home late at night. Roi confided that among the clergy, he trusted only Temenos and the Pontiff. Roi revealed the Darkblood Bow, which he had discovered while inspecting the church interior alongside the Pontiff. Warning Temenos of the church's dark secrets, Roi explained that he intended to hide the weapon and urged Temenos to carry on his mission if he failed to return. Roi subsequently vanished and was killed, leaving Temenos with deep doubts regarding the church and the Sacred Flame.

### The Murder of Pontiff Jörg (Chapter 1)

Temenos begins his day in [Flamechurch](../locations/flamechurch.md) recounting the scripture of [Aelfric](../world/aelfric.md)'s victory against the dark god [Vide](../world/vide.md) to a group of village children. Despite his theatrical presentation, he forgets the concluding lines, drawing laughter from the children and exasperation from Sister [Mindt](arcanette.md). Soon after, [Pontiff Jörg](jorg.md) summons Temenos to the [cathedral](../locations/flamechurch_cathedral.md). Outside, Temenos observes the Pontiff in serious conversation with the theologian [Lucian](lucian.md).

En route to the cathedral, Temenos encounters a religious insurgent preaching heresy to a crowd. When the insurgent attempts to take Temenos hostage, a young Sanctum Knight named [Crick Wellsley](crick_wellsley.md) intervenes, though Temenos effortlessly incapacitates the heretic himself. Crick recognizes Temenos as the church's Inquisitor by his Staff of Judgment and explains that he too is bound for an audience with the Pontiff.

Arriving at the cathedral, the two find the doors mysteriously barred. Temenos gathers intelligence from townspeople—including [Vados the Architect](vados.md)—to uncover a secret passage inside. Within the cathedral, they discover the stained glass shattered and Pontiff Jörg murdered, bearing massive claw wounds across his back. An [arcane beast](felvarg.md) ambushes them, but Temenos and Crick successfully slay the creature.

While Crick mourns the tragedy, Temenos's deductive eye spots signs of premeditated murder rather than a random beast attack. Investigating the pulpit, Temenos discovers a Bible turned open to a specific scripture passage. Before they can analyze the scene further, [Deputy Cubaryi](cubaryi.md) of the Sacred Guard arrives, seizing control of the investigation and ordering them to leave without touching anything. However, Temenos secretly slips the book into his robes.

Following Jörg's funeral, Temenos and Crick meet in the church library. Inside the confiscated book, Temenos discovers a hidden slip inscribed with the words: *"And soon, night shall fall."* Recognizing this as an intentional clue left by the Pontiff, Temenos resolves to track down Lucian, the last person seen with Jörg. With Crick reassigned elsewhere, Temenos bids farewell to Mindt and departs Flamechurch to unravel the conspiracy.

### Murders in Canalbrine (Chapter 2)

Temenos travels to [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md) in search of Lucian. Upon arrival, he encounters a crowd gathered near the local church, where the town apothecary has been murdered. Temenos crosses paths with Crick, who has been stationed in the port town. Temenos suspects a connection to the Pontiff's death, noting that Lucian authored a treatise entitled *The Final Night*, and learns from an informant that the apothecary met with Lucian shortly before dying.

The duo heads to Lucian's residence, where silence greets their knocks. Temenos convinces Crick to breach the door, only to find Lucian already dead. Searching Lucian's notes, Temenos finds records concerning Canalbrine's Keepers of the Flame and another occurrence of the phrase *"And soon, night shall fall."* He also discovers a liturgical text outlining the traditional order in which the eight deities are invoked in prayer: [Bifelgan](../world/bifelgan.md), [Aeber](../world/aeber.md), [Draefendi](../world/draefendi.md), [Brand](../world/brand.md), [Sealticge](../world/sealticge.md), [Alephan](../world/alephan.md), [Dohter](../world/dohter.md), and Aelfric. A memo warns that reciting this prayer in reverse order invites a curse.

Reviewing the victims, Temenos deduces that the killer is assassinating individuals representing each deity in reverse sequence: Jörg represented Aelfric, the apothecary represented Dohter, and Lucian represented Alephan. The next target in the cycle represents Sealticge—the patron deity of dancers.

Recalling a street hawker promoting the performance of [Hermes](hermes.md) the dancer, Temenos and Crick rush to the tavern. Temenos exposes [Vados the Architect](vados.md) as the culprit just as Vados attempts to murder Hermes in the name of Vide, "the one true god." Crick intercepts the blow, and Vados flees to a [Sacred Guard Ship](../locations/sacred_guard_ship.md) in the harbor. Temenos and Crick corner Vados on board and defeat him. Before being subdued, Vados mutters: *"Surrender yourself not unto silent dusk. For the light shall fade."* [Captain Kaldena](kaldena.md) arrives and takes Vados into Sacred Guard custody.

Crick prepares to escort Vados to the Sacred Guard headquarters in [Stormhail](../locations/stormhail.md). Temenos notes two separate investigative threads: interrogating Vados in Stormhail, or tracing Lucian's notes toward the [Fellsun Ruins](../locations/fellsun_ruins.md) near [Crackridge](../locations/crackridge.md).

### The Kal Ruins (Chapter 3: Crackridge Route)

Temenos travels to [Crackridge](../locations/crackridge.md), where the locals treat him with hostility and refuse to speak about the [Fellsun Ruins](../locations/fellsun_ruins.md). He notices several villagers wearing crescent moon necklaces. That night at the inn, he reflects on Roi's warnings. The next morning, a woman named [Reiza](reiza.md) approaches him, asking for an escort along the [Abandoned Road](../locations/abandoned_road.md).

Along the path, Reiza attempts to flirt with him, but Temenos rebuffs her, pointing out that her crescent necklace exposes her as a village local. Reiza attacks, but Temenos deflects her assault. She confesses that the villagers are members of the [Moonshade Order](../world/moonshade_order.md). Sensing that Reiza is conflicted and seeks atonement, Temenos accepts her offer to guide him to the ruins, which she identifies as the "Kal Ruins," constructed by the ancient Kal tribe, the original Keepers of the Flame.

Inside the ruins, Temenos discovers human skeletons strewn along the corridors—the remains of the slaughtered Kal people. At the heart of the ruins, he finds a mural depicting the Kal opposing the Archmage [D'arqest](darqest.md), alongside a warning of a terrible darkness sealed within the [Nameless Village](../locations/nameless_village.md). A recovered note mentions the *Book of Night* and the verse *"Surrender yourself not unto silent dusk. For the light shall fade,"* noting that missing pages from the tome were retrieved by Vados.

Returning to the entrance, Temenos questions Reiza about the Moonshade Order's leadership. In reply, she recites their doctrine: *"Surrender yourself not unto silent dusk. For the light shall fade. And soon, night shall fall."* Realizing that Jörg intended to expose the deep link between the Moonshade Order and the *Book of Night*, Temenos presses onward.

### Shadows in the Sacred Guard (Chapter 3: Stormhail Route)

Arriving at the Sacred Guard headquarters in [Stormhail](../locations/stormhail.md) following a correspondence from Mindt, Temenos is reunited with Crick. Inside, they discover that Vados's arrival was never recorded in the official registry. Temenos deduces that high-ranking members of the Sacred Guard erased Vados from the records, pointing to treason within the order.

While searching the grounds, Temenos discovers a blood trail and is ambushed by a hooded figure, whom Crick fends off before the assailant escapes. Following the trail, they discover Vados's corpse. Shaken by the undeniable corruption in his order, Crick experiences a profound crisis of faith. Temenos consoles him by comparing Crick's integrity to that of Roi, explaining how Roi's final words set him on his quest. Bidding Crick good night, Temenos retires to the inn.

The next morning, Temenos finds a horror-struck crowd outside headquarters: Crick has been murdered. Clutched in the young knight's hand is a bloodstained scrap reading *"Surrender yourself not unto silent dusk. For the light shall fade."* Using his deductive vision, Temenos tracks the spiritual remnants of Crick's final steps to a concealed chamber lined with forbidden tomes.

There, [Deputy Cubaryi](cubaryi.md) confronts him, revealing that [Captain Kaldena](kaldena.md) murdered Crick and orchestrated the entire conspiracy. Tasked with silencing Temenos, Cubaryi attacks him. Temenos defeats the deputy, but she dies refusing to disclose Kaldena's ultimate design. Afterward, Temenos attends Crick's memorial, vowing to honor his fallen friend and bring the truth to light.

### The Rite of the Shadow (Chapter 4)

Temenos pursues Kaldena to the [Nameless Village](../locations/nameless_village.md) on the island of [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md). Using clues found in the Kal Ruins, he enters the treacherous [Wandering Wood](../locations/wandering_wood.md). He enlists the aid of a beastling named [Shirlutto](shirlutto.md), who guides him through the forest maze and reveals the origins of the beastlings and the perilous nature of the dark rite Kaldena plans to enact.

Within the cavern of [Rifted Rock](../locations/rifted_rock.md), Temenos discovers Sacred Guard knights either massacred by their comrades or warped into shadow monstrosities. A dying knight, [Ort](ort.md), begs Temenos to stop their captain. Deep within the cavern, Temenos confronts Kaldena, deducing that she is the sole survivor of the Kal massacre who turned to the dark in vengeance.

Kaldena invites Temenos to join her cause, but he steadfastly refuses, drawing the Staff of Judgment to avenge Crick and uphold his duty. Kaldena attempts to channel the raw power of the Shadow, but the dark energy consumes her, transforming her into a monstrous fiend. Temenos slays the beast.

In the aftermath, Temenos makes the truth public, causing the Sacred Guard to be stripped of their authority. However, deeper questions linger: the ultimate purpose of the Moonshade Order and the true essence of the Shadow remain veiled. Bidding a quiet farewell to the memories of Crick and Roi, Temenos resumes his journey.

### Crossed Paths: Alpates and the Cloudy Mirror

- **Flamechurch (Part 1)**: Temenos teams up with [Throné Anguis](throne.md) in Flamechurch to investigate rumors of a sacred treasure known as the "[Alpates](alpates.md)" hidden in the cathedral. Coercing a former carpenter, they find a secret door behind a bookshelf in the Pontiff's chambers. The passage leads to an upper loft where they discover Alpates is not a relic, but a young woman from a clan of the same name whom Jörg had sheltered in secret. Sensing sacred flames within both travelers, Alpates gives them a fragment of a cloudy mirror and asks if they hope for the dawn before slipping away into the night.
- **Conning Creek (Part 2)**: In [Conning Creek](../locations/conning_creek.md), Temenos and Throné investigate the murder of Alpates. On her body, they discover a map pointing to the [Cavern of the Moon and Sun](../locations/cavern_of_the_moon_and_sun.md) inscribed with the phrase *"Where day meets night."* At the cavern, Temenos solves an environmental riddle, locating a stone illuminated simultaneously by the sun and moon. There they retrieve a note reading *"To those who hope for the dawn"* and a second fragment that locks with their mirror piece. Temenos relates the ancient legend of Alpates, an emissary left behind by [Aelfric the Flamebringer](../world/aelfric.md) whose body was forged into a mirror capable of dispelling shadow.

### The Journey for the Dawn

When the travelers gather to relight the Sacred Flames and confront the eternal night, Temenos discovers that Sister [Mindt](arcanette.md) of Flamechurch was in truth Arcanette, a high-ranking Moonshade conspirator who orchestrated many of the tragedies behind the scenes. Temenos interrogates her before she reveals her true form and battles the party.

Facing the dark god [Vide](../world/vide.md) at the culmination of the crisis, Temenos declares that he never expected to pass judgment on a god, but will show no mercy in his verdict. Following Vide's defeat and the return of the dawn, Temenos bids farewell to his companions during the epilogue, setting off once more to investigate unsolved truths across the world.

## Lore & Banter

- **The Seventh Source**: When discussing magical theory with [Osvald](osvald.md), Temenos cheekily posits that the [seventh source](../world/one_true_magic.md) of magic is sheep: the more sheep one counts, the more rest one attains, thereby granting theoretically infinite power.
- **Physical Labor**: Temenos openly laments that physical toil has never agreed with him, preferring mental deduction and restful leisure whenever possible.
- **Gossip and Scriveners**: Early in his career as an Inquisitor, scriveners relentlessly hounded him hoping for church gossip, which earned his lasting disgust.
- **The Children of Flamechurch**: In Flamechurch, the village children view Temenos as both an object of adoration and the butt of their jokes. One young girl with braids who frequents his scripture readings secretly carries a torch for him.
