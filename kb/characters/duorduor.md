# Duorduor

The **Duorduor** is a monstrous, aggressive giant [boar](../world/ungulates.md) inhabiting the [Veil of Trees](../locations/veil_of_trees.md) forest surrounding [Cropdale](../locations/cropdale.md) in the [Leaflands](../locations/leaflands.md).

## Profile

### Nature and Behavior
The duorduor is a massive wild beast known for its aggressive charges and insatiable appetite. Attracted by the scent of food, it is notorious among the villagers of Cropdale for straying into civilized areas in search of berries, nuts, and crops.

## Story

Years prior to the events of [Agnea Bristarni](agnea.md)'s journey, a rampaging duorduor attacked Cropdale, requiring the combined efforts of the entire village to drive the beast back into the woods.

During the preparations for Cropdale's annual village festival, [Gus](gus.md) discovers fresh duorduor hoofprints near the settlement, realizing the creature has been lured toward the [Festival Grounds](../locations/festival_grounds.md) by the aroma of cooking food. When Agnea's younger sister [Pala Bristarni](pala_bristarni.md) fails to return from foraging for raspberries in the forest, Agnea and Gus enter the [Veil of Trees](../locations/veil_of_trees.md) to find her.

The pair confront the hungry beast within the woods, fighting it off to prevent it from reaching the town or harming Pala. After driving the boar away, Agnea kindly promises to bring it nuts in the future to keep it satisfied.
