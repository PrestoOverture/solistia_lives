# Floyd

> *"That's right! Prosperity for all, and all shall benefit! That's... my dream."*  
> — Floyd

**Floyd** is a character in *Octopath Traveler II*. A brilliant young engineer and inventor employed by the [Roque Company](../world/roque_company.md), he is the creator who perfected the Neo Steam Engine.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 21
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Origin**: A declining coal mining town
- **Location**: [Clockbank](../locations/clockbank.md) (initially), [Roque Island](../locations/roque_island.md) (post-story)
- **Occupation**: Engineer, Inventor ([Roque Company](../world/roque_company.md))
- **Relatives**: Grandfather (inventor of the original steam engine), father (theorist of steam mechanics)

### Appearance
Floyd has light brown hair and wears brown overalls suited for mechanical workshop labor.

### Personality
Floyd is an earnest, humble, and fiercely dedicated young man. He dislikes being praised as a prodigy or genius, maintaining that he is simply standing on the shoulders of giants: his grandfather built the original steam engine, and his father developed the theoretical principles behind it. When absorbed in engineering problems, Floyd becomes utterly oblivious to his surroundings—routinely walking into people without looking and working to such exhaustion that he neglects sleep and meals.

## History

### Background
Floyd and his family of inventors originated from a coal mining settlement long past its prime. Eight years prior to current events, his master's inventions were funded by the industrialist [Roque](roque_brilliante.md), leading Floyd to enter the service of the [Roque Company](../world/roque_company.md) to work on advanced mechanical engineering.

### Partitio's Tale
Floyd first crosses paths with [Partitio](partitio.md) in [Clockbank](../locations/clockbank.md), literally bumping into him while engrossed in mental calculations. After Partitio inquires into his absentmindedness, Floyd reveals his ambition: designing a simplified, mass-producible steam engine that could benefit everyone, rather than just the wealthy elite. The project was stalled by the lack of a material combining low cost with sufficient durability. Partitio solves this impasse by procuring clockite, enabling Floyd to successfully build and complete the Neo Steam Engine.

Following the events of Partitio's journey, Floyd relocates to the company facilities on [Roque Island](../locations/roque_island.md), continuing his development work. His obsessive work habits and disregard for his own health eventually force [Thurston](thurston.md) to seek outside assistance to ensure his wellbeing during the side story [Floyd and Thurston's Next Chapter](../stories/floyd_and_thurstons_next_chapter.md). Floyd also invents Floyd's Neo Bow.

During the epilogue, Floyd is seen admiring the astronomical telescope crafted by [Regulus](regulus.md).
