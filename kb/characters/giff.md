# Giff

> *"Another hard day of work. I've gone honest, I tell you."*  
> — Giff, working in [Oresrush](../locations/oresrush.md)

**Giff** is a character in *Octopath Traveler II*. A former gang leader who operated as an extortionist and tax collector in [Oresrush](../locations/oresrush.md), he was defeated by [Partitio](partitio.md) and subsequently put to work by [Papp](papp_yellowil.md) to pay off his debts.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 27
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Oresrush](../locations/oresrush.md)
- **Occupation**: Former thug leader and enforcer, later store assistant and laborer under Papp

### Appearance
Giff is a portly man with upswept blond hair. During his time as a gang leader, he dressed in a black coat with a red scarf. After being put to work as an honest laborer, he trades this attire for a plain white work shirt and overalls.

### Personality
Originally a cruel, opportunistic bully, Giff preyed upon the townspeople of Oresrush and enriched himself through intimidation and theft. Despite posturing aggressively and brutally beating anyone who dared resist him, he proved cowardly in defeat, begging for his life once overpowered.

Following his downfall, Giff was forced into honest manual labor to repay his debts. Over time, his dogged work ethic at the town store began gradually warming the townspeople to him. Despite his rough demeanor, he worries about being perceived as lazy, and harbors a lingering insecurity about whether Partitio approves of his reformation.

## History

### Background
During Oresrush's silver boom, Giff led a local gang of ruffians who frequently harassed the miners and attempted to plunder the silver shipments. Eight years prior to current events, Giff led a nighttime raid on the silver mines, but was thwarted and driven off by Partitio and the miners, swearing vengeance before retreating.

When the silver mines fell under the control of a predatory contract, Giff and his lackeys were hired as enforcers and tax collectors for the landowner. Exploiting the townsfolk's worsening poverty, Giff grew wealthy while ordinary miners starved.

### Partitio's Tale
In the present day, Giff confronted Partitio and several unemployed miners in the streets of Oresrush, demanding extortionate taxes. When one miner attempted to resist, Giff knocked him down and brutally stomped on him before leaving with threats to demand an even higher cut on his next visit.

That evening, Partitio and a group of miners stormed [Giff's Manse](../locations/giffs_manse.md). Confronting the gang, Partitio defeated Giff and his men in battle. Terrified for his life, Giff surrendered the contents of his safe, yielding enough wealth to fund the revival of Oresrush. Under interrogation, Giff reluctantly confessed that the true landowner orchestrating the exploitation was [Roque](roque_brilliante.md).

Following his surrender, Papp put Giff to work at the town store to work off his accumulated debts to the community. When Partitio later returned to Oresrush to construct the steam locomotive, Giff joined Papp, Roque, and [Thurston](thurston.md) to assist with the project.

During the epilogue, Giff is seen drinking heavily at [Gil](gil.md)'s tavern, drunkenly lamenting his fear that Partitio might still be disappointed in him.
