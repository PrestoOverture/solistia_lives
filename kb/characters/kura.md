# Kura

> *"There's no need for titles. I was born here, too, lest you forget."*  
> — Kura, speaking to the commoners of [Ku](../world/ku.md)

**Kura** was the second wife of King [Jigo Ku](jigo_ku.md) and the mother of [Hikari Ku](hikari.md) in *Octopath Traveler II*.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Gender**: Female
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Spouse**: [Jigo Ku](jigo_ku.md)
- **Children**: [Hikari Ku](hikari.md)

### Appearance
Kura wore her long black hair styled into an intricate traditional arrangement. She dressed in a sweeping cream-colored robe decorated with vibrant red, pink, and gold accents.

### Personality
Renowned throughout the realm for her gentleness and egalitarian spirit, Kura treated highborn nobles and commoners with identical warmth. Despite rising from humble beginnings to become royal consort, she never forgot her roots or distanced herself from the common folk. As a mother, she nurtured Hikari with unwavering affection and moral guidance.

## Biography

Kura rose from modest origins in [Ku](../world/ku.md) to marry King Jigo Ku as his second wife, later giving birth to Prince Hikari. She raised her son to value compassion, justice, and the lives of ordinary citizens, while gently tending to the injuries he sustained during combat training without discouraging his growth as a warrior. When [Clan Mei](../world/clan_mei.md) pledged fealty to Ku, Kura personally introduced young Hikari to [Jin Mei](jin_mei.md) and [Rai Mei](rai_mei.md). Her gentle wisdom had a transformative effect on King Jigo, convincing the fierce conqueror to turn away from perpetual warfare and pursue a lasting peace for his realm.

Tragedy struck when Kura was walking through the streets of Ku with her young son. In a plot orchestrated by [General Mugen](mugen.md), bandits hired through Jin Mei ambushed them and murdered Kura before turning on the young prince. Overwhelmed with shock and sorrow over his mother's murder, Hikari succumbed to his ancestral curse, manifesting his violent alter-ego, ["Hikari"](hikari_shadow.md), who butchered the assassins.

## Lore & Heritage

- According to Mugen, Kura belonged to an ancient bloodline that sought to protect the world from the [Shadow](../world/shadow.md) in antiquity, connecting her to the [Lumina Clan](../world/lumina_clan.md) and the Lineage of Light.
