# Castti Florenz

> *"Saving these people comes first. As long as I can heal the sick, who I am is ultimately trivial."*
> — Castti Florenz, affirming her duty as an apothecary

**Castti Florenz** is an Apothecary and one of the eight playable travelers in *Octopath Traveler II*. Discovered adrift at sea in a small boat, she awoke with severe amnesia; her only clues to her identity were her satchel and her instinctual medical skills. Embarking on a journey to recover her forgotten past, she unravels the tragic fate of [Eir's Apothecaries](../world/eirs_apothecaries.md), a humanitarian order she once led, and races to stop a former comrade from unleashing a catastrophic poisoned rain across the realm.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 29
- **Gender**: Female
- **Status**: Alive
- **Occupation**: Apothecary
- **Origin / Starting Location**: [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md)
- **Affiliation**: [Eir's Apothecaries](../world/eirs_apothecaries.md) (Leader / "Chief")

### Appearance
Castti has blonde hair styled into a neat bun at the back of her head. She wears the uniform of Eir's Apothecaries: a light blue dress with a high collar, rolled-up sleeves cuffed below the elbow, a full-length white apron with a blue stripe, and a white nurse's cap accented with a matching blue stripe. Her name is stitched onto her uniform. Over her dress, she wears a light blue hooded capelet featuring a pocket on the left side, accompanied by black stockings and sturdy brown boots.

Castti carries a brown satchel resembling a messenger bag, along with a smaller pouch secured to a belt at her waist. Unknown to most, the satchel contains a concealed bottom pocket where she keeps her travel log and medical treatment records. On one of her arms, she bears a dark, bruise-like mark—scar tissue resulting from unnatural swelling, a remnant of severe poison she survived prior to her memory loss.

### Personality
Despite suffering from profound amnesia at the start of her travels, Castti retains her gentle, kind, and patient temperament. She is fiercely dedicated to aiding the sick and wounded, showing low tolerance for anyone who hinders medical treatment, regardless of whether they are friend, foe, or military commander. As a veteran healer of immense experience, she naturally assumes a leadership role during health crises, directing triage and organizing care without hesitation.

Castti's amnesia initially weighs heavily on her. While determined to rediscover her past, she privately fears what she might uncover, worried that her own mind may have buried her memories to protect her from terrible trauma. As fragmented recollections of purple rain, desolate villages, and betrayal begin to resurface, she confronts them with steady courage.

Maternal and nurturing, Castti instinctively watches over the well-being of those around her, earning the nickname "Mother Hen" among her fellow apothecaries and "Mama" or "Ma" from [Ochette](ochette.md). Her heart flutters whenever someone displays vulnerability, sparking an urge to protect them. Despite her maturity and composure, she possesses an unexpected mischievous side: when [Partitio Yellowil](partitio.md) confessed he could not imagine what amnesia felt like, she playfully tricked him into believing they had engaged in wild, drunken antics together the previous night. She is also prone to becoming unruly and giggly when intoxicated.

Castti's passion for healing extends to nutrition. She taught herself to cook after realizing that wholesome food accelerates recovery, becoming particularly adept at preparing medicinal herb stews. In her youth, she was inspired to pursue medicine after reading an illustrated picture book. Her devotion to her craft led her to routinely test new remedies on herself; though these experiments occasionally brought her to death's door, they granted her an extraordinary resistance to nearly all conventional poisons. Beneath her gentle exterior lies a deep, lingering guilt over patients she was unable to save in the past—a sorrow that the dark deity [Vide](../world/vide.md) attempts to exploit during her journey.

### Prior to Story
Castti served as the leader ("Chief") of [Eir's Apothecaries](../world/eirs_apothecaries.md), a humanitarian order of six medical practitioners committed to the creed of "extending a helping hand to all in need." Based out of a house of healing in [Healeaks](../locations/abandoned_village.md), the group comprised Castti, [Malaya](malaya.md), brothers [Andy](andy.md) and [Randy](randy.md), [Elma](elma.md), and a promising recruit named [Trousseau](trousseau.md). Castti habitually carried skybalm to soothe and refresh her colleagues when their selfless duties pushed them to exhaustion.

Four years prior to the main narrative, the apothecaries provided medical relief during a war near [Sai](../locations/sai.md), earning the lasting gratitude of the local populace. Later, in [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md), Castti met Lady [Rosa](rosa.md), who was suffering from an incurable terminal illness; Castti successfully slowed the disease's progression and established a medicinal herb garden for Rosa's daughter, [Melia](melia.md), to tend. During their stay in Winterbloom, Castti and Malaya encountered Trousseau and invited him to join the order.

Later, in Healeaks, a young girl named Sally fell deathly ill. Castti and Trousseau braved the hazardous heights of [Mount Liphia](../locations/mount_liphia.md) to gather rare santanejo blossoms, successfully crafting a cure that saved the girl's life.

Not long after, tragedy struck Healeaks. Returning to town, the apothecaries discovered that Sally and her brother Temm had been slain near the mountains, and a toxic purple rain began pouring over the village. Villagers collapsed and died in agony. Spotting purple smoke rising from Mount Liphia, Castti, Malaya, Andy, and Randy climbed the mountain, leaving Elma to tend to the town. At the peak, they found Trousseau stoking a poisoned bonfire. Driven to nihilistic madness after an encounter with a cloaked man who convinced him that life was endless torment and death was ultimate salvation, Trousseau declared his plan to spread the lethal purple rain across the continent, starting at the upcoming royal coronation in [Timberain](../locations/timberain.md).

As Castti attempted to reason with him, she succumbed to the airborne toxins. Trousseau fled, leaving Andy and Randy to sacrifice their lives by leaping onto the venomous pyre to extinguish it. Malaya carried the dying Castti down the mountain, only to find Healeaks devastated and Elma dead after defending the team's horse, [Jeyah](jeyah.md). Administering snowdrop petals to stall the poison, Malaya transported Castti on Jeyah to [New Delsta Harbor](../locations/new_delsta_harbor_anchorage.md). Believing Castti was the sole apothecary capable of synthesizing a cure to counter Trousseau, Malaya placed her aboard a small skiff with her satchel and a handful of snowdrops, setting her adrift into the open sea. Malaya subsequently succumbed to the poison.

### Journey for Memories

#### Chapter 1: Awakening in Canalbrine
Castti was discovered drifting unconscious at sea by a ship's crew and brought ashore at the port town of [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md). Waking with complete amnesia, she inspected her satchel and uniform, determining her trade as an apothecary.

While walking through the town, Castti witnessed a young boy named [Senah](senah.md) collapse from a sudden illness. Aided by an apothecary named [Malaya](malaya.md), Castti administered lycanthe antipyretic to stabilize him. When Senah's older brother [Sesque](sesque.md) arrived, he recognized Castti's uniform with hostility, accusing her and [Eir's Apothecaries](../world/eirs_apothecaries.md) of being murderers who poisoned an entire town. A mob gathered outside, echoing the accusations until several townspeople abruptly collapsed.

Realizing an epidemic was spreading, Castti tended to the sick and deduced that the town's water supply had been compromised. She ventured into the [Canalbrine: Water Source](../locations/canalbrine_water_source.md), where she defeated two venomous beasts, [Veron and Doron](veron_and_doron.md), and purified the reservoir using purebalm. Her heroism dispelled the townsfolk's suspicions, earning their deep gratitude.

Before departing Canalbrine, Castti confronted Malaya to ask how she knew about Eir's Apothecaries. An inquiry triggered a sudden flashback revealing that Malaya had placed her in the skiff. When Malaya walked away without further explanation, Castti discovered a blood-spattered medical journal in the bottom of her satchel—written in her own hand—noting past travels to Sai and Winterbloom. She resolved to journey to both settlements to recover her past.

*(If Castti is not chosen as the initial traveler, the protagonist encounters her in Canalbrine tending to an ailing sailor; after receiving a Healing Grape, she joins the party.)*

#### Chapter 2: Sai Route
Arriving in [Sai](../locations/sai.md), Castti found the settlement engulfed in a fierce conflict between local defenders and foreign "outsiders." At a military triage tent, she encountered Commander [Edmund](edmund.md), a hard-bitten officer overworking injured soldiers. Speaking with the resident apothecary [Mao](mao.md), Castti recovered a vivid memory confirming that she had not merely belonged to Eir's Apothecaries, but had been their revered leader.

When word came of more casualties on the front lines, Castti rushed into the fray to treat injured combatants from both factions, including the enemy commander, [Griff](griff.md). As Griff revived and explained that his force had been ambushed by a monstrous [Sand Lion](../world/sand_lion.md), the ground suddenly gave way, swallowing Edmund and a squad of soldiers into the monster's underground lair. Castti descended into the sinkhole, safeguarded the wounded soldiers, and slew the Sand Lion.

Following the battle, Griff recounted hearing rumors regarding Eir's Apothecaries' downfall. A deeper inquiry unlocked fragmented memories of purple rain, a ghost town, and a sinister cloaked stranger. Shaken but resolute, Castti pressed on.

#### Chapter 2: Winterbloom Route
Upon reaching [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md), Castti was welcomed warmly by townsfolk who remembered her previous visit. She visited the manor of Lady [Rosa](rosa.md), finding her bedridden and in the final stages of a terminal illness. Rosa's young daughter, [Melia](melia.md), was harvesting herbs from the garden Castti had once planted for them.

Their reunion was interrupted by Rosa's covetous nephew, [Greg](greg.md), who plotted to seize the family inheritance. Soon after Castti administered medicine to soothe Rosa's pain, Melia was abducted. Following a ransom demand delivered by the housekeeper [Lily](lily_octopath_traveler_ii.md), Castti entered the [Winterbloom: Thieves' Quarter](../locations/winterbloom_thieves_quarter.md) alone. She confronted Greg and defeated the mercenary thugs [Plukk](plukk.md), [Mikk](mikk_octopath_traveler_ii.md), and [Makk](makk_octopath_traveler_ii.md) hired to kidnap Melia. Rather than punishing Greg with violence, Castti delivered a stern moral rebuke, urging him to reform his heart.

On Melia's twelfth birthday, Rosa bequeathed her estate to Melia and peacefully passed away. In the garden following the funeral, Melia mentioned Trousseau, sparking a memory of how Castti and Malaya had first met and recruited the idealistic youth in Winterbloom. As Castti prepared to leave, the apparition of Malaya appeared before her, urging her to travel to their former base in Healeaks.

#### Chapter 3: The Tragedy of Healeaks
Castti arrived at [Healeaks](../locations/abandoned_village.md) to find the town completely abandoned and overgrown with ruin. Rejoining Malaya outside their former clinic, Castti unlocked the full, painful truth of her past through deep contemplation.

She remembered the vibrant days of Eir's Apothecaries, their successful treatment of Sally with Mount Liphia's santanejo blossoms, and Trousseau's sudden descent into nihilism. She relived the horrors of the purple rain, Trousseau's confession that he had murdered Sally and Temm to trigger the catastrophe, the fatal sacrifices of Andy and Randy to douse the toxic flames, and Elma's death protecting Jeyah. Finally, she remembered Malaya saving her life by putting her out to sea.

With this revelation came a heartbreaking realization: Malaya had died of poisoning four years ago. The "Malaya" who had accompanied and guided her since Canalbrine was merely a psychological phantom—a manifestation of Castti's own subconscious memories steering her toward the truth. Embracing her grief and her sacred duty, Castti swore to stop Trousseau before he could unleash his poison on Timberain.

#### Chapter 4: Salvation at Timberain
Castti arrived in [Timberain](../locations/timberain.md) just as royal coronation ceremonies were underway. As the sky darkened and toxic purple rain began to drizzle over the city, Castti joined forces with Commander Edmund and Griff, whose soldiers evacuated the panicked populace into shelters.

Tracing the wind patterns and altitude required to disperse the toxins, Castti ascended to the heights of Timberain Castle. Atop the roof, she confronted Trousseau beside his poisoned cauldron. Castti defeated Trousseau in combat, putting an end to his madness. However, prolonged exposure to the concentrated purple rain caused Castti to collapse, her body succumbing to the lethal venom.

In her delirium, the spirit of Malaya appeared to guide her thoughts. Malaya reminded Castti that throughout her travels, she had already gathered every ingredient necessary to concoct a universal panacea:
- **Purebalm**: used to purify Canalbrine's poisoned water reservoir.
- **Scalebark leaf**: used to soothe Rosa's terminal cough in Winterbloom.
- **Skybalm**: carried by every member of Eir's Apothecaries to refresh exhausted bodies.
- **Santanejo blossom**: gathered from Mount Liphia to heal Sally in Healeaks.
- **Snowdrop petals**: preserved from the flowers Malaya placed in her hands before setting her adrift.

Channeling her skills, Castti concocted the antidote and drank it, completely neutralizing the poison. Working alongside Edmund and medical volunteers, she mass-produced and administered the remedy to every stricken citizen, saving Timberain from annihilation.

While resting in the town tavern afterward, Castti experienced a peaceful dream in which Malaya, Andy, Randy, and Elma appeared before her. They expressed their gratitude and asked to be put to rest. Castti offered them skybalm one last time, allowing their spirits to peacefully depart the realm.

Upon waking, Castti walked into the coronation festival with Edmund. The traveling scrivener [Ori](ori.md) approached her, asking for a comment on her heroic deed. Castti humbly declined personal glory, declaring that the city had not been saved by her alone, but by Eir's Apothecaries.

### Crossed Paths: Castti and Ochette

#### Part 1: Cropdale
Traveling through the Leaflands, Castti and [Ochette](ochette.md) arrived in [Cropdale](../locations/cropdale.md). When Ochette turned her nose up at stale meat offered by a local peddler due to poor hunting, she volunteered to catch fresh game in the [Animal Trail](../locations/animal_trail.md). Castti accompanied her, chiding her impetuous manners with motherly concern, prompting Ochette to affectionately call her "Ma."

Venturing into the woods, they found the local wildlife agitated and in hiding. After subduing a hostile creature, they tracked down an injured duorduor that Castti nursed back to health, affectionately naming it "Wooly-Ooly." Communicating through Ochette, Wooly-Ooly revealed that an unnatural dark entity had invaded the forest, terrifying the beasts.

#### Part 2: Shadows in the Forest
Returning to Cropdale later, the pair discovered the meat peddler missing and the Animal Trail shrouded in oppressive gloom. Castti was stricken by sudden dizziness and miasma, requiring Ochette to fight past shadowy beasts. Deeper in the forest, they found Wooly-Ooly suffering from symptoms identical to Trousseau's purple poison rain. Castti administered her leftover antidote, curing the beast.

As they pursued the shadow deeper into the grove, Castti became separated from Ochette in an ethereal void. Haunted by the hallucinatory voices of patients she had failed to save, Castti was confronted by a manifestation of the dark god [Vide](../world/vide.md), who offered to extinguish her emotional pain in eternal shadow. Before Castti could surrender to despair, Ochette tracked her by scent, breaking the dark thrall. Fighting side-by-side, Castti and Ochette vanquished the shadow entity, restoring light and tranquility to the woods.

### The Journey for the Dawn
During the descent of the [Night of the Sorrow](../stories/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md), Castti gathered with the other seven travelers to confront the cultists orchestrating the resurrection of [Vide](../world/vide.md). She confronted the conspirator [Oboro](oboro.md), rejecting the nihilistic philosophy of ending suffering through extinction that had claimed Trousseau.

In the final battle against Vide, Castti fought at the vanguard, affirming that hope, compassion, and human resilience would prevail over darkness. Following Vide's defeat, Castti resolved to rebuild [Eir's Apothecaries](../world/eirs_apothecaries.md), traveling the world to recruit like-minded healers dedicated to easing pain and saving lives.

## Relationships

- **[Malaya](malaya.md)**: A senior member of Eir's Apothecaries and Castti's closest confidante. Malaya held supreme faith in Castti's extraordinary healing talents, choosing to sacrifice her own life to send Castti to safety when Healeaks fell. Following her death, Malaya's memory manifested as a persistent hallucination that guided Castti until her memories were fully restored.
- **[Trousseau](trousseau.md)**: A gifted young apothecary recruited into Eir's Apothecaries in Winterbloom. Once idealistic and eager to help others, he was corrupted by nihilistic dogma and came to believe that true mercy lay in liberating humanity from suffering through death. He became Castti's primary antagonist, orchestrating the massacre of Healeaks and the attempted poisoning of Timberain.
- **[Edmund](edmund.md)**: A hardened commander who initially looked down upon apothecaries, derisively calling Castti a "bonemender." After witnessing her selfless courage in saving his men and slaying the Sand Lion, he developed deep respect for her, serving as her steadfast ally during the defense of Timberain.
- **[Rosa](rosa.md) & [Melia](melia.md)**: A noblewoman and her young daughter in Winterbloom. Castti cared deeply for Rosa, establishing a medicinal garden for Melia and slowing Rosa's terminal illness. Castti later rescued Melia from kidnappers, fulfilling her promise to look after the family.
- **[Ochette](ochette.md)**: A traveling companion who views Castti as a surrogate mother, affectionately calling her "Ma" or "Mama." Castti frequently frets over Ochette's diet and behavior, while Ochette's sharp instincts and loyalty protect Castti in moments of supernatural danger.
- **[Andy](andy.md), [Randy](randy.md), & [Elma](elma.md)**: Devoted members of Eir's Apothecaries who laid down their lives during Trousseau's attack on Healeaks—Andy and Randy dousing the poison fire with their bodies, and Elma protecting the team's steed [Jeyah](jeyah.md).
- **[Hikari Ku](hikari.md)**: Fellow traveler who observed the dark scar on Castti's arm and shared thoughtful discussions with her regarding trauma, buried memories, and their shared burdens of leadership.
- **[Partitio Yellowil](partitio.md)**: Traveling companion whom Castti fondly teased by inventing stories of drunken escapades to lighten the mood regarding her amnesia.
- **[Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md)**: Observed Castti during her journey, describing her eyes as being "filled with a motherly strength: deep wells of kindness that belied a sense of fierce determination."

## Lore & Trivia

- **Age and Seniority**: At 29 years old, Castti is the oldest female traveler among the protagonists of Solistia.
- **Poison Immunity**: Castti has routinely used her own body to test experimental medicines and antitoxins. Though several trials pushed her to the brink of death, they granted her an extraordinary natural resistance to nearly all known toxins and venoms.
- **Secret Compartment**: The bottom of Castti's satchel contains a concealed pocket where she kept her travel log, patient case files, and treatment records.
- **Culinary Skill**: Castti taught herself culinary medicine after noticing that proper nourishment accelerated patient recovery, developing a specialty in making flavorful, herb-infused medicinal stews.
- **Inspiration**: According to conversations with [Temenos Mistral](temenos.md), Castti was originally inspired to become an apothecary as a child after reading a picture book.
- **Drunken Antics**: Despite her professional demeanor, Castti becomes unruly, giggly, and mischievous when intoxicated.
- **Rebuilding the Order**: Following the conclusion of her journey, Castti dedicated her life to re-establishing Eir's Apothecaries by gathering altruistic practitioners across Solistia to provide humanitarian aid to all in need.
