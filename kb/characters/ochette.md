# Ochette

> *"I'm starting to look forward to this… It's gonna be my greatest hunt yet!"*
> — Ochette, preparing to leave [Beasting Village](../locations/beasting_village.md)

**Ochette** is a twenty-year-old [beastling](../world/beastling.md) hunter hailing from [Beasting Village](../locations/beasting_village.md) on the island of [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md). Despite ongoing land disputes between the native beastlings and human settlers, she lives a carefree life providing food for her community. Her life changes when she learns of the encroaching calamity known as the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../world/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md), a disaster that threatens the island once every four hundred years. Chosen by her mentor, [Master Juvah](juvah.md), to succeed him as guardian of Toto'haha, Ochette sets sail across Solistia to seek out three [Creatures of Legend](../world/creatures_of_legend.md)—[Cateracta](../world/cateracta.md), [Tera](../world/tera.md), and [Glacis](../world/glacis.md)—in order to save her home.

Depending on her choice in youth, Ochette is accompanied throughout her journey by either the lajackal [Akalā](akala.md) or the malamaowl [Mahina](mahina.md).

## Profile

### Appearance
Ochette has pale gray hair styled in a bob with bangs, brown skin, and bright green eyes. Standing around 130 cm (4'3") tall—the typical height for a beastling—she possesses cat-like ears and snout, a squirrel-like nose, a dog-like tail, and black claws on her hands. 

She wears a beige, white, and pink dress over brown shorts, brown gladiator sandals strapped around her calves, and jeweled armbands. Around her neck hangs a decorative charm fashioned from gathered sea stones and string. The colorful feathers adorning her armbands were taken from Toto'haha parrots, said to bring good fortune and happiness.

### Personality
Ochette is cheerful, eager, and energetic, defined by an insatiable appetite for meat and an intense dislike of fruits and vegetables. She frequently rushes through any non-food tasks so she can eat as soon as possible. Despite her gluttony, she is kind, empathetic, and generous, readily sharing her hunted bounty with villagers, travelers, and hungry strangers. Her heart contains no trace of darkness, greed, or malice, rendering her completely immune to corruption by the [Shadow](../world/shadow.md).

Her sunny disposition falters only when witnessing the cruelty of human hunters who kill out of hatred or greed rather than survival and hunger, calling such spite "unforgivable." Even so, she refuses to nurse grudges, preferring to win over former adversaries by sharing roasted jerky.

Unlike most beastlings on Toto'haha, Ochette does not speak in broken syntax; Master Juvah taught her fluent human speech, a trait shared by the master of the Hunters' Guild. Childlike in demeanor, Ochette readily embraces close companions as family: she affectionately views [Castti Florenz](castti.md) as a mother ("Mama" or "Ma") and [Osvald V. Vanstein](osvald.md) as a father ("Pops"), while forming a close sisterly bond with [Throné Anguis](throne.md). She also gives playful nicknames to her traveling companions, referring to [Partitio Yellowil](partitio.md) as "Parti," [Agnea Bristarni](agnea.md) as "Aggie," and [Hikari Ku](hikari.md) as "Hikarin."

### Abilities
As an accomplished huntress and village guardian, Ochette is a skilled tracker and marksman with bow and axe. She possesses the ability to befriend and tame wild beasts, commanding them in combat or preparing captured game into meat and provisions. Her keen animal instincts allow her to unleash raw bestial ferocity in battle. Outside of combat, she can provoke opponents with her beasts or win people over by offering jerky.

## History

### Background
As an infant, Ochette was taken in and raised by [Master Juvah](juvah.md), who received a premonition regarding her destiny and trained her as a hunter. During her childhood, Juvah brought her to the ancient ruins and presented two rare creatures—the lajackal [Akalā](akala.md) and the malamaowl [Mahina](mahina.md)—allowing her to choose one as her lifelong partner. Upon Ochette choosing and befriending her companion, the unchosen beast was suddenly consumed by a dark aura, turned hostile toward her, and fled deep into the wilderness.

### Chapter 1
Ten years later, Ochette has grown into the foremost hunter of [Beasting Village](../locations/beasting_village.md). Accompanied by her partner, she tracks down and slays a massive [king iguana](../world/king_iguana.md) terrorizing the forest. Returning with the carcass, she discovers a group of human settlers from the neighboring village trespassing and threatening the beastlings over land. Ochette provokes and defeats their leader, driving the intruders off.

After distributing the meat among her people, Ochette meets Master Juvah on the western hill. Juvah names her his successor as Toto'haha's guardian, expressing concern over disturbing changes across the island's ecosystem. They are interrupted by [Cohazeh](cohazeh.md), chief of the neighboring human village, who apologizes for the earlier incident and asks for help finding a young human girl lost inside the [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../locations/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md). Despite Juvah's misgivings, Ochette volunteers. Deep in the tombs, she discovers the frightened child and wins her trust by sharing jerky.

As night falls on their return, strange dark beasts attack the forest path. Outside the village, Ochette learns an entity has assaulted Beasting Village and injured Juvah. Leaving the child in safety, Ochette engages the [Dark Entity](dark_entity.md) and defeats it. Cohazeh and her people arrive shortly after, expressing gratitude for the girl's rescue and the defense of the village, and Cohazeh declares a temporary truce in their territorial dispute.

Juvah then escorts Ochette to the ancient ruins to reveal the prophecy of the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../world/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md)—a calamity recurring every four centuries where legions of monsters invade the island. Juvah tasks Ochette with crossing the sea to locate three ancient [Creatures of Legend](../world/creatures_of_legend.md) to defend Toto'haha, and Ochette departs on her journey.

### Chapter 2: Cateracta's Route
Ochette travels to [Conning Creek](../locations/conning_creek.md) in search of the legendary sea beast [Cateracta](../world/cateracta.md). Hearing an eerie weeping sound echoing across the waters, she attempts to sail to an offshore islet but is blocked by a guardian named [Alpione](alpione.md). After knocking Alpione out in combat, Ochette reaches the islet, only to discover the sound is merely sea wind whistling through Cateracta's massive skeleton washed ashore.

Alpione recovers and confronts Ochette, assuming she came to scavenge the corpse, but softens upon hearing of Ochette's mission. Inviting her into the [Cavern of the Sea God](../locations/cavern_of_the_sea_god.md) and sharing food, Alpione reveals that Cateracta was slain single-handedly by an enigmatic hunter known as the [Dark Hunter](petrichor.md), who wiped out Cateracta's guardians—leaving Alpione the sole survivor—and departed without taking meat or bounty. Alpione then reveals that Cateracta laid an egg just before dying. As Ochette approaches, the egg hatches into a newborn sea dragon whom Ochette names [Acta](acta.md). Entrusting the hatchling to Ochette's care, Alpione bids them farewell.

### Chapter 2: Tera's Route
Arriving in [Crackridge](../locations/crackridge.md), Ochette inquires about the legendary beast [Tera](../world/tera.md). The terrified villagers refuse to speak of the creature, fearful of waking him; Tera sleeps deep beneath the valley, and any disturbance triggers violent earthquakes. Ochette meets a starving young man named [Pom](pom.md), who agrees to reveal Tera's resting place in exchange for aromatic jerky.

Pom leads Ochette into the valley to a massive fissure. Hearing Tera's deep snoring below, Ochette lets out a mighty yowl down into the chasm. Awakened in a fury, Tera emerges and attacks, but Ochette and her companions subdue him. Once calmed, Tera recognizes Ochette as a beastling and deduces she seeks his aid for the Night of the Scarlet Moon. Tera reveals that another hunter had challenged him previously, leaving both severely injured and forcing Tera to slumber to recuperate. Agreeing to lend his power, Tera joins Ochette. Back in Crackridge, Ochette assures the villagers that the quakes will cease, while Pom, thoroughly shaken by the encounter, decides to abandon hunting to become a cattle rancher instead.

### Chapter 2: Glacis' Route
Ochette journeys to [Stormhail](../locations/stormhail.md) to ascend [Sacred Peak Altahe](../locations/sacred_peak_altahe.md), the domain of [Glacis](../world/glacis.md). Overcoming a [Sanctum Knight](../world/sacred_guard.md) guarding the gate, she climbs the snowy slopes and howls to rally her spirits, inadvertently triggering an avalanche. The noise draws [Heig](heig.md), a hunter who warns her that Altahe is gripped by a blizzard born of pure hatred. Heig explains that his hunting party attempted to slay Glacis for wealth and prestige, but Glacis slaughtered them all; the avalanche uncovered the frozen body of his nephew, the last missing hunter. Consumed by vengeance, Heig presses on toward the summit.

At the mountain peak, Ochette finds Heig gravely wounded before Glacis. Noticing shattered dragon eggs in the nest, Ochette learns from Heig that the hunters broke them during their prior raid. Disgusted by their cruelty, Ochette attempts to reason with Glacis, but the grief-stricken guardian attacks. After subduing Glacis, Ochette reminds her of her ancient duty as a guardian. Glacis agrees to accompany Ochette to protect Toto'haha. Ochette escorts the wounded Heig back to Stormhail, listening as he expresses deep remorse over his actions and recounts his past encounter with the Dark Hunter.

### Chapter 3: The Night of the Scarlet Moon
With Acta, Tera, and Glacis gathered, Ochette returns to Toto'haha, where Juvah and the villagers have prepared for battle. Cohazeh arrives again over border tensions, dismissing warnings of catastrophe, but Juvah urges her to set aside petty feuds. Soon after, the moon turns blood-red, heralding the Night of the Scarlet Moon.

A horde of monsters swarms Toto'haha. Ochette, Juvah, and the legendary beasts fight desperately to hold the line. The human settlement is overrun, forcing Cohazeh and her survivors to flee to Beasting Village. Seeing the beastlings risk their lives to protect the humans, Cohazeh rallies her people to fight alongside them.

Amidst the fighting, Ochette catches a familiar scent and confronts the companion she did not choose ten years prior—now twisted and mutated by darkness into the monstrous [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../world/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md). The Darkling's immense power overwhelms Juvah, the legendary beasts, and Ochette, hurling Ochette and her companion into the ocean.

Beneath the waves, the [Sacred Flame](../world/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) awakens within Ochette's companion. Rising from the waters infused with sacred light, Ochette realizes what she truly fights for: the protection of her home and all who dwell upon it. Aided by her empowered partner and a matured Acta, Ochette battles and defeats the Darkling to end its agony.

As the beast lies dying, Ochette feels profound grief, recognizing that her partner would have met the same fate had her choice been reversed. She comforts the dying beast with food, assuring it that its suffering is over and praying they may meet again as friends in their next life. With the creature at peace, its soul dissolves away.

As the crimson moon fades, Cohazeh pledges lasting peace and coexistence between humans and beastlings. Ochette fully embraces her role as guardian of Toto'haha, setting out on new hunts alongside her companion.

### Crossed Paths (with Castti Florenz)
- **Part 1:** Ochette and [Castti Florenz](castti.md) visit [Cropdale](../locations/cropdale.md), where Ochette finds the local meat skewers stale due to poor hunting yields. Investigating the [Animal Trail](../locations/animal_trail.md), they encounter a frightened [dire duordour](dire_duorduor.md). After subduing it, Ochette translates warnings from local animals fleeing a shadowy presence corrupting the forest. Castti treats the wounded beast—naming it "Wooly-Ooly," much to Ochette's skepticism—and the two learn the forest wildlife is in peril.
- **Part 2:** Returning later to Cropdale, they find the meat stall abandoned and the Animal Trail shrouded in dark miasma. After driving off a shadowy entity, they discover Wooly-Ooly heavily injured after defending retreating wildlife. As they venture deeper, Castti falls into a dark trance. Because Ochette's pure heart is immune to shadow corruption, she calls Castti back to her senses. Together, they destroy the shadowy manifestation, restoring peace to the forest.

### The Journey for the Dawn
During the final conflict against [Vide](../world/vide.md), when [Oboro](oboro.md) questions whether the dawn is worth the agonizing journey, Ochette answers without hesitation: *"It is—no question about it.... What's wrong? You look like someone just ate your lunch..."* Before the battle with Vide, she reaffirms her resolve: *"I've found my hope... my dream. I'll keep everyone safe. That's what a hunter does."*

## Relationships

- **[Master Juvah](juvah.md):** Leader of the beastlings on Toto'haha and Ochette's teacher. He raised and trained her from infancy, recognizing her potential and naming her his successor as island guardian.
- **[Castti Florenz](castti.md):** An apothecary whom Ochette looks up to as a mother figure, affectionately calling her "Mama" and "Ma."
- **[Osvald V. Vanstein](osvald.md):** A scholar whom Ochette views as a father figure, referring to him as "Pops."
- **[Throné Anguis](throne.md):** A thief with whom Ochette shares a close, sisterly bond, reflecting the beastling tendency to adopt companions as family.
- **[Akalā](akala.md) and [Mahina](mahina.md):** Rare creatures presented to Ochette in childhood. Whichever one she selects becomes her faithful hunting companion, while the unchosen beast falls prey to dark forces and becomes the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../world/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md).
- **[Cohazeh](cohazeh.md):** Chief of the neighboring human settlement on Toto'haha. Although initially locked in territorial disputes with the beastlings, she develops mutual respect for Ochette and Juvah during the Scarlet Moon crisis, leading to lasting peace between their peoples.

## Lore & Trivia

- **Voice and Volume:** Ochette is known to possess the loudest voice on Toto'haha.
- **Childhood Incident:** In her youth, an errant arrow shot by Ochette sheared off a portion of Master Juvah's mane. She suspects this incident was what prompted him to teach her proper human speech in order to improve communication.
- **Belief in Reincarnation:** Ochette believes in the rebirth of souls, comforting the dying Darkling with the promise that they might meet and be friends in their next life.
- **Beastling Romance:** Describing beastling romance, Ochette explains that her parents bonded over their mutual love of meat. She notes that romantic affection makes a beastling's tail wag uncontrollably, which she considers embarrassing.
- **Artistic Passion:** During travel banter with [Temenos Mistral](temenos.md), Ochette is shown to draw simplistic, childlike scribbles, yet approaches her artwork with immense passion.
- **"Projectiles by Pops":** Ochette once proposed to [Osvald V. Vanstein](osvald.md) that he enchant her arrows with magic, suggesting they brand the technique "Projectiles by Pops"—causing Osvald to lose interest immediately.
- **Partitio's Nickname:** [Partitio Yellowil](partitio.md) affectionately calls Ochette a "young cowgirl."
