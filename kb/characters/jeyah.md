# Jeyah

**Jeyah** was the faithful horse belonging to [Eir's Apothecaries](../world/eirs_apothecaries.md). Accompanying the traveling healers throughout their journey across Solistia, Jeyah played a vital role in carrying the wounded [Castti Florenz](castti.md) to safety following the tragedy of [Healeaks](../locations/abandoned_village.md).

## Profile

### Appearance
Jeyah was a gray horse distinguished by a prominent white stripe running down its nose.

### Bonds
Jeyah shared a deep, special bond with [Elma](elma.md), the veterinarian and apothecary who tended to animals within Eir's Apothecaries.

## Story

During their relief mission in the village of Healeaks, Jeyah remained behind in the settlement under Elma's care while Castti and the rest of Eir's Apothecaries climbed [Mount Liphia](../locations/mount_liphia.md) to investigate the source of the lethal poisonous rain. When the toxic downpour engulfed the village, Elma and Jeyah were exposed to the deadly fumes. In her final moments before dying, Elma covered Jeyah with a heavy blanket to shield the horse from the toxic rain.

Because of Elma's sacrifice, Jeyah survived long enough for [Malaya](malaya.md) to return to the devastated village carrying a severely poisoned and traumatized Castti. Finding Jeyah still alive, Malaya removed the protective blanket and mounted the horse with Castti, and Jeyah carried them westward across the [Brightlands](../locations/brightlands.md) to [New Delsta Harbor: Anchorage](../locations/new_delsta_harbor_anchorage.md).

At the anchorage, Malaya placed the unconscious Castti into a small skiff and cast her adrift into the sea to save her from the poison. Exhausted and fatally stricken by the toxins inhaled during their flight, Malaya and Jeyah rested together beneath a seaside tree, where both peacefully succumbed to the poison.
