# Claude

**Claude** is the immortal founder of the [Blacksnakes](../world/blacksnakes.md) thieves' guild, a high-ranking conspirator within the [Moonshade Order](../world/moonshade_order.md), and the biological father of [Throné Anguis](throne.md). Operating from the secluded, desolate settlement of [Lostseed](../locations/lostseed.md), Claude orchestrated decades of bloodshed and debauchery to produce a progeny powerful enough to slay him and inherit his cursed destiny as the living vessel for [Vide](../world/vide.md).

## Profile

### Appearance
Claude has long, silvery-white hair and a muscular physique. He dresses in a high-collared brown coat, black trousers, and boots, wearing his shirt unbuttoned to expose his chest. In battle, he wields a pair of heavy bladed chains. He carries an aura so foul and malevolent that [Ochette](ochette.md) described his lingering scent at a cemetery in [New Delsta](../locations/new_delsta.md) as the darkest and most twisted thing she had ever encountered.

### Personality
Beneath a veneer of aristocratic politeness and soft-spoken civility, Claude is a narcissistic, hedonistic sociopath entirely devoid of empathy or remorse. He views human beings purely as tools to cultivate his bloodline, forcing scores of women to bear his children across generations. He takes a perverse delight in his offspring, not out of love, but out of satisfaction in watching them butcher one another to prove their worth. He is deeply indolent and detached, preferring to remain hidden in the shadows while manipulating events through intermediaries until forced to act directly. He regards the decaying misery of Lostseed as a serene sanctuary, leisurely strolling through its squalor as though enjoying a pristine meadow.

## History

### The Immortal Lineage
Claude is the grandson of the legendary archmage [D'arqest](darqest.md). Before Claude was born, D'arqest infused him with the blood of the dark god [Vide](../world/vide.md), granting him eternal youth and unnatural immortality. Lore recounted by the gondola Guide at the [Deserted Highroad](../locations/deserted_highroad.md) suggests Claude was the runaway prince from foreign kingdom wars who fled into the valley alongside a pregnant princess to establish [Lostseed](../locations/lostseed.md).

Weary of his endless existence and tasked with preparing the vessel for Vide's resurrection, Claude founded the Blacksnakes syndicate. Over decades, he fathered countless children across Solistia—including [Father](father.md) (Sebastian), [Mother](mother.md), [Pirro](pirro.md), [Scaracci](scaracci.md), [Donnie](donnie.md), the [Masked Boy](masked_boy.md), the [Elderly Guard](elderly_guard.md), and the [Guide](../stories/guide_thrones_story.md). He placed them into criminal trials and deadly succession games, pruning the weak in search of an heir formidable enough to kill him.

### The Moonshade Order and Other Invasions
Claude was an active collaborator in the [Moonshade Order](../world/moonshade_order.md)'s overarching plan to extinguish the dawn. When [Castti Florenz](castti.md) and [Trousseau](trousseau.md) led a relief mission to aid the destitute citizens of Lostseed, Claude seized on Trousseau's growing despair, handing him the *Book of Night* and triggering the apothecary's descent into nihilistic madness. Shortly thereafter, Claude met the writer [Ori](ori.md), entrusting her with the Darkblood Staff to convey to [Arcanette](arcanette.md).

### Confrontation in Lostseed
Among his many consorts was the Blacksnake operative [Marietta](marietta.md), who bore his daughter Throné. Decades later, after Throné cut down both Mother and Father to claim their collar keys, she followed Claude's trail to the depths of Lostseed. There she found Claude accepting another newborn from a woman of [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md). 

Claude revealed the horrifying truth: that he was the biological father of nearly every major Blacksnake Throné had known, making her rivals and abusive caretakers her half-siblings. When Throné rejected his dark purpose and attacked him, Claude conjured phantasms of her fallen comrades and family to shatter her resolve. However, Throné overcame the illusions and dealt him a mortal blow. Recognizing her supreme strength as his true heir, Claude welcomed his demise and permitted her to claim the second key to unlock her collar.
