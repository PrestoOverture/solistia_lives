# Vados the Architect

> "*'[Dohter the Charitable](../world/dohter.md)… did suffer grievous wounds in the fight with the evil god [Vide](../world/vide.md), and fell into eternal slumber.' Heh… Thought I would send you off with a bit of scripture. All in the name of Lord Vide.*"
> — Vados, after murdering the Canalbrine apothecary

**Vados the Architect** is an architect, religious zealot, and member of the [Moonshade Order](../world/moonshade_order.md) in *Octopath Traveler II*. Despite being one of the final descendants of the [Kal](../world/kal.md), he secretly served [Vide](../world/vide.md), utilizing his role in cathedral renovations to build subterranean access beneath [Flamechurch: Cathedral](../locations/flamechurch_cathedral.md) and orchestrating the murder of [Pontiff Jörg](jorg.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 33
- **Gender**: Male
- **Occupation**: Architect
- **Affiliation**: [Moonshade Order](../world/moonshade_order.md), [Kal](../world/kal.md)
- **Status**: Deceased

### Appearance
Vados wears a brown robe secured with a long white sash. On the back of his hand, he bears the tattooed mark of the Keepers of the Flame, signifying his heritage as a descendant of the Kal.

### Personality
Vados is a quiet man who rarely speaks unless he deems it necessary. He is fanatically devoted to the teachings of the [Moonshade Order](../world/moonshade_order.md) and to [Vide the Wicked](../world/vide.md), pursuing Vide's resurrection with ruthless commitment. In travel banter, [Throné](throne.md) observes that Vados's messy killings are the work of an amateur unskilled with a dagger, a deduction corroborated by his failure to kill Hermes before help arrived and his reliance on magical elementals in combat rather than physical attacks.

## Biography

### Cathedral Renovations
Two years prior to the events of *Octopath Traveler II*, Vados was placed in charge of renovating [Flamechurch: Cathedral](../locations/flamechurch_cathedral.md). During the project, he excavated the [Cathedral Cellars](../locations/cathedral_cellars.md)—an underground passage designed to grant his crew unrestricted access into the cathedral's interior, reachable through a small building to the cathedral's east. Though born of the sacred Kal lineage, Vados was inducted into the Moonshade Order and turned against the church.

### The Canalbrine Murders
Following the murder of [Pontiff Jörg](jorg.md), Vados was cornered outside the cathedral square by Inquisitor Temenos and Sanctum Knight [Crick](crick_wellsley.md), who coerced him into divulging information regarding the underground passage.

After the pontiff's funeral, Vados traveled to [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md) to conduct a forbidden ritual requiring multiple sacrifices. There, he murdered [Lucian](lucian.md)—a scholar who was last seen speaking with the pontiff—and an unknown apothecary. The following night, he entered a local tavern aiming to kill a popular dancer named [Hermes](hermes.md), but was interrupted by Temenos and Crick.

### Subjugation and Death
Fleeing the tavern, Vados escaped to the [Sacred Guard's ship](../locations/sacred_guard_ship.md) docked at an adjacent harbor. When Temenos and Crick cornered him, Vados shouted "*For my lord!*" and summoned large elementals to battle them. He was defeated and subdued.

The Sacred Guard, led by [Captain Kaldena](kaldena.md), promptly arrived to take Vados into custody, and Crick was tasked with escorting him to Stormhail. However, upon their arrival in the city, Kaldena secretly expunged all records of Vados's entry, murdered him to obtain his page of the Book of Night, and discarded his body outside the Sacred Guard headquarters, where Temenos and Crick later found him half-buried in snow.
