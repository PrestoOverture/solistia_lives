# Astell

**Judge Astell** was a high magistrate who presided over the courthouse in the royal city of [Timberain](../locations/timberain.md) in the [Leaflands](../locations/leaflands.md).

## Profile

- **Title**: Judge Astell
- **Occupation**: Magistrate, Art Collector
- **Location**: [Timberain](../locations/timberain.md)
- **Status**: Deceased

### Appearance
Judge Astell was a bald, white-bearded elderly man who dressed in standard black judicial robes.

### Personality
Publicly presenting himself as an unyielding and righteous arbiter of the law, Astell was an avid art collector and a fierce rival of Count Rousseau. In secret, however, he was utterly corrupt. Astell exploited his judicial authority to embezzle rare heirlooms and cultural relics, ruthlessly handing down death sentences to innocent scapegoats to conceal his own thievery.

## History

During his tenure on the bench in Timberain, Judge Astell presided over the high-profile trial concerning the theft of the Sapphire Wreath, a priceless heirloom belonging to the kingdom's royal family. To cover his own tracks—having stolen the artifact for his private hoard—Astell sentenced an innocent couple to death for the crime.

Fifteen years later, following a courtroom verdict, Astell was overheard outside the courthouse conversing with the court prosecutor about the missing wreath. During the exchange, Astell inadvertently described the relic as a "black treasure." The slip was overheard by Meylan, a courthouse gate guard who was the orphaned son of the executed couple.

Shortly after Astell handed down an eighty-year prison sentence to a thief named Nero for stealing the Azure Sun Sword, Meylan exacted revenge, assassinating the corrupt judge. During Meylan's ensuing trial, the truth of Astell's crimes and his theft of the Sapphire Wreath was laid bare before the court, though Meylan was ultimately sentenced to life imprisonment for the killing.
