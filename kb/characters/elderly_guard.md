# Elderly Guard

> *"I hope the children can come to see me as family."*
> — Elderly Guard

## Overview

- **Age**: 72
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Origin / Location**: [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](../locations/mothers_garden_orphanage.md) and [Road to Mother's Garden](../locations/road_to_mothers_garden.md), [Leaflands](../locations/leaflands.md)
- **Occupation**: Gatekeeper and gardener of Mother's Garden; later caretaker of the reformed orphanage
- **Relatives**:
  - [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md) (father)
  - [Guide](../stories/guide_thrones_story.md) (twin brother)

The **Elderly Guard** is a character in *Octopath Traveler II* who serves as the gatekeeper of [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](../locations/mothers_garden_orphanage.md) in the story of [Throné Anguis](throne.md). Unbeknownst to most, he is one of the many offspring of [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md) and has tended the grounds of Mother's Garden since it was merely a rose bed. Following [Mother](mother.md)'s downfall, he dedicates his remaining years to protecting and nurturing the orphaned children as a surrogate father.

## Appearance

The Elderly Guard is a seventy-two-year-old man with long gray hair and a matching beard. He wears a simple green tunic, trousers, and a leather belt with an attached pouch. He shares an identical appearance with his twin brother, the [Guide](../stories/guide_thrones_story.md) of the [Deserted Highroad](../locations/deserted_highroad.md).

## Personality

The Elderly Guard possesses a quiet, gentle equanimity and a deep sense of responsibility toward the children under his care. While he dutifully kept watch at the gates to protect the facility from outside threats, he lived with deep personal remorse over his inability to protect the children from the brutal punishments inflicted within by Mother. Once freed from her reign of terror, his protective paternal instincts emerge fully: he travels across Solistia to save [Mira](mira.md) from self-destruction, embraces the role of surrogate father, and commits himself to ensuring the children grow up in warmth and safety.

## History

### The Rose Bed and the Blacksnakes

Long before the orphanage became a training den for thieves and assassins, the Elderly Guard watched over [Mother's Garden](../locations/mothers_garden.md) when it was a simple plot of black roses. In those days, the lethal poison sealed within the [Blacksnakes](../world/blacksnakes.md)' collars was extracted from the black roses he cultivated there. Over decades, as the guild evolved and his father [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md) orchestrated the cycle of violence from the shadows, all others who knew the origins of the garden's poison passed away, leaving the guard as the sole keeper of the garden's history.

### The Downfall of Mother

When Throné infiltrates the orphanage in search of Mother, the Elderly Guard meets her at the entrance. Hearing the tolling of the distant courtyard bell, Throné comments on its lovely resonance; the guard grimly lets her pass, withholding the dark truth that the bell chimes only when Mother is administering disciplinary beatings to the children.

After Throné confronts and slays Mother, the Elderly Guard steps forward to take charge of the abandoned children. Under his gentle guidance, the facility is completely transformed from a criminal grooming institution into a peaceful, compassionate sanctuary maintained alongside local nuns.

### Rescuing Mira

Sometime after Mother's death, [Mira](mira.md) vanishes from the orphanage, consumed by a desire for vengeance. Distressed, the Elderly Guard tracks her to [Wellgrove](../locations/wellgrove.md) and enlists the travelers to help locate her. Following her trail to the ruined enclave of [Lostseed](../locations/lostseed.md), the guard crosses paths with his twin brother, who operates the gondola across the [Deserted Highroad](../locations/deserted_highroad.md).

Within Lostseed, the Elderly Guard finds Mira after she has learned the horrifying reality of her parentage and Claude's lineage. Offering warmth, forgiveness, and understanding, the guard convinces Mira to abandon her quest for bloodshed and return with him to [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](../locations/mothers_garden_orphanage.md). Together, they dedicate their lives to raising the children and providing them with a genuine family.
