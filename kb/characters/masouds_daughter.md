# Masoud's Daughter

**Masoud's Daughter** is a 27-year-old resident of [Sai](../locations/sai.md) and the only child of the legendary merchant [Masoud](masoud.md). She plays an instrumental role in [Partitio Yellowil](partitio.md)'s Scent of Commerce journey in [Hinoeuma](../locations/hinoeuma.md).

## Profile

### Personality and Background
Growing up in the shadow of Masoud's illustrious mercantile career, she witnessed her father neglect both herself and her mother in his single-minded dedication to commercial travel across Solistia. While she harbors no open hatred or bitterness toward him, she holds no respect for his choices either, having resolved from a young age never to live her life consumed by work the way he did. Nevertheless, having lived alongside him, she understands his psychology and worldview far better than anyone else.

## Story

When Partitio arrives in Sai seeking to study Masoud's famous [Mercantile Manuscript](../stories/masoud_script.md), Masoud challenges the young merchant to identify and bring him the treasure he values above all else. Believing that family must be a person's greatest treasure, Partitio hires Masoud's daughter and brings her to the hilltop estate. Masoud promptly dismisses the notion, bluntly stating that while ordinary folk may treasure their family above all, he is not ordinary folk.

Recognizing Partitio's genuine passion for trade, Masoud's daughter offers him a vital insight: her father is not testing what a typical merchant would prize, but rather what is unique and precious to Masoud himself. Guided by her advice, Partitio realizes that Masoud's greatest treasure is his own lifetime of acquired knowledge, skill, and mercantile spirit, successfully solving the riddle and earning access to the manuscript archives.
