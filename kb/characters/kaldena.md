# Kaldena

> *"I will make the [Shadow](../world/shadow.md) mine and avenge the [Kal](../world/kal.md) people! I will kill as many as it takes! I will become whatever I must to see my will done!"*
> — Captain Kaldena, confronting Temenos Mistral

**Captain Kaldena**, born **Dena**, was the supreme commander of the [Sacred Guard](../world/sacred_guard.md) and the primary antagonist of [Temenos Mistral](temenos.md)'s journey. Driven by a thirst to avenge the genocide of her people, she secretly subverted the church's holy order from within, conspiring to wield the power of the [Shadow](../world/shadow.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Original Name**: Dena
- **Title**: Captain of the Sacred Guard
- **Gender**: Female
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Heritage**: The [Kal](../world/kal.md) (Keepers of the Flame)
- **Origin**: [Fellsun Ruins](../locations/fellsun_ruins.md) (near [Crackridge](../locations/crackridge.md))
- **Relatives**: [Kaldena's Father](kaldenas_father.md) (deceased)

### Appearance
Kaldena had dark skin and long white hair parted to veil one eye. Inscribed upon her forehead was a ceremonial tattoo honoring the azure Sacred Flame of the Kal. In contrast to the royal blue attire of standard knights, Kaldena wore a specialized Sacred Guard uniform accented with rich tyrian purple, accompanied by a flowing black-and-purple cloak and a mulberry sash at her waist. She wielded an immense greatsword rumored to contain the power of a god.

### Personality
To the public and her subordinates, Kaldena presented herself as an austere, formidable, and peerlessly efficient commander, commanding deep devotion and respect from officers such as [Deputy Cubaryi](cubaryi.md) and her personal bodyguard [Ort](ort.md).

In secret, Kaldena harbored utter contempt for the Sacred Flame and loathed the gods for creating humanity. The traumatic extermination of her clan filled her with bitter misanthropy and an insatiable desire for power. Consumed by hatred, she showed no qualms about butchering her own loyal knights or breaking sacred taboos to harness the Shadow. Her obsession blinded her to the fact that she had become an unwitting pawn of [Arcanette](arcanette.md), collaborating with the very Moonshade conspiracy that had murdered her clan.

## History

### The Fall of the Kal
Born under the name Dena, she was raised in the secluded sanctuary of the Kal—the Keepers of the Flame—situated at what is now known as the [Fellsun Ruins](../locations/fellsun_ruins.md) near [Crackridge](../locations/crackridge.md). Thirty years prior to the events of the story, her people were slaughtered in a merciless pogrom orchestrated by the [Moonshade Order](../world/moonshade_order.md). Her [father](kaldenas_father.md) sacrificed his life holding off the attackers, urging her to escape and survive.

Vowing to avenge her fallen kin, Dena adopted the name "Kaldena" in their memory. She enlisted in the Sacred Guard, concealing her hatred for the church while rising through the ranks until she was appointed captain. In that position, she mentored promising young knights, including [Crick Wellsley](crick_wellsley.md) and Ort, knighting them and selecting Ort as her personal bodyguard.

At some point, Kaldena was contacted by Arcanette, who concealed her true identity as the leader of the Moonshade Order. Arcanette instructed Kaldena in rituals from the *Book of Night* to summon the Shadow, introducing her to the architect [Vados](vados.md) to assemble the missing pages of the grimoire.

### The Canalbrine Murders and Cover-Up
Following the assassination of [Pontiff Jörg](jorg.md) in [Flamechurch](../locations/flamechurch.md), Kaldena traveled to [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md) under the pretext of leading the investigation into the murders of a local apothecary and the theologian [Lucian](lucian.md). Returning to the [Sacred Guard Ship](../locations/sacred_guard_ship.md) at night, she encountered Temenos and Crick, who had already apprehended Vados.

Feigning cooperation, Kaldena ordered Crick to escort Vados to headquarters in [Stormhail](../locations/stormhail.md) and promised Temenos permission to interrogate him. Once in Stormhail, however, Kaldena had Vados quietly assassinated and purged all documentation of his arrest.

### Betrayal in the Rifted Rock
In Stormhail, Crick uncovered Kaldena's clandestine occult library and realized she was orchestrating the murders. Before he could alert Temenos, Kaldena murdered Crick in cold blood, instructing Cubaryi to detain the inquisitor.

Kaldena then led a cohort of knights to the [Nameless Village](../locations/nameless_village.md) on Toto'haha, penetrating the subterranean caverns of the [Rifted Rock](../locations/rifted_rock.md). At an ancient altar, Ort confronted her over the suspicious circumstances of Crick's death. Kaldena openly mocked his loyalty, drawing her sword and slaughtering her escort, leaving Ort critically wounded.

Using verses from the *Book of Night*, Kaldena unleashed the Shadow onto her remaining soldiers, driving them into a murderous frenzy where they butchered one another. Temenos arrived in the chamber as the carnage ended. When Kaldena's offer for Temenos to join her was rebuffed, she drew on the Shadow to annihilate him.

However, the dark power surged out of her control, mutating her into a towering, demonic abomination of pure darkness. Temenos struck down the monstrous form, and Kaldena's body disintegrated into ash as she died.

### Epilogue
Records discovered in [Ori](ori.md)'s journal later revealed that Arcanette had deliberately provided Kaldena with a corrupted, unstable ritual, ensuring that she would be consumed and destroyed by the Shadow once she served her purpose. Following Kaldena's demise, Ori seized the *Book of Night* and bound it with Harvey's *Book of Demons* to forge the *Darkblood Grimoire*.
