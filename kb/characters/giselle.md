# Giselle

**Giselle** is the leader of Giselle's Traveling Troupe, who appears as a supporting ally in [Agnea Bristarni](agnea.md)'s journey. Formerly a noblewoman, she abandoned an arranged marriage to pursue life as an itinerant entertainer, striving to bring smiles across Solistia.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 28
- **Gender**: Female
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [New Delsta Harbor: Anchorage](../locations/new_delsta_harbor_anchorage.md)
- **Occupation**: Troupe Leader, Street Performer

### Appearance
Giselle has short blonde hair and wears a red half-cape from which she conjures her trained dove, Weiss. She carries a sword at her side.

### Personality
Giselle lives by her troupe's guiding motto: "Bringing smiles to every corner of the realm." Despite her cheerful public facade, she wrestles with deep insecurity and stage fright, frequently terrified of forgetting her lines and letting down the audience and troupe members who depend on her. Throughout her life, this anxiety often tempted her to flee difficult situations. She has a poetic manner of speaking, often using metaphors and similes—describing smiles as brighter than gold, stars as symbols of steadfast hope, or daydreaming of resting peacefully like a seashell on the ocean floor to escape worry and pain.

## Story

Born the daughter of a noble family, Giselle faced a future with a predetermined marriage partner. Unwilling to accept a life mapped out for her, she fled home to carve her own destiny. Her wanderings initially lacked direction until she began performing street magic with her pet dove, Weiss. Developing a passion for entertainment, she founded Giselle's Traveling Troupe alongside fellow performers [Rico](rico.md), [Coda](coda.md), and [Tanzy](tanzy.md).

Upon arriving in [Tropu'hopu](../locations/tropuhopu.md), the troupe's cart gets stuck in the sand. After Tanzy fails to dig it out, Agnea offers her help, and together they dislodge the wagon. In the ensuing introductions, Giselle recognizes Agnea's surname, recalling the fame of her late mother, [Cuani Bristarni](cuani_bristarni.md).

The troupe prepares to perform at the [Tropu'hopu: Floating Theater](../locations/tropuhopu_floating_theater.md), but as showtime nears, Giselle suffers a crisis of confidence. Terrified that she will bungle her lines, she flees to a nearby islet. Agnea tracks her down and listens to her fears, comforting Giselle with words of hope and reminding her that even when performers stumble, they can always rise again. Reinvigorated, Giselle returns to the theater and delivers a vibrant show alongside Rico and Coda, inviting Agnea on stage to dance. Afterward, the troupe celebrates under the moonlight before setting off toward their next venue.

In [Merry Hills](../locations/merry_hills.md), Giselle and her troupe cross paths with Agnea once more. When ruffians attempt to harass Agnea before her competition, Giselle's performers release a flurry of doves to create a diversion, allowing Agnea to slip past. Later, Giselle and the troupe enthusiastically cheer from the audience during Agnea's climactic contest against [Dolcinaea Luciel](dolcinaea_luciel.md).

Following Agnea's triumph, Giselle's troupe flourishes, expanding rapidly with thirty new members within a month. Giselle can later be found at [New Delsta Harbor: Anchorage](../locations/new_delsta_harbor_anchorage.md) with Rico and Coda, and is even seen playfully attempting to recruit the beastling leader [Juvah](juvah.md) into her troupe.
