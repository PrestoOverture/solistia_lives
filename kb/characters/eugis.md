# Eugis

> *"My goddess is not in this town... Which is to say, none of them would talk to me, really."*
> — Eugis, in Merry Hills

## Overview

- **Age**: 29
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Origin / Location**: [Merry Hills](../locations/merry_hills.md), [Crestlands](../locations/crestlands.md); previously active in [Stormhail](../locations/stormhail.md), [Winterlands](../locations/winterlands.md)
- **Occupation**: Itinerant charmer and grifter

**Eugis** is a wandering womanizer and scoundrel in *Octopath Traveler II* featured in the side story *[Lingering Love](../stories/lingering_love.md)*. Skilled at winning women's affection with hollow flattery and gifts, he wheedles his way into their lives and homes before vanishing when convenience suits him.

## Appearance

Eugis is a twenty-nine-year-old man with bright red hair. He wears a blue button-down shirt beneath an open red vest, trousers, and a white bandanna knotted loosely around his neck.

## Personality

Eugis is an insincere, deceitful flatterer driven by a desperate, childish hunger for romantic validation. He drifts rapidly from one infatuation to the next, grandiosely praising each woman as his "goddess." Despite his theatrical declarations of romantic passion, his behavior is shallow and manipulative. When his charm fails and local women ignore him, his self-absorbed facade quickly crumbles into pathetic whining.

## Story

Prior to his appearance in the Crestlands, Eugis romanced a woman in [Stormhail](../locations/stormhail.md), presenting her with a keepsake necklace before abruptly vanishing without explanation. In the side story *[Lingering Love](../stories/lingering_love.md)*, the abandoned woman is left in deep melancholy, obsessing over the jewelry he left behind. Her sorrow is resolved either by parting with the necklace and realizing her freedom, or by traveling to confront Eugis directly. Upon meeting him again, she observes his hollow, recycled promises and shallow desperation firsthand, finally shattering her illusion of love and finding peace without him.

Eugis is found idling in [Merry Hills](../locations/merry_hills.md), bemoaning the fact that none of the town's women will entertain his advances.
