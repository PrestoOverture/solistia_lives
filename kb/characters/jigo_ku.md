# Jigo Ku

> "*[Hikari](hikari.md), I have devoted my life to expanding our borders. Many a dawn has been stained crimson by my ambition. Now, at the end of that path... I realize that it has all led to nothing.*"
> — King Jigo Ku

## Overview

**King Jigo Ku** was the aging monarch of [Ku](../world/ku.md) in the desert realm of [Hinoeuma](../locations/hinoeuma.md). He is the father of half-brothers [Mugen](mugen.md) and [Hikari Ku](hikari.md), and the partner of Lady [Kura](kura.md). After spending much of his life waging aggressive wars to expand his kingdom, he came to regret the bloodshed and sought a peaceful future for Ku, ultimately leading to his betrayal and death during Mugen's coup d'état.

## Profile

### Appearance
King Jigo wears an ornate black-and-red royal robe decorated with golden patterns. He has a full white beard, a red cap, and a golden crown.

### Personality
In his earlier years, King Jigo believed that sheer strength and martial power were essential, dedicating decades of his reign to endless territorial expansion. However, as age caught up with him, Jigo grew weary of perpetual conflict and felt deep remorse for the world of strife and suffering he had helped create. Recognising the destructive path of unchecked militarism, he chose Hikari as his successor over his ruthless eldest son Mugen, hoping Hikari's compassionate heart would usher in an era of peace and light.

## History

### Early Rule and Family
Early in his reign, Jigo married and had his firstborn son, Mugen. During one of Ku's campaigns that laid waste to a settlement, Jigo was targeted for assassination by a surviving youth named [Kazan](oboro.md). Impressed by Kazan's audacity and sharp intellect, Jigo spared his life and appointed him to Ku's court as a strategist. Later, Jigo took [Kura](kura.md) as his consort and fathered his second son, Hikari. Tragically, Kura was murdered by bandits in an ambush secretly orchestrated by [Jin Mei](jin_mei.md) and Mugen.

Kazan once looked King Jigo directly in the eye and asked, "Shall we begin digging for salt?" Though initially taken aback by the unusual counsel, Jigo trusted his strategist and commanded the mining of salt to provide relief and economic support for the realm's impoverished citizens.

Following a decisive victory against the southern nation, King Jigo appeared before his troops and sons to commend their victory, visibly straining under the punishing heat. Soon after, Jigo took the momentous step of declaring an indefinite ceasefire, bringing Ku's long wars to an end.

### Mugen's Coup and Death
Three years after concluding the war, King Jigo met with Hikari on the western side of the castle town. Reflecting somberly on his past ambitions and the crimson dawns his wars had brought, Jigo asked Hikari what vision he held for the kingdom. Satisfied with Hikari's desire for peace and prosperity for the people, Jigo declared Hikari his rightful successor and cautioned him that Mugen and his loyalists were harboring dangerous designs.

That night, Jigo summoned Mugen to test his intentions. When Mugen reiterated his desire to rule purely by force and conquest, Jigo deemed him unworthy of the throne. Mugen promptly launched a coup, setting the town ablaze with his faction. King Jigo fought fiercely, cutting down insurgent soldiers before confronting Mugen in a duel. Weakened by age, Jigo was defeated and mortally wounded. As Hikari rushed to his side, the dying king ordered his younger son to flee, gather trusted allies, and return when he was ready to reclaim Ku and restore light to the kingdom. King Jigo then drew his final breath in Hikari's arms.
