# Georges Lazuli

> *"Study the world's ruins, as I have, and you pick up a few things. All the other linguistic scholars look like children, if you ask me!"*
> — Georges Lazuli, on the Nameless Isle

## Overview

- **Age**: 72
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Origin / Location**: [Nameless Isle](../locations/nameless_isle.md), [Sundering Sea](../locations/the_sundering_sea.md); later [Montwise: Library](../locations/montwise_library.md), [Crestlands](../locations/crestlands.md)
- **Occupation**: Archaeologist, linguist, and codebreaker

**Georges Lazuli** is an intrepid archaeologist, master linguist, and cryptographer in *Octopath Traveler II*. Despite his advanced age, his nimble physique and boundless curiosity keep him exploring the most perilous ancient ruins across Solistia. He possesses an unmatched expertise in deciphering forgotten scripts and ancient documents, playing a vital role in translating forbidden literature.

## Appearance

Georges is a lively seventy-two-year-old man with silver-gray hair. He dresses in durable brown travel garments practical for exploring crumbling ruins and maritime wilderness.

## Personality

Proud, boisterous, and fiercely self-assured, Georges possesses a jovial demeanor paired with sharp scholarly acumen. He takes great pride in his linguistic capabilities, dismissively comparing rival academic scholars to children. Driven by an unquenchable thirst for knowledge, he fears neither isolation nor dangerous beasts in his pursuit of forgotten history, greeting visitors with booming laughter and an eager willingness to share his hard-won expertise.

## Story

Georges spends his elder years scouring ancient ruins throughout Solistia to master dead languages. His travels eventually take him across the [Sundering Sea](../locations/the_sundering_sea.md) to the remote [Nameless Isle](../locations/nameless_isle.md), where he investigates the deep subterranean ruins guarded by the titanic creature [Gigantes](../world/gigantes.md).

When travelers reach the heart of the island after overcoming Gigantes, Georges offers his academic aid. If guided to the grand library of [Montwise](../locations/montwise_library.md) to meet the researcher [Al](alfred_octopath_traveler_ii.md) following the retrieval of rare texts in the quest *[Procuring Peculiar Tomes](../stories/procuring_peculiar_tomes.md)*, Georges provides the crucial breakthrough needed to decode *[From the Far Reaches of Hell](../stories/from_the_far_reaches_of_hell.md)*. His masterful translation sheds light on ancient dark lore that ordinary scholars were utterly powerless to decipher.
