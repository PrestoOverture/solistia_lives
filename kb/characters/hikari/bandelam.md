# Bandelam

> *"Defeat is death."*
> — Bandelam

## Overview

- **Age**: 39
- **Gender**: Male
- **Aliases**: Bandelam the Reaper, The Reaper
- **Occupation**: Gladiator, arena champion, wandering warrior
- **Location**: [Crackridge Harbor: Anchorage](../../locations/wildlands/wilds/crackridge_harbor_anchorage.md); formerly [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md)

**Bandelam the Reaper** is a legendary veteran warrior and the reigning champion of the [Montwise: Underground Arena](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/montwise_underground_arena.md). Over the course of a long and bloody martial career, he rose from a young recruit to a celebrated national hero, was subsequently branded a traitor, and eventually emerged as an undefeated arena gladiator, having slain over a hundred foes without ever tasting defeat.

## Appearance

Bandelam is clad in heavy gray armor and has dark hair, with a prominent scar running vertically down his face through his right eye.

## Personality

Bandelam is driven by an intense lust for battle, claiming that he lives solely to fight—a bloodlust that initially led him to serve the unscrupulous Lord [Borneau](borneau.md). He strictly abides by an unyielding arena creed that dictates "defeat is death."

Despite his brutality, Bandelam is a man of deep martial honor. When bested, he fully expects and demands execution to preserve his warrior's code. However, witnessing [Hikari](hikari.md)'s selflessness and refusal to engage in needless slaughter profoundly alters his perspective on combat and purpose.

## Story

In [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md), [Hikari Ku](hikari.md) seeks out the strategist [Kazan](oboro.md), who is encumbered by crushing gambling debts owed to arena proprietor Lord [Borneau](borneau.md). To settle Kazan's accounts, Hikari enters the underground arena tournaments, aiming to challenge Borneau's personal champion, Bandelam.

After winning his match against [Zeto the Butcher](zeto.md), Hikari refuses to kill his defeated opponent, defying both the bloodthirsty spectators and the dark whispers of his [inner shadow](hikari_shadow.md). Bandelam enters the ring to execute Zeto himself, but Hikari intervenes and issues a formal challenge. Bandelam accepts, relishing the prospect of facing a worthy adversary.

In their duel, Bandelam is impressed by Hikari's prowess and inquires about his motivations. Hikari replies that he fights not for slaughter, but for fellowship and the restoration of his homeland. Even as escaped gladiators storm the stands and seize Borneau hostage, the two warriors conclude their duel. Hikari emerges victorious.

True to his creed, Bandelam demands to be slain, but Hikari refuses to kill him and convinces the vengeful gladiators to lay down their weapons and abandon the cycle of bloodshed. Kazan takes possession of the arena in settlement of the debt and liberates all the fighters.

Spared and deeply affected by Hikari's ideals, Bandelam abandons Borneau's service and the arena life. Having finally tasted defeat, he wanders Solistia searching for a new calling, eventually taking up quiet reflection overlooking the sea at [Crackridge Harbor: Anchorage](../../locations/wildlands/wilds/crackridge_harbor_anchorage.md).
