# Tsuki

> *"Lord [Hikari](../travelers/hikari.md)... Save our kingdom... Only your gentle hands... can guide us back...."*  
> — Tsuki's final words

**Tsuki** is a character in *Octopath Traveler II*. A palace attendant and tavern maid from the castle town of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md), she is the twin sister of [Yomi](../others/yomi.md) and a close friend of Prince [Hikari Ku](../travelers/hikari.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: Unknown
- **Gender**: Female
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Location**: [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md)
- **Occupation**: Royal palace attendant, tavern worker
- **Relatives**: [Yomi](../others/yomi.md) (twin sister)

### Personality
Tsuki was gentle, deeply empathetic, and selfless. Gifted with musical talent, she frequently played heartwarming melodies on the lute to soothe Hikari. Her devotion to others was demonstrated early in life when she sacrificed her own place in her household so that her twin sister could remain with their family.

## History

### Background
Tsuki and her twin sister [Yomi](../others/yomi.md) were born to an impoverished lowborn family in the desert realm of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md). During their childhood, they discovered a battered lute while scavenging, which ignited a lifelong love of music. When their destitute parents argued over which twin to keep and which to cast out, Tsuki comforted her sister, waited until Yomi fell asleep, and slipped away into the night so her sister could stay.

Over the ensuing years, Tsuki found work as a palace servant to the Ku royal family, residing in the castle town and supplementing her income by working at the town tavern. During Yomi's travels across Solistia, the sisters eventually reunited. Tsuki also became a trusted confidante of the gentle second prince, Hikari Ku.

### Hikari's Tale
Three years following the southern border campaign, an unruly customer harassed patrons at the tavern. Tsuki sought out Hikari, who stepped in and subdued the disruptive warrior, an officer under [General Mugen](mugen.md).

That night, Mugen executed a violent coup against King Jigo Ku, sending ruthless legionnaires to burn the castle town and slaughter its inhabitants. During the massacre, Tsuki was mortally wounded. As Hikari hurried back from the palace, he discovered Tsuki collapsed on the blood-soaked streets. Tsuki died in Hikari's arms, using her last breath to plead with him to save the kingdom and restore peace with his gentle hands.
