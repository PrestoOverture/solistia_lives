# Azuma

> *"... Thank you, thank you. Any small business you can afford me is much appreciated."*
> — Azuma

## Overview

- **Age**: 34
- **Gender**: Male
- **Occupation**: Merchant

**Azuma** is a merchant and an old acquaintance and drinking buddy of [Kazan](oboro.md).

## Appearance

Azuma wears a green merchant's outfit paired with a feathered hat.

## Personality

Shrewd and self-serving, Azuma has keen ears for underground commerce and alerts Kazan to [Mugen](mugen.md)'s illicit arms deals. Despite their friendship, Azuma is an opportunist who operates on under-the-table dealings, demanding financial compensation before disclosing vital information.

## Story

Some time after [Hikari](hikari.md) flees [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md), Azuma alerts Kazan that Mugen has arranged to purchase a massive cache of weapons. Later, during Hikari's journey, Azuma meets the exiled prince at the tavern in [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md). After receiving a bribe, Azuma reveals that the arms transfer is set to take place under heavy guard deep within the [Secret Forest](../../locations/leaflands/dungeons/secret_forest.md).
