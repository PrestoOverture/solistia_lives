# Ritsu Mishuyo

> "*The powerless will always be stepped on by the strong! You have to lift their boots off you with your own hands! You think your 'friends' care about you? That's all lies! Strength is all that matters!*"
> — Ritsu Mishuyo

**Ritsu Mishuyo** is a warrior of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md) and [Hikari Ku](../travelers/hikari.md)'s rival, sparring partner, and former stalwart ally on the battlefield. Born as a commoner, Ritsu harbors deep resentment toward Ku's rigid social hierarchy that oppresses the lowborn. His desperate desire to overcome powerlessness and carve his name into history ultimately leads him to abandon his bond with Hikari and align himself with the ruthless [Mugen](mugen.md).

## Profile

### Appearance

Ritsu wears a red tunic paired with brown sandals, a flaxen scarf, and black pants. He is distinguished by a red headband set with metal inlays. Following his promotion to general in the Ku army, he replaces his red tunic with a black one.

### Personality

During his youth, Ritsu deeply admired Hikari and his swordsmanship, aspiring to rival his skill in combat. Despite Ritsu's lowborn origin, Hikari treated him with genuine equality and friendship, a gesture for which Ritsu was initially grateful. Beneath this camaraderie, however, Ritsu harbored simmering frustration at never once defeating Hikari in a duel.

As the years passed, that frustration curdled into bitter jealousy. Convinced that strength was the only truth in the world and that the weak would inevitably be crushed unless they seized power by force, Ritsu chose Mugen over Hikari. Ambition eroded his moral boundaries: he became willing to betray comrades and execute his own wounded commander, [General Rou](rou.md), fabricating a narrative of treason to advance his standing. Yet even in his final moments, a remnant of his original longing for a just Ku remained, urging Hikari to create the better world he himself could not.

## Biography

### Early Life

Eight years prior to the events of *Octopath Traveler II*, Ritsu and his younger sister, [Mikka Mishuyo](mikka_mishuyo.md), erected a memorial for their deceased father, who had been slain by a Ku soldier simply for being lowborn. Prince [Hikari](hikari_shadow.md) encountered them while visiting to pay his respects, vowing to hold those responsible accountable. From that encounter, Hikari and Ritsu forged a bond as training partners and companions, frequently sparring despite their differing stations.

Five years later, Ritsu accompanied Hikari during the Ku army's campaign to conquer the southern region of Hinoeuma. When Ritsu severely wounded an enemy general in battle, he eagerly moved to deliver a killing blow, but Hikari intervened and spared the man. Following the battle, the two friends shared a quiet moment reminiscing about their childhood.

### Betrayal and Rise

When Mugen initiated his coup against King [Jigo Ku](jigo_ku.md), Ritsu chose to cast his lot with Mugen, seeking status and power as an officer. Mugen dispatched Ritsu to spring an ambush on Hikari and execute him. Ritsu confronted his former friend alongside two subordinate Ku soldiers, but was defeated. Hikari resisted the darkness within himself and refrained from slaying Ritsu, allowing him to retreat.

Ritsu was subsequently assigned to [General Rou](rou.md)'s division. In the [Secret Forest](../../locations/leaflands/dungeons/secret_forest.md), the unit conducted a clandestine weapons purchase from an arms merchant. Hikari confronted them immediately following the deal. Ritsu stood by as Rou personally fought Hikari, witnessing Rou fall when Hikari's latent dark power surged. As Rou lay defeated and dying, he ordered Ritsu to fall back and revealed the grim reality of the Ku clan's cursed bloodline. Rather than obey Rou's withdrawal order or aid his commander, Ritsu killed the defenseless general on the spot to protect his own career. 

Ritsu brought Rou's severed head before Mugen, falsely claiming that Rou had secretly aided Hikari and that he had executed him for treason. Though Mugen saw through Ritsu's lie, he nonetheless rewarded Ritsu's ruthless initiative by promoting him to the rank of general.

### Fall

Prior to Hikari's final assault on Castle Ku, Ritsu paid a visit to his sister Mikka. Mugen subsequently summoned Ritsu to serve as his personal vanguard guarding the entrance to the throne room. When Hikari breached the castle and reached the chamber doors, he pleaded with Ritsu to step aside. Refusing to yield, Ritsu engaged his former comrade in a final sword duel. Hikari struck him down, inflicting a mortal wound. As he lay dying, Ritsu acknowledged Hikari's conviction and implored him to change Ku for the better.

### Legacy

Following the fall of Mugen and the restoration of Ku, a young boy in [Ku: Castle Town](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/ku_castle_town.md) speaks of having been saved during the turmoil by a Ku soldier wearing a distinctive headband. Unable to locate his benefactor to express his gratitude, the boy remains unaware that Ritsu gave his life in the throne room.
