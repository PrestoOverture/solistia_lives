# Rou

> *"If you mean to take these weapons from us, then it will have to be by force. But be prepared to find out... Why they call me the Wild Bull of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md)! Better men than you have crumpled before my charge!"*
> — General Rou, before battling [Hikari](hikari_shadow.md)

**General Rou**, also known as the **Wild Bull of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md)**, was a senior military commander of Ku. Renowned as [King Jigo](jigo_ku.md)'s most trusted general, his fierce battlefield charges and dual-blade prowess earned him legendary status among Ku's forces.

## Profile

### Appearance
Rou had long brown hair, a full brown beard, and wore heavy dark armor into battle. He fought wielding a sword in each hand.

### Personality
Rou was a seasoned warrior whose loyalty lay steadfastly with the throne of Ku. He relished military challenge, openly expressing disappointment when illicit arms transactions proceeded without interference. Yet beneath his martial ferocity, he possessed the wisdom to recognize his limits and concede defeat when bested. Although he transferred his service to [Mugen](mugen.md) after the regicide, Rou harbored genuine dread for Ku's fate and for [Hikari](hikari_shadow.md), understanding the dark curse inherent to Clan Ku's lineage.

## History

### Background
Rou joined Ku's army in his youth and steadily ascended through the ranks through sheer martial skill. His relentless charges on the battlefield earned him the epithet "Wild Bull of Ku," ultimately rising to become King Jigo Ku's most dependable and trusted general.

### Fall in the Forest of Wellgrove
Following King Jigo's death in Prince Mugen's coup, Rou maintained his loyalty to the throne, serving under Mugen. Rou was dispatched alongside [Ritsu Mishuyo](ritsu_mishuyo.md) to [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md) to oversee a secret weapons cache hidden in the forest outside of town. Prince Hikari tracked down the shipment and dispatched Rou's platoon before appealing to the veteran general to stand down.

Pushing Ritsu aside, Rou chose instead to test Hikari's resolve in a one-on-one duel. During the clash, Hikari was forced to tap into the shadowed power of the Ku bloodline to defeat him. Wounded, Rou accepted his loss and prepared to withdraw, warning Hikari that the inner darkness would inevitably consume him if he was not careful.

As they departed through the woods, Ritsu harshly confronted Rou for retreating. Rou confided in Ritsu regarding the terrifying reality of Clan Ku's shadowed bloodline, but Ritsu dismissed the revelation and drew his sword, executing Rou on the spot for "showing weakness." Ritsu delivered Rou's severed head to Mugen with a fabricated claim that the general was conspiring with Hikari. Though Mugen saw through Ritsu's falsehood, he was pleased with the youth's ruthless ambition and rewarded Ritsu with Rou's former post as general.
