# Hikari Ku

**Hikari Ku** is the younger prince of the martial realm of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md) in the desert region of [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md), and one of eight travelers in Solistia. A kindhearted and honorable Warrior who strives for a world without conflict or bloodshed, he was driven from his homeland following a violent coup orchestrated by his half-brother [General Mugen](mugen.md). In exile, Hikari journeys across the realm to gather his former comrades-in-arms, liberate Ku from tyranny, and put an end to his country's ceaseless warmongering.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 21
- **Gender**: Male
- **Occupation**: Prince of Ku, Warrior
- **Status**: Alive
- **Origin**: [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md), [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md)
- **Relatives**:
  - [King Jigo Ku](jigo_ku.md) (father, deceased)
  - [Kura](kura.md) (mother, deceased)
  - [General Mugen](mugen.md) (half-brother, deceased)
  - ["Hikari"](hikari_shadow.md) (shadow alter ego)

### Appearance
Hikari is a young man of slender build, standing approximately 170 cm (5'7") tall. He wears a red robe with golden accents over a black uniform, around the center of which rests the sheath for his sword. He wears black leggings, boots, and black gloves, with his black hair tied back in a ponytail. His attire is suited for desert conditions, featuring sand-guard tabi socks with leather sandals.

### Personality
Hikari represents none of Clan Ku's ruthless ambition and ferocity. Unlike his half-brother Mugen, he is a gentle, compassionate, and honorable prince who believes that a kingdom requires allies, community, and peace for all people rather than enemies and perpetual warfare—an outlook that earned him the love of the common folk and led his father to choose him as heir to the throne.

Despite his gentle nature, Hikari harbors a violent, sadistic inner demon—["Hikari"](hikari_shadow.md)—spawned by Clan Ku's "accursed blood," representing the very bloodshed and brutality he opposes. Resisting this dark self requires immense willpower, which Hikari exercises at every turn. While he detests senseless violence and rarely instigates combat, he will not hesitate to fight in self-defense or when diplomatic appeals fail, as demonstrated in his clashes with [Ritsu Mishuyo](ritsu_mishuyo.md) and [Rai Mei](rai_mei.md). Once he resolves that ending a threat is unavoidable, he carries through with absolute resolve.

Hikari places supreme value on friendship, firmly believing that solidarity among allies is essential to stopping Clan Ku's warmongering. He is a prodigiously skilled warrior capable of mastering advanced martial techniques on his first attempt. In daily life, he enjoys reading military chronicles (an interest cultivated in his youth by Kazan through mock war councils), tends to become increasingly solemn when drunk, and once suggested to [Osvald V. Vanstein](../osvald/osvald.md) that the fabled [seventh source](../../world/lore/one_true_magic.md) of magic might simply be friendship. His companion [Ochette](../ochette/ochette.md), who nicknames him "Hikarin," detects two distinct scents on him: one very warm, and another deep, pungent scent that she finds unpleasant, stemming from his cursed blood.

## History

### Prior to Story
Hikari is the son of [King Jigo Ku](jigo_ku.md) and a lowborn woman named [Kura](kura.md). During his youth in Ku Castle, he met his new vassals, Rai Mei and her brother [Jin Mei](jin_mei.md). Challenging Jin Mei to a duel, Hikari mastered Clan Mei's advanced sword technique, *Hachimonjigiri*, on his first try. Days later, as Hikari sparred with Rai Mei, the Bell of Dawn sounded, calling Clan Mei to war. Hikari wished to accompany them, but Jin Mei urged him to remain behind until he was ready.

Returning to town at Jin Mei's request, Hikari found his mother conversing with villagers when bandits suddenly attacked to assassinate the young prince. Hikari defended himself, but Kura was fatally wounded. In his grief, Hikari succumbed to his dark shadow self, ["Hikari"](hikari_shadow.md), slaughtering the bandits in a blood frenzy before being restrained by Jin Mei. Retaining no memory of the massacre, Hikari was left terrified of the cursed bloodline within him. Around this time, Hikari also met [Ritsu Mishuyo](ritsu_mishuyo.md), a commoner mourning the murder of his own father, and the two formed a close bond.

Years later, appointed as captain, Hikari fought alongside Ritsu and Rai Mei in Ku's war against a rival nation. Ku was victorious, but the dying enemy general denounced Clan Ku as "accursed"—a warning that haunted Hikari despite Mugen's dismissal. Afterward, Hikari gathered with Ritsu, Rai Mei, and the military tactician [Kazan](oboro.md), promising to share a victory toast once lasting peace was brought to Ku. However, Rai Mei and Kazan both departed the military before that promise could be fulfilled.

### Story

#### Chapter 1
Appointed lord over a district in Ku, Hikari earned the devotion of his people by treating commoners as equals. After defusing a tavern altercation instigated by one of Mugen's soldiers, Hikari was summoned by his retainer [Benkei](benkei.md) to a private meeting with [King Jigo](jigo_ku.md). Expressing remorse over the blood spilled during Ku's expansion, Jigo revealed that Mugen was orchestrating a coup d'état and declared his wish for Hikari to succeed him.

Bribing the castle merchant to confirm his brother's plot, Hikari traveled to the Crest of Heroes—a memorial for Ku's fallen soldiers—to intercept Mugen. Instead, he was ambushed by Ritsu, now a captain under Mugen. Ritsu revealed the intel was a ruse to draw Hikari away while Mugen's soldiers set Ku ablaze. Hikari defeated Ritsu in combat, actively resisting the dark urge within him to execute his friend, and rushed back to the burning capital. Passing numerous fallen citizens, including his friend [Tsuki](tsuki.md), Hikari reached the throne room only to watch Mugen strike King Jigo down. With his dying breath, Jigo urged Hikari to flee, seek out trustworthy allies, and reclaim the nation. Mugen's forces surrounded Hikari, but Benkei intervened, allowing the prince to escape. Fleeing to [Ryu](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/ryu.md), Hikari resolved to find Kazan in [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md).

*(If Hikari is not chosen as the primary protagonist, the traveler encounters him in Ryu defending a local villager from corrupt soldiers, assisting him before he joins the party.)*

#### Chapter 2
In Montwise, Hikari located Kazan in the [Underground Arena](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/montwise_underground_arena.md), a subterranean pit where spectators wagered on death matches. When Hikari implored Kazan to join his cause, Kazan shoved him into the ring, forcing him to fight. After Hikari emerged victorious, Kazan confessed that he was drowning in gambling debts and needed Hikari's martial skills as a gladiator to clear them. While there, Hikari witnessed [Bandelam the Reaper](bandelam.md) dispatch four opponents simultaneously, exchanging glances with the formidable champion.

Entering the tournament, Hikari fought through multiple combatants. When pitted against [Zeto the Butcher](zeto.md), Hikari spared his life, defying the demands of the audience, Zeto, and his inner shadow to kill. He then stepped in to protect Zeto from Bandelam, who challenged Hikari to a duel the following day.

During their arena duel, Hikari declared that he fought for his comrades, intending to become "a king unlike any other." Mid-battle, an armed revolt of gladiators erupted, orchestrated behind the scenes by Kazan. Hikari defeated Bandelam and spared him despite his plea for an honorable death. Kazan collected a windfall of three billion leaves from arena broker [Borneau](borneau.md), revealing his true motive was to liberate the enslaved fighters. Moved by Hikari's mercy, Zeto and the gladiators pledged their loyalty to his banner. While Kazan led the men to Ryu to prepare an army, he alerted Hikari to Mugen's covert weapon shipments in [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md).

#### Chapter 3
Arriving in Wellgrove, Hikari recalled meeting Ritsu at his father's memorial eight years earlier. Bribing the merchant [Azuma](azuma.md), Hikari learned the weapons were being transported through the [Secret Forest](../../locations/leaflands/dungeons/secret_forest.md). Intercepting the caravan, Hikari appealed to [General Rou](rou.md), who rebuffed him, asserting unquestioned loyalty to Ku regardless of who ruled.

During their duel, Hikari's shadow self briefly seized control of his body. Regaining mastery over himself, Hikari halted his blade before killing General Rou. Rou fled, warning Hikari that the darkness in his blood would eventually consume him—a warning Rou later repeated to Ritsu concerning Clan Ku's terrifying battlefield madness. Hikari delivered the captured weapons to Kazan in Ryu, where he was informed that Rai Mei had taken command of [Castle Mei](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail_castle_mei.md) in [Stormhail](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail.md).

#### Chapter 4
Hikari traveled to Stormhail to enlist Clan Mei's aid, but was barred at Castle Mei by [Kunzo](kunzo.md) on Rai Mei's orders. After subduing Kunzo, Rai Mei herself appeared, declaring that Mugen had ordered Hikari's execution on sight. She destroyed the bridge beneath him, sending him plummeting into the chasm. Unconscious, Hikari relived the memory of his childhood encounter with Rai and Jin Mei and the bandit raid that claimed his mother's life.

Hikari awakened in a dungeon cell, where Rai Mei informed him of his scheduled execution. While acknowledging Hikari's strength of character, she insisted she could not abandon her clan or her brother's legacy. Shortly afterward, Kunzo unlocked the cell, begging Hikari to free Rai Mei from the fear dictating her actions. Fighting through the castle, Hikari confronted Rai Mei. Revealing he had long known Mugen orchestrated his mother's murder, Hikari affirmed he harbored no hatred toward Clan Mei, assuring Rai Mei that he had complete faith in her and would await her arrival at Ku.

#### Chapter 5
Reuniting with Kazan, Benkei, and an allied army of gladiators and rebel citizens, Hikari launched a full assault on Castle Ku under cover of a massive sandstorm engineered by Kazan. Pushing through the front gate, Hikari's forces reached the Bell of Dawn, where Mugen's general [Ageha](ageha.md) attempted to trap them. Hikari rang the bell, signaling their hidden reinforcements: Rai Mei and Clan Mei arrived, engaging Ageha so Hikari could advance.

Advancing toward the throne room, Hikari was intercepted by Ritsu, now promoted to [General Ritsu](ritsu_mishuyo.md). Forced into a duel to the death against his old friend, Hikari struck down Ritsu. Dying, Ritsu implored Hikari to build a world where his sister [Mikka Mishuyo](mikka_mishuyo.md) could live happily, and Hikari promised to forge a nation Ritsu would be proud of.

Hikari [confronted Mugen](mugen.md) and overpowered him. In fury, Mugen revealed he had arranged Kura's murder because of her lineage, stating that her clan had once attempted to save the world in ancient times. Hikari's shadow self urged him to slaughter Mugen and prove the supremacy of their bloodline. Hikari resisted, engaging in an internal battle for his soul. Though the darkness nearly overtook him, memories of his friends gave Hikari the strength to purge and destroy his shadow self for good.

Undaunted, Mugen seized the Darkblood Blade and was mutated by its dark power into the monstrous [Enshrouded King](mugen.md). Hikari stood firm and defeated his brother in one final, decisive clash, catching a glimpse of Mugen's aged face as he perished.

At Kazan's urging, Hikari declared victory and the end of the war. During his coronation, Hikari removed his crown, stating it was "too heavy" and proclaiming that he would serve not merely as king, but as a friend to all his subjects. He renounced Ku's bloody past, liberated subjugated clans and territories, and honored the fallen. Later, Hikari met Mikka at the Crest of Heroes to express his sorrow over Ritsu's death, standing together beneath a clear, cloudless sky.

### Crossed Paths: Hikari and Agnea

#### Part 1: Ryu
In Ryu, Hikari and [Agnea Bristarni](../agnea/agnea.md) heard the music of a biwa lute and encountered the traveling musician [Yomi](../others/yomi.md). Hikari paid to hear another song, but Yomi asked them to return the following evening so she could prepare. When they returned, Yomi explained that her lute string was broken; after Agnea procured horsehair to repair it, Yomi asked them to wait once more. On the third evening atop a hill, Yomi performed "By the Light of the Heart," a melody Hikari recognized as the favorite song of his late friend Tsuki.

#### Part 2: Ku
Following Ku's liberation, Hikari and Agnea surveyed the capital as paper lanterns were floated into the night sky to honor human souls. When Benkei suggested a victory celebration, Hikari declined, feeling celebration was inappropriate following such immense loss of life. Agnea proposed a festival of remembrance to commemorate the fallen, which Hikari and Benkei warmly accepted.

Seeking music for Agnea's dance, they investigated reports of lute music at the [Tranquil Grotto](../../locations/hinoeuma/dungeons/tranquil_grotto.md). There they found Yomi, who revealed she was Tsuki's sister and blamed Clan Ku for her death. Yomi challenged Hikari to combat, but Hikari subdued her without anger and invited her to play at the memorial. At the festival, Yomi performed "By the Light of the Heart" while Agnea danced in remembrance of the departed.

### The Journey for the Dawn
During the gathering of the travelers to halt the eternal night, Hikari discovered the bitter truth behind Kazan's identity: the tactician was actually [Oboro](oboro.md), the mastermind who had manipulated Ku's wars, Mugen's descent, and Hikari's rebellion to unseal the Darkblood Blade and resurrect the dark god [Vide](../../world/gods/vide.md). Despite the betrayal, Hikari stood firm with his comrades, confronting Oboro and declaring that the light would prevail over the darkness.

## Relationships

- **[Ritsu Mishuyo](ritsu_mishuyo.md)**: Hikari's childhood friend and fellow warrior. Despite the divide between prince and commoner, they trained together and shared dreams of a peaceful future. Driven by ambition to protect his sister, Ritsu joined Mugen and eventually viewed Hikari as an enemy. Even after multiple betrayals, Hikari sought reconciliation, refusing to execute Ritsu until their unavoidable final duel. In his dying moments, Ritsu entrusted Mikka's happiness to Hikari, and his memory aided Hikari during his spiritual victory over the Ku curse.
- **[Rai Mei](rai_mei.md)**: A close friend and head of Clan Mei. Though compelled by duty to obey Mugen's orders to execute Hikari, she harbored deep respect for the prince. Hikari refused to blame Clan Mei for his mother's murder, placing absolute trust in Rai Mei until she threw off Mugen's shackles and rallied her forces to his side at the Bell of Dawn.
- **[Kazan / Oboro](oboro.md)**: The brilliant tactician who orchestrated Hikari's military campaigns and the liberation of Ku. While Hikari was occasionally bewildered by Kazan's schemes and gambling habits, he admired his genius and trusted him implicitly. This confidence resulted in profound heartache when Kazan was revealed to be Oboro, the architect of Solistia's suffering who used Hikari's crusade to obtain the Darkblood Blade for Vide.
- **[General Mugen](mugen.md)**: Hikari's ruthless older half-brother. Where Hikari envisioned peace and community, Mugen sought conquest and destruction. Mugen despised Hikari as a "half-breed" whose mother tainted Clan Ku's lineage, ordering Kura's assassination and burning Ku to seize power. Hikari regarded Mugen as an irredeemable monster and fought him to liberate their homeland.
- **[King Jigo Ku](jigo_ku.md)**: Hikari's father and former King of Ku. Although their interactions appeared formal in public, Jigo recognized Hikari's compassion as the only way to save Ku from ruin. On his deathbed, Jigo entrusted the nation to Hikari, who wept and addressed him as "father."
- **[Kura](kura.md)**: Hikari's mother, a commoner whose values shaped Hikari into a kind and humble leader. Her murder by Mugen's assassins triggered Hikari's first awakening of the cursed bloodline. Hikari cherishes her memory, attributing his love for the common folk to her guidance.
- **[Benkei](benkei.md)**: A loyal retainer who watched over Hikari from boyhood. Benkei repeatedly placed his life on the line to safeguard the prince—most notably helping him escape Mugen's coup—and fought valiantly during the retaking of Castle Ku.
- **[Agnea Bristarni](../agnea/agnea.md)**: Fellow traveler and partner during their Crossed Paths. They share a warm and supportive friendship; Hikari introduces Agnea to Ku's customs, while Agnea helps Hikari channel his grief into a festival of remembrance for fallen souls.
- **[Ochette](../ochette/ochette.md)**: A traveling companion who affectionately calls him "Hikarin." Her keen beastly senses detect two distinct odors on him: a warm, gentle presence and an unsettling, pungent smell originating from his cursed blood.
- **[Osvald V. Vanstein](../osvald/osvald.md)**: Fellow traveler with whom Hikari shares intellectual camaraderie. Osvald recommended historical military chronicles to Hikari, and Hikari once playfully suggested to Osvald that "friendship" was the fabled seventh source of magic.
- **[Mikka Mishuyo](mikka_mishuyo.md)**: Ritsu's younger sister. Hikari promised Ritsu to build a peaceful Ku where Mikka could thrive, later visiting her at the Crest of Heroes to offer his condolences.
- **[Tsuki](tsuki.md)** & **[Yomi](../others/yomi.md)**: Tsuki was a close friend from Ku who was killed during Mugen's coup. Her sister Yomi, a wandering biwa musician, initially blamed Clan Ku for Tsuki's death, but reconciled with Hikari after dueling him and performing at Ku's memorial festival.
