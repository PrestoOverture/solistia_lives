# Rai Mei

"*My brother and I used to have faith in the future as well. But now there is only me. Not everyone can walk a path of their convictions. It never ends - one must be willing to leave everything else behind in their journey. And so instead of placing my faith in the future, I've chosen to put the safety of my clan above all.*" —Rai Mei

**Rai Mei** is the ruler and matriarch of [Clan Mei](../../world/factions/clan_mei.md), seated at [Castle Mei](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail_castle_mei.md) in [Stormhail](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail.md). A master spearwoman and former comrade-in-arms of [Hikari Ku](../travelers/hikari.md), she wields the renowned Spear of Levin.

## Profile

### Appearance
Rai Mei is a 28-year-old warrior with brown hair. She wears practical brown clothing suited for battle, an animal fur mantle across her shoulders, and a bright blue sash tied at her waist.

### Personality
Rai Mei is a resolute warrior and protective leader. In her youth, she shared Hikari's idealistic vision of peace, fellowship, and honor. However, following the death of her older brother [Jin Mei](jin_mei.md), she became gripped by pragmatic caution, prioritizing the preservation and survival of Clan Mei above all personal convictions.

Fearing the violent might of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md) and its royal bloodline, she bent the knee to the usurper King [Mugen](mugen.md) in hopes that loyalty would shield her clan from destruction. Despite preparing to execute Hikari to demonstrate fealty to Mugen, Rai Mei remained tormented by guilt, viewing herself as an unprincipled coward and begging Hikari to execute her after their duel. When Hikari spared her and reaffirmed his faith in her honor, Rai Mei regained her courage, resolving to risk everything to fight for a just future.

Towards her subjects in Stormhail, Rai Mei is warm, dutiful, and protective. In personal matters, she gives a icy reception to her bodyguard [Kunzo](kunzo.md) whenever he meddles in her private life by trying to arrange suitors for her.

## History

### Background
In her youth, Rai Mei received instruction in the martial arts from Kunzo, who later became her devoted retainer and personal bodyguard.

Three years before the main events, Rai Mei fought alongside Prince Hikari and [Ritsu Mishuyo](ritsu_mishuyo.md) on the battlefield against rival border forces. Following Mugen's execution of the opposing general, the three comrades gathered at the Crest of Heroes, promising to share a victory toast once lasting peace was established across [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md). However, the war concluded abruptly, and the companions parted ways. Rai Mei returned north to [Stormhail](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail.md) to assume leadership of Clan Mei following the death of her older brother Jin Mei.

### Confrontation at Castle Mei
When Hikari traveled to Stormhail seeking Clan Mei's support to depose King Mugen, Rai Mei adamantly refused. Believing resistance against Ku was futile and determined to safeguard her clan, she struck Hikari unconscious with her spear—destroying the fortress drawbridge in the process—and remanded him to Castle Mei's dungeons to await execution as an offering of loyalty to Mugen.

Kunzo secretly liberated Hikari from the dungeon, unwilling to watch his lady succumb entirely to fear. Hikari confronted Rai Mei atop the [Castle Mei: Gallows](../../locations/winterlands/dungeons/castle_mei_gallows.md). Despite Hikari's pleas for reconciliation, Rai Mei drew her spear and attacked him. Upon her defeat, Rai Mei broke down, confessing that Clan Mei bore historical responsibility for the assassination of Hikari's mother. Convinced that she was stained beyond redemption, she begged Hikari to execute her. Hikari refused, asserting that they could forge a new path together, and departed while expressing complete confidence that she would choose honor.

### Battle for Ku and Restoration
Moved by Hikari's unyielding trust, Rai Mei marshaled Clan Mei's warriors and marched to Castle Ku. She arrived in the midst of the decisive battle shortly after Hikari rang the Bell of Dawn, breaking through the enemy ranks and dueling General [Ageha](ageha.md) to clear a path for Hikari into the castle keep.

Following Mugen's defeat, Rai Mei celebrated the end of the conflict and witnessed Hikari's coronation as King of Ku. With peace restored, Clan Mei reestablished close diplomatic ties with Ku, with Rai Mei impressing the new monarch with her statesmanship and diplomatic finesse.

During the realm-wide festivities following the return of the dawn, Rai Mei journeyed on horseback to [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md), determined to arrive on time for [Agnea Bristarni](../travelers/agnea.md)'s gala performance.
