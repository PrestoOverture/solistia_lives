# Zeto

> *"Nothing personal, but this isn't over until one of us is dead. This prison hungers for blood."*
> — Zeto

## Overview

- **Age**: 33
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Aliases**: Zeto the Butcher, The Butcher
- **Occupation**: Gladiator, warrior, builder
- **Location**: [Ku: Castle Town](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/ku_castle_town.md) (day); [Castle Ku](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/castle_ku.md) (night); formerly [Montwise arena](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/montwise_underground_arena.md)

**Zeto the Butcher** is a character in *Octopath Traveler II* who features prominently during [Hikari](../travelers/hikari.md)'s journey. A seasoned gladiator in the [Montwise arena](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/montwise_underground_arena.md) boasting a streak of 22 consecutive victories, he is defined by an unwavering dedication to his comrades-in-arms.

## Profile

### Appearance

Zeto wears a green and brown tunic with leather accents, accompanied by a green headband. He fights wielding a pair of razor claws.

### Personality

Zeto is a calm and honorable warrior with a profound sense of integrity. He cares deeply for his comrades, referring to those he would risk his life for as his "brothers." This protective devotion unwittingly stems from his inability to protect his biological brothers from the ravages of war. When bested in combat by Hikari, Zeto is ready to surrender his life to honor arena custom, but he chooses to take up arms beside Hikari after the prince spares him and helps free the gladiators from Lord [Borneau](borneau.md)'s arena.

## Story

In Chapter 2 of Hikari's story, Zeto stands as Hikari's penultimate arena opponent. While harboring no personal malice toward the swordsman, Zeto maintains that only one combatant can leave the arena alive. Despite his formidable combat skills, Zeto is defeated and urges Hikari to finish him. Hikari refuses to execute him, defying both the bloodthirsty crowd and his own inner darkness.

Infuriated by Hikari's refusal, arena master Borneau sends his champion [Bandelam](bandelam.md) to execute Zeto. Hikari intervenes and takes Zeto's place as Bandelam's opponent. On the day of the duel, Zeto participates in a gladiatorial uprising, holding Borneau and his men hostage. Following Bandelam's defeat, Hikari persuades Zeto to abandon his grievances and start anew. After [Kazan](oboro.md) secures the gladiators' freedom, Zeto and his men pledge their loyalty to Hikari, vowing to fight alongside him to repay the debt they owe.

In Chapter 5, Zeto joins the coalition of warriors fighting alongside Hikari to reclaim [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md). After the victory is won, Zeto remains in [Ku: Castle Town](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/ku_castle_town.md), devoting himself to rebuilding the town and [Castle Ku](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/castle_ku.md) brick by brick and establishing a new home for himself and his brothers. During the Epilogue, he can be seen complimenting [Alpione](../ochette/alpione.md) on her "beautiful muscles."
