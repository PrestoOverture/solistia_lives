# Benkei

> "*For the true king! Your Majesty, leave these curs to me! You hold the future of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md) in your hands. Your father chose to put his faith in you, and so shall I!*"
> — Benkei, defending Hikari from Mugen's troops

**Benkei** is the staunch retainer and personal bodyguard to King [Jigo Ku](jigo_ku.md) and later to Prince [Hikari Ku](hikari.md) in *Octopath Traveler II*. Regarded by Hikari as the strongest person in all of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md), he serves the royal court with unwavering fidelity.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 38
- **Gender**: Male
- **Occupation**: Retainer and Bodyguard
- **Affiliation**: Kingdom of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md)
- **Status**: Alive

### Appearance
Benkei is a tall, bald warrior who dresses in a dark robe draped with a white shawl. In battle, he wields a spear.

### Personality
Benkei is steadfast, intensely loyal, and deeply patriotic, always standing ready at his master's side. Though normally composed and dignified, travel banter notes that he unleashes a thunderous war cry in the heat of battle. He possesses immense courage, unafraid to confront multiple hostile soldiers simultaneously and gladly risking his life for the rightful ruler of Ku.

## Biography

### Youth as Musashi
In his youth, Benkei went by the name Musashi. He spent his early years wandering the realm and testing his combat prowess against fellow warriors. He remained undefeated until the young King [Jigo Ku](jigo_ku.md), having heard tales of his strength, sought him out and challenged him to a duel. Following his defeat, Musashi changed his name to Benkei and swore fealty to Jigo, rising over the years to become the king's most trusted advisor and paramount bodyguard.

### Mugen's Coup
In Hikari's youth, Benkei summoned the prince to a private audience with his father. Benkei stood guard outside the chamber while King Jigo disclosed his plan to name Hikari as his successor, despite the impending coup being organized by Hikari's warlike half-brother, [General Mugen](mugen.md).

When Mugen launched his assault on the palace and assassinated King Jigo, Benkei was absent and unable to shield his lord. Discovering Hikari encircled by Mugen's soldiers, Benkei charged in, holding off the soldiers alone so Hikari could escape Castle Ku. Before parting, Benkei exhorted Hikari to gather trusted allies across Solistia and return to liberate Ku from Mugen's tyrannical grip.

### Liberation of Ku
When Hikari returned to Ku with his assembled allies, Benkei reunited with the prince and fought by his side during the siege of Castle Ku. Together, they defeated Mugen, reclaimed the kingdom, and placed Hikari upon the throne.

Following the victory, Benkei suggested holding a celebration to honor those who lost their lives in the war. Within the restored castle, he reflected upon sealing away the Bell of Dawn—an ancient instrument whose chime had summoned Ku to war for generations—hoping it would never toll again, while remaining mindful of the arduous tasks still needed to rebuild the realm.
