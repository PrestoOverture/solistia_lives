# Kunzo

> *"This castle belongs to [House Mei](../../world/factions/clan_mei.md). There shall be no violence here."*  
> — Kunzo, at [Stormhail: Castle Mei](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail_castle_mei.md)

**Kunzo** is a longtime vassal of [Clan Mei](../../world/factions/clan_mei.md) in *Octopath Traveler II*. He serves as a devoted retainer and bodyguard to [Rai Mei](rai_mei.md) during [Hikari Ku](hikari.md)'s storyline.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 58
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Stormhail: Castle Mei](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail_castle_mei.md)
- **Affiliation**: [Clan Mei](../../world/factions/clan_mei.md)

### Appearance
Kunzo is an older warrior who styles his blond hair into a mohawk paired with a beard. He wears blue robes suited to Clan Mei retainers and wields a spear.

### Personality
Kunzo is a loyal, honorable, and fair-minded protector. Having raised Rai Mei and her brother from childhood, he prioritizes her true well-being above blind obedience, demonstrating the resolve to disobey her orders when he sees she is being consumed by fear and trapped in a destructive path.

## Biography

Kunzo served Clan Mei for decades and stepped into a paternal role for both [Jin Mei](jin_mei.md) and Rai Mei after they were orphaned at an early age, training both siblings in martial arts.

During Chapter 4 of Hikari's journey, Hikari arrives in [Stormhail](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail.md) hoping to secure Clan Mei's alliance against [Mugen](mugen.md). Kunzo and his guards confront Hikari on the castle bridge, compelled by Rai Mei's commands to attack. Although Hikari overcomes them in battle, Rai Mei collapses the bridge, capturing Hikari and slating him for execution to demonstrate fealty to Mugen.

Recognizing that Rai Mei's loyalty to Mugen is motivated purely by fear for her clan following her brother Jin's tragic death, Kunzo defies her orders. He infiltrates the castle dungeon, frees Hikari, and implores the exiled prince to liberate Rai Mei from the fear that holds her captive. Kunzo's intervention allows Hikari to confront and reconcile with Rai Mei, securing Clan Mei's allegiance in the final assault against Mugen.

With peace restored between Clan Mei and [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md), Kunzo considers the heavy burden lifted from his shoulders. His remaining goal in life is to see Rai Mei settled with a worthy partner.

## Lore & Details

- Kunzo's twenty-two-year-old nephew resides in Stormhail and harbors a secret love for Rai Mei, aligning with Kunzo's desire to find a suitable match for his lady.
- A Clan Mei soldier's account indicates that defeating Kunzo in single combat is considered a rite of passage to become a recognized vassal of Clan Mei.
