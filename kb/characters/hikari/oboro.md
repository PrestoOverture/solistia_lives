# Oboro

**Oboro**, widely known across [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md) under the guise of **Kazan**, the "Eagle of Ku," was a brilliant military strategist for the realm of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md). Secretly an elite agent of the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md), he served as the overarching antagonist of [Hikari Ku](../travelers/hikari.md)'s journey and a chief instigator behind the awakening of [Vide the Wicked](../../world/gods/vide.md) during [The Journey for the Dawn](../../stories/main/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md).

## Overview

- **True Identity**: Oboro (agent of the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md))
- **Alias**: Kazan, the "Eagle of Ku"
- **Age**: 35
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Relatives**: [Ori](../partitio/ori.md) (adoptive younger sister)

> "*The Eagle of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md) is not bound by myth - he makes it.*"
> — Kazan to Hikari Ku

### Appearance

Oboro appears prematurely aged, with graying hair tied back in a ponytail and a scruffy beard. He wore a loose white strategist's robe over simple traveler's garments, with a liquor gourd hanging perpetually at his hip.

### Personality

As the tactician Kazan, he cultivated a reputation as an eccentric, irreverent savant who indulged heavily in gambling and wine while crafting masterstrokes of battlefield strategy and statecraft. Hikari observed that Kazan possessed a distinct tell when lying or hiding his true intentions: he would unconsciously conceal or fidget with his drinking cup.

Beneath the playful facade, Oboro was deeply scarred by the violence, subjugation, and bloodshed perpetuated by Ku's expansionist wars. This prolonged trauma turned him into an embittered, patient nihilist who concluded that human nature was irredeemably brutal and unworthy of survival. Believing that the world deserved to be swallowed by [Shadow](../../world/lore/shadow.md), Oboro spent decades maneuvering within enemy courts, waiting for the right pieces to align. In his own words, there is no inherent good or evil in the mortal world—only belief.

## History

### Early Life and the Fall of U

Oboro and his younger adoptive sister, [Ori](../partitio/ori.md), were lowborn citizens of the kingdom of [U](../../world/nations/u.md) (likely hailing from [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md)) whose homeland was ravaged and conquered by the kingdom of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md). Consumed by vengeance, the young Oboro attempted to assassinate King [Jigo Ku](jigo_ku.md). While the attempt failed, Jigo was intrigued by the youth's resourcefulness and daring escape tactics. Instead of executing him, Jigo appointed Oboro as his court strategist, giving rise to the persona of Kazan.

In his early years serving Ku, Kazan occasionally directed policy toward helping the downtrodden. On one occasion, he startled King Jigo by abruptly asking, *"Shall we begin digging for salt?"* Recognizing the desperate poverty of the common people before anyone else, Kazan convinced the king to begin domestic salt mining. However, witnessing the continuous carnage sanctioned by Jigo and his ruthless elder son [Mugen](mugen.md) extinguished Oboro's lingering faith in mankind.

### Alignment with the Moonshade Order

Approached by emissaries of the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md), Oboro was given the *Book of Night*. Rather than being shocked by the cult's apocalyptic teachings, Oboro found them underwhelming, as he had already independently arrived at the same nihilistic conclusions. He allied with the order, covertly encouraging Mugen's ambitions to secure the Darkblood Blade, an ancient ritual weapon required to extinguish the [Sacred Flames](../../world/lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md). When Oboro realized that Mugen intended to keep the blade to fuel his own tyranny rather than hand it over, Oboro feigned disinterest and departed for [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md) after the war ended. His thoughts and schemes were recorded in [Oboro's Journal](../../stories/documents/oboros_journal_script.md), kept hidden in [Castle Ku](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/castle_ku.md).

### The Montwise Underground Arena

In Montwise, Kazan formed a sympathetic bond with the enslaved gladiators of the [Underground Arena](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/montwise_underground_arena.md). When Prince Hikari arrived in search of allies, Kazan coerced him into fighting in the arena ring under the pretense of paying off Kazan's exorbitant gambling debts to the corrupt proprietor [Borneau](borneau.md). 

Using Hikari's winning streak, Kazan placed increasingly enormous wagers, culminating in a colossal bet on Hikari's showdown against [Bandelam the Reaper](bandelam.md) that netted three billion leaves. When Borneau proved unable to pay out the prize, Kazan claimed ownership of the entire arena instead, freeing the combatants. He then presented the hardened gladiators—including [Zeto the Butcher](zeto.md)—to Hikari as a devoted force of loyal soldiers. Kazan advised Hikari to sabotage Ku's weapons trade in [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md) and win over Clan Mei through [Rai Mei](rai_mei.md), while he traveled to [Ryu](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/ryu.md).

### The Liberation of Ku and the Darkblood Blade

When Hikari returned to liberate Ku, Kazan met him at the Crest of Heroes and unveiled his siege plan: creating an artificial sandstorm to blind the fortress defenses and allow Hikari's forces to breach the gate. The strategy succeeded, culminating in Mugen's death at Hikari's hands.

While Hikari went to announce the tyrant's fall to the soldiers, Oboro slipped into the throne room and claimed the Darkblood Blade. Confronting [Ageha](ageha.md), Oboro revealed his true identity, disclosing that he had orchestrated both Mugen's rise and his overthrow purely to bring the Darkblood Blade within his grasp. Oboro sacrificed Ageha with the blade to extinguish the Sacred Flame of Ku.

### The Journey for the Dawn and Sacrificial Death

During the eternal night of [The Journey for the Dawn](../../stories/main/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md), the eight travelers journeyed to the island altar of [Vidania](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/vidania.md). Oboro confronted them at the summit, asking whether the blood-soaked road they had walked to find the dawn was worth everything they had suffered and destroyed. Receiving their steadfast reply and lamenting the irredeemable state of the world, Oboro plunged the dagger into his own chest, sacrificing his life upon the altar to fully awaken [Vide](../../world/gods/vide.md).
