# Jin Mei

> *"The failure was entirely mine, and I accept my punishment. I only beg you spare my clan... May [Clan Mei](../../world/factions/clan_mei.md) live on..."*
> — Jin Mei, final words to [Mugen](mugen.md)

**Jin Mei** was the elder brother of [Rai Mei](rai_mei.md) and a prominent warrior of [Clan Mei](../../world/factions/clan_mei.md). A dedicated swordsman and mentor to the young prince [Hikari Ku](hikari.md), Jin Mei sought peace and prosperity for his clan through their alliance with [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md), but was ultimately coerced into a tragic political conspiracy that cost him his life.

## Profile

### Appearance
Jin Mei had short brown hair and wore practical brown-and-blue armor. While the warriors of Clan Mei traditionally specialize in the spear, Jin Mei stood out by choosing the sword as his weapon of choice.

### Personality
Jin Mei was a disciplined, honorable idealist who believed that Clan Mei's submission and loyalty to the kingdom of Ku would foster genuine fellowship and bring long-lasting peace to [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md). A man of action rather than words, he preferred to communicate through his blade and disliked deceit. As Hikari's sword instructor, he emphasized patience, control, and technique over raw physical power. However, his absolute sense of fealty to the ruling house of Ku bound him to follow the dark orders of [General Mugen](mugen.md) against his own conscience. When the scheme unraveled, Jin Mei displayed immense nobility, willingly facing execution to shield his sister and clan from retribution.

## Story

After Clan Mei swore fealty to Ku, Jin Mei was entrusted with training the young prince Hikari in the art of the sword, teaching him the signature martial technique *Hachimonjigiri*.

When war broke out between Ku and its regional rivals, Clan Mei was summoned to the front lines. Before departing, Jin Mei bid young Hikari farewell, advising him to stay vigilant against cutthroats in the lower districts. In secret, however, Jin Mei had been ordered by Mugen to eliminate Hikari and his mother, [Kura](kura.md), staging the assassination as a random street robbery by contracting local bandits.

The ambush quickly turned into catastrophe. The cutthroats fatally wounded Lady Kura, and the boy's overwhelming trauma unleashed the dormant shadow curse within Clan Ku's royal bloodline. The awakened entity, ["Hikari"](hikari_shadow.md), slaughtered the assassins in a feral frenzy.

When Jin Mei arrived at the scene, he found the assassins dead and the young prince possessed by uncontrollable bloodlust. Ordering Rai Mei to stay back, Jin Mei confessed his role in the tragic bloodshed and fought desperately to subdue the feral prince, barely managing to fend off the dark entity until Hikari lost consciousness.

Afterward, Jin Mei returned alone to Mugen and accepted sole responsibility for the botched assassination. Facing death with stoic dignity, Jin Mei offered his life in exchange for the safety of his people, begging Mugen to spare Clan Mei. Mugen granted the request and had Jin Mei executed, leaving Rai Mei to inherit the leadership of the clan burdened by the truth of her brother's sacrifice.
