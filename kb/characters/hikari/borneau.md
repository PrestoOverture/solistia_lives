# Borneau

> *"I've no use for product that avails me no profit. Your flesh and blood are mine, and you will devote it to seeing our guests delighted!"*
> — Borneau, to his gladiators

## Overview

- **Age**: 55
- **Gender**: Male
- **Occupation**: Arena master, showman
- **Location**: [Crackridge Harbor: Anchorage](../../locations/wildlands/wilds/crackridge_harbor_anchorage.md); formerly [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md)

**Borneau** is a ruthless showman and former master of the [underground arena](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/montwise_underground_arena.md) in [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md). Born into a traveling circus troupe and exposed to theatrical spectacles from an early age, he concluded that violence drew the largest crowds and yielded the highest profits.

## Appearance

Borneau is a portly man dressed in a dark coat and a feathered hat.

## Personality

Callous, greedy, and devoid of empathy, Borneau views human life purely as commercial merchandise. He coerces individuals trapped by gambling debts into entering gladiatorial death matches, declaring that their blood and flesh belong to him. Knowing his cruelty makes him loathed by the fighters, he relies on his champion [Bandelam](bandelam.md) for security; once deprived of his bodyguard, his arrogance collapses into pathetic cowardice.

## Story

During [Hikari](hikari.md)'s visit to Montwise, the strategist [Kazan](oboro.md) enters Hikari into underground matches to clear substantial gambling debts owed to Borneau. Kazan repeatedly places escalating wagers on Hikari's triumphs.

Borneau is first encountered berating his captive gladiators, demanding they fight for his profit. When [Zeto the Butcher](zeto.md) steps forward and is defeated, Hikari refuses to execute him. Viewing mercy as an insult to paying spectators, Borneau orders Bandelam to finish Zeto, but Hikari intervenes and challenges Bandelam directly. To appease the baying audience, Borneau sanctions the championship bout.

During the battle, mutinous gladiators covertly organized by Kazan breach the viewing balcony and take Borneau hostage. Deprived of Bandelam's protection, the cowardly arena master is helpless. Following Bandelam's defeat, the vengeful gladiators bring Borneau down into the ring to kill him, but Hikari once again advocates for mercy, ending the violence. Borneau is forced to relinquish the arena's deed and liberate all captive fighters to pay out Kazan's winning wager.

Having lost everything in Montwise, Borneau flees across the sea to [Crackridge Harbor: Anchorage](../../locations/wildlands/wilds/crackridge_harbor_anchorage.md), where he schemes to build a new fortune by establishing an arena for monster fights.
