# Bandit Chief

## Overview

- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Occupation**: Bandit leader, hired assassin

The **Bandit Chief** was the leader of a band of cutthroats hired by [Jin Mei](jin_mei.md) on the secret orders of [Mugen](mugen.md) to assassinate Lady [Kura](kura.md) and the young prince [Hikari Ku](../travelers/hikari.md).

## Appearance

He had long black hair and fought wielding a dagger.

## Personality

Ruthless, greedy, and merciless, the Bandit Chief showed no hesitation in slaughtering an unarmed mother and attempting to murder a young child to stage an assassination as a common street robbery.

## Story

Years before the events of the main story, Prince [Mugen](mugen.md) plotted to eliminate his younger half-brother Hikari and Hikari's mother, [Kura](kura.md). To disguise the political assassination as an ordinary crime, Mugen instructed [Jin Mei](jin_mei.md) to contract local ruffians.

The Bandit Chief and two accomplices ambushed Kura and Hikari in the streets of [Ku: Castle Town](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/ku_castle_town.md), fatally cutting down Kura before turning their blades toward the young prince. Overcome by trauma and grief, Hikari's latent cursed blood awakened, releasing his bloodthirsty shadow self, ["Hikari"](hikari_shadow.md). In a feral frenzy of violence, the young prince struck down one of the thugs before slaughtering the Bandit Chief and his remaining cohort, making them the first victims claimed by Hikari's awakened shadow.
