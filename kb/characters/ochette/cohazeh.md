# Cohazeh

**Cohazeh** is the 42-year-old village chief of the human settlement adjacent to [Beasting Village](../../locations/totohaha/towns/beasting_village.md) on the island of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md). A sharp, pragmatic leader with a background in economics, she initially clashes with [Juvah](juvah.md) and the native [Beastlings](../../world/species/beastling.md) over land and resources before uniting with them during the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../../world/lore/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 42
- **Gender**: Female
- **Occupation**: Village Chief, Pioneer
- **Location**: [Beasting Village](../../locations/totohaha/towns/beasting_village.md), [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md)
- **Status**: Alive

### Appearance
Cohazeh wears a purple robe and a golden circlet signifying her authority as chief. She carries a bow for defense.

### Personality
Cohazeh is a cold, strict, and calculating leader whose authority is dreaded by her fellow villagers. Driven by the memory of her father—a merchant who went bankrupt—she holds a firm conviction that constant economic expansion and material development are essential to security and happiness. 

Cool and collected, Cohazeh favors diplomatic maneuvering over open conflict, often utilizing appeasement and bargaining to advance her goals. In the early days of the settlement, she harbored prejudice toward the Beastlings, dismissing them as "animals" lacking ambition and desire because they lived in harmony with nature rather than exploiting it. However, beneath her harsh demeanor lies a genuine dedication to protecting her people. When real danger threatens the island, she proves capable of setting aside grievances to fight alongside the beastlings and broker lasting peace.

## History

In her youth, Cohazeh studied economics at the academy in [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md). During her studies, she formed a close academic partnership and friendship with [Clarissa](../osvald/clarissa.md), using her personal finances and analytical acumen to keep their research endeavors afloat. The two parting ways after an argument caused when Cohazeh accidentally spilled tea across Clarissa's manuscript, last seeing one another at the Pit of [D'arqest](../others/darqest.md).

Aiming to use her education to secure a lordship, Cohazeh led a contingent of human pioneers to Toto'haha, where she met her late husband. Settling on the perimeter of Beasting Village, Cohazeh established a human enclave, frequently clashing with the island's warden, Master Juvah, over territorial boundaries and her settlers' attempts to dismantle ancient ruins to construct homes.

## Story

### Ochette's Tale
When tensions flare after several human settlers act aggressively toward the beastlings, Cohazeh brings an offering of meat to Juvah as a gesture of goodwill. In exchange, she requests assistance in locating a young human girl who went missing near the [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md). Juvah remains wary, but his apprentice [Ochette](ochette.md) eagerly volunteers. After Ochette rescues the child and vanquishes the [Dark Entity](dark_entity.md) threatening the woods, Cohazeh returns to express her gratitude and agrees to a temporary truce.

As the beastlings later brace for the cataclysmic [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../../world/lore/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md), Cohazeh approaches Juvah and Ochette to resume discussions over territorial rights. She scoffs at Juvah's warnings of an impending cosmic disaster, only to return home and find her settlement under assault by shadowy fiends. Frantic, Cohazeh initially accuses Juvah of concealing information, but quickly regains her composure and orders her settlers to join forces with the beastlings. Following the defeat of the Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon, Cohazeh witnesses the dawn of peace and formally ends hostilities with the beastlings, pledging that both communities will live and work together as equals.

### Lady Clarissa's Visit
Following the crisis, Cohazeh reunites at the village entrance with Lady Clarissa, who travels to Toto'haha to examine the local ruins. Touring the ancient structures together, the two women reminisce about their academy days in Montwise and explore the historical lore of the beastlings, parting on warm terms with plans to meet again.
