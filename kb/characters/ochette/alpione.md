# Alpione

**Alpione**, also known as the **Guardian of the Waves**, is a 30-year-old guardian and hunter who watches over the sacred resting grounds of the sea god [Cateracta](../../world/beasts/cateracta.md).

## Identity and Heritage

Alpione is the descendant of a line of guardians who have watched over Cateracta for generations. Her ancestral roots trace back thousands of years to [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md); her human ancestors were originally [beastlings](../../world/species/beastling.md) who departed the island alongside Cateracta. Because of this ancestry, traces of beastling blood flow through her veins, granting her an unusually keen sense of smell that allows her to detect the distinct scent of beastlings.

Alpione possesses slate-grey eyes, brown skin, and short brown hair secured by a blue headband. She wears a greyish-blue dress over black shorts, bound with a teal sash, along with brown sandals. She possesses a conspicuously powerful, muscular physique.

## Personality

Deeply devoted to the natural order, Alpione views predation and survival as natural facets of existence, but harbors profound contempt for human greed:

> "*That's life for you. Every living thing devours life. Simply to stay alive. But humans are ugly. They kill out of avarice and greed.*"

Having witnessed the butchery and looting of Cateracta's remains by hunters, Alpione grew isolated, cynical, and hostile toward outsiders, fiercely barring all travelers from approaching the sacred grounds. Beneath her gruff exterior, however, she possesses a protective and compassionate spirit that reawakens upon encountering genuine selflessness.

## Story

### Vigil over Cateracta

Years before, Cateracta exhausted her divine strength saving [Conning Creek](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md) from a devastating tsunami. While weakened, the sea god and her guardians were ambushed by hunters led by the [Dark Hunter](../others/petrichor.md). The assailants slaughtered the defenders and killed Cateracta, leaving Alpione as the sole survivor. Before perishing, Cateracta left behind a single unhatched egg within the [Cavern of the Sea God](../../locations/harborlands/dungeons/cavern_of_the_sea_god.md). Alpione retreated into solitary isolation, dedicating her life to protecting the egg from desecration.

When [Ochette](../travelers/ochette.md) arrives in Conning Creek seeking Cateracta's aid against the Night of the Scarlet Moon, Alpione suspects her of being another poacher and refuses to permit passage to the island cavern. After Ochette bests her in combat, Alpione follows her into the cavern and discloses that Cateracta is already dead.

Upon realizing Ochette's sincere bond with the legendary beasts and her mission to save Solistia, Alpione relents. She shares a meal with Ochette and guides her deep into the cavern to reveal Cateracta's egg. Alpione watches as Ochette hatches the newborn divine beast, naming him [Acta](acta.md). Relieved of the crushing weight of her vigil, Alpione entrusts Acta to Ochette's care.

### A New Journey

With her sacred duty fulfilled, Alpione travels the continent, eventually reaching [Gravell](../../locations/wildlands/towns/gravell.md). Unaccustomed to idleness, she seeks work suited to her tracking abilities. Taking up a job posting from local merchants regarding a dangerous beast, she discovers that the merchants are tormenting an Armor Eater. Alpione drives off the merchants, soothes the beast—noting that its mannerisms remind her of Cateracta—and befriends it.

Alpione and her Armor Eater companion dedicate themselves to traveling the world to protect and rescue mistreated beasts. They later settle near the coast at [Beasting Bay: Anchorage](../../locations/totohaha/wilds/beasting_bay_anchorage.md), where Alpione finds peace listening to the ocean surf.
