# Akalā

> *"Grrrrrr… I am… strong."*
> — Akalā, before [Ochette](../travelers/ochette.md) chooses her companion

**Akalā** is a male lājackal described as radiant in the sun's grace, with formidable fangs that tear the flesh of his prey. If [Ochette](../travelers/ochette.md) selects him as her companion, he serves as both a younger and older brother to her throughout her journey across Solistia.

## Profile

### Appearance
Akalā is an orange-brown jackal with yellow eyes and a white sun marking on his back. He wears a pair of blue bracers around his forelegs. In his awakened form, he resembles a healthy version of the [Lājackal of the Sorrowful Moon](../../world/beasts/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md), bearing a strip of blue fur along the base of his tail.

### Personality
Akalā is calm, collected, and steadfast. Whenever Ochette becomes impatient or impulsive, he steps in to remind her of her duties and responsibilities. According to Ochette, he is as honest as they come, acting as a loyal friend who plays the straight man to her wise girl persona. Ochette considers him the brains of their partnership, though she occasionally chides him for his innate mistrust and hostility toward humans. Akalā does not enjoy being petted by strangers, maintaining that as long as he has Ochette, he needs no other companions.

Despite having a keen sense of smell, Akalā admits that Ochette's nose is even sharper than his own. Though he denies it, Ochette notices that he secretly enjoys frolicking in the snow.

## History

### Background
Ten years prior to the main story, [Juvah](juvah.md) brought the young hunter Ochette to the [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md), where Akalā and the malamaowl [Mahina](mahina.md) were waiting. Juvah allowed Ochette to select one beast to be her lifelong hunting partner.

If Ochette selects Akalā, Mahina is immediately overtaken by darkness, attacks Ochette, and flees into the wilderness. If Ochette chooses Mahina instead, Akalā is shrouded in darkness, goes berserk, attacks Ochette, and flees, eventually becoming corrupted into the [Lājackal of the Sorrowful Moon](../../world/beasts/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md)—the final adversary Ochette must face to save the island.

### Journey with Ochette
If chosen as Ochette's companion, Akalā accompanies her throughout all of her story chapters, including her Crossed Paths chapters with [Castti Florenz](../travelers/castti.md).

Ten years after their partnership begins, Akalā and Ochette hunt down the gluttonous [King Iguana](../../world/beasts/king_iguana.md), whose excessive eating threatens the island's natural order. When Ochette is tempted to eat the captured creature herself, Akalā reminds her that the King Iguana's meat is meant for the people of [Beasting Village](../../locations/totohaha/towns/beasting_village.md).

Akalā subsequently journeys alongside Ochette across Solistia to locate the three [Creatures of Legend](../../world/lore/creatures_of_legend.md): [Cateracta](../../world/beasts/cateracta.md), [Tera](../../world/beasts/tera.md), and [Glacis](../../world/beasts/glacis.md).

During the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../../world/lore/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md), Akalā and Ochette dive together into the ocean, where Akalā receives the power of the [First Flame](../../world/lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md). With its power, Akalā helps Ochette defeat the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../../world/beasts/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md), saving [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md) from the calamity.
