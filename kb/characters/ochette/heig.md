# Heig

> *"Humans and beasts are made to hate and kill each other."*
> — Heig, to [Ochette](ochette.md)

**Heig** is a 58-year-old veteran hunter from [Stormhail](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail.md) who plays a central role in [Ochette](ochette.md)'s quest to find the legendary beast [Glacis](../../world/beasts/glacis.md) atop [Sacred Peak Altahe](../../locations/winterlands/dungeons/sacred_peak_altahe.md). A longtime resident of the region, he earns a living guiding foreign hunters on expeditions into the treacherous mountains.

## Profile

### Appearance
Heig has greying hair tied back into a ponytail, a matching grey beard, and wears a thick navy blue parka suited for the subzero climate of the Winterlands. He is an archer and carries a hunting bow.

### Personality
Traumatized by a catastrophic expedition years earlier that claimed the lives of his entire hunting crew, Heig became bitter, cynical, and fatalistic. He came to believe that humans and beasts are destined only for mutual slaughter and hatred, dismissing noble motives for killing. Carrying immense survivor's guilt, he harbored a death wish, resolving to die on the mountain once he finished giving his fallen comrades proper burials. After Ochette challenges his cynicism, soothes Glacis, and drags him to safety, Heig gradually confronts his guilt, rediscovers his respect for hunting for sustenance, and finds the will to live.

## Story

In his youth, Heig was once trapped in a blinding blizzard during a hunt and believed he would perish, but the storm cleared miraculously, sparing his life. Years later, he was approached by the [Dark Hunter](../others/petrichor.md), who planted the idea of slaying the legendary frost-beast Glacis for fame and wealth. Swept up in the promise of glory, Heig led an expedition up Sacred Peak Altahe. The hunt ended in catastrophe: Glacis slaughtered the entire party, leaving Heig as the sole survivor. Haunted by guilt, Heig spent years returning to Altahe's peaks to locate the frozen bodies of his comrades and give them proper burials.

When Ochette arrives in Stormhail seeking Glacis, her loud howl triggers a small avalanche on the lower slopes. Heig investigates the noise and encounters the young beastling, sternly warning her to turn back because the mountain "snows with hate."

Higher up the slopes, Ochette encounters Heig speaking to a frozen corpse in the snow—his nephew, the last missing hunter of the ill-fated expedition. With his nephew finally laid to rest and his duty complete, Heig resolves to climb to the summit to kill Glacis or perish in the attempt.

At the summit, Ochette discovers Heig collapsed and gravely wounded before the enraged guardian. Spotting broken eggs within Glacis's nest, Ochette learns from Heig that his party smashed them in spiteful retribution when they realized they could not fell the beast. Horrified by the hunters' cruelty, Ochette subdues Glacis in battle and reminds her of her sacred duty to protect the realm, calming her wrath.

Witnessing Glacis pacified, Heig loses his purpose and insists on staying behind to freeze to death on the summit. Refusing to let him die, Ochette drags him down the mountain against his protests and brings him safely home to his cabin near [Stormhail: Bridge](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail_bridge.md).

Resting in his cabin, Heig opens up about his past, confessing his regret over listening to the Dark Hunter and being blinded by greed. Comforted by Ochette, who shares dried jerky with him, Heig finds peace and befriends the young hunter.

## Relationships & Banter

- **[Ochette](ochette.md):** Although initially dismissing her and warning her away from Altahe, Heig is profoundly moved by her ability to quell Glacis without hatred. Ochette's refusal to leave him behind restores his desire to live.
- **[Castti Florenz](../castti/castti.md):** While Castti treated his wounds following the ordeal on Altahe, Heig was flustered and could not stop blushing in her presence.
- **[Partitio Yellowil](../partitio/partitio.md):** Heig shared a sample of the local Winterlands liquor with Partitio, which Partitio and Ochette subsequently drank together.
- **[Agnea Bristarni](../agnea/agnea.md):** Heig has visited [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md) in the past, and he later travels there to attend Agnea's grand theatrical performance.
