# Acta

**Acta** is a newly-hatched [sea serpent](../../world/species/aquatics.md) and the offspring of the late [Cateracta](../../world/beasts/cateracta.md). 

## Profile

- **Species**: [Sea serpent](../../world/species/aquatics.md)
- **Status**: Alive
- **Relatives**: [Cateracta](../../world/beasts/cateracta.md) (mother)

### Characteristics
Despite being a sea dragon, Acta meows and purrs like a cat. 

## History
[Ochette](ochette.md) discovers Acta's egg within the [Cavern of the Sea God](../../locations/harborlands/dungeons/cavern_of_the_sea_god.md), hatches the infant serpent, and brings it along on her journey. 

Acta is eventually brought back to [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md) in preparation to ward off the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../../world/lore/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md). When facing the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../../world/beasts/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md), Acta manifests the majestic form and power of a full sea serpent to protect and aid Ochette and her companions.

During the Epilogue, Acta is present at the celebration in the [New Delsta Theater](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md), being the only non-human and non-[beastling](../../world/species/beastling.md) creature to attend.
