# Dark Entity

The **Dark Entity** is a demonic shadow beast that attacks [Beasting Village](../locations/beasting_village.md) on [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md) prior to the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../world/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md). Encountered as the climactic threat in [Ochette](ochette.md)'s first chapter, the creature is strongly implied to be the tragic, corrupted form of [Roi](roi.md).

## Profile

### Nature and Appearance
The Dark Entity is a grotesque, monstrous demon wreathed in dark miasma that can produce shrill-screaming fledgling spawn. Pieces of what appear to be a tattered scholar's or traveler's robe cling to its tail, hinting at its human origins. It bears striking physiological similarities to the [Grotesque Monster](../world/grotesque_monster.md) encountered elsewhere on Toto'haha, another creature born of unnatural shadow corruption.

### Identity
Evidence within the narrative strongly indicates that the Dark Entity is the mutated form of Roi, an associate of [Temenos Mistral](temenos.md) who went missing. Following its defeat, the beast utters broken words—*"no longer... trust... cannot... be allowed... to... exist..."*—which closely mirror the final words spoken by Roi before his disappearance.

## Story

One night prior to the Night of the Scarlet Moon, the Dark Entity descends upon [Beasting Village](../locations/beasting_village.md). Master [Juvah](juvah.md) steps forward to battle the beast alone, holding it at bay so the village inhabitants can escape. Juvah sustains grave injuries during the struggle and is on the verge of being struck down when his apprentice Ochette arrives. 

Ochette confronts the monster and defeats it. In the aftermath, the creature dissolves into a cloud of dark smoke, though village records note that the beast fled into the darkness.
