# Grieving Golem

The **Grieving Golem** is a monstrous chimera encountered as the climactic boss of [Osvald V. Vanstein](../travelers/osvald.md)'s Chapter 4. Created through the forbidden experiments of [Harvey](harvey.md), it is the horrific transformation of Osvald's murdered wife, [Rita Lumina](rita_lumina.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Form**: Chimera / [Remnants](../../world/species/remnants.md) golem
- **Origin**: [Underground Laboratory](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/underground_laboratory.md), [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md)
- **Creator**: [Harvey](harvey.md)
- **Source**: [Rita Lumina](rita_lumina.md)
- **Status**: Destroyed

### Appearance
The Grieving Golem is a towering, shadowy remnant of arcane manufacture. It bears a notable resemblance to ancient Revenant entities and the Guardian of the First Flame, exuding a dark flame and an aura of profound sorrow.

## Story

Upon infiltrating the secret underground laboratory beneath [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md), Osvald confronts Harvey to demand answers for the murder of his family and the theft of his life's research. Rather than fighting directly, Harvey summons the Grieving Golem to destroy his former colleague.

During the battle, Osvald is horrified to discover that Rita did not die in the arson that consumed their home in [Conning Creek](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md). Instead, Harvey kept her alive in captivity, using her body and blood in horrific chimera experiments that culminated in her transformation into the golem. Backed into a corner, Osvald is forced to destroy the construct, pleading for Rita's forgiveness as the creature collapses, weakly crying out its final words of affection to him.

From the shattered remains of the golem, Osvald detects an alien arcane essence. Harvey reveals that this energy is the [One True Magic](../../world/lore/one_true_magic.md)—the power of the [Shadow](../../world/lore/shadow.md). Harvey confesses that he originally befriended Osvald solely to gain access to Rita and their daughter [Elena Vanstein](elena_vanstein.md), as the blood of the [Lumina](../../world/factions/lumina_clan.md) bloodline was indispensable for unlocking the Shadow's ultimate power.
