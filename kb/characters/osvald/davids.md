# Warden Davids

> "*Ohoho! You're not the first, you know. I've seen countless like you. So many have tried, and every last one died a tragic death. And so it would be my honor to put you back in your place!*"
> — Warden Davids, before battle

**Warden Davids** is the sadistic prison warden of [Frigit Isle](../../locations/winterlands/towns/frigit_isle.md) and the Chapter 1 antagonist in [Osvald V. Vanstein](../travelers/osvald.md)'s story in *Octopath Traveler II*.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Occupation**: Prison Warden ([Frigit Isle](../../locations/winterlands/towns/frigit_isle.md))

### Appearance
Warden Davids wears a thick, fur-lined cape and hat to stave off the extreme cold. He wields an enormous club, which he uses liberally to punish troublesome prisoners.

### Personality
Davids is a cold, uncaring, and sadistic warden who takes intense pleasure in tormenting and punishing the inmates under his charge, once beating a collapsed prisoner who had already frozen to death. Despite his brutish nature, he possesses a vicious intelligence, successfully anticipating Osvald's escape route and sabotaging the subterranean ladder before the escapees could reach it.

## Biography

### Prior to Story
During his tenure as warden of the frozen penal colony on [Frigit Isle](../../locations/winterlands/towns/frigit_isle.md), Davids received [Osvald](../travelers/osvald.md) into custody following his life sentence for the murder of his wife and child. To prevent Osvald from casting magic, Davids had him fitted with an iron muzzle of his own design. For five years, Davids abused and tormented Osvald and the other inmates, forcing them to labor in the prison mines extracting materials for extra profit.

### Osvald's Escape
One day, Davids and his guards overheard another inmate crying for help after being mugged by Osvald, who was retrieving a stolen notebook. Entering the cell, Davids administered a beating to Osvald before leaving. The next day, when that same inmate collapsed outside the mines from the extreme cold, Davids beat the fallen man repeatedly and demanded he resume working, stopping only when a guard informed him the prisoner had already died.

A few days later, Davids observed Osvald speaking with [Emerald](emerald.md) during work duty and beat them both, relenting only when notified that prison inspectors had arrived on the island.

Davids was later alerted by subordinates that Osvald and Emerald had been sighted in an underground passage beneath the prison. Warning the visiting inspectors of the escape attempt, Davids moved to intercept the convicts, sabotaging the escape ladder to trap them. Accompanied by his guards, he confronted the two escapees in battle, but was defeated and mortally wounded. In his dying breath, Davids taunted them, declaring that there was no escape from Frigit Isle.
