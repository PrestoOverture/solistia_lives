# Bale (Octopath Traveler II)

> *"I'm not here for you to talk to whenever you feel like it. Unless you *want* to get hurt."*
> — Bale

## Overview

- **Age**: 35
- **Gender**: Male
- **Origin**: [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md)
- **Location**: [Crackridge Harbor: Anchorage](../../locations/wildlands/wilds/crackridge_harbor_anchorage.md); formerly [Frigit Isle](../../locations/winterlands/towns/frigit_isle.md)
- **Occupation**: Thief, prison contraband supplier

**Bale** is a petty criminal originally from [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md) who was imprisoned on the frozen penitentiary of [Frigit Isle](../../locations/winterlands/towns/frigit_isle.md). Boasting a record of 126 separate offenses—including pickpocketing, swindling, petty theft, and identity fraud—he leveraged the sheer volume of his misdemeanor record to elevate his status and establish himself as a black-market supplier among the inmates.

## Appearance

Bale has messy brown hair and wears a plain white shirt with brown trousers.

## Personality

Wary, guarded, and prickly, Bale prefers to keep to himself and maintain an intimidating front. Despite posturing as a hardened convict, his record consists entirely of minor infractions.

## Story

During [Osvald](osvald.md)'s imprisonment on Frigit Isle, Bale runs an internal trade in smuggled contraband. Osvald blackmails him using knowledge of these illicit transactions, forcing Bale to provide cold-weather clothing capable of withstanding the island's lethal blizzards, a vital component of Osvald's escape plan.

Bale later makes his own escape from Frigit Isle during a massive prison riot, ultimately finding refuge at [Crackridge Harbor: Anchorage](../../locations/wildlands/wilds/crackridge_harbor_anchorage.md).
