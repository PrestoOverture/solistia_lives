# Lady Clarissa

> "*Revenge is nothing, [Osvald](osvald.md). It is empty and hollow.*"
> — Lady Clarissa

**Lady Clarissa** is a scholar residing in [Conning Creek](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md) and the former research assistant of [Osvald V. Vanstein](osvald.md). Devoted, keenly observant, and intellectually formidable, she maintained unwavering faith in Osvald's innocence throughout his five-year wrongful imprisonment on Frigit Isle and guarded his ransacked study against looters.

## Profile

### Appearance

Lady Clarissa is a forty-two-year-old woman with short blonde hair. She wears an elegant teal dress and is capable of wielding a long staff in combat when defending herself or her home.

### Personality

Beneath her refined and ladylike demeanor, Clarissa possesses a sharp intellect and an uncompromising tongue. In academic debates she does not hesitate to speak hard truths, having reportedly brought numerous scholars to tears during discussions. The only person ever known to best her in an argument was her late husband, Ethan.

Clarissa is also deeply empathetic and morally grounded. Guided by the memory and gentle philosophy of her late husband, she believes in forgiveness and moving forward rather than succumbing to resentment. Consequently, she attempts to steer Osvald away from his burning desire for vengeance against [Harvey](harvey.md), warning him that revenge is hollow and cannot heal the void left by loss. As a scholar of magical theory, she harbors an enduring curiosity toward the nature of magic, particularly the concept of the seventh source.

Clarissa's perceptiveness is extraordinary; according to [Ori's Journal Fragments](../../stories/documents/oris_journal_fragments.md), she was the only person ever to spot the disguised scrivener [Ori](../partitio/ori.md) when Ori wished to remain undetected, and she regarded the writer with immediate suspicion.

## Biography

### Early Life and Research

In her youth, Clarissa studied at an academy alongside [Cohazeh](../ochette/cohazeh.md). The two became close friends until their paths diverged, last seeing each other at the Pit of [D'arqest](../others/darqest.md). Their single recorded quarrel occurred when Cohazeh accidentally spilled tea across Clarissa's research papers.

Clarissa eventually settled in the coastal town of [Conning Creek](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md), where she married Ethan, an honorable member of the local guard. Ethan introduced her to Osvald, and Clarissa became Osvald's assistant as he researched the [One True Magic](../../world/lore/one_true_magic.md).

Tragedy struck when Harvey murdered Osvald's wife, [Rita](rita_lumina.md), framed Osvald for the crime, and plundered his research. When Ethan grew suspicious of illicit dealings between Harvey and the local guard captain, [Stenvar](stenvar.md), Stenvar arranged Ethan's murder. Over the ensuing five years while Osvald was imprisoned in the penal colony of Frigit Isle, Clarissa kept watch over Osvald's ransacked study to safeguard whatever remnants of his work remained, living in Conning Creek with an elderly grey-haired attendant.

### Osvald's Return

When an escaped Osvald finally returned to Conning Creek five years later, Clarissa challenged him to a duel to verify his identity. Upon confirming he was indeed her former mentor, she welcomed him warmly into his study. However, she was saddened to find Osvald entirely consumed by vengeance against Harvey and Stenvar. Though she counseled him that revenge would yield only emptiness, she could not dissuade him, and she watched him depart for [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md).

Following Harvey's downfall, Clarissa took Osvald's surviving young daughter, [Elena Vanstein](elena_vanstein.md), into her home in Conning Creek. Clarissa dedicated herself to nursing Elena back to health and helping the child recover from the trauma and memory manipulation inflicted by Harvey. She later saw Osvald off on his continued travels to investigate the wider conspiracy linked to Harvey's experiments.

### Journey to Toto'haha

Encouraged by Elena after paying respects at her husband's grave, Clarissa embarked on a scholarly expedition to [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md) under armed escort to study ancient ruins. In the human settlement, she happily reunited with her former classmate Cohazeh. Together, the two scholars explored the ruins near the [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md), comparing notes on the history of the [beastlings](../../world/species/beastling.md) and reminiscing over their shared youth, agreeing to continue their scholarly collaboration in the future.
