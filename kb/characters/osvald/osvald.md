# Osvald V. Vanstein

**Osvald V. Vanstein** is a renowned Scholar who was falsely convicted of the murder of his wife and daughter. After enduring 1,879 days—over five years—of harsh imprisonment on [Frigit Isle](../../locations/winterlands/towns/frigit_isle.md) in the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md), he orchestrated a prison escape to hunt down his former colleague [Harvey](harvey.md), avenge his fallen kin, and uncover the truth behind their stolen research on the "[One True Magic](../../world/lore/one_true_magic.md)".

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 38
- **Occupation**: Scholar
- **Status**: Alive
- **Origin**: [Conning Creek](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md)
- **Relatives**:
  - [Rita Lumina](rita_lumina.md) (wife, deceased)
  - [Elena Vanstein](elena_vanstein.md) (daughter)

### Appearance
Osvald is a hulking, heavily muscled man with long, dull-muddy blond hair, a beard, and spectacles. His physique is the deliberate result of consistent exercise, born of his conviction that a scholar requires three fundamental attributes: discipline, stamina, and willpower.

Around his neck he wears a heavy iron shackle from his time as an inmate, a stark symbol of his captivity. His clothing consists of black pants and boots, worn beneath his tattered prison rags and covered by a voluminous brown coat given to him by an elderly ex-scholar who rescued him in [Cape Cold](../../locations/winterlands/towns/cape_cold.md).

### Personality
Osvald is aloof, quiet, and blunt, frequently communicating in low grunts, terse mumbles, or unbroken silence. He disdains idle chatter that he considers an unproductive waste of time, though he relishes intellectual debates and deep discussions of differing perspectives. Beneath his gruff and intimidating exterior, however, lies a kind and sympathetic heart. Among the traveling party, he often acts as a stern yet deeply caring father figure to younger members such as [Agnea Bristarni](../agnea/agnea.md) and [Ochette](../ochette/ochette.md).

He possesses an extraordinary intellect, particularly in mathematics and analytical calculation. Osvald is prone to memorizing intricate formulas, calculating prison guard patrols down to the second, and recording meticulous mental tallies of his environment. In internal contemplation, he frequently holds debates between multiple mental versions of himself, each considering problems from distinct angles. He has no concern for material wealth, valuing only knowledge. As a poor student in his youth, he habitually chose books over food, once collapsing from malnutrition. In adulthood, he drinks black coffee for the mental stimulation of its bitterness and shuns alcohol because it impairs the intellect. He also dislikes crowds and harbors a fond weakness for goulash, a dish his wife frequently prepared.

The psychological toll of his family's loss and five years of inhumane brutality on Frigit Isle left Osvald hardened and morally pragmatic. He shows no hesitation in physically mugging individuals when necessity dictates, such as taking back his stolen notebook from a cellmate or reclaiming a friend's pawned telescope from a predatory moneylender.

Despite being initially consumed by a burning desire to kill Harvey, Osvald avoids reckless actions or taking innocent lives. His logical mind once struggled to conceptualize intangibles like love—he admitted to [Throné Anguis](../throne/throne.md) that he never spoke a traditional vow of love at his wedding because he could not define it mathematically. Nevertheless, his affection for his family defines him: upon learning that his daughter Elena is still alive, his thirst for vengeance immediately gives way to an overriding determination to protect and rescue her.

## History

### Prior to Story
Before his incarceration, Osvald was a respected scholar living peacefully in [Conning Creek](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md) with his wife [Rita](rita_lumina.md) and daughter [Elena](elena_vanstein.md). His scholarly life was dedicated to solving the riddle of the "[One True Magic](../../world/lore/one_true_magic.md)", a theorized seventh element existing beyond the recognized six arcane elements. Osvald formed an academic partnership with [Harvey](harvey.md) to advance this research.

Tragedy struck when Osvald returned to his home to find it engulfed in flames. Harvey was present at the scene, taunting Osvald and declaring that he would claim the One True Magic for himself. Framed for the arson and the murder of his own wife and child, Osvald was arrested by [Captain Stenvar](stenvar.md), convicted on fabricated evidence, and sentenced to life imprisonment at the remote penitentiary of [Frigit Isle](../../locations/winterlands/towns/frigit_isle.md).

### Escape from Frigit Isle

#### Chapter 1
Locked away for 1,879 days, Osvald endured freezing conditions and cruel labor. To suppress his spellcasting, the sadistic warden [Davids](davids.md) forced an iron muzzle onto his face. Throughout his captivity, Osvald meticulously scouted the prison layout, memorized the rosters and response times of the guards, and formulated an escape plan. He recruited [Emerald](emerald.md), a skilled thief and prison informant, who managed to steal the key to Osvald's muzzle.

When the two made their move, they found their intended escape route compromised: the ladder to the exterior had been sabotaged. Warden Davids ambushed them, having monitored Emerald's movements. Osvald overpowered Davids in close combat and cast fire magic to blast an opening through a stone wall, allowing the pair to evade arriving reinforcements.

#### Chapter 2
With the prison placed on high alert, Osvald and Emerald hid in a coastal shed. Inspired by a casual remark from Emerald, Osvald constructed a makeshift vessel using buoyant straw bound together with ice magic. Before they could launch, Emerald chose to depart on a separate gambit to seize and torch the prison inspectors' ship, sacrificing his own route to create a diversion.

As the inspectors' ship went up in flames, Osvald launched into the icy waters on his ice boat. Drifting across the frozen sea, he experienced a vision of his late wife Rita, who told him that his task was unfinished and that they could not be reunited yet. Osvald eventually washed ashore near [Cape Cold](../../locations/winterlands/towns/cape_cold.md), where an elderly ex-scholar pulled him from the surf and nursed him to health. His savior gave him a warm coat and pleaded with him to lead an honest life. While thanking the man, Osvald silently resolved that he could never live peacefully until Harvey was dead.

*(If Osvald is not chosen as the primary protagonist, the traveler discovers him collapsed in the snow outside Cape Cold, reviving him so he can join the journey.)*

### Pursuit of Harvey

#### Chapter 3
Osvald returned to Conning Creek to search for traces of Harvey. He found his home reduced to charred ruins, but his personal study remained intact—and emptied of all his notes regarding the One True Magic. There he was confronted by his former assistant, [Clarissa](clarissa.md), who challenged him to a duel to verify his identity. Upon recognizing him, Clarissa embraced his return, having always believed in his innocence.

Investigating the circumstances of his framing, Osvald deduced that [Captain Stenvar](stenvar.md) of the town guard had been bribed by Harvey. Clarissa revealed that her own husband, Ethan, had died under mysterious circumstances that the guards refused to investigate; she pleaded with Osvald to renounce vengeance, warning that revenge is hollow. Unwavering, Osvald confronted Stenvar at the [guard's outpost](../../locations/harborlands/dungeons/guard_outpost.md). Stenvar confessed to his corruption and attacked, but Osvald subdued him. Under interrogation, Stenvar disclosed that Osvald's stolen research had been dispatched to [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md). Recognizing that Montwise was the city where he and Harvey had first met, Osvald knew he was being baited into a trap, yet set out nonetheless.

#### Chapter 4
Arriving in Montwise, Osvald investigated the [library](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise_library.md) and discovered a concealed passageway leading into an underground laboratory. Within, he discovered horrific chimeras—forbidden magical experiments created using his stolen formulae. Osvald fought through the creatures and confronted Harvey directly.

Harvey gloated over the academic acclaim and prestige he had garnered from Osvald's work. When Osvald demanded why Harvey had destroyed his family, Harvey summoned a monstrous creation: the [Grieving Golem](grieving_golem.md). Osvald was horrified to discover that Harvey had faked Rita's death, keeping her alive to harvest her blood. As a scion of the Lumina bloodline—the "Lineage of Light"—her blood held the power to channel the magic of the [shadow](../../world/lore/shadow.md). Osvald was forced to destroy the golem.

Before Osvald could apprehend Harvey, his daughter [Elena](elena_vanstein.md) appeared. She was alive, but completely brainwashed into believing Harvey was her true father. Harvey announced that Elena's Lumina blood was even more potent than Rita's and declared his intent to use her in his ultimate experiment in [Gravell](../../locations/wildlands/towns/gravell.md), where the remains of the ancient sorcerer [D'arqest](../others/darqest.md) lay buried. Harvey fled with Elena. Heartbroken yet resolute, Osvald abandoned his singular obsession with revenge and committed himself fully to saving his daughter.

#### Chapter 5
Osvald arrived in Gravell to find the townspeople driven mad by black crystals. After confiscating the crystals to restore the residents' sanity, he learned that dark fog was issuing from the [Duskruin Shrine](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/duskruin_shrine.md). Entering the ancient ruins, Osvald reached the resting place of D'arqest, where Harvey was conducting the ritual to awaken the One True Magic using the Book of Demons and Elena's blood.

Harvey cut Elena to manifest two dark golems to stall Osvald. Osvald struck down the golems and reached his unconscious daughter. Harvey then completed his calculations, unleashing his shadow-infused version of the One True Magic. Overmatched and backed into a corner, Osvald recalled a cherished memory of Elena asking him to continue teaching her magic. Driven by his love for his daughter and an unyielding resolve to protect her, Osvald achieved the final variable in his calculations, manifesting his own radiant form of the One True Magic to counter Harvey. Unable to accept defeat, Harvey attempted to draw upon even more dark power and was entirely consumed by it.

In the aftermath, Osvald returned to Conning Creek and placed Elena in Clarissa's care, allowing her time to heal from Harvey's manipulation without overwhelming her with the truth of his identity. When discussing with Clarissa the force that allowed him to complete the One True Magic, Clarissa suggested that the missing variable had been love—an assertion Osvald playfully deflected through mathematical rationale. Examining Harvey's notes, Osvald deduced that Harvey had not worked alone, and resolved to continue investigating the wider conspiracy.

### Crossed Paths: Osvald and Partitio

#### Part 1: New Delsta
While visiting [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md) alongside [Partitio Yellowil](../partitio/partitio.md), Osvald came across his old academic acquaintance [Regulus](../others/regulus.md), who had collapsed on the street from starvation. After feeding him at the local tavern, Regulus explained that he had designed an astronomical telescope capable of bringing the stars closer to mankind, but lacked the money to finish it. Over Osvald's skeptical objections, Partitio funded the remaining parts, and Regulus departed for Montwise to complete his work.

#### Part 2: Montwise
In Montwise, Osvald and Partitio visited Regulus's workshop, only to find the astronomer destitute once again. Regulus admitted he had turned over his finished telescope as collateral to a moneylender. Osvald and Partitio tracked down the moneylender's manse, overhearing hirelings admit that Regulus had already paid off his debt. Osvald promptly mugged the moneylender, reclaimed the telescope, and warned the lender never to approach Regulus again.

That night, Osvald, Partitio, and Regulus gazed into the heavens through the telescope. Osvald spotted the cross of Cetus—a constellation that should only have been visible during the daytime at that time of year. Regulus revealed that daylight hours across the realm had been steadily diminishing, pointing toward an unnatural lengthening of the night.

### The Journey for the Dawn
During the gathering of the eight travelers to stop the eternal night, Osvald confronted the conspirator [Oboro](../hikari/oboro.md). When asked whether the dawn was truly worth the agonizing journey, Osvald countered that those who march toward the dawn will be welcomed by it, while those who do not will be left behind.

In the final confrontation against the dark god [Vide](../../world/gods/vide.md), Osvald stood at the vanguard, declaring that love would be the source of Vide's destruction and invoking the One True Magic to banish the shadow from Solistia.

## Relationships

- **[Rita Lumina](rita_lumina.md)**: Osvald's deceased wife, whom he loved deeply despite his difficulty expressing emotion. Her Lumina heritage made her a target for Harvey, who used her blood in dark chimera experiments. Osvald cherishes her memory, dreaming of her during his escape from Frigit Isle.
- **[Elena Vanstein](elena_vanstein.md)**: Osvald's beloved daughter. Osvald homeschooled her in mathematics and magic, though he quietly doubted his adequacy as a parent. After rescuing her from Harvey's brainwashing, he chose to watch over her from a distance in Clarissa's care until she could fully recover.
- **[Harvey](harvey.md)**: Osvald's former colleague and bitter nemesis. Driven by envy of Osvald's intellect, Harvey murdered Osvald's wife, framed him for the crime, stole his research, and brainwashed his daughter.
- **[Clarissa](clarissa.md)**: Osvald's loyal research assistant in Conning Creek. She never doubted Osvald's innocence and willingly took charge of Elena's care.
- **[Emerald](emerald.md)**: A clever thief and informant imprisoned on Frigit Isle who aided Osvald's escape and sacrificed his own route by burning the inspectors' ship to buy Osvald time.
- **[Captain Stenvar](stenvar.md)**: The corrupt guard captain in Conning Creek who accepted Harvey's bribes to falsify the investigation against Osvald.
- **[Partitio Yellowil](../partitio/partitio.md)**: Fellow traveler and partner during their shared endeavors. Osvald's austere, analytical temperament acts as a grounding foil to Partitio's sunny optimism.
- **[Regulus](../others/regulus.md)**: An eccentric astronomer and old colleague of Osvald's whose obsessive devotion to studying the stars frequently leaves him broke.
- **[Agnea Bristarni](../agnea/agnea.md)** & **[Ochette](../ochette/ochette.md)**: Younger traveling companions whom Osvald treats with paternal warmth and gentle guidance. He recommended *The Western Dance Compendium* to Agnea, and Ochette playfully referred to his magical craft as "Projectiles by Pops."
- **[Hikari Ku](../hikari/hikari.md)**: Fellow traveler to whom Osvald recommended the historical military chronicle *The War of the Great Wall*.
