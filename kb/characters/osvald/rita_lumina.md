# Rita Lumina

> "*Oh, [Osvald](osvald.md)... How I long to be with you. To ease your pain. To get you looking presentable again. To make you some goulash, with [Elena](elena_vanstein.md). Alas, my dear, I cannot. You have unfinished business. So you cannot stay here. For it is not yet time. I love you, my dear.*"
> — Rita, to Osvald after her death

## Overview

**Rita Lumina** was the wife of [Osvald V. Vanstein](osvald.md), mother of [Elena Vanstein](elena_vanstein.md), and a descendant of the [Lumina Clan](../../world/factions/lumina_clan.md).

## Profile

### Appearance
Rita has long brown hair and wears a violet dress with white sleeves and an apron.

### Personality
Rita was a kind and loving wife, caring for her husband's needs and often offering him tea or goulash. As revealed in travel banter, she also loved dancing.

## History

Not much is known about Rita's early life, though at some point she met Osvald before settling down with him and together having a daughter, [Elena](elena_vanstein.md).

One day, Osvald's colleague, [Harvey](harvey.md), discovered her connection to the Lumina bloodline and had her and Elena kidnapped whilst staging it as a death by immolation by setting the Vanstein home on fire and placing two bodies similar to them to make it look convincing.

While Harvey's prisoner, she was subjected to his experiments before he killed her in an attempt to complete the *Book of Demons*. Although it failed, he used her blood and essence to create the [Grieving Golem](grieving_golem.md), which was eventually destroyed by Osvald, allowing her to finally move on in peace.
