# Harvey

**Harvey**, also known as **Professor Harvey**, is a scholar of magic and the primary antagonist of [Osvald V. Vanstein](osvald.md)'s story. Operating as a professor in [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md), Harvey pursued research into the [One True Magic](../../world/lore/one_true_magic.md), seeking a "seventh source" distinct from the six known elements of fire, ice, lightning, wind, light, and darkness. Driven by a deep inferiority complex toward Osvald, Harvey betrayed him, stole his research, and murdered his family to advance his own arcane ambitions.

## Profile

### Appearance
Harvey wears a large black cloak with a long cape. He has a goatee and a red combover drooping down one side of his face.

### Personality
Harvey initially presented himself as an intelligent, cultured scholar eager to debate theories of magic. Beneath this facade, he is brutal, narcissistic, and sadistic, feeling no remorse for manipulating, experimenting on, or murdering innocent people. [Ori](../partitio/ori.md) noted in her journal that he gleefully tortured the animal that was transformed into the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../../world/beasts/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md).

Harvey's defining motivation is a bitter, obsessive inferiority complex toward Osvald. Recognizing Osvald as the only scholar capable of standing as his equal in uncovering the One True Magic, Harvey became obsessed with proving his superiority. This fixation drove him to extreme lengths: framing Osvald for the murder of his family, and bribing both [Captain Stenvar](stenvar.md) and the trial judge to sentence Osvald to life imprisonment on [Frigit Isle](../../locations/winterlands/towns/frigit_isle.md) rather than death, so that Osvald would live long enough to watch Harvey master the One True Magic first.

## History

### Background
In [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md), Harvey studied Gratton's Hypothesis to discover the One True Magic, experimenting on monsters to understand their innate magical capabilities. He deduced that monsters' magic is derived from their blood and began breeding chimeras in secret—crossbreeding beasts such as Frost Bears, Salamanders, Manticores, and Kingfishers. He kept a dark crystal in his laboratory, which he utilized to control his creations.

At some point, Harvey was commissioned by [Oboro](../hikari/oboro.md) of the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md) to recreate the *Book of Demons*. On their instructions, he experimented on a captured beast from [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md) (the companion animal that [Ochette](../ochette/ochette.md) did not choose), mutating it into the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../../world/beasts/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md).

Five years prior to the main events, Harvey attended a lecture in Montwise where he met Osvald. Recognizing Osvald's brilliant research into the One True Magic, Harvey proposed a partnership. However, this alliance was a pretext: Harvey had discovered that Osvald's wife, [Rita Lumina](rita_lumina.md), belonged to the ancient [Lumina](../../world/factions/lumina_clan.md) bloodline, whose blood held the key to unlocking the seventh source—[Shadow](../../world/lore/shadow.md) magic. Harvey murdered two strangers resembling Rita and her young daughter [Elena](elena_vanstein.md), set Osvald's home ablaze, and abducted Rita and Elena. He framed Osvald for the crime, bribed the guards and the judge to send Osvald to Frigit Isle, and claimed Osvald's research notes for himself.

In the ensuing years, Harvey used Rita's blood to create the [Grieving Golem](grieving_golem.md), though he considered the creature a failure despite its ability to manifest trace Shadow magic. Rita died as a result of these experiments. Harvey then placed Elena under a memory-altering spell, raising her to believe he was her father so she would remain obedient until her blood was needed.

### Confrontation in Montwise
Five years later, Osvald escaped Frigit Isle and tracked Harvey to his secret underground laboratory beneath the library in Montwise. Harvey taunted Osvald with the stolen research and unleashed the Grieving Golem, revealing that Rita had been sacrificed to create it. After Osvald was forced to destroy the creature, Harvey admitted the truth of Rita's death and explained that Lumina blood was necessary to unlock Shadow magic. He revealed that Elena was alive under his spell and declared his intention to sacrifice her to complete his research, before fleeing with her to [Gravell](../../locations/wildlands/towns/gravell.md).

### Duskruin Shrine and Demise
In Gravell, Harvey entered the [Duskruin Shrine](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/duskruin_shrine.md), releasing a black fog that created dark crystals possessing nearby worshipers. Deep inside, he desecrated the resting place of [D'arqest](../others/darqest.md) to complete the *Book of Demons*, addressing D'arqest's remains as "O great ancestor" and proclaiming that his covenant would be fulfilled.

When Osvald arrived, Harvey stabbed Elena to bathe the tome in her blood, gaining command over Shadow magic. Harvey confessed his long-standing envy and attacked Osvald with devastating dark power. However, Osvald, driven by love and a desire to protect Elena, unlocked the true One True Magic and defeated him. Refusing defeat, Harvey attempted to draw even more dark power from the book, losing control and consuming himself in a catastrophic explosion of Shadow magic. 

While Osvald's quest records note that Harvey was consumed by his own out-of-control magic, a conflicting note among [Ori's Journal Fragments](../../stories/documents/oris_journal_fragments.md) in the [Fellsun Ruins](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/fellsun_ruins.md) suggested Harvey may have survived and escaped after the battle.
