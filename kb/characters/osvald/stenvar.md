# Captain Stenvar

> *"Facts are malleable things, you see. The powerful can bend 'em to their will. Provided they have enough coin, that is."*  
> — Captain Stenvar

**Captain Stenvar** is a character in *Octopath Traveler II*. A corrupt officer stationed at [Conning Creek](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md), he conspired with [Harvey](harvey.md) to frame [Osvald](osvald.md) for murder in exchange for substantial bribes.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 45
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Unknown
- **Location**: [Conning Creek](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md)
- **Occupation**: Captain of the guard

### Appearance
Stenvar has blond hair styled into a ponytail and wears a distinctive red cape over his uniform. In combat, he wields a large polearm.

### Personality
Stenvar is a completely venal and unscrupulous man driven purely by monetary greed. He views justice and truth as commodities to be bought and sold, brazenly asserting that the wealthy and powerful can bend facts to their will so long as they offer sufficient coin. For Stenvar, money is the only reliable and unchanging store of value. His obsession with his hoard is so intense that witnessing its destruction drives him into hysterical despair.

## History

### Background
Stenvar served as a member of the local security forces in the Harborlands port town of [Conning Creek](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md). Five years prior to the main narrative, he met with [Harvey](harvey.md), a scholar from [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md) and member of the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md). Bribed handsomely, Stenvar orchestrated the arrest and wrongful conviction of [Osvald](osvald.md) for the murder of Osvald's wife [Rita](rita_lumina.md) and daughter [Elena](elena_vanstein.md).

Following Osvald's sentencing and imprisonment on [Frigit Isle](../../locations/winterlands/towns/frigit_isle.md), Stenvar rose through the ranks to become captain of the town guard. To secure his grip on the garrison, he replaced most of the honorable municipal guards with mercenary ruffians brought over from the eastern continent. When Ethan, one of his subordinates and the husband of [Clarissa](clarissa.md), confronted Stenvar after discovering his clandestine meetings with Harvey, Stenvar murdered him and staged the scene to appear as a suicide.

### Osvald's Tale
During Osvald's return to Conning Creek, Osvald infiltrated the guard headquarters and confronted Stenvar in his private office. Unrepentant, Stenvar openly admitted to conspiring with Harvey and summoned his guards to eliminate the fugitive scholar. Osvald overpowered Stenvar and his men in combat.

Fearing for his life, Stenvar confessed that while he did not know Harvey's current whereabouts, Osvald's stolen research materials had been forwarded to Montwise on Harvey's instructions. As retribution, Osvald set Stenvar's hoard of bribe money ablaze, burning it to ashes before the horrified captain's eyes.

Stenvar disappears from Conning Creek following these events, and his subsequent fate is unknown.
