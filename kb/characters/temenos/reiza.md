# Reiza

> "*I have betrayed my brethren, but I do not regret it. Meeting you was our lord's will, cleric. And now I have been given a chance to atone...*"
> — Reiza, to Temenos

## Overview

**Reiza** is a thirty-seven-year-old woman residing in [Crackridge](../../locations/wildlands/towns/crackridge.md) and a operative of the enigmatic [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md). She encounters [Temenos Mistral](temenos.md) during his investigation in the [Wildlands](../../locations/wildlands/wildlands.md).

## Profile

### Appearance
Reiza wears a black dress accompanied by a necklace bearing the crescent moon symbol of the Moonshade Order.

### Personality
Reiza is an enigmatic, deceptive woman accustomed to adopting different personas. Tasked with stopping Temenos, she initially plays the part of a helpless traveler seeking escort, attempting to manipulate him through charm and flirtatious innuendo before launching a sudden ambush. However, when Temenos deflects her blow and points out her inner hesitation, she relents and undergoes what appears to be a genuine change of heart. Later, overwhelmed with grief over her complicity in the massacre of the [Kal](../../world/lore/kal.md) tribe, she begs for judgment and atonement. Nonetheless, her swift transitions between seduction, violence, and remorse leave some question as to how deeply her penitence runs.

## History

Prior to settling in Crackridge, Reiza lived in a village that was completely razed by fire. In the aftermath of the catastrophe, a mysterious figure referred to as "lord" spoke to the surviving villagers, deceiving them into believing that the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../../world/factions/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) was responsible for the devastation. Broken by grief and manipulated by lies, Reiza and her fellow survivors converted to the Moonshade Order, ultimately aiding in the cult's horrific slaughter of the Kal people.

Years later, the Moonshade Order deployed Reiza to intercept Temenos Mistral and prevent him from investigating the [Fellsun Ruins](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/fellsun_ruins.md) near Crackridge. Posing as an ordinary townsfolk who needed a guide outside the village, she accompanied him along the path, attempting to distract him before attacking when he saw through her pretense. Temenos parried her strike and noted her lack of resolve. Yielding to his insight, Reiza abandoned her orders and guided the cleric directly into the ruins.

After Temenos completed his investigation of the horrific truth behind the Kal massacre, Reiza confessed her past and her role in the atrocity. Stricken with guilt, she begged Temenos to pronounce judgment upon her. The inquisitor refused to pass sentence, instructing her instead that she must bear the weight of her deeds and find her own path to atonement. Left alone at the entrance of the ruins, Reiza subsequently disappeared from the area, leaving her ultimate fate unknown.
