# Ort

"*The only reason I'm here is because Captain [Kaldena](kaldena.md) saw fit to raise me up. Just as the [inquisitor](roi.md) brought you into the church. 'The flame lights the way.' We just happen to be following our own flames. May the [Sacred Flame](../../world/lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) guide you, [Crick](crick_wellsley.md).*" —Ort, to Crick

**Ort Edgeworth** is a Sanctum Knight of the [Sacred Guard](../../world/factions/sacred_guard.md) in *Octopath Traveler II*. He entered the order alongside his close friend [Crick Wellsley](crick_wellsley.md) and was appointed as the personal bodyguard to [Captain Kaldena](kaldena.md).

## Profile

### Appearance
Ort is a 22-year-old knight with long black hair. He wears standard-issue Sacred Guard plate armor customized with deep wine-red trim instead of the traditional blue accents, reflecting his status in Captain Kaldena's retinue alongside [Deputy Cubaryi](cubaryi.md).

### Personality
Ort is an honorable, steadfast knight who does not hesitate to throw himself into harm's way to safeguard others. Initially, he held unwavering loyalty toward Captain Kaldena for mentoring and elevating him within the ranks. However, upon discovering her involvement in sinister plots and the murder of his dear friend Crick, Ort turns decisively against her.

Following the downfall of the church's leadership and the widespread demoralization of the Sacred Guard, Ort perseveres through grief. Rather than abandoning his post, he rededicates his sword to honoring Crick's memory and restoring the public's lost faith in the Sacred Guard.

## History

### Canalbrine and Stormhail
Ort is first deployed to [Canalbrine](../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md) alongside Sacred Guard detachments investigating the serial murders of an apothecary and a scholar. Following the arrest of the architect [Vados](vados.md) by Inquisitor [Temenos Mistral](../travelers/temenos.md) and Crick, Ort reunites with Crick outside Kaldena's quarters, where the two friends exchange heartfelt encouragement.

Later, Ort attends Crick's funeral in [Stormhail](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail.md) after Crick's murdered body is discovered discarded in the snow outside the Sacred Guard headquarters.

### Confrontation at the Rifted Rock
While accompanying Kaldena on an expedition to the Rifted Rock outside the [Nameless Village](../../locations/totohaha/towns/nameless_village.md), Ort directly confronts her regarding Crick's murder, having recognized that the mortal wounds on Crick's corpse matched the distinctive blade wielded by Kaldena herself. Rather than answering, Kaldena attacks Ort and the accompanying knights, cutting them down with dark power. When Temenos arrives shortly afterward, a grievously wounded Ort urges the Inquisitor to pursue Kaldena and stop her scheme before it is too late.

### Ort's Next Chapter
Following Kaldena's demise and his own recovery, Ort learns of insurgent cultist activity near [Flamechurch](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch.md) through hidden records left by Vados. Traveling to the cathedral, Ort tracks two suspicious conspirators to an underground passage. Infiltrating the cellars, Ort subdues the intruders before an altar statue.

With the threat contained, Ort returns to Stormhail with renewed determination to root out corruption and walk a righteous path in tribute to Crick. In Stormhail, he attends to his duties at the [Sacred Guard Headquarters](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail_sacred_guard_headquarters.md) by day and maintains a quiet vigil at Crick's gravesite by night.
