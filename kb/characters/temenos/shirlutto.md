# Shirlutto

> *"Humans, complicated. Therefore deep?"*  
> — Shirlutto

**Shirlutto** is a character in *Octopath Traveler II*. A [Beastling](../../world/species/beastling.md) living in the [Nameless Village](../../locations/totohaha/towns/nameless_village.md) on the island of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md), he conducts the traditional trials that guide outsiders through the perilous [Wandering Wood](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/wandering_wood.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 42
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Nameless Village](../../locations/totohaha/towns/nameless_village.md)
- **Race**: [Beastling](../../world/species/beastling.md)
- **Occupation**: Guide / Trial-giver

### Appearance
Like most male Beastlings, Shirlutto goes bare-chested and wears dark leggings. He has long white hair bound into a ponytail.

### Personality
Like many of his kin, Shirlutto speaks in fragmented sentences and expresses his emotions straightforwardly. He is supportive and encouraging to those undertaking the forest trial, praising their endurance and navigation. Though possessed of a profound curiosity about human civilization and culture, he has never traveled beyond the forest entirely due to his own laziness. He cannot read, but displays great sensitivity to human art; upon first hearing a lute played, he compares its melodies to birdsong.

## History

### Temenos's Tale
When [Temenos](../travelers/temenos.md) ventures into the labyrinthine [Wandering Wood](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/wandering_wood.md) in pursuit of [Kaldena](kaldena.md), he finds himself looping back through the trees. Heeding Kaldena's clue to consult the wisdom of forebears, Temenos returns to the Nameless Village and recruits Shirlutto on the settlement's eastern edge. Welcoming Temenos to undertake the trial, Shirlutto leads him safely through the magical forest toward the [Rifted Rock](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/rifted_rock.md).

Before leaving Temenos at the ruins' entrance, Shirlutto shares the origin lore of the Beastling race, recounting how they were brought into existence through the sorcery of [D'arqest](../others/darqest.md). He also mentions that Kaldena and members of the [Sacred Guard](../../world/factions/sacred_guard.md) entered before them, though he remains neutral and indifferent to their presence.

### Post-Story
Later, Shirlutto can be found at the summit of the Nameless Village contemplating human nature ([Shirlutto's Next Chapter](../../stories/next_chapters/shirluttos_next_chapter.md)). Curious about human society, he asks travelers to bring him cultural artifacts: a lute, a paper theater play, and a chronicler's account of human history (recounting the ancient story of two lovers fleeing warring kingdoms). Absorbing these works deeply affects him.

Reflecting on human behavior, Shirlutto confesses that humans had once killed his close friend with an arrow in an act of senseless hostility. Confronting that memory through human art, Shirlutto experiences two sensations previously alien to him: a thirst for vengeance and deep sorrow. Disturbed yet enlightened, he muses that experiencing such conflicting pain means he is becoming more like a human, wondering aloud whether becoming human is truly a good thing.

During the epilogue, Shirlutto is seen peacefully watching [Pom](../ochette/pom.md) eat jerky in the village.
