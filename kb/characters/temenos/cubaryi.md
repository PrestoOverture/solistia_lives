# Deputy Cubaryi

> "*Ha ha ha! Face the [gods](../../world/gods/gods.md)' wrath!*"
> — Deputy Cubaryi, in battle

**Deputy Cubaryi** is a high-ranking officer in the [Sacred Guard](../../world/factions/sacred_guard.md) and the secondary antagonist of [Temenos](../travelers/temenos.md)'s path in *Octopath Traveler II*. While posing as a pious knight upholding church authority, she secretly conspired with [Captain Kaldena](kaldena.md) to infiltrate the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../../world/factions/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) and bring about the return of the [Shadow](../../world/lore/shadow.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 28
- **Gender**: Female
- **Occupation**: Deputy of the [Sacred Guard](../../world/factions/sacred_guard.md)
- **Affiliation**: Sacred Guard, Kaldena's cult / [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md)
- **Status**: Deceased

### Appearance
As a high-ranking member of the Sacred Guard, Cubaryi wears a pink Sacred Guard uniform layered with armor and a colored cloak. She wears a distinct full-face helmet that completely hides her facial features.

### Personality
At first glance, Cubaryi appears to be a pious yet abrasive knight enforcing the teachings of the [Sacred Flame](../../world/lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md). She and Temenos share strong mutual disdain, trading barbs: she calls him a "hound," while Temenos refers to her and her fellow guards as "crows."

In truth, Cubaryi is cruel and sadistic, relishing the malicious deeds she carries out for the cult. She took pleasure in witnessing the killing of [Crick Wellsley](crick_wellsley.md) and eagerly relished the opportunity to kill Temenos. Despite her depravity, she is deeply respectful and completely loyal to Kaldena. Even after being defeated and mortally wounded, she refuses to yield to Temenos's interrogation regarding Kaldena's whereabouts, telling him to "go to hell" before she dies.

## Biography

### The Pontiff's Murder
Shortly after [Pontiff Jörg](jorg.md) was found dead within [Flamechurch: Cathedral](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch_cathedral.md) by Temenos and Crick, Cubaryi and the Sacred Guard arrived to take control of the investigation after the monster was slain. She ordered Temenos and Crick to vacate immediately, taking nothing with them.

Following the pontiff's funeral, Cubaryi approached Temenos and Crick to ask whether they had found anything unusual in the cathedral, but Temenos dismissed her. Later, she intercepted Crick to inform him that he had been reassigned and ordered him to pack his belongings.

### Operations in Canalbrine
Cubaryi accompanied Captain Kaldena, Crick, and [Ort](ort.md) to [Canalbrine](../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md) under the auspices of investigating a string of murders. After Temenos and Crick subdued the culprit, [Vados the Architect](vados.md), aboard the [Sacred Guard's ship](../../locations/harborlands/dungeons/sacred_guard_ship.md), Cubaryi and Kaldena arrived with guard reinforcements. They arrested Vados, taunted Temenos, and took the prisoner away.

### Ambush in Stormhail
Inside the subterranean chambers of Sacred Guard Headquarters in Stormhail, Cubaryi confronted Temenos while he was investigating Crick's death. She dropped all pretense, revealing her true allegiance to Kaldena and their cult, and openly stated that Kaldena personally killed Crick.

Taking satisfaction in her orders to "bring the hound to heel," Cubaryi drew her blade and engaged Temenos in combat. She was defeated and mortally wounded. When Temenos pressed her for information on Kaldena's plans and location, Cubaryi refused to answer, telling him to "go to hell" as she died.
