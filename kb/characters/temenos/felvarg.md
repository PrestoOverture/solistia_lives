# Felvarg

## Overview

- **Creature Type**: Arcane beast, [lupine](../../world/species/direwolves.md)
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Location**: [Flamechurch: Cathedral](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch_cathedral.md), [Crestlands](../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md)

**Felvarg** is a monstrous, lupine arcane beast in *Octopath Traveler II* that serves as the instrument in the assassination of [Pontiff Jörg](jorg.md) during Chapter 1 of [Temenos Mistral](temenos.md)'s story.

## History

During the conspiracy orchestrated against the leadership of the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../../world/factions/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md), Felvarg was covertly lured into the sanctum of [Flamechurch: Cathedral](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch_cathedral.md) using the enticing scent of fool's poppy incense. Driven into a frenzy within the barred sanctuary, the beast ambushed and brutally slaughtered Pontiff Jörg, tearing open his back with massive claw wounds.

Shortly afterward, Inquisitor [Temenos Mistral](temenos.md) and Sanctum Knight [Crick Wellsley](crick_wellsley.md) breached the cathedral through a hidden passageway. Felvarg ambushed the two men among the shattered stained glass and church ruins, but was slain after a fierce battle. 

Examining the beast's corpse and the surrounding crime scene, Temenos deduced that the creature had not wandered in by chance, but had been deliberately guided into the church to disguise a calculated political assassination as a wild monster attack.

## Related Lore

Felvarg bears a strong physical resemblance to Auðnvarg, a legendary arcane beast sealed within the [Five-Tiered Tower](../../locations/hinoeuma/dungeons/five_tiered_tower.md) of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md).
