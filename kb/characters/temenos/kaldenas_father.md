# Kaldena's Father

**Kaldena's Father** was a member of the [Kal](../../world/lore/kal.md) tribe and the father of [Kaldena](kaldena.md) (originally named Dena). He sacrificed his life to save his daughter during the massacre of their people at the [Fellsun Ruins](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/fellsun_ruins.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Heritage**: The [Kal](../../world/lore/kal.md)
- **Origin**: [Fellsun Ruins](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/fellsun_ruins.md) (near [Crackridge](../../locations/wildlands/towns/crackridge.md))
- **Relatives**: [Kaldena](kaldena.md) (daughter)

### Appearance
As a member of the Kal, he bore a ceremonial tattoo of the Sacred Flame inscribed upon his forehead. He had short gray hair and dressed in a simple white tunic and trousers.

### Personality
Kaldena's father was a peaceful, devoted member of the Kal tribe. Driven by paternal love, he placed his daughter's survival above all else, selflessly throwing himself into harm's way without hesitation. Unskilled in combat, he stepped forward without a weapon, choosing to give his daughter precious moments to escape rather than cowering in fear.

## Story

Thirty years prior to the events of the story, the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md) descended upon the hidden sanctuary of the Kal in the Fellsun Ruins. As the cultists commenced their brutal slaughter of the villagers, Kaldena's father urged his young daughter, then called Dena, to run for her life. Stepping unarmed into the path of the encroaching assassins to buy her time, he was cut down by a single swift blow.

His sacrifice allowed Dena to escape the massacre. Grief-stricken and shaped by his death, she renamed herself Kaldena in memory of their fallen clan and dedicated her entire life to seeking vengeance against the killers of the Kal.
