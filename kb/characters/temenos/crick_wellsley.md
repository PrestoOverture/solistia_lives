# Crick Wellsley

"*The world is a cruel and irrational place. I wanted something to believe in. Something to hold fast to. I want to extend a hand to the weak and cleave the wickedness from this world. I wish to be that sword for the people.*" —Crick

**Crick Wellsley** is a newly anointed Sanctum Knight of the [Sacred Guard](../../world/factions/sacred_guard.md) who serves as a close companion and investigator alongside Inquisitor [Temenos Mistral](../travelers/temenos.md).

## Profile

### Appearance
Crick is a 22-year-old young man standing 182 cm (6'0") tall, with shaggy brown hair and blue eyes. He is consistently outfitted in the standard-issue armor of the Sanctum Knights, equipped with a full suit of plate body armor and a knightsword.

### Personality
Crick is earnest, courteous, and deeply principled, committed to upholding the code of the Sanctum Knights without compromise. He firmly rejects deceit and unnecessary violence, striving to be an honorable protector of the vulnerable.

Initially, Crick finds Temenos's methods maddening—particularly Temenos's habit of calling him "little lamb," daydreaming during active investigations, mocking Sacred Guard leadership, and freely tampering with crime scenes. These eccentricities lead Crick to question whether Temenos possesses true devotion. However, witnessing the Inquisitor's deductive brilliance wins Crick's profound trust. Over time, Crick allows himself to be drawn into Temenos's unorthodox investigations, including forcing open locked doors and infiltrating [Sacred Guard Headquarters](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail_sacred_guard_headquarters.md). Before parting ways for the last time, Crick opens up to Temenos regarding his past and how he arrived at his faith.

## History

### Prior to Story
Crick was born into a noble lineage that had fallen into destitution and disgrace. His parents instructed him to steal and exploit others to get ahead, but Crick rejected their opportunistic values in search of genuine purpose. A pivotal encounter with Inquisitor [Roi](roi.md) altered the course of his life when Roi told him, "A noble flame burns within you."

Moved by Roi's words, Crick enlisted in the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../../world/factions/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) to defend the innocent and combat wickedness. Alongside his fellow initiate and close friend [Ort](ort.md), Crick trained diligently. Both were anointed personally by [Captain Kaldena](kaldena.md) after passing their final trials. While Ort ascended rapidly through the ranks by obeying orders without question, Crick held fast to his personal moral compass.

### Flamechurch
Crick first meets Temenos in [Flamechurch](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch.md), where the young knight steps in to defend townsfolk against heretics hostile to the Sacred Flame. Temenos intervenes, incapacitating the heretic leader with divine magic. Together, they proceed to [Flamechurch: Cathedral](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch_cathedral.md) to meet [Pontiff Jörg](jorg.md), finding the sanctuary locked. By confronting the architect [Vados](vados.md), they locate a hidden underground passage into the cathedral.

Inside the sanctuary, they discover Pontiff Jörg murdered and are immediately attacked by the monstrous [Felvarg](felvarg.md). After subduing the beast, Temenos determines that the Pontiff was deliberately assassinated. [Deputy Cubaryi](cubaryi.md) of the Sacred Guard abruptly arrives, barring further investigation and demanding they leave the scene untouched. Days later, following the Pontiff's rites, Temenos and Crick secretly inspect a scripture volume recovered from the crime scene, uncovering a cryptic inscription: "*Soon, night shall fall*". Immediately afterward, Cubaryi reassigns Crick away from Flamechurch, forcing him to take leave of Temenos.

### Canalbrine
Crick crosses paths with Temenos again in [Canalbrine](../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md), intervening while local sentries question the Inquisitor. Joining forces once more, they visit the home of the theologian [Lucian](lucian.md), finding him murdered. Deducing that a serial killer is ritualistically targeting prominent figures in specific disciplines to perform a dark ceremony, they rush to the local tavern where the dancer [Hermes](hermes.md) is scheduled to perform.

At the tavern, they thwart an attack by the assassin, who is revealed to be the architect Vados. Pursuing him to a [Sacred Guard Ship](../../locations/harborlands/dungeons/sacred_guard_ship.md), Crick and Temenos defeat Vados in battle. Captain Kaldena and the Sacred Guard arrive to take Vados into custody, and Kaldena orders Crick to escort the prisoner to headquarters in [Stormhail](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail.md). Before departing, Crick reunites briefly with Ort and ensures that Temenos will be granted authorization to interrogate Vados upon his arrival in Stormhail.

### Stormhail and Death
Upon Temenos's arrival at Stormhail, Crick discovers that headquarters has no official intake record for Vados despite Crick having handed him over directly. Suspicious, the pair scour the surrounding grounds and locate Vados's murdered body discarded in the snow near the graveyard.

Realizing the corruption within the Sacred Guard, Crick conducts a covert search of the headquarters library. He uncovers a concealed passage leading to an underground archive containing forbidden heretical texts. Recognizing Kaldena's treachery, Crick attempts to escape and alert Temenos. Confronted by corrupted Sanctum Knights loyal to Kaldena's conspiracy, Crick fights valiantly through their ranks until Captain Kaldena herself confronts him. Wielding dark shadow magic, Kaldena overpowers and mortally strikes him down, leaving his body discarded in the snow outside headquarters.

Before succumbing to his wounds, Crick manages to leave behind a trail of clues that directs Temenos straight to the hidden archive, exposing Kaldena's conspiracy.
