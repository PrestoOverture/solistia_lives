# Morozov

**Morozov** is an assistant to [Mother](mother.md) at the [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](../locations/mothers_garden_orphanage.md) who appears during [Throné](throne.md)'s Chapter 3: Mother's Route in *Octopath Traveler II*.

## Profile

### Appearance
Morozov is a 24-year-old man with long black hair, wearing a white shirt and brown pants.

### Personality
Morozov demonstrates complete loyalty to Mother. He oversees her "convent" and acts as the doorkeeper for Mother's Garden. He places very little value on human life, casually remarking that he killed a nun.

## Story

When Throné enters the [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](../locations/mothers_garden_orphanage.md) in disguise, she observes Morozov standing beside Mother and [Mira](mira.md) as Mother praises Mira before the assembled orphans. After Mother passes through the doorway leading into Mother's Garden, Throné steals Morozov's key in order to follow her through the gate.

Morozov does not appear again following this encounter, leaving his eventual fate unknown after the downfall of the orphanage.
