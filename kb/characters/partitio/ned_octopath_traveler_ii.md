# Ned (Octopath Traveler II)

"*Hey, Boss? Got a job for me? Any job?*" —Ned, during hard times in Oresrush

**Ned** is a resident of [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md) and a loyal member of [Partitio](../travelers/partitio.md)'s posse, alongside [Joe](joe.md), [Harry](harry_octopath_traveler_ii.md), and [Nikki](nikki.md).

## Profile

### Appearance
Ned is a 20-year-old youth with short black hair. He wears a brown tunic paired with green pants.

### Personality
Ned is an earnest, hopeful young man who remains loyal and optimistic even during severe hardship. He was the very first person Partitio took in and the first to address him as "Boss," though he is occasionally vexed by having a lower profile compared to other members of the gang. Highly supportive of his companions, he backs Joe in resisting [Giff](giff.md) and frequently praises Partitio's generous nature. He is eager for work and quick to offer romantic encouragement or advice to his friends.

## Story

As a longstanding friend of Partitio in Oresrush, Ned helped stand against Giff and the ruffians attempting to claim the town's profits. When silver prices collapsed and the settlement plunged into deep poverty under Giff's exploitation, Ned and his companions struggled to survive, scraping by on whatever scraps of food they could find.

The prolonged famine created strain within the posse, culminating in Harry stealing a bread crust from Nikki. Partitio resolved the dispute by having Harry return the food and dividing his own remaining bread amongst the group. When Giff arrived shortly after to taunt the starving miners, Partitio rallied Ned and the rest of the gang to confront him.

Together, Partitio and his posse ousted Giff and exposed how the landowner had forged his contracts to defraud the townsfolk of their silver earnings. With prosperity returning to Oresrush, Ned celebrated alongside the town. Inspired by Joe's successful courtship of the woman he loved, Ned set out to woo the local tavern maid in Oresrush.
