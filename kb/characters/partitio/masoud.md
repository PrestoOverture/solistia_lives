# Masoud

**Masoud** is a 60-year-old legendary retired merchant residing at the top of the hill in [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md) in [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md). During his active years, he became renowned for pioneering trade across both continents when travel routes were still perilous and undeveloped. He authored *[The Mercantile Manuscript](../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md)*, a multi-volume treatise revered by merchants throughout Solistia. He is featured prominently in [Partitio Yellowil](partitio.md)'s Scent of Commerce storyline in Sai.

## Profile

### Appearance
Masoud has gray hair and dresses in practical traveler's clothing suited to the desert climates: a durable brown overcoat, matching trousers, and a wide-brimmed brown hat.

### Personality
Gruff, cantankerous, and cynical, Masoud spent his retirement turning away hopeful apprentices and curious merchants who swarmed his hilltop estate to peruse his writings. Having sacrificed much of his personal life and family time during his [daughter](masouds_daughter.md)'s upbringing in single-minded pursuit of his trade, Masoud grew disappointed with younger generations, lamenting that modern merchants had grown dull and complacent. Despite his prickly exterior, he retains a deep love for trade—often spending hours painstakingly examining wares in local shops—and respects anyone possessing true entrepreneurial wit.

## Story

In his youth, Masoud accomplished the unprecedented feat of establishing commercial networks across all eight regions of Solistia, documenting his philosophies and economic observations in *The Mercantile Manuscript*. 

When Partitio visits Sai seeking access to the manuscripts, Masoud dismisses him as a boastful nobody and issues a riddle: Partitio will only gain entrance to the archives if he presents Masoud with the treasure he values above all else. Partitio initially hires Masoud's estranged daughter and brings her before him, believing family to be the ultimate treasure. Masoud rejects the gesture, acknowledging that while most people cherish family above all, his own life had been spent prioritizing commerce.

Following a subtle hint from Masoud's daughter that the riddle concerns what Masoud himself prizes rather than conventional sentiment, Partitio realizes the true answer: Masoud's greatest treasure is Masoud himself—the accumulated knowledge, instincts, and indomitable spirit forged through a lifetime of commerce. Impressed that a merchant still exists who grasps the true essence of trade, Masoud concedes defeat and hands Partitio the archive key to study *The Mercantile Manuscript*.
