# Papp Yellowil

> *"You'll never lose if you never try, son. But you'll never win, either."*  
> — Papp

**Papp Yellowil** is a character in *Octopath Traveler II*. He is [Partitio](partitio.md)'s father and mentor in commerce, having helped found and build the pioneer mining town of [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md) from the ground up.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 56
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md)
- **Occupation**: Merchant / Silver merchant
- **Relatives**: [Partitio](partitio.md) (son), unnamed wife (deceased)

### Appearance
Papp is bald with a bushy black beard. He originally wore a yellow-banded white hat—which concealed a full head of hair attached to the inside to cover his baldness—before gifting it to Partitio prior to his son's departure on his journey.

### Personality
Papp is a hardworking businessman with deep devotion to his son and community. Affectionately calling Partitio "chickadee," he encourages his son to venture into the world and put his mercantile talents to good use. He possesses a resilient spirit despite personal setbacks and health struggles.

## History

### Background
Forty-eight years prior to the events of the game, at the age of eight, Papp ran away from his abusive, alcoholic father. By age sixteen, he had established his own shop, but lost everything when it burned down when he was twenty-four. Sometime after the fire, he met [Roque Brilliante](roque_brilliante.md) and started a new business partnership that earned them a fortune. Papp had his son Partitio at age thirty-two, but his wife passed away eight years later, just before he turned forty.

Sixteen years prior to the main story, Papp and young Partitio surveyed the barren Wildlands territory that would become [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md). Papp explained that he bought the land due to rumors of rich silver deposits in the hills. When Roque reminded Partitio that purchased land could not simply be given away for free, Papp tested his son's mercantile acumen by challenging him to purchase silver ore using leaves. Partitio succeeded, earning praise from both men.

Eight years later, Oresrush had grown prosperous through silver mining. When a mine worker reported that a local gang was causing trouble, Papp strictly cautioned Partitio against reckless action, though his son rushed off to confront them anyway. That night, Roque informed Papp that he was leaving town, believing its fortunes were beginning to fade, and warned Papp that the contract he signed contained a critical flaw.

Shortly after Roque's departure, the former landowner exploited the contractual loophole to reclaim the land and imposed heavy taxes on silver. Oresrush fell into severe poverty, leaving residents jobless and the town in decay. Papp was forced to sell his possessions and labor in the mines once more to survive, which severely degraded his health through overwork.

### Partitio's Tale
In the present day, Papp's illness flared up during a meal with Partitio, prompting his son to search for medicine. When Partitio returned dispirited from an encounter with [Giff](giff.md)—who had become the landowner's ruthless enforcer—Papp urged his son to take a stand and pursue what he truly wanted in life, prompting Partitio to confront Giff and his gang.

Following Giff's defeat by Partitio and his posse, Papp put Giff to work in the town store to pay off his debts. Papp and the townspeople then urged Partitio to embark on a worldwide journey to eradicate poverty.

Later, when Partitio returned to Oresrush while gathering merchandise for a new department store in [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md), Papp met him outside their home and sold him high-grade silver to support the enterprise.

Papp subsequently teamed up with a reformed Roque to take track measurements for the steam locomotive, aiding Partitio's vision of connecting the world. In the epilogue, Papp and Roque reveal plans to retire together and manage a small shop.
