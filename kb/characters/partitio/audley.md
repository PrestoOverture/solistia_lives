# Audley

> *"Fantastic! Sometimes soft, sometimes strong, the sweet sounds of sultry strings soothed my soul."*
> — Audley, upon listening to the violinist

## Overview

- **Age**: 31
- **Gender**: Male
- **Location**: [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md)
- **Occupation**: Merchant

**Audley** is an eccentric merchant residing in [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md). Dealing exclusively in odd and unusual commodities, he has procured peculiar items such as a puppet engineered to automatically offer toasts at parties and a dog-shaped threading machine. Although many of these curiosities fail to find buyers, his venture remains afloat thanks to the occasional sensational hit.

## Appearance

Audley wears a bright blue merchant's attire paired with a feathered hat, and carries a bow.

## Personality

Enthusiastic and eloquent, Audley possesses a boundless passion for oddities and novel inventions, despite the frequent commercial struggles of his unusual inventory. He is fond of vibrant, alliterative speech and is deeply inspired by [Partitio Yellowil](partitio.md)'s populist commercial vision.

## Story

During his travels through [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md), [Partitio](partitio.md) hears repeated cries for help coming from a house. Rushing inside, he discovers the building is deserted and realizes the looped sound is issuing from an unfamiliar mechanical device.

Audley returns home and introduces the machine as a "phonographic articulator." He explains that he has struggled to commercialize it because prospective clients prefer paper as a cheaper and more straightforward recording medium. Partitio suggests redirecting the invention toward preserving music rather than spoken words. Partitio sets out across Solistia to recruit three performers to test the device: a pianist from [Flamechurch](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch.md), a violinist from [Canalbrine](../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md), and a guitarist from [Crackridge](../../locations/wildlands/towns/crackridge.md).

Enchanted by the recordings, Audley initially considers selling the machines exclusively to wealthy elites. Partitio instead recommends placing the devices in taverns across the realm, popularizing recorded music among ordinary people until every household desires one of its own. Inspired by the idea of bringing music to everyone, Audley asks Partitio to choose a name for the device; Partitio dubs it the "gramophone." Following their collaboration, gramophones spread across taverns throughout the realm.
