# Roque Brilliante

**Roque Brilliante** is a wealthy industrial magnate and business tycoon who serves as the primary antagonist of [Partitio Yellowil](../travelers/partitio.md)'s story.

## Overview

- **Age**: 60
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md) (post-story)

A visionary yet ruthless merchant known in his prime as "Rainbow Tongue," Roque helped [Papp Yellowil](papp_yellowil.md) establish the mining town of [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md) before founding the [Roque Company](../../world/factions/roque_company.md) in [Clockbank](../../locations/brightlands/towns/clockbank.md) and ushering in the realm's "Age of Steam." 

Following the events of Partitio's journey, Roque steps down from executive leadership to become a consultant for the restructured firm Partitio & Roque, whose corporate charter states:
1. Put the happiness of others above all.
2. Be a safe haven for dreamers.
3. Show charity at every opportunity.
4. Never rewrite this pledge.

### Appearance

Roque has gray hair tied back in a ponytail and wears a silver-rimmed monocle. He walks with a cane and can wield a staff in self-defense. In his capacity as corporate magnate, he dressed in an imposing black-and-gold hat and cape, though as an ordinary working merchant he dresses in a practical gray vest and trousers.

### Personality

Roque is a calculating, pragmatically driven capitalist obsessed with maximum efficiency and continuous progress. Believing that history is shaped solely by the rich and powerful, he spent decades pursuing profit at any cost, ultimately disregarding the welfare of laborers, manipulating contracts, and swindling friends. Partitio notes that Roque earned the moniker "Rainbow Tongue" due to his spellbinding persuasive power, capable of convincing anyone that fortune lay at the end of his proposals.

Roque recognized Partitio's exceptional business acumen early on, but regarded Partitio's charitable philosophy as a waste of talent, believing wealth was meant to be hoarded rather than shared. When Partitio successfully met his exorbitant terms to purchase the steam engine patent, Roque's frustration drove him to deploy military hardware against him. Following his defeat, however, Roque confessed that he had become so consumed by boundless greed that he had forgotten why he ever desired wealth in the first place.

Along with [Dolcinaea Luciel](../agnea/dolcinaea_luciel.md), Roque is one of the only major antagonists whose actions and ambitions bear no connection to the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md).

## History

### Early Life and Rise to Wealth

In his youth, Roque became enamored with a shimmering pocket watch in a shop window. To purchase it, he worked tirelessly as a bootblack until his hands were stained indelibly black with polish. However, the moment he acquired the watch, his satisfaction vanished when another luxury caught his eye.

Roque spent decades repeating this cycle, working harsh jobs—including mining silver to the point of coughing up blood—to fund increasingly ambitious investments. Though he initially dreamed of bringing happiness to his family, his town, and the world, his commercial success gradually stripped away his scruples, until wealth became its own purpose.

### The Silver Boom of Oresrush

Sixteen years prior to the main story, Roque partnered with [Papp Yellowil](papp_yellowil.md) to develop a rugged desert outpost into the silver-mining town of [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md). When Partitio was a boy, Roque taught him that land was an asset acquired fair and square that could never be surrendered lightly. He tested Partitio's mercantile instincts and praised the youth's keen eye, heralding the arrival of an "Age of Silver."

Eight years later, as silver production began to peak, Roque decided the town's best days were behind it. He informed Papp that he was leaving for the eastern continent, where revolutionary steam technology was being patented. Before leaving, Roque cryptically warned Papp that the contract they signed for Oresrush contained a fatal loophole. Roque offered to bring Partitio with him into the new "Age of Steam," but Partitio refused to abandon the townspeople.

### The Roque Company and Roque Island

Relocating to [Clockbank](../../locations/brightlands/towns/clockbank.md), Roque established the [Roque Company](../../world/factions/roque_company.md), monopolizing the development and manufacture of steam engines while levying heavy fees and tariffs on local industry. His immense fortune enabled him to purchase an entire private territory, [Roque Island](../../locations/harborlands/towns/roque_island.md), where he built large-scale industrial factories and a company-owned town. He also secretly retained ownership of Oresrush through the altered contract clause, employing the gang leader [Giff](giff.md) to enforce crippling taxes on the struggling settlement.

### The Steam Engine Contract

Partitio, seeking to eliminate poverty worldwide, pursued Roque to [Clockbank](../../locations/brightlands/towns/clockbank.md) to purchase the manufacturing rights for the advanced steam engine developed by engineer [Floyd](floyd.md). Amused by Partitio's idealism, Roque drafted a legally binding agreement setting the patent price at an impossible sum: eighty billion leaves.

Some time later, Roque hosted an international showcase on [Roque Island](../../locations/harborlands/towns/roque_island.md) to debut his commercial locomotive. Partitio arrived to interrupt the event, accompanied by the eccentric billionaire [Alrond](alrond_rondwell.md), who delivered eighty billion leaves in cash to finalize the sale. Refusing to lose control of his crowning achievement, Roque attempted to void the contract, but the assembling scriveners and spectators upheld the transaction.

Desperate, Roque unleashed the [Steam Tank Obsidian](../../world/beasts/steam_tank_obsidian.md), an armored military combat machine powered by a steam engine. Partitio and his comrades destroyed the tank in battle. Defeated, Roque yielded the future to Partitio and prepared for ruin. Instead, Partitio invited Roque to partner with him in developing a continental rail network.

### Retirement and Reconciliation

Roque stepped down as corporate president, allowing Partitio to assume leadership of the reorganized Partitio & Roque company while remaining aboard as chief technical consultant. He eventually returned to [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md), carrying a commemorative silver coin gifted by Partitio. There, he reunited with Papp, and the two aging partners agreed to retire together and run a simple general shop.
