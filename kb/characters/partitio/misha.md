# Misha

> *"Master [Partitio](partitio.md), I take it. Word has reached me you would be coming. The master's study is this way. If you'd follow me."*
> — Misha, welcoming Partitio

> *"I enjoy the night. ... The master is much less of a handful."*
> — Misha, in [Wellgrove: Alrond's Estate](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove_alronds_estate.md)

**Misha** is the 25-year-old butler serving the wealthy nobleman [Alrond](alrond_rondwell.md) at [Wellgrove: Alrond's Estate](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove_alronds_estate.md) in [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md). His meticulous thoroughness in executing his duties has led townspeople to believe he has been a butler all his life. Although Alrond views Misha as a childhood friend, Misha does his best to maintain professional distance and avoid overt familiarity.

## Profile

### Appearance
Misha wears a formal black-and-white butler uniform. His brown hair is kept short with a bang parted to his left side.

### Personality
Misha is completely devoted to Alrond and the welfare of Wellgrove. He is modest and unpretentious, having spent his youth cleaning the city streets, and remains willing to take physical harm—such as withstanding [Thurston](thurston.md)'s assault—to keep his master safe. He is easily embarrassed by theatrics and regularly cautions Alrond against causing dramatic scenes in public, though Alrond largely dismisses these pleas.

## History

### Background
In his youth, Misha supported his family by collecting trash around Wellgrove to keep the streets clean. While working one day, a young man stopped to ask him about his labor and aspirations. After listening to Misha's story and ideals, the young man revealed himself to be [Alrond](alrond_rondwell.md) and offered him employment as his personal butler, a position Misha gratefully accepted.

### Journey with Partitio
Years later, Misha greets the merchant [Partitio Yellowil](partitio.md) at Alrond's estate, having been alerted by Alrond to expect his arrival after his master tested Partitio's character. Escorting Partitio to the study, Misha observes the negotiation over purchasing the steam engine rights for eighty billion leaves. Despite Misha's sensible objections to such an exorbitant sum, Alrond agrees to fund the contract on the condition that Partitio revitalize Wellgrove's stagnant commerce.

While Partitio gathers supplies for the town's industrial development, Thurston—a former subordinate of [Roque](roque_brilliante.md)—attacks the estate. Misha steps between them to protect Alrond, enduring injuries and being subdued before Partitio arrives to defeat Thurston.

Later, Misha travels with Alrond to [Roque Island](../../locations/harborlands/towns/roque_island.md), arriving just in time to reinforce Partitio when Roque attempts to corner him regarding the steam engine contract. Misha witnesses Partitio close the deal and subsequently defeat Roque's Steam Tank Obsidian.

### The Master's Night Outings
Following these events, Misha grows worried over Alrond's persistent, secretive departures from the manor at night. After gathering information on his master's movements, Misha confronts Alrond outside the estate, discovering that the nobleman has taken to sneaking into town to collect trash under the guise of "treasure hunting." Following a poignant heart-to-heart regarding their past, the two companions agree to venture into Wellgrove together on nightly treasure hunts.
