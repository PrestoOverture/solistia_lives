# Partitio Yellowil

> *"I'll be back once I eliminate that devil called poverty from the world!"*  
> — Partitio Yellowil, upon leaving [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md)

**Partitio Yellowil** is one of eight playable characters in *Octopath Traveler II*. A charismatic and compassionate Merchant who follows in the footsteps of [his father](papp_yellowil.md) and partner [Roque Brilliante](roque_brilliante.md), he sets out to eliminate poverty and share wealth and prosperity across the whole of Solistia after experiencing hardship in his hometown of [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 24
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Origin**: [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md)
- **Occupation**: Merchant
- **Relatives**: [Papp](papp_yellowil.md) (father), unnamed mother (deceased)

### Appearance
Partitio wears a gray vest over a white undershirt and tie, accompanied by white gloves, black trousers, and brown shoes. Bright yellow is his hallmark color, visible in both his vibrant coat—worn draped over his shoulders with loose hanging sleeves—and the yellow band around his white fedora, a gift from his father. Over the course of his early life, his appearance evolved from an eight-year-old child and a sixteen-year-old youth (both dressed in simpler attire without coat or hat), to a young man wearing the yellow coat, and finally his signature look with his father's hat.

He prefers durable clothing made with double-stitching, which he considers a mark of the care and pride weavers put into their craft. In English, he speaks with an American southern accent.

### Personality
Partitio is an energetic, hardworking, and deeply generous man endowed with natural charisma and commercial acumen. Having lived through severe poverty, he despises the suffering it brings and makes it his life's ambition to eliminate it entirely. He firmly believes that the ultimate goal of a merchant is not personal wealth, but spreading happiness and shared prosperity to others.

Despite his gentle and easygoing disposition, Partitio possesses a fiery temper when someone "gets his goat," becoming enraged when witnessing injustice, greed, or the exploitation of ordinary laborers. At the same time, he is profoundly empathetic and emotional; the hardships of his neighbors and friends in Oresrush once moved him to tears. He is also exceptionally forgiving, willing to offer redemption and employment to former adversaries such as [Thurston](thurston.md) and Roque.

Partitio is an innovative thinker who continually seeks new ways to conduct business, solve community problems, and harness emerging technology for the common good. Whenever he meets someone new, he hands them a silver coin, describing it as a "mercantile investment" to ensure they will remember his face. His relentless optimism and generosity inspire almost everyone he encounters.

## History

### Background
Sixteen years prior to current events, eight-year-old Partitio, his father [Papp](papp_yellowil.md), and Papp's business partner [Roque Brilliante](roque_brilliante.md) traveled to the Wildlands following rumors of silver deposits, laying the foundation for what would become the pioneer mining town of [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md). To test the boy's mercantile instincts, Papp challenged him to purchase silver ore using leaves. Partitio succeeded, earning praise from both men.

Eight years later, Oresrush had blossomed into a prosperous boomtown centered on silver extraction, with sixteen-year-old Partitio working as an accomplished local merchant. When a gang led by [Giff](giff.md) began harassing the town's workers, Partitio helped drive them off. Shortly after, Roque announced he was leaving for the east to pursue the steam engine, warning that the silver age was drawing to a close. Roque invited Partitio to join him, but Partitio refused, choosing to remain with his community.

Following Roque's departure, the former landowner exploited a loophole in the purchase contract to reclaim the land and impose an exorbitant tax on silver mining. Deprived of their livelihood, the people of Oresrush fell into abject poverty.

### Main Story

#### Chapter 1
At age twenty-four, Partitio spends his days caring for his ailing father, whose health has broken down from overwork. While retrieving medicine, he encounters his childhood friends reduced to begging and stealing food just to survive. Frustrated and moved to tears by their misery, Partitio shares his own food with them. Giff and his enforcers arrive to announce another increase in silver taxes, mocking the destitute townsfolk.

Returning home, Partitio receives guidance from Papp, who encourages him to "do whatever it is you wanna do most." Inspired, Partitio gathers his friends to storm [Giff's Manse](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/giffs_manse.md). Partitio lectures Giff on the importance of the common workers who create wealth, warning that treating them with contempt will ultimately destroy everything. After defeating Giff in battle, Partitio coerces the terrified thug into confessing that his true employer is Roque, and that the contract clause seizing the town's mine was fraudulent.

With the unfair taxes overturned, prosperity returns to Oresrush, and Giff is put to work running errands for Papp. Papp takes Partitio to the cliffside overlooking their town and urges him to see the wider world and fulfill his potential. He gifts Partitio his white fedora, declaring him a full merchant and imploring him to help those suffering elsewhere. Partitio vows to eliminate the devil called poverty and departs Oresrush.

#### Chapter 2
Partitio arrives in the bustling industrial city of [Clockbank](../../locations/brightlands/towns/clockbank.md), astonished by its scale and modernity. While exploring, he stops company official [Thurston](thurston.md) from physically assaulting a boy named [Will](will.md) over unpaid Roque Company taxes. He is then approached by [Ori](ori.md), an inquisitive scrivener who introduces him to business cards, newspapers, and the history of how the Roque Company harnessed the steam engine to achieve immense wealth. Disturbed that poverty persists alongside such wealth, Partitio resolves to meet Roque.

At the company factory, Partitio meets [Floyd](floyd.md), an earnest engineer attempting to make the steam engine cheap and accessible for mass production. Partitio eagerly offers to help, helping Floyd secure clockite—a heat-resistant mineral—and partnering with a clockmaker to finish the prototype. When they return from celebrating, however, Thurston has confiscated the engine under company contract rules and sics the guard hound [Garnet](garnet.md) on Partitio. Partitio intimidates the hound and confronts Roque in person.

Roque admits his past deception in Oresrush without remorse. Partitio proposes a commercial transaction: he will purchase the rights to the steam engine outright. Roque sets an impossible price of eighty billion leaves. Unshaken, Partitio accepts the terms and promises to return with eighty billion in hand. Ori gives him a crucial lead: Lord [Alrond](alrond_rondwell.md) of [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md), a nobleman of legendary wealth. Before setting out, Partitio promises Will that he will change the world into a place where people can freely pursue their dreams.

#### Chapter 3
In [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md), Partitio finds the local economy strangely depressed. He intervenes when a young nobleman is nearly cheated by a merchant selling counterfeit goods, exposing the fraud. The grateful young man offers to help Partitio meet Lord Alrond, but only after Partitio proves his commercial achievements. Partitio shares his mercantile successes, and the man directs him to Alrond's estate.

Upon arriving at the mansion, Partitio discovers that the young man is Lord Alrond himself, having worn a disguise to assess the true character of those seeking his patronage. Alrond is moved by Partitio's sincerity, but conditions any financial aid on saving Wellgrove from economic ruin. Partitio devises a solution: consolidating the town's merchants into a single central venue. After refurbishing an abandoned building, securing wares from across Solistia, and hiring staff, he opens "Alrond's Department Store," transforming the town into a bustling commercial hub.

Returning to the manor, Partitio finds the estate shrouded in scalding steam and Alrond's butler, [Misha](misha.md), gravely injured. The attacker is Thurston, who was fired by Roque after the failure in Clockbank and sought revenge on Partitio. Partitio defeats Thurston in combat, reprimands him for relying on underhanded methods, and promises to hire him once he obtains the steam engine rights.

Alrond rewards Partitio with an eighty-billion-leaf promissory note, warning him never to trust Roque. Ori then arrives with breaking news: Roque is hosting an exclusive industrial convention on his private island, [Roque Island](../../locations/harborlands/towns/roque_island.md), to showcase his technology.

#### Chapter 4
Using his personal vessel, Partitio sails to [Roque Island](../../locations/harborlands/towns/roque_island.md). When entry to the convention is barred, Ori guides him through a secret tavern passageway and creates a distraction by feigning illness so Partitio can bypass the guards.

Partitio confronts Roque before the assembled press and presents Alrond's eighty-billion promissory note. Roque refuses the note, claiming he will only accept tangible currency. At that moment, Alrond arrives in harbor aboard his private ship alongside Misha, carrying eighty billion leaves in cold, hard cash. Trapped by his own conditions, Roque cancels the deal in breach of his contract and flees into his warehouse. He emerges piloting his ultimate war engine, the [Steam Tank Obsidian](../../world/beasts/steam_tank_obsidian.md). Partitio battles the mechanical behemoth and destroys it.

Defeated, Roque reveals the root of his greed: as an impoverished child working as a bootblack, he saved tirelessly to purchase an expensive pocket watch, only to feel an insatiable void the moment he obtained it, plunging him into a lifelong obsession with material accumulation. Expecting to be discarded, Roque tells Partitio to leave him in the ruins of his empire. Partitio refuses, inviting Roque to become his business partner and use the steam engine for the public good.

A few days later, Roque and Papp reconcile in Oresrush, drawing up blueprints for a transcontinental steam railway. In Clockbank, Partitio meets Will once more, offering him a job on the future steam train.

### The Scent of Commerce
Alongside his primary journey, Partitio pursues three independent commercial ventures:

- **Tropu'hopu ([Tropu'hopu Route](../../stories/side_stories/totohaha/the_scent_of_commerce_tropuhopu.md))**: In the [Tropu'hopu Shipyard](../../locations/totohaha/towns/tropuhopu_shipyard.md), Partitio encounters [Terry](terry.md), the apprentice of a legendary shipwright, whose unfinished vessel is rejected by wealthy buyers. Partitio recognizes the ship's exceptional quality and raises 100,000 leaves to purchase it. Deeply moved by his appreciation of her craft, Terry completes the ship, which Partitio names *The Grand Terry*, granting him the ability to traverse [The Sundering Sea](../../locations/sundering_sea/the_sundering_sea.md).
- **Winterbloom ([Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md) Route)**: In Winterbloom, Partitio meets an inventor named [Audley](audley.md) who has developed a "phonographic articulator" capable of recording voices, but cannot find a market for it. Partitio envisions using the machine to bring music into homes and taverns across the world. He recruits three musicians—a pianist from [Flamechurch](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch.md), a violinist from [Canalbrine](../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md), and a guitarist from [Crackridge](../../locations/wildlands/towns/crackridge.md)—to record performances, suggests distributing the machines to taverns, and coins the name "gramophone."
- **Sai ([Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md) Route)**: In Sai, Partitio seeks access to the *[Mercantile Manuscript](../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md)*, written by the legendary merchant [Masoud](masoud.md). Masoud tests Partitio by asking him to identify what a merchant treasures most. After considering various treasures, Partitio concludes that what a merchant treasures most is himself, and offers to hire Masoud on the spot. Pleased with the answer, Masoud grants him access to his manuscript.

### Crossed Paths: Osvald & Partitio

#### Part 1
In [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md), Partitio and [Osvald V. Vanstein](../osvald/osvald.md) discover [Regulus](../others/regulus.md), an astronomer and former colleague of Osvald, collapsed in the street from starvation. After feeding him, Regulus reveals that he is constructing an astronomical telescope to observe the stars, but has run out of funds. Over Osvald's reservations, Partitio purchases the necessary lenses and materials with Regulus's remaining coin, and Regulus heads to [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md) to complete the instrument.

#### Part 2
In Montwise, Partitio and Osvald visit Regulus's laboratory, only to find the scholar impoverished once more. Regulus confesses that he pawned his telescope to an exploitative moneylender to cover living expenses. Investigating outside the lender's manse, the duo overhear thugs admitting that Regulus has already paid off his debt. Inside, Partitio prepares to negotiate a repurchase, but Osvald forcibly mugs the moneylender instead, with Partitio opting to look the other way.

After returning the telescope to Regulus, the three look up at the night sky. Osvald and Regulus spot the cross-shaped constellation of Cetus, which should normally be visible only during daylight hours. Regulus reveals that he had observed the daylight hours across Solistia gradually shortening, suggesting that an unnatural night is overtaking the world.

## Relationships

- **[Papp](papp_yellowil.md)**: Partitio's father and foundational mentor. Papp instilled in Partitio his core values of compassion, community solidarity, and using wealth to uplift others. Papp gifts Partitio his signature white fedora before Partitio embarks on his journey.
- **[Roque Brilliante](roque_brilliante.md)**: A close family friend and co-founder of Oresrush who became corrupted by greed, abandoning the town and subjecting it to ruinous taxes. Despite Roque's antagonism, Partitio refuses to leave him in ruin, hiring him to work alongside Papp in designing a continental railway.
- **[Ori](ori.md)**: A cheerful and ubiquitous journalist who assists Partitio with information regarding Alrond and Roque Island. In her [journal fragments](../../stories/documents/oris_journal_fragments.md), she describes Partitio as "an honest fellow with laughing eyes." When her involvement with the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md) and the dark ritual at [Fellsun Ruins](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/fellsun_ruins.md) is revealed, Partitio is dispirited by her betrayal but deeply relieved that she survives.
- **[Floyd](floyd.md)**: An earnest engineer at the Roque Company whose dream of mass-producing affordable steam engines matches Partitio's vision for shared prosperity. Partitio assists him in sourcing materials and completing his prototype.
- **[Alrond](alrond_rondwell.md)**: The eccentric and fabulously wealthy lord of Wellgrove who disguises himself as a commoner to test Partitio's character. After Partitio saves Wellgrove's economy by opening a department store, Alrond provides the eighty billion leaves needed to purchase the steam engine.
- **[Thurston](thurston.md)**: Roque's ruthless factory enforcer. After being fired and attempting to kill Partitio in Wellgrove, Partitio reprimands him for his deceitful methods but promises him a legitimate job once he secures the steam engine rights.
- **[Will](will.md)**: A young boy in Clockbank harassed for taxes. Partitio protects him and promises to create a world where people can freely choose their livelihoods, later offering him a position working on the steam train.
- **[Terry](terry.md)**: An apprentice shipwright in Tropu'hopu whose talent is overlooked by other buyers. Partitio purchases her ship for 100,000 leaves and names it *The Grand Terry* in her honor.
- **[Audley](audley.md)**: An inventor in Winterbloom whom Partitio helps commercialize the phonograph, creating the "gramophone."
- **[Masoud](masoud.md)**: A legendary merchant in Sai whose riddle Partitio solves by offering to hire him, earning access to the *[Mercantile Manuscript](../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md)*.
- **[Osvald V. Vanstein](../osvald/osvald.md)**: Fellow traveler and partner during their shared journey to assist Regulus. Osvald's brooding rationality contrasts with Partitio's sunny warmth, though the two share a mutual respect.

## Lore & Trivia

- Partitio is one of only two protagonists who does not kill any of their chapter antagonists, alongside [Agnea Bristarni](../agnea/agnea.md). Neither of their personal storylines directly pit them against agents of the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md).
- In her [journal fragments](../../stories/documents/oris_journal_fragments.md), Ori writes of Partitio as "an honest fellow with laughing eyes."
- In travel banter with [Ochette](../ochette/ochette.md), the beastling finds Partitio's name difficult to pronounce and nicknames him "Partihardy" (shortening it to "Parti").
- In travel banter with [Throné Anguis](../throne/throne.md), Partitio entrusts Lord Alrond's eighty-billion-leaf promissory note to her safekeeping.
- When asked by Osvald to theorize on the mythical nature of magic, Partitio jokingly suggests that the [seventh source](../../world/lore/one_true_magic.md) is money.
- Many townspeople and acquaintances connected to Partitio's story carry a silver coin in their possessions, in keeping with his habit of giving a silver coin to everyone he meets.
- During his efforts to stock Alrond's Department Store in [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md), Partitio's extraordinary salesmanship leads a local merchant to revere him as [Bifelgan](../../world/gods/bifelgan.md) the Trader, the god of commerce.
- Despite his mercantile instincts, Partitio had never encountered modern conventions like business cards or newspapers until visiting [Clockbank](../../locations/brightlands/towns/clockbank.md), having lived his entire life in the isolated frontier town of Oresrush.
