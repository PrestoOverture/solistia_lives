# Thurston

> *"Forget your silly dreams, [Floyd](floyd.md). They're not needed here."*  
> — Thurston

**Thurston** is a character in *Octopath Traveler II*. He initially serves as the ruthless, hyper-efficient secretary to industrial magnate [Roque](roque_brilliante.md) before reforming and entering [Partitio](partitio.md)'s employment.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 35
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Roque Island: Headquarters](../../locations/harborlands/towns/roque_island_headquarters.md) (day), [Roque Island](../../locations/harborlands/towns/roque_island.md) (night)
- **Occupation**: Secretary ([Roque Company](../../world/factions/roque_company.md)), later manager/administrator under Partitio

### Appearance
Thurston is a sharp-featured man who wears a neat white business suit and a pair of glasses.

### Personality
Thurston is exceptionally capable, known for managing the workload of ten men with relentless efficiency. Early on, however, his fierce devotion to Roque and insatiable drive for results translated into cruel, predatory business practices. He had no qualms about demanding inhuman daily quotas from workers or extorting taxes from children like [Will](will.md), even threatening physical violence if they failed to pay. He was also fiercely vindictive; when Roque fired him for his corrupt conduct in Clockbank, Thurston sought to murder Partitio to reclaim his standing.

Following his defeat at Wellgrove, Partitio forgave him and recognized his tenacity as an invaluable asset, offering him employment. Moved by Partitio's benevolence, Thurston turned over a new leaf. He adopted Partitio's vision of shared prosperity and began advocating for humane working conditions, ensuring laborers work strictly within their limits. His administrative style remains intensely analytical, relying heavily on data collection and meticulous employee interviews to monitor the wellbeing of his staff.

## History

### Background
For many years, Thurston served as the right-hand secretary to [Roque](roque_brilliante.md) at the [Roque Company](../../world/factions/roque_company.md). His unyielding enforcement of productivity quotas earned him enmity among workers in [Clockbank: Industrial District](../../locations/brightlands/towns/clockbank_industrial_district.md), where one employee was fired after punching Thurston during a dispute over grueling working hours.

### Partitio's Tale
Thurston first encounters [Partitio](partitio.md) in [Clockbank](../../locations/brightlands/towns/clockbank.md), where he attempts to extort taxes from the youth [Will](will.md). Partitio drives him away, but Thurston soon attempts to seize control of [Floyd](floyd.md)'s newly designed steam engine on behalf of the Roque Company. When Partitio steps in to protect Floyd, Thurston unleashes the company attack hound [Garnet](garnet.md), though Partitio overcomes the beast. Along with Floyd and the journalist [Ori](ori.md), Thurston witnesses Roque agreeing to sell the steam engine rights to Partitio for eighty billion leaves.

Following the scandal in Clockbank, Roque summarily fired Thurston. Blaming Partitio for his ruined career, Thurston tracked him to [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md) in an attempt to assassinate both Partitio and the wealthy noble [Alrond](alrond_rondwell.md) to win back Roque's favor. Operating a concealed steam-powered machine shrouded in fog (Enemy in the Mist), Thurston was defeated once the engine was destroyed. Instead of abandoning him, Partitio reprimanded Thurston's misdeeds but praised his persistence and offered him a job once the steam engine contract was secured. Utterly humbled, Thurston accepted.

After Partitio successfully purchased the rights to the engine, Thurston joined [Papp](papp_yellowil.md), Roque, and [Giff](giff.md) in [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md) to assist with layout planning and logistics for the steam locomotive.

### Post-Story
Now managing operations on [Roque Island](../../locations/harborlands/towns/roque_island.md), Thurston spends his days conducting employee interviews and acquiring clients, and his nights walking Garnet and feeding her biscuits. Concerned that Floyd is working himself to exhaustion without rest, Thurston seeks outside assistance to compel the stubborn engineer to sleep. When Floyd awakes, Thurston expresses sincere concern for his health, astonishing Floyd at how thoroughly his former tormentor has changed.

During the epilogue, [Greg](../castti/greg.md) approaches Thurston seeking employment, and Thurston promises to put in a good word on behalf of his passionate spirit.
