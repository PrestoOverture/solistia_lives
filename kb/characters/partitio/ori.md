# Ori

"*You there! Yes, you! I saw the whole thing! 'Mysterious Man in Yellow Coat Chases Off Villainous Roque Company Collector!' What a scoop! I can see the headline now!*" —Ori, upon meeting [Partitio](../travelers/partitio.md)

**Ori** is an industrious scrivener for the *Delsta Times* who features prominently in [Partitio Yellowil](../travelers/partitio.md)'s journey across Solistia, while secretly acting as an operative for the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md).

## Profile

### Appearance
Ori is a short young woman who wears a brown cap, a white shirt, brown trousers with suspenders, a blue ascot, and brown boots.

### Personality
To the public, Ori appears as an enthusiastic, curious scrivener perpetually hunting for her next big scoop. She chases leads with relentless energy and is always eager to report on significant events across the realm. She shows deep admiration and support for Partitio, charmed by his altruistic ambitions.

Beneath her cheerful reporter facade, Ori harbored a deeply nihilistic outlook born from severe childhood trauma. Like her adoptive older brother [Oboro](../hikari/oboro.md), she came to believe the world was beyond saving and sought its total destruction through the revival of the dark god [Vide](../../world/gods/vide.md). However, witnessing Partitio's boundless kindness and sincere desire to eradicate poverty sparked genuine hope within her, leading her to question the cult's apocalyptic mission.

## History

### Background
Ori's earliest memory is of terror and slaughter—a flashing spray of crimson blood amidst screaming crowds as invading soldiers from [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md) butchered fleeing innocents, some laughing as they struck down victims. Her adoptive older brother, Oboro, guided her through the carnage and helped her escape.

Growing up as impoverished citizens in Ku-occupied territory, the siblings suffered profound despair and misery, leading them to abandon hope for the future. Inducted into the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md), Ori secured a position as a reporter for the *Delsta Times* to serve as an intelligence gatherer. In this role, she tracked major events across the continent and documented traveler activities in her notes (later found as [Ori's Journal Fragments](../../stories/documents/oris_journal_fragments.md)). Among her observations, she attended [Osvald V. Vanstein](../travelers/osvald.md)'s trial five years prior to the main story, witnessing him being sentenced to [Frigit Isle](../../locations/winterlands/towns/frigit_isle.md), and later interviewed [Castti Florenz](../travelers/castti.md) following the apothecary's defense of [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md) against [Trousseau](../castti/trousseau.md)'s toxic rain. Her prolific reporting drew envious remarks from other writers, including a rival scrivener in Timberain.

### Partitio's Journey
Ori took a keen interest in Partitio from their first encounter, actively reporting on his initiatives and providing timely assistance. She provided him crucial information about the reclusive noble [Alrond](alrond_rondwell.md), publicized the opening of the [Wellgrove: Department Store](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove_department_store.md), assisted him in penetrating [Roque](roque_brilliante.md)'s factory on [Roque Island](../../locations/harborlands/towns/roque_island.md), and created a distraction among the factory guards to allow Partitio to proceed.

### The Sacred Flame and Dawn
Ori was chosen by the Moonshade Order to sacrifice herself to extinguish the Sacred Flame located at the [Fellsun Ruins](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/fellsun_ruins.md). Although the ritual was meant to end in death, her newfound faith in humanity—awakened by Partitio's selflessness—allowed her to survive the darkness. She was discovered and sheltered by villagers from nearby [Crackridge](../../locations/wildlands/towns/crackridge.md), where she rested to recuperate at the local inn.

Following the defeat of Vide and the return of the dawn, Ori attended [Agnea Bristarni](../travelers/agnea.md)'s celebratory performance at the [New Delsta: Theater](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md), watching contentedly from near the back exit.

## Lore Details

- **Writing Reputation**: [Throné Anguis](../travelers/throne.md) has read Ori's articles in the *Delsta Times*, praising them as detailed, well-constructed, and charged with an extraordinary energy.
- **Preferences**: As recorded in her journals, Ori strongly disliked the bitter, windswept climate of [Stormhail](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail.md).
