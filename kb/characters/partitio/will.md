# Will

**Will** is an eight-year-old boy living in [Clockbank](../../locations/brightlands/towns/clockbank.md) who works as a bootblack in [Clockbank: Industrial District](../../locations/brightlands/towns/clockbank_industrial_district.md). Following the death of his grandfather several years prior, Will took up shoe shining to provide for his younger sister and siblings. Despite his difficult circumstances, he harbors a deep love for learning and dreams of traveling beyond the industrial city.

> *"I'll polish up a storm and make a bundle!"*

## Profile

### Appearance
Will has black hair and wears a white shirt. [Partitio](../travelers/partitio.md) notes that the boy possesses deft fingers suited for craftsmanship and labor.

### Personality
Despite his youth, Will possesses a fierce and stubborn determination to protect his family. He refuses to submit to intimidation from extortionists like [Thurston](thurston.md), clinging stubbornly to his hard-earned coin because it represents food and shelter for his younger sister and siblings. He is proud of honest labor and takes diligently to shoe shining. Will also has a keen intellectual curiosity, treasuring the academic tomes left behind by his grandfather and reading his collection of mathematics books countless times. Deep down, he longs to escape the factory town and see the wider world.

## Story

Following his grandfather's passing, Will became the sole provider for his younger siblings, working every day in the industrial district to put food on the table.

During [Partitio](../travelers/partitio.md)'s arrival in Clockbank, Partitio discovers Will being badgered and extorted by [Thurston](thurston.md), a ruthless collector for the [Roque Company](../../world/factions/roque_company.md). Thurston demands additional taxes and threatens to break the young boy's knees when he refuses to surrender his earnings. Partitio intervenes, driving the tax collector away and sparing Will from harm.

Later, Partitio encounters Will shining shoes near the entrance of Clockbank. Partitio sits down for a shine and asks the boy about his hopes for the future. Will confesses his dream of traveling the world and leaving behind the city where he has spent his entire life. Before setting off, Partitio promises Will that he will help build a world where anyone can freely pursue the work they desire.

After Partitio successfully secures the rights to the steam engine and partners with [Roque](roque_brilliante.md), he returns to Clockbank to visit Will once more. Partitio introduces the boy to the concept of locomotives and the coming railway age, inviting him to take a future job working on the railroad so he can fulfill his dream of seeing the world. Partitio also gifts him a silver coin, which Will keeps as a prized possession.

Following the conclusion of Partitio's journey, Will continues to reside in the upper portion of [Clockbank: Industrial District](../../locations/brightlands/towns/clockbank_industrial_district.md). In the epilogue, he is shown to have formed a friendship with [Melia](../castti/melia.md).
