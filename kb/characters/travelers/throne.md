# Throné Anguis

> *"Ever since then… I killed, just the way [Father](../throne/father.md) taught me. I can't remember how many lives I've taken. My hands are forever tainted with the stench of blood, but soon… soon I will put this life behind me."*
> — Throné, recalling her first kill

## Overview

- **Age**: 23
- **Gender**: Female
- **Origin / Location**: [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md), [Brightlands](../../locations/brightlands/brightlands.md)
- **Occupation**: Thief, assassin of the [Blacksnakes](../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md)
- **Status**: Alive
- **Relatives**:
  - [Claude](../throne/claude_blacksnakes.md) (biological father, deceased)
  - [Marietta](../throne/marietta.md) (biological mother, deceased)
  - [Father](../throne/father.md) / [Sebastian](../throne/father.md) (adoptive father / half-brother, deceased)
  - [Mother](../throne/mother.md) (adoptive mother / half-sister, deceased)
  - [Pirro](../throne/pirro.md) (half-brother, deceased)
  - [Scaracci](../throne/scaracci.md) (half-brother, deceased)

**Throné Anguis** is a thief and assassin operating from the shadows of [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md), and one of eight travelers in *Octopath Traveler II*. Raised from childhood within the [Blacksnakes](../../world/factions/blacksnakes.md)—a criminal guild ruled by leaders known as "[Mother](../throne/mother.md)" and "[Father](../throne/father.md)"—she was groomed into a lethally skilled killer despite her deep-seated hatred for violence. Bound by a poison-filled collar locked by two separate keys held by her adoptive parents, Throné sets out to reclaim the keys, break the guild's cycle of bloodshed, and win her freedom.

## Appearance

Throné is a twenty-three-year-old woman with neck-length brown hair that falls across her right eye and dangling earrings. She wears a full-length purple dress featuring long sleeves with loose ruffled cuffs, a low-cut back, and a neckline that plunges into a fitted brown leather bodice. The dress is split high up the left side, exposing stockings secured by ruffled garters customized to conceal a dagger, worn alongside tall, dark brown heeled boots. She is frequently depicted draped in a long white fur stole. She is left-handed, using her dominant left hand for her work.

A prominent and oppressive feature of her appearance is her collar—a snug black choker bearing a dangling pendant at the front. The collar is secured by two independent locks, each keyed separately to Mother and Father. It cannot simply be cut or forced off, as tampering with the mechanism releases a lethal poison that would kill her instantly.

Spiraling around her left arm is the tattoo marking her as a Blacksnake: a long black serpent with its tail resting beneath her left collarbone and its head coiling onto the back of her hand. Though her profession usually prevents her from wearing cosmetics or scents, she occasionally wears a light perfume distilled from shoreflowers as a personal pick-me-up.

## Personality

Calm, composed, and ruthlessly focused, Throné carries the emotional scars of a childhood steeped in physical and psychological abuse. She displays an unwavering resolve in pursuit of her goals, willing to endure physical danger and personal degradation if it brings her closer to her mark. She does not fear death; her only dread is surrendering her will and resigning herself to remain a slave to the Blacksnakes.

Despite her lethal training, Throné harbors an intense revulsion toward bloodshed. Since her first kill as a child, she has been nauseated by the stench of blood, feeling as though her lungs are rotting whenever she smells it. This moral friction leads her to exercise mercy whenever possible: she convinces [Scaracci](../throne/scaracci.md) to spare an unconscious guard, empathizes with mistreated animals, and vows to obliterate the Blacksnakes entirely to halt the practice of grooming orphaned children into killers. She feels a deep, unspoken kinship with others seeking to escape captivity or break cycles of violence, such as [Osvald V. Vanstein](osvald.md) and [Hikari Ku](hikari.md).

Though cynical and guarded by necessity, Throné is capable of genuine warmth, politeness, and playful humor with those she trusts. She adopts a caring, older-sisterly attitude toward [Ochette](ochette.md) (who affectionately nicknames her "Néné"), treats [Castti Florenz](castti.md) with the respect due to a nurturing mother figure, and shares an effortless rapport with [Temenos Mistral](temenos.md), whom she dubs "Detective."

Throné possesses distinct personal tastes: she is fascinated by high fashion, jewelry, and gems, believing that finely crafted clothes capture the souls and artistry of their makers. She also loves dogs and raspberries, and once befriended a stray puppy after feeding it dried strawberries. Conversely, she dislikes crowded places, despises uninvited sexual advances (coldly brushing off or knocking out lecherous men), and holds open contempt for people she considers weaklings, such as [Greg](../castti/greg.md) and [Bergomi](../throne/bergomi.md). While she despises murder, she has no moral qualms about thievery, though she restricts her targets to those who can afford the loss. Privately, she longs to leave the world of shadows behind and escape to a secluded haven absent from every map.

Mother used beatings and whips to terrorize the children into absolute subservience. ## History

### Prior to Story

Born to Marietta and Claude in [Lostseed](../../locations/brightlands/towns/lostseed.md), Throné was taken in by Sebastian, the man she knew as "Father." Raised alongside "Mother," she was aware that neither was her biological parent. Father trained her in the arts of assassination despite her revulsion toward killing, while Mother subjected her to relentless physical abuse. After committing her first kill as a child, Throné was formally inducted into the Blacksnakes with a locked collar; Mother threatened severe pain if she rebelled, while Father encouraged her to view assassination as a means of ridding the world of evil. During her adulthood, she placed a wager with her guild comrades over whether a rumored treasure in a cathedral was real, keeping three coins as collateral.

### Chapter 1: The Blacksnakes of New Delsta

Throné, along with [Pirro](../throne/pirro.md), [Scaracci](../throne/scaracci.md), and a severely wounded [Donnie](../throne/donnie.md), flee pursuers through the [Underground Waterway](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/underground_waterway.md) beneath [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md). Upon reaching a locked iron gate, they defeat their attackers, only to find that Donnie has died from blood loss. Realizing that the heavy guard presence indicates a leak, the surviving trio suspect a traitor in their ranks and scatter across New Delsta to return undetected to the Blacksnakes' [Game Parlor](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta_game_parlor.md).

Inside the parlor, Father ambushes Throné to test her reflexes, praising her sharpness and declaring his intention to name her his successor. When Throné complains that her collar feels tighter than usual and asks him to loosen it, Father refuses, reminding her that the mechanism requires two keys and that Mother holds the second.

That night, Mother convenes the guild to review the botched mission. Based on Scaracci's testimony, Mother blames Throné and repeatedly whips her before the assembled guild. Throné refuses to flinch or show pain. Pirro intervenes by claiming responsibility for the failure and endures the lash himself. Mother abruptly ends the gathering to travel the continent in search of fresh recruits.

The following day, Throné visits a rooftop terrace and feeds a collarless puppy, envying its unburdened freedom. Pirro arrives, offering breakfast and a cigarette, both of which she declines, stating her desire for a long life. When Pirro asks what she wants from life, Throné is unable to answer.

Father soon informs Throné that Scaracci has betrayed the guild to conspire with Lord [Diamante](../throne/diamante.md). Father directs her to eliminate both men at [Diamante's Estate](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/diamantes_estate.md) before rendezvousing with him in [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md). Disguising herself as a brothel worker, Throné infiltrates the manor, only to discover both Scaracci and Diamante already slain by Pirro. Pirro reveals that the rumor of a traitor was an elaborate fabrication designed by leadership to force a deadly duel and weed out a single successor. Desperate to take power and reshape the guild, Pirro attacks Throné, forcing her to kill him in self-defense. In his dying moments, Pirro asks what she desires, and Throné finally confesses her true wish: to be free. Resolving to end the cycle of bloodshed, she embarks on a quest to kill both Father and Mother and claim the keys to unlock her collar.

*(If Throné is not chosen as the primary traveler, she is encountered in New Delsta rescuing a stray dog from abusive thugs before joining the party.)*

### Chapter 2: Mother's Route (The Slaver in Oresrush)

Throné travels to [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md) in pursuit of Mother's recruitment trail, noticing a masked child following her. Inquiring at a local saddlery, she is met with feigned ignorance until she steals a horse coin from a customer to pressure the shopkeeper. Intimidated, the owner confesses that Mother is meeting [the Slaver](../throne/the_slaver.md), who operates a human trafficking trade inside the town's [old foundry](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush_foundry.md).

Approaching the foundry, Throné sneaks up on the stalking child and slips off his mask, startled by his face. Entering the hideout, she learns Mother has already departed. The Slaver refuses to disclose her destination without "compensation," challenging Throné to a game of risk with two goblets, one laced with lethal poison. Throné wins the wager. As the poisoned Slaver succumbs, he reveals that Mother has returned to [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](../../locations/leaflands/wilds/mothers_garden_orphanage.md) in [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md) to groom children into Blacksnakes. He also mentions that the guild's previous leader was a "good woman" murdered by Mother.

Outside the foundry, Throné encounters the masked boy again. Examining him closely, she realizes his features bear an uncanny resemblance to Pirro as a child. The boy admits he trailed her because she felt familiar and important. When asked who his father is, the boy replies that he does not know—a confession Throné echoes regarding her own lineage.

### Chapter 2: Father's Route (The Snowhares in Winterbloom)

Throné arrives in [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md) and ventures into the [Winterbloom: Thieves' Quarter](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom_thieves_quarter.md) to find Father. She is accosted by three lecherous thugs, whom she swiftly knocks unconscious. Father reveals himself, admitting he staged the ambush to evaluate her readiness. When Throné confronts him over Pirro's and Scaracci's deaths, Father reiterates that she can claim leadership after completing one final assignment: eliminating [Bergomi](../throne/bergomi.md), leader of the rival [Snowhares](../../world/factions/snowhares.md) guild, who has encroached on Blacksnake territory. Disrupting Throné's intention to ambush him from behind, Father orders her to take the lead.

En route to the hideout, Throné reminisces about her first assassination: as a child, Father coerced her into stabbing a man he labeled "scum." When Father asked how she felt, she replied that she hated the smell of blood. Entering the [Snowhares' Den](../../locations/winterlands/dungeons/snowhares_den.md), she encounters a terrified young captive woman pleading for help, reminding Throné of herself; she promises to return for her.

Throné confronts and defeats Bergomi. Just as she prepares to strike the fatal blow, a thrown dagger intercepts her blade—Father's dagger. Father admits he anticipated her ambush all along. Undeterred, Throné openly declares her intention to kill him to secure her freedom. Father challenges her to meet him in [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md) when she is prepared for their final reckoning. Returning to the den's entrance, Throné discovers the captive young woman has vanished.

### Crossed Paths: Throné & Temenos (Part 1)

Throné travels with [Temenos Mistral](temenos.md) to [Flamechurch](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch.md). Seeing the cathedral, she recalls her past wager regarding a fabled treasure named "Alpates." Despite Temenos's initial skepticism, she persuades him to investigate. An elderly local carpenter directs them to a hidden mechanism behind a bookshelf in the pontiff's former chambers, revealing a subterranean passage.

The passage leads to a concealed room where a woman named [Alpates](../others/alpates.md) has been sheltered. Alpates belongs to the [Lumina Clan](../../world/factions/lumina_clan.md), whose members have borne the name Alpates for generations. Sensing a sacred flame within Throné and Temenos, Alpates entrusts them with an obscured, cloudy mirror fragment and asks if they hope for the dawn before taking her leave. Outside the cathedral, Throné realizes the rumored "treasure" was a living guardian rather than gold or gems. Admitting defeat in her wager with her late comrades Donnie, Scaracci, and Pirro, she casts their coins from a high cliff.

### Chapter 3: Mother's Route (Mother's Garden)

In [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md), Throné steals a nun's habit to infiltrate the orphanage at [Mother's Garden](../../locations/leaflands/dungeons/mothers_garden.md). In town, she observes a young girl named [Mira](../throne/mira.md) exhibiting exceptional thieving skills. Approaching the orphanage gates, Throné remarks on the beautiful chiming of the facility's bell, a sentiment an [Elderly Guard](../throne/elderly_guard.md) grimly dismisses.

Inside, Throné witnesses Mother affixing a Blacksnake collar onto Mira, inducting her into the guild. When Mira steps away to tend to an injured boy punished by Mother, she reveals that disciplinary beatings occur behind a locked heavy door tended by [Morozov](../throne/morozov.md). Throné picks Morozov's pocket for the key and slips through into the garden. There, she discovers Mother mercilessly whipping Mira, who has been tied to the bell as bait to lure Throné out.

Throné overpowers Mother in combat. Defeated, Mother reveals that Throné's birth mother was the previous head of the Blacksnakes, whom Mother murdered. Mother explains that she spared Throné only because Father threatened to slay Mother if any harm befell the child. As Throné raises her blade, Mira tearfully begs for Mother's life, pleading that Mother is the only family she knows. Steeling her resolve, Throné executes Mother, giving Mira her true name so the girl may seek vengeance in the future if she chooses. Throné then claims Mother's key.

### Chapter 3: Father's Route (The Abandoned Church)

In [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md), Throné finds Father waiting upon a stone bridge in the [Abandoned Traverse](../../locations/crestlands/wilds/abandoned_traverse.md). Father recounts his past, describing a fated meeting with an assassin named Marietta who struck down his target before he could reach it. The two fell deeply in love and resolved to abandon the Blacksnakes together. Father instructs Throné to meet him at the [Abandoned Church](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/abandoned_church.md) where he and Marietta once exchanged vows.

At the altar, Father recalls a riddle Marietta once posed to him regarding something that could never be stolen, through which she revealed her pregnancy. However, Father reveals that Marietta subsequently abandoned him for another man—the shadowy founder of the Blacksnakes—and aborted their unborn child before giving birth to Throné by the founder. Consumed by grief and wrath, Father killed Marietta and raised Throné as his own, deliberately training her to become the instrument of his own death.

Father attacks, and Throné fatally wounds him. Dying, Father witnesses Throné's grief; she admits her fear of losing him and asks if she may call him "Dad." Overjoyed to finally be recognized as a true father, he gives her his key, but warns that his death will not bring her true liberation, urging her to seek out her biological father.

Alone with both keys, Throné attempts to unlock her collar, only to find that neither key fits the mechanism. Bewildered and grief-stricken, she returns to New Delsta to unravel the truth.

### Chapter 4: The Truth in Lostseed

Returning to New Delsta, Throné checks on the fractured remnants of the Blacksnakes. A familiar scent of tobacco draws her to Pirro's grave, where she incapacitates a Blacksnake attempting to murder a comrade in a bid for leadership. Pirro's gravestone bears a carved message: *"I await where it all began."* Contemplating the words, Throné recalls the locked gate in the [Underground Waterway](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/underground_waterway.md) that halted their escape during the mission where Donnie died.

Testing Mother's and Father's keys on the subterranean door, Throné discovers they unlock the passage. Beyond lies a ropeway watched over by a familiar-looking [Guide](../throne/guide_thrones_story.md). The Guide operates a gondola spanning an abyssal ravine, explaining that it leads "home" to [Lostseed](../../locations/brightlands/towns/lostseed.md)—a hidden enclave founded centuries earlier by a prince and princess of warring realms. Upon disembarking, Throné notes the Guide's resemblance to the elderly guard at Mother's Garden; the man replies that he knows no such person, musing that all humanity descends from common blood if traced back far enough.

Entering Lostseed, Throné is horrified by the ruined city and its emaciated, hollow-eyed citizens. An impoverished woman begs Throné to kill her infant child. Disturbed, Throné follows the wailing of a baby into [Lostseed Castle](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/lostseed_castle.md). At the heart of the stronghold, she confronts a man bearing a striking resemblance to Pirro, who introduces himself as [Claude](../throne/claude_blacksnakes.md).

Claude reveals that he has lived an unnaturally prolonged life spanning centuries. Growing weary of existence, he sought to produce a worthy successor before dying. To that end, he sired countless children across Solistia—including Pirro, Father (Sebastian), Mother, the Blacksnake assassins, and Throné herself. He created the Blacksnakes as a crucible to force his offspring to slaughter one another until a single child proved capable of killing him and serving as an empty vessel for [Vide](../../world/gods/vide.md). Throné is the fourth child to reach him.

Horrified by the monstrous truth, Throné engages Claude and slays him, recovering the true key to her collar. As Claude dies, he poses Marietta's ancient riddle: what is something that can never be stolen? Throné answers: "the dawn." Unlocking the collar from her neck at last, Throné reflects grimly that her hard-won freedom still carries the suffocating stench of blood.

### Crossed Paths: Throné & Temenos (Part 2)

In [Conning Creek](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md), Throné and Temenos investigate a commotion and discover that [Alpates](../others/alpates.md) has been murdered. Searching her body, they uncover a handwritten map marked "the Cavern of the Moon and Sun" alongside the notation *"Where day meets night."* Recognizing that Alpates sacrificed her life to protect this secret, the pair set out for the cavern.

Within the [Cavern of the Moon and Sun](../../locations/harborlands/dungeons/cavern_of_the_moon_and_sun.md), Temenos uses his deductive vision to solve an inscription at an altar illuminated simultaneously by sun and moon. There they discover an inscribed verse—*"To those who hope for the dawn"*—and a second mirror fragment that seamlessly joins with the piece Alpates gave them in Flamechurch. Temenos recounts holy scripture concerning Alpates, a divine being left behind by [Aelfric](../../world/gods/aelfric.md) the Flamebringer to walk among mortals, whose physical form was fashioned into a sacred mirror. Recognizing the weight of Alpates' sacrifice, Throné promises to protect the relic.

### The Journey for the Dawn

During the journey to rekindle the Sacred Flames in Flamechurch, Throné and Temenos confront Sister [Mindt](../temenos/arcanette.md). When Mindt unmasks herself as Arcanette, the ageless founder of the Moonshade Order, Throné recalls Claude's unnatural longevity, recognizing that eternal life is no mere myth. When [Oboro](../hikari/oboro.md) questions whether the dawn is worth the agony of the journey, Throné replies that the dawn is undeniably worth it, though she knows words cannot sway him.

Before confronting the dark entity [Vide](../../world/gods/vide.md), Throné steels her resolve, declaring that she will slay even a god to preserve the dawn. Following the battle, having broken the chains of her lineage and guild, Throné sets her sights on a new beginning, seeking an uncharted sanctuary absent from any map where she can finally live in peace.
