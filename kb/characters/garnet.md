# Garnet

> *"This is Garnet, the boss's favorite hound. Garnet here has chased off everyone who interferes with our bottom line."*
> — [Thurston](thurston.md)

## Overview

- **Species**: Canine ([dog](../world/canines.md))
- **Gender**: Female
- **Status**: Alive
- **Affiliation**: [The Roque Company](../locations/the_roque_company_factory.md)
- **Owner**: [Roque](roque_brilliante.md)
- **Caretaker**: [Thurston](thurston.md)

**Garnet** is an aggressive guard hound in *Octopath Traveler II* belonging to industrialist [Roque](roque_brilliante.md). Pampered by her owner and tasked with defending company interests, she is unleashed by Roque's secretary [Thurston](thurston.md) to attack intruders and business rivals during [Partitio Yellowil](partitio.md)'s story in [Clockbank](../locations/clockbank.md).

## History

Garnet serves as Roque's primary guard dog, trained to intimidate and drive off anyone perceived as a threat to company profits. When Partitio infiltrates [The Roque Company: Factory](../locations/the_roque_company_factory.md) to confront Roque and protect [Floyd](floyd.md)'s rights to the Neo Steam Engine, Thurston sics Garnet on him to chase the merchant out. After being subdued in battle, Garnet flees the factory floor whimpering.

Despite her ferocity in defense of the factory, Garnet enjoys a pampered existence. Thurston regularly takes her on evening strolls and feeds her biscuits as part of his daily routine. She can later be seen accompanying Thurston at night on [Roque Island](../locations/roque_island.md), and remains loyally at Roque's side following his relocation to [Oresrush](../locations/oresrush.md).
