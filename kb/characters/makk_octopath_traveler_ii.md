# Makk (Octopath Traveler II)

> *"I never know honest work was so much fun!"*  
> — Makk, in [Winterbloom: Thieves' Quarter](../locations/winterbloom_thieves_quarter.md)

**Makk** is a former bandit and loyal follower of [Plukk](plukk.md) in *Octopath Traveler II*. He appears alongside Plukk and [Mikk](mikk_octopath_traveler_ii.md) during the [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md) chapter of [Castti Florenz](castti.md)'s tale.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 30
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md)
- **Affiliation**: Plukk's gang

### Personality
Makk is an earnest, simplehearted man whose rough exterior masks genuine gratitude and loyalty. Having been spared a life of enslavement by Plukk, his chief motivation is to support and please her. When later offered legitimate employment, he discovers an unexpected joy in honest manual labor.

## Biography

In his youth, Makk was captured and destined to be sold abroad into slavery. Before the transaction could occur, Plukk intervened and purchased him outright for the full asking price. Instead of keeping him bound in servitude, Plukk told him he was free to live however and wherever he wished. Deeply moved by her generosity, Makk chose to remain at her side and dedicate himself to her service alongside Mikk.

In Winterbloom, Makk initially engages in petty thievery and extortion under Plukk's leadership in the [Winterbloom: Thieves' Quarter](../locations/winterbloom_thieves_quarter.md). Following their encounter with the apothecary [Castti Florenz](castti.md), the gang reforms and turns away from crime. Makk finds honest employment tending plants in Lady [Melia](melia.md)'s manor herb garden, taking immense pride in his new role as a gardener.

During the realm-wide festivities in [New Delsta](../locations/new_delsta.md), Makk marvels at being able to walk freely through the bustling metropolis, hinting at past legal troubles in the city before finding an honest path in life.
