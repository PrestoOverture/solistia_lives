# Nikki

"*I've gotta find a way to make this last measly crust of bread keep me going...*" —Nikki, during the famine in Oresrush

**Nikki** is a resident of [Oresrush](../locations/oresrush.md) and a close friend of [Partitio Yellowil](partitio.md), belonging to his loyal posse alongside [Ned](ned_octopath_traveler_ii.md), [Joe](joe.md), and [Harry](harry_octopath_traveler_ii.md).

## Profile

### Appearance
Nikki is a 23-year-old young man with short red hair. He wears a blue collared shirt layered beneath a red vest.

### Personality
Nikki is frugal, determined, and impressionable. He holds great admiration for Partitio's open-handed generosity, though he occasionally teases Partitio for being "too quick by half" to part with his money. Deeply attached to Partitio's favor, Nikki accidentally lost the very first coin Partitio ever gave him; unable to bear the embarrassment of admitting the loss, he carries a substitute coin that he pretends is the original keepsake.

## Story

As a longstanding companion in Partitio's circle, Nikki helped defend Oresrush against [Giff](giff.md) and the ruffians preying on the town's mining profits. When silver prices plummeted and Oresrush was strangled by Giff's exploitative control, Nikki and the posse endured severe poverty, struggling to scrape together enough food to survive.

Desperation reached a breaking point when Harry stole Nikki's solitary crust of bread. Partitio quickly quelled the dispute, ordering Harry to surrender the bread back to Nikki while breaking his own remaining loaf to feed the group. When Giff subsequently arrived to mock their poverty, Partitio rallied Nikki and the rest of the gang to take a stand.

Confronting Giff at his manor, the group brought down the landowner and uncovered proof that Giff had forged contracts to swindle the miners out of their rightful earnings. With prosperity returning to Oresrush, Nikki joined his companions in celebrating, sharing jokes with Joe and encouraging him as he pursued the woman of his dreams.
