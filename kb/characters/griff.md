# Griff

**Commander Griff** is the commander of [Timberain](../locations/timberain.md)'s foreign expedition corps who plays an important supporting role in [Castti Florenz](castti.md)'s storyline. Initially leading military campaigns against [Sai](../locations/sai.md), he undergoes a change of heart and becomes an advocate for diplomacy and civilian protection.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 45
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Sandflow Pass](../locations/sandflow_pass.md) (day), [Sai](../locations/sai.md) (night)
- **Occupation**: Military Commander of Timberain

### Appearance
Griff wears a distinctive red-plumed helmet with brown and gray plate armor, carrying a sword at his waist.

### Personality
Despite commanding Timberain's frontline expeditionary forces, Griff harbors a strong distaste for senseless conflict. While he once viewed foreigners with deep suspicion—characterizing outsiders as locusts devouring his homeland's sustenance—his views change after witnessing the selfless compassion of Castti and the honor of enemy commander [Edmund](edmund.md). He is a proactive and inspirational leader who petitions his king for peace, respects former adversaries, and commands enough loyalty that his men willingly follow his orders during extreme emergencies.

## Story

During Castti's journey through [Sandflow Pass](../locations/sandflow_pass.md), Griff leads his expeditionary troops in a fierce skirmish against the warriors of [Sai](../locations/sai.md). The violence disturbs a territorial [Sand Lion](../world/sand_lion.md), which rampages through the canyon and severely injures soldiers on both sides. Castti intervenes, treating the wounded and defeating the beast alongside Sai's commander, Edmund. Deeply moved by the cooperation that saved his men, Griff makes peace with Edmund on the spot. Before departing, Griff shares a disturbing rumor he caught wind of regarding a strange, noxious purple rain associated with [Eir's Apothecaries](../world/eirs_apothecaries.md).

Griff returns to Timberain to manage guard details for the upcoming coronation festivities. When Castti arrives with urgent news that Trousseau plans to release deadly toxic rain upon the celebratory crowds, Griff immediately trusts her word. Marshalling his troops, Griff collaborates with Edmund's contingent to coordinate an emergency evacuation of the city, successfully ushering the populace to safety before the toxic downpour begins.

Following the crisis, Griff remains stationed between Sandflow Pass and Sai, working tirelessly to solidify the fragile ceasefire and prevent further conflict between the two nations.
