# Coda

**Coda** is a 19-year-old performer and flutist in [Giselle](giselle.md)'s Traveling Troupe who appears during [Agnea Bristarni](agnea.md)'s journey. Clumsy yet charming, she brings warmth and entertainment to the stage, often performing in tandem with fellow performer [Rico](rico.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 19
- **Gender**: Female
- **Occupation**: Performer, Flutist
- **Affiliation**: [Giselle](giselle.md)'s Traveling Troupe
- **Status**: Alive

### Appearance
Coda has purple hair and wears a performer's outfit styled similarly to [Rico](rico.md)'s.

### Personality
Coda is eager to bring joy through her musical acts. Abandoned as a child, she lost her smile early in life, but learned how to laugh again after meeting Rico. On stage, her clumsy yet charming nature endears her to audiences. She frequently speaks and acts in unison with Rico, playing her flute while he performs his comedy routine.

## Story

### The Traveling Troupe
Upon arriving at [Tropu'hopu](../locations/tropuhopu.md), Giselle's troupe cart becomes stuck in the sand. After [Tanzy](tanzy.md) fails to excavate the cart, Agnea assists the troupe in freeing it, and the members exchange warm introductions.

The troupe prepares to stage a nighttime performance at the [Tropu'hopu: Floating Theater](../locations/tropuhopu_floating_theater.md), but Giselle suffers a crisis of confidence and flees to a nearby island, sending the troupe into a panic. Agnea follows Giselle and restores her faith with words of hope. Heartened by Agnea's encouragement, Giselle returns to perform alongside Rico and Coda. The troupe invites Agnea to dance with them, and afterward they celebrate with drinks under the moonlight before parting ways with plans to meet again at the [Grand Gala](../world/grand_gala.md).

### The Grand Gala
In [Merry Hills](../locations/merry_hills.md), the troupe reunites with Agnea. When kidnappers sent by [La'mani](lamani.md) corner her, Rico, Coda, and Giselle release their stage doves to create a diversion, allowing Agnea to escape toward the stage. During the climactic dance contest between Agnea and [Dolcinaea Luciel](dolcinaea_luciel.md), Coda and the troupe cheer loudly for Agnea from the audience.

Following the conclusion of Agnea's journey, Coda can be found during the day at [New Delsta Harbor: Anchorage](../locations/new_delsta_harbor_anchorage.md) alongside Giselle and Rico.
