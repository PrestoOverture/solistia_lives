# Father

**Father**, real name **Sebastian** (often called **Seb**), is a master assassin and co-leader of the [Blacksnakes](../world/blacksnakes.md) thieves' guild in [New Delsta](../locations/new_delsta.md). Alongside [Mother](mother.md), he serves as a secondary antagonist in [Throné Anguis](throne.md)'s story. As the guild commander overseeing assassination contracts, Father trained Throné from childhood in the lethal arts, raising her as his daughter while harboring a tragic, bloody secret.

## Profile

### Appearance
Father is an older man with long white hair, a goatee, and an eyepatch covering his right eye. He wears a dark trench coat lined with numerous hidden throwing knives and daggers, including a cherished blade named after [Marietta](marietta.md).

### Personality
Calm, soft-spoken, and calculating, Father maintains an impenetrable composure that conceals his true motives. He possesses an exceptionally sharp intellect, able to manipulate targets with ease and discern Throné's intentions before she ever speaks them. In contrast to his composed exterior, in battle he becomes feral and ruthless, reveling in the visceral exchange of pain and bloodshed. He cares little for the Blacksnakes as an organization, viewing it primarily as a haven to indulge his violent nature.

Despite his brutality, Father adheres to a personal fatalism. He believes the [gods](../world/gods.md) endow each soul with an inescapable gift, asserting that Throné—like himself—was born with a destiny to wield a dagger.

His relationship with Throné is complex and twisted. Unlike Mother, who viewed Throné purely as a nuisance, Father harbored genuine paternal affection for her. Knowing that she was fathered by [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md) rather than himself, Father still raised her as his own most precious charge. Driven by guilt over murdering her mother, he raised Throné into an elite killer with the deliberate intention that she would one day take vengeance and end his life, granting them both closure.

## History

### Early Life and Heartbreak
Born in a small provincial village, Sebastian learned to kill at an early age. He arrived in [New Delsta](../locations/new_delsta.md) at seventeen as a rank-and-file operative in the Blacksnakes. During an assignment to assassinate a local noble, he met and fell in love with a fellow operative named [Marietta](marietta.md). Desiring to leave the criminal underworld, Sebastian took Marietta to an abandoned church outside [Montwise](../locations/montwise.md), declaring his love and learning she was pregnant with a child he believed was his own.

Before they could depart, the operative known as [Mother](mother.md) intervened. Mother revealed that Marietta had aborted Sebastian's unborn child and given herself to the Blacksnakes' hidden founder—Sebastian's own biological father, [Claude](claude_blacksnakes.md). Devastated by the betrayal, Sebastian succumbed to murderous rage and slit Marietta's throat. When Marietta's daughter Throné was born, Sebastian took her in, threatening Mother to never harm the child or divulge the truth of her parentage. Ascending to co-command the syndicate under the alias "Father," he resolved to raise Throné as his successor and eventual executioner.

### The Successor's Test
Years later, Father receives "the Mark" retrieved by Throné from a heist against Lord Diamante. He warns her of a suspected informant within their ranks and announces he has nominated her as his successor. In coordination with Mother, he arranges a deadly trial pitting Throné against her comrades [Pirro](pirro.md) and [Scaracci](scaracci.md), instructing the sole survivor to meet him in [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md).

### Final Encounter in Montwise
Aware that Throné seeks to kill him to win her freedom, Father uses the guild's contract against [Bergomi](bergomi.md), leader of the [Snowhares](../world/snowhares.md), to test her. After Throné incapacitates Bergomi, Father finishes off the gang leader and stabs Throné in the back to demonstrate the ruthlessness required of an assassin. He commands her to pursue him to [Montwise](../locations/montwise.md).

At the abandoned church where he had once proposed to Marietta, Father recounts his history to Throné, revealing how he killed her mother. Lamenting that Throné should have been his own flesh and blood, he draws his blades and forces her into a fight to the death. When Throné strikes the fatal blow, Father welcomes his end in peace. With his dying breath, he presents her with his key to her collar and reveals that the second key is held by her true father.
