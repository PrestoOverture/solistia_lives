# Melia

**Lady Melia Glenville** is the young ruler of [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md) who appears during [Castti Florenz](castti.md)'s journey.

## Overview

- **Age**: 12
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md)
- **Relatives**: [Rosa](rosa.md) Glenville (mother, deceased), [Greg](greg.md) (cousin)

> "*I promise you I'll grow up to be a kind and just ruler... just like you. So don't worry about me, Mother.*"
> — Melia to Rosa Glenville

Taking up the mantle of leadership at House Glenville at just twelve years old, Melia governs Winterbloom with the counsel of her cousin Greg, her retainer [Lily](lily_octopath_traveler_ii.md), and the support of the townspeople. She spends her quiet evenings in her botanical garden, speaking to her late mother about the events and joys of her daily life. The epilogue reveals that she is also friends with [Will](will.md).

### Appearance

Melia has short brown hair and wears a simple, bright orange dress.

### Personality

Despite her tender years, Melia possesses poise, solemnity, and maturity well beyond her age. Having cared for her ailing mother for much of her childhood, her greatest aspiration is to match Rosa's reputation as a benevolent, equitable ruler. When Castti breaks the grim truth about Rosa's terminal affliction, Melia thanks the apothecary for her forthrightness rather than desiring comforting falsehoods. She is exceptionally forgiving and pragmatic, welcoming Greg as her administrative co-ruler even after he betrayed and abducted her.

## History

### Early Life and Eir's Apothecaries

Melia grew up in Winterbloom as the daughter of Lady [Rosa](rosa.md) Glenville. When Rosa fell ill with a debilitating, degenerative disease that slowly paralyzed her limbs, numerous physicians and herbalists attempted to heal her without success. Among them was the compassionate apothecary [Trousseau](trousseau.md), with whom Melia developed a close bond.

Eventually, [Castti Florenz](castti.md) and [Malaya](malaya.md) of [Eir's Apothecaries](../world/eirs_apothecaries.md) arrived in town and formulated a medicinal regimen capable of arresting the illness's progression. Before departing, Castti provided herbal seeds and helped Melia cultivate a dedicated greenhouse garden at the Glenville manor to guarantee an enduring supply of medicine for Rosa.

### The Abduction and Succession

Years later, Rosa became entirely bedridden. While tending to the garden, Melia encountered Castti once again, though the apothecary was now suffering from severe amnesia. The two gathered fresh ingredients to prepare an elixir for Rosa. Upon returning inside, they found Melia's estranged cousin [Greg](greg.md) attempting to extort Rosa over the inheritance. Melia ordered him off the premises and agreed to escort him out, but Greg ambushed her, rendering her unconscious.

Greg took Melia to the [Winterbloom: Thieves' Quarter](../locations/winterbloom_thieves_quarter.md) and sent a ransom note demanding Castti arrive alone. Castti fought and subdued the three thieves Greg had hired. After Castti sternly reprimanded Greg for his cowardice, Melia revived and returned home. 

Following a heart-to-heart conversation with Lily and offering full forgiveness to Greg, Melia celebrated her twelfth birthday. On her deathbed, Rosa formally bequeathed the Glenville estate, title, and lands jointly to Melia and Greg, gifting Melia a final memento before passing away peacefully. In the garden afterward, Melia inquired about Trousseau's whereabouts, which helped jog Castti's fragmented memories before Castti left the city.

### House Glenville and the Snowhares

Melia shouldered the heavy responsibilities of governing Winterbloom, working tirelessly while Greg struggled with self-doubt. When Lily brought word that the town had suffered a wave of thefts orchestrated by a faction of the [Snowhares](../world/snowhares.md) led by [Plukk](plukk.md), Melia investigated their motives. 

Recognizing that the bandits were driven by desperation, Melia invited the gang directly into the manor garden and revealed she had uncovered their plans. Rather than turning them over to the authorities, she offered them legitimate employment as estate gardeners. As Melia nearly collapsed from sheer exhaustion, Plukk caught her, praised her wisdom and character, returned the stolen goods, and formally accepted the position.
