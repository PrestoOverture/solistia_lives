# Alrond Rondwell

"*Welcome to my manor, [Partitio](partitio.md). I am none other than Alrond himself. Don't think ill of me, Partitio. I had my reasons to disguise myself as the everyman. As one of the wealthiest people on the continent, I find that most put on a false mask when speaking to me. It's much easier to judge someone's true character by how they treat you when they think you're nothing special.*" —Alrond, revealing his true identity

**Lord Alrond Rondwell** is the presiding lord of [Wellgrove](../locations/wellgrove.md) and the heir to the Rondwell family fortune. He is regarded as one of the wealthiest nobles in all of Solistia and the richest individual on the western continent.

## Profile

### Appearance
Alrond is a 35-year-old nobleman with silver hair styled in a sweeping combover drooping over one side of his face. In formal settings, he wears an opulent red coat draped in fine jewelry. When traversing Wellgrove incognito, he dresses in an unpretentious merchant's outfit and a plain cap.

### Personality
Alrond is a spirited, discerning aristocrat with a keen eye for human nature. Aware that most people fawn over his immense fortune and wear false pretenses, he regularly wanders Wellgrove in humble disguises to observe how people act when they believe he is an ordinary commoner. He enjoys "treasure hunting" in the streets, frequently collecting unusual knick-knacks and discarded curiosities.

Though self-indulgent in the arts and hopeless at practical chores such as laundering his own clothes, Alrond possesses a generous spirit and a genuine desire to see his community thrive. He also harbors a taste for dramatic flair, as displayed during his theatrical entrance on [Roque Island](../locations/roque_island.md).

## History

### Background
During Alrond's youth, Wellgrove was among the most prosperous commercial centers in Solistia. However, when the market crashed, Alrond's father attempted to resolve the downturn by distributing vast sums of money directly to the citizens. The handout proved disastrous: with ample money in hand, many residents moved away or abandoned work entirely, reducing the town's commerce to a handful of demoralized and opportunistic vendors.

Following the death of his parents, Alrond fell into profound despondency. He was brought back to his senses when his faithful butler struck him with a forceful slap, inspiring Alrond to adopt an energetic, high-spirited demeanor so as not to worry his household. Taking over the family estate, he inherited the manor and its staggering fortune.

One night, while wandering the town in disguise, Alrond met [Misha](misha.md), an earnest youth collecting street rubbish to support his family. Deeply impressed by Misha's diligence and character, Alrond disclosed his true identity and appointed him as his personal butler.

### Meeting Partitio
While strolling through Wellgrove in disguise, Alrond was targeted by an unscrupulous merchant attempting to sell him a fraudulent silver statue of [Oresrush](../locations/oresrush.md). The traveling merchant [Partitio Yellowil](partitio.md) stepped in, exposing the forgery using genuine silver and running the scammer off. Grateful, Alrond invited Partitio to the tavern for drinks. Upon learning that Partitio had come seeking an audience with Lord Alrond to pitch an ambitious commercial enterprise, Alrond agreed to arrange an introduction and returned to his manor.

At [Wellgrove: Alrond's Estate](../locations/wellgrove_alronds_estate.md), Alrond revealed that he was the lord Partitio sought. Partitio outlined his plan to purchase the steam engine patent from [Roque](roque_brilliante.md) for eighty billion leaves. Alrond agreed to provide the capital, but set a condition to verify Partitio's capability: reviving Wellgrove's dead economy and returning the town to its former glory.

### The Wellgrove Department Store
Alrond observed Partitio's renovation of an abandoned building and was captivated by the concept of a multi-merchant "[department store](../locations/wellgrove_department_store.md)". While Partitio was away gathering goods, [Thurston](thurston.md), a disgruntled former factory manager dismissed by Roque, ambushed Alrond and took him hostage to derail Partitio's efforts. Partitio defeated Thurston, disabled his steam weapon, and freed Alrond.

With the successful grand opening of the Wellgrove Department Store, commerce flooded back into the city. Honoring his agreement, Alrond handed Partitio a promissory note for eighty billion leaves while cautioning him to stay wary of Roque's deceit.

### The Roque Island Deal
Recognizing that Roque would never surrender the steam engine without a fight, Alrond transported the eighty billion leaves in physical currency by ship to [Roque Island](../locations/roque_island.md). He arrived just as Roque attempted to void the agreement under the pretense that Partitio lacked immediate liquid funds. Publicly shamed by Alrond's timely delivery, Roque breached his contract before the gathered press and attacked using the [Steam Tank Obsidian](../world/steam_tank_obsidian.md), but was decisively stopped.

Following the venture's success, Alrond returned to his customary life in Wellgrove, continuing his habit of sneaking out in disguise with Misha to help clean up the town's streets.
