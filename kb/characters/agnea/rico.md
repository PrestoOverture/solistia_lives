# Rico

> "*The show's about to begin!*"
> — Rico, in New Delsta Harbor

## Overview

**Rico** is a twenty-year-old entertainer and key performer in [Giselle](giselle.md)'s Traveling Troupe. She and her fellow troupe members form a close bond with [Agnea Bristarni](agnea.md) during her journey across Solistia.

## Profile

### Appearance
Rico has brown hair and wears a vibrant traveling performer's costume matching the theatrical style worn by her stage partner [Coda](coda.md).

### Personality
Rico is renowned as the life of the party in the troupe—a bottomless well of good cheer whose quick wit allows her to smooth over onstage accidents with effortless charm. Her specialty acts include comedic acting, storytelling, and sleight-of-hand magic. 

Though outwardly buoyant, Rico endured severe parental abuse during her childhood. She only rediscovered her ability to laugh and trust others after meeting Coda, forging a lifelong friendship so close that the two often speak and act in synchronized harmony.

## History

### Tropu'hopu
While traveling to [Tropu'hopu](../../locations/totohaha/towns/tropuhopu.md) on the island of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md), the troupe's wagon becomes hopelessly mired in the beach sand. After fellow performer [Tanzy](tanzy.md) fails to dig it free, Agnea arrives and assists them in hauling the cart out, sparking an immediate friendship.

The troupe readies an evening performance at the [Tropu'hopu: Floating Theater](../../locations/totohaha/towns/tropuhopu_floating_theater.md), but troupe leader Giselle suffers a sudden crisis of confidence and flees to an offshore islet. With the rest of the cast in a panic, Agnea tracks Giselle down and rekindles her passion for the stage. Giselle returns to perform alongside Rico and Coda, inviting Agnea onto the stage to dance with them. Following a triumphant performance, the group shares drinks beneath the moon before parting ways.

### The Grand Gala in Merry Hills
In [Merry Hills](../../locations/crestlands/towns/merry_hills.md), Rico and the troupe reunite with Agnea. When thugs harass the young dancer on her way to the stage, Rico, Coda, and Giselle unleash their trained performance doves to create a flurry of distraction, allowing Agnea to slip past unharmed. During the climactic dance contest against superstar [Dolcinaea Luciel](dolcinaea_luciel.md), Rico enthusiastically cheers Agnea on from the audience.

After the conclusion of Agnea's travels, Rico and the troupe can be found performing for delighted crowds at the [New Delsta Harbor: Anchorage](../../locations/brightlands/wilds/new_delsta_harbor_anchorage.md).
