# Dolcinaea Luciel

**Dolcinaea Luciel** is a world-renowned dancer and opera singer in Solistia who serves as [Agnea Bristarni](../travelers/agnea.md)'s rival and the primary antagonist of her tale.

## Overview

- **Age**: 28
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [New Delsta: Theater](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md)

> "*I am Dolcinaea Luciel, continental superstar! The realm rejoices when I dance. There is none who can compare to my splendor.*"
> — Dolcinaea Luciel

A superstar celebrated across the realm, Dolcinaea was catapulted to fame by her hit debut song, "All For You." Despite amassing immense wealth, she channels much of her fortune into beauty regimens, lavish costumes, and donations to various charitable causes.

### Appearance

Dolcinaea has blue eyes and voluminous blonde hair styled into an elaborate bun. She is typically seen wearing an opulent blue gown.

### Personality

Dolcinaea radiates an aura of poise, elegance, and beauty, though her immense fame often gives way to arrogance and conceited behavior. Her haughty demeanor conceals deep insecurity stemming from her impoverished childhood as an orphaned runaway in [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md), a past she desperately sought to leave behind. Despite her cultured exterior, she can be surprisingly eccentric and unworldly in practical matters—such as mistaking a heavy workout dumbbell given by her bodyguard [Veronica](veronica.md) for an exotic musical instrument.

Unlike many of the realm's antagonists, Dolcinaea's ambitions have no ties to the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md). According to [Ochette](../travelers/ochette.md), beneath her proud facade, Dolcinaea "smells lonely." After her defeat by Agnea, she lets go of her vanity and re-embraces the pure joy of dance first instilled in her by [Cuani](cuani_bristarni.md).

## History

### Early Life in Sai

Dolcinaea grew up as an impoverished orphan in the slums of [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md), where she was eventually taken in by [Platt](platt.md) and his wife. Twenty years before the events of the story, the celebrated traveling dancer [Cuani](cuani_bristarni.md) visited the settlement. Mesmerized by Cuani's performance, the young Dolcinaea began practicing dance in secret. Upon discovering her efforts, Cuani took her under her wing and taught her how to dance. Dolcinaea eventually departed Sai to seek fame, dedicating herself entirely to song and stagecraft until she became an international sensation. Her thoughts and struggles during her rise were recorded in her [Dancer's Journal](../../stories/documents/dancers_journal_script.md), later left behind in an abandoned warehouse in [Sai: East District](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai_east_district.md).

### Clash of Dancers

Dolcinaea arrived in [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md) to headline a performance at the local theater, dazzling the crowd with "All for You." Among the audience was Agnea, an aspiring dancer from Cropdale. Later, after Agnea defeated the corrupt theater manager [La'mani](lamani.md) in an altercation, Dolcinaea intervened alongside her bodyguard [Veronica](veronica.md), sternly warning La'mani to cease harassing a local tavern owner.

Seeking to erase the painful memories of her childhood, Dolcinaea returned to [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md) with plans to bulldoze the eastern slums and construct an opulent entertainment park dubbed "Dolcinaealand." When the local residents protested, Dolcinaea stationed herself atop [Dragonridge](../../locations/hinoeuma/dungeons/dragonridge.md) with Veronica to oversee the project. Agnea rallied the townspeople and confronted Dolcinaea on the ridge. Dolcinaea dispatched Veronica to drive Agnea off, but Veronica was bested in combat. Respecting Agnea's conviction, Dolcinaea challenged her to settle their artistic clash at the upcoming [Grand Gala](../../world/lore/grand_gala.md) in [Merry Hills](../../locations/crestlands/towns/merry_hills.md). Before leaving town, however, Dolcinaea had Cuani's commemorative statue destroyed.

At the Stage of the Moon and Sun in Merry Hills, Dolcinaea opened the gala performance. When Agnea arrived on stage—having bypassed La'mani's underhanded schemes to stop her—the two engaged in a dramatic dance showdown. While the audience originally favored the superstar, Agnea's heartfelt performance gradually united the crowd and earned their applause. Bested on the stage, Dolcinaea accepted defeat with grace, finding peace and rediscovering her original love of dancing before departing with Veronica.

### Redemption and Return

Following the Grand Gala, Dolcinaea traveled back to the [Sai: East District](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai_east_district.md). Rather than bulldozing the town, she sang for the local children and contributed funds to rebuild the community.

Dolcinaea and Veronica subsequently returned to [New Delsta: Theater](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md) to continue their theatrical careers. On the anniversary of their first meeting, Veronica presented Dolcinaea with a dumbbell as a token of gratitude. Delighted by the heavy iron gift, Dolcinaea eagerly made plans to incorporate the object into her next musical performance.
