# Garud Bristarni

> "*A tailor sews in silence. The garments speak for themselves.*"
> — Garud Bristarni

## Overview

**Garud Bristarni** is a forty-eight-year-old master tailor residing in [Cropdale](../../locations/leaflands/towns/cropdale.md). He is the widower of the renowned dancer [Cuani Bristarni](cuani_bristarni.md) and the father of [Agnea](../travelers/agnea.md) and [Pala Bristarni](pala_bristarni.md). His tailoring business is known locally as Garud Garments.

## Profile

### Appearance
Garud wears a brown coat and hat accented with a red headband, paired with a red-and-black shirt, gray trousers, red stockings, and dark gray boots.

### Personality
Garud is a gruff, taciturn man of very few words, often communicating in short grunts and blunt phrases that can make him appear cold or uncaring. In truth, Garud pours his emotions directly into his craft; his profound love for his wife and daughters is expressed through the meticulous care and artistry he devotes to their clothing. Though initially resistant to the idea of Agnea leaving home, his stoic facade crumbles when he recognizes how much she resembles her late mother, parting with tears of love and pride rather than grand speeches.

## History

### Early Life and Marriage
Since his youth, Garud's meticulous craftsmanship was celebrated by connoisseurs of fine garments. His renown spread across the eastern continent, eventually prompting the famous travelling dancer [Cuani](cuani_bristarni.md) to seek him out in Cropdale twenty years ago to order a custom-made dress. According to Agnea, Cuani fell in love at first wear and proposed to Garud immediately after trying on the outfit. The two married and had two daughters, [Agnea](../travelers/agnea.md) and [Pala](pala_bristarni.md).

As Cuani toured Solistia, Garud accompanied her alongside their children, tailoring all of her ceremonial garments as they traveled. When Cuani fell fatally ill, the family returned to their home in Cropdale, where Garud remained by her side through her final moments. Cuani's death devastated him, causing him to withdraw inward and adopt a gruff, guarded demeanor.

### Agnea's Departure
When Agnea began saving leaves to embark on her own dancing journey to become a star, Garud treated her ambition with brusque indifference, reluctant to see another family member walk the difficult road that took his wife. However, during Cropdale's annual festival, Agnea defended the village by subduing a rampaging [duorduor](duorduor.md), tearing her clothes in the process. Moved by her bravery and determination, Garud presented her with Cuani's original dancing dress, which he had secretly retailored to fit Agnea perfectly. The next morning, Garud stood with the rest of the village at the edge of town, weeping silent tears as he watched Agnea set out on her adventure.
