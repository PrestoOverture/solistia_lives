# La'mani

> *"Welcome to the Delsta Theater, where our guests want for nothing!"*  
> — La'mani, at the [New Delsta: Theater](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md)

**La'mani** is the flamboyant and egotistical manager of the [Delsta Theater](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md) in *Octopath Traveler II*. He serves as an early antagonist and recurring foil in [Agnea Bristarni](../travelers/agnea.md)'s storyline.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 44
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Occupation**: Theater Manager
- **Location**: [New Delsta: Theater](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md)

### Appearance
La'mani is a portly gentleman sporting stylized blond hair and a meticulously groomed mustache that he habitually twirls. He dresses in an extravagant black formal suit paired with a matching top hat.

### Personality
La'mani views the stage arts as sacred, harboring immense reverence for world-class opera, high drama, and elite performers, while openly disdaining common folk art and tavern entertainers. He is insecure, vain, and petty, fiercely believing the Delsta Theater to be the sole artistic beacon of [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md). When rival establishments gain attention, he readily resorts to vandalism and underhanded intimidation. Despite his bluster, La'mani possesses virtually no combat ability and proves cowardly when physically challenged. Nevertheless, his passion for performance is genuine enough that extraordinary artistry can penetrate his arrogance, eventually compelling him to admire performers he once scorned.

## Biography

### Clash in New Delsta
During Chapter 2 of Agnea's journey, La'mani is first seen berating [Gil](gil.md), the proprietor of a local tavern, for attempting to view a performance without a ticket. When Gil's tavern later surges in popularity thanks to Agnea's captivating dancing, jealousy drives La'mani to ransack Gil's tavern. 

Agnea confronts La'mani directly inside the Delsta Theater and defeats him in combat. Superstar dancer [Dolcinaea Luciel](dolcinaea_luciel.md) witnesses the aftermath and threatens to cancel all future engagements at his venue unless La'mani swears never to harass Gil's establishment again, leaving the manager humiliated before his patrons.

### Merry Hills
Nursing wounded pride, La'mani travels to [Merry Hills](../../locations/crestlands/towns/merry_hills.md) during Chapter 4, hiring mercenaries to kidnap Agnea before her upcoming performance. When he confronts Agnea backstage, she completely fails to remember him, infuriating him further. He laments his string of misfortunes—including being chased through the streets by stray dogs following his prior humiliation—and orders his thugs to attack. Before violence breaks out, Dolcinaea's bodyguard [Veronica](veronica.md) intervenes, subduing La'mani instantly with a single blow.

Despite his scheming, La'mani watches Agnea's performance from the audience. The brilliance of her dancing profoundly moves him, bringing him to tears. Conceding defeat, he approaches Agnea after the show to confess that, entirely against his pride, he has become one of her most devoted fans.

## Lore & Details

- La'mani has a six-year-old son who waits outside the Delsta Theater gates. The polite young boy admires his father deeply, having learned an encyclopedic knowledge of theater productions and acting directly from him.
- Persistent public criticisms of La'mani's conduct and management prompted one of the theater's wealthy financiers to visit the establishment in disguise as an ordinary theatergoer to inspect operations.
