# Gus

**Gus** is the tavernkeeper of [Cropdale](../../locations/leaflands/towns/cropdale.md) and a childhood friend of [Agnea Bristarni](agnea.md), who plays a supportive role in the opening and conclusion of her journey.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: Unknown
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Cropdale](../../locations/leaflands/towns/cropdale.md)
- **Occupation**: Tavernkeeper

### Appearance
Gus has long brown hair tied back in a ponytail and wields a woodcutting axe when forced into combat.

### Personality
Gus is a kindhearted, generous, and dependable friend. Though he is naturally averse to physical danger and prefers the quiet comfort of his tavern, he shows true courage when the people of Cropdale need him. He is fiercely supportive of Agnea, giving up a substantial sum of his own savings to ensure she can pursue her aspirations.

## Story

Having grown up alongside Agnea in Cropdale, Gus runs the local tavern where Agnea frequently performs her dances for the villagers. Aware of her burning ambition to become a star like her late mother [Cuani Bristarni](cuani_bristarni.md), Gus counts out the tips from a dance performance and gifts Agnea the remaining 1,000 leaves she needs to reach her savings target of 10,000 leaves, making her journey possible.

The following morning, during preparations for Cropdale's annual festival, Gus rushes to Agnea after discovering that her younger sister [Pala Bristarni](pala_bristarni.md) has gone missing while picking raspberries. Having spotted fresh beast tracks, Gus joins Agnea in venturing into the [Veil of Trees](../../locations/leaflands/dungeons/veil_of_trees.md). Deep in the forest, the pair encounter and defeat an aggressive [duorduor](duorduor.md), successfully rescuing Pala and returning her safely to town for the festival.

The next day, Gus gathers with Agnea's family at the village entrance to see her off on her travels. He later makes the long journey to [Merry Hills](../../locations/crestlands/towns/merry_hills.md) alongside Agnea's loved ones to support her at the Grand Gala, cheering her on from the crowd during her performance against [Dolcinaea Luciel](dolcinaea_luciel.md) at the [Stage of the Moon and Sun](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/stage_of_the_moon_and_sun.md).
