# Cuani Bristarni

> "*I must be on my way. But goodbye I shall not say. For you and I will meet again someday.*"
> — Cuani's song

## Overview

**Cuani Bristarni** was a renowned star dancer across Solistia, the wife of [Garud Bristarni](garud_bristarni.md), and the mother of [Agnea Bristarni](agnea.md) and [Pala Bristarni](pala_bristarni.md). Known for bringing joy and smiles to audiences wherever she traveled, she passed away from illness prior to the events of the story, but her artistic legacy deeply influenced both of her daughters and many others throughout the realm.

## Profile

### Appearance
Cuani bore a strong resemblance to her eldest daughter Agnea, right down to wearing the same style of dress and flower hairpin.

### Personality
Cuani's core creed as a performer was to bring smiles to the world. Generous and uplifting, she served as a lasting inspiration to everyone she met, leaving an enduring impression long after her passing.

## History

Not much is known about Cuani's early life, though at some point she took up singing and dancing, traveling the world to perform for many audiences. During her career, she wrote many of her own songs and mastered the difficult dance technique known as the moonstep.

Twenty years before the events of the story, Cuani visited [Sai: East District](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai_east_district.md), where she first met a young [Dolcinaea](dolcinaea_luciel.md). Seeing her potential, Cuani took Dolcinaea under her wing and began training her to dance, and Dolcinaea continued as a dancer after Cuani left to continue her tour. The townspeople, seeing Cuani as a symbol of hope, made a statue of her and placed it in the town square.

While touring, Cuani met [Garud](garud_bristarni.md), a skilled tailor who crafted the dresses she wore on stage. According to Agnea, Cuani proposed to Garud after trying on one of his dresses, describing it as "love at first wear." The two married and raised two daughters, [Agnea](agnea.md) and [Pala](pala_bristarni.md). Just as she had with Dolcinaea, Cuani inspired Agnea to pursue dancing and gifted her the cherished flower pin that she always wore.

As time went on, Cuani continued her performance tours across the whole of Solistia with her family accompanying her. However, the physical toll of her relentless performances and her drive to make others happy wore down her health, and she became severely ill. She eventually passed away, leaving her beloved family behind.
