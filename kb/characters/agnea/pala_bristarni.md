# Pala Bristarni

> "*Welcome home, [Agnie](../travelers/agnea.md). You were workin' late tonight again.*"
> — Pala, to Agnea

## Overview

**Pala Bristarni** is a fourteen-year-old resident of [Cropdale](../../locations/leaflands/towns/cropdale.md) in the [Leaflands](../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md). She is the younger daughter of [Garud Bristarni](garud_bristarni.md) and the late [Cuani Bristarni](cuani_bristarni.md), and the younger sister of [Agnea Bristarni](../travelers/agnea.md). Precocious and spirited, she affectionately calls her elder sister "Agnie" and supports her dream of becoming a star with all her heart.

## Profile

### Appearance
Pala wears a pink-and-white apron dress and keeps her brown hair tied into twin ponytails.

### Personality
Pala is kindhearted, outgoing, and encouraging, with a witty tongue and a rough-and-ready attitude. Meticulous domestic chores like cooking, cleaning, and laundry normally feel like a bother to her, so she often left them to her sister. Tailoring and sewing are the exception, however; she displays intense, otherworldly concentration when assisting her father with his work.

Though she puts on a tough exterior, Pala is emotionally sensitive; Agnea notes that Pala gets sad easily and cried long after their mother's death. She respects Agnea above all others and secretly practiced handling all household chores on her own so that Agnea could depart on her journey without worry. Her cooking skills remain notoriously limited; after Agnea left home, Pala only knew how to make a single recipe, resulting in her and Garud eating nothing but raspberry pie for three days and nights.

## History

### Early Life
Pala was born in [Cropdale](../../locations/leaflands/towns/cropdale.md) and grew up alongside Agnea. In their childhood, the family accompanied their mother, the famous dancer [Cuani](cuani_bristarni.md), on her performance tours across Solistia until Cuani fell ill and passed away.

### Agnea's Journey
While preparing for the annual village festival in Cropdale, Pala ventured alone into the [Veil of Trees](../../locations/leaflands/dungeons/veil_of_trees.md) to gather raspberries for festival pies, unaware that a ferocious [duorduor](duorduor.md) had escaped into the forest. Agnea and the village tavern keeper [Gus](gus.md) tracked her down and subdued the beast. Upon returning safely to the village, Pala watched Garud present Agnea with Cuani's remade dance dress, and cheered as Agnea performed at the festival. The following morning, Pala and the villagers gathered at the village bridge to see Agnea off on her travels.

Later, Pala traveled with her father to [Merry Hills](../../locations/crestlands/towns/merry_hills.md) to support Agnea in the Grand Gala dance competition against superstar [Dolcinaea Luciel](dolcinaea_luciel.md), witnessing her sister's triumph.

Following the defeat of [Vide](../../world/gods/vide.md), Pala greeted the travelers in [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md) before joining the audience inside the grand theater to watch Agnea's premier performance.

### Mikka's Next Chapter
While helping her father in Cropdale, Pala heard of [Mikka Mishuyo](../hikari/mikka_mishuyo.md), an overworked young woman living in [Ku: Castle Town](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/ku_castle_town.md) who needed new clothes. Garud tailored a beautiful new kimono for her, and Pala personally made the long journey to Ku with an escort to deliver it. Touched by the gift, Mikka invited Pala to her home for a meal. Recognizing each other as kindred spirits who deeply care for their family, the two formed a lasting friendship, and Mikka gave Pala one of her earrings as a keepsake before Pala returned home to Cropdale.
