# Laila

> *"When I dance... my heart feels warm. All the bad thoughts in my head disappear. And I'm filled with happiness."*  
> — Laila

**Laila** is an orphaned dancer from [Sai: East District](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai_east_district.md) in *Octopath Traveler II*. She plays a prominent supporting role in [Agnea Bristarni](agnea.md)'s tale.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 13
- **Gender**: Female
- **Status**: Alive
- **Origin**: [Sai: East District](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai_east_district.md)
- **Guardians**: [Platt](platt.md) and his wife

### Appearance
Laila is a young girl with wild, unruly red hair. She wears a simple brown two-piece outfit consisting of a cropped top and a short skirt suited for mobility in the desert heat.

### Personality
Laila was initially bitter, rebellious, and prone to vandalism and mischief around town—reactions born from the pain of being abandoned by her birth parents and living in poverty. After encountering Agnea, she discovers dance as a positive outlet for her bottled-up grief and anger. Beneath her prickly exterior, Laila is deeply devoted to her adoptive guardians, Platt and his wife, eagerly entering competitions to earn money to improve their lives.

## Biography

### Meeting Agnea
Abandoned as a child, Laila was taken in by Platt and his wife, who operated an orphanage in the impoverished eastern district of [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md). Harboring deep resentment over her plight, Laila often acted out. When Agnea visited Sai and paused before the town square's statue, Laila shoved past her and defaced the monument, fleeing when Platt and his wife intervened.

Later, [Veronica](veronica.md), bodyguard to superstar dancer [Dolcinaea Luciel](dolcinaea_luciel.md), arrived at the orphanage to threaten the residents with eviction so the land could be redeveloped. Laila bravely stood up to Veronica, causing the bodyguard to withdraw to avoid creating a scene. Overcome with despair and convinced the town was hopeless, Laila ran back to the town square to mimic the dance moves she had seen earlier.

Agnea found her practicing and praised her innate rhythm. Noticing Laila's worn, battered shoes, Agnea gifted her a fresh pair, and the two practiced dancing side by side. After Agnea thwarted Dolcinaea's eviction attempt, the villagers were devastated to discover that Dolcinaea's retinue had smashed the town's beloved statue. Seeing her neighbors in despair, Laila stepped forward onto the ruined pedestal and danced alongside Agnea, restoring hope to the community.

Laila subsequently traveled to [Merry Hills](../../locations/crestlands/towns/merry_hills.md) to support Agnea, helping to fend off thugs dispatched by [La'mani](lamani.md) and cheering Agnea on from the audience during her climactic victory at the [Grand Gala](../../world/lore/grand_gala.md).

### Laila's Next Chapter
Hoping to win prize money to help support her adoptive family and village, Laila returned to Merry Hills to enter a prestigious dance competition. Before her performance, a corrupt judge forced her to switch into competition-issued shoes. The rigged footwear secreted oil, causing Laila to slip repeatedly on stage. Humiliated, she fled to a nearby bridge in tears.

There, she was consoled by [Hermes](../temenos/hermes.md), a fellow competitor who suspected foul play. Investigating together, they caught Hermes' manager conspiring with the corrupt judge to sabotage rival performers. The manager was dismissed and the dishonest judge exposed. Although Hermes was technically disqualified due to her manager's misconduct, Laila invited her to share the stage. Their joint performance captivated the audience and judges, leading both young dancers to be declared joint winners.
