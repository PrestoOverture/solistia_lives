# Gil

**Gil** is an aspiring pianist and tavern keeper in [New Delsta: Backstreets](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta_backstreets.md), who plays a prominent supporting role in [Agnea Bristarni](../travelers/agnea.md)'s journey. He operates Montraine's Tavern, a modest establishment inherited from his late mentor.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 36
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [New Delsta: Backstreets](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta_backstreets.md)
- **Occupation**: Tavern Keeper, Pianist

### Appearance
Gil has shoulder-length blond hair and wears a brown hat and vest.

### Personality
Despite an inclination toward pessimism, Gil is a dreamer at heart who loves music and wishes to bring hope to others. Undervaluing his own musicianship, he long believed he lacked the talent to measure up to his mentor and abandoned his ambitions. However, his cynicism softens when he witnesses Agnea's radiant spirit. Her optimism reawakens his passion for music, inspiring him to believe in dreams once more and dedicate his craft to supporting others.

Despite running a tavern that serves cheap liquors and simple fare, Gil rarely partakes of alcohol himself, preferring piping hot coffee—which his patrons praise as top-notch. He avoids drinking because he despises playing the piano while drunk, following the personal rule of his mentor, Montraine. Gil also shares a long history with a local handyman who frequently works off an immense bar tab through manual labor, leading some patrons to mistake him for full-time staff. According to [Ochette](../travelers/ochette.md), Gil's piano playing has a soothing warmth that makes listeners feel warm inside.

## Story

As a young boy living in the impoverished backstreets of New Delsta, Gil was captivated by the sound of piano music drifting down an alleyway. Following the melody to a small tavern, he met its owner, the renowned retired pianist Montraine. Montraine took the boy under his wing and tutored him in piano. Following Montraine's death, Gil took over management of the tavern after encountering numerous setbacks. Feeling his musicianship paled in comparison to his teacher's mastery, Gil eventually ceased playing, discouraged by his perceived lack of talent.

During Agnea's arrival in New Delsta, Gil attempts to slip into the [New Delsta: Theater](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md) without a ticket and is thrown out by the theater manager, [La'mani](lamani.md). Outside, Gil encounters Agnea dancing in the street. Entranced by her buoyant performance, he invites her to his tavern and serves her coffee, warning her that high-reaching dreams often end in heartbreak, leaving dreamers like "stardust in the wind." Undeterred, Agnea offers to dance at the tavern to draw in patrons. Gil agrees to accompany her on the piano, and their joint performance fills the establishment with lively customers.

The tavern's success attracts the ire of La'mani, who sends thugs to vandalize the venue, smashing the furniture and wrecking Gil's beloved piano. Sunk back into despair, Gil is defended by Agnea, who storms the theater to demand restitution from La'mani. When the manager refuses, star performer [Dolcinaea Luciel](dolcinaea_luciel.md) intervenes, threatening to walk away from the theater unless La'mani ceases harassing the tavern. Before Agnea departs New Delsta, Gil presents her with a piece of music he composed specifically for her: the "Song of Hope."

Later, Gil travels to [Merry Hills](../../locations/crestlands/towns/merry_hills.md) to support Agnea during the Grand Gala. Performing piano on an outdoor stage alongside the dancer [Laila](laila.md), Gil gathers a massive crowd, creating an effective diversion that prevents ruffians from intercepting Agnea. At the competition, Gil joins Agnea's companions and loved ones to cheer her on against Dolcinaea, inspiring her performance of the ultimate "Song of Hope." Following the conclusion of Agnea's journey, Gil continues to play at his tavern in the backstreets, where he keeps the record "Gil on the Keys."
