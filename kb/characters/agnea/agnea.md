# Agnea Bristarni

**Agnea Bristarni** is a tavern dancer from [Cropdale](../../locations/leaflands/towns/cropdale.md) in the verdant [Leaflands](../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md) who sets out on a journey across Solistia to follow in the footsteps of her late mother, [Cuani Bristarni](cuani_bristarni.md), and achieve stardom. A cheerful and compassionate young woman, Agnea seeks to bring smiles and hope to people's faces through song and dance.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 18
- **Occupation**: Dancer
- **Status**: Alive
- **Origin**: [Cropdale](../../locations/leaflands/towns/cropdale.md), [Leaflands](../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md)
- **Relatives**:
  - [Cuani Bristarni](cuani_bristarni.md) (mother, deceased)
  - [Garud Bristarni](garud_bristarni.md) (father)
  - [Pala Bristarni](pala_bristarni.md) (younger sister)

### Appearance
Agnea has brown hair worn in braids, two silver hoop earrings, and a black headband adorned with a rose on the left side, which was given to her by her mother as a good luck charm. Her dress is re-tailored from her mother's, featuring white shoulder straps, a black bodice with orange threads, a long orange skirt with a darker hem, and a cyan sash with tassels tied around her waist. She wears black medium-heeled pumps that occasionally cause her to stumble.

### Personality
Agnea is a cheerful, optimistic, and dreamy young woman who aspires to become a star like her mother. Deeply compassionate, she stands up for what is right and readily assists those in need of help or hope, acting as a wellspring of inspiration for those in despair. She is also notably clumsy, frequently tripping over her own feet.

In her pursuit of stardom, Agnea deliberately suppresses her native rural accent in favor of speaking like "city folk," though she tends to lapse back into her country dialect whenever startled or surprised. While she does not object to others drinking around her, Agnea herself prefers to forgo alcohol in favor of raspberry juice. Among her traveling companions, she is affectionately called "Aggie" by [Ochette](../ochette/ochette.md). Her story is also notable in that she never kills any of her story antagonists, and her journey remains entirely free from entanglement with the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md).

## History

### Prior to Story
Agnea grew up traveling alongside her mother Cuani, a renowned traveling singer and dancer, her father Garud, a tailor who crafted Cuani's stage costumes, and her younger sister Pala. Cuani tragically fell ill and died from overworking herself in her desire to bring happiness to the world. Before passing away, Cuani gave Agnea her headband as a good luck charm, inspiring Agnea to become a dancer.

Fearing that Agnea would suffer the same fate from overwork, Garud strictly opposed her ambitions, forbidding her from embarking on a journey until she could raise 10,000 leaves entirely on her own. Determined to fulfill her dream, Agnea took up work as a dancer at the local tavern in Cropdale.

### Journey to Stardom

#### Chapter 1
One evening following a tavern performance, Agnea receives generous tips from the village patrons, and her employer and childhood friend [Gus](gus.md) provides the final funds needed to reach her 10,000-leaf goal. Excited, Agnea shares the news with her sister [Pala](pala_bristarni.md) and their father, though Garud is visibly dismayed.

The following morning, Agnea assists with preparations for the local festival. Gus soon rushes in with distressing news: Pala has not returned from foraging for raspberries in the forest, and tracks of the monstrous boar known as the [Duorduor](duorduor.md) have been spotted. Agnea and Gus hurry into the woods and confront the beast, managing to drive it off before it can endanger the village, though Agnea's dress is torn and dirtied in the struggle. Pala returns safely with the berries, apologizing for her absence. Returning to Cropdale, Garud presents Agnea with Cuani's performance dress, which he had lovingly altered and re-tailored for her to wear at the festival. With her father's blessing, Agnea delivers a dazzling performance singing her mother's songs as the village celebrates through the night.

The next morning, Agnea bids a fond farewell to her family and neighbors, setting out on her journey to stardom with a promise to bring smiles to the world with an original song of her own making.

*(If Agnea is not chosen as the starting traveler, the party encounters her in Cropdale after finding her dropped purse. Pala asks the party to take the flustered Agnea along with them, and she eagerly joins the journey.)*

#### Chapter 2
Agnea arrives in the bustling city of [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md), where she learns of the reigning superstar [Dolcinaea Luciel](dolcinaea_luciel.md). Unable to afford a ticket to Dolcinaea's sold-out show at the New Delsta Theatre, Agnea manages to entreat a ticket from a patron. Deeply moved and inspired by Dolcinaea's performance, Agnea dances and sings in the streets outside the theatre, catching the attention of [Gil](gil.md), the proprietor of a run-down tavern.

Gil invites Agnea to his establishment, where they discuss their shared artistic dreams. Agnea offers to perform at his tavern to help revive business, drawing city patrons inside while Gil accompanies her on the piano. The tavern's sudden revival angers the New Delsta Theatre, whose manager, [La'mani](lamani.md), sends thugs to trash the tavern and smash Gil's piano, plunging Gil back into despair. Furious, Agnea storms the New Delsta Theatre, pushes backstage, and aggressively confronts La'mani, forcing him to agree to compensate Gil for the damages. Dolcinaea observes the commotion from the wings and intervenes, commanding La'mani to leave Gil's establishment alone.

Before Agnea departs New Delsta, Gil presents her with a song he composed in her honor, titled the *Song of Hope*, encouraging her to one day write lyrics to accompany the melody.

#### Chapter 3
Agnea travels to [Tropu'hopu](../../locations/totohaha/towns/tropuhopu.md), where [Giselle](giselle.md)'s Traveling Troupe is attempting to stage a performance on the village's floating stage. The troupe's cart has broken down, and a disheartened Giselle flees to a secluded island in despair. Agnea assists the troupe in repairing their cart and tracks down Giselle, restoring her hope and enthusiasm. Grateful for her aid, the troupe warmly invites Agnea to perform alongside them at the upcoming [Grand Gala](../../world/lore/grand_gala.md).

#### Chapter 4
Arriving in [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md), Agnea discovers the town mired in gloom. A mischievous young girl named [Laila](laila.md) has been causing trouble, defacing a plaza statue of a dancer that seems familiar to Agnea. After an impromptu dance fails to cheer the sullen townsfolk, an elderly couple running the local orphanage invites Agnea into their home. They explain that twenty years prior, a dancer named Cuani visited Sai and brought joy to the town—confirming that the defaced statue is of Agnea's mother. They also mention that Cuani had taught a young orphan girl to dance, who went on to achieve fame.

Dolcinaea's bodyguard, [Veronica](veronica.md), interrupts the conversation, demanding the couple vacate the premises. Laila scares Veronica off, but Veronica warns there will be no mercy on her next visit. Hearing Laila's tragic background, Agnea finds the young girl practicing dances in secret before Cuani's statue. Noticing Laila's worn footwear, Agnea entreats a new pair of shoes for her and teaches her how to dance—a moment that echoes Cuani teaching a young Dolcinaea twenty years earlier.

Dolcinaea's hired ruffians soon menace the slums, revealing that Dolcinaea has purchased the deed to the entire town and intends to raze it to construct "Dolcinaealand," claiming no true star could ever originate from such squalor. Entrusting Laila to protect the orphanage children, Agnea ascends the hill overlooking Sai to confront Dolcinaea. Agnea rejects Dolcinaea's callous disregard for the townspeople, prompting Veronica to attack. After Agnea defeats Veronica, Dolcinaea extends a formal invitation for Agnea to compete against her at the Festival of Grace during the Grand Gala.

#### Chapter 5
Agnea reaches [Merry Hills](../../locations/crestlands/towns/merry_hills.md) for the Grand Gala, finding herself recognized as Dolcinaea's rival while also being targeted by abductors hired to take her out of the competition. After driving off the first pair of thugs, Agnea reunites with Giselle's Traveling Troupe, who volunteer to escort her toward the Stage of the Moon and Sun.

When the ruffians pursue them again, [Tanzy](tanzy.md) stages a clever distraction to help Agnea slip away. Near the stage, Gil and Laila make a surprise appearance; Gil plays the piano while Laila dances, captivating the crowd and diverting the pursuers. Just outside the stage, the mastermind reveals himself: La'mani, the embittered theatre manager from New Delsta. Before he can seize Agnea, Veronica intervenes, knocking him unconscious and urging Agnea to take her place on stage beside Dolcinaea.

Facing the immense crowd, Agnea's confidence wavers as the audience cheers exclusively for Dolcinaea. Dolcinaea challenges Agnea to prove who the true star is. Taunted by Dolcinaea and overwhelmed by the crowd's singular adoration for her opponent, Agnea nearly falters—until she hears the voices of her family and friends calling out her name: Giselle's troupe, Gil, Laila, Gus, Pala, and her father Garud, who cheers louder than anyone. Reinvigorated, Agnea debuts the finished *Song of Hope*, engaging Dolcinaea in an intense dance contest until both are exhausted. Despite her physical exhaustion, Agnea manages to pull herself back up, prompting Dolcinaea to concede defeat and acknowledge Agnea as a genuine star.

As the crowd demands an encore, Agnea offers to share the stage, but Dolcinaea steps aside, telling Agnea that this encore is her time to shine. Backstage, La'mani reluctantly admits that he has become Agnea's fan. Urged on by Laila, Agnea returns to the stage to an enraptured audience.

### Crossed Paths: Agnea and Hikari

#### Part 1: Ryu
Agnea and [Hikari Ku](../hikari/hikari.md) visit [Ryu](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/ryu.md), where they are captivated by a soothing melody played on a lute by a traveling musician named [Yomi](../others/yomi.md). At Agnea's urging, Hikari pays for another song, but Yomi asks them to return the next night. When they meet again, Yomi reveals her lute has been damaged and requires horse hair for repairs. Agnea entreats the necessary material from a local merchant. The following night on a nearby hill, Yomi plays "By the Light of the Heart," a song Hikari recognizes as one composed by his late companion, [Tsuki](../hikari/tsuki.md).

#### Part 2: Ku
Following the liberation of [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md) from Mugen's rule, Agnea and Hikari visit the city. Agnea inquires about the glowing paper lanterns throughout the town, which Hikari explains represent human souls. [Benkei](../hikari/benkei.md) approaches and suggests hosting a festival to celebrate their victory, but Hikari hesitates, feeling it too soon to celebrate so many losses. Agnea suggests framing the event as a festival to honor the departed, an idea both men warmly accept, and Benkei invites Agnea to dance.

While gathering supplies, Hikari notes that an accompanist is needed. Hearing rumors of lute music echoing from the Tranquil Grotto, the pair investigate and find Yomi mourning Tsuki, who was her sister. Hikari duels Yomi and earns her respect, reassuring her that Tsuki was happy serving by his side. Yomi agrees to perform at the memorial festival, playing "By the Light of the Heart" alongside Agnea's dance.

### The Journey for the Dawn
When the eight travelers gather to confront the encroaching eternal night, Agnea confronts the nihilistic conspirator [Oboro](../hikari/oboro.md). When Oboro asks whether the dawn is truly worth the suffering of the journey, Agnea firmly replies that every new day brings another chance to get back up after stumbling and another chance to shine. Standing before the dark god [Vide](../../world/gods/vide.md), Agnea encourages her companions with unwavering optimism, declaring that they can accomplish anything they set their minds to, including making the sun rise once more.

During the epilogue, Agnea busily prepares for a grand performance in [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md). (If Agnea was chosen as the starting protagonist, Pala searches New Delsta in her place to assemble the other seven travelers for the performance.)

## Relationships

- **[Cuani Bristarni](cuani_bristarni.md)**: Agnea's late mother, a beloved dancer and singer whose passion and songs inspired Agnea's dream of stardom. Before succumbing to illness caused by overwork, Cuani passed her headband and legacy to Agnea.
- **[Garud Bristarni](garud_bristarni.md)**: Agnea's father, a tailor who met Cuani while sewing her stage dresses. Traumatized by Cuani's death, he initially tried to deter Agnea from dancing by requiring her to earn 10,000 leaves. When she succeeded, he re-tailored Cuani's dress as a parting gift and traveled to Merry Hills to cheer the loudest for his daughter.
- **[Pala Bristarni](pala_bristarni.md)**: Agnea's younger sister, who often acts as the grounded, practical voice of reason between the two. Though fond of teasing Agnea for her clumsy antics, she cares deeply for her older sister and helps assemble the travelers in New Delsta during the epilogue.
- **[Gus](gus.md)**: Agnea's childhood friend and employer at the Cropdale tavern. He provides the remaining money Agnea needs for her journey and braves the forest alongside her to rescue Pala from the Duorduor.
- **[Dolcinaea Luciel](dolcinaea_luciel.md)**: The reigning superstar of Solistia. Once an orphan in Sai whom Cuani taught how to dance, Dolcinaea grew arrogant, determined to erase her past and asserting that true stardom is reserved for a chosen few. After competing against Agnea at the Grand Gala, she is humbled by Agnea's perseverance and warmly concedes the stage to her.
- **[Gil](gil.md)**: A down-on-his-luck tavern owner and pianist in New Delsta whose passion is rekindled by Agnea. He composes the instrumental melody of *Song of Hope* for her and helps distract pursuers at Merry Hills by playing piano.
- **[Laila](laila.md)**: A spirited orphan girl in Sai who initially resented Cuani's statue. After Agnea gifts her new dancing shoes and teaches her to dance, Laila becomes fiercely loyal to Agnea, assisting Gil in creating a diversion during the Grand Gala.
- **[Veronica](veronica.md)**: Dolcinaea's stern bodyguard who initially threatens the Sai orphanage. After being defeated by Agnea, she recognizes Agnea's talent and later knocks out La'mani backstage at Merry Hills to protect her.
- **[Giselle](giselle.md)**: Leader of Giselle's Traveling Troupe. Agnea helps repair the troupe's cart and restores Giselle's confidence in Tropu'hopu, earning the troupe's enduring support at the Grand Gala.
- **[Tanzy](tanzy.md)**: A quick-witted performer in Giselle's troupe who stages a diversion against kidnappers targeting Agnea in Merry Hills.
- **[La'mani](lamani.md)**: The unscrupulous New Delsta Theatre manager who vandalized Gil's tavern and attempted to kidnap Agnea in Merry Hills. After being foiled by Veronica, he is moved by Agnea's performance and sheepishly confesses to becoming her fan.
- **[Hikari Ku](../hikari/hikari.md)**: Agnea's traveling companion during their Crossed Paths in Ryu and Ku, where they help Yomi find solace and collaborate to host a festival honoring the fallen.
- **[Ochette](../ochette/ochette.md)**: A traveling companion who affectionately refers to Agnea as "Aggie."
- **[Partitio Yellowil](../partitio/partitio.md)**: A fellow traveler whose path mirrors Agnea's; both set out with gifts of clothing from their fathers, resolve their stories without taking a life, and avoid entanglements with the Moonshade Order.
