# Veronica

> *"Let the others know there will be no mercy next time.... You've been warned."*
> — Veronica, threatening to evict [Platt](platt.md)

> *"I will dedicate my life to protecting Madam Dolcinaea."*
> — Veronica, in [New Delsta: Theater](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md)

> *"A bodyguard must be ever vigilant."*
> — Veronica, in [New Delsta: Theater](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md)

**Veronica** is the 34-year-old personal bodyguard of the superstar dancer [Dolcinaea Luciel](dolcinaea_luciel.md). Born with exceptional martial talent, she once guarded another person of import, but was dismissed simply in favor of a man. Dolcinaea understood her frustrations and urged her to embrace who she was, inspiring Veronica to pledge her life and complete devotion to her mistress.

## Profile

### Appearance
Veronica is tall with a muscular build, brown eyes, and long black hair tied back into a ponytail. She wears a white shirt and black trousers held by shoulder straps. Distinctively, she wields no weapon in battle, relying entirely on bare-handed martial combat.

### Personality
Veronica is straightforward, businesslike, and unconcerned with trivialities. Her devotion to Dolcinaea is absolute, obeying her commands unconditionally. Having honed her combat skills above all else from a young age, Veronica struggled in past positions where society expected her to behave in a delicate or ladylike manner. Because Dolcinaea recognized her raw strength and encouraged her to be herself, Veronica harbors deep gratitude and fidelity toward her mistress.

## History

### Background
Veronica was previously employed as a guard for another prominent figure, but was abruptly dismissed so that a male guard could take her position. Recognizing her strength and empathizing with her plight, Dolcinaea recruited Veronica into her service and encouraged her to live authentically without compromising her warrior identity.

### Agnea's Journey
Veronica stands faithfully by Dolcinaea during her travels across Solistia. In [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md), she accompanies her mistress when Dolcinaea confronts the impresario [La'mani](lamani.md) following his altercation with a dancer, later reassuring Dolcinaea that all matters are proceeding according to plan.

Later, Veronica arrives in [Sai: East District](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai_east_district.md) to enforce the eviction of a local orphanage, warning the residents to vacate. When [Laila](laila.md) confronts her, Veronica withdraws to avoid making an unnecessary scene, though she leaves behind a stern warning. That night, Veronica returns with hired enforcers to forcefully drive the residents from their homes, watching the destruction alongside Dolcinaea from [Dragonridge](../../locations/hinoeuma/dungeons/dragonridge.md). [Agnea Bristarni](agnea.md) confronts them there, seeking to halt the injustice. When Agnea refuses to leave, Dolcinaea commands Veronica to eliminate her. Despite Veronica's formidable physical strength, the young dancer overcomes her in combat. Following the defeat, Dolcinaea destroys the statue of [Cuani](cuani_bristarni.md) and leaves an invitation for Agnea to meet her at the [Grand Gala](../../world/lore/grand_gala.md).

At the [Stage of the Moon and Sun](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/stage_of_the_moon_and_sun.md) in [Merry Hills](../../locations/crestlands/towns/merry_hills.md), Veronica waits among the audience as Dolcinaea prepares backstage. When La'mani attempts to accost Agnea outside the auditorium, Veronica swiftly intervenes and knocks him unconscious with a single blow. Dismissing La'mani as irrelevant, she directs Agnea toward the stage where Dolcinaea is waiting.

After Agnea triumphs over Dolcinaea in their climactic dance duel, Veronica escorts her defeated mistress from the hall, pausing to express sincere gratitude to Agnea for finally allowing Dolcinaea to find peace.

### Gift for Madam Dolcinaea
Following the events at Merry Hills, Veronica and Dolcinaea return to New Delsta, eventually taking up residence at the [New Delsta: Theater](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/new_delsta_theater.md). While maintaining a strict workout regimen, Veronica contemplates a meaningful gift to commemorate the anniversary of the day they first met. After seeking advice from an acquaintance close to her mistress, Veronica presents Dolcinaea with a heavy dumbbell. Dolcinaea is thrilled by the present and fondly reminisces with Veronica about their journey together, though to Veronica's mild dismay, Dolcinaea intends to incorporate the dumbbell as a musical instrument for her upcoming performance rather than using it for exercise.
