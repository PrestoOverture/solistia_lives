# Regulus

> "*To the stars! To which we mere mortals turn our gaze, and use to delineate our dreams. Though they are far, far beyond our grasp. Hehe... And so, I have devised a device that will allow us to bring them right before our eyes. I call it... the astronomical telescope!*"
> — Regulus

## Overview

**Regulus** is a thirty-eight-year-old scholar and astronomer residing in the [Montwise: Library](../locations/montwise_library.md). An old university friend of [Osvald V. Vanstein](osvald.md), he is the central figure in [Osvald](osvald.md) and [Partitio Yellowil](partitio.md)'s Crossed Paths storyline.

## Profile

### Appearance
Regulus is a middle-aged man with black hair and grey eyes. He dresses in a scholar's blue coat, brown waistcoat, white cravat, black trousers, and black leather shoes.

### Personality
Regulus possesses a brilliant, analytical mind when contemplating the cosmos, yet is hopelessly inept when dealing with practical life. He struggles constantly with finances, having once gambled away nearly all of his funds at the [New Delsta: Game Parlor](../locations/new_delsta_game_parlor.md). Even when facing utter starvation, he remains obsessively meticulous, tracking the passage of time down to the exact day, hour, and minute since his last meal. 

Regulus speaks about celestial bodies with dramatic, poetic melodrama, prompting Osvald to caution others against indulging his grandiloquence. His devotion to astronomy is so total that he often neglects basic living needs; his former girlfriend eventually grew tired of being ignored and left him. A fellow scholar in [Crackridge](../locations/crackridge.md) is implied to be this estranged partner, noting that she still gazes up at the stars every night before sleeping.

## History

### Early Life
Fascinated by the night sky since boyhood, Regulus has kept a continuous, unbroken daily log of celestial observations for decades. During his academic career, he studied alongside Osvald, forging a lasting friendship.

### The Scholar and the Merchant
While staying in [New Delsta](../locations/new_delsta.md) to obtain specialty lenses and parts for his new invention—an "astronomical telescope"—Regulus collapsed on the street from starvation after going three days, nine hours, and twenty-three minutes without food. Osvald and [Partitio](partitio.md) revived him. With only 600 leaves remaining to his name, Regulus entrusted the travelers to purchase the final components needed to finish his instrument, inviting them to Montwise to see the completed apparatus.

When Osvald and Partitio arrived at the [Montwise: Library](../locations/montwise_library.md), they found that Regulus had once again collapsed from hunger and that his finished telescope was missing. Though he initially claimed it had been stolen, Regulus confessed in embarrassment that he had pawned it to a local moneylender as collateral. Investigating the lender's estate, Osvald and Partitio discovered that Regulus had already paid off the loan in full, but the dishonest moneylender was exploiting Regulus's gullibility to extort further coin. After defeating the moneylender and recovering the telescope, the companions set up the device atop Montwise. Gazing upon the heavens, they noticed a subtle, troubling omen: the nights over Solistia had been gradually lengthening over the years.

In the aftermath of their journey, metallic plates from Partitio's Neo Steam Engine proved instrumental in furthering Regulus's celestial research.
