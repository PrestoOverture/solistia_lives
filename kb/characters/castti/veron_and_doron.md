# Veron and Doron

**Veron** and **Doron** are a pair of corrupted simian beasts encountered in the waterways beneath [Canalbrine](../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md).

## Overview & Story

Prior to [Castti Florenz](../travelers/castti.md)'s arrival in Canalbrine, Veron and Doron contracted a virulent infection and broke into the cavern containing the town's primary [water source](../../locations/harborlands/dungeons/canalbrine_water_source.md). Their foul, toxic bodily secretions seeped into the reservoir, poisoning the municipal water supply and inflicting a severe, unexplained illness upon many of Canalbrine's inhabitants.

When Castti entered the underground spring to diagnose and eliminate the source of the epidemic, the two beasts ambushed her. Driven mad by infection and feral ferocity, they fought in tandem; when one beast suffered grievous wounds, the other viciously cannibalized its weakened partner to absorb its strength. Castti ultimately slew the monstrous pair and purified the tainted reservoir with purebalm leaves, ending the blight threatening the port town.
