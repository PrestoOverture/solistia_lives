# Lily (Octopath Traveler II)

> *"I... wish to live to watch Lady Melia grow and thrive, for as long as I am able."*  
> — Lily, in [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md)

**Lily** is the devoted butler and assistant to Lady [Rosa](rosa.md) of House Glenville in *Octopath Traveler II*. She plays a key supporting role in [Castti Florenz](castti.md)'s storyline in [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 58
- **Gender**: Female
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md)
- **Affiliation**: House Glenville
- **Role**: Butler and guardian to [Melia](melia.md)

### Appearance
Lily is an older woman with gray hair, dressed in a neat white domestic gown appropriate for the head steward of the Glenville manor.

### Personality
Fiercely devoted and loyal, Lily has served Lady Rosa like a shadow since their youth. Following Rosa's illness and eventual death, Lily strives to protect the family legacy and serve as a surrogate guardian to young Melia. While she tries to balance affection with firmness, she feels deeply the heartache of knowing she cannot fully replace Melia's mother when she overhears the young girl crying in the herb garden at night.

## Biography

When Castti Florenz arrives in Winterbloom searching for clues regarding her past, Lily greets her at the estate gates and welcomes her inside to attend to the ailing Lady Rosa.

During Castti's stay, Rosa's spiteful nephew [Greg](greg.md) arrives at the manor, coldly urging Rosa to pass away quickly so he can inherit the estate. Lily stands by distressed and helpless until young Melia confronts her cousin and forces him out. Later, Lily discovers a ransom note near the mansion's foyer revealing that Greg has abducted Melia to blackmail Castti into leaving town. Castti tracks down the kidnapper and rescues Melia safely.

Following the ordeal, Greg returns to the manor in contrition. On Melia's twelfth birthday, Lily is present alongside Castti as Rosa bequeaths joint guardianship of the town to Melia and Greg, presents Melia with the Brooch of Joy, and peacefully passes away. Lily subsequently assumes full guardianship over Melia, vowing to protect and guide the young heir as she grows.
