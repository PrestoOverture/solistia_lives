# Yorna

> *"Though my life could not be saved, your hands are those of a healer. Help those in need."*
> — Yorna

## Overview

- **Age**: Unknown
- **Gender**: Female
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Relatives**: [Trousseau](trousseau.md) (brother)
- **Location**: [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md)

**Yorna** is a character in *Octopath Traveler II*. She lived in [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md) with her brother [Trousseau](trousseau.md), before eventually succumbing to an incurable disease.

## History

Yorna lived in [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md) alongside her brother [Trousseau](trousseau.md). She contracted an incurable disease that ultimately proved fatal. Before she passed away, she urged her brother to dedicate his life to healing others, telling him that while she could not be saved, his hands were those of a healer meant to help those in need.
