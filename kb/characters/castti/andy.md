# Andy

**Andy** was an apothecary of [Eir's Apothecaries](../../world/factions/eirs_apothecaries.md) and the older brother of [Randy](randy.md).

## Profile

- **Age**: 30
- **Occupation**: Apothecary
- **Affiliation**: [Eir's Apothecaries](../../world/factions/eirs_apothecaries.md)
- **Relatives**: [Randy](randy.md) (younger brother)
- **Status**: Deceased

### Background & Personality
Andy was known for his brash, headstrong personality. In his youth, he aspired to become a soldier, driven toward battle until [Castti Florenz](../travelers/castti.md) taught him the irreplaceable value of life. Whether swayed by her philosophy or by his deep affection for Castti herself, Andy turned away from violence and dedicated his life to healing others alongside his brother.

## Story

As a dedicated member of Eir's Apothecaries, Andy traveled across Solistia delivering medical care to afflicted communities under Castti's leadership.

When [Trousseau](trousseau.md) succumbed to madness and unleashed a deadly, toxic purple rain upon [Healeaks](../../locations/brightlands/towns/abandoned_village.md), the village was engulfed in poison-fueled flame. In an act of supreme courage, Andy and his brother Randy threw themselves into the inferno to extinguish the accursed blaze and protect the surviving villagers, giving their lives so that others might live.
