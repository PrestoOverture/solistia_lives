# Senah

> *"I've got my eye on a job in a ship's galley as a cook."*
> — Senah, in [Canalbrine](../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md)

> *"My big brother's a sailor."*
> — Senah, in [Canalbrine](../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md)

**Senah** is a seventeen-year-old resident of the port city of [Canalbrine](../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md) and the younger brother of [Sesque](sesque.md). He looks up to his older brother—a sailor on a large vessel—more than anyone else in the world, and dreams of serving as a chef in the galley of a ship under Sesque's command. In pursuit of this dream, he has already begun studying culinary arts.

## Profile

### Appearance
Senah has short brown hair.

### Personality
Earnest, ambitious, and proud of his brother, Senah speaks frequently of his brother's seafaring work. He is polite and deeply grateful to those who assist him, showing heartfelt appreciation to [Castti Florenz](castti.md) after she cures his affliction.

## History

### The Water Contamination in Canalbrine
Shortly after Castti reaches Canalbrine, Senah suddenly falls violently ill and collapses in the street. With the help of [Malaya](malaya.md), Castti carries the youth to his home to administer care. Although Malaya initially suspects the boy has succumbed to sun fever, Castti determines that his rash and nausea are caused by systemic poisoning, traced to Canalbrine's tainted water supply.

Castti administers a compound of lycanthe antipyretic, successfully breaking his fever and halting the poison's spread. Before Senah can fully awaken, his protective older brother Sesque arrives. Finding an unfamiliar apothecary administering medicines, Sesque assumes Castti poisoned him and furiously drives her from the house.

Thanks to Castti's timely treatment, Senah makes a complete recovery. After learning what truly occurred, Sesque apologizes to Castti for the misunderstanding, and the brothers return to their peaceful lives together in Canalbrine.
