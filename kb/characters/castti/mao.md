# Mao

**Mao** is a 27-year-old apothecary living in the old quarters of [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md). She appears in [Castti Florenz](castti.md)'s Chapter 2 (Sai Route) as the town's resident healer, providing medical care for wounded soldiers and townspeople during the region's protracted conflicts.

## Profile

### Appearance
Mao wears a white apothecary's robe, carries a medicine satchel, and keeps her hair bundled in a bun beneath a protective bandana.

### Personality
Compassionate, patient, and deeply dedicated to her calling, Mao works tirelessly to treat Sai's injured despite constant supply shortages and noisy interruptions from military figures like [Edmund](edmund.md). Having tended to countless casualties over the years, she carries the emotional weight of her vocation: she never forgets the faces of patients she was unable to save, though she admits to quickly forgetting those who make a full recovery.

## Story

Though Mao never intended to remain in Sai permanently, the continuous influx of wounded from regional strife kept her in the old quarters for over ten years. Four years prior to the story, she welcomed [Eir's Apothecaries](../../world/factions/eirs_apothecaries.md) when the traveling medical guild arrived in Sai to aid the wounded, meeting Castti for the first time as Castti spearheaded the treatment efforts.

When an amnesiac Castti returns to Sai years later, Mao readily accepts her assistance in treating injured soldiers. Defending her patient-centered approach against Edmund's objections, Mao helps refresh Castti's lost memories by sharing details of Castti's previous visit with Eir's Apothecaries and the lives they touched together.
