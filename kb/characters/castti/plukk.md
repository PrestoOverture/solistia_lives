# Plukk

> *"Little Miss Tough Girl here's on her way out of town. Show her the boot, and take her purse for the trouble!"*
> — Plukk, confronting [Castti Florenz](../travelers/castti.md)

> *"... No one loses their way in life on purpose."*
> — Plukk, in [Winterbloom: Thieves' Quarter](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom_thieves_quarter.md)

> *"No more larceny. The lady's orders."*
> — Plukk, in [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md)

**Plukk** is a 38-year-old thief based in [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md). Though she spent years living as a hardened outlaw, she secretly harbored a lifelong desire to be a florist.

## Profile

### Appearance
Plukk has messy blond hair and wears an eyepatch over her left eye. She is dressed in a green dress with a high thigh slit, a ragged scarf of matching green, brown leather gloves reaching up to her shoulders, and brown leather boots.

### Personality
Rowdy, loud-mouthed, and quick to violence, Plukk appears to be a standard ruffian at first glance. However, her aggressive exterior is largely a defense mechanism developed to cope with past trauma. When treated with dignity, she can be understanding, gentle, and respectful. Despite having spent years in criminality, she possesses crude handwriting and never lost her private dream of cultivating flowers.

## History

### Background
In her youth, Plukk was sold to a brothel to settle family debts. While there, she suffered the loss of her left eye after lying to protect her closest companion. In an effort to take control of her own destiny and no longer remain at the mercy of fate, she abandoned that life and turned to banditry.

### Castti's Journey
Plukk first appears during [Castti Florenz](../travelers/castti.md)'s travels in Winterbloom, working alongside her lackeys [Mikk](mikk_octopath_traveler_ii.md) and [Makk](makk_octopath_traveler_ii.md) as enforcers for [Greg](greg.md). Under Greg's direction, Plukk assists in kidnapping [Melia](melia.md) to coerce Castti into allowing Greg to inherit the town's estate from Lady [Rosa](rosa.md). Growing impatient with Greg's hesitation and speeches, Plukk disobeys his orders and attempts to force Castti out of town by violence. Castti soundly defeats Plukk and her henchmen, leaving Greg to answer for the plot.

### Redemption
Following the conclusion of Castti's journey and the demise of [Bergomi](../throne/bergomi.md), Plukk assumes command of the surviving remnants of the [Snowhares](../../world/factions/snowhares.md) in Winterbloom. Although thieving has significantly slowed down, Plukk staunchly refuses to revive the brutal human trafficking trade operated by the syndicate's former leader.

Hoping to orchestrate a lucrative robbery, Plukk devises a plan to infiltrate Melia's manor: she pens a crudely written letter feigning repentance and requesting employment so her gang can gain easy access to the estate. Upon receiving the letter, Melia contemplates whether to have Plukk arrested. Instead, recognizing a genuine possibility for reform, Melia offers Plukk, Mikk, and Makk honest employment as manor gardeners. Deeply moved and impressed by Melia's compassion and trust, Plukk renounces banditry for good, happily embracing an honest life tending flowers alongside Mikk and Makk.
