# Rosa

> "*[Melia](melia.md)... I have a present for you. I give this to you... the Brooch of Joy. Happy birthday, my angel.*"
> — Lady Rosa Glenville, to Melia

## Overview

**Lady Rosa Glenville** was the thirty-five-year-old ruler of [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md) in the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md), head of House Glenville, and mother to [Melia](melia.md). Despite suffering from a progressive and fatal paralysis, she fought tenaciously to survive until her daughter's twelfth birthday. She plays a pivotal role in [Castti Florenz](../travelers/castti.md)'s journey through Winterbloom.

## Profile

### Appearance
Lady Rosa has long brown hair and wears a stately green coat over a white dress while resting in her bedchamber at the Glenville manor.

### Personality
Despite suffering chronic, debilitating physical torment, Rosa was regarded by the people of Winterbloom as a kind, compassionate, and noble governor. Above all, she loved her young daughter Melia, drawing on immense inner willpower to endure the agony of her illness so that Melia would not be left alone and dispossessed before reaching her coming of age.

## History

### The Onset of Illness
After assuming leadership of Winterbloom, Rosa gave birth to Melia and raised her as a single mother; her husband's identity and status remained unknown. However, Rosa was stricken with an incurable degenerative malady that slowly paralyzed her limbs and body. Numerous physicians and apothecaries were consulted across the realm, yet none could arrest the disease.

Eventually, [Trousseau](trousseau.md) and members of [Eir's Apothecaries](../../world/factions/eirs_apothecaries.md), led by Castti Florenz, visited House Glenville. Recognizing the condition was fatal, Castti formulated a specialized palliative treatment that slowed its advancement. She also established an herb garden on the estate grounds so the household could cultivate fresh balm leaves for Rosa's daily remedies.

### Castti's Return
As years passed, Rosa's illness rendered her completely bedridden. When an amnesiac Castti visited Winterbloom tracing her forgotten memories, she examined Rosa once more. Castti and Melia went to harvest fresh herbs, leaving Rosa resting. During their absence, Rosa's nephew [Greg](greg.md) entered the bedchamber, callously urging his aunt to succumb to her illness so he could inherit the estate. Melia returned in time to rebuke Greg and cast him out. Castti then brewed a soothing elixir using scalebark leaf, snowgrass, and bloodberries, allowing Rosa to sleep peacefully free of pain.

Taking advantage of Rosa's slumber, Greg orchestrated Melia's abduction in a bid to seize the Glenville inheritance by force. Castti tracked down the kidnappers and safely rescued the young heiress. Overcome with guilt and chastened by his defeat, Greg returned to Rosa's bedside to confess and seek forgiveness.

On the day of Melia's twelfth birthday, Rosa reached the milestone she had fought so long to see. Gathering her family, she entrusted the future governance of the town and estate jointly to Melia and a repentant Greg. She then presented Melia with her birthday gift—the cherished family heirloom known as the Brooch of Joy—before passing away peacefully.
