# Edmund

> *"A man's gotta be strong if he wants to make it in this world. Gotta be a provider, y'get me? You don't work, you don't eat. No excuses."*
> — Edmund

## Overview

- **Age**: 40
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Origin / Location**: [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md), [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md); later [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md), [Leaflands](../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md) (also seen at [Sandflow Pass](../../locations/hinoeuma/wilds/sandflow_pass.md))
- **Occupation**: Military commander, leader of Sai's immigrant forces; former soldier of Timberain

**Master Edmund** is a military commander in Octopath Traveler II who leads the immigrant forces of [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md) and plays a key role in the story of [Castti Florenz](../travelers/castti.md). A battle-hardened veteran who formerly served the kingdom of [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md), Edmund initially scorns weakness and injury before witnessing Castti's selfless healing, which inspires a profound change of heart.

## Appearance

Edmund is a broad, rugged forty-year-old warrior with long brown hair, a full beard, and a weathered countenance. He wears a sturdy green cloak over practical combat armor suited for desert warfare.

## Personality

Outwardly rough, boisterous, and quick-tempered, Edmund initially projects a harsh, unyielding philosophy: that survival demands physical strength, that men must provide and protect, and that those who cannot work or fight have no excuses. He harbored intense disdain for perceived spinelessness, believing sick and wounded soldiers were merely feigning illness, and dismissed apothecaries as useless "bonemenders."

However, beneath his abrasive exterior lies a deep capacity for honor and conscience. His morality previously drove him to defect from Timberain when ordered to purge defenseless outsiders. After seeing Castti's unflinching resolve to treat wounded soldiers from both sides of the conflict, Edmund sets aside his pride, embraces compassion, and actively aids in saving lives. Even after mellowing, he maintains his blustery demeanor, still barking at injured troops while ensuring they receive adequate rest and medical care.

## Relationships

- **[Castti Florenz](../travelers/castti.md)**: Initially spurned by Edmund as a meddlesome "bonemender," Castti earns his deep respect and loyalty after convincing him to rescue trapped enemy soldiers in the quicksand and saving him from the sand lion. Edmund later serves as a crucial ally during the crisis in Timberain.
- **[Griff](griff.md)** / **[Commander Griff](griff.md)**: The commander of the opposing forces during the border war near Sai. After being rescued by Castti and Edmund from the sand lion, Griff reconciles with Edmund, and the two work together to convince the king of Timberain to halt hostilities and welcome outsiders.
- **[Mao](mao.md)**: An apothecary in Sai whose aid Edmund initially dismissed before his change of perspective.

## History

### Early Life and Defection

Edmund originally served as a loyal soldier in the royal army of [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md). When the crown ordered the military to expel and purge foreign immigrants deemed economic and social liabilities, Edmund initially followed his superiors' commands without question. Over time, however, the cruelty of these purges sowed seeds of moral doubt. Refusing to continue victimizing innocent outsiders, Edmund defected from Timberain and journeyed south to the desert city of [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md), where he took charge of the immigrant militia defending the region against Timberain's expansionism.

### The Border Conflict (Sai)

During Castti's journey through Sai, Edmund is first encountered berating wounded soldiers in an apothecary tent, furious at their inability to fight and dismissing both [Mao](mao.md) and Castti. Soon after, Edmund leads his troops outside Sai, celebrating what appears to be a total victory after an opposing contingent led by [Commander Griff](griff.md) is swallowed by sudden quicksand.

When Castti rushes into the dunes to treat the dying enemy soldiers, Edmund follows to forcibly drag her away. Castti confronts his callousness, urging him to put aside his pride and recognize that suffering men require aid regardless of their uniform. Moved by her conviction, Edmund relents and begins helping extract the trapped men. In the midst of the rescue, a monstrous sand lion erupts from the earth and drags Edmund beneath the dunes. After Castti slays the beast, she pulls Edmund and the remaining soldiers to safety. Shaken and humbled, Edmund makes peace with Griff, who pledges to petition the king of Timberain to open the borders and end the persecution of immigrants.

### The Crisis in Timberain

Following the armistice, the king of Timberain opens the borders to outsiders, prompting Edmund to relocate to Timberain to help integrate the migrant population. When Castti arrives in the city ahead of the royal coronation to stop [Trousseau](trousseau.md)'s scheme to release toxic rain, she reunites with Edmund. Edmund immediately mobilizes to assist her. Alongside Griff, who rallies skeptical tavern soldiers, Edmund coordinates a mass evacuation of the city's populace into shelters just as the poisonous deluge begins.

Following Castti's victory over Trousseau and the curing of the poisoned citizens, Edmund is at Castti's bedside in the local tavern when she awakens. He escorts her outside into the town square, allowing her to take in the coronation festivities and witness the thriving city she fought so hard to preserve. In his later days, he remains vigilant in protecting travelers, patrolling [Sandflow Pass](../../locations/hinoeuma/wilds/sandflow_pass.md) by day and safeguarding Sai by night.
