# Mikk (Octopath Traveler II)

**Mikk** is a thief and devoted henchman of [Plukk](plukk.md) who appears in [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md) during [Castti Florenz](../travelers/castti.md)'s story.

## Overview

- **Age**: 31
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md)
- **Affiliation**: [Plukk](plukk.md)'s gang, [Snowhares](../../world/factions/snowhares.md) (formerly); House Glenville (as a gardener)

> "*If the boss is happy, I'm happy.*"
> — Mikk in the Thieves' Quarter

Mikk is a thief who holds deep respect and affection for his gang leader, Plukk. Formerly a crewman aboard a corrupt merchant ship, he attempted to steal funds and flee, but was captured and faced brutal punishment before Plukk intervened and rescued his life. From that day forward, Mikk worked loyally by her side alongside [Makk](makk_octopath_traveler_ii.md). Despite his criminal lifestyle, Mikk harbors a sincere wish that Plukk could leave thievery behind and find genuine peace and happiness.

## History

### The Winterbloom Ransom Plot

In Winterbloom, Mikk and Makk assisted Plukk when they were hired by Lady [Melia](melia.md)'s estranged cousin [Greg](greg.md) to hold the young heiress hostage in the [Winterbloom: Thieves' Quarter](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom_thieves_quarter.md). Confronted by Castti, the trio fought the apothecary in battle and were subdued.

### House Glenville Gardener

Following their defeat and subsequent burglary attempts in town, Plukk's group was summoned to the Glenville estate by Melia. Rather than arresting them, Melia offered Mikk, Makk, and Plukk legitimate employment as estate gardeners. Mikk accepted the new lifestyle, spending his days tending the botanical gardens at the Glenville manor and his evenings resting in the Thieves' Quarter, bemused by his unlikely transition from hardened bandit to gardener.
