# Elma

> *"Oh, no... The animals! Chief! I'll see to them!"*
> — Elma, discovering the poisoned animals in Healeaks

## Overview

- **Age**: 26
- **Gender**: Female
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Affiliation**: [Eir's Apothecaries](../../world/factions/eirs_apothecaries.md)
- **Occupation**: Veterinarian, Apothecary
- **Partner**: [Randy](randy.md) (betrothed)

**Elma** is a member of [Eir's Apothecaries](../../world/factions/eirs_apothecaries.md) in *Octopath Traveler II*. A skilled veterinarian known for her soothing demeanor, she dedicated her medical expertise to treating and caring for animals alongside her fellow apothecaries until her tragic death in the village of [Healeaks](../../locations/brightlands/towns/abandoned_village.md).

## Personality & Backstory

Elma possessed an exceptionally calming and gentle presence that put both patients and animals at ease. She was inspired to join Eir's Apothecaries after watching [Castti Florenz](castti.md) miraculously cure an entire family afflicted by a deadly sickness. Within the organization, she and fellow apothecary [Randy](randy.md) fell in love and pledged their lives to one another.

## Story

As the dedicated veterinarian of Eir's Apothecaries, Elma tended to the group's pack animals and local livestock across Solistia. When the apothecaries arrived in [Healeaks](../../locations/brightlands/towns/abandoned_village.md) during the purple rain crisis unleashed by [Trousseau](trousseau.md), Elma was horrified to discover the village's animals collapsing from poison. Rushing out to tend to them, she gave her life in a desperate effort to save the team's horse, [Jeyah](jeyah.md), succumbing to the toxic deluge alongside most of her companions.
