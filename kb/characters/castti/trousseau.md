# Trousseau

**Trousseau** is an apothecary and former member of [Eir's Apothecaries](../../world/factions/eirs_apothecaries.md) who serves as the primary antagonist of [Castti Florenz](castti.md)'s story.

## Overview

- **Age**: 26
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Relatives**: [Yorna](yorna.md) (younger sister, deceased)

> "*If she can be saved, I want to do it... with my own hands.*"
> — Trousseau, before gathering Santanejo Blossoms for [Sally](sally.md)

### Appearance

Trousseau has white hair and dark red eyes. During his time with [Eir's Apothecaries](../../world/factions/eirs_apothecaries.md), he wore the group's signature blue uniform. Following his descent into madness, he began wearing a dark hooded robe along with a plague doctor's mask to shield himself from his own toxins.

### Personality

Before falling to darkness, Trousseau was a kind, deeply empathetic young man who cared intensely for his patients. He was driven by an idealistic conviction to heal and "extend a helping hand to all those in need," often neglecting his own well-being in the process. His passionate zeal led him into reckless, self-destructive behavior—such as endangering his own life to gather remedies for his patient [Sally](sally.md). Furthermore, he was exceptionally unforgiving toward himself whenever he faced failure, a trait worsened by low self-esteem and emotional volatility.

His encounter with [Claude](../throne/claude_blacksnakes.md) in [Lostseed](../../locations/brightlands/towns/lostseed.md) shattered his fragile psyche. Corrupted by the Book of Night, Trousseau's desire to end suffering warped into a nihilistic obsession with death, which he came to regard as the sole true "salvation" for humanity. Sinking into despair and madness, he weaponized purple rain infused with poisonous [Shadow](../../world/lore/shadow.md) energy to eradicate suffering by annihilating life.

## History

### Early Life and Eir's Apothecaries

Before joining [Eir's Apothecaries](../../world/factions/eirs_apothecaries.md), Trousseau lived with his younger sister, [Yorna](yorna.md), who suffered from an incurable disease. Desperate to heal her, Trousseau took up the study of medicine, but failed to find a cure before she passed away. On her deathbed, Yorna told him: *"Though my life could not be saved, your hands are those of a healer. Help those in need."* Bound by this promise, Trousseau dedicated himself to the healing arts.

He later met [Castti Florenz](castti.md) and [Malaya](malaya.md) in [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md) while treating Lady [Rosa](rosa.md). Finding that they shared an identical dedication to helping others, Trousseau joined their traveling association and moved to their base in [Healeaks](../../locations/brightlands/towns/abandoned_village.md). He became widely respected for his reliability, kindness, and devotion, especially toward the local children.

### Corruption and the Fall of Healeaks

While gathering medicinal herbs together, Trousseau and Castti inadvertently entered the desolate town of [Lostseed](../../locations/brightlands/towns/lostseed.md), where they encountered [Claude](../throne/claude_blacksnakes.md), an operative of the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md). While Castti dismissed Claude's dark rhetoric, Trousseau proved impressionable. Claude presented him with the Book of Night, which thoroughly corrupted his worldview. Believing that existence was defined solely by agony, Trousseau concluded that the only true mercy was death.

When the rest of [Eir's Apothecaries](../../world/factions/eirs_apothecaries.md) departed on an errand, Trousseau lured the children of Healeaks away, poisoned them, and abandoned their bodies behind [Mount Liphia](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/mount_liphia.md). When the adults searched for their missing children and brought the corpses back to town, Trousseau unleashed his toxic Purple Rain from a high mountain cavern, poisoning the remaining villagers.

Castti and the other apothecaries returned just as the calamity unfolded. Following the smoke rising from [Mount Liphia](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/mount_liphia.md), they confronted Trousseau. Castti pleaded with him to stop, but Trousseau remained resolute in his madness. As Castti succumbed to the poisonous haze, Trousseau vanished into the smoke, declaring his plan to unleash his rain upon [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md) during its crowded royal coronation.

### Downfall at Timberain Castle

Enhancing the potency of his poison, Trousseau infiltrated [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md) and climbed to the highest open ramparts of the castle to cast down his Purple Rain. Castti—having survived the tragedy at Healeaks despite losing her memory—confronted him atop the tower. Mocking her efforts to shepherd citizens indoors, Trousseau warned that his poison would eventually penetrate every shelter.

Castti engaged him in combat and defeated him. His protective mask was shattered during the struggle, exposing him to his own lethal toxins. Mortally wounded, Trousseau questioned what it felt like for Castti to take a life, wondering how long she could uphold her ideals in a world full of decay, before succumbing to death.
