# Mikka Mishuyo

**Mikka Mishuyo** is a lowborn resident of [Ku: Castle Town](../locations/ku_castle_town.md) and the younger sister of the swordsman [Ritsu Mishuyo](ritsu_mishuyo.md).

## Overview

- **Age**: 17
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Ku: Castle Town](../locations/ku_castle_town.md)
- **Relatives**: [Ritsu Mishuyo](ritsu_mishuyo.md) (older brother, deceased), Father (oil peddler, deceased)

> "*War is something I've learned to live with. My brother... I know he killed a lot of people.... But I don't understand why. Why did we have to fight for so long?*"
> — Mikka

Her given name means "sky" in the ancient tongue of [Ku](../world/ku.md), the only legacy her impoverished father was able to bestow upon her before his death. Mikka carries a quiet, profound hatred of war in her heart. She tends tirelessly to the injured—not purely out of natural benevolence, but out of an overwhelming burden of guilt regarding the violence her brother committed.

### Appearance

Mikka has brown hair and wears a traditional red kimono tied with a golden sash.

### Personality

Mikka is gentle and empathetic, though forced from an early age to endure the harshness of military conflict and lowborn poverty. While she learned to live amidst constant strife, she remains heartbroken by its cruelty and questions why people must fight. Beneath her gentle demeanor is a deep resilience and devotion to the welfare of others, though she is prone to exhausting herself through overwork.

## History

### Early Life in Ku

Mikka was raised in Ku by her father, a struggling oil peddler, alongside her elder brother Ritsu. Her father was murdered by Ku soldiers after attempting to advertise his wares to them. While weeping at his grave, Mikka and Ritsu met young Prince [Hikari Ku](hikari.md), who came to pay respects and swore to bring the murderers to justice. 

As Ritsu and Hikari trained and sparred together throughout their youth, Mikka often brought them handmade rice balls, worried that they pushed themselves past their limits. During this time, Ritsu gave Mikka a pair of matching "reunion earrings," said to ensure that those who wear them will inevitably cross paths again, telling her to save one for someone truly special.

### Mugen's Coup and the Fall of Ku

Eight years later, [General Mugen](mugen.md) staged a bloody coup against King Jigo Ku. Mikka survived the slaughter largely due to Ritsu's allegiance to Mugen. Following his ascension to general under Mugen's regime, Ritsu visited Mikka to present her with a new kimono on the eve of Hikari's siege of the capital, promising he would return alive.

When Hikari's forces stormed Ku and defeated Mugen, Ritsu was killed in combat. Hikari met Mikka at the Crest of Heroes to offer his profound apologies for her brother's death. Mikka accepted his condolences with grace and understanding, recalling their first meeting at her father's grave and remarking that the sky above was clear and cloudless.

### Rebuilding Ku

During the reconstruction of Ku, Mikka threw herself into relief efforts, assisting the injured and impoverished until she was worn down by exhaustion and her kimono fell into tatters. Hearing of her dedication, the weaver [Garud Bristarni](garud_bristarni.md) in [Cropdale](../locations/cropdale.md) crafted a fine new kimono for her, which his daughter [Pala](pala_bristarni.md) delivered across the continent. 

Grateful for the kindness, Mikka hosted Pala in her home for a meal. Recognizing each other as kindred spirits, the two young women formed a lasting friendship. Before Pala returned to the Leaflands, Mikka gifted her one of her treasured reunion earrings.
