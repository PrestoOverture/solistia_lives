# The Slaver

> *"Even the most disciplined are exposed by their own scent. Humans are overcome with fear before their demise, and their sweat reeks something awful."*  
> — The Slaver

**The Slaver** is a character in *Octopath Traveler II*. A member of the [Blacksnakes](../world/blacksnakes.md) syndicate in charge of human trafficking, he serves as an antagonist encountered by [Throné Anguis](throne.md) during her hunt for [Mother](mother.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: Unknown
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Affiliation**: [Blacksnakes](../world/blacksnakes.md)
- **Occupation**: Human trafficker

### Appearance
The Slaver has short black hair and wears a plain white collared shirt over dark trousers.

### Personality
The Slaver is a cruel, calculating criminal who derives sadistic satisfaction from orchestrating deadly gambles with human lives. He summary executes anyone who refuses to participate in his lethal parlor games. He possesses an exceptionally acute olfactory sense, claiming he can smell the stench of mortal fear sweating out of victims facing death, which he uses to detect poisoned drink.

Despite his brutality, he adheres to a strict fatalistic honor code. When bested fairly in a gamble of life and death, he accepts his loss without resistance or deceit, upholding his end of the bargain to the very end.

## History

### Blacksnakes
The Slaver's origins and true name are unrecorded. Within the Blacksnakes organization, he managed the capture and sale of human captives to outside buyers. For his own amusement and criminal dealings, he established "Death's Table," a lethal drinking game in which players took turns drinking from a selection of cups, at least one of which was laced with deadly poison.

### Throné's Tale
While tracking down Mother to obtain her collar key, Throné confronts The Slaver to extract Mother's whereabouts. Intrigued by her absolute lack of fearful scent, The Slaver refuses to speak unless Throné wagers her life at Death's Table.

Throughout the game, he attempts to intimidate and unsettle her. When the contest narrows down to the final two cups, Throné calmly selects the safe cup, leaving The Slaver with the poisoned vessel. Honoring the rules of the game, The Slaver consumes the poison without hesitation. As the toxin paralyzes his body, he reveals Mother's location to Throné, laments the senseless internal war consuming the Blacksnakes and his own inability to ever achieve freedom from the syndicate's mastermind, and wishes Throné well before succumbing to death.
