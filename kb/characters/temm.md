# Temm

**Temm** is a character in *Octopath Traveler II*. He was a ten-year-old boy from the village of [Healeaks](../locations/abandoned_village.md) and the closest companion of [Sally](sally.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 10
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Deceased
- **Location**: [Healeaks](../locations/abandoned_village.md)
- **Relationships**: [Sally](sally.md) (close friend)

### Appearance
Temm was a young boy with brown hair, dressed in simple gray clothing.

### Personality
Temm was deeply devoted to Sally, attuned to the loneliness she concealed and eager to support her. He felt frequent frustration whenever Sally treated him like an annoying younger brother rather than an equal friend.

## History

### Background
Temm grew up in the peaceful village of Healeaks alongside Sally. When Sally suffered severe complications and collapsed from an untreated injury, Temm cried out for help, alerting [Eir's Apothecaries](../world/eirs_apothecaries.md). He watched with relief as [Castti Florenz](castti.md) and [Trousseau](trousseau.md) saved Sally's life using Santanejo Blossoms.

### Death
Years later, while Castti and the majority of Eir's Apothecaries were away on a medical mission, Trousseau—having succumbed to nihilistic madness under the influence of the [Moonshade Order](../world/moonshade_order.md)—lured Temm and Sally up the mountain overlooking the village. There, Trousseau poisoned both children, killing them.

Their disappearance was orchestrated as bait to lure the remaining villagers out into the mountain paths during toxic purple rainfall. The search party found Temm and Sally's bodies shortly before succumbing to the lethal rain themselves, resulting in the annihilation of Healeaks.
