# Hermes

> *"Ahaha... Hello, my little fishies... Are you having the time of your lives this evening?"*
> — Hermes

**Hermes** is a 24-year-old dancer based in [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md), acclaimed as the "Goddess of Canalbrine." Renowned as one of the five most beautiful and talented dancers in Solistia, her popularity rivals that of the superstar [Dolcinaea Luciel](dolcinaea_luciel.md). Despite reaching the pinnacle of celebrity, she remains deeply grateful to her fans, gladly performing in humble local taverns to bring joy to ordinary patrons. She has a 26-year-old older sister who works as a scholar in [Montwise](../locations/montwise.md) and offers her ongoing support.

## Profile

### Appearance
Hermes has long, flowing black hair and performs in striking crimson dancer's attire. She carries a dancer's dagger for self-defense.

### Personality
Though her immense fame and theatrical moniker might suggest arrogance, Hermes is gracious, fair-minded, and grounded. She holds deep artistic reverence for dance, describing her trance-like state while performing as akin to a dolphin gliding freely through an ocean of pure emotion. She affectionately addresses her audiences as "my little fishies" while denouncing those who cheat or corrupt the performing arts as "predators of the deep." She is fiercely protective of artistic integrity, displaying furious indignance toward anyone who attempts to rig competitions or exploit other dancers.

## History

Hermes honed her dancing craft at the Dancers' Guild in [Wellgrove](../locations/wellgrove.md) before rising to continental stardom.

One evening, while performing at the tavern in Canalbrine, Hermes was ambushed by [Vados the Architect](vados.md), an agent of the [Moonshade Order](../world/moonshade_order.md) seeking to murder her as part of a blood ritual. She was saved when Sanctum Knight [Crick Wellsley](crick_wellsley.md) and Inquisitor [Temenos Mistral](temenos.md) intervened, subduing the assassin and exposing the conspiracy.

Later, Hermes was invited to perform at the [Stage of the Moon and Sun](../locations/stage_of_the_moon_and_sun.md) during the [Grand Gala](../world/grand_gala.md), serving as the featured opening act preceding the climactic dance between [Agnea Bristarni](agnea.md) and [Dolcinaea Luciel](dolcinaea_luciel.md).

Subsequently, Hermes entered a prestigious dancing competition in [Merry Hills](../locations/merry_hills.md). During the event, a promising young dancer named [Laila](laila.md) took the stage but suffered multiple humiliating falls due to uncontrollable slipping. Troubled by the spectacle, Hermes sought out Laila on the bridge overlooking the town to check on her well-being. Examining Laila's footwear, Hermes discovered that the slippers had been rigged to secrete slick oil. 

Returning backstage, Hermes and Laila caught Hermes's own manager conspiring with a corrupt judge to sabotage all rival competitors and guarantee Hermes's victory. Outraged by their betrayal of the art of dance, Hermes struck and dismissed her manager immediately. After the crooked judge was apprehended, Hermes confessed the sabotage to the judging committee, accepting full disqualification for her manager's crimes. Touched by her honor, Laila requested that Hermes join her on stage for a collaborative duet. Their shared performance captivated the judges and audience, leading the panel to award both dancers first prize.

During the grand celebration following the dawn, Hermes can be seen admiring and complimenting [Yomi](yomi.md)'s musical performance on the lute.
