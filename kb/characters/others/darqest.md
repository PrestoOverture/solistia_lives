# D'arqest

The **Archmage D'arqest** is a legendary ancient sorcerer whose mastery of the [Shadow](../../world/lore/shadow.md) and dark sorcery shaped the history of Solistia. Through unholy rituals and cataclysmic magical feats, D'arqest unleashed corruptive forces and created blights that continue to afflict the realm centuries after his demise.

## History

D'arqest's dark deeds and magical achievements spanned multiple continents and eras. Centered in Solistian myth and dark history, he was an ancient figure of potentially royal lineage, whose bloodline extends to his grandson [Claude](../throne/claude_blacksnakes.md), his great-granddaughter [Throné Anguis](../travelers/throne.md), and those cursed with the shadow-tainted blood of Clan [Ku](../../world/nations/ku.md). Later dark scholars, including [Harvey](../osvald/harvey.md), revered him as an ancestor.

### The Kal and the Curse of the Beastlings
In the distant past, D'arqest visited the [Kal](../../world/lore/kal.md) people and attempted to summon an eternal "night," summoning hordes of ravenous dark beasts to overwhelm the land. A legendary hero named Kal took up arms against him, channeling the radiance of the [Sacred Flame](../../world/lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) to thwart the ritual and drive the sorcerer away. Amidst the chaos, D'arqest placed a curse upon the greedy humans who squabbled around him, transforming them into the [Beastlings](../../world/species/beastling.md).

### The Pit of D'arqest
Eight centuries ago, D'arqest unleashed a cataclysmic surge of magic that carved a titanic abyss into the earth, known thereafter as the Pit of D'arqest. Four hundred years after its creation, the scholar Gratton theorized that an event of such staggering magnitude could only have been wrought by harnessing the [One True Magic](../../world/lore/one_true_magic.md), sparking intense academic and mystical interest in the nearby [Great Wall](../../world/lore/great_wall.md).

### Resting Place
Following his death, D'arqest's body was interred deep within the [Duskruin Shrine](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/duskruin_shrine.md) in the eastern reaches of the [Wildlands](../../locations/wildlands/wildlands.md), beyond the town of [Gravell](../../locations/wildlands/towns/gravell.md). His tomb eventually became a secret sanctum for the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md), whose excavation of the shrine's dark relics inflicted mysterious afflictions, madness, and misfortune upon the people of Gravell.
