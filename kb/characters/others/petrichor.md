# Petrichor

"*I met [Arcanette](../temenos/arcanette.md) when I was young, and it was then that I swore myself to her service. Ever since, I have hunted for her and her alone, even slaying the legendary monster known as the 'Guardian of the First Flame' at her behest. Sometimes, I hunted people too, like the time I came to possess the Darkblood Bow. Once again, my lady has use of me, and so I shall gladly obey.*" —Petrichor

**Petrichor**, known as the **Dark Hunter**, is an operative of the [Moonshade Order](../../world/factions/moonshade_order.md) and the primary instigator behind the catastrophic events unfolding throughout [Ochette](../ochette/ochette.md)'s storyline.

## Profile

### Personality
Petrichor was entirely consumed by fanatical loyalty to [Arcanette](../temenos/arcanette.md) and her nihilistic "Great Wish" to destroy the world. Viewing herself solely as an instrument of Arcanette's will, she felt neither remorse nor hesitation in hunting beasts or butchering humans, ultimately laying down her own life without hesitation as an offering to darkness.

## History

### The Hunter of Arcanette
Taken in by Arcanette as a youth, Petrichor swore eternal service to the immortal sorceress. Operating as Arcanette's personal hunter, Petrichor was dispatched on lethal assignments across the realm. At Arcanette's behest, she hunted down and slew the legendary Guardian of the First Flame. She also hunted human quarry, tracking down the Inquisitor [Roi](../temenos/roi.md) to seize the Darkblood Bow, slaying him in the process.

### Plot Against the Wardenbeasts
To prepare the way for the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../../world/lore/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md), Petrichor worked behind the scenes to disrupt the guardian beasts of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md). She targeted the [Creatures of Legend](../../world/lore/creatures_of_legend.md), mortally wounding [Cateracta](../../world/beasts/cateracta.md) and crushing the egg of [Glacis](../../world/beasts/glacis.md).

Ten years prior to the main story, Petrichor captured Ochette's unchosen companion beast (either [Mahina](../ochette/mahina.md) or [Akalā](../ochette/akala.md)) on Toto'haha and delivered the creature to [Harvey](../osvald/harvey.md). Under Harvey's cruel experiments, the animal was twisted by suffering and malice, mutating into the monstrous [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../../world/beasts/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md).

### Sacrifice on Toto'haha
During the culmination of the Night of the Scarlet Moon, Petrichor carried out her final directive. She presented herself to the Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon at the altar of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md)'s [Sacred Flame](../../world/lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md), willingly allowing the beast to slaughter her so that her sacrifice would snuff out the holy flame. Petrichor was the only prominent operative of the Moonshade Order who never directly crossed paths with the eight travelers while alive.

During the subsequent [Journey for the Dawn](../../stories/main/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md), a corrupted incarnation known as the [Grotesque Monster](../../world/beasts/grotesque_monster.md)—wielding the Black Bow—guarded the darkened flame where Petrichor fell.
