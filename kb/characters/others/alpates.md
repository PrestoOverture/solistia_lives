# Alpates

**Alpates** is a secluded descendant of the [Lumina Clan](../../world/factions/lumina_clan.md) whose lineage traces directly back to the god [Aelfric](../../world/gods/aelfric.md). She plays a pivotal role in the Crossed Paths journey of [Temenos Mistral](../temenos/temenos.md) and [Throné Anguis](../throne/throne.md).

## Profile

- **Heritage**: [Lumina Clan](../../world/factions/lumina_clan.md)
- **Status**: Deceased

### Appearance
Alpates is a frail woman with brown hair, dressed in simple dark robes.

### Personality
Raised in absolute isolation, Alpates is fragile and largely unfamiliar with the wider world. Despite her gentle and trusting nature, she possesses fierce spiritual devotion and mental fortitude: she places immediate faith in those who carry a pure flame in their hearts, and when captured by malicious forces, she endures torture and execution rather than betray her sacred charge.

## Background & Heritage

According to the scripture of the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../../world/factions/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md), [Aelfric](../../world/gods/aelfric.md) the Flamebringer once left a divine being named Alpates in Solistia to live alongside humankind. She became the matriarch of the Lumina Clan, a bloodline blessed with the blood of Aelfric. When the first Alpates died, her children were plunged into sorrow, prompting Aelfric to fashion a sacred mirror from her remains whose radiance soothed the clan's grief.

The Alpates encountered in Solistia is a modern descendant bearing both the sacred name and the divine bloodline. She was sheltered and hidden away from the world within a secret chamber high above the [Flamechurch Cathedral](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch_cathedral.md), protected by [Pontiff Jörg](../temenos/jorg.md).

## Story

During their investigation in [Flamechurch](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch.md), Temenos and Throné follow rumors of a holy relic named "Alpates." Uncovering a concealed stairway in the pontiff's chambers, they enter the upper loft and discover Alpates living in seclusion. Sensing the burning purity of the flame within their spirits, Alpates entrusts them with a fragment of an ancient, cloudy mirror before leaving the cathedral to reunite with her clan and complete her mission.

Later, in [Conning Creek](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek_harbor.md), Temenos and Throné discover Alpates's lifeless body held inside an isolated shack. Her hands and feet were bound, indicating she had been interrogated and executed when she refused to speak. Inspecting her clothing, Throné discovers a scrap of parchment sewn into a hidden seam.

The parchment points toward the [Cavern of the Moon and Sun](../../locations/harborlands/dungeons/cavern_of_the_moon_and_sun.md) with the phrase "where day meets night." Within the cavern's depths, Temenos and Throné uncover the second fragment of the mirror that Alpates had secreted away. When joined, the two shards restore the sacred mirror whole. Honoring her sacrifice, the companions vow to carry out her mission.

During the [Journey for the Dawn](../../stories/main/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md), the travelers bring Alpates's restored mirror to rekindle the four [Sacred Flames](../../world/lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) across Solistia, turning back the creeping [Shadow](../../world/lore/shadow.md).
