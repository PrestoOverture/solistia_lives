# Joe

> *"Barterin' or bruisin', no one can beat [Partitio](partitio.md) the Wild Stallion!"*
> — Joe, to the posse

**Joe** is a resident of [Oresrush](../locations/oresrush.md) in the [Wildlands](../locations/wildlands.md) and a loyal member of [Partitio Yellowil](partitio.md)'s posse, alongside [Nikki](nikki.md), [Ned](ned_octopath_traveler_ii.md), and [Harry](harry_octopath_traveler_ii.md).

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 23
- **Gender**: Male
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Oresrush](../locations/oresrush.md)
- **Occupation**: Miner / Laborer

### Appearance
Joe has short black hair and a muscular build developed from years of heavy mining labor. He wears an orange hat, a white collared work shirt, and blue denim overalls.

### Personality
Joe is a vivacious, spirited young man who speaks his mind plainly, occasionally cursing when pushed to desperation. Confident in his storytelling abilities, he is fiercely loyal to his friends. After losing a duel to Partitio in the past, Joe gained immense admiration for the merchant's strength and character; his most prized possession is a coin Partitio once gave him. Despite facing grueling poverty, he possesses strong principles, standing up against bullies like [Giff](giff.md) and refusing to surrender any more leaves to extortion.

## Story

As a longstanding member of Partitio's posse, Joe stood alongside Partitio to defend Oresrush's profits from Giff and his gang of thugs. However, when silver prices plummeted, the mining settlement fell into crushing poverty under the greedy landowner's control. Joe and his companions struggled to find work, scraping by on whatever meager scraps of food they could find.

Tensions eventually boiled over among the starving posse when Harry stole a bread crust from Nikki. Partitio intervened, having Harry return the crust before dividing his own portion of bread equally among them. When Giff arrived to mock their starvation, Partitio rallied Joe and the rest of the gang to take a stand.

Together, the posse marched on Giff's hideout and ousted him from power, uncovering evidence that the landowner had falsified contracts to cheat the townsfolk out of their rightful mining earnings. With the town liberated and on the path to recovery, Joe's friends later teased him and offered advice on how to woo a local woman who had caught his eye while tending to his injuries.
