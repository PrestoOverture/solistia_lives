# Porta

> "*All six divine weapons have finally found their way into your hands! These are legendary weapons, forged in the days of yore. Only the armsmasters are worthy enough to wield them. Take care that they do not fall into the wrong hands. Master the power of these divine weapons, and claim the title of armsmaster for your own!*"
> — Porta

**Porta** is a legendary blacksmith dwelling in the rough mining town of [Gravell](../../locations/wildlands/towns/gravell.md) in the Wildlands. Descended from a venerated lineage of master smiths, he serves as the custodian and restorer of the six ancient Divine Weapons of legend, guiding worthy warriors to awaken the secret skills of the Armsmaster.

## Profile

### Appearance

Porta is an elder craftsman with tanned skin and long grey hair. He wears practical brown work attire beneath a flowing white shoulder cape.

### Personality

Porta is fiercely proud of his craft and his family's sacred legacy. He considers the maintenance and restoration of the divine arms to be a solemn duty rather than a commercial trade; he has staunchly rebuffed lucrative requests from common soldiers to forge ordinary blades, reserving his hammer strictly for the work entrusted to his ancestors. He possesses a strong sense of responsibility, warning those who recover the relics never to let them fall into corrupt hands.

## Lore and Craft

### The Six Divine Weapons

According to Porta's teachings, the six divine weapons were originally forged in antiquity and bestowed upon mortal champions by the [gods](../../world/gods/gods.md) to vanquish evil and maintain balance throughout Solistia. The six armaments comprise:
- The Conqueror's Sword
- The Warlord's Spear
- The Dancer's Blade
- The Lionheart's Axe
- The Hunter's Bow
- The Spiritlord's Staff

In time, however, human greed and ambition corrupted the champions who carried them. As nations warred and dynasties collapsed, the weapons passed from hand to hand and were eventually lost to history, scattering across the continent to slowly rust and degrade into seemingly ruined husks.

### The Forge in Gravell

In [Gravell](../../locations/wildlands/towns/gravell.md), Porta established his forge to await the recovery of the lost relics. Prior to receiving visitors, his workshop was troubled by a thuggish debt collector who blockaded the doorstep until driven off by travelers.

Upon meeting travelers who present him with one of the rusted relics, Porta recounts the history of the weapons. Drawing upon secret metallurgical techniques passed down through generations, Porta cleanses and reforges each rusted weapon into its divine form, allowing its wielder to unlock the dormant techniques of the Armsmaster tradition.
