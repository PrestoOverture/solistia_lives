# Tiffany

> *"I went to speak to [Margello](margello.md), and it turns out... He's even more wonderful than you said he was!"*  
> — Tiffany

**Tiffany** is a character in *Octopath Traveler II*. A twenty-year-old noblewoman from [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md), she is the protagonist of the side story *[A Forced Hand](../../stories/side_stories/leaflands/a_forced_hand.md)* regarding an arranged marriage.

## Profile

### Identity
- **Age**: 20
- **Gender**: Female
- **Status**: Alive
- **Location**: [Timberain Castle: Town Square](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain_castle_town_square.md)
- **Origin**: Aristocratic family of [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md)
- **Relationships**: Unnamed father, [Margello](margello.md) (betrothed suitor)

### Appearance
Tiffany is a young woman with brown hair who dresses in an elegant gray-blue noble gown.

### Personality
Tiffany possesses a romantic, imaginative imagination paired with a spirited and competitive streak. Despite being prone to flights of fancy, she retains enough emotional maturity to evaluate herself honestly. She frequently bickers with her father due to their identical stubborn temperaments, though the two remain deeply attached. Initially resentful of being betrothed to a stranger, she defensively presumes her prospective partner must be an obnoxious boor until proven otherwise.

## History

### A Forced Hand
As the daughter of a prominent patrician family in Timberain, Tiffany's hand is promised in an arranged marriage to [Margello](margello.md), an intellectual researching at the [Montwise: Library](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise_library.md). Unwilling to wed blindly without knowing her fiancé's true character, Tiffany seeks information or an introduction to the young man.

Through traveler intervention, Tiffany investigates Margello's character. Upon meeting Margello or learning of his genuine nobility and scholarship, Tiffany is enchanted by his personality, enthusiastically agreeing to pursue a courtship with him. In an alternate turn of events where Margello travels to Timberain, the two converse honestly and conclude that they would prefer to part ways on amicable terms. Following the resolution of her engagement, Tiffany continues to reside in the [Timberain Castle: Town Square](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain_castle_town_square.md).
