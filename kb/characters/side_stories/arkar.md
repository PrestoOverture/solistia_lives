# Arkar

**Arkar** is an eccentric, brilliant inventor residing in a workshop along the [Eastern New Delsta Highroad](../../locations/brightlands/wilds/eastern_new_delsta_highroad.md) in the [Brightlands](../../locations/brightlands/brightlands.md).

## Profile

- **Occupation**: Inventor
- **Location**: [Eastern New Delsta Highroad](../../locations/brightlands/wilds/eastern_new_delsta_highroad.md)
- **Status**: Alive

### Appearance
Arkar has brown hair and wears spectacles beneath a pair of brass inventor's goggles perched on his forehead. He sports a distinctive feathered black hat, a tailored black suit coat, and brown-and-orange striped trousers. A pet dog often sits dutifully by the steps of his roadside home.

### Personality
Arkar possesses an inventive, sharp intellect paired with a grandiose ego. Convinced of his own unparalleled genius, he routinely boasts of his craftsmanship and firmly believes that humanity deserves to marvel at his creations. He is enthusiastic about taking on willing apprentices who can venture out into Solistia to gather rare mechanical parts and showcase his work to the world.

## Inventions

Throughout his career, Arkar designs and constructs a series of unique contraptions:
- **Changeable Catapult**
- **Springy Boots**
- **Critical Scope**
- **Elemental Bomb Bottle**
- **Tin Horn**
- **Hastening Hammer**
- **Arkar's Coil**
- **All-Purpose Tool**

## Quotes

- *"I hope you haven't forgotten your mission, my dear apprentice. The world needs - *deserves* - to know about my inventions! So go out there and make me proud! Build until you can build no more! Ha ha ha!"*
