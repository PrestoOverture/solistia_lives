# Margello

**Margello** is a 25-year-old scholar and the heir to a wealthy merchant enterprise based in the [Crestlands](../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md). Residing in the library at [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md), he is a central figure in the side story *[A Forced Hand](../../stories/side_stories/leaflands/a_forced_hand.md)*.

## Profile

### Appearance
Margello has neatly kept brown hair and dresses in refined, scholarly attire consisting of a brown shirt, black vest, tailored black trousers, and a red bow tie.

### Personality
Earnest, honest, and gentle, Margello is a studious and likable young man who upholds his family's long tradition of valuing scholarship and literature. His trusting innocence is so profound that his personal aide—originally a bandit who infiltrated the family's inner circle to plunder their fortunes—was deeply moved by Margello's goodness, renounced his criminal life, and chose to become his devoted servant instead.

## Story

Margello's family has long funded academic endeavors across the Crestlands, serving as generous patrons of the grand library in [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md). In an arranged match orchestrated by their families, Margello was betrothed to [Tiffany](tiffany.md), a noblewoman residing in [Timberain Castle: Town Square](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain_castle_town_square.md). Reluctant to wed a stranger, Tiffany sought information about her suitor.

When Tiffany learns of Margello's sterling reputation and travels to Montwise to meet him in person, she finds him endearing and agrees to give their courtship an earnest chance. In an alternate encounter where Margello travels to Timberain to see her, both parties converse openly and choose to part ways on friendly terms without pressure to wed. Following the resolution of their courtship, Margello continues his studies in the Montwise library alongside his faithful aide.
