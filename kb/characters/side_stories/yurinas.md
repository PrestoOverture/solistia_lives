# Yurinas

> *"I will do whatever it takes to keep my dear friend safe."*
> — Yurinas

## Overview

- **Age**: 25
- **Gender**: Female
- **Status**: Alive
- **Aliases**: The Unbowed Blade
- **Occupation**: Gladiator, arena champion
- **Location**: [Montwise: Underground Arena](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/montwise_underground_arena.md), [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md)

**Yurinas**, known in the arena as "the Unbowed Blade," is a character in *Octopath Traveler II*. Following the defeat of [Bandelam](../hikari/bandelam.md), she serves as the reigning champion of the [Montwise: Underground Arena](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/montwise_underground_arena.md), standing as the mightiest fighter in the tournament, utterly without peer.

## Profile

### Appearance

Yurinas wears a suit of knight's armor with a concealing helmet and a white cloak.

### Personality

Yurinas is a proud, self-reliant warrior who strongly prefers the role of protector rather than being protected. She cares deeply for her timid yet kind childhood friend, the Fainthearted Youth, who secretly harbors hopes of opening a restaurant and proposing to her. Desperate to keep him safe from harm and unwilling to bear the thought of hurting him in the ring, Yurinas conceals her identity as the arena's reigning champion even from him.

## Story

During the "[Tourney Champion](../../stories/side_stories/crestlands/tourney_champion.md)" quest, Yurinas first appears as an unnamed young woman on the ramparts of [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md), expressing distress that her companion is intent on entering the gladiatorial arena. In the [Montwise: Underground Arena](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/montwise_underground_arena.md), one of the travelers—[Hikari](../travelers/hikari.md), [Ochette](../travelers/ochette.md), [Throné](../travelers/throne.md), or [Castti](../travelers/castti.md)—knocks out the Fainthearted Youth to spare him from harm and is compelled to enter the ring in his stead.

Concealed behind her helmet, Yurinas enters the sands as "the Unbowed Blade" to defend her title in a one-on-one duel. After the battle concludes, Yurinas and the traveler retire to a private room. Impressed by the traveler's martial prowess, she removes her helmet and reveals her true identity as the anxious woman from the ramparts.
