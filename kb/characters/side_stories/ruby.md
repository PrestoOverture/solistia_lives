# Ruby

> *"I wonder what happened to the man my son tried to save... I hope he's alive and well."*
> — Ruby, in [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md)

**Ruby** is a 61-year-old resident of [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md) and the mother of the convict [Emerald](../osvald/emerald.md). Once part of a full household with her son, daughter-in-law, and grandchild, she was left to live alone after tragedy shattered her family.

## Profile

### Appearance
Ruby is an elderly woman who wears her graying hair gathered into a bun.

### Personality
Ever since Emerald's arrest and the subsequent deaths of his wife and young child, Ruby harbored deep bitterness and estrangement toward him, unable to forgive his reckless choices. However, upon learning of his final selfless sacrifice, her resentment dissolves into sorrow and understanding, allowing her to make peace with his memory.

## History

### Background
Ruby's son Emerald—named after a gemstone just like her—committed a crime and was condemned to the remote penal colony on [Frigit Isle](../../locations/winterlands/towns/frigit_isle.md). Following his exile, his wife and daughter passed away, leaving Ruby isolated and grieving in Timberain, nursing a deep grudge over the destruction of their family.

### A Message in a Bottle
Before sacrificing his life to ensure [Osvald V. Vanstein](../travelers/osvald.md)'s escape from Frigit Isle, Emerald wrote a final letter to his mother, placed it inside a bottle, and cast it adrift into the sea. The bottle was eventually recovered by the lighthouse keeper stationed at [Lighthouse Island](../../locations/sundering_sea/wilds/lighthouse_island.md).

When the letter is brought to Ruby in Timberain, she learns the truth of Emerald's end. Reading his final words, she realizes that her son gave his life to save another father so that he might reunite with his own child, viewing the deed as atonement for his past failures toward his family. At last understanding his remorse, Ruby forgives her son and finds closure, wishing well to the fugitive he died to save.
