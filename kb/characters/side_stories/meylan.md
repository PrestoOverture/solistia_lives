# Meylan

**Meylan** is a courthouse guard in [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md) who features prominently in a local murder trial side story.

## Overview

- **Age**: 28
- **Status**: Alive (imprisoned)
- **Location**: [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md)
- **Relatives**: Parents (deceased), Aunt (residing in [New Delsta: Backstreets](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta_backstreets.md))

### Appearance

Meylan is an adult man who wears the standard uniform of a Timberain town guard.

### Personality

Meylan is an introverted, solitary individual driven by an unyielding obsession with retribution. Outwardly responsible and dutiful in his role as a courthouse sentry, he harbored a decade-long vendetta against the judiciary that destroyed his family, demonstrating patient calculation in executing his plot.

## History

### The Sapphire Wreath and Vendetta

Fifteen years before the events of the story, Meylan's parents were wrongfully convicted of stealing the prized Sapphire Wreath and condemned to death by [Judge Astell](astell.md). Devastated by the execution of his parents, Meylan was taken in by his aunt in [New Delsta: Backstreets](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta_backstreets.md). Ten years later, consumed by a desire to avenge his mother and father, Meylan left his aunt behind without repaying her for her care, leaving her deeply resentful.

Relocating to [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md), Meylan secured a position as a courthouse guard to observe Judge Astell from close quarters. Over years of service, he observed judicial proceedings and waited for his chance. Conspiring with the infamous thief Nero, Meylan stole a sword from a local armorer to serve as an untraceable murder weapon, while Nero took the blame for the break-in. Seizing his opportunity, Meylan confronted and murdered Judge Astell before being arrested as a prime suspect.

### The Trial

Meylan was placed on trial for Astell's murder. At first, the prosecution lacked conclusive evidence, as neither the murder weapon nor a clear motive could be established. However, investigators eventually recovered the discarded blade downstream along the [Southern Timberain Trail](../../locations/leaflands/wilds/southern_timberain_trail.md). Meylan's estranged aunt provided testimony detailing his childhood vendetta, and Nero testified against him as his co-conspirator in the armory theft.

Confronted with the incriminating evidence, Meylan confessed to the killing and laid bare Astell's corruption. Meylan revealed that Judge Astell had been the true thief of the Sapphire Wreath all along: Astell had mistakenly described the wreath in his past notes as "black," a distinct visual truth kept strictly confidential within the royal line. Having provided the authorities with directions to the concealed vault where Astell hid the stolen treasures, Meylan was sentenced by the tribunal to life imprisonment.
