# Priestess Hinoekagura

**Priestess Hinoekagura**, also revered as the **Conjurer of the Tower**, is the guardian of the fifth and uppermost tier of the [Five-Tiered Tower](../../locations/hinoeuma/dungeons/five_tiered_tower.md) in [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md). 

## Lore and Legend

According to Solistian legend, Priestess Hinoekagura is the ancient conjurer goddess after whom the entire desert region of [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md) was named. As the master of elemental conjuration and ritual dance, she embodies the celestial and seasonal rites practiced in the realm.

Within the [Five-Tiered Tower](../../locations/hinoeuma/dungeons/five_tiered_tower.md), she awaits challengers at the pinnacle of the trial known as A Tower of Trials. During combat, she shifts between the guise of a serene young woman and a fierce onslaught form, channeling the elements of fire, ice, and lightning into her blade and invoking divine light and storm magic.

Upon being bested in ritual combat, Hinoekagura acknowledges the victor's strength and bestows the Proof of the Conjurer, imparting the forgotten arts of the Conjurer tradition to the travelers.
