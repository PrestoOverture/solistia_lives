# Alfred (Octopath Traveler II)

**Alfred**, initially known by the alias **Al**, is a mysterious thirty-year-old traveler who journeys across Solistia from a distant continent. In truth, he is [King Alfred](alfred_octopath_traveler_ii.md) of the fallen kingdom of Hornburg, hailing from the realm of Orsterra.

## Profile

- **Aliases**: Al, Alfred Hornburg
- **Age**: 30
- **Origin**: Hornburg, Orsterra
- **Status**: Alive

### Background
Al wanders Solistia claiming to be a prince from a kingdom on a distant continent across the sea, though he initially refuses to provide details about his homeland or the true purpose of his journey. During conversations, he accidentally lets slip that his native land begins with "Orst-", hinting at his Orsterran roots.

## Story

Al first encounters the travelers outside their starting town, desperately requesting assistance to recover his stolen satchel ([The Traveler's Bag](../../stories/side_stories/the_travelers_bag.md)). After the travelers defeat the bandit responsible and return his belongings, Al expresses his gratitude and continues on his way.

Later, Al resurfaces inside the [Montwise Library](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise_library.md) following the quest [Procuring Peculiar Tomes](../../stories/side_stories/crestlands/procuring_peculiar_tomes.md). He seeks a scholar capable of translating an ancient and dangerous grimoire in his possession, *[From the Far Reaches of Hell](../../stories/documents/from_the_far_reaches_of_hell_book.md)*. With the scholarly aid of [Georges Lazuli](georges_lazuli.md), the text is successfully deciphered. Once the contents are unlocked, Al takes the tome and abruptly slips away.

Guided by the forbidden knowledge within the text, Al locates and opens the [Gate of Finis](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/the_gate_of_finis_octopath_traveler_ii.md) to confront the ancient evil sealed within. However, the dark power of the fallen god [Galdera](../../world/gods/galdera.md) quickly overwhelms him. Forced to flee back to the threshold, Al warns the travelers against advancing before collapsing unconscious.

Following Galdera's defeat by the eight travelers, Al regains consciousness and congratulates them on sealing away the dark deity. In gratitude, he discloses his true name—Alfred Hornburg—and confesses that his journey across Solistia was dedicated to tracking down *From the Far Reaches of Hell* and halting the threat at the Gate of Finis. Having seen the world saved, Alfred vanishes within a pillar of light to return to his distant home.
