# Mahina

> *"Hoowoo… are you… a girl, too?"*
> — Mahina, before [Ochette](ochette.md) chooses her companion

**Mahina** is a female malamaowl described as serene in the grace of the moon, with wings that shape the air's currents and possess magical power. If selected as [Ochette](ochette.md)'s companion, Mahina acts as both a younger and older sister to her along her journey across Solistia.

## Profile

### Appearance
Mahina is a white-and-brown owl with a crescent moon marking on her chest. In her awakened form, she resembles a healthy version of the [Malamaowl of the Sorrowful Moon](../world/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md), bearing pink stripes along her feathers and a pair of pink leg cuffs.

### Personality
Mahina is friendlier than [Akalā](akala.md) and fiercely loyal, though she is less calm than him in tense scenarios. Whenever Ochette becomes impatient or impulsive, Mahina helps remind her of her duties and responsibilities.

## History

### Background
When Ochette was ten years old, [Juvah](juvah.md) took her to the [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../locations/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md) to receive an animal companion to aid her during hunts. Presented with two rare beasts—the malamaowl Mahina and the lājackal [Akalā](akala.md)—Ochette was permitted to choose only one.

If Ochette selects Mahina, Akalā is shrouded in darkness, goes berserk, attacks Ochette, and flees into the wilderness. If Akalā is chosen instead, Mahina is overtaken by darkness, attacks Ochette, and disappears into the wild, eventually becoming corrupted into the [Malamaowl of the Sorrowful Moon](../world/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md)—the final adversary Ochette must defeat to save the island.

### Journey with Ochette
If chosen as Ochette's companion, Mahina accompanies her throughout all of her story chapters, including her Crossed Paths chapters with [Castti Florenz](castti.md).

Ten years after being chosen, Mahina helps Ochette track down and capture the gluttonous [King Iguana](../world/king_iguana.md), which threatens the natural order by overconsuming prey. When Ochette is tempted to eat the captured lizard herself, Mahina reminds her that the catch belongs to the people of [Beasting Village](../locations/beasting_village.md).

Mahina then accompanies Ochette on her quest to gather the three ancient [Creatures of Legend](../world/creatures_of_legend.md): [Cateracta](../world/cateracta.md), [Tera](../world/tera.md), and [Glacis](../world/glacis.md).

During the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../world/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md), Mahina and Ochette plunge together into the ocean depths, where Mahina receives the power of the [First Flame](../world/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md). With its power, Mahina aids Ochette in defeating the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../world/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md), saving [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md) from the calamity.
