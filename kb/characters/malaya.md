# Malaya

> "*[Castti](castti.md)... You have the power to save lives. More than any other. That's why...you have to live.*"
> — Malaya

**Malaya** was an exceptionally gifted apothecary and a prominent member of [Eir's Apothecaries](../world/eirs_apothecaries.md). Originally a fierce rival of [Castti Florenz](castti.md), she grew to become one of her most devoted companions and confidantes. When tragedy struck the order at [Healeaks](../locations/abandoned_village.md), Malaya sacrificed her own life to save Castti, and later lingered as a guiding phantom in Castti's amnesiac mind.

## Profile

### Appearance

Malaya was a woman in her late twenties with brown hair tied in a practical ponytail and brown eyes. In life, she wore the standard uniform of Eir's Apothecaries: a vibrant blue hooded cloak over a dark blue tunic and sturdy brown traveling boots.

When appearing as a figment of Castti's subconscious memory, her appearance is noticeably desaturated—her cloak appears faded grey and her tunic a washed-out slate blue, reflecting her spectral, illusory nature.

### Personality

Intelligent, level-headed, and exceptionally skilled in pharmacology and healing arts, Malaya possessed a cool, pragmatic temperament that balanced Castti's boundless idealism. Though she initially saw Castti as an academic and professional rival, Castti's genuine, unwavering concern for patients gradually melted Malaya's competitive edge, earning her profound respect and loyalty. 

Unlike Castti, Malaya harbored a somewhat cynical and realist perspective on human nature. She was the first to recognize that their colleague [Trousseau](trousseau.md) had descended into irredeemable madness, astutely noting that his extreme moral innocence and purity left him tragically vulnerable to corruption and nihilism.

## Biography

### The Healing Troupe

Before joining Eir's Apothecaries, Malaya was known as an independent apothecary whose skill rivaled Castti's. After uniting under Castti's leadership as chief, Malaya traveled alongside her throughout Solistia, providing medical care and saving countless lives.

Disaster struck upon their return to their mountain base at [Healeaks](../locations/abandoned_village.md). They arrived to discover two village children dead and the local populace collapsing from an unknown, violent malady. Scaling the peak above the settlement, the apothecaries discovered Trousseau burning toxic reagents into the sky, tainting the rain clouds with a deadly purple hue. As the team attempted to intervene, the toxic mist overwhelmed them. When Castti began to succumb to the poison, Malaya administered Snowdrop petals to slow the affliction and carried the chief down the mountain. Finding that their pack horse [Jeyah](jeyah.md) had survived thanks to Elma's intervention, Malaya mounted the incapacitated Castti onto the beast and reached the harbor. Determined that Castti possessed the rare ability to save lives and must live to formulate a cure, Malaya secured Castti into an open skiff and sent her out to sea before finally collapsing and dying from the poisonous rain.

### A Memory's Guidance

Following her drift at sea, Castti was pulled ashore at [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md) with total retrograde amnesia. When Castti rushed to treat an ailing child in the street, an illusion of Malaya appeared alongside her to offer guidance. While Castti believed Malaya was physically present, outsiders did not notice or acknowledge her; the boy's brother, [Sesque](sesque.md), completely disregarded Malaya when she addressed him, and [Osvald](osvald.md) once remarked during their travels on Castti's curious habit of holding conversations with empty air.

"Malaya" assisted Castti in addressing the outbreak in Canalbrine, monitoring the sick at the tavern while Castti investigated the corrupted water reservoir. Once the immediate crisis passed, Castti questioned Malaya about their shared past, but the vision vanished before providing answers.

### The Truth at Healeaks

After Castti journeyed across [Sai](../locations/sai.md) and [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md) recovering piecemeal memories, the manifestation of Malaya reappeared, instructing her to journey to the deserted ruins of [Healeaks](../locations/abandoned_village.md). There, standing among the graves of their fallen order, Malaya gently confronted Castti with the truth: she was merely an echo created by Castti's fractured mind, and she, along with the rest of Eir's Apothecaries, had perished during Trousseau's massacre. With Castti's memories restored and her purpose reaffirmed, the specter of Malaya dissolved.

### The Final Remedy

During the climactic confrontation in [Timberain](../locations/timberain.md), where Trousseau unleashed his lethal purple downpour on the city, Castti struck him down but was exposed to the fatal toxins. As her strength waned, the spectral image of Malaya appeared one final time. Malaya guided Castti's mind through the remedies, plants, and treatments she had witnessed throughout Solistia, helping her synthesize the miraculous panacea. 

After dispensing the antidote to save the citizens of Timberain, Castti retired to rest in the tavern. In a peaceful dream, Malaya appeared alongside the departed spirits of Eir's Apothecaries, bidding Castti to soothe their souls and release them. With their legacy redeemed and entrusted to Castti, Malaya smiled and faded away for good.
