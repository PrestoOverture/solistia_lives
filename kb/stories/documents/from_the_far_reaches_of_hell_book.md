# From the Far Reaches of Hell (Book)

***From the Far Reaches of Hell*** is an ancient, cursed grimoire containing the forbidden rites of [Galdera](../../world/gods/galdera.md). Originally penned centuries ago in archaic High Hornburgian by Sage Salomon, this infernal text details arcane secrets over life and death, the creation of [demons](../../world/species/demons.md) via blood crystals, and incantations for opening the [Gate of Finis](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/the_gate_of_finis_octopath_traveler_ii.md).

## In Solistia

Through means unknown, the legendary text crossed the ocean into Solistia, where it came into the hands of an unstable scholar in [Crackridge](../../locations/wildlands/towns/crackridge.md). The tome's horrific contents drove the scholar to total madness, leaving him fixated on pain, death, and ruin.

During the search for lost knowledge in the [Crestlands](../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md), a librarian at the [Montwise: Library](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise_library.md) requests the retrieval of the volume alongside two other rare texts. The tome was deemed entirely unreadable by local academics, written in a cryptic script unknown to Solistia. The foreign youth [Al](../../characters/side_stories/alfred_octopath_traveler_ii.md) also inspects the book, confirming its language is alien to Solistian scholars.

After the grimoire is placed in the library's collection, Al enlists assistance to interpret it during the quest [From the Far Reaches of Hell](../side_stories/crestlands/from_the_far_reaches_of_hell.md). Utilizing decoding methods provided by the linguist [Georges Lazuli](../../characters/side_stories/georges_lazuli.md) from the [Nameless Isle](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/nameless_isle.md), Al succeeds in translating the ancient runes.

Armed with the incantations within, Al takes the grimoire and travels to the island housing the [Gate of Finis](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/the_gate_of_finis_octopath_traveler_ii.md), opening the dreaded doorway in an attempt to conquer the darkness beyond. Though he fails and requires rescue, Al later admits that he traveled to Solistia specifically to hunt down *From the Far Reaches of Hell*, using its instructions to locate and breach the Gate.
