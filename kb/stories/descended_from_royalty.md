# Descended from Royalty

**Descended from Royalty** is a side story in the [Brightlands](../locations/brightlands.md).

## Overview

In [Clockbank: Industrial District](../locations/clockbank_industrial_district.md), a man known as the Royal Descendant seeks proof of his noble ancestry, believing his forebears once ruled the region:

> "*My ancestors were royalty in this region! Dammit... If only I had some sort of proof...*"

## Resolutions

The truth behind his heritage can be uncovered through two distinct paths:

- **The Nyx Royal Family Tree**: In the [Abandoned Village](../locations/abandoned_village.md), a villager holds the Nyx Royal Family Tree. When presented with the genealogical record, the man is armed with definitive proof of his royal descent and vows to live a life worthy of his lineage.
- **The Nyx Family History**: At the library in [Montwise](../locations/montwise_library.md), a historian preserves the Nyx Family History. Upon learning the historical record, the man discovers that while he is indeed royalty, he is descended from a prince who destroyed the kingdom. Confronted with this truth, he vows to value his own life and deeds over his lineage.
