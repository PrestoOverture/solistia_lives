# The Baby's Coming

**The Baby's Coming** is a side story in [Cape Cold](../locations/cape_cold.md) within the [Winterlands](../locations/winterlands.md).

## Story

In the snowy seaside village of Cape Cold, a frantic villager is thrown into panic when his wife enters unexpected premature labor:

> *"We were told it wasn't due yet! We let our guard down! And our midwife is off in Flamechurch on a pilgrimage!"*

With no one in the remote settlement equipped to manage the sudden birth, the expectant parents face a crisis, as the local midwife had departed for [Flamechurch](../locations/flamechurch.md) on a religious pilgrimage.

Travelers make the journey to Flamechurch and locate the Midwife resting at the village inn. Alerted to the emergency, she is swiftly guided back across the wintry roads to Cape Cold. Arriving in the nick of time, the midwife attends to the mother and safely delivers the infant, bringing immense relief and joy to the grateful family.
