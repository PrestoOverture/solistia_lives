# Ort's Next Chapter

> "*Vados may have been the perpetrator, but he didn't work alone. Will you help me search this town for clues?*"  
> —*[Ort](../characters/ort.md)*

**Ort's Next Chapter** is a side story taking place at [Flamechurch: Cathedral](../locations/flamechurch_cathedral.md) in the [Crestlands](../locations/crestlands.md).

## Story

Following the murder of Pontiff Jörg and the unraveling of corruption within the church hierarchy during [Temenos Mistral](../characters/temenos.md)'s journey, the Sanctum Knight [Ort](../characters/ort.md) remains troubled by lingering doubts. Convinced that the architect [Vados](../characters/vados.md) could not have executed his subversive schemes entirely alone, Ort sets out to uncover any accomplices who might still be hiding within Flamechurch.

Ort's search leads toward the subterranean [Cathedral Cellars](../locations/cathedral_cellars.md), where suspicious figures linger near the entrance. Venturing into the dark passages beneath the cathedral, Ort confronts a gathering of covert [Moonshade Order](../world/moonshade_order.md) adherents. After overcoming the infiltrators, Ort confirms his suspicions that the cult's cell had been actively collaborating with Vados to undermine the Sacred Flame from within the church's own foundations. In honor of their shared oath to protect the faithful, Ort bestows the Sword of Oaths.
