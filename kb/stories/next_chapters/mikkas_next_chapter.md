# Mikka's Next Chapter

"*Really, there's nothing to worry about. Please, let me continue working.*"
—*[Mikka Mishuyo](../../characters/hikari/mikka_mishuyo.md)*

**Mikka's Next Chapter** is a tale set in [Ku: Castle Town](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/ku_castle_town.md) in [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md), occurring after the completion of [Hikari](../../characters/travelers/hikari.md) and [Agnea](../../characters/travelers/agnea.md)'s Crossed Paths.

## A Solitary Burden

In the wake of the war in Ku and the loss of her elder brother [Ritsu](../../characters/hikari/ritsu_mishuyo.md), Mikka throws herself relentlessly into labor. Though exhaustion and grief weigh heavily on her, she insists to those around her that she is fine and attempts to bury her sorrow in endless chores.

## Resolution

Seeking to ease her spirit, travelers arrange for [Pala](../../characters/agnea/pala_bristarni.md)—Agnea's younger sister from [Cropdale](../../locations/leaflands/towns/cropdale.md)—to travel to Ku. Pala brings a finely crafted kimono to Mikka, and a pair of stolen earrings is recovered from a local bandit. Meeting face to face, the two young women share a heartfelt meal together, comforting one another over their personal trials and forming an unbreakable bond of companionship.
