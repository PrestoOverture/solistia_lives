# Floyd and Thurston's Next Chapter

> "*Floyd has worked for days on end without sleep. I'd like him to rest, even if I must resort to...drastic measures.*"
> — *[Thurston](../../characters/partitio/thurston.md)*

**Floyd and Thurston's Next Chapter** is a side story set on [Roque Island](../../locations/harborlands/towns/roque_island.md) within the [Harborlands](../../locations/harborlands/harborlands.md), taking place after the conclusion of [Partitio Yellowil](../../characters/travelers/partitio.md)'s journey.

## Overview

On Roque Island, the inventor [Floyd](../../characters/partitio/floyd.md) becomes so consumed by his engineering work that he labors for days on end without sleeping. Deeply concerned for his health, [Thurston](../../characters/partitio/thurston.md) seeks assistance to force Floyd to rest, even if drastic measures are needed to render him unconscious.

Once Floyd is subdued and allowed to sleep, Thurston continues his role assisting the other employees across the island's facilities. In gratitude, Floyd's Neo Bow is obtained.
