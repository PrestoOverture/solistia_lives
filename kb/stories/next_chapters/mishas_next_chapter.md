# Misha's Next Chapter

"*Could you investigate Master Alrond's nighttime activities for me? Night after night, it appears he's sneaking out...*"
—*[Misha](../../characters/partitio/misha.md)*

**Misha's Next Chapter** is a tale in [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md) in the [Leaflands](../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md), taking place following the conclusion of [Partitio](../../characters/partitio/partitio.md)'s journey.

## The Aristocrat's Nightly Outings

In Wellgrove, the devoted butler Misha becomes deeply perplexed by the eccentric behavior of his master, the wealthy aristocrat Lord [Alrond](../../characters/partitio/alrond_rondwell.md). Night after night, Alrond slips out of the estate in secret, refusing to disclose where he goes or what he is doing, prompting Misha to request a discreet investigation.

## Resolution

Investigating among the townsfolk near the [Wellgrove: Department Store](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove_department_store.md), travelers learn of a mysterious figure in a brown coat who wanders the streets after dark, acting as an eccentric scavenger and clearing refuse. When these sightings are reported to Misha, he realizes that Lord Alrond has been secretly sneaking out on nightly "treasure hunting" ventures throughout the town. The discovery brings a smile to the loyal butler's face, reminding him of a fond and similar escapade from their shared past.
