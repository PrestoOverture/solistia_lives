# Shirlutto's Next Chapter

**Shirlutto's Next Chapter** is a side story in the [Nameless Village](../../locations/totohaha/towns/nameless_village.md) on the island of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md), accessible after the conclusion of [Temenos](../../characters/travelers/temenos.md)'s fourth chapter.

## Story

Following the climactic events that unfolded in the village, the beastling elder [Shirlutto](../../characters/temenos/shirlutto.md) finds himself pondering the complex inner world and motives of humanity. Yearning to understand human empathy and expression, he wonders aloud:

> *"Human feelings. Feeling what?
> Would know. But how?"*

To aid Shirlutto in comprehending the nuances of the human heart, travelers gather three distinct cultural artifacts from across Solistia:

- A **Lute** from a merchant in [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md), showcasing how humans convey passion, longing, and grief through melody.
- A **Paper Play** from a cleric in [Flamechurch](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch.md), demonstrating the use of visual storytelling and theatrical performance to impart moral lessons.
- A **History Book** from a merchant in [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md), recording the collective triumphs, failures, and memories of civilizations.

Upon receiving these works of music, theater, and history, Shirlutto immerses himself in their study. Delving into human culture stirs profound new feelings and insights within the beastling elder, fostering deeper mutual understanding between beastlings and humans. In gratitude, he rewards the travelers with the Befuddling Greatbow.
