# Lady Clarissa's Next Chapter

> *"I would love to study the island where the beastlings live. Though I should very much like to have a guide..."*
> — Lady Clarissa

**Lady Clarissa's Next Chapter** is a side story in *Octopath Traveler II* taking place in [Conning Creek](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md) within the [Harborlands](../../locations/harborlands/harborlands.md).

## Overview

Following the conclusion of [Osvald V. Vanstein](../../characters/travelers/osvald.md)'s and [Ochette](../../characters/travelers/ochette.md)'s journeys, scholar [Lady Clarissa](../../characters/osvald/clarissa.md) expresses an ardent desire to visit [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md) to conduct scholarly research into the island and its native beastling inhabitants. However, unfamiliar with the island's wilderness, she requires an escort to guide her safely across the sea.

Upon being guided to [Beasting Village](../../locations/totohaha/towns/beasting_village.md), Lady Clarissa carries out her field study of Toto'haha and reunites for a long-overdue conversation with her old friend, the beastling elder [Cohazeh](../../characters/ochette/cohazeh.md).
