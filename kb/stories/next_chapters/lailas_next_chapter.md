# Laila's Next Chapter

> *"I won't let that judge ruin the show with his dirty tricks! Let's go teach that guy a lesson."*
> — Laila

**Laila's Next Chapter** is a side story in *Octopath Traveler II* taking place in [Merry Hills](../../locations/crestlands/towns/merry_hills.md) within the [Crestlands](../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md), following the completion of [Agnea Bristarni](../../characters/agnea/agnea.md)'s journey.

## Overview

[Laila](../../characters/agnea/laila.md) returns to Merry Hills to compete in a prestigious dance competition, intending to use the prize winnings to aid her home village. Before her turn to perform, a contest judge approaches her and demands she swap into an official pair of shoes to comply with contest regulations. Reluctantly doing so, Laila takes the stage, only to repeatedly slip and lose her footing. Humiliated by the botched dance, she flees the stage in despair and retreats to a bridge overlooking the town's main road.

While sulking, she is approached by fellow competitor [Hermes](../../characters/temenos/hermes.md), who is deeply concerned by the strange performance. Inspecting the footwear, the two dancers realize that the shoes had been rigged to secrete oil.

Returning to the theater, Laila and Hermes discover the deceptive judge secretly conferring with Hermes's own manager. The two men had conspired to sabotage rival dancers in order to guarantee Hermes's victory—an underhanded scheme that infuriates both performers. Hermes immediately fires her manager, and the crooked judge is subdued.

Hermes confesses the plot and apologizes to the contest panel, leading to her disqualification. Refusing to let the contest end in disgrace, Laila invites Hermes to share the stage. In light of the judge's malfeasance, the competition is held anew, and the two dancers deliver an outstanding joint routine, earning both of them the championship crown.

## Aftermath

Following their victory:
- Laila resides in [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md), spending her days at the orphanage and visiting the eastern town square at night.
- Hermes returns to [Canalbrine](../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md), spending daytime at the tavern bar and performing on stage by night.
