# Veronica's Next Chapter

> *"What would make my lady happiest on such a momentous day? Perhaps someone close to her heart would know what best to give..."*
> — Veronica

**Veronica's Next Chapter** is a side story in *Octopath Traveler II* taking place in [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md) within the [Brightlands](../../locations/brightlands/brightlands.md), following the completion of [Agnea Bristarni](../../characters/travelers/agnea.md)'s journey.

## Overview

Following the grand dance festival in New Delsta, [Veronica](../../characters/agnea/veronica.md) stands near the city inn, fretting over what present to offer [Dolcinaea Luciel](../../characters/agnea/dolcinaea_luciel.md) on a momentous personal occasion. Desperate to choose something that will bring genuine joy to her lady rather than shallow luxury, she ponders consulting someone who knew Dolcinaea before her rise to fame.

In [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md), travelers consult [Platt's Wife](../../characters/agnea/platt.md) in the eastern district, gaining insight into *Dolcinaea and Gifts*. Learning of what truly matters to Dolcinaea from someone close to her childhood roots, Veronica prepares a gift from the heart.

Upon receiving the heartfelt token, Dolcinaea welcomes it warmly, to Veronica's profound relief and delight.
