# Melia's Next Chapter

"*It seems the Snowhares are up to no good again. Would you be so kind as to investigate them for me?*"
—*[Melia](../../characters/castti/melia.md)*

**Melia's Next Chapter** is a tale in [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md) in the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md), taking place after the conclusion of [Castti](../../characters/travelers/castti.md)'s journey.

## The Resurgent Snowhares

Following the passing of her mother [Rosa](../../characters/castti/rosa.md) and the restoration of her estate, young Lady Melia learns of rumors that the local bandit gang, the Snowhares, are stirring up unrest once more. Concerned for the safety of Winterbloom, she asks for an investigation into their hideout in the [Winterbloom: Thieves Quarter](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom_thieves_quarter.md).

## Resolution

Upon tracking the bandits down, travelers confront [Plukk](../../characters/castti/plukk.md), the leader of the Snowhares. Rather than plotting further violence, Plukk entrusts them with a "Letter from the Snowhares" addressed to Melia. Having witnessed Melia's compassion and steadfast belief in redemption, Plukk and her bandits resolve to renounce their criminal past entirely, choosing instead to serve as gardeners tending the grounds of the botanical estate.
