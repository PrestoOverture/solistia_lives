# Ghormf!

> "*His bi yiu foy "ghormf"? E zufg mris!*"
> — *Beastling*

**Ghormf!** is a side story set in the [Nameless Village](../locations/nameless_village.md) on the island of [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md).

## Overview

In the secluded Nameless Village, a lonely Beastling attempts to communicate, speaking phrases in the native Beastling tongue that human travelers cannot comprehend: *"His bi yiu foy 'ghormf'? E zufg mris!"* Struggling against the language barrier, the Beastling longs to find someone who understands his words.

In [Beasting Village](../locations/beasting_village.md), travelers locate a fellow Beastling who speaks the tongue and escort him back to the Nameless Village. Upon meeting, the two converse warmly in their shared language. Though travelers can only pick out a sincere "thank you" amidst the dialogue, the lonely Beastling is thrilled to finally be understood.

Following their conversation, the visiting Beastling chooses to remain permanently in the Nameless Village with his kindred companion, and travelers receive a Stupefying Stone Axe in gratitude.
