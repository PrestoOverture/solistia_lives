# Stolen Goods

**Stolen Goods** is a side story in [Oresrush](../locations/oresrush.md) within the [Wildlands](../locations/wildlands.md).

## Story

In the dusty frontier town of Oresrush, an honest peddler is driven to the verge of bankruptcy when his entire trade inventory is stolen:

> *"Some lying thief has absconded with the goods I worked so hard to procure! Oh please, you must return my wares to me or I fear I shall lose everything..."*

The peddler had poured all his labor and life savings into acquiring the merchandise, and losing his stock threatened to leave him destitute.

Investigating near the [Oresrush: Foundry](../locations/oresrush_foundry.md), travelers encounter a boorish merchant aggressively guarding the entrance to a private dwelling. Subduing the guard allows the party to enter the house, where they recover the stolen Stocked Goods.

Upon having his wares safely returned, the grateful peddler is spared financial disaster, allowing the upstanding merchant to continue his honest trade in town.
