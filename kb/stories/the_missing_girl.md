# The Missing Girl

**The Missing Girl** is a side story in [Gravell](../locations/gravell.md), located in the [Wildlands](../locations/wildlands.md).

## Overview

In the rugged settlement of Gravell, a Worried Mother is frantic over her young daughter's disappearance, fearing the worst:

> "*I hope she hasn't run off to the valley in the West Gravell Wilds...*"

## Story

Venturing into the [Western Gravell Wilds](../locations/western_gravell_wilds.md) and descending into the treacherous depths of the [Ivory Ravine](../locations/ivory_ravine.md), the travelers discover the Lost Girl stranded on a small islet amidst dark waters. Lurking in the ravine is a monstrous aquatic creature known as the Deep One.

Upon vanquishing the beast, the travelers retrieve a Cloudy Crystal Bracelet. Approaching the girl, they learn that she had wandered into the perilous ravine in search of her beloved older sister. Handing her the recovered bracelet—a solemn relic of her sister—the travelers guide her safely out of the ravine and return her to Gravell.

Reunited with her relieved mother, the child is restored to safety, known afterward as the Rescued Girl.
