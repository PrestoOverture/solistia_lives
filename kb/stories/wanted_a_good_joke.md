# Wanted: A Good Joke

> *"I'm sure one of my best friends'll have something that'll have her slappin' her knee..."*
> — Joe

**Wanted: A Good Joke** is a side story in *Octopath Traveler II* taking place in [Oresrush](../locations/oresrush.md) within the [Wildlands](../locations/wildlands.md).

## Overview

In the mining town of Oresrush, [Joe](../characters/joe.md) seeks to win the affections of a girl he is sweet on by making her laugh. Unable to think of good comedic material himself, he hopes to gather humor and guidance from his circle of close childhood friends.

Consulting his three friends around town yields a variety of perspectives:
- *Nikki's Special Joke* from [Nikki](../characters/nikki.md)
- *Harry's Self-Deprecating Joke* from [Harry](../characters/harry_octopath_traveler_ii.md)
- *Ned's Romantic Advice* from [Ned](../characters/ned_octopath_traveler_ii.md)

Joe adapts the jokes and courtship tips from Nikki, Harry, and Ned into an energetic performance. His comedic routine succeeds in making the girl laugh, breaking the ice and bringing the two of them closer together.
