# The Strongest

**The Strongest** is a side story in [Ku: Castle Town](../locations/ku_castle_town.md), located in [Hinoeuma](../locations/hinoeuma.md), taking place following the events of [Hikari](../characters/hikari.md)'s story.

## Overview

In the castle town of Ku, a local known as Childhood Friend is driven to distraction by two competitive companions—the Boastful Shieldman and the Proud Pikeman—who perpetually argue over whose martial prowess reigns supreme:

> "*There they go again. I swear, those two set my teeth on edge. I wish I could talk some sense into them, but they won't listen to a word I say.*"

## Resolution

Tired of trying to reason with them, the travelers challenge and knock both warriors unconscious. With both men knocked out, their relentless squabbling finally ceases. While the two are steadfast friends beneath their bravado, the humbling lesson serves as a reminder to show greater consideration for those around them.
