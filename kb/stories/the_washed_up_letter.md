# The Washed-Up Letter

**The Washed-Up Letter** is a side story in the [Harborlands](../locations/harborlands.md).

## Overview

A drifting bottle containing a sealed letter washes ashore along the coast, bearing a simple inscription:

> "*\"For Ruby in Timberain,\" it says here on the bottle. If it's not too much trouble, could you see that it gets to her?*"

## Story

Taking the bottle to [Timberain](../locations/timberain.md), the travelers deliver the message to Ruby, an elderly woman residing in town. Reading the missive, she realizes it was penned by her estranged son, communicating his remorse and deepest feelings. Moved by his words, Ruby finds it in her heart to forgive him.

The author of the letter is implied to be [Emerald](../characters/emerald.md), who was imprisoned on [Frigit Isle](../locations/frigit_isle.md), as Ruby notes that she named her child after a precious gemstone.
