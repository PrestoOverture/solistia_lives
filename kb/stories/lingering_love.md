# Lingering Love

"*Eugis... You left me with nothing more than this precious necklace. Whatever shall I do with all my unspent love for you?*"
—*Dismal Woman*

**Lingering Love** is a tale set in the mountain city of [Stormhail](../locations/stormhail.md) in the [Winterlands](../locations/winterlands.md).

## The Abandoned Lover

In Stormhail, a Dismal Woman languishes in despair, mourning her unresolved feelings for a man named [Eugis](../characters/eugis.md). After Eugis departed, he left her with nothing to remember him by except a Treasured Necklace, leaving her consumed by grief and longing.

## Resolution

The woman can find peace through two possible outcomes:

- **Parting with the Keepsake:** By letting go of the Treasured Necklace, the woman severs her attachment to the physical reminder of her past love. Relinquishing the token allows her to release her unspent sorrow and find emotional freedom.
- **Confronting Eugis:** Alternatively, travelers can learn from a local Sanctum Knight that Eugis has relocated to [Merry Hills](../locations/merry_hills.md). When Eugis is brought back to Stormhail to face her in person, the illusion of his empty romantic promises is shattered. Seeing him for what he truly is frees her from her obsession, replacing her despair with lasting peace.
