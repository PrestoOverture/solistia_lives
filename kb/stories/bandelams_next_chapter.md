# Bandelam's Next Chapter

> *"It won't be long till we say goodbye to this place for good..."*
> —[Bandelam](../characters/bandelam.md)

**Bandelam's Next Chapter** is an unused side story from *Octopath Traveler II* concerning the gladiator Bandelam and an escape plot from the penal colony of [Frigit Isle](../locations/frigit_isle.md).

## Story

The storyline centers on the imprisoned warrior Bandelam and his fellow inmates conspiring to stage a mass breakout from the island penitentiary of Frigit Isle.

The narrative outlines two contrasting resolutions:
- **Failed Escape**: Bandelam and his co-conspirators fail in their bid for freedom, remaining confined behind bars while order is reaffirmed across the prison facility.
- **Successful Breakout**: With outside assistance and guidance from Sacred Guard deputy [Cubaryi](../characters/cubaryi.md), Bandelam and his cohorts successfully break out of their cells and flee the island prison into parts unknown.

In the released game, this side story went cut and unplayable: Bandelam is instead encountered as a free man at [Crackridge Harbor: Anchorage](../locations/crackridge_harbor_anchorage.md), Cubaryi is killed during [Temenos](../characters/temenos.md)'s investigation, and Frigit Isle is shut down as an active penal facility.
