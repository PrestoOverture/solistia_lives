# Pilgrim Protection

> "*I'm pondering how best to protect the pilgrims coming to the cathedral. Perhaps if I procure some candles of the Sacred Flame...*"  
> —*Elder*

**Pilgrim Protection** is a side story taking place in [Flamechurch](../locations/flamechurch.md) within the [Crestlands](../locations/crestlands.md).

## Story

In the pious settlement of [Flamechurch](../locations/flamechurch.md), a village elder frets over the dangers faced by faithful travelers ascending the road toward [Flamechurch: Cathedral](../locations/flamechurch_cathedral.md). Wandering wild monsters frequently harass and endanger pilgrims along the mountain pass, casting a shadow over worship at the cathedral. The elder conceives a plan to consecrate the road by placing candles imbued with the protective aura of the Sacred Flame along the trail to repel beasts.

After gathering three blessed candles from devout clerics throughout the village church and cathedral grounds, the elder places them along the road. The holy light successfully drives the monsters away from the pilgrimage trail, revitalizing sacred travel and guaranteeing safe passage for devout pilgrims and local officials alike.
