# From the Far Reaches of Hell

> "*How might I possibly go about deciphering such a work?*"
> — *[Al](../characters/alfred_octopath_traveler_ii.md)*

**From the Far Reaches of Hell** is a side story set in [Montwise: Library](../locations/montwise_library.md) within the [Crestlands](../locations/crestlands.md), following the events of [Procuring Peculiar Tomes](procuring_peculiar_tomes.md). It serves as the direct prerequisite to [A Gate Between Worlds](a_gate_between_worlds.md).

## Overview

After acquiring the forbidden grimoire titled *[From the Far Reaches of Hell (Book)](from_the_far_reaches_of_hell_book.md)* for the library, the scholar [Al](../characters/alfred_octopath_traveler_ii.md) becomes determined to decipher its contents. However, he is stymied by its ancient, unknown script and seeks a way to interpret the text.

In search of someone capable of deciphering forgotten scripts, travelers journey to the [Nameless Isle](../locations/nameless_isle.md) situated north of [Beasting Bay: Anchorage](../locations/beasting_bay_anchorage.md). After defeating a [Gigantes](../world/gigantes.md) inhabiting the isle, they encounter the traveler [Georges Lazuli](../characters/georges_lazuli.md), who imparts critical knowledge regarding how to decipher unknown languages.

Upon returning to Montwise and conveying Georges's methods, Al experiences a sudden breakthrough revelation regarding the dark tome. Soon thereafter, Al departs the library to unlock the [Gate of Finis](../locations/the_gate_of_finis_octopath_traveler_ii.md) on the mysterious island in the Sundering Sea.
