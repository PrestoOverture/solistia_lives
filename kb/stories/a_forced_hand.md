# A Forced Hand

**A Forced Hand** is a side story in [Timberain](../locations/timberain_castle_town_square.md) within the [Leaflands](../locations/leaflands.md).

## Story

In the bustling town square outside Timberain Castle, a young woman named Tiffany despairs over an arranged marriage brokered with Margello, a suitor from [Montwise](../locations/montwise_library.md) whom she has never seen:

> *"How can I be expected to marry this Margello from Montwise when I've never met him!?"*

Her dilemma can reach resolution through two distinct paths:

- **Courting Through Mutual Respect**: Inquiries with Margello's Aide yield insight into Margello's Personality. When Tiffany learns of his admirable qualities and true nature, he makes a masterful and lasting first impression on her. Reassured of his character, Tiffany gladly agrees to begin courting Margello with an eye toward marriage.
- **An Amicable Parting**: Alternatively, Tiffany and Margello can be brought face-to-face to speak candidly. Upon conversing, Margello confesses that in his heart of hearts, he harbors no wish to marry either. Relieved to discover they share the same feelings, the pair part amicably, freeing themselves from the arranged match to follow their own paths.
