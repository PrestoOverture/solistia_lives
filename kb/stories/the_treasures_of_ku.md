# The Treasures of Ku

**The Treasures of Ku** is a side story in [Ku: Castle Town](../locations/ku_castle_town.md), located in [Hinoeuma](../locations/hinoeuma.md), taking place after the events of [Hikari](../characters/hikari.md)'s story.

## Overview

Following the restoration of peace in Ku, the realm's Treasurer is in profound anguish over the theft of two sacred royal heirlooms:

> "*I shall live my life in shame if I cannot reclaim the Dragon Vase and Phoenix Fan!*"

## Story

The stolen artifacts have been smuggled far across Solistia by unscrupulous black market dealers:

- The Dragon Vase is tracked to the port town of [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md), held by a suspicious peddler.
- The Phoenix Fan is located at [Crackridge Harbor: Anchorage](../locations/crackridge_harbor_anchorage.md), in the possession of an equally shady dealer.

After securing both relics and returning them to Ku, the Treasurer's honor is preserved, and he vows that Ku's four national treasures will be guarded with the utmost care from that day forward.
