# In Search of the Divine Weapons

> *"I had hoped to work on a divine weapon in my lifetime, but I suppose now that's a dream I'll take to the grave..."*
> — Porta

**In Search of the Divine Weapons** is a side story in *Octopath Traveler II* taking place in [Gravell](../locations/gravell.md) within the [Wildlands](../locations/wildlands.md).

## Overview

In the town of Gravell resides a retired blacksmith named [Porta](../characters/porta.md), who is descended from a line of legendary smiths capable of forging divine armaments. Having grown old without ever practicing his ancestral craft on a true divine relic, Porta lamented that his lifelong ambition would die with him.

When travelers present him with an ancient, corroded weapon, Porta recognizes its true heritage and refashions it into a legendary armament. Upon restoring the first weapon, he presents the travelers with the Proof of the Armsmaster, inducting them into the path of the Armsmaster.

## The Divine Weapons

Over time, six ancient rusty weapons scattered across Solistia can be brought to Porta in Gravell to be restored to their divine forms:

- **Conqueror's Sword** — Restored from a Rusty Sword.
- **Warlord's Spear** — Restored from a Rusty Polearm.
- **Dancer's Blade** — Restored from a Rusty Dagger.
- **Lionheart's Axe** — Restored from a Rusty Axe.
- **Hunter's Bow** — Restored from a Rusty Bow.
- **Spiritlord's Staff** — Restored from a Rusty Staff.

Once all six weapons are reforged, Porta fulfills his lifelong dream of carrying on his family's sacred craft.
