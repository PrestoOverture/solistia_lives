# For Whom the Clock Tower Tolls

> "*I was supposed to fix the clock tower bell on the double. I wonder if there's another way to get there...*"
> — *Clock Tower Technician*

**For Whom the Clock Tower Tolls** is a side story set in [Clockbank](../locations/clockbank.md) within the [Brightlands](../locations/brightlands.md).

## Overview

In Clockbank, the prominent clock tower's chimes have fallen silent. A local Clock Tower Technician is tasked with urgently repairing the mechanism, but finding safe passage to the interior works proves difficult.

By navigating the waterways beneath the town to the [Old Clock Tower](../locations/old_clock_tower.md), the true cause of the malfunction is revealed: a ferocious avian beast known as the [Heavenwing](../world/heavenwing.md) has nested directly amidst the central gears, damaging the clockwork and silencing the bell.

After Heavenwing is slain and the technician is escorted to the mechanism, he repairs the gears, successfully returning the familiar chime of the clock tower to the people of Clockbank.
