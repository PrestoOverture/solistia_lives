# Sword Hunter in the Decaying Temple

**Sword Hunter in the Decaying Temple** is a side story originating in [Sai](../locations/sai.md) within [Hinoeuma](../locations/hinoeuma.md).

## Story

In the desert town of Sai, an aggrieved young warrior seethes over the theft of his family's most treasured possession:

> *"I can't believe that bastard! He stole my father's sword...! He should be at the Decaying Temple in the Eastern Sai Sands, but..."*

The culprit is an infamous sword collector and thief known as [Karma](../characters/karma.md), who has taken refuge inside the [Decaying Temple](../locations/decaying_temple.md) across the [Eastern Sai Sands](../locations/eastern_sai_sands.md). The ancient sanctuary imposes an unyielding trial upon visitors: only a single traveler is permitted to enter its sacred halls alone.

Undaunted by the spiritual restriction, a lone warrior ventures into the ruins and confronts Karma in single combat. Upon defeating the sword hunter, the traveler recovers the stolen Keepsake Sword.

Returning the heirloom to Sai restores the young man's family honor and pride, rekindling his passion and inspiring him to pursue his martial path with renewed purpose.
