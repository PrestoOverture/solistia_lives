# The Traveler's Bag

**The Traveler's Bag** is a side story encountered near the outskirts of each traveler's starting settlement.

## Overview

Just outside the town exit, a young traveler named [Al](../characters/alfred_octopath_traveler_ii.md) finds himself stranded after an opportunistic thief makes off with his belongings:

> "*Damn, that rotten thief! He took my bag! I've heard this area is quite dangerous at night, so you had best be careful.*"

## Story

Under cover of darkness, the traveler tracks down the culprit—a suspicious hooded figure lurking near a campfire. When confronted, the man reveals himself as a cutpurse Brigand and attacks.

Upon being defeated, the bandit flees into the shadows, abandoning Al's Bag. Returning the stolen pack to Al restores his possessions and morale, allowing the aspiring adventurer to embark on his grand journey across Solistia.
