# Utterly Exhausted!

> *"Is there a bad spirit following me around...? Should I get one of those special invigorating stones? \*sigh\* I'm so exhausted... What should I do...?"*
> — Exhausted Man

**Utterly Exhausted!** is a side story in *Octopath Traveler II* taking place in [New Delsta](../locations/new_delsta.md) within the [Brightlands](../locations/brightlands.md).

## Overview

In the bustling city of New Delsta, a chronically fatigued man feels completely drained of energy, wondering whether a malevolent spirit is stalking him or if he should seek out a purported invigorating mineral to cure his ailment.

The situation can be resolved in two distinct ways:

- **Uncovering the Stalker**: Lurking behind a nearby tree is an aggravated woman who has been secretly stalking the man. When she is confronted and knocked unconscious, the man discovers her presence. Rather than becoming alarmed, he falls in love with her at first sight and confesses his feelings on the spot. His chronic fatigue abruptly vanishes as the two embark on an unsettling courtship.
- **The Miracle Stone**: Travelers can procure a Miracle Stone from a suspicious merchant in [Crackridge](../locations/crackridge.md) and present it to the man. Believing wholeheartedly in its invigorating properties, the stone revitalizes him, prompting him to re-evaluate his life and relocate to the tranquil countryside to start afresh.
