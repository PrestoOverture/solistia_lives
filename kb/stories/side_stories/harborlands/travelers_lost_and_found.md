# Traveler's Lost and Found

> *"Where could I have dropped it!? Somewhere in the Brightlands? Toto'haha? This town?"*
> — Young Traveler

**Traveler's Lost and Found** is a side story in *Octopath Traveler II* taking place in [Canalbrine](../../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md) within the [Harborlands](../../../locations/harborlands/harborlands.md).

## Overview

Standing near the bridge above the general store in Canalbrine, a distraught young traveler panics over a vital possession he misplaced during his travels between the Brightlands, Toto'haha, and the harbor town.

The lost item, a Bottle of White Powder, is recovered from the ferry staff at [Beasting Bay: Anchorage](../../../locations/totohaha/wilds/beasting_bay_anchorage.md) and returned to him. Upon receiving it, the traveler reveals that the bottle holds the cremated ashes of his deceased friend. With his friend's remains safely back in his possession, the young man sets off for [Hinoeuma](../../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md) to honor a final promise he made to the departed.
