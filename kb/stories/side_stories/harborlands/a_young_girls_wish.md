# A Young Girl's Wish

**A Young Girl's Wish** is a side story set in [Conning Creek](../../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek_outskirts.md) within the [Harborlands](../../../locations/harborlands/harborlands.md).

## Story

Among the gravestones in the misty outskirts of Conning Creek stands a severely ill, cynical child who has abandoned hope:

> *"I'm very sick, and pretty soon I'll... My parents said there are things worth living for, but they aren't fooling me!"*

Believing her death is near, she adopts an attitude of stubborn apathy to shield herself from despair. Beneath her detachment, however, she secretly longs to experience three simple dreams:

1. To read the sequel to her favorite book, *Adventures of an Apprentice Cleric*.
2. To taste a sweet specialty crepe from New Delsta.
3. To meet Roland, a celebrated floral designer.

To bring her comfort, travelers journey across the continent to fulfill her three wishes:
- At the Cleric Guild in [Borderfall](../../../locations/crestlands/wilds/borderfall.md), a resident cleric provides an account of the *Adventures of an Apprentice Cleric* Sequel.
- In [New Delsta](../../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md), a fresh Deluxe Crepe is obtained from a baker near the grand theater.
- In [Timberain Castle: Town Square](../../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain_castle_town_square.md), the renowned floral designer **Roland** is found beside the fountain and escorted to Conning Creek to meet his young admirer.

When the stories, sweet pastry, and floral artist are brought to her bedside, the young girl is deeply touched by the kindness of strangers. Experiencing firsthand the wonders of the wider world, her gloom dissolves, inspiring a fierce determination to overcome her illness.

## Epilogue

Her faith in life restored, the formerly apathetic girl sheds her despair and proudly adopts the outlook of a "Hopeful Girl," presenting the travelers with the Vest of Joy in gratitude.
