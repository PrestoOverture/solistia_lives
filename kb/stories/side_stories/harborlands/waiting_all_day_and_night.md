# Waiting All Day and Night

> *"I've been waiting ages... What if something happened to him?"*
> — Waiting Youth

**Waiting All Day and Night** is a side story in *Octopath Traveler II* taking place outside the tavern in [Canalbrine](../../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md) within the [Harborlands](../../../locations/harborlands/harborlands.md).

## Overview

At an outdoor table beside the Canalbrine tavern, an anxious young man frets that something terrible has happened to the companion he arranged to meet, having waited endlessly without any sign of him.

In truth, the youth is waiting to meet his twin brother. While both brothers had correctly agreed on the meeting spot outside the tavern, they had failed to specify the time of day: one twin arrived by day, and the other by night. Once the misunderstanding is unraveled and the brothers are brought together, the pair laugh off the blunder.

## Aftermath

Following their reunion, the brothers—now known as the Younger Twin and Older Twin—can be found together enjoying each other's company in front of the Canalbrine tavern.
