# A Genius Inventor

**A Genius Inventor** is a side story along the [Eastern New Delsta Highroad](../../../locations/brightlands/wilds/eastern_new_delsta_highroad.md) within the [Brightlands](../../../locations/brightlands/brightlands.md).

## Story

In a workshop along the Eastern New Delsta Highroad lives [Arkar](../../../characters/side_stories/arkar.md), an eccentric craftsman and self-proclaimed genius. Upon welcoming visitors, he bestows upon them the Proof of the Inventor, unlocking the unique Inventor profession and inviting them into his apprenticeship:

> *"Bring me the materials from the lists on my display cases and I shall create things beyond your imagining!"*

Across his workshop, display cases catalog the specialized materials, salvage, and curios required to construct his mechanical marvels:

- **Critical Scope**: Built using optical focus lenses fashioned from an Unerring Earring and a Critical Earring.
- **Elemental Bomb Bottle**: A volatile projectile crafted by infusing fire, ice, and thunder soulstones into a Rainbow Glass Bottle discovered in [Conning Creek](../../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md).
- **Tin Horn**: An acoustic gadget constructed from an Empowering Lychee, a Tin Toy obtained in [Wellgrove](../../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove_department_store.md), and a Mythical Horn preserved in the [Nameless Village](../../../locations/totohaha/towns/nameless_village.md).
- **Hastening Hammer**: A heavy mechanized hammer constructed by reforging martial weaponry: a Soldier's Bow, a Great Blade, and a Guardian's Axe.
- **Arkar's Coil**: A complex electromagnetic wonder built from Scrap Metal from the factories of [Clockbank](../../../locations/brightlands/towns/clockbank.md), an Ancient Cog salvaged from [Roque Island](../../../locations/harborlands/towns/roque_island.md), and Natural Magnetite mined from the [Unfinished Tunnel](../../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/unfinished_tunnel.md).

Once every component is procured across Solistia and all five contraptions are fully realized, Arkar formally recognizes the travelers as his accomplished apprentices, presenting them with his prized Mechanical Top Hat.
