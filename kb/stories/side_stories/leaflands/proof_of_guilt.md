# Proof of Guilt

> "*With the right proof, we could lock him up, but alas...*"  
> —*[Meylan](../../../characters/side_stories/meylan.md)*

**Proof of Guilt** is a side story set outside the courthouse of [Timberain](../../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md) in the [Leaflands](../../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md), forming the second chapter of a judicial trilogy preceded by [Proof of Innocence](proof_of_innocence.md).

## Story

Following the exoneration of a falsely accused man, [Meylan](../../../characters/side_stories/meylan.md) is found lingering outside the Timberain courthouse under the cover of night, frustrated by a criminal case on the verge of collapsing. A culpable defendant appears likely to escape judgment due to an absence of decisive incriminating evidence.

Investigation through town uncovers a remorseful old man near the tavern who carries knowledge of the crime. Guided by his conscience, the man reveals where critical evidence was stashed near the local inn: the Azure Sun Sword.

When the weapon is delivered to Meylan and entered into evidence before the court, it secures an indisputable guilty verdict against the defendant. Meylan rejoices that the rule of law has been upheld, unaware that the courtroom's gaze would soon turn upon himself in [Proof of Justice](proof_of_justice.md).
