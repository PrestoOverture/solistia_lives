# Through a Child's Eyes

> *"Mama and Papa don't get along very well these days. Maybe if we all get together, we can figure something out..."*
> — Brooding Girl

**Through a Child's Eyes** is a side story in *Octopath Traveler II* taking place in [Wellgrove](../../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md) within the [Leaflands](../../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md).

## Overview

In Wellgrove, a brooding young girl is distressed by the deteriorating relationship and constant bickering between her mother and stepfather. Longing for peace in her home, she hopes that gathering her parents in one place might allow them to talk through their differences.

When her mother and stepfather are brought before her, the couple is confronted by the depth of their daughter's sadness. Realizing the pain their constant quarreling has inflicted upon her, the parents set aside their arguments, resolving to foster a harmonious home for the young family.
