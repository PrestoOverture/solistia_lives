# Proof of Innocence

> "*If only there were some evidence to support his alibi, he could prove his innocence...*"  
> —*[Meylan](../../../characters/side_stories/meylan.md)*

**Proof of Innocence** is a side story taking place outside the courthouse in [Timberain](../../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md) within the [Leaflands](../../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md), initiating a judicial trilogy that continues in [Proof of Guilt](proof_of_guilt.md) and concludes in [Proof of Justice](proof_of_justice.md).

## Story

Outside the courthouse of [Timberain](../../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md), [Meylan](../../../characters/side_stories/meylan.md) frets over a pending trial in which a defendant faces imminent conviction for a crime he insists he did not commit. Although the man maintains his innocence, he lacks any tangible evidence or witnesses to substantiate his alibi before the court.

While scouring the city for leads, travelers encounter a sickly woman standing on a bridge in town. Upon hearing of the trial, she realizes that she had indeed crossed paths with the defendant at the exact time of the alleged incident. Escorted to the courthouse, her earnest testimony confirms the man's alibi beyond dispute. 

Exonerated by her words, the defendant is cleared of all charges, and Meylan expresses deep satisfaction that an innocent life was spared from unjust punishment.
