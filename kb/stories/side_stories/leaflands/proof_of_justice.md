# Proof of Justice

> "*These are false accusations; there's neither a motive nor a murder weapon. I wish there were a way to prove Meylan's innocence!*"  
> —*Courthouse Spectator*

**Proof of Justice** is a side story taking place inside the courthouse of [Timberain](../../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md) in the [Leaflands](../../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md), serving as the dramatic conclusion to the judicial trilogy preceded by [Proof of Innocence](proof_of_innocence.md) and [Proof of Guilt](proof_of_guilt.md).

## Story

Inside the courtroom of [Timberain](../../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md), a sensational trial unfolds: [Meylan](../../../characters/side_stories/meylan.md), the man who previously sought the court's justice in other trials, has himself been arrested and indicted for murder. An earnest spectator in the gallery passionately protests the charges, maintaining that Meylan is the victim of malicious fabrications, as the prosecution can produce neither a murder weapon nor a discernible motive.

However, uncovering the objective truth reveals a darker reality behind the self-righteous jurist:

- **The Murder Weapon:** Speaking with an observant reporter inside the courthouse yields a crucial deduction: an astute killer disposing of evidence in Timberain would cast the weapon into the river, where the powerful rapids would sweep it downstream. Searching along the [Southern Timberain Trail](../../../locations/leaflands/wilds/southern_timberain_trail.md) near the waterfall, investigators dredge up the discarded Stolen Sword.
- **The Motive:** A court clerk notes that Meylan recently lodged with his aunt in [New Delsta](../../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md). Journeying to the grime-slicked alleyways of [New Delsta: Backstreets](../../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta_backstreets.md), inquiry with a dour elderly woman exposes Meylan's hidden vendetta, uncovering the knowledge *A Killer's Motive*.

When the recovered blade and the motive are submitted to the court, Meylan's guilt is irrefutably exposed. His facade of impartial righteousness crumbles before the bench, and he is convicted of the killing, permanently removed from society as true justice is finally served.
