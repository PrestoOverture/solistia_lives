# The Soused Nobleman

**The Soused Nobleman** is a side story in [Cropdale](../../../locations/leaflands/towns/cropdale.md), located in the [Leaflands](../../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md).

## Overview

At the tavern in Cropdale, the Tavern Assistant struggles to deal with a belligerent Drunk Aristocrat who causes disturbances every evening:

> "*That noble's in here every night, carryin' on in the loudest voice you ever heard and givin' folks headaches. Gets mad as a hornet if you tell him to mind his manners, though...*"

## Resolution

After being knocked unconscious during the night, the nobleman is forcefully sobered up. Faced with the reality of his disruptive conduct, he listens to reason and opens up about his troubles, ultimately promising to cease drowning his sorrows in ale and bothering the patrons of Cropdale.
