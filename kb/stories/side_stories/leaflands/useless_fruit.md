# Useless Fruit

> *"I wish we could put these smelly, inedible well iris fruits to good use somehow, but...alas."*
> — Considerate Youth

**Useless Fruit** is a side story in *Octopath Traveler II* taking place in [Wellgrove](../../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md) within the [Leaflands](../../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md).

## Overview

In Wellgrove, a considerate young resident is troubled by the abundance of well iris fruit, an unpalatable and foul-smelling crop that grows locally. Unable to consume them, he searches for a way to put the plants to productive use instead of discarding them.

Inquiries among the farmers of nearby [Cropdale](../../../locations/leaflands/towns/cropdale.md) reveal the hidden properties of the plant: the pigments extracted from the well iris fruit function as a powerful natural insect-repellent dye. Armed with this knowledge, the young man begins developing the repellent dye into a marketable commodity for Wellgrove.
