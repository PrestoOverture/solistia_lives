# Ruffians' Redoubt

> "*All manner of ruffians hide along this road. They'll hurt my business for sure if they're allowed to continue pillaging as they please.*"  
> —*Traveling Peddler*

**Ruffians' Redoubt** is a side story taking place in the [Eastern Cape Cold Snows](../../../locations/winterlands/wilds/eastern_cape_cold_snows.md) within the [Winterlands](../../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md).

## Story

Along the windswept snowy trails outside Cape Cold, an itinerant merchant finds his livelihood in jeopardy due to a marauding gang of outlaws. The bandits have set up camp nearby, ambushing trade caravans and pillaging cargo at will, threatening to strangle commerce along the icy regional thoroughfare.

Venturing east into the [Ruffians' Hideout](../../../locations/winterlands/dungeons/ruffians_hideout.md), travelers breach the den and confront the bandit leader and his henchmen surrounded by stolen goods. After subduing the band in battle and routing them from their hideaway, peace is restored to the snowy road. 

Grateful that the commercial route is once again safe for honest traders and travelers, the merchant presents the party with a Peddler's Feathered Cap.
