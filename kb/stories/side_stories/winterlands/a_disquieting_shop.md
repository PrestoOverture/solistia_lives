# A Disquieting Shop

**A Disquieting Shop** is a side story in [Stormhail](../../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail.md) within the [Winterlands](../../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md).

## Story

In a local provision shop in the fortress city of Stormhail, a young shopkeeper's daughter greets customers with visible hesitation and distress:

> *"Ah, er, hel...lo!"*

Behind the counter hovers a clingy woman who poses as the girl's mother, closely watching her every interaction. In truth, the woman is an opportunistic thief who has infiltrated the shop and intimidated the child into silence.

When the suspicious intruder is confronted and subdued, her fraudulent identity is unmasked. She is turned over to the Sacred Guard, removing the threat from the premises and restoring peace and order to the family shop.
