# Plans from a Ruined Nation

> "*This stronghold has definitely seen better days. It needs repairs, and quick. I could try to fix it myself, but I'd need the actual plans from that era to do that...*"  
> —*Master Carpenter*

**Plans from a Ruined Nation** is a side story taking place in [Sai](../../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md) within [Hinoeuma](../../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md).

## Story

In the desert town of [Sai](../../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md), a local master carpenter inspects the ancient, deteriorating fortifications of the stronghold. The fort has suffered severe damage and requires urgent repair, but the complex architectural style dates back to the conquered Kingdom of [U](../../../world/nations/u.md), which originally held Sai before its fall to [Ku](../../../world/nations/ku.md). Without the original historical schematics, modern builders cannot fathom how to execute the intricate repairs safely.

The search for the lost blueprints leads across the sea to [Canalbrine](../../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md) in the Harborlands, where many craftsmen and citizens of U sought refuge following their homeland's destruction. There, a former carpenter of U still safeguards the Fort Orochi Plans, which preserve the kingdom's renowned engineering methods. 

When the plans are brought back to Sai, the master carpenter rejoices, now equipped with the precise engineering blueprints required to restore the historic fortress to its full structural integrity.
