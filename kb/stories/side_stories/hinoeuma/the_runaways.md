# The Runaways

**The Runaways** is a side story in [Ku: Castle Town](../../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/ku_castle_town.md), located in [Hinoeuma](../../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md), taking place following the events of [Hikari](../../../characters/hikari/hikari.md)'s story.

## Overview

In the castle town of Ku, a woman known as the Horse Lender grieves over the sudden loss of the animals bequeathed to her by her deceased spouse, contemplating abandoning her trade entirely:

> "*The horses my late husband left me are gone... Maybe this is a sign for me to hang up my hat 'n' find a new line of work.*"

## Resolutions

The woman's predicament can be addressed through two distinct paths:

- **Recovering the Horses**: Along the [Eastern Wellgrove Trail](../../../locations/leaflands/wilds/eastern_wellgrove_trail.md), the travelers discover the stolen livestock in the possession of an unscrupulous horse rustler. After subduing the man, the horses are returned to their grateful owner, restoring her livelihood.
- **A New Partnership**: In [Oresrush](../../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md), the travelers meet a compassionate Ranch Owner who dedicates his life to rehabilitating and caring for horses injured in warfare. Escorting the rancher to Ku, the travelers introduce the two, and she is thrilled by the prospect of collaborating with him to continue caring for the gentle beasts.
