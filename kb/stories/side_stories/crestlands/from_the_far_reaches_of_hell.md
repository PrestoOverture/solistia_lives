# From the Far Reaches of Hell

> "*How might I possibly go about deciphering such a work?*"
> — *[Al](../../../characters/side_stories/alfred_octopath_traveler_ii.md)*

**From the Far Reaches of Hell** is a side story set in [Montwise: Library](../../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise_library.md) within the [Crestlands](../../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md), following the events of [Procuring Peculiar Tomes](procuring_peculiar_tomes.md). It serves as the direct prerequisite to [A Gate Between Worlds](../sundering_sea/a_gate_between_worlds.md).

## Overview

After acquiring the forbidden grimoire titled *[From the Far Reaches of Hell (Book)](../../documents/from_the_far_reaches_of_hell_book.md)* for the library, the scholar [Al](../../../characters/side_stories/alfred_octopath_traveler_ii.md) becomes determined to decipher its contents. However, he is stymied by its ancient, unknown script and seeks a way to interpret the text.

In search of someone capable of deciphering forgotten scripts, travelers journey to the [Nameless Isle](../../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/nameless_isle.md) situated north of [Beasting Bay: Anchorage](../../../locations/totohaha/wilds/beasting_bay_anchorage.md). After defeating a [Gigantes](../../../world/beasts/gigantes.md) inhabiting the isle, they encounter the traveler [Georges Lazuli](../../../characters/side_stories/georges_lazuli.md), who imparts critical knowledge regarding how to decipher unknown languages.

Upon returning to Montwise and conveying Georges's methods, Al experiences a sudden breakthrough revelation regarding the dark tome. Soon thereafter, Al departs the library to unlock the [Gate of Finis](../../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/the_gate_of_finis_octopath_traveler_ii.md) on the mysterious island in the Sundering Sea.
