# Cathedral Window Repair

> *"If we had one large soulstone of each type—fire, ice, thunder, and wind—the window could be restored, but alas..."*
> —Cleric of Flamechurch

**Cathedral Window Repair** is a side story taking place in [Flamechurch: Cathedral](../../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch_cathedral.md) within the [Crestlands](../../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md).

## Story

Inside the historic sanctuary of Flamechurch: Cathedral, a resident cleric laments damage sustained by one of the cathedral's celebrated stained-glass windows. Renowned for its beauty and intricate spiritual craftsmanship, the window cannot be repaired through conventional glazing methods; its restoration requires the rare elemental pigments and magical luminance distilled from four large soulstones—one each of fire, ice, thunder, and wind.

Once the four elemental soulstones are gathered and provided, the stained-glass window is completely restored to its original splendor. Overjoyed by the revival of the sacred artwork, the cleric eagerly recounts the venerable history and artistic lore of the window in exhaustive detail to any who visit the nave.
