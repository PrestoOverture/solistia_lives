# Will Research for Money

> *"Surely someone will want to fund my research into the octopus-esque creatures known as octopuffs..."*
> — Octopus Researcher

**Will Research for Money** is a side story in *Octopath Traveler II* taking place in the [Montwise: Library](../../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise_library.md) within the [Crestlands](../../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md).

## Overview

In the Grand Library of Montwise, an earnest researcher is dedicated to investigating the enigmatic, cephalopod-like creatures known across Solistia as [Octopuff Travelers](../../../world/species/octopuff_travelers.md). However, his academic work is stalled due to a total lack of monetary funding, leaving him searching for a generous benefactor willing to sponsor his field research.

In [Conning Creek](../../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md), a wealthy octopus enthusiast residing at the local tavern possesses both a fascination with cephalopods and the financial resources to back scientific endeavors. When introduced to the researcher in Montwise, the patron is thrilled to sponsor the work, providing ample financial support. With funding secured, the researcher resumes unraveling the myriad mysteries surrounding the octopuff.

## Aftermath

Following the partnership, the octopus enthusiast relocates from Conning Creek to Montwise to closely follow the researcher's findings.
