# Tourney Champion

> *"Someone has to stop my friend before he's gravely injured in the arena!"*
> — Worried Woman

**Tourney Champion** is a side story in *Octopath Traveler II* taking place in [Montwise](../../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md) and the [Montwise: Underground Arena](../../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/montwise_underground_arena.md) within the [Crestlands](../../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md).

## Overview

In Montwise, a seemingly frantic woman pleads for someone to intervene before her childhood friend, a timid youth, is grievously wounded in the brutal underground gladiatorial games.

To prevent the fainthearted young man from entering the ring, a traveler knocks him unconscious and takes his place in the bout. In the championship duel, the traveler faces off against the reigning underground arena champion, [Yurinas](../../../characters/side_stories/yurinas.md). Following the battle, it is revealed that the champion is none other than the worried woman herself. She had orchestrated the intervention to protect her friend from harm while keeping her secret life as a gladiator hidden from him.

## Aftermath

Following the duel, the woman drops her facade and openly embraces her identity as Yurinas, while her childhood friend safely returns to Montwise.
