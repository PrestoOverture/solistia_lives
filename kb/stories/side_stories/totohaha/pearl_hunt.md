# Pearl Hunt

> "*The thread on my necklace broke and the pearls scattered everywhere! P-please! If you see any pearls, bring them to me!*"  
> —*Man Searching for Pearls*

**Pearl Hunt** is a side story taking place at [Tropu'hopu: Floating Theater](../../../locations/totohaha/towns/tropuhopu_floating_theater.md) on the island of [Toto'haha](../../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md).

## Story

While visiting the vibrant seaside settlement of [Tropu'hopu](../../../locations/totohaha/towns/tropuhopu.md), travelers encounter a distressed young man frantically searching the wooden planks near the floating theater. The cord on his treasured necklace had snapped, scattering lustrous pearls across the village, where they were found and kept by various locals—including a resident fisherwoman and an inquisitive [beastling](../../../world/species/beastling.md).

The young man had crafted the necklace specifically as a gift for a marriage proposal to his beloved. With a Pretty Pearl recovered and returned to him to mend the heirloom, the man takes heart and proposes. To his boundless joy, she accepts his hand in marriage. In heartfelt gratitude for saving his romantic endeavor, he gifts the travelers Cupid's Bow.
