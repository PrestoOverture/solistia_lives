# Stage Actors

**Stage Actors** is a side story set at the [Tropu'hopu: Floating Theater](../../../locations/totohaha/towns/tropuhopu_floating_theater.md) on the island of [Toto'haha](../../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md).

## Story

On the wooden stage of the Floating Theater in Tropu'hopu, an enthusiastic Stage Director searches for authentic martial talent to bring visceral realism to his productions:

> *"I want on-stage conflict so real and riveting it leaves the audience absolutely speechless! So I need actors who really know how to brawl."*

To prove their physical prowess, a traveler engages the director in single combat. Thoroughly impressed by their skill, the director immediately recruits the victor to star in a specialized theatrical debut, with the script adapting to reflect each traveler's personality:

### Temenos the Diviner
If [Temenos](../../../characters/travelers/temenos.md) takes the role, he stars as a psychic mind-reader named "Temenos the Diviner." When three hopeful citizens approach seeking his divine insight, Temenos systematically deconstructs their facades, laying bare the uncomfortable truths and dark secrets that ruined their dreams of happiness. The production concludes with a cautionary warning to the audience that no one's secrets are safe from his gaze.

### Hikari the Bodyguard
If [Hikari](../../../characters/travelers/hikari.md) performs, he is cast as "Hikari the Bodyguard," defending the play's antagonist. When scripted to duel the righteous paladin protagonist and lose, Hikari's martial discipline takes over. He inadvertently overpowers the paladin, breaking the script by winning the clash. Despite the unexpected deviation from the text, the sheer intensity and mastery of the combat bring down the house in roaring applause.

### Little Ochette
If [Ochette](../../../characters/travelers/ochette.md) stars, she takes the part of "Little Ochette," an apparently defenseless young [beastling](../../../world/species/beastling.md) guarded by a monster companion. When a hunter arrives to slay her beast, Ochette bares her fangs and lets loose a savage roar that terrifies the hunter—and several front-row patrons—right out of the theater. The director marvels at the twist, praising the revelation that the innocent girl was the deadliest predator of them all.

### Osvald the Thieving Gentlemage
If [Osvald](../../../characters/travelers/osvald.md) takes the spotlight, he is cast as "Osvald the Thieving Gentlemage," confronting bandits who preyed upon the destitute. Initially uninspired and on the verge of walking off mid-scene, Osvald hesitates when children in the audience begin passionately cheering for his character. Galvanized, he unleashes a spectacular blast of arcane ice to subdue the thieves. When the ecstatic director eagerly proposes making the gentlemage a permanent recurring theatrical star, Osvald silently shakes his head in refusal.
