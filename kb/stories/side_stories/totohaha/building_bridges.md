# Building Bridges

> *"I wanna know more about beastlings, and be their friend, but how?"*
> —Human Boy

**Building Bridges** is a side story in [Beasting Village](../../../locations/totohaha/towns/beasting_village.md) on the island of [Toto'haha](../../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md).

## Story

Within the divided settlement of Beasting Village, where historical tensions often separate human settlers from the native beastling populace, a young human boy longs to learn more about the beastlings and become their friend. Unfamiliar with their customs and fearful of causing offense, he is at a loss as to how to initiate contact.

On the beastling side of the settlement, a young beastling girl possesses an Amulet of Affection, a handmade token of goodwill. When the amulet is presented to the human boy on her behalf, the gift dissolves the barriers of unfamiliarity between the two children, forging a fast bond of cross-cultural friendship.
