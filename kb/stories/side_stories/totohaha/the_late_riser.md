# The Late Riser

**The Late Riser** is a side story in [Tropu'hopu](../../../locations/totohaha/towns/tropuhopu.md) on [Toto'haha](../../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md).

## Overview

In Tropu'hopu, the Master Fisherman is exasperated with an apprentice who chronically oversleeps and arrives late for work:

> "*That damn sluggard! Late again! I wish he'd just wake up on time!*"

## Resolution

After being knocked unconscious during the day, the Sleepy Fisherman comes to understand that his body naturally requires far more rest than the average person. Realizing this, he adjusts his daily routine and sleep schedule accordingly, allowing him to wake on time and fully regain his employer's trust. 

Following this change, he becomes known as the Lively Fisherman.
