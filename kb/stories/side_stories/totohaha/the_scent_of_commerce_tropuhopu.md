# The Scent of Commerce: Tropu'hopu

**The Scent of Commerce: Tropu'hopu** is a storyline quest in [Tropu'hopu: Shipyard](../../../locations/totohaha/towns/tropuhopu_shipyard.md) on [Toto'haha](../../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md), centered on the merchant [Partitio Yellowil](../../../characters/partitio/partitio.md).

## Overview

Upon visiting the shipyard in Tropu'hopu, Partitio witnesses a customer rejecting an unfinished vessel, scoffing at its steep asking price and deeming it junk. Trusting his own eye for value, Partitio seeks out the shipwright, [Terry](../../../characters/partitio/terry.md), to examine the craft for himself.

Terry initially turns him away, insisting the vessel is not for sale and expressing bitter doubt that her handiwork was ever meant to take to the open waters. Furthermore, she states that even if she were to sell it, the price would be a daunting 100,000 leaves.

## Resolution

Recognizing Terry's skill and the ship's true worth, Partitio purchases the unfinished vessel outright. Emboldened by his faith in her craft and equipped with the necessary capital, Terry is filled with renewed vigor and sets to work finishing the vessel.

Upon its completion, Partitio christens the ship **The Grand Terry** in honor of its creator. The seaworthy vessel grants the travelers the freedom to navigate across the [Sundering Sea](../../../locations/sundering_sea/the_sundering_sea.md) at will.
