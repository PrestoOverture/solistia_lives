# Reaching for the Stars

> "*Why do the grown-ups keep stopping me from leaving the village!? If the whole village went to sleep, I bet I could slip out with no one the wiser....*"  
> —*Ambitious Youth*

**Reaching for the Stars** is a side story taking place in the frontier settlement of [Gravell](../../../locations/wildlands/towns/gravell.md) within the [Wildlands](../../../locations/wildlands/wildlands.md).

## Story

In the remote mining outpost of [Gravell](../../../locations/wildlands/towns/gravell.md), an ambitious youth yearns to break free from the bleak isolation of his hometown to chase dreams of stardom, theatrical renown, and grand adventure. However, the strict and protective adults of the village—distrustful of the wider world and insistent that he remain—continually intercept him at the town borders and thwart his attempts to leave.

Frustrated by their interference, the young man laments that he could easily slip away under the cover of night if only the stubborn villagers and sentries keeping watch over the streets were asleep. Taking advantage of nighttime slumber when the remaining watchmen and guards are rendered unconscious, the youth quietly slips through the darkened gates undetected.

Breaking free of Gravell at last, the ambitious youth journeys east across the continent to the theatrical haven of [Merry Hills](../../../locations/crestlands/towns/merry_hills.md) in the Crestlands to pursue his aspirations on the grand stage, bestowing a Gaudy Hat in commemoration of his newfound freedom.
