# The Sword in the Stone

**The Sword in the Stone** is a side story in [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md), located in the [Winterlands](../locations/winterlands.md).

## Overview

In Winterbloom, a Troubled Woman finds a mysterious blade lodged firmly into a boulder in the middle of her plot, preventing her from tending her garden:

> "*I tried pulling it out, but it won't budge. Maybe I'm just not strong enough. I wonder if I can use one of those pointy things the miners have...*"

## Resolutions

The sword can be dislodged from the rock through two different approaches:

- **The Miner's Pickaxe**: In the mining town of [Oresrush](../locations/oresrush.md), the travelers obtain a Sturdy Pickaxe from a retired miner. By chipping away at the tough boulder, the sword is extracted cleanly from the rock.
- **Sheer Numbers**: By enlisting four townspeople to aid the travelers, the united strength of eight people proves powerful enough to wrench the stubborn blade straight from the stone.

With the sword removed, the grateful woman is finally free to tend to her garden. The weapon dislodged from the stone is revealed to be a ancient Rusty Sword.
