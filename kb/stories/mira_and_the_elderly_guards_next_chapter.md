# Mira and the Elderly Guard's Next Chapter

"*Could you help me find [Mira](../characters/mira.md)? I remember her saying something about a gondola...*"
—*[Elderly Guard](../characters/elderly_guard.md)*

**Mira and the Elderly Guard's Next Chapter** is a tale in the [Leaflands](../locations/leaflands.md), taking place following the conclusion of [Throné](../characters/throne.md)'s journey.

## The Lost Orphan

Following the downfall of [Mother](../characters/mother.md) and the disruption of the [Blacksnakes](../world/blacksnakes.md), the Elderly Guard remains in [Wellgrove](../locations/wellgrove.md), plagued by anxiety over the disappearance of [Mira](../characters/mira.md). The young orphan girl had wandered away from [Mother's Garden: Orphanage](../locations/mothers_garden_orphanage.md), mentioning a desire to see the distant gondola.

## Resolution

Accompanied by travelers, the Elderly Guard heads to the gondola station along the [Deserted Highroad](../locations/deserted_highroad.md), speaking with the gondola [Guide](guide_thrones_story.md) before crossing the deep chasm toward [Lostseed](../locations/lostseed.md). There they find Mira safe from harm. Reunited, the Elderly Guard brings Mira back to Mother's Garden, resolving to abandon the cruel practices of the past and dedicate the rest of his life to raising every child in the orphanage with the genuine love and tenderness of a parent.
