# Lighthouse Restoration

"*I can't believe it! The lighthouse's reflector is broken! Maybe another lighthouse has a spare...*"
—*Town Lighthouse Keeper*

**Lighthouse Restoration** is a tale in the [Harborlands](../locations/harborlands.md).

## The Broken Reflector

In the port town of [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md), the Town Lighthouse Keeper is thrown into distress when the lighthouse's primary reflector plate breaks. Without a functioning reflector to cast a guiding beam across the waters, passing ships face danger at night. Hoping that a sister installation might possess a replacement, the keeper seeks someone to locate an extra reflector.

## Resolution

Across the sea on snowy [Lighthouse Island](../locations/lighthouse_island.md), the Island Lighthouse Keeper possesses an Aelmorite Reflector as a spare component. Upon obtaining this plate and delivering it to Canalbrine, the broken lighthouse is restored to working order, ensuring that maritime vessels navigating the surrounding waters can sail safely once more.
