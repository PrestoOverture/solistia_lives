# The Mercantile Manuscript

The **Mercantile Manuscript** is a comprehensive historical and geographical compendium preserved by the merchant [Masoud](../characters/masoud.md). It details the natural history, cultures, industries, legends, and regions of Solistia.

---

## [Demihumans](../world/demihumans.md): A Study

Demihumans are sentient creatures that resemble humans, yet are entirely different beasts. There are eight major types recognized: the verminous [ratkin](../world/ratkin.md), the reptilian [lizardmen](../world/lizardmen.md), the avian [birdians](../world/birdians.md), the amphibious [froggens](../world/froggens.md), the simian [apes](../world/apes.md), the subterranean [moles](../world/moles.md), the feline [caits](../world/caits.md), and the cephalopodan [octopuffs](../world/octopuff_travelers.md).

Demihumans display a significantly higher level of intelligence than ordinary beasts and utilize crafted tools in their daily lives. Their true origins remain shrouded in mystery; historical records note that they seemed to appear suddenly across the world.

### On Caits

Caits are the most timid and skittish of all demihumans. According to legend, during an ancient war fought between humanity and demihumans, the caits chose to side with humankind, securing a place within human settlements following humanity's victory. However, their clever nature eventually provoked resentment and mistrust from their neighbors, leading to their ostracization by both human and demihuman societies. Labeled as traitors by all sides, caits retreated into deep isolation, venturing out primarily when tempted by caitnip.

### All About Octopuffs

Octopuffs are independent demihumans who abstained from the mythical war between humans and demihumans, preferring perpetual travel across the world until the end of their days. Regarded in popular folklore as symbols of good fortune, rumors claim they reveal themselves only to the pure of heart. Pragmatic travelers often use dark octopuff pots to lure them from their hiding spots.

### Riddles of the Demihumans

While ratkin scavenge human gear and birdians craft primitive stone axes, demihuman leaders often wear ornate crowns and accessories whose craftsmanship and materials match neither human nor known demihuman manufacturing techniques, fueling scholarly speculation that their ancestry may trace to another realm entirely.

## History, Myth, and Ancient Works

### The [Great Wall](../world/great_wall.md) in Myth and Legend

Standing guard over the city of [Stormhail](../locations/stormhail.md) in the [Winterlands](../locations/winterlands.md), the colossal Great Wall has long protected the region from outside invasions. Local myth claims the fortification was raised in a single night by the gods. Some legends maintain that the visible wall is merely an outer battlement of an immense subterranean fortress whose central citadel lies undiscovered. Approximately 820 years ago, a massive cavity known as the "Pit of [D'arqest](../characters/darqest.md)" suddenly opened in the structure, triggering the War of the Great Wall between the fallen kingdoms of [Fj'all](../world/fjall.md) and [Delstasch](../world/delstasch.md).

### Gates to the Netherworld

A century ago, the poet and adventurer Kaie recorded verses warning of an otherworldly gate:
> *"On an island lost in sea split twain, lies a portal to an alien plane. Beware! Be warned! Ye fools who'd approach, for none should draw near, this gate encroach."*

While long dismissed as poetic fancy, Kaie's confirmed travels have prompted scholars to consider whether his writing described an actual supernatural portal.

### Divine Weapons and Armsmasters

In antiquity, the gods entrusted humanity with six sacred armaments to defend against evil: the Conqueror's Sword, the Warlord's Spear, the Dancer's Blade, the Lionheart's Axe, the Hunter's Bow, and the Spiritlord's Staff. Those capable of mastering these weapons were known as armsmasters. In time, ambition and infighting scattered the artifacts across Solistia, though lineages of chosen heirs continue to secretly guard the martial techniques required to wield them.

## Arms, Craft, and Materials

### Weaponsmithing and Blacksmithing Guilds

Solistia's martial traditions recognize six orthodox weapon disciplines: swords, polearms, daggers, axes, bows, and staves. While officially codified under the religious justification that these reflect the weapons of the gods, the restriction also serves commercial monopolies held by blacksmithing guilds, suppressing alternative weapons like flails and whips.

### Enchanted and Battle-Tested Armaments

Enchanted weapons are forged either by folding magic into the metal during smelting or through decades of continuous bonding with a wielder of extraordinary willpower. When a weapon survives countless battles in the hands of a master without tasting defeat, it awakens into a "battle-tested armament," transcending its original metallurgy through the sheer accumulation of martial history.

### Sacred Ornamentation

True ornamentation refers to sacred jewels and talismans fashioned by dedicated craftsmen. Worn by leaders and heroes, these relics are imbued with defensive or augmenting enchantments, though the techniques for crafting them have gradually declined over generations.

### Soulstones

Soulstones discharge concentrated elemental magic upon suffering heavy physical impact. While historically thought to house nature spirits, contemporary research posits they may be the crystallized mineral remnants of magically dense monsters.

## Botany, Medicine, and Trade Goods

### Erbweed and the Currency of "Leaves"

Erbweed is a versatile medicinal plant capable of addressing varied ailments depending on its preparation, as well as serving as a culinary seasoning. Due to its scarcity and vital importance to healing, regional rulers historically stepped in to regulate and stabilize trade prices per leaf. Over time, the standardized exchange of dried medicinal leaves gave rise to the term "leaf" as Solistia's common currency.

### Concoction and Apothecary Arts

Attributed mythically to [Dohter the Charitable](../world/dohter.md), the art of concoction developed through centuries of human trial, observation, and self-experimentation. True mastery relies on diagnosing symptoms and calculating exact proportions; blending three ingredients marks a master apothecary, while four signifies genius.

### Sacred and Rare Flora

- **Grapes and Plums:** Celebrated for physical and spiritual restoration, ancient myths claim their cultivation was originally forbidden by the gods to prevent intoxication and militarized supplies.
- **Lychees:** Succulent fruits imported from the Outer Continent that awaken latent physical power. Because they resist conventional farming, superstitions labeled them "vampire fruit," spurring sinister rumors that they required blood to grow.
- **Rare Nuts:** Wild tree nuts that enhance physical and mental attributes, defying all agricultural attempts at domestic cultivation.

## Regional Histories and Lore

### [Hinoeuma](../locations/hinoeuma.md)
The desert region of southwest Solistia is defined by river valleys where barley and corn flourish, surrounded by vast arid wastes. Named after the legends of [Priestess Hinoekagura](../characters/priestess_hinoekagura.md), it has seen the rise and fall of countless kingdoms. The fortress of [Sai](../locations/sai.md) (formerly the Orochi Bastion) was captured from the fallen kingdom of U by [Ku](../world/ku.md) twenty years ago, while [Ryu](../locations/ryu.md) remains an essential trading oasis. The region is also the birthplace of "bottling," an art developed by the Rekohn Clan, who defended their lightweight water containers by hurling bottled poisons.

### The [Wildlands](../locations/wildlands.md)
A volcanic wasteland in northwest Solistia beneath Mount Fiatsuro. The harsh terrain is home to the golt, an oversized goat prized above cattle and sheep for its abundant milk, fleece, and meat. Mineral riches in the soil led to the founding of mining settlements such as [Oresrush](../locations/oresrush.md), established by silver prospectors [Papp](../characters/papp_yellowil.md) and [Roque Brilliante](../characters/roque_brilliante.md).

### The [Leaflands](../locations/leaflands.md)
A lush western territory centered around the kingdom of [Timberain](../locations/timberain.md). Rich soil supports extensive lumber and paper mills. [Cropdale](../locations/cropdale.md) is renowned for berries and an abundance of peach culinary specialties, while [Wellgrove](../locations/wellgrove.md) in the south grew wealthy by trading timber and goods with the desert clans, creating border friction.

### The [Harborlands](../locations/harborlands.md)
A winding southern coastline anchored by [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md), the launching point for the Age of Exploration a century ago. Its colossal lighthouse was built twenty years ago by refugee engineers from U using a polished mirror-sheen Aelmorite Reflector. The town serves as a major hub for missionaries spreading the faith of the [Sacred Flame](../world/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md).

### [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md)
A southeastern island territory home to the [beastlings](../world/beastling.md). The northern island was traditionally called Haha and the southern Toto, with the compound name adopted following a cultural festival celebrating family unity. The town of [Tropu'hopu](../locations/tropuhopu.md) was founded by continental settlers twenty years ago, evolving into a luxury resort known for its coffee trade. Beastlings preserve distinct culinary traditions, including lava steaming and underground fermentation.

### The [Brightlands](../locations/brightlands.md)
Eastern Solistia's temperate urban center. Its prosperity gave rise to grand theaters, game parlors, and commerce. Its premier metropolis, [New Delsta](../locations/new_delsta.md), was established fifty years ago by emigrants from the collapsed realm of [Delstasch](../world/delstasch.md), while [Clockbank](../locations/clockbank.md) developed as a major manufacturing and clockwork hub.

### The [Crestlands](../locations/crestlands.md)
A mountainous northeastern territory dominated by [Aelfric Cathedral](../locations/flamechurch_cathedral.md), where the Sacred Flame has been venerated for over 1,500 years. The settlement of [Flamechurch](../locations/flamechurch.md) thrives at the cathedral's foot, catering to pilgrims.

### The [Winterlands](../locations/winterlands.md)
A frozen northern territory anchored by the capital [Stormhail](../locations/stormhail.md), seat of the [Sacred Guard](../world/sacred_guard.md) founded 380 years ago by Archbishop Kinchler. Legend attributes the city's survival during a catastrophic ancient freeze to the guardian beast [Glacis](../world/glacis.md) atop [Sacred Peak Altahe](../locations/sacred_peak_altahe.md). Coastal [Cape Cold](../locations/cape_cold.md) thrives on fishing and cold-hardy barley cultivation.

### The Outer Continent
Vast uncharted regions beyond the mapped edges of Solistia, separated by impassable ranges and perilous ocean currents. Despite limited formal exploration, foreign trade, southern wars involving Ku, and foreign immigrants in New Delsta prove the existence of thriving civilizations beyond Solistia's known boundaries.
