# A Tower of Trials

**A Tower of Trials** is a side story set at the [Five-Tiered Tower](../locations/five_tiered_tower.md) in [Hinoeuma](../locations/hinoeuma.md).

## Story

In the arid reaches of Hinoeuma stands the ancient Five-Tiered Tower, a revered and deadly proving ground. At the tower's entrance, a solemn Guide issues a grave warning to any who seek to test their might:

> *"Only the greatest of heroes can challenge the five trials of this tower. Few who enter ever return. Proceed at your own peril."*

To conquer the monument, challengers must battle their way up each tier of the structure, defeating a formidable guardian stationed on every floor:

1. **First Tier**: Gken, a formidable martial challenger.
2. **Second Tier**: Ichchadhari the Snake Charmer, who commands lethal serpent arts.
3. **Third Tier**: Tyran the Seeker, an ascetic warrior wielding tonfas.
4. **Fourth Tier**: Auðnvarg, a ferocious, lupine arcane beast.
5. **Fifth Tier**: [Priestess Hinoekagura](../characters/priestess_hinoekagura.md), the supreme master and guardian awaiting at the summit.

Upon overcoming all five guardians and prevailing in the final clash against Priestess Hinoekagura, the travelers conquer the tower. Having demonstrated supreme skill and fortitude, they are rewarded with the mystical traditions of the Conjurer profession and gifted the sacred Conjurer's Raiment.
