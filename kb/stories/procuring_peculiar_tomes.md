# Procuring Peculiar Tomes

> "*I wish to procure Dispatches from Beastling Island, The Curious Legend of the Great Wall, and From the Far Reaches of Hell.*"  
> —*Unusual Tome Specialist*

**Procuring Peculiar Tomes** is a side story taking place at [Montwise: Library](../locations/montwise_library.md) in the [Crestlands](../locations/crestlands.md).

## Story

Inside the renowned archives of the library of [Montwise](../locations/montwise.md), an Unusual Tome Specialist seeks to enrich the institution's collection by tracking down three scarce and esoteric volumes scattered across Solistia:

- ***Dispatches from Beastling Island*:** A cultural chronicle possessed by a foreign traveler staying in [Beasting Village](../locations/beasting_village.md) on [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md).
- ***The Curious Legend of the Great Wall*:** An ancient historical study retained by an archeologist conducting field research in the [Southern Stormhail Snows](../locations/southern_stormhail_snows.md) near [Northern Montwise Pass](../locations/northern_montwise_pass.md).
- ***[From the Far Reaches of Hell](from_the_far_reaches_of_hell_book.md)*:** An ominous, forbidden grimoire locked away in a guarded dwelling in [Crackridge](../locations/crackridge.md) within the [Wildlands](../locations/wildlands.md).

After all three disparate tomes are recovered and presented to the specialist, they are cataloged and given a permanent home on the library's shelves, preserving their unique lore for future generations of scholars.
