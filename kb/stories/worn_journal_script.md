# Worn Journal/Script

> *"The diary of a young woman. Only the letters '[llows](../locations/house_wellows_manor.md)' can be made out on the cover."*
> — Item Description

The **Worn Journal** is a collectible text found within [House Wellows Manor](../locations/house_wellows_manor.md) in the [Leaflands](../locations/leaflands.md). It contains the diary entries of a young noble girl leading up to the violent tragedy that destroyed her family and household.

Today I wrote invitations to my birthday party. I'm going to invite all of my friends. We're going to eat a big cake. I can't wait! I'm so excited to open my presents. I wonder what I'll get!
