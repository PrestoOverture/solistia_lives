# A Present for My Son

**A Present for My Son** is a side story in [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md) within the [Winterlands](../locations/winterlands.md).

## Story

In the snowy town of Winterbloom, a devoted father frets over his son's imminent departure from home:

> *"My son'll be leaving soon to pursue his dream of being a scholar. Isn't there anything I can give my boy to wish him well on his journey...?"*

Desiring to support the boy's ambition to study scholarship, the father searches for a meaningful parting gift to encourage and guide him on the arduous road ahead.

In neighboring [Cape Cold](../locations/cape_cold.md), an elderly retired scholar living quietly in a hillside cabin preserves a cherished Silver Quill—a fine instrument that faithfully served him through decades of academic study.

When the silver quill is brought to Winterbloom, the father is filled with gratitude. With this symbol of scholarly achievement in hand, he is able to convey his heartfelt blessings and pride as his son sets off on his lifelong journey of learning.
