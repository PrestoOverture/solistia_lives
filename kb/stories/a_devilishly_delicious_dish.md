# A Devilishly Delicious Dish

**A Devilishly Delicious Dish** is a side story in [New Delsta](../locations/new_delsta.md) within the [Brightlands](../locations/brightlands.md).

## Story

In the tavern of New Delsta, an ambitious cook seeks the finest ingredients to create an extraordinary culinary creation:

> *"First things first: I've gotta find the tastiest ingredients around. Maybe a Delsta Devil from the cave out east would do the trick?"*

The creature in question, the Delsta Devil, lurks within the subterranean waters of the [Sunken Maw](../locations/sunken_maw.md), located off the [Western Clockbank Highroad](../locations/western_clockbank_highroad.md). Deep inside the cavern dwells an elderly fishing enthusiast who possesses Special Bait capable of drawing the elusive aquatic monster from the depths.

Once the beast is drawn forth and overcome, the Delsta Devil is brought back to the New Delsta tavern. The cook crafts a delectable dish from the rare catch, causing business at the tavern to boom and inspiring the chef to revise his entire menu from tip to tail.
