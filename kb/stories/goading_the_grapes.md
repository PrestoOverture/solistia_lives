# Goading the Grapes

> "*How in the world do I make these grapes grow? Someone, please tell me...*"
> — *Grape Farmer*

**Goading the Grapes** is a side story set along the [Western Conning Creek Coast](../locations/western_conning_creek_coast.md) within the [Harborlands](../locations/harborlands.md).

## Overview

At a vineyard along the Western Conning Creek Coast, a struggling Grape Farmer is at his wit's end trying to cultivate his crop. Desperately wondering how to make his vines bear fruit, he seeks advice from anyone with agricultural expertise. A nearby traveling merchant mentions having heard of an elderly grape expert residing across the waters in the village of [Cropdale](../locations/cropdale.md).

Travelers visit the tavern in Cropdale and consult the seasoned Grape Expert, obtaining his specialized knowledge (*Tricks to Growing Grapes*).

Upon returning to the Harborlands and sharing the expert's techniques, the farmer successfully revitalizes his vineyard, producing a bountiful harvest of fine grapes and flourishing vines.
