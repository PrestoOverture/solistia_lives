# The Bourgeois Boy

**The Bourgeois Boy** is a side story in [New Delsta](../locations/new_delsta.md) within the [Brightlands](../locations/brightlands.md).

## Story

In the affluent district of New Delsta, an arrogant and entitled young heir treats everyone around him like hired help, ordering passersby about:

> *"You want money, don't you? I'll pay you, so bring it here on the double!"*

Believing his family's wealth permits him to treat others with contempt, the boy constantly pesters locals to run petty errands for common sundries like grapes and plums, tossing small change in return without ever displaying genuine gratitude.

Recognizing that catering to his demands will never teach him humility, travelers journey to [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md) and locate the Wealthy Boy's Father inside the northern church. When brought back to New Delsta to see how his son conducts himself, the father is appalled and delivers a stern, uncompromising tongue-lashing.

Chastened and thoroughly humbled by his father's reprimand, the boy finally recognizes the error of his ways and resolves to change his conduct.

## Epilogue

Following the confrontation, the youth reforms his behavior entirely, becoming known as the **Reformed Boy**. He personally visits each neighbor and townsperson he previously inconvenienced or looked down upon:

> *"I've apologized to everyone I used. I won't do it again, truly..."*

His bewildered neighbors marvel at the sudden turnaround, grateful that the obnoxious nuisance has grown into a respectful, considerate young man.
