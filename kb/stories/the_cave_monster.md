# The Cave Monster

**The Cave Monster** is a side story originating in the [Southern Crackridge Wilds](../locations/southern_crackridge_wilds.md) within the [Wildlands](../locations/wildlands.md).

## Story

In the rugged bluffs of the Southern Crackridge Wilds, critical excavation work on the railway has ground to an abrupt halt at the [Unfinished Tunnel](../locations/unfinished_tunnel.md). Near the barricaded entrance, a shaken construction Foreman urges passersby to flee:

> *"You'd best get out of here as quick as you can. No one stands a chance against that beast!"*

A monstrous subterranean predator known as Manymaws has made its lair deep within the freshly carved passage, ambushing the laborers and threatening to halt railroad progress permanently.

Venturing into the subterranean shadows of the tunnel, the travelers confront and destroy the multi-mouthed horror. With the beast slain and their lives no longer in jeopardy, the grateful laborers celebrate their deliverance and resume laying track through the mountains.
