# The Journey for the Dawn

**The Journey for the Dawn** is the climactic final chapter and overarching Extra Story of *Octopath Traveler II*, bringing all eight travelers together to defeat the [Moonshade Order](../world/moonshade_order.md) and dispel the eternal night.

## Overview

Following the completion of each traveler's personal path and their Crossed Paths, the eight companions reunite around a campfire along the [Southern Cropdale Trail](../locations/southern_cropdale_trail.md). Sharing their hopes and aspirations under the open sky, they fall asleep together.

During the night, all eight experience a shared prophetic nightmare: one by one, Solistia's four [Sacred Flames](../world/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) are snuffed out, culminating in a vision of a dark, forgotten island rising from the sea. Upon waking, the travelers discover that the nightmare has become reality: the sun fails to appear, and the entire continent is enveloped in an unnatural, endless darkness known as the Shadow.

## Rekindling the Sacred Flames

To pierce the encroaching darkness, the travelers embark across Solistia to relight the four snuffed Sacred Flames using [Alpates](../characters/alpates.md)'s sacred mirror. Each flame requires the combined resolve of a pair of travelers, and upon being rekindled, each altar imparts a memory revealing the dark sacrifice enacted by the Moonshade Order to extinguish it:

### The Tombs of the Wardenbeasts
Within the [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../locations/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md) on [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md), [Castti Florenz](../characters/castti.md) and [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md) defeat a [Grotesque Monster](../world/grotesque_monster.md) guarding the shrine. Reigniting the flame reveals the memory of the Dark Hunter [Petrichor](../characters/petrichor.md), who gave herself to the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../world/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md), channeling the beast's agony and despair to extinguish the holy light.

### The Tranquil Grotto
Deep inside the [Tranquil Grotto](../locations/tranquil_grotto.md) in [Hinoeuma](../locations/hinoeuma.md), [Agnea Bristarni](../characters/agnea.md) and [Hikari Ku](../characters/hikari.md) rekindle the altar. The restored light unveils a vision of the military strategist [Kazan](../characters/oboro.md)—whose true identity is Oboro of Clan Ku. Having orchestrated King [Mugen](../characters/mugen.md)'s bloody ascension to obtain the sacred Darkblood Blade, Oboro ruthlessly cut down [Ageha](../characters/ageha.md) to quench the flame.

### The Fellsun Ruins
At the [Fellsun Ruins](../locations/fellsun_ruins.md) in the [Wildlands](../locations/wildlands.md), the party discovers [Ori's Journal Fragments](oris_journal_fragments.md). The records reveal that the scrivener [Ori](../characters/ori.md) was a secret Moonshade Order operative tasked with monitoring the travelers, though her encounters with [Partitio Yellowil](../characters/partitio.md) had profoundly shaken her nihilism. When Partitio and [Osvald V. Vanstein](../characters/osvald.md) relight the altar, the memory shows Ori attempting suicide with the *Darkblood Grimoire* and a dagger; because her resolve wavered due to Partitio's influence, she survived her self-inflicted wound.

### Flamechurch Cathedral
Arriving in [Flamechurch](../locations/flamechurch.md), the companions find the town barricaded in terror. Sister [Mindt](../characters/arcanette.md) confronts them, dropping her pious facade to reveal her true identity as Arcanette, leader of the Moonshade Order. After the travelers defeat Arcanette, they find [Tanzy's Journal/Script](tanzys_journal_script.md) near the cathedral entrance. [Temenos Mistral](../characters/temenos.md) and [Throné Anguis](../characters/throne.md) rekindle the final flame, viewing the memory of Mindt sacrificing [Tanzy](../characters/tanzy.md) in a cloud of black smoke to extinguish the altar.

## The Rise of Vidania

Once all four Sacred Flames are restored, beams of brilliant blue light converge above the [Sundering Sea](../locations/the_sundering_sea.md). The breaking of the ancient seals forces the sunken island of [Vidania](../locations/vidania.md)—the primordial prison of the wicked god—to rise above the waves.

Sailing to the risen isle, the eight travelers ascend to the summit of Castle Vidania. There they confront Oboro, who challenges their belief in the dawn and decries the world as cruel and unsightly. Unwavering in his nihilism, Oboro casts himself backward into the dark blaze, offering his own soul as the final sacrifice required to awaken the Wicked God.

## The Battle for the Dawn

From the dark flames, [Vide](../world/vide.md) awakens to consume Solistia in eternal ruin. Standing united as one, all eight travelers fight side by side against the dark deity and the swirling shadows of the abyss.

Through their combined strength and faith in tomorrow, the travelers banish Vide once and for all. As the wicked god dissipates, the unnatural darkness recedes, and the sun rises across Solistia, bringing the dawn back to the world.
