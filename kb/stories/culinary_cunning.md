# Culinary Cunning

> *"Tingly meat... Wanting recipe! Making myself!"*
> —Beastling Cooking Enthusiast

**Culinary Cunning** is a side story set in [Beasting Village](../locations/beasting_village.md) on the island of [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md).

## Story

While beastling culinary tradition primarily focuses on simple roasted game, a budding beastling culinary enthusiast in Beasting Village becomes utterly fascinated by the sophisticated seasonings of human cuisine. Craving a dish remembered fondly as "tingly meat," the beastling longs to acquire the exact cooking instructions to prepare it at home.

The dish in question originates from the food stands near the [Tropu'hopu: Floating Theater](../locations/tropuhopu_floating_theater.md) in the neighboring coastal settlement of [Tropu'hopu](../locations/tropuhopu.md), where a local street vendor crafts a famous spicy seasoned fowl. Once the chef's culinary secrets are learned and conveyed back to Beasting Village, the aspiring beastling gourmet is overjoyed, taking the first steps toward pioneering seasoned gastronomy among their kin.
