# A Gate Between Worlds

**A Gate Between Worlds** is a climactic side story set on an uncharted isle within the [Sundering Sea](../locations/the_sundering_sea.md) (categorized under Toto'haha side stories in the traveler's journal). It serves as the continuation and culmination of [From the Far Reaches of Hell](from_the_far_reaches_of_hell.md) and [Procuring Peculiar Tomes](procuring_peculiar_tomes.md).

## Story

Following their investigation into the forbidden tome *From the Far Reaches of Hell*, the mysterious traveler [Al](../characters/alfred_octopath_traveler_ii.md) locates an isolated, uncharted island in the Sundering Sea, where the ancient and foreboding [Gate of Finis](../locations/the_gate_of_finis_octopath_traveler_ii.md) resides.

Upon approaching the unsealed portal, Al urgently warns the travelers of the cosmic peril that lies beyond:

> *"No. You must stop. It's too dangerous to go any further...!"*

Crossing the threshold leads into an otherworldly dimension where the dark deity Galdera, the Fallen prepares to bring ruin to the realm. The travelers must first overcome the nightmarish Omniscient Eye before confronting Galdera in his full spectral majesty, flanked by the Abyssal Maw, the Blade of the Fallen, and the Sundering Arm.

Through immense valor, the eight travelers defeat the otherworldly entity, permanently preventing the resurrection of the dark god.

## Epilogue

With the cataclysm averted and his long mission across worlds finally complete, Al discloses his true name and lineage: **Alfred Hornburg**. Having fulfilled his purpose, Alfred departs peacefully from Solistia forever, leaving behind the Spurning Ribbon.
