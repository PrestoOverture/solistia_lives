# A Mysterious Box

**A Mysterious Box** is a side story beginning in [Merry Hills](../locations/merry_hills.md) within the [Crestlands](../locations/crestlands.md) and concluding at [House Wellows Manor](../locations/house_wellows_manor.md) along the [Southern Timberain Trail](../locations/southern_timberain_trail.md).

## Story

The tale begins when travelers obtain a strange curio called the Mysterious Box from a young collector in Merry Hills. Weathered and carrying an ominous aura, the box bears the faded inscription *"We...ws"* along with a persistent spectral echo:

> *"Give it back... Give it back..."*

The inscription points to the tragic, derelict ruins of House Wellows Manor on the Southern Timberain Trail. Under cover of night, phantom apparitions linger among the decaying halls, unearthly noises echo through the estate, and a spectral figure known as the Woman in the Ruins wanders the second floor, trapped in sorrow.

When the travelers return the Mysterious Box into her outstretched hands, a look of profound serenity washes over her countenance. The box held an article of immense emotional significance to her in life. With her treasured keepsake finally restored, the restless spirit dissolves peacefully into the ether.

## Epilogue

With the woman's soul laid to rest, the supernatural phenomena haunting the estate subside: the phantom specters and chilling sounds throughout House Wellows Manor and the Southern Timberain Trail vanish permanently.

In the aftermath, a Worn Journal is found resting near a pergola in the stream along the Southern Timberain Trail, recording the tragic events and final days of House Wellows.
