# Dancer's Journal/Script

The **Dancer's Journal** is an in-game personal record written by a young dancer following the departure of her instructor, [Cuani Bristarni](../characters/cuani_bristarni.md), detailing her life in the impoverished [East District of Sai](../locations/sai_east_district.md) and her determination to become a star.

## Journal Text

> *"I don't know how much time has passed since [my teacher](../characters/cuani_bristarni.md) left. But even though no one is left to instruct me, I will continue my training. I practice the steps she taught me over and over again in hopes that I will be able to move just as she did, and I will never forget the dance that changed my life. In my memories, my teacher shines like the sun. I've decided that I, too, will be as radiant as she was, and no one will ever shake that resolve.*
>
> *My teacher spoke of a [grand theater](../locations/new_delsta_theater.md) on the eastern continent. She said it could hold hundreds and was home to a gorgeous stage on which only the most resplendent of performers could ever set foot. That is a world that I—in [this poor, dirty town](../locations/sai_east_district.md)—cannot even begin to imagine. And I doubt I'm on the only one. [Platt](../characters/platt.md), his wife, the orphans, even the townspeople... That world is just too dazzling for us. It shines too brightly. But if it truly is that beautiful, I want to show it to everyone.*
>
> *That's why I'm going to become a star and change everything. When I'm rich, I can rebuild this place. I can light up every corner of this dark, dingy, leafless little town and make it shine! I'll build a grand theater just for us, then everyone can live in happiness.*
>
> *But to do that, I must practice. I must dance and sing with all my might until I become a star. I will dedicate all that I am to this town.*
>
> *So today, tomorrow, and the day after, I will keep dancing."*
