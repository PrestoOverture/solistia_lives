# My Beloved Catharine

"*Please reunite me with my dear Catharine! She is my everything! Life is meaningless without her!*"
—*Haggard Man*

**My Beloved Catharine** is a tale in [Clockbank](../locations/clockbank.md) in the [Brightlands](../locations/brightlands.md).

## The Desperate Search

In the industrial city of Clockbank, a haggard man is driven to near-madness by the loss of his beloved "Catharine." Overwhelmed with longing, he pleads with passing travelers to help reunite him with the one companion who gave meaning to his life.

## Resolution

Leading the distraught man across town to the western edge of the [Clockbank: Industrial District](../locations/clockbank_industrial_district.md), travelers guide him to an abandoned corner where a lonely white cat is found prowling the alleys. It is revealed that "Cat"harine was a pet feline all along. Overjoyed to have his precious companion returned, the man's spirits are entirely restored, and he happily takes up residence in the industrial district beside Catharine, presenting travelers with Cait Powder in gratitude.
