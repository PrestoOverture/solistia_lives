# Eir's Apothecaries

> *"Eir's Apothecaries. We're here to help."*
> — Castti Florenz

**Eir's Apothecaries** was an order of altruistic traveling healers dedicated to the humanitarian creed to "extend a helping hand to all in need." Founded and led by [Castti Florenz](../../characters/travelers/castti.md), the fellowship journeyed throughout Solistia providing free medical relief to the sick and wounded.

## History

### Foundation and Missions
Operating from a central house of healing in the small village of [Healeaks](../../locations/brightlands/towns/abandoned_village.md) near the [Brightlands](../../locations/brightlands/brightlands.md), Eir's Apothecaries traveled extensively across the continent. Four years prior to the events of the story, they provided critical medical aid to soldiers and civilians alike during a conflict near [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md). In [Winterbloom](../../locations/winterlands/towns/winterbloom.md), Castti treated Lady [Rosa](../../characters/castti/rosa.md)'s terminal illness and established a medicinal herb garden for her daughter [Melia](../../characters/castti/melia.md). During their stay in Winterbloom, Castti and her longtime colleague [Malaya](../../characters/castti/malaya.md) recruited a compassionate and talented young healer named [Trousseau](../../characters/castti/trousseau.md).

Back in Healeaks, when a young girl named [Sally](../../characters/castti/sally.md) fell deathly ill, Castti and Trousseau climbed the dangerous heights of [Mount Liphia](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/mount_liphia.md) to harvest rare santanejo blossoms, concocting a remedy that cured her.

### The Downfall
During a medical mission to the derelict settlement of [Lostseed](../../locations/brightlands/towns/lostseed.md), Trousseau encountered [Claude](../../characters/throne/claude_blacksnakes.md). Claude's nihilistic teachings corrupted the young apothecary's mind, convincing him that life was ceaseless suffering and that death was the ultimate mercy.

Some time later, while the rest of the company was returning from another relief mission, Trousseau enacted his monstrous scheme. Returning to Healeaks, the apothecaries discovered that Sally and her brother Temm had been slain, and an unnatural purple rain began descending upon the settlement. Despite desperate attempts at triage, the villagers perished one by one.

Noticing smoke billowing from Mount Liphia, Castti, Malaya, [Andy](../../characters/castti/andy.md), and [Randy](../../characters/castti/randy.md) ascended the mountain, leaving [Elma](../../characters/castti/elma.md) to attend to the village. At the summit, they found Trousseau burning a virulent toxin into the sky to poison the rain. As Castti succumbed to the fumes, Trousseau escaped, announcing plans to poison the royal coronation in [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md). Andy and Randy sacrificed their lives by hurling themselves onto the venomous pyre to extinguish the flames.

Malaya carried the dying Castti back down the mountain, only to find Healeaks decimated and Elma dead after shielding the order's horse, [Jeyah](../../characters/castti/jeyah.md), from the toxic downpour. Administering snowdrops to slow the poison, Malaya transported Castti to the coast on Jeyah. Believing Castti was the sole apothecary capable of discovering an antidote, Malaya placed her aboard a skiff with her satchel and snowdrops, casting her out to sea. Malaya and Jeyah then succumbed to the poison on the shore.

### Legacy and Redemption
Following the disaster, malicious rumors spread across Solistia falsely accusing Eir's Apothecaries of having deliberately poisoned Healeaks, causing survivors in towns such as [Canalbrine](../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md) to revile their uniform.

Castti, having awakened with amnesia after being found adrift at sea, slowly recovered her memories of the order. She ultimately tracked Trousseau to Timberain, defeated him, and synthesized a universal cure using the order's foundational remedies, saving the city and clearing the fallen order's name. In a dream following the victory, Castti anointed the spirits of Malaya, Andy, Randy, and Elma with skybalm, allowing her companions to rest in peace. She later resolved to rebuild Eir's Apothecaries by recruiting like-minded healers across Solistia.

## Members

- **[Castti Florenz](../../characters/travelers/castti.md)**: Founder and leader ("Chief") of the order; sole survivor.
- **[Malaya](../../characters/castti/malaya.md)**: Senior apothecary and Castti's former rival turned closest friend; died saving Castti.
- **[Andy](../../characters/castti/andy.md)**: Apothecary brother; sacrificed his life to extinguish Trousseau's poisoned fire on Mount Liphia.
- **[Randy](../../characters/castti/randy.md)**: Apothecary brother; sacrificed his life alongside Andy on Mount Liphia.
- **[Elma](../../characters/castti/elma.md)**: Resident apothecary; died shielding the team's steed from the poisoned rain in Healeaks.
- **[Trousseau](../../characters/castti/trousseau.md)** (former member): Recruited in Winterbloom; fell to nihilism and orchestrated the massacre of Healeaks.
- **[Jeyah](../../characters/castti/jeyah.md)**: The loyal horse of the order.
