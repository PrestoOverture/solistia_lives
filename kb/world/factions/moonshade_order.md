# Moonshade Order

The **Moonshade Order** is an ancient nihilistic death cult dedicated to the revival of [Vide the Wicked](../gods/vide.md) and the primary antagonistic faction operating behind the scenes throughout Solistia.

## Overview

- **Founder**: [Archmage D'arqest](../../characters/others/darqest.md)
- **Leader**: [Arcanette](../../characters/temenos/arcanette.md) (deceased)
- **Primary Objective**: Extinguish the four [Sacred Flames](../lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) through ritual sacrifice and resurrect [Vide](../gods/vide.md) to destroy the mortal world.

> "*Surrender yourself not unto silent dusk. For the light shall fade. And soon, night shall fall.*"
> — Inscription from the *Book of Night*

Originating on the western continent, the Order worships eternal darkness, asserting that human existence is filled with irredeemable suffering and cruelty. The cult established a strong clandestine following in [Crackridge](../../locations/wildlands/towns/crackridge.md), where masked adherents gather at night wearing crescent moon talismans. Adherents in Crackridge and [Gravell](../../locations/wildlands/towns/gravell.md) view their devotion not as malice, but as fidelity to primordial rites, regarding the [Sacred Flame](../lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) as an unnatural, opposing blaze that prolongs suffering.

## Key Figures

### Main Conspirators
The core inner circle of the Order forms the acronym "OCTOPATH":
- **[Oboro](../../characters/hikari/oboro.md)**: Infiltrated Ku as the mastermind strategist Kazan ("Eagle of Ku").
- **[Claude](../../characters/throne/claude_blacksnakes.md)**: Grandson of D'arqest and intended vessel of Vide; founder of the [Blacksnakes](blacksnakes.md).
- **[Tanzy](../../characters/agnea/tanzy.md)**: Recruited by Arcanette following her fiancé's death; used as a scout and sacrificial pawn.
- **[Ori](../../characters/partitio/ori.md)**: Adoptive sister of Oboro; worked as a roving scrivener to gather intelligence on the Sacred Flames.
- **[Petrichor](../../characters/others/petrichor.md)**: Hunter tasked with capturing the fallen Wardenbeast in Toto'haha.
- **[Arcanette](../../characters/temenos/arcanette.md)**: Supreme leader of the Order; disguised as Sister Mindt in [Flamechurch](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch.md).
- **[Trousseau](../../characters/castti/trousseau.md)**: Former healer of [Eir's Apothecaries](eirs_apothecaries.md) driven mad by the *Book of Night*.
- **[Harvey](../../characters/osvald/harvey.md)**: Ambitious scholar and alchemist who reconstructed the *Book of Demons*.

### Associates and Pawns
- **[Kaldena](../../characters/temenos/kaldena.md)**: Captain of the Sacred Guard, manipulated into uncovering the *Book of Night*.
- **[Vados](../../characters/temenos/vados.md)**: Architect and assassin employed by Arcanette to eliminate [Pontiff Jörg](../../characters/temenos/jorg.md).
- **[Reiza](../../characters/temenos/reiza.md)**: Conspirator tied to Kaldena's faction.

## History

### The Kal Massacre and the Book of Night

Thirty years prior to the main story, the Moonshade Order descended upon the [Kal](../lore/kal.md) people inhabiting the [Fellsun Ruins](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/fellsun_ruins.md), slaughtering their clan to extract dark secrets. Arcanette orchestrated the massacre and later exploited the survivors, particularly Captain [Kaldena](../../characters/temenos/kaldena.md). Presenting Kaldena with a corrupted ritual to master [Shadow](../lore/shadow.md), Arcanette manipulated her into assembling the lost fragments of the **Book of Night**. Kaldena's transformation and defeat at [Rifted Rock](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/rifted_rock.md) by [Temenos Mistral](../../characters/travelers/temenos.md) delivered the fully restored grimoire into the Order's hands.

### Claude and the Blacksnakes

As the direct descendant of Archmage D'arqest, [Claude](../../characters/throne/claude_blacksnakes.md) was chosen by the Order to serve as Vide's physical vessel. Refusing this sacrificial destiny, Claude retreated into seclusion in [Lostseed](../../locations/brightlands/towns/lostseed.md). There, he founded the [Blacksnakes](blacksnakes.md) guild, siring countless offspring across the realm with the intention that one of his children would prove resilient enough to inherit his cursed bloodline and take his place as the sacrifice. He was eventually assassinated in [Lostseed Castle](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/lostseed_castle.md) by his daughter [Throné Anguis](../../characters/travelers/throne.md).

### Corruption of Trousseau

Encountering the impressionable apothecary [Trousseau](../../characters/castti/trousseau.md) in Lostseed, Claude provided him with the *Book of Night*. Corrupted by its nihilistic teachings, Trousseau came to view death as the sole mercy for human agony. Incorporating toxic [Shadow](../lore/shadow.md) energy into chemical concoctions, Trousseau wiped out the population of [Healeaks](../../locations/brightlands/towns/abandoned_village.md) with a lethal Purple Rain, seeking to repeat the calamity in [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md) before being struck down by [Castti Florenz](../../characters/travelers/castti.md).

### The Book of Demons and the Lumina Clan

The Order enlisted the scholar [Harvey](../../characters/osvald/harvey.md) to reconstruct the ancient **Book of Demons**. Harvey ransacked [D'arqest's tomb](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/duskruin_shrine.md) in the Wildlands and murdered the wife of [Osvald V. Vanstein](../../characters/travelers/osvald.md), exploiting the ancient bloodline of the [Lumina Clan](lumina_clan.md) through Osvald's daughter [Elena Vanstein](../../characters/osvald/elena_vanstein.md). Harvey successfully merged the *Book of Night* and *Book of Demons* into the **Darkblood Grimoire**. To prevent the Lumina Clan from interfering with their plans, the Order also tracked down and assassinated [Alpates](../../characters/others/alpates.md), though she hid her clan's sacred mirror before she died.

### Extinguishing the Four Sacred Flames

To break the barrier imprisoning Vide, the Order deployed ancient Darkblood weapons to snuff out Solistia's four Sacred Flames through ritual human murder:

1. **Toto'haha ([Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md))**: The hunter [Petrichor](../../characters/others/petrichor.md) captured the Wardenbeast cub unchosen by [Ochette](../../characters/travelers/ochette.md), which Harvey mutated into the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../beasts/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md). In the sacred tombs, Petrichor was sacrificed to extinguish the island's flame using the **Darkblood Bow**.
2. **Crestlands ([Flamechurch: Cathedral](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch_cathedral.md))**: Arcanette manipulated the grieving widow [Tanzy](../../characters/agnea/tanzy.md) into tracking down the holy flame sites under the guise of an entertainment troupe manager. At the cathedral, Arcanette used the **Darkblood Staff** to ritually murder Tanzy and douse the flame.
3. **Hinoeuma ([Tranquil Grotto](../../locations/hinoeuma/dungeons/tranquil_grotto.md))**: Oboro, posing as the Ku strategist Kazan, orchestrated the civil war between [Prince Hikari](../../characters/travelers/hikari.md) and [General Mugen](../../characters/hikari/mugen.md) to secure the **Darkblood Blade**. Following Mugen's defeat, Oboro sacrificed [Ageha](../../characters/hikari/ageha.md) with the blade to snuff out the flame of Ku.
4. **Wildlands ([Fellsun Ruins](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/fellsun_ruins.md))**: The scrivener [Ori](../../characters/partitio/ori.md), having documented flame lore for the Order, stabbed herself at the altar of the ruins using the Darkblood Grimoire to extinguish the final flame, though she miraculously survived the attempt.

### The Night of the Raven and Vide's Fall

With all four Sacred Flames doused, the realm was submerged in the Night of the Raven—an unbroken eclipse. The eight travelers united during [The Journey for the Dawn](../../stories/main/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md), using Alpates's sacred mirror to reignite the braziers across Solistia. Arcanette confronted them in an effort to safeguard the eclipse, but was defeated and perished.

The travelers reached the sunken shrine of [Vidania](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/vidania.md) as Oboro impaled himself upon the altar, giving his life to serve as the vessel for [Vide](../gods/vide.md)'s revival. Despite the god's catastrophic awakening, the eight travelers overcame Vide, shattering the cult's grand ambition and leaving the surviving adherents scattered and leaderless.
