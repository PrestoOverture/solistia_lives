# Order of the Sacred Flame (Octopath Traveler II)

> "*And the [gods](../gods/gods.md) did give their messengers flame to light the way forward.*"
> — *Scripture from the Order*

The **Order of the Sacred Flame** is the largest religion in Solistia, centered around the worship of [Aelfric](../gods/aelfric.md) the Flamebringer and his [Sacred Flame](../lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md). Its seat of power is [Flamechurch: Cathedral](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch_cathedral.md), located north of the city of [Flamechurch](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch.md), where pilgrims visit on a regular basis.

The Order of the Sacred Flame and the [Sacred Guard](sacred_guard.md) have upheld order on the eastern continent for the last hundred years. Members of the Order are protected by the Sacred Guard, a military organization formed to defend the church and punish insurgents. However, heresy has grown increasingly rampant in the modern day, leading [Pontiff Jörg](../../characters/temenos/jorg.md) to handpick a number of Inquisitors to root it out.

## Influence and Presence

The Order's reach is primarily centered in the [Crestlands](../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md), with only one church established on the western continent. The church at [Flamechurch](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch.md) has faced insurgent attacks and internal corruption, necessitating both the policing of the Sacred Guard and the investigations of the Inquisition. 

The Order is also among the more generous donors funding the [Grand Gala](../lore/grand_gala.md). In [Borderfall](../../locations/crestlands/wilds/borderfall.md), a Cleric Novelist stationed at the church is responsible for managing the church's public image; she authored a popular children's book titled *Adventures of an Apprentice Cleric*.

Legends within the Order also speak of an "isle of illusions"—widely dismissed by the academic community—which refers to the lost isle of [Vidania](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/vidania.md).
