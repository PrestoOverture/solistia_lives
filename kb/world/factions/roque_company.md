# Roque Company

> "*The Roque Company is kind enough to provide for you. That puts you in our debt.*"
> — *[Thurston](../../characters/partitio/thurston.md)*

The **Roque Company** is a major industrial enterprise founded by [Roque Brilliante](../../characters/partitio/roque_brilliante.md) dedicated to the development and manufacturing of steam engines. Following the events of Partitio's journey, it is reorganized into **Partitio & Roque**.

## History

### Foundation and Monopoly
After departing [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md), Roque relocated to [Clockbank](../../locations/brightlands/towns/clockbank.md), where he invested in the experimental steam engine developed by the inventor [Floyd](../../characters/partitio/floyd.md) and Floyd's grandfather. Recognizing the revolutionary potential of the machine, Roque established the headquarters of the Roque Company in Clockbank eight years prior to the events of *Octopath Traveler II*.

The company manufactured steam engines exclusively for wealthy clients and high-end industrial enterprises. Drawing immense wealth from heavy taxation in Oresrush and Clockbank alongside profits from the engine monopoly, Roque eventually purchased his own private island, [Roque Island](../../locations/harborlands/towns/roque_island.md), where he established a company town and a state-of-the-art production factory. In Clockbank's industrial sector, prospective employees were required to present formal reference letters to secure employment within the company.

### The Neo Steam Engine and the Buyout
Floyd, with the assistance of [Partitio Yellowil](../../characters/travelers/partitio.md), developed the "Neo Steam Engine" utilizing clockite, creating an engine suitable for mass production. However, Roque's high-ranking executive [Thurston](../../characters/partitio/thurston.md) sought to monopolize the technology and reneged on Floyd's wish to share the engine with impoverished settlements. Partitio intervened to protect Floyd's vision, defeating Thurston's guard dog [Garnet](../../characters/partitio/garnet.md). Roque then intervened directly, agreeing to sell all rights to the steam engine to Partitio for eighty billion leaves, believing the sum impossible to acquire. Roque subsequently dismissed Thurston for his failure to halt Partitio's interference.

Later, Roque held a grand press conference on Roque Island to unveil a new steam engine model. Partitio arrived to claim the rights, presenting an eighty-billion-leaf promissory note backed by Lord [Alrond](../../characters/partitio/alrond_rondwell.md). When Roque attempted to dismiss the note by claiming he only accepted physical tender, Alrond arrived by sea bearing the full eighty billion leaves in cash. In desperation, Roque attempted to renege on the signed contract, losing the confidence and respect of the attending scriveners and press. Fleeing into his factory, Roque deployed his ultimate war machine, the [Steam Tank Obsidian](../beasts/steam_tank_obsidian.md), but Partitio and his companions destroyed the machine and subdued Roque.

### Partitio & Roque
Following their clash, Roque and Partitio reconciled. Roque insisted on stepping into the background and transferring the presidency of the reorganized firm—renamed **Partitio & Roque**—to Partitio. Under Partitio's leadership, the company eliminated oppressive tariffs and taxes on the steam engine, broadened distribution to benefit everyday citizens, and actively hired workers from the most impoverished communities. Partitio characterized their new enterprise as being "bigger than a Cetus Maximus."

### Company Pledge
The foundational values of Partitio & Roque are codified in a four-point company pledge:
1. *Put the happiness of others above all.*
2. *Be a safe haven for dreamers.*
3. *Show charity at every opportunity.*
4. *Never rewrite this pledge.*
