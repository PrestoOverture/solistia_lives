# Blacksnakes

"*There's no such thing as freedom, [Throné](../../characters/throne/throne.md)... Once a Snake, always a Snake...!*"
—*[Mother](../../characters/throne/mother.md)*

The **Blacksnakes** are an underworld criminal organization of thieves and assassins operating primarily in [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md), founded by [Claude](../../characters/throne/claude_blacksnakes.md) of the [Moonshade Order](moonshade_order.md).

## Organization and Methods

Claude populated the syndicate with his own illegitimate offspring fathered across Solistia. Members were rigorously trained in theft, infiltration, and assassination from childhood. Unbeknownst to most members, they were deliberately pitted against each other in ruthless trials designed to cull the weak and cultivate a worthy successor.

- **Headquarters:** The gang conducts its primary operations from the [Game Parlor](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta_game_parlor.md) in New Delsta.
- **Recruitment and Training:** The syndicate gathered orphaned and abandoned children at [Mother's Garden](../../locations/leaflands/dungeons/mothers_garden.md), an isolated facility on the outskirts of [Wellgrove](../../locations/leaflands/towns/wellgrove.md), where [Mother](../../characters/throne/mother.md) periodically inducted new recruits when numbers dwindled.
- **The Collars:** To ensure obedience and prevent desertion, members are fitted with locked metal collars around their necks. Tampering with a collar triggers an injection of lethal poison, brewed by the [Elderly Guard](../../characters/throne/elderly_guard.md) from black roses grown at Mother's Garden. While members are told that only the two keys carried by Mother and [Father](../../characters/throne/father.md) can release them, Claude retains the sole functional key.
- **Mark of the Syndicate:** Members bear a distinctive tattoo on their dominant working arm, depicting a serpent winding upward in pursuit of prey until its tail reaches the chest.

## History and Succession

Before the events of Throné's journey, the Blacksnakes were led by [Marietta](../../characters/throne/marietta.md), Father's former lover. Mother and Father killed Marietta and took control as co-leaders. Under their joint rule, the syndicate split into bitter factional rivalry, with spies loyal to either Mother or Father watching each other at the Game Parlor.

When [Throné Anguis](../../characters/throne/throne.md) sought her freedom, she tracked down and killed both Mother and Father. Deprived of leadership, the Blacksnakes devolved into chaos, with surviving members turning violently against one another in street-level fighting across New Delsta. Following Throné's subsequent slaying of Claude, the organization fell apart entirely.
