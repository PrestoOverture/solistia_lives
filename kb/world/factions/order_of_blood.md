# Order of Blood

"*The Ouma of Blood is a secret technique that uses the Ouma Logs as a medium. Although it isn't fully completed yet, when all the Ouma Logs are gathered... Ouma will devour this entire continent! At that time, everyone will abandon their free will... And the Great Will shall rule over all. The world will be flawless. There will be no desires, and no conflicts. When everyone thinks the same way, the world will finally know true peace. This is the vision of the Great Will! This is the world for which we desire!*" —The Order's dogma according to Lucian

The **Order of Blood** is an extremist cult active across Solistia that preaches an absolute destiny governed by the "Great Will." The cult seeks to eliminate individual desire, conflict, and free will by bathing the continent in Ouma, preparing a chosen vessel named Sail to embody the [Dark Will](../gods/galdera.md) and rule over both Solistia and Orsterra.

## Doctrines and Properties

The cult derives its name from its sacred baptism ritual, in which initiates drink the "blood of the [Gods](../gods/gods.md)" to forge an unbreakable pact with the Great Will. This blood bestows supernatural physical enhancements, immunity to the corruptive effects of Ouma, and unnatural longevity.

Followers who survive an intense version of the Baptism of Blood can summon black flames and transform into monstrous entities. Through the blood oath, all baptized individuals share a metaphysical link: the master who bestows the dark blood senses when any member of their "family" dies and possesses the power to reanimate and control their corpses like puppets. Upon death, the physical bodies of baptized cult members dissolve into violet flames.

## History

### Origins and Rise
The Order began as an obscure sect venerating a local deity in the [Wildlands](../../locations/wildlands/wildlands.md). Leadership was subsequently usurped by Lucian, who redefined the cult's mythology around the "Twin Gods," modeled after himself and his Orsterran shadow. Under his guidance, the sect transformed into an organized, aggressive religious movement with extensive resources.

To fulfill their vision of a world stripped of conflict and desire, Lucian and a devout follower named Norma resolved to raise an Orsterran infant, Sail, feeding him the blood of the gods from birth so he would become the vessel of the Dark Will. Concurrently, Lucian organized searches across Solistia for the eight Ouma Logs—mystical manuscripts containing the essence of the twilight phenomenon. Assembled upon dedicated altars, eight logs could complete the Codex of Ouma and manifest the phenomenon on a continental scale.

To monitor the [Order of the Sacred Flame](order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) and the [Sacred Guard](sacred_guard.md), the cult excavated an Underground Sanctuary in the [Crestlands](../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md) near [Flamechurch](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch.md).

### Western Expansion
Seeking to expand into the western continent, the Order allied with Shangpio, master of the Merchant Guild in [Canalbrine](../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md). Together, they established an extortion racket exploiting local merchants, establishing a monopoly over the city's trade. Using these illicit funds, the Order constructed a hidden chapel behind Shangpio Company's Manse for dark ceremonies and human sacrifices.

In Canalbrine, Ignazio underwent baptism, subsequently overseeing the cult's operations in the [Harborlands](../../locations/harborlands/harborlands.md). Around this time, the roaming warrior Aval—who had previously fought against the cult—chose to undergo baptism to test his strength and explore Lucian's philosophy of salvation.

### The Awakening of Sail
Sail was raised in Holden, a settlement in the [Brightlands](../../locations/brightlands/brightlands.md) that served as home to many cult members. Over time, Norma grew to view Sail as a true son and began questioning the cult's purpose, secretly praying to the Sacred Flame and giving Sail a protective divine ring.

When Sail's resonance with Ouma first manifested, Ignazio murdered Sail's childhood companions Joey and Bez to sever the youth's emotional attachments, while Norma vanished. Sail fled Holden under the constant, clandestine watch of Ignazio and Aval. Due to his early baptism, Sail manifested an alternate nocturnal persona attuned to the Ouma Logs, inadvertently locating four manuscripts for the cult.

Following Ignazio's defeat, the Order's hold on Canalbrine collapsed. Shangpio's crimes were exposed, and numerous cultists were apprehended by Sanctum Knights.

### Schism and Escalation
Internal dissent fractured the Order when Bishop Gaffaud attempted to supplant Sail as the vessel. Seizing five Ouma Logs, Gaffaud constructed altars in [Cape Cold](../../locations/winterlands/towns/cape_cold.md) and the Absolute Icefield to summon Ouma across the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md). Sail disrupted the rite and defeated him, after which Aval executed Gaffaud for treachery.

At the Sanctum Knights Garrison in [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md), Lucian demonstrated his ability to resurrect fallen Sanctum Knights and Gaffaud as undead abominations. In [Cropdale](../../locations/leaflands/towns/cropdale.md), the final log was contested by Holden refugees Gabriel and Shaymi; Aval retrieved the manuscript after killing them.

### Confrontation and Fall
In the cult's Crestlands chapel, Lucian revealed to Sail that Norma had administered gods' blood to him throughout his upbringing, presenting Sail's entire life as a calculated pilgrimage. Enraged, Sail fought and defeated Lucian, temporarily scattering the manuscripts.

Surviving zealots made a pilgrimage to their ancestral roots at Ashen Ridge in the Wildlands. There, Norma told Sail that her death was required to dissolve the blood oath, transforming into a beast to force Sail to strike her down in self-defense.

When Sail returned to Holden, Lucian reappeared alive, explaining that Norma's sacrifice had been an elaborate ruse designed to crush Sail's will to resist. Broken by despair, Sail surrendered to his fate. Lucian utilized the completed Codex of Ouma to summon an immense spire of bone and flesh above Holden, encasing Sail in a chrysalis on the upper tiers. The ritual was ultimately derailed when Aval attacked the cocoon, forcing the entity Warrior of Black Corrosion to awaken prematurely, precipitating the Order's final ruin.

## Key Members

- **Lucian the Glorious**: Supreme leader and high priest.
- **Aval the Conqueror**: Volatile warrior who embraced the baptism for combat prowess.
- **Ignazio of Greed**: Operative overseeing Harborlands affairs.
- **Norma**: Devotee who raised Sail as an adoptive mother.
- **Gaffaud the Blood Bishop**: Ambitious leader who led a splinter faction in the Winterlands.
- **Sail**: Youth groomed from infancy to serve as the vessel of the Dark Will.
- **Shangpio**: Wealthy merchant confederate who financed cult operations in Canalbrine.

## Known Strongholds

- **Holden**: Ancestral village in the Brightlands where Sail was raised.
- **Underground Sanctuary**: Hidden fortress in the Crestlands near Flamechurch.
- **Order of Blood Chapel**: Secret ceremonial site located behind Shangpio Company's Manse in Canalbrine.
- **Ashen Ridge**: Cult redoubt situated in the Wildlands.
