# Canines

**Canines** are a family of [mammalian](mammalians.md) doglike creatures found throughout Solistia. Although many are feral monsters, some can be tamed or kept as domestic pets.

One notable type of canine are [direwolves](direwolves.md), which hunt in packs throughout the land. Ordinary domesticated dogs can also be found in cities across the realm, including [New Delsta](../locations/new_delsta.md) and [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md). [Throné](../characters/throne.md) notably keeps a pet dog, which she feeds dried strawberries.

## Varieties

### Guard Dogs
**Guard dogs** are ferocious canines trained to patrol restricted facilities. Most resemble dobermans, possessing brown fur and lean bodies, and wear collars or chains around their necks. They can inflict terror with their clawing attacks. A notable example is [Garnet](../characters/garnet.md), a trained hound belonging to the [Roque Company](roque_company.md).

### Hyaenas
**Hyaenas** are small, gray-brown canines found in dusty regions such as the [Wildlands](../locations/wildlands.md). They attack by biting and can poison their prey.

### Foxes
**Foxes** are small, agile canines with bristling fur and fluffy black-tipped tails. Woodlands counterparts come in shades of orange and brown and can inflict blindness, while those that dwell in cold climates are white-furred and can harness ice magic. Stronger species feature bushier tails.

### Lājackals
**Lājackals** are a unique species of jackal native to the island of [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md). Associated with the sun, they possess orange-brown fur and bear a distinctive white sun-shaped marking on their backs. The only known lājackal, [Akalā](../characters/akala.md), is capable of human speech, though whether this trait is common to his species or unique to him is unknown.
