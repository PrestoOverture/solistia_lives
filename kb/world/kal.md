# Kal

> *"Long ago... on this land... there came an uninvited guest. The Archmage D'arqest. He called forth the night. The night brought beasts. Beasts that devoured the people. In our hour of need, only one rose to challenge the Archmage. One by the name of Kal. Pure of heart, Kal conjured the azure flame and ushered in a new dawn upon the land. The power of night now rests in the Nameless Village. It awaits the dark rite that shall awaken it once more. None must go there. The azure flame must be protected. We follow in the footsteps of our hero. We are the Keepers of the Flame. The People of Kal."*
> —Mural in the [Fellsun Ruins](../locations/fellsun_ruins.md)

The **Kal**, also known as the **Keepers of the Flame**, were an ancient lineage and order dedicated to safeguarding the azure Sacred Flame against the darkness. They established their stronghold in the [Wildlands](../locations/wildlands.md) at the Kal Ruins, later known as the [Fellsun Ruins](../locations/fellsun_ruins.md). Members of the Kal were recognizable by tattoos depicting the Sacred Flame marked upon the backs of their hands or across their foreheads.

## History

In the distant past, the wicked archmage [D'arqest](../characters/darqest.md) summoned the night and unleashed hordes of predatory beasts that devoured the inhabitants of the land. In this dark hour, a pure-hearted hero named Kal rose to challenge the archmage. Kal conjured the azure flame—one of the aspects of the [Sacred Flame](sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md)—and brought a new dawn to Solistia, vanquishing D'arqest. The sealed power of that ancient night was left dormant in the [Nameless Village](../locations/nameless_village.md), under strict injunction that none should disturb it.

Following Kal's victory, his successors formed the Keepers of the Flame. They swore to protect the azure flame and guard Solistia from the encroaching [Shadow](shadow.md), inhabiting the sacred Kal sanctuary for generations.

Approximately thirty years before the journey of the eight travelers, the [Moonshade Order](moonshade_order.md) discovered the sanctuary and launched a brutal slaughter against the Kal, murdering the inhabitants and scattering their bones throughout the ruins.

Decades later, the inquisitor [Temenos](../characters/temenos.md) journeyed into the Fellsun Ruins during his investigation into the Moonshade Order. There he deciphered the murals recording Kal's duel with D'arqest, which concealed the chamber housing one of Solistia's four Sacred Flames.

## Known Members

- [Kaldena](../characters/kaldena.md): Former captain of the Sacred Guard who secretly belonged to the Kal, but whose despair led her to serve the Moonshade Order.
- [Vados](../characters/vados.md): An architect and descendant of the Kal recruited into the Moonshade Order's schemes.
