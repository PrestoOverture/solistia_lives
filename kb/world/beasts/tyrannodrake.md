# Tyrannodrake

The **Tyrannodrake** is a massive, heavily built winged [drake](../species/drakes.md) and powerful optional beast encountered in *Octopath Traveler II*. It makes its lair within the sea cavern of the [Curious Nest](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/curious_nest.md) across the waters of Solistia, where it jealously guards a secluded treasure trove.

## Characteristics & Behavior

As an apex variety of drake, the Tyrannodrake possesses a thick reptilian hide and ferocious offensive power:
- **Rotting Breath**: The drake can gather vast reserves of power to exhale a noxious, vitality-draining cloud that erodes the life force and defenses of its prey.
- **Debilitating Attacks**: It assaults intruders with vicious claw strikes capable of inflicting blindness or instilling mortal terror, alongside dark elemental attacks such as Eclipse.
- **Adaptive Scales**: Its scales wriggle and undergo rapid physical mutation, demonstrating an adaptive evolution that alters and reinforces its defensive fortitude as it sustains injury.
