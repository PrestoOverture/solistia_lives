# Steam Tank Obsidian

The **Steam Tank Obsidian** is a heavily armored steam-powered train developed by the [Roque Company](../factions/roque_company.md) as its secret weapon and the personal project of company head [Roque Brilliante](../../characters/partitio/roque_brilliante.md).

> "*Behold the pinnacle of the [Roque Company](../factions/roque_company.md)'s technological wizardry... Steam Tank Obsidian...!*"
> — Roque Brilliante

## Design and Features

Representing the pinnacle of the Roque Company's mechanical engineering, the tank is constructed around a powerful steam engine. Its formidable exterior consists of heavy forward armor—a reinforced glacis plate—alongside an exhaust smokestack and a mounted artillery cannon capable of heavy bombardment. The machine is piloted directly by Roque.

## Story

When [Partitio Yellowil](../../characters/travelers/partitio.md) raises the staggering sum of 80 billion leaves to purchase the patent rights to the steam engine to benefit the world, Roque refuses to relinquish the technology. Instead, Roque boards the Steam Tank Obsidian at the [Roque Island: Headquarters](../../locations/harborlands/towns/roque_island_headquarters.md) on [Roque Island](../../locations/harborlands/towns/roque_island.md) and unleashes the war machine to eliminate Partitio, leading to its destruction.
