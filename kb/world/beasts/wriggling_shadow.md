# Wriggling Shadow

The **Wriggling Shadow** is a dark manifestation of the [Shadow](../lore/shadow.md) encountered along the [Animal Trail](../../locations/leaflands/dungeons/animal_trail.md) during Part II of the apothecary and hunter Crossed Paths storyline.

## Characteristics

Unlike normal beasts of the wild, the Wriggling Shadow possesses no natural animal scent, as observed by [Ochette](../../characters/travelers/ochette.md). It radiates an unnatural darkness capable of shrouding surrounding woodland paths and disorienting travelers.

## Story

When [Ochette](../../characters/travelers/ochette.md) and [Castti Florenz](../../characters/travelers/castti.md) return to the [Animal Trail](../../locations/leaflands/dungeons/animal_trail.md), they discover the entire forest cloaked in deep shadow, with the Wriggling Shadow blocking their way. Before they can fight together, Castti is incapacitated by a sudden dizzy spell, leaving Ochette to battle and drive off the shadowy being alone.

Though wounded and repelled, the entity retreats into the gloom after having already injured [Wooly-Ooly](../../characters/others/dire_duorduor.md). Deep within the woods, the entity subsequently morphs into a larger, more menacing form known as the [Creeping Shadow](creeping_shadow.md). Only after the travelers destroy this evolved form is the oppressive darkness lifted and the forest restored to normal.
