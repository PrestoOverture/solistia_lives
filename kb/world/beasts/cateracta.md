# Cateracta

**Cateracta**, also revered as the **Sea God**, is one of the three [Creatures of Legend](../lore/creatures_of_legend.md) of Solistia, alongside [Tera](tera.md) and [Glacis](glacis.md). Four centuries ago, she played a vital role in defending the realm against the catastrophic [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../lore/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md).

## History

Four hundred years ago, Cateracta joined the other legendary beasts to prevent the Night of the Scarlet Moon from overwhelming the island of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md). Following the calamity's end, the three guardian beasts dispersed across Solistia, with Cateracta making her domain in the waters surrounding [Conning Creek](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md) in the Harborlands.

Years later, a catastrophic tidal wave threatened to wipe out Conning Creek. Cateracta intervened to shield the town and struck down the deluge with a titanic blow, saving the settlement at the cost of exhausting her immense vitality. In her weakened and vulnerable state, she was ambushed by [Petrichor](../../characters/others/petrichor.md), the deadly Dark Hunter. A band of hunters led by [Alpione](../../characters/ochette/alpione.md) fought desperately to protect the divine beast, but Petrichor ultimately slew Cateracta, wiping out all of Alpione's companions in the process.

Before she perished, Cateracta managed to lay a single egg within the nearby [Cavern of the Sea God](../../locations/harborlands/dungeons/cavern_of_the_sea_god.md) so her lineage and sacred guardian duty would endure. Alpione, the sole survivor of the defense, took upon herself the burden of watching over the nest for years. During [Ochette](../../characters/ochette/ochette.md)'s journey, the hunter reaches the cavern and helps hatch the egg into the young beast [Acta](../../characters/ochette/acta.md). Ochette welcomes Acta to her journey to avert the return of the Scarlet Moon, finally allowing Alpione to lay her long burden to rest.

### The Bones of the Sea God

Cateracta's colossal skeleton remains visible on an islet in [Conning Creek: Harbor](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek_harbor.md). When coastal winds gust through her hollow ribs, they produce a haunting acoustic reverberation reminiscent of a mournful wail. Over the decades, opportunistic scavengers and looters stripped her remains of valuable scales, whiskers, and claws for commercial gain.
