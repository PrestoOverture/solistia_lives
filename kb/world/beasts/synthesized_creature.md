# Synthesized Creature

The **Synthesized Creature** is a chimera created by [Harvey](../../characters/osvald/harvey.md) and kept within his [Underground Laboratory](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/underground_laboratory.md). During the events of Chapter 4 of [Osvald](../../characters/travelers/osvald.md)'s story, it burst free alongside the [Cobbled Creature](cobbled_creature.md) and [Deformed Creature](deformed_creature.md) to confront Osvald and his companions.

## Overview

The Synthesized Creature appears to be a monstrous synthesis of a regular Frost Bear and a Manticore.
