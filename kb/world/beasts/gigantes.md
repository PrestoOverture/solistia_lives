# Gigantes

The **Gigantes** is a colossal, armored giant [lizard](../species/reptilians.md) encountered in *Octopath Traveler II*. It makes its lair in the remote reaches of the [Nameless Isle](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/nameless_isle.md).

## Nature & Characteristics

Classified among the greater reptilians of the realm, the Gigantes is an apex beast defined by its heavy armored plating and immense physical resilience. The creature constantly hardens its scales throughout battle, reinforcing its natural shields against harm and locking away its bodily vulnerabilities.

## Behavior & Combat

In battle, the Gigantes combines raw physical brute force with elemental fire. It can exhale searing flames to engulf entire groups of travelers, shake the ground with stunning seismic tremors, and consume vitality to sustain itself. When pushed to gather its vast power, the beast unleashes a devastating assault known as Fell Swoop, greatly bolstering its physical and elemental offensive strength.
