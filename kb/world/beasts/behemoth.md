# Behemoth

The **Behemoth** is a cyclopean [mammoth](../species/ungulates.md) and powerful optional beast encountered in *Octopath Traveler II*, where it makes its lair in the depths of the [Sinking Ruins](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/sinking_ruins.md) in Solistia.

## Biology & Adaptation

Unlike earlier documented specimens from Orsterra that inhabited the frozen Tomb of the Imperator, the Behemoth residing in Solistia has adapted to the humid, sweltering heat of jungle biomes. In adapting to this jungle climate, the Solistian Behemoth developed an acute vulnerability to ice.

Apothecaries know the Behemoth for its eye, which possesses sinister petrifying properties similar to Redeye. When harvested, an apothecary can brew a behemoth's eye into a concoction capable of inducing petrification in foes.

## Behavior & Combat

In the depths of the Sinking Ruins, the Behemoth is an imposing physical threat. It assaults intruders with silences, seismic tremors, fissures, and violent stomps. Most dangerous is its single eye, which can cast a petrifying spell upon its prey. If a victim is turned to stone, the beast attempts to immediately shatter them with a crushing blow. It is also capable of rejuvenating itself in battle through hydration.
