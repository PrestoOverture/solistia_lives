# Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon

The **Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon** is a tragic, monstrous creature born from the corruption and torture of [Ochette](../../characters/ochette/ochette.md)'s unchosen companion ([Akalā](../../characters/ochette/akala.md) or [Mahina](../../characters/ochette/mahina.md)). It serves as the catastrophic harbinger of the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../lore/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md).

## Origin & Corruption

During Ochette's youth on Toto'haha, the village warden [Juvah](../../characters/ochette/juvah.md) presented her with two rare animals, inviting her to select one as a lifelong companion. Upon Ochette choosing and naming one, the rejected creature was consumed by an instantaneous shroud of dark energy, turning violently upon the young hunter before fleeing into the wilderness.

The dark aura emanating from the abandoned animal drew the attention of the [Moonshade Order](../factions/moonshade_order.md). Cult acolyte [Petrichor](../../characters/others/petrichor.md) captured the beast and delivered it to [Harvey](../../characters/osvald/harvey.md), who subjected it to years of alchemical torture and experiments to amplify and mutate its malice.

Driven mad by agony, resentment, and the shadowy power forced into its body, the creature transformed into the Darkling. To extinguish one of the realm's [Sacred Flames](../lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md), Petrichor ultimately sacrificed herself to the beast, feeding its dark appetite.

## The Night of the Scarlet Moon

During the onset of the Night of the Scarlet Moon, the Darkling returns to Toto'haha and confronts Ochette atop [Moonview Cliff](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/moonview_cliff.md). Infused with horrific power, the beast effortlessly deflects attacks from the mighty Creatures of Legend, [Tera](tera.md) and [Glacis](glacis.md). Ochette observes that the creature reeks intensely of malice, while her animal partner can hear the tortured creature weeping and wailing from deep within its corrupted spirit.

Depending on Ochette's original companion choice, the beast takes one of two forms:
- **Lājackal of the Sorrowful Moon**: The corrupted form taken if Ochette chose Mahina, manifesting ferocious physical aggression and shadow claws.
- **Malamaowl of the Sorrowful Moon**: The corrupted form taken if Ochette chose Akalā, wielding twisted elemental magics of frost, storm, and darkness.

## Fate

After an agonizing clash on the shore, the lingering alchemical mutations collapse, and the Darkling is defeated. Lying mortally wounded upon the beach, the beast's frenzy finally clears. Ochette's companion reflects that but for a single choice years prior, their fates would have been reversed. Ochette approaches and gently consoles the dying creature in its final moments, granting it peaceful solace before its physical form dissipates away into the night, expressing the tender hope that they might meet under kinder stars if it is ever reborn into the world.
