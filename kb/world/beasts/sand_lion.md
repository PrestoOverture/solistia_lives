# Sand Lion

> "*That sounds like...a sand lion. A beast that lives in the sands 'round here. Usually pretty docile. All the fightin' must've set the thing off. Once one of 'em goes berserk, nothin' to do but run for the hills until it calms.*"  
> —*[Edmund](../../characters/castti/edmund.md)*

The **Sand Lion** is a massive antlion-like beast that resides in the sands of [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md) in Solistia, categorized among [arthropods](../species/arthropods.md).

## Overview & Behavior

Although sand lions are normally docile creatures that make their home in desert areas such as the [Sand Lion's Den](../../locations/hinoeuma/dungeons/sand_lions_den.md), violent disturbances can drive them into a frenzy. Once a sand lion goes berserk, escaping its territory until it calms is typically the only safe recourse.

In combat, a sand lion can harden its armored carapace and assault prey with its crushing vice grip, summon falling rocks, whip up blinding sandstorms, and drag its victims down into treacherous pits of shifting quicksand.

## Story

During [Castti Florenz](../../characters/castti/castti.md)'s travels through [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md), the brutal civil war in the region agitated a local sand lion. Driven into a frenzy by the surrounding conflict, the beast began prowling near the fighting, dragging wounded soldiers into quicksand pits. Castti ventured into the [Sand Lion's Den](../../locations/hinoeuma/dungeons/sand_lions_den.md) and fended off the monster to protect the surviving troops and townsfolk.
