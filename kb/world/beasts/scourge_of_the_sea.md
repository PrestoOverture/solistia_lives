# Scourge of the Sea

The **Scourge of the Sea** is a mythical, colossal [aquatic](../species/aquatics.md) monster dwelling within [The Sundering Sea](../../locations/sundering_sea/the_sundering_sea.md). It lurks within a massive whirlpool churning in the ocean strait that divides the island of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md) from the [Brightlands](../../locations/brightlands/brightlands.md).

## Lore & Ecology

Alongside the [Battle-Worn Shark](battle_worn_shark.md), the Scourge of the Sea is one of the greatest apex threats terrorizing maritime routes across Solistia. Emerging under the cover of night, the beast churns towering dark vortexes and tidal surges capable of destroying ships and dragging whole vessels down into the deeps.

Sailors and adventurers must conquer the beast in order to fulfill a trial for the Hunter Guild and obtain a Hunter License. Vanquishing this aquatic terror calms the impassable whirlpool, granting maritime navigators safe passage toward [The Lost Isle](../../locations/sundering_sea/wilds/the_lost_isle.md) and the [Gate of Finis](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/the_gate_of_finis_octopath_traveler_ii.md).
