# Tera

**Tera** is one of the three ancient [Creatures of Legend](../lore/creatures_of_legend.md) native to Solistia, alongside [Glacis](glacis.md) and [Cateracta](cateracta.md). Described as an embodiment of raw earthen power, he resembles a colossal tortoise with a stocky body and a rugged carapace formed of hardened volcanic rock and lava. Though referred to impersonally as "it" in general speech, Tera possesses a masculine voice and an easily provoked, fiery temper.

> *"Tera... A vision of fierce valor, as broad and mighty as the earth!"*
> — Master [Juvah](../../characters/ochette/juvah.md)

## Lore and History

In the distant past, Tera saved the surrounding lands from catastrophe when a violently erupting volcano threatened total destruction. With a single colossal strike, Tera shattered the mountain itself, reducing the fiery peak into the vast canyon valley surrounding [Crackridge](../../locations/wildlands/towns/crackridge.md). Following this feat, Tera made his home deep underground within the magma-carved caverns known as the [Bed of the Titan](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/bed_of_the_titan.md).

Prior to the events of [Ochette](../../characters/travelers/ochette.md)'s journey, Tera engaged in a ferocious struggle against the Moonshade Order's [Dark Hunter](../../characters/others/petrichor.md), leaving both combatants heavily wounded. Tera retreated into a deep hibernation within the Bed of the Titan to heal his grievous injuries. Over the eons, his shifting movements beneath the earth were immense enough to send tremors and earthquakes through the town of Crackridge above.

## Ochette's Journey

Seeking to gather the legendary beasts to ward off the encroaching darkness of the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../lore/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md), Ochette travels to the Wildlands. Venturing into the subterranean heat of the Bed of the Titan, she awakens Tera with the help of her animal companion ([Mahina](../../characters/ochette/mahina.md) or [Akalā](../../characters/ochette/akala.md)). Infuriated by the rude disruption to his recovery, Tera attacks the hunter in a blind fury.

After being subdued by Ochette, Tera calms down, acknowledges her strength and purpose, and agrees to lend his strength to the defense of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md). During the climax of the Night of the Scarlet Moon, Tera proves instrumental in safeguarding the island, smashing a massive chasm into the earth to crush and swallow swarms of monsters corrupted by the [Shadow](../lore/shadow.md).
