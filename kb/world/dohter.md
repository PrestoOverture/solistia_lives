# Dohter

**Dohter**, also known as **The Charitable**, is one of the eight patron gods of Solistia. Her divine domains are Nature and Harmony, and she is worshipped by apothecaries. Her sacred sigil is the Winding Tree.

## Lore and Depiction

Dohter is depicted as a smiling apothecary holding a mortar and pestle. According to Solistian legend, she bestowed the holy art of concoction upon humanity.

In the sacred play during the opening sequence of [Temenos](../characters/temenos.md)'s journey, she appears shrouded in an indistinct white robe.

## History

In ancient Solistian myth, Dohter fought alongside the other gods against the wicked god [Vide](vide.md). She was among the eight deities who sacrificed their power to defeat Vide, entrusting what remained to [Aelfric](aelfric.md) before falling into a deep slumber:

> *"We give our power...to you... You must...stop...[Vide](vide.md)..."*

In modern Solistia, she is venerated at the [Altar of the Charitable](../locations/altar_of_the_charitable.md).
