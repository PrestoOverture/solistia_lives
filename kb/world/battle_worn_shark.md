# Battle-Worn Shark

The **Battle-Worn Shark** is a vicious [shark](aquatics.md) encountered in *Octopath Traveler II*. Alongside the [Scourge of the Sea](scourge_of_the_sea.md), it is one of two formidable bosses prowling the open waters of [The Sundering Sea](../locations/the_sundering_sea.md).

## Habitat & Sightings

The Battle-Worn Shark can be encountered while sailing [The Sundering Sea](../locations/the_sundering_sea.md) by approaching a massive dorsal fin patrolling the entrance to the bay immediately south of [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md). After its initial defeat, it can rarely appear as a daytime encounter on the Sundering Sea. Felling the beast yields the ancient Lost Tribe's Blade.

## Behavior & Characteristics

The Battle-Worn Shark is an aggressive marine predator that attacks seafaring travelers and ships. In combat, it can ram and bite ships directly, crush travelers with its mandibles, and inflict debilitating terror. The shark possesses a keen sense of smell for prey that is frightened; upon detecting fear in a traveler, it singles them out and unleashes a ferocious feeding frenzy. When pushed to its limits, the beast gathers vast reserves of power to feast upon its opponents, draining their life force to sustain its own health.
