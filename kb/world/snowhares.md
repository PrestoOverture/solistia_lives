# Snowhares

> "*The [Blacksnakes](blacksnakes.md) have long been our allies, but the Snowhares... We're capricious creatures. The Snakes have grown old. They're not as tough as they used to be. The fact that they've sent a weak little girl like you just proves it.*"  
> —*[Bergomi](../characters/bergomi.md)*

The **Snowhares** are a gang of thieves based out of [Winterbloom: Thieves Quarter](../locations/winterbloom_thieves_quarter.md). Although historically allied with the [Blacksnakes](blacksnakes.md), their capricious nature and mounting ambition eventually created bitter rivalry between the two criminal organizations.

## History

Under the ruthless leadership of [Bergomi](../characters/bergomi.md), the Snowhares dealt heavily in burglary, narcotics, and human trafficking. Recognizing the rising threat to their supremacy, [Father](../characters/father.md), one of the heads of the Blacksnakes, dispatched [Throné Anguis](../characters/throne.md) to the [Snowhares' Den](../locations/snowhares_den.md) to eliminate Bergomi. Throné bested Bergomi in combat, and Father delivered the finishing blow, though surviving gang members fled with a captive woman held near the entrance of their hideout.

Following Bergomi's death, [Plukk](../characters/plukk.md) took command of the remnant Snowhares alongside her companions, [Mikk](../characters/mikk_octopath_traveler_ii.md) and [Makk](../characters/makk_octopath_traveler_ii.md). Plukk abolished the gang's human trafficking trade, redirecting their efforts toward local burglaries in [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md). 

Unsatisfied with their meager takings, Plukk devised a scheme to infiltrate and plunder Lady [Melia](../characters/melia.md)'s estate. She sent Melia a letter claiming that she and the Snowhares had reformed and wished to enter honest service as house staff. Melia immediately saw through the ruse, yet chose to extend genuine mercy by offering them positions as her estate gardeners. Plukk accepted the offer, permanently retiring the Snowhares from their life of crime.
