# Birdians

**Birdians** are [avian](avians.md) [demihumans](demihumans.md) found throughout Solistia, resembling owls, ravens, and seagulls. According to the [Mercantile Manuscript](../stories/masoud_script.md), they possess the ingenuity to craft primitive tools and axes, and they wield crafted weapons in battle. Their tribes are led by dominant leaders known as birdkings.

## Breeds

- **Vagrant Birdians:** Dwelling across the [Sundering Sea](../locations/the_sundering_sea.md) and roosting among maritime ruins such as the [Shipwreck of the Empress](../locations/shipwreck_of_the_empress.md), these birdians resemble seagulls with black-tipped white plumage. They dress in cloth garments, turbans, and feathered headdresses; common members fight with bows, while their birdkings wield curved scimitars.
- **Ancient Birdians:** Native to the arid [Wildlands](../locations/wildlands.md), this dark-feathered breed resembles ravens or crows. Having adapted to deep subterranean environments, they make their homes within the [Unfinished Tunnel](../locations/unfinished_tunnel.md) and the [Ivory Ravine](../locations/ivory_ravine.md). Adorned in feathered garments and elaborate headdresses, common warriors wield daggers and blades, whereas birdkings wield bows.
- **Woodland Birdians:** Inhabiting the lush forests of the [Leaflands](../locations/leaflands.md), these birdians resemble brown-feathered owls with piercing gazes. They adorn themselves with necklaces and trinkets woven from leaves and foliage, and fight primarily with spears.
