# Items (Solistia)

In Solistia, various concoctions, minerals, and flora carry distinct historical, cultural, and scientific lore.

## Lore and Traditions

### Bottling Arts
The practice of bottling—specifically the tactical deployment of powdered substances such as poison dust, sleeping dust, and befuddling dust—was developed as a specialized defensive art by the Rekohn Clan of Solistia.

### Soulstones
According to popular myth in Solistia, soulstones are thought to hold the spirits of slumbering entities. Modern scientific inquiry, however, suggests they are created from the crystallized remains of magically potent monsters, presenting the theoretical foundation for their artificial or mass production.

### Divine Prohibition on Cultivation
According to Solistian legend, the gods decreed a ban on the cultivation of grapes and plums, seeking to curb intemperance, strife, and the escalation of warfare among mortals.

## Notable Items and Flora

- **Lump of Silver**: The first official purchase secured by the merchant [Partitio](../../characters/partitio/partitio.md).
- **Purse of Hope**: The pouch containing the travel savings gathered by [Agnea](../../characters/agnea/agnea.md) for her journey.
- **Medicinal Herbs**: Wild plants such as Slumber Sage and the valuable Herb of Serenity, gathered across Solistia to calm and heal the sick.
