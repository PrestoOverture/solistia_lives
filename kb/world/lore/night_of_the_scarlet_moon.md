# Night of the Scarlet Moon

> *"Moon and sun cross paths in the sky, world crosses path with world, and calamity comes to the island."*
> —Master [Juvah](../../characters/ochette/juvah.md)

The **Night of the Scarlet Moon** is a recurring cataclysmic phenomenon that befalls the island of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md) once every four hundred years. It forms the central threat during the journey of the hunter [Ochette](../../characters/travelers/ochette.md).

## Phenomenon and Origin

According to island lore, the catastrophe occurs when the otherworldly [Shadow](shadow.md) surges across Toto'haha, dyeing the celestial moon blood-red. Under the influence of this dark power, the island's indigenous wildlife and fauna are warped into ferocious dark beasts driven by mindless aggression. The recurrence is tied directly to the Shadow, believed to coincide with the periodic waxing of the dark god [Vide](../gods/vide.md) from within the sunken seal of [Vidania](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/vidania.md).

In the months leading up to the fateful night, unnatural portents manifest across the wilderness, including corrupted hatchlings and the emergence of the [Dark Entity](../../characters/ochette/dark_entity.md).

## The Prophecy of the Three Wardens

Ancient tradition among the beastlings holds that the cataclysm can be repelled only by uniting the power of Toto'haha's three Creatures of Legend:
- [Cateracta](../beasts/cateracta.md), Warden of the Sea
- [Tera](../beasts/tera.md), Warden of Terra
- [Glacis](../beasts/glacis.md), Warden of Ice

Under the guidance of Village Chief [Juvah](../../characters/ochette/juvah.md), Ochette journeys across Solistia to seek out the three legendary beasts and earn their trust.

## The Defense of Toto'haha

When the scarlet moon finally rises, hordes of corrupted beasts descend upon [Beasting Village](../../locations/totohaha/towns/beasting_village.md). With the assistance of the three Creatures of Legend and her companion, Ochette battles the invading swarm and confronts the apex corrupted horror, the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../beasts/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md). Upon the darkling's defeat, the encroaching darkness dissipates, dawn breaks across the horizon, and the crimson glow recedes from the moon, restoring peace to the island.
