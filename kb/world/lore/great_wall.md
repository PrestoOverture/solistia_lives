# Great Wall

> *"A local legend states this massive fortification was raised in a single night by the [gods](../gods/gods.md) themselves. Sturdy and strong, yet intricate in its design, it is clear that this wall is indeed a feat of engineering beyond what modern civilization is capable of."*
> —The [Mercantile Manuscript](../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md)

The **Great Wall** is an ancient, colossal fortification situated in the [Southern Stormhail Snows](../../locations/winterlands/wilds/southern_stormhail_snows.md) of the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md). Renowned for its imposing scale and intricate masonry, popular Solistian folklore claims the wall was erected by the [gods](../gods/gods.md) in a single night. However, scholars—including the head of the scholars' guild—attribute its construction to the fallen northern realm of [Fj'all](../nations/fjall.md).

## History and Lore

Constructed centuries ago at an undetermined point in history, the wall is widely believed to be merely a surviving fragment of a much vaster fortress complex, drawing archeologists and scholars who scour its expanse for ancient knowledge.

Approximately 820 years ago, a massive cavity opened at the site, which came to be known as the Pit of [D'arqest](../../characters/others/darqest.md). This geographic and mystical anomaly ignited a bitter conflict between the kingdoms of Fj'all and [Delstasch](../nations/delstasch.md). Over the subsequent centuries, countless researchers lost their lives attempting to plumb the depths of the pit, fueling widespread fascination and study into the mysteries of the [One True Magic](one_true_magic.md).

Directly [Beneath the Wall](../../locations/winterlands/dungeons/beneath_the_wall.md) lies the subterranean [Infernal Castle](../../locations/winterlands/dungeons/infernal_castle.md), which shelters the ancient relic known as the Stone of Truth. The presence of these sunken ramparts suggests that the Infernal Castle may be the forgotten citadel to which the Great Wall was once anchored.

## Legends

According to the local "Legend of the Eaten Eight," an expedition party of eight individuals was once mysteriously swallowed whole by the Great Wall. Because of this ominous superstition, contemporary research teams operating in the area strictly limit the size of their survey parties, wary of repeating the fate of their predecessors.
