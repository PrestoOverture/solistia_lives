# Shadow

> "*The Shadow is all-devouring. It swallows the light of which our world is woven.*"  
> —*Master [Juvah](../../characters/ochette/juvah.md)*

The **Shadow** is an otherworldly malevolent power associated with the wicked god [Vide](../gods/vide.md) and his disciples.

Often channeled through blood sacrifice, the Shadow manifests in the form of dark mist or black fire, corrupting its victims and driving its wielders mad with bloodlust. It feeds on the basest instincts of the human heart: greed, hatred, resentment, jealousy, and rage. Beings consumed or corrupted by the Shadow typically dissolve into dark mist upon their death. The primary gateway to the Shadow in Solistia is situated on [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md) at [Rifted Rock](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/rifted_rock.md), where the archmage [D'arqest](../../characters/others/darqest.md) first summoned its power in ancient times.

## Manifestations in Solistia

### The Night of the Scarlet Moon

As prophesied every four centuries, the island of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md) suffers a calamity known as the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md). During this occurrence, the moon turns blood red and shadow beasts surge across the island to slaughter its inhabitants. Historically, the three Creatures of Legend—[Cateracta](../beasts/cateracta.md), [Tera](../beasts/tera.md), and [Glacis](../beasts/glacis.md)—intervened to repel the darkness.

To prevent their interference during the next cycle, [Petrichor](../../characters/others/petrichor.md), the Dark Hunter of the [Moonshade Order](../factions/moonshade_order.md), struck down a weakened Cateracta, wounded Tera into a deep regenerative slumber, and drove Glacis mad with grief by having hunters crush her egg. Petrichor also captured a shadow-tainted beast and delivered it to [Harvey](../../characters/osvald/harvey.md), who transformed it into the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../beasts/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md). Petrichor eventually sacrificed herself to fuel the darkling and extinguish the [Sacred Flame](sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) in the [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md). The calamity was averted by the young hunter [Ochette](../../characters/travelers/ochette.md), who rallied the legendary beasts, hatched Cateracta's offspring, and destroyed the darkling.

### The Poisonous Purple Rain

[Trousseau](../../characters/castti/trousseau.md), an apothecary of [Eir's Apothecaries](../factions/eirs_apothecaries.md), was swayed by [Claude](../../characters/throne/claude_blacksnakes.md) at [Lostseed](../../locations/brightlands/towns/lostseed.md) and inducted into the Moonshade Order after reading the *Book of Night*. Believing life to be pure suffering and death the only release, he combined phosphoal with Shadow energy. When burned into rain clouds, the substance formed a toxic purple downpour. Exposure caused progressive fatigue, paralysis, swelling black markings, and severe hemorrhage as victims' blood turned black before death. After annihilating the town of [Healeaks](../../locations/brightlands/towns/abandoned_village.md) and the majority of his comrades, Trousseau brought the mixture to [Timberain Castle](../../locations/leaflands/dungeons/timberain_castle.md) to drench [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md), where he was defeated and killed by [Castti Florenz](../../characters/travelers/castti.md).

### The Perversion of Magic

Scholar [Harvey](../../characters/osvald/harvey.md) of [Montwise](../../locations/crestlands/towns/montwise.md) joined the Moonshade Order while studying the [One True Magic](one_true_magic.md) alongside [Osvald V. Vanstein](../../characters/travelers/osvald.md). Believing Osvald's family held the secret keys to this arcane power, Harvey burned Osvald's home, framed Osvald for their murders, used his wife [Rita](../../characters/osvald/rita_lumina.md)'s blood to craft the [Grieving Golem](../../characters/osvald/grieving_golem.md), and brainwashed his daughter [Elena](../../characters/osvald/elena_vanstein.md). Harvey came to believe that the Shadow itself was the One True Magic, utilizing the remains of [D'arqest](../../characters/others/darqest.md) and Elena's blood at [Duskruin Shrine](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/duskruin_shrine.md) to complete the *Book of Demons*. Osvald countered Harvey's dark arts with the genuine One True Magic, defeating Harvey, who vanished when his attempt to summon further shadow power consumed him.

### The Blood of Ku

Generations prior, the ruling Ku clan intermingled with the lineage of archmage [D'arqest](../../characters/others/darqest.md), granting their royal line access to Shadow arts at the risk of succumbing to inward darkness, symbolized by the heirloom Darkblood Blade. King [Jigo Ku](../../characters/hikari/jigo_ku.md) fathered [Mugen](../../characters/hikari/mugen.md) and [Hikari](../../characters/hikari/hikari_shadow.md). While Hikari continually wrestled to restrain a violent shadow alter ego within himself, Mugen embraced the dark arts, overthrowing and murdering Jigo. When Hikari returned to reclaim the capital, Mugen drew upon the Darkblood Blade to become the monstrous [Enshrouded King](../../characters/hikari/mugen.md). Hikari defeated Mugen and conquered his own inner darkness through the strength of his companions.

### The Journey for the Dawn

In an ultimate effort to resurrect their deity, leaders of the Moonshade Order extinguished the four Sacred Flames across Solistia—at [Flamechurch](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch.md), [Fellsun Ruins](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/fellsun_ruins.md), [Tranquil Grotto](../../locations/hinoeuma/dungeons/tranquil_grotto.md), and the [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md). This plunged Solistia into the Endless Night, where shadowy creatures infested the lands. 

The eight travelers restored each sacred fire, unmasked and defeated [Arcanette](../../characters/temenos/arcanette.md) at Flamechurch, and pursued the cult to the submerged island of [Vidania](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/vidania.md). There, strategist Kazan unveiled his true persona as [Oboro](../../characters/hikari/oboro.md) and offered himself as a sacrifice to awaken [Vide](../gods/vide.md). The eight travelers destroyed Vide in a final battle, dispelling the darkness and bringing dawn back to the realm.

## The Shadow Phenomenon

Prior to the Endless Night, travelers throughout Solistia occasionally encountered sudden manifestations known as the Shadow Phenomenon, where the landscape was cloaked in a deep violet twilight haze, local inhabitants fled, and vicious shadow creatures attacked. Defeating the lingering shadow monstrosity dispersed the haze and restored normal light. Following the ultimate defeat of Vide, the phenomenon vanished entirely from Solistia.
