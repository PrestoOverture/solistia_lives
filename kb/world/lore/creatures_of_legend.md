# Creatures of Legend

"*Long ago... there were three Creatures of Legend. [Tera](../beasts/tera.md). [Cateracta](../beasts/cateracta.md). [Glacis](../beasts/glacis.md). The three creatures watched over the island for many years before departing to become guardians of other lands. Should their might be needed again they must be called back to the island. Before the calamity of the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md).*"
—*Mural in the [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md)*

The **Creatures of Legend** are three colossal, primordial beasts venerated as the ancient protectors of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md). Every 400 years, they are summoned to assemble and shield the realm from the apocalyptic calamity known as the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md).

Each creature commands a force of nature tied to an elemental power: [Cateracta](../beasts/cateracta.md) embodies water, [Tera](../beasts/tera.md) commands earth and fire, and [Glacis](../beasts/glacis.md) controls ice and wind. Both Cateracta and Glacis reproduce via eggs despite being the sole known entities of their kind. Following an earlier age of calamity, the three beasts dispersed across Solistia to watch over disparate regions, and rallying them is the purpose of [Ochette](../../characters/travelers/ochette.md)'s pilgrimage.

## The Three Guardians

### Cateracta
Cateracta, the great sea dragon, settled near [Conning Creek: Harbor](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek_harbor.md), where she exhausted her strength shielding the settlement from a cataclysmic tidal wave. A clan of [beastlings](../species/beastling.md) journeyed from Toto'haha to serve and protect her, gradually intermingling with local humans to produce mixed lineages such as that of [Alpione](../../characters/ochette/alpione.md). Revered as the Sea God, Cateracta was eventually ambushed by human poachers led by the [Dark Hunter](../../characters/others/petrichor.md). Her defenders were massacred with Alpione as the sole survivor, and Cateracta was slain, her skeleton abandoned to beach scavengers. Before dying, however, Cateracta laid an egg, which Alpione guarded within the [Cavern of the Sea God](../../locations/harborlands/dungeons/cavern_of_the_sea_god.md).

When Ochette journeys to Conning Creek, she discovers Cateracta's remains and gains Alpione's trust. The egg hatches into a young sea serpent named [Acta](../../characters/ochette/acta.md), who journeys with Ochette to honor its mother's legacy. During the Night of the Scarlet Moon, Acta undergoes a metamorphosis, temporarily attaining Cateracta's colossal scale and power against the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](../beasts/darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md).

### Tera
Tera, the titanic horned beast, nested in the subterranean magma caverns of the [Bed of the Titan](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/bed_of_the_titan.md) near [Crackridge](../../locations/wildlands/towns/crackridge.md), expending his power to halt an erupting volcano. When the Dark Hunter tracked and assaulted him, Tera drove her off despite sustaining grievous wounds, then retreated into a centuries-long slumber. Surface activity above caused deep subterranean tremors throughout Crackridge.

Guided by the youth [Pom](../../characters/ochette/pom.md), Ochette descends into the chasm and rouses Tera with an arrow. Overcoming his fury, she befriends the titan, who travels to Toto'haha. When the Night of the Scarlet Moon falls, Tera sunders the earth with towering rifts, crushing hordes of [Shadow](shadow.md)-spawned beasts.

### Glacis
Glacis, the divine avian beast, made her roost atop the frozen crags of [Sacred Peak Altahe](../../locations/winterlands/dungeons/sacred_peak_altahe.md) north of [Stormhail](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail.md), where she poured out her magic to quell an apocalyptic blizzard. The Dark Hunter led a hunting troupe—including the hunter [Heig](../../characters/ochette/heig.md)—to destroy her. Unable to best Glacis directly, the hunters smashed Glacis's unhatched egg out of malice. In blinding anguish, Glacis buried the hunters under a massive avalanche, with only Heig and the Dark Hunter surviving. Sinking into deep grief and resentment, Glacis spent decades buffeting Stormhail with icy gales.

Ochette scales Altahe and confronts the grief-stricken beast. By reminding Glacis of her solemn oath to Toto'haha and that there is still life worth defending, Ochette heals her spirit. Glacis joins Ochette on Toto'haha, where she conjures an immense bridge of frost across the ocean waters of the [Stormy Cape](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/stormy_cape.md) to turn the tide against the encroaching darkness.
