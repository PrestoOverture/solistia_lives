# Sacred Flame (Octopath Traveler II)

> "*The truth... lies in the flame.*"
> — *[Temenos Mistral](../../characters/temenos/temenos.md)*

The **Sacred Flame** is a divine azure fire that protects Solistia, warding off the [Shadow](shadow.md) and keeping the wicked god [Vide](../gods/vide.md) imprisoned.

According to legend, after sacrificing their divine strength to defeat Vide in the mythical age, the eight [gods](../gods/gods.md) fell into a deep slumber. Their divine power endured across the realm in the form of four Sacred Flames burning at four corners of the land.

## Lore and Veneration

The Sacred Flame has been venerated in Solistia since ancient times, with historical records dating back over 1,500 years. Its largest following is organized under the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../factions/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md), guarded by the holy knights of the [Sacred Guard](../factions/sacred_guard.md). In addition, the [Kal](kal.md) people—known historically as the Keepers of the Flame—dedicated their civilization to tending and guarding the azure flame. Candles lit from the Sacred Flame possess sacred properties that ward away monsters.

Beyond its physical manifestation as holy fire, the Sacred Flame is intrinsically tied to the light residing within the human heart. Clerics in [Canalbrine](../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md) teach that "the True Sacred Flame burns always within your heart." This internal light is embodied by the eight travelers: [Temenos](../../characters/temenos/temenos.md) channels the Flame to pierce illusions and uncover hidden truths during his investigations, while [Ochette](../../characters/ochette/ochette.md) is acknowledged and protected by the First Flame, empowering her animal companion with azure flame. [Juvah](../../characters/ochette/juvah.md) recognizes this blessing as the mark of a true hunter, revealing that the isolated beastlings of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md) retain ancient understanding of the Flame.

## The Four Sacred Flames

The four Sacred Flames burn at key locations across Solistia, projecting an enduring seal over the sunken prison isle of [Vidania](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/vidania.md). While the flame in the Crestlands was placed in the public sanctuary of the Cathedral, the remaining three were concealed behind hidden murals in remote ruins. Each shrine is guarded by stone statues representing two of the eight gods:

- **The Lofty Flame**: Located at [Flamechurch: Cathedral Entrance](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch_cathedral_entrance.md), associated with [Aelfric](../gods/aelfric.md) and [Aeber](../gods/aeber.md).
- **The Warm Flame**: Located in the [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md) on Toto'haha, associated with [Draefendi](../gods/draefendi.md) and [Dohter](../gods/dohter.md).
- **The Bequeathed Flame**: Located in the [Fellsun Ruins](../../locations/wildlands/dungeons/fellsun_ruins.md) of the Wildlands, associated with [Bifelgan](../gods/bifelgan.md) and [Alephan](../gods/alephan.md).
- **The Tranquil Flame**: Located in the [Tranquil Grotto](../../locations/hinoeuma/dungeons/tranquil_grotto.md) of Hinoeuma, associated with [Brand](../gods/brand.md) and [Sealticge](../gods/sealticge.md).

## The Journey for the Dawn

In the events leading up to [The Journey for the Dawn](../../stories/main/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md), operatives of the [Moonshade Order](../factions/moonshade_order.md) infiltrated the hidden ruins of the three concealed flames. Using cursed Darkblood weapons, they carried out ritual sacrifices that snuffed out the sacred fires one by one, destabilizing the seal on Vidania. Finally, [Arcanette](../../characters/temenos/arcanette.md) performed a blood sacrifice at [Flamechurch](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch.md) to extinguish the Cathedral's Lofty Flame, plunging all of Solistia into an unbroken night of Shadow.

Guided by the Sacred Flame burning within their own spirits, the eight travelers used fragments of [Alpates](../../characters/others/alpates.md)'s sacred mirror to channel their inner light and reignite each extinguished flame. The Sacred Flames possess an innate sentience, sharing memories of the moments leading up to each sacrifice with the travelers upon rekindling. Once all four Flames were restored, beams of holy light lanced across the [Sundering Sea](../../locations/sundering_sea/the_sundering_sea.md), piercing the darkness and uncovering Vidania as it rose from the depths.
