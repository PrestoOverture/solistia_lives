# Grand Gala

> *"It's the greatest festival on the eastern continent! Entertainers and dancers from all across the realm gather there! Standing upon that stage is the greatest honor there is for performers like us!"*
> —[Tanzy](../../characters/agnea/tanzy.md)

The **Grand Gala** is an annual cultural festival held in [Merry Hills](../../locations/crestlands/towns/merry_hills.md) on the eastern continent of Solistia. Considered the premier stage for performers of all disciplines, the gala attracts dancers, musicians, and artists from across the realm. It is funded by various patrons of the arts, including the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../factions/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md).

The centerpiece of the gala is the **Festival of Grace**, a prestigious showcase held in honor of [Sealticge](../gods/sealticge.md), the Lady of Grace. Performing upon this stage is regarded as the highest possible honor for a dancer, with only a select few chosen to take part.

## History and Events

During her journey, [Agnea Bristarni](../../characters/agnea/agnea.md) first learns of the Grand Gala from [Giselle](../../characters/agnea/giselle.md)'s Traveling Troupe, resolving to travel to Merry Hills to attend the event. Renowned star dancer [Dolcinaea Luciel](../../characters/agnea/dolcinaea_luciel.md) subsequently extends a personal invitation to Agnea to perform in the Festival of Grace, framing it as a direct contest between their conflicting philosophies of stardom.

At the festival, Agnea competes against Dolcinaea before the assembled crowds. Supported by her traveling companions, friends, and family, Agnea performs her signature debut piece, "Song of Hope," overcoming Dolcinaea's performance of "All for You" and winning the adoration of the audience.
