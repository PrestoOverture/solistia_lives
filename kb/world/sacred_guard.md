# Sacred Guard

> "*The Sanctum Knights are handpicked from among the church's best. They're even called 'Godsblade' by some, and reportedly capable of felling a thousand beasts.*"  
> —*[Temenos Mistral](../characters/temenos.md)*

The **Sacred Guard** is a military organization sworn to protect all within the [Order of the Sacred Flame](order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md).

## History

The Sacred Guard was established 380 years ago by Archbishop Kinchler as an independent organization to protect the believers of the [Sacred Flame](sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) and all within the church's purview. Their influence spread throughout most of Solistia, where they would protect and take up investigations as needed. Their main headquarters are located in [Stormhail](../locations/stormhail.md), from which they have upheld order on the eastern continent for the last hundred years.

The Sacred Guard's main members are known as **Sanctum Knights**, also called **Godsblades**, who are known as "heroes of the scripture" and considered to be among the best of the organization. The leadership is responsible for testing promising members to give them a chance at knighthood through a series of trials.

After the leadership was killed by the church's inquisitor, [Temenos](../characters/temenos.md), and a lengthy investigation was conducted into their dark dealings, the Sacred Guard was stripped of its authority. Many of its members who were loyal to [Kaldena](../characters/kaldena.md) and [Cubaryi](../characters/cubaryi.md) were tried in court, forcing many of them to resign after their crimes were brought to light.

## Organization and Traditions

The Sanctum Knights operate in the name of [Brand, the Thunderblade](brand.md). 

While regular members wear blue, elite members of the Sanctum Knights—such as [Kaldena](../characters/kaldena.md), [Cubaryi](../characters/cubaryi.md), and [Ort](../characters/ort.md)—wear pink garments, distinguishing them from the rest of the guard and hinting at their secret ties to the [Shadow](shadow.md) rather than the Sacred Flame.

According to an arms merchant in Stormhail, many Sanctum Knights prefer to purchase ornate, high-priced weapons with impressive appearances. Equipment associated with the order includes the Sanctum Sword, Sanctum Knight's Armor, Sanctum Knight's Helm, and the Tattered Sacred Shield.

[Temenos](../characters/temenos.md) derisively refers to the Sacred Guard as "crows," and a crow appears in the stained glass of his story Journal.
