# Fj'all

**Fj'all** is a fallen kingdom of the [Winterlands](../locations/winterlands.md).

## History

According to the head of the Scholars Guild, Fj'all was responsible for constructing the [Great Wall](great_wall.md) across the [Southern Stormhail Snows](../locations/southern_stormhail_snows.md). Ancient texts attest that the only successfully crafted Stone of Truth—a legendary talisman said to heighten its bearer's magical powers—was created in Fj'all. In the modern era, this Stone of Truth rests within the [Infernal Castle](../locations/infernal_castle.md) beneath the Great Wall.

The Pit of [D'arqest](../characters/darqest.md) served as the catalyst for a conflict between Fj'all and the [Brightlands](../locations/brightlands.md) kingdom of [Delstasch](delstasch.md). This struggle came to be known as the War of the Great Wall.

## Lore and Connections

- Given the presence of the Stone of Truth within the Infernal Castle, the talisman may have played a pivotal role in the war between Fj'all and Delstasch. If the Infernal Castle lies within the Pit of D'arqest, the two nations may have fought over the artifact or sought the mysterious power of D'arqest's [One True Magic](one_true_magic.md).
- According to a [Clan Mei](clan_mei.md) guard, a "castle from a great empire"—likely Fj'all—once stood upon the grounds of [Castle Mei](../locations/stormhail_castle_mei.md).
- Fj'all and Delstasch are probably the two warring realms from which the prince and princess who founded the [Blacksnakes](blacksnakes.md) fled, as both kingdoms share the continent with [Lostseed](../locations/lostseed.md), the hidden settlement established by the royal runaways.
