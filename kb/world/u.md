# U

The **Kingdom of U** was an ancient nation in [Hinoeuma](../locations/hinoeuma.md) that was conquered and destroyed by the rival kingdom of [Ku](ku.md) many years earlier.

## History & Culture

The city of [Sai](../locations/sai.md) was formerly part of U. Following its conquest by Ku, many citizens of U fled eastward to the [Harborlands](../locations/harborlands.md), settling extensively in the port town of [Canalbrine](../locations/canalbrine.md).

U was renowned for its advanced engineering and technological prowess. The kingdom's architectural legacy remains evident in Canalbrine's modern canal network and lighthouse. Surviving blueprints and architectural techniques preserved by craftsmen from U are also used to restore damaged buildings in [Sai: East District](../locations/sai_east_district.md).

The strategist [Oboro](../characters/oboro.md) was originally a native of U.
