# King Iguana

> *"Aha! There you are, Mr. King Iguana! You eat too much, and you've gotten too big! It's wrong to take more than your share. The forest belongs to everyone. Sorry, but I'm here to hunt you. And then get back in time for breakfast."* — [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md)

The **King Iguana** is an abnormally massive [reptilian](reptilians.md) beast that inhabits the untamed wilds of [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md).

## Lore and Characteristics

Found along the [Path to the Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../locations/path_to_the_tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md), the King Iguana gorged itself until it grew to immense proportions, disrupting the natural harmony of the jungle. Under the hunting traditions of the beastlings, no creature may consume more than its rightful share at the expense of others. Because its gluttony endangered the balance of the ecosystem, [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md) tracked down and hunted the creature.

Later, a shadow-corrupted incarnation of a King Iguana was sighted within the blighted [Verdant Wood](../locations/verdant_wood.md).
