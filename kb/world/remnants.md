# Remnants

**Ancient Remnants** and **Constructs** are types of magical, mechanical beings found across Solistia, often serving as guardians within ancient ruins, dungeons, and fortresses. Powered by magic, they take on a variety of elemental affinities and attack any intruders who enter their domain.

## Ancient Remnants

Ancient Remnants are ancient stone automatons that resemble animated statues veined with glowing cracks. Typically found in long-abandoned ruins and dungeons, they exist in three primary forms:

### Remnants
Remnants are quadrupedal stone beasts resembling predatory animals, featuring stone tails, claws, and gaping jaws framed by rocky neck collars that give the appearance of having two mouths. Their surface is often weathered with patches of moss and marked by blue luminous fissures. Advanced Mk. II models feature sharper teeth, elongated tails, dorsal spines, and heavier front claws.

### Revenants
Revenants are colossal golems assembled from fused stone, with boulder-like torsos, elongated spindly limbs, and spirals carved into their heads where eyes would be. Like their quadruped counterparts, their stone bodies display moss and luminous fractures.

### Guardians
Guardians are Revenants armed with massive stone weaponry, said to have been crafted by the [gods](gods.md) in ancient times to protect sacred and divine sites.

## Constructs

Unlike the stone remnants of antiquity, Constructs are mechanized automatons forged from metal and animated by arcane means. Powered by magical lantern-like cores, they were originally crafted by mortal hands to patrol and defend estates, fortresses, and laboratories.

### Sentinels
Sentinels are small, hovering lantern-shaped constructs ringed with razor blades. Glowing with internal elemental energy, they typically operate and attack in swarms. According to historical lore, sentinels were engineered by an ancient civilization for automated security, and those encountered in modern times were reconstructed from surviving schematics after ancient wars. Upgraded Mk. II models feature additional blades and extended top lanterns.

### Curators
Curators are large, winged constructs with elongated metallic necks and feathers that hover aloft, trailing long streamers of cloth to present a bizarre, bird-like silhouette. Advanced Mk. II variants glow with concentrated energy and feature single glowing ocular sensors.

### Construct Guardians
Construct Guardians are massive metal bipedal automata featuring lantern-like heads and armed with giant axes. They have heavy, armored torsos draped with cloth waistbands. Upgraded Mk. II variants boast reinforced armor plating, crackling arcane energy, and elaborate axeblades.

## Other Varieties

### Torches
Torches resemble blade-less Sentinels and are illuminated and powered by the [Sacred Flame](sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md).

### Shadow-Powered Golems
In modern Solistia, constructs have also been artificially engineered through dark arcana. Professor [Harvey](../characters/harvey.md) created artificial golems powered by the [Shadow](shadow.md). His smaller constructs, Small Golems, are patterned after quadruped Remnants, while larger models such as the [Grieving Golem](../characters/grieving_golem.md) are patterned after Revenants, wielding potent dark magic.
