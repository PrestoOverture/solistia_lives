# Aelfric

**Aelfric**, also revered as the **Flamebringer** and the **Flamebearer**, is the supreme deity of the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../factions/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) and one of the eight patron [gods](gods.md) of Solistia. He is worshiped as the lord of all that is holy and the patron of the Cleric calling.

## Myth & Lore

### The Sealing of Vide
According to Solistian legend, Aelfric and seven fellow deities joined together to confront the apocalyptic wicked god [Vide](vide.md). As the destructive struggle unfolded, Aelfric's seven divine siblings sacrificed themselves one by one, imparting their remaining essence to him. Standing as the last surviving god of the eight, Aelfric drew upon their combined power to defeat Vide and seal the dark entity away in [Vidania](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/vidania.md) beneath four holy [Sacred Flames](../lore/sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md). 

In religious dialogue and folklore, it is noted that after Aelfric summoned forth the Sacred Flame, the celestial fire died down and the Flamebringer was left ragged and weary from the exertion.

### The Lumina Clan and the Sacred Mirror
Scripture maintained by the [Order of the Sacred Flame](../factions/order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) records that Aelfric once placed an immortal being in the world to consort with humankind: a woman named [Alpates](../../characters/others/alpates.md). She became the founding mother of the [Lumina Clan](../factions/lumina_clan.md), endowing her descendants with the divine bloodline of the gods. 

When Alpates died, her grieving descendants were plunged into despair. In compassion, Aelfric fashioned a sacred mirror from her remains; its warm, radiant light soothed the clan's sorrow and served in later ages as a holy relic capable of rekindling the four Sacred Flames.

## Worship and Iconography

Aelfric is venerated across Solistia, with his holy power honored at the [Altar of the Flamebringer](../../locations/crestlands/wilds/altar_of_the_flamebringer.md). At the [Flamechurch: Cathedral Entrance](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch_cathedral_entrance.md), his effigy stands beside a statue of [Aeber](aeber.md), marking them as the two deities honored at the seat of the church.

In traditional religious depictions and clerical narration, Aelfric is often envisioned as an austere, white-haired elder priest carrying a lantern or staff. The sacred relic known as the Flamebringer's Staff is named in his honor.
