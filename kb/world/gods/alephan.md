# Alephan

**Alephan**, known as the **Scholarking**, the **Lord of Learning**, or **the Sage**, is one of the eight patron [gods](gods.md) of Solistia and the divine patron of the Scholar calling. In addition to scholarship and arcane study, Alephan serves as the patron deity of Solistia's court and judicial systems.

## Myth & Lore

According to ancient legends, Alephan was among the eight deities who took up arms against the calamitous god [Vide](vide.md). During the harrowing struggle to protect the realm, the gods sacrificed their divine strength to empower [Aelfric](aelfric.md) and seal Vide away, falling into a profound, ages-long slumber. In the mythical accounts of the battle, Alephan's dying breath urged Aelfric to finish the task against Vide's wickedness.

In Solistia, Alephan is venerated by academics, mages, and justices alike. Devotees offer prayers at the sacred [Altar of the Scholarking](../../locations/winterlands/wilds/altar_of_the_scholarking.md).

During the opening mythical narration recounted by [Temenos Mistral](../../characters/travelers/temenos.md), Alephan is depicted as a divine presence shrouded within indistinct white robes. The rare arcane implement known as the Great Sage's Staff is associated with his divine legacy.
