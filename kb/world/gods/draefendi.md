# Draefendi

**Draefendi**, known as **The Huntress**, is one of the eight patron gods of Solistia. Her divine domains encompass the Moon and Hunting, and she is worshipped by hunters. Her sacred sigil is the bow that she wields into battle, and she is traditionally depicted as a hunter accompanied by a falcon and a wolf.

## History

In Solistian legend, Draefendi was among the eight deities who sacrificed their power to defeat the wicked god [Vide](vide.md), after which the gods fell into a deep slumber.

In modern Solistia, she is enshrined at the [Altar of the Huntress](../../locations/totohaha/wilds/altar_of_the_huntress.md). Weapons such as the Huntress's Longbow are named in her honor.
