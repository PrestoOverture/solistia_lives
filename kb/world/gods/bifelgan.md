# Bifelgan

**Bifelgan**, known as the Trader and the Great Merchant, is one of the eight patron [gods](gods.md) of Solistia and the patron deity of the Merchant calling.

## Myth and History

In ancient times, Bifelgan joined the other seven guardian gods of Solistia in a cataclysmic battle against the wicked god [Vide](vide.md). To vanquish Vide and seal the threat away, the eight deities sacrificed their strength and subsequently fell into a deep, centuries-long slumber. In the present day, his divine essence is venerated at the [Altar of the Trader](../../locations/wildlands/wilds/altar_of_the_trader.md).

The divine skill Bifelgan's Bounty channels his favor to strike foes and reap monetary wealth.

## Legends and Legacy

According to ancient lore inscribed upon statues in the Eternal Quicksand, the Trader is traditionally accompanied by two sacred camel-like beasts: one tasked with protecting his wares, and the other with punishing greedy travelers.

During his travels across Solistia, the merchant [Partitio Yellowil](../../characters/travelers/partitio.md) earned renown for his extraordinary mercantile genius, being hailed by onlookers as Bifelgan the Trader reborn.
