# Brand

**Brand**, known as **the Thunderblade**, is one of the eight patron gods of Solistia and the divine embodiment of martial valor and the Warrior lineage. He is the patron deity of the [Sanctum Knights](../factions/sacred_guard.md).

## Lore

In the primordial mythos of Solistia, Brand stood alongside the other seven benevolent deities to combat the wicked god [Vide](vide.md). The eight gods sacrificed their physical presence to cast down and seal the dark entity, subsequently entering a deep slumber.

In religious sculpture and iconography across Solistia, Brand is depicted as a majestic, winged-helmed warrior deity bearing a great divine blade. His divine blessing is enshrined at the [Altar of the Thunderblade](../../locations/hinoeuma/wilds/altar_of_the_thunderblade.md), where warriors seek communion with his sacred might. His divine power is traditionally invoked through the legendary martial technique known as *Brand's Blade*.
