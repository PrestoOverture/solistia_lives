# Galdera

**Galdera**, known as **The Fallen** or the **Dark God**, is a malevolent deity of infernal magic, ruin, and darkness sealed away beyond the Gate of Finis. In Solistia, his presence is that of an otherworldly entity originating from another dimension.

## Appearance and Nature

Galdera embodies bottomless darkness and greed, seeking to consume and assimilate all living beings into himself until nothing remains but him.

When first encountered beyond the Gate of Finis, Galdera manifests in the form of a colossal burning eye reminiscent of [Vide](vide.md). In confrontation, he initially fights through the Omniscient Eye before revealing his full form: a gargantuan armored behemoth formed from corpses and tormented spirits. His lower half tapers into a skeletal spine conjoined with writhing souls, while his left hand terminates in the eyed Blade of the Fallen. White horns curve from his visage, sharp fangs protrude from his maw, and red light gleams from his eyes.

In Solistia, Galdera manifests in a spectral gray coloration, appearing as a ghostly entity that has not bound itself to a living mortal vessel.

## Encounter in Solistia

Galdera's origins in Solistia remain shrouded in mystery. He can be found deep beyond the Gate of Finis after the gate is opened by the traveler [Al](../../characters/side_stories/alfred_octopath_traveler_ii.md). When confronted in this otherworldly expanse, the Dark God issues a final warning:

> *"Brave souls... Why have you come here? If you seek to prevent my return... Then I shall consume you all!"*
