# Sealticge

**Sealticge**, known as **the Lady of Grace** or **Lady Grace**, is one of the eight patron gods of Solistia. She is the divine patroness of dance and the Dancer lineage.

## Lore

In Solistian myth, Sealticge was among the eight deities who sacrificed their power to defeat the wicked god [Vide](vide.md), after which the gods entered a deep slumber. In modern Solistia, she is enshrined at the [Altar of the Lady of Grace](../../locations/leaflands/wilds/altar_of_the_lady_of_grace.md).

Her divine power is traditionally invoked through the dance technique Sealticge's Seduction.

## Culture and Worship

The [Festival of Grace](../lore/grand_gala.md) in [Merry Hills](../../locations/crestlands/towns/merry_hills.md) is held in honor of Sealticge and hosted at the [Shrine of Ul'sterra](../../locations/crestlands/dungeons/shrine_of_ulsterra.md), which houses an altar dedicated to her.

In contemporary Solistia, multiple renowned dancers consider the original Lady of Grace to be "no more" following the gods' ancient slumber, claiming her mantle for themselves. Both [Hermes](../../characters/temenos/hermes.md) and [Dolcinaea](../../characters/agnea/dolcinaea_luciel.md) purport to be the new Lady of Grace, citing their skill and widespread renown.
