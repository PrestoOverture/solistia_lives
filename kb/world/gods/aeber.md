# Aeber

**Aeber**, known as the **Prince of Thieves**, is one of the eight patron [gods](gods.md) of Solistia and the divine patron of the Thief calling.

## Myth & Lore

In the ancient mythos of Solistia, Aeber stood among the eight benevolent deities who battled the dark deity [Vide](vide.md). In order to overcome and seal away the wicked god, Aeber and his divine peers sacrificed their strength, subsequently lapsing into a profound and enduring slumber. 

Devotees seek his blessing across the continent, venerating him at the sacred [Altar of the Prince of Thieves](../../locations/brightlands/wilds/altar_of_the_prince_of_thieves.md). Uniquely, a statue of Aeber stands prominently alongside that of [Aelfric](aelfric.md) at the [Flamechurch: Cathedral Entrance](../../locations/crestlands/towns/flamechurch_cathedral_entrance.md), making him one of only two gods honored with an effigy at the entrance of the cathedral.

During the events of the [Journey for the Dawn](../../stories/main/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md), the cleric [Temenos](../../characters/temenos/temenos.md) remarks upon [Throné Anguis](../../characters/throne/throne.md)'s lethal grace and agility by referring to her as "Aeber come again."
