# Elementals

**Elementals** are manifestations of condensed ambient magic that roam the diverse environments of Solistia. Formed when floating magical energy coalesces into living form, these ethereal beings are intrinsically linked to the magical currents of the realm. Smaller, immature forms of these beings are commonly referred to as **wisps**.

When vanquished, elementals often leave behind soulstones corresponding to their affinity, suggesting that soulstones are formed from their crystallized magical essence.

## Types of Elementals

Natural elementals embody the six foundational elements of nature, often congregating in regions that reflect their affinity:

### Fire
Composed of flickering tongues of flame surrounding a core of crimson crystals, Fire Elementals and wisps burn with vibrant shades of red, orange, and yellow. They typically inhabit subterranean depths, geothermal caverns, and scorched arid deserts.
- Fire Wisp
- Red Wisp
- Fire Elemental
- Red Elemental

### Ice
Ice Elementals assume an ovular shape, characterized by swirling rings of frost and glacial winds encircling dark blue crystal cores. They dwell primarily in freezing climates and snowbound wastes. Exceptional varieties include Blue Elementals, known to be conjured by guardian entities such as [Glacis](glacis.md).
- Ice Wisp
- Ice Elemental
- Snow Elemental
- Blue Elemental

### Thunder
Lightning Elementals appear as crackling, pale blue nimbuses of raw electrical charge orbiting a dark blue nucleus. Highly versatile, they can manifest across diverse terrains, though they show a marked preference for subterranean caverns and cold regions.
- Thunder Wisp
- Lightning Elemental

### Wind
Shaped like swirling miniature tornadoes, Wind Elementals consist of pale green gales twisting around a dense green eye. They travel freely across open landscapes, highlands, and woodlands with no strict territorial preference.
- Wind Wisp
- Wind Elemental

### Light
Light Elementals manifest as shimmering, sparkling clouds of rose-gold radiance encasing luminous spherical orbs. They drift freely through forests, ancient ruins, open fields, and indoor sanctuaries.
- Light Wisp
- Light Elemental

### Dark
Dark Elementals take on crescent or horned silhouettes forged of flickering violet flames and scintillating blue energy. Natural varieties—such as Shadow Wisps and Dark Elementals—frequent deep chasms and places touched by supernatural shadows. More malevolent variants, often designated as Black Wisps or Black Elementals, are typically manifested when summoned by dark sorcerers or corrupt ritualists.
- Shadow Wisp
- Dark Elemental
- Dark Wisp
- Black Wisp
- Black Elemental

## Arcane & Higher Entities

Beyond the six primal elements, certain rare elementals draw upon divine or esoteric forces rather than natural mana:

- **Sacred Flame Elementals**: Resembling azure flame wisps, these beings channel the divine radiance of the [Sacred Flame](sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md) and are closely attuned to the blessing of the god [Aelfric](aelfric.md).
- **Pure Elementals**: Distinctive ash-gray beings that channel all fundamental elements simultaneously. Rather than occurring naturally in the wild, these creatures are believed to be arcane artificial constructs created by scholars seeking to harness the [One True Magic](one_true_magic.md).
