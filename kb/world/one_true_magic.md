# One True Magic

The **One True Magic** is a legendary, ultimate form of magic in Solistia said to transcend the six known elemental sources. Centuries of scholars and magi sought to identify and unlock this elusive power, which was often referred to as the "seventh source."

> *"In this world, magic is derived from six elements. Fire, ice, lightning, wind, light, and darkness. However, there is one phenomenon that cannot be explained by the magic power of these elements. The pit of [D'arqest](../characters/darqest.md). Four hundred years ago, the sage Gratton observed the pit, and his conclusion was thus: 'The answer is clear: only power beyond the six sources could open a pit of this size. There exists a seventh. I am sure of it.' Thus was born Gratton's Hypothesis, the cornerstone of our magical studies. It was he who suggested the existence of the One True Magic."*
> — [Harvey](../characters/harvey.md)

## History and Gratton's Hypothesis

Approximately eight hundred years ago, the ancient Archmage [D'arqest](../characters/darqest.md) unleashed a cataclysmic magical event that opened an enormous chasm known as the Pit of D'arqest. Because none of the six fundamental elements—fire, ice, lightning, wind, light, and darkness—possessed sufficient destructive potential to create such a rift, scholars were left baffled.

Four centuries later, the sage Gratton examined the pit and formulated "Gratton's Hypothesis," proposing that an unknown seventh source of power must exist beyond the known elements. This hypothesis became the foundation of modern magical theory, inspiring generations of scholars to search for the One True Magic.

## Paths of Research

Two prominent scholars at the Royal Academy dedicated their lives to uncovering the seventh source:

- **[Osvald V. Vanstein](../characters/osvald.md)**: Osvald posited that because the six known elements are physical in nature, their energy output is inherently finite. He reasoned that the seventh source must be entirely immaterial to harness boundless magical power. Osvald pursued a theoretical and mathematical methodology, formulating intricate equations to compress and concentrate elemental energies in search of the underlying constant.
- **[Harvey](../characters/harvey.md)**: Harvey pursued a biological approach, theorizing that monster biology held the secret, as monsters channel innate magical powers without verbal incantations. He synthesized purified crystals of monster blood and conducted horrific crossbreeding experiments to create chimeras. By stealing Osvald's research formulae and merging them with his own monster blood studies, Harvey earned public acclaim as "Gratton's second coming."

## Awakening and the Two Magics

Harvey ultimately deduced that the key to unlocking the power lay in human bloodlines, specifically the blood of the ancient [Lumina Clan](lumina_clan.md). By capturing Osvald's daughter, [Elena](../characters/elena_vanstein.md), and combining her blood with the physical remains of Archmage [D'arqest](../characters/darqest.md), Harvey succeeded in channeling the [Shadow](shadow.md). Harvey proclaimed this dark force to be the true seventh source, though it manifested as a corrosive, obsidian power that severely drained and damaged the caster upon invocation.

During their final confrontation, [Osvald](../characters/osvald.md) manifested the genuine One True Magic. Rather than relying on hatred, greed, or sacrifice, Osvald's magic was unlocked by his unconditional love for his daughter and his resolve to protect her. Unlike Harvey's self-destructive dark art, Osvald's One True Magic manifested as a radiant, benevolent force that harmed only his foes, proving that genuine One True Magic is rooted in altruism and an immaterial bond of love.

## Lore and Theories

- **[Lumina Clan](lumina_clan.md) Heritage**: Notes within [Ori's Journal Fragments](../stories/oris_journal_fragments.md) speculate that [Elena](../characters/elena_vanstein.md) herself inherently possesses an affinity for the One True Magic, inherited through her mother [Rita](../characters/rita_lumina.md)'s [Lumina Clan](lumina_clan.md) lineage.
- **Greed vs. Selflessness**: Differing interpretations exist regarding D'arqest's ancient feats. [Clarissa](../characters/clarissa.md) theorized that the seventh source is human greed, which D'arqest concentrated by stripping humans of their avarice and turning them into [Beastlings](beastling.md). Conversely, the Beastling elder [Cohazeh](../characters/cohazeh.md) theorized that the seventh source represents the pure opposite of greed, brought forth when avarice was eradicated. These opposing perspectives align with the dual nature of the magic: Harvey's covetous pursuit leading to the harmful Shadow, and Osvald's selfless sacrifice unveiling the true power.
