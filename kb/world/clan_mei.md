# Clan Mei

"*Clan Mei shall be the spear which leads the charge into the future. And it shall be wielded by the desert's future rulers.*"
—*[Jin Mei](../characters/jin_mei.md)*

**Clan Mei** is a martial clan of elite spearmen sworn to the kingdom of [Ku](ku.md). Originally hailing from [Hinoeuma](../locations/hinoeuma.md), the clan established a fortified northern seat at [Castle Mei](../locations/stormhail_castle_mei.md) in [Stormhail](../locations/stormhail.md). Following the death of its former leader [Jin Mei](../characters/jin_mei.md), command fell to his younger sister [Rai Mei](../characters/rai_mei.md).

## History

Seeking stability and protection for his people, Jin Mei pledged Clan Mei to the royal house of Ku. However, political machinations soon entangled the clan; [General Mugen](../characters/mugen.md) secretly ordered Jin to assassinate Prince [Hikari](../characters/hikari.md). The plot ended in tragedy: Jin perished alongside Hikari's mother, Lady [Kura](../characters/kura.md), and Rai Mei was forced to assume leadership of the grieving clan.

Following these events, King [Jigo](../characters/jigo_ku.md) dispatched Clan Mei across the sea to establish a military foothold on the eastern continent. Taking up residence at Castle Mei in the mountainous city of Stormhail, the clan succeeded in mediating peace among the region's warring factions, earning widespread acclaim from the citizenry and the [Order of the Sacred Flame](order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md). This sudden ascendancy sparked deep resentment and political friction with the [Sacred Guard](sacred_guard.md), who had long held undisputed authority over the city.

When Mugen usurped the throne of Ku, Rai Mei maintained outward allegiance to his brutal regime, believing obedience was the only way to shield Clan Mei from slaughter. During Hikari's journey to reclaim Ku, she cornered and captured him in Stormhail to offer his head to Mugen. However, after Hikari broke free, defeated her in combat, and spared her life, Rai Mei recognized his true nobility and committed Clan Mei to his cause. The clan's warriors marched on Ku alongside Hikari's coalition, helping to overthrow Mugen and secure an era of peace.

## Culture and Traditions

- **Martial Prowess:** Clan Mei's soldiers are widely celebrated for their disciplined spear techniques. Rai Mei herself wields the Spear of Levin, a renowned ancestral spear that crackles with lightning. The clan's armory is represented by signature weapons including the Clan Mei Sword and the Clan Mei Spear.
- **Austerity:** Due to a precarious financial standing, Clan Mei enforces rigorous discipline and frugality, strictly avoiding luxuries or excess.
- **Meritocratic Recruitment:** Despite being a distinct clan, they readily recruit outsiders based on martial competence. Their ranks include fighters hailing from [Winterbloom](../locations/winterbloom.md) and former prison guards from [Frigit Isle](../locations/frigit_isle.md).
