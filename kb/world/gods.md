# Gods

The **Gods** are supernatural entities worshiped throughout Solistia. The religious traditions of the continent—most prominently the [Order of the Sacred Flame](order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md)—primarily venerate eight main benevolent deities. Unlike depictions in other lands where deities wear masks, the eight gods are portrayed unmasked in Solistian tradition.

In addition to the eight holy gods, ancient Solistian cosmology recognizes three deities corresponding to the times of day:
- **Solis**: God of Light, associated with the "Dawn" and "Hope".
- **[Galdera](galdera.md)**: The Dark God, associated with the "Twilight" and "Stillness".
- **[Vide](vide.md)**: The Wicked God, associated with the "Night" and "Nothingness".

## The Eight Gods of Solistia

According to legend, each of the eight deities bestowed a distinct gift upon humanity:
- **[Aelfric, the Flamebringer](aelfric.md)**: Brought order.
- **[Alephan, the Scholarking](alephan.md)**: Brought wisdom.
- **[Bifelgan, the Trader](bifelgan.md)**: Brought commerce.
- **[Brand, the Thunderblade](brand.md)**: Brought war.
- **[Dohter, the Charitable](dohter.md)**: Brought balance.
- **[Draefendi, the Huntress](draefendi.md)**: Brought the hunt.
- **[Aeber, the Prince of Thieves](aeber.md)**: Brought travel.
- **[Sealticge, the Lady of Grace](sealticge.md)**: Brought dance.

To help memorize the names of the eight deities, the cleric [Temenos](../characters/temenos.md) teaches [Agnea](../characters/agnea.md) a mnemonic:
> *"Warriors **brand**ish their blades. Scholars **ale**ways forget things. Fortune **bifel**s traders. Apothecaries **doht** upon all. Thieves steal **a burr**. Hunters **drae** their quarry away. Dancers **seal** with a kiss. Clerics heal what **aels** you."*

### Faded Deities
While ancient myths of other realms recall four additional gods—Steorra, Dreisang, Balogar, and Winnehild—these deities are not officially recognized among the gods of Solistia and have largely faded from religious belief. Their memories survive solely in traditional arts and performances, such as "Sterra's Fantasia", "Nocturne of Sang", "Sonata of Balogar", and "Hild's Hymn" (alongside "Sagelord's Duet", a tribute to Alephan).

## History and Mythology

### The Battle Against Vide
According to legend, the eight gods sacrificed themselves in an ancient war against the wicked god [Vide](vide.md). In the course of the clash, seven of the gods fell one after another: Bifelgan fell first, followed in order by Aeber, Draefendi, Brand, Sealticge, Alephan, and Dohter.

Aelfric stood as the final god facing Vide. Utilizing the strength entrusted to him by his fallen siblings, Aelfric sealed Vide away in [Vidania](../locations/vidania.md). Following the battle, the eight gods entered a deep slumber. Their divine power persisted in Solistia in the form of the four Sacred Flames, which burn across the continent to keep the [Shadow](shadow.md) at bay.

Because of this legend, prayer to the gods is governed by strict tradition. Reciting the gods' names in the chronological order of their fall (Bifelgan, Aeber, Draefendi, Brand, Sealticge, Alephan, Dohter, Aelfric) is said to bring good fortune. Conversely, praying their names in reverse order invokes a curse.

### Lore and Decrees
- **Prohibition of Grapes and Plums**: Legend states that the gods forbade mortals from cultivating grapes and plums to prevent intoxication and rampant warfare.
- **The Six Divine Weapons**: Another Solistian myth holds that there are only six recognized primary weapon types because the gods were jealous of weapons other than their own. In antiquity, the gods granted Solistia six special weapons to defend against evil: the Conqueror's Sword, the Warlord's Spear, the Dancer's Blade, the Lionheart's Axe, the Hunter's Bow, and the Spiritlord's Staff. Greedy human wielders corrupted the weapons, and all six were eventually lost to history.
- **The Great Wall**: The gods are said to have constructed the massive [Great Wall](great_wall.md) near [Stormhail](../locations/stormhail.md) in a single day, achieving an engineering feat impossible with mortal methods.
- **Blood of the Dark Gods**: Mortals can attain a form of immortality, manifested as eternal youth, by drinking the blood of the dark gods Vide or Galdera.

### The Moonshade Order and the Journey for the Dawn
During the era of the eight travelers, the [Moonshade Order](moonshade_order.md) worked across Solistia to break Vide's ancient seal and plunge the world into an eternal night. Their efforts culminated in the extinguishing of all four Sacred Flames during the [Journey for the Dawn](../stories/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md), which caused Vidania to rise from the sea. On the resurfaced island, [Oboro](../characters/oboro.md) hurled himself into Vide's dark fire to complete the resurrection of the wicked deity. The eight travelers confronted and defeated Vide in a climactic conflict, banishing the darkness and restoring the dawn.
