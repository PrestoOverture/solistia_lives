# Reptilians

**Reptilians** are a broad family of scaled creatures and monsters that dwell across Solistia. In addition to common reptiles such as lizards, snakes, and tortoises, the reptilian lineage encompasses greater creatures including dragons, [drakes](drakes.md), [lizardmen](lizardmen.md), and [sea serpents](aquatics.md).

## Crocodilians

### Gators
Gators are large, moss-covered alligators inhabiting swamps and wetlands. Their backs bristle with sharp ridges and spikes, and their powerful jaws allow them to deliver crushing bites or swallow prey whole.

### Gigantes and Iguanas
Gigantes are colossal, crocodilian reptiles armed with curved horns, spiked tails, and heavily armored hides in shades of brown, green, and gray. They rely on brutal physical power and elemental attacks. On the island of [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md), a distinct iguana branch flourishes—including the [King Iguana](king_iguana.md)—which possesses spiny head crests in place of horns.

## Lizards

### Chameleons
Chameleons are large, colorful arboreal lizards equipped with independent rolling eyes, horns, and prehensile tongues. Skilled at blending into their surroundings, they employ natural camouflage and defend themselves with venomous or disorienting toxins, inflicting poison and confusion. A chameleon and snake coils are emblazoned upon the regional emblem of [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md).

### Skinks
Skinks are sturdy lizards covered in protective spikes. When threatened or attacking, they curl their bodies into armored spheres to barrel into foes.

## Serpents

### Ettins
Ettins are formidable two-headed serpents marked by striped scales and prominent head crests, found in varying colorations including violet, green, blue, and white. In Solistia, ettins are strongly associated with the criminal guild known as the [Blacksnakes](blacksnakes.md), frequenting areas tied to their operations such as [Mother's Garden](../locations/mothers_garden.md), the [Abandoned Church](../locations/abandoned_church.md), and [Lostseed Castle](../locations/lostseed_castle.md).

### Vipers and Giant Serpents
Solistian serpents range from small, horned vipers to colossal snakes. Many varieties secrete virulent or soporific venoms, while some species channel fire or ice magic. Certain skilled snake charmers, such as Ichchadhari the Snake Charmer, are able to tame and direct these serpents.

## Tortoises
Tortoises are heavy terrestrial cheloneans equipped with thick, jagged shells and petrified-looking gray hide. Commonly inhabiting coastal grottoes and beaches, they can discharge electrical currents to defend themselves.

## Cultural Significance
Reptilian motifs are widespread in Solistian martial and cultural life. Toto'haha features reptilian imagery on its crest, while several weapons—such as Snakebite and the Serpent Slayer—draw names from them. In addition, some warriors practice the spear technique known as "Twin Serpents."
