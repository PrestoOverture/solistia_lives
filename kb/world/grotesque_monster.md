# Grotesque Monster

The **Grotesque Monster** is a monstrous dark entity and boss in *Octopath Traveler II*. It appears during [The Journey for the Dawn](../stories/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md) at the [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../locations/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md) on [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md).

## Story

During the deep night caused by the [Moonshade Order](moonshade_order.md), the eight travelers journey across Solistia to rekindle the four extinguished Sacred Flames. Upon arriving at the Tombs of the Wardenbeasts, their approach to the altar is blocked by a horrifying abomination guarding the darkened flame. Following a fierce battle, the beast is slain, allowing [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md) and [Castti](../characters/castti.md) to relight [The Sacred Flame](sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md).

## Nature & Origin

The Grotesque Monster is heavily implied to be the twisted, corrupted remnants of the Dark Hunter [Petrichor](../characters/petrichor.md), who had sacrificed her life at the Tombs of the Wardenbeasts to extinguish the island's Sacred Flame. 

Upon defeat, the monstrosity drops the Black Bow, a formidable weapon suited for a hunter. In combat, the beast is designated as a "darkling"—a classification shared with other human victims consumed by shadow, such as the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md) and the [Dark Entity](../characters/dark_entity.md).

## Behavior & Abilities

The Grotesque Monster wields oppressive shadow and foul miasmas. In battle, it summons brood swarms known as Twisted Spawn that assail prey with shrill shrieks. The monster can devour these spawn to augment its own dark magic, venting noxious poisonous clouds, oppressive waves of darkness, and blinding darkstorms.
