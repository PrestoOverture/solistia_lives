# Sa

The **Kingdom of Sa** was a small nation located in [Hinoeuma](../locations/hinoeuma.md). Despite its modest territory, Sa possessed numerous fertile oases and abundant natural resources, which made it a frequent target for foreign conquest.

## History

During the reign of King [Jigo Ku](../characters/jigo_ku.md), the neighboring warlike nation of [Ku](ku.md) established a formal peace treaty with Sa. Under the terms of this accord, Ku pledged military protection to Sa in exchange for a designated portion of its resources.

However, when [Mugen](../characters/mugen.md) seized power in Ku, he tore up the peace treaty and launched an aggressive invasion of Sa. Although his advisor [Ageha](../characters/ageha.md) recommended conscripting the conquered populace into Ku's expanding war machine, Mugen rejected the counsel and ordered the wholesale massacre of Sa's people to crush any chance of rebellion. 

Ku's invading forces captured the [King of Sa](../characters/king_of_sa.md), whom Mugen executed personally in his own throne room, bringing an end to the kingdom.
