# Direwolves

**Direwolves** are a group of [canines](canines.md) known for their size, intelligence, and strength. They travel in packs in the wintry or forested regions of Solistia, preying on livestock and attacking travelers who enter their territory.

Direwolves tend to be gray, black, or white-furred. Their larger pack leaders sport thick manes, bushy tails, and long canine fangs.

## Types of Direwolves

### Dreadwolves
**Dreadwolves** are enormous wolves with massive ribboned manes, long tails, stripes, and curving orange eyes. Most live in cold areas and come in shades of blue or gray, and some can harness ice magic. In Solistia, a powerful Dreadwolf is found within the [Infernal Castle](../../locations/winterlands/dungeons/infernal_castle.md).

### Vargs
**Vargs** are large, lean lupines whose features resemble skinnier and mangier wolves. Referred to as "arcane beasts," vargs are found only in Solistia. Their bodies are either blue-gray or yellow-brown, their maws bristle with narrow teeth, and their front paws are tipped with long red claws. When slashing at foes, they adopt a bipedal stance. Solistian breeds include:
- [Felvarg](../../characters/temenos/felvarg.md)
- Auðnvarg

## Cultural Significance

- A wolf features prominently in the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md)' regional emblem.
- Solistian gear named after the creature includes the White Wolf Scarf.
