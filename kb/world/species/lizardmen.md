# Lizardmen

**Lizardmen** are [reptilian](reptilians.md) [demihumans](demihumans.md) that resemble humanoid lizards. Often found in environmental extremes—ranging from freezing tundra and arid deserts to coastal grottos—they organize into territorial tribal bands. Depending on their regional environment, they fight using maces, clubs, hammers, axes, spears, swords, or daggers. Their tribal leaders are known as lizardkings.

## Regional Breeds

### Ocean Lizardmen
Native to the coastal waters and caves of the [Harborlands](../../locations/harborlands/harborlands.md), ocean lizardmen are far thinner and more sinuous than other varieties. Possessing sleek blue skin that resembles serpents more than lizards, they wield curved daggers in battle. Their lizardkings distinguish themselves by wearing elaborate dressings of carved bone and brightly colored tassels.

### Ancient Lizardmen
Adapted to frigid northern climates, ancient lizardmen (also known as ice lizardmen) inhabit the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md), dwelling in locations such as the [Ruffians' Hideout](../../locations/winterlands/dungeons/ruffians_hideout.md) and the sunken ruins of the [Infernal Castle](../../locations/winterlands/dungeons/infernal_castle.md). They possess gray-blue scales, crimson eyes, and fur garments. Common warriors sport head crests and carry spears, whereas their stouter lizardkings wield massive hooked greatswords.

### Vagrant and Twisted Lizardmen
Adapted to subterranean caverns and arid badlands, these reptilians represent Solistian branches of sand-dwelling lizardmen. In particular, twisted lizardmen dwell within caves and underground ruins across the [Brightlands](../../locations/brightlands/brightlands.md). Bearing gray-green scales, they wield heavy bludgeons, maces, and battle axes into combat.

## Culture and Armament

Lizardmen are known as wary, mistrustful beings that fiercely defend their territorial bounds against human intrusion. Their most renowned martial chieftain relics include the Lizardking's Axe.
