# Demihumans

> *"Demihumans have displayed a propensity for much higher levels of intelligence than monsters, and are known to employ tools to some extent in their daily lives. And yet, to this day their origins remain shrouded in mystery. According to the histories, they simply appeared one day - as if by magic - throughout the world."*
> — The [Mercantile Manuscript](../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md)

**Demihumans** are bipedal sentient beings in *Octopath Traveler II* that bridge the divide between beasts and humankind. Distinct from wild beasts and monsters, demihumans walk upright, wear clothing, wield weapons, and utilize crafted tools. In Solistia, eight major kinds of demihumans are known to dwell across the wilderness: [ratkin](ratkin.md), [lizardmen](lizardmen.md), [birdians](birdians.md), [froggens](froggens.md), [apes](apes.md), [moles](moles.md), [caits](caits.md), and [octopuffs](octopuff_travelers.md).

## Traits and Society

Demihumans possess significantly greater intelligence than standard fauna. Most species live and hunt in organized tribal bands, often hostile toward human travelers encroaching on their territory. In contrast, wild caits and octopuffs are solitary and timid, quickly fleeing from encounters with outsiders. While most demihumans communicate via guttural animal cries, rare cait sages have demonstrated the ability to speak human languages fluently.

Demihuman groups scavenge manufactured items from human settlements and craft their own rudimentary weapons and implements. Their leaders, recognized as "kings," wear crowns and ornate jewelry. Curiously, the intricate design and foreign metals of these regal adornments far surpass the primitive technology of the tribes, and the craftsmanship bears no resemblance to human manufacture. This anomaly has led Solistian scholars to speculate that demihumans or their regalia may have originated from another world entirely.

Furthermore, evidence of demihuman souls enduring in the afterlife exists in Hell, where demihuman spirits manifest among the hellfiends.

## History and Mythology

According to the historical lore recorded in the [Mercantile Manuscript](../../stories/documents/masoud_script.md), the ultimate origin of demihumans is shrouded in mystery; ancient annals claim they simply appeared across the continents one day as if conjured by magic.

Ancient myths recount that a cataclysmic, protracted war was once fought between mankind and the demihuman races. Although humanity ultimately prevailed, the [caits](caits.md) alone chose to ally with human beings during the conflict. In the war's aftermath, the cait earned a brief place within human towns and societies. However, their clever and cunning nature eventually provoked suspicion and resentment among their human neighbors. Ostracized by humans and branded as traitors by fellow demihumans, the caits withdrew entirely into seclusion, living hidden, solitary lives in the wilderness.

## Solistian Demihuman Species

The eight demihuman races inhabiting the wilderness of Solistia comprise:

- **[Ratkin](ratkin.md)**: Rodent-like tribes dwelling in plains and caverns, including sickly, erratic strains known as "mad" ratkin.
- **[Lizardmen](lizardmen.md)**: Reptilian warriors found in desert and coastal biomes.
- **[Birdians](birdians.md)**: Avian bipeds inhabiting crags and cliffside aeries.
- **[Froggens](froggens.md)**: Amphibious tribes dwelling near wetlands and riverbanks.
- **[Apes](apes.md)**: Powerful simian tribes native to the dense forests of Solistia.
- **[Moles](moles.md)**: Subterranean demihumans indigenous to Solistian cave systems and mining regions.
- **[Caits](caits.md)**: Rare, elusive feline nomads prized for their luck and magical affinity.
- **[Octopuffs](octopuff_travelers.md)**: Mysterious, timid cephalopod demihumans native to Solistian waters.
