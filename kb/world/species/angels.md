# Angels

**Angels** are mythical winged beings in the lore of Solistia. Associated with divine power, they are closely linked to the [gods](../gods/gods.md) and the Heavens.

## Manifestations

While angelic forms are rare among mortals, singing superstar [Dolcinaea](../../characters/agnea/dolcinaea_luciel.md) manifests a pair of blue angel wings onstage to complement her voice. Prior to revealing her wings, she proclaims herself the successor to [Sealticge](../gods/sealticge.md), Lady of Grace, with the angelic appearance symbolizing her claimed divine status.

## Statues and Art

Winged angel statues are scattered throughout Solistia, frequently placed near churches, grand mansions, and major cities. Three primary depictions exist across the realm:
- Statues holding pots, some of which have lost their heads to weathering or vandalism over time.
- Statues in an attitude of prayer.
- Smaller, childlike statues shown crouching with wings folded.
