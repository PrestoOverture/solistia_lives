# Octopuff Travelers

**Octopuff Travelers** are [octopean](aquatics.md) [demihumans](demihumans.md).

## Lore

Octopuffs are the most independent of Solistia's demihumans. According to legend, they did not fight in the great war between demihumans and humans, instead preferring to continue their lives of constant travel. Octopuffs are viewed as symbols of good fortune, and some say that only the pure of heart can encounter them. Their preference for small, dark spaces attracts them to Octopuff Pots.

## Varieties

Notable varieties of Octopuffs found across Solistia include:
