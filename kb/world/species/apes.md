# Apes

**Apes** are [mammalian](mammalians.md) [demihumans](demihumans.md) inhabiting Solistia. Found primarily in [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md) and the [Crestlands](../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md), their tribes are led by dominant figures known as apekings.

## Breeds

- **Desert Apes:** Inhabiting the arid dunes of Hinoeuma, Desert Apes have tan fur and red skin. They possess a lithe build compared to their mountain kin and wield bows in combat.
- **Mountain Apes:** Native to the rugged Crestlands, Mountain Apes have dark fur and a heavy, stocky frame closely resembling gorillas. In battle, they wield chain-axes.

## Related Species

The bipedal monkeys [Veron and Doron](../../characters/castti/veron_and_doron.md) share potential affinities with apes, though they differ markedly by possessing white fur, tails, and longer tongues, while lacking tools, weapons, and clothing.
