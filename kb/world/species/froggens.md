# Froggens

**Froggens** are amphibious [demihumans](demihumans.md) that resemble upright frogs. Inhabiting watery regions with lush vegetation, rivers, or coastal caves, they are organized tribal creatures whose leaders are designated as frogkings. Depending on their habitat, they equip themselves with spears, daggers, axes, poleaxes, or bows.

## Solistian Varieties

### Ancient Froggens
Ancient Froggens possess light green skin and yellow eyes, while their frogkings feature sharp teeth and striking red eyes. Both common warriors and kings wear armored skirts and adorn themselves with headgear ranging from tribal masks to elaborate headdresses. Common froggens fight with spears, whereas frogkings wield immense poleaxes.

### Vagrant Froggens
Vagrant Froggens dwell within caves and subterranean grottos throughout the [Harborlands](../../locations/harborlands/harborlands.md). They have teal-green skin with pale underbellies and red eyes with horizontal pupils. Common froggens wield daggers and wear distinctive headgear, while their gray-blue frogkings don elaborate winged or plumed armor and swing heavy axes.

### Island Froggens
Native to the wild expanses of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md), Island Froggens resemble vibrant poison dart frogs, displaying vivid coloration in hues of pink, yellow-green, and blue. They fight as archers using bows, often firing poison-tipped arrows by pulling back the bowstrings with their elastic tongues.

## Material Lore

- Organic materials harvested from these amphibians are used in crafting protective gear, such as the Frog Membrane worn by the hunter [Ochette](../../characters/travelers/ochette.md) at the start of her journey.
