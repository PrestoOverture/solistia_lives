# Ratkin

**Ratkin** are [mammalian](mammalians.md) [demihumans](demihumans.md) that resemble rats. Regarded as verminous pests, they are known to scavenge gear and equipment from humans. Ratkin live in tribal hierarchies led by larger and more formidable leaders known as ratkings. Though wild and hostile, they can be tamed using a specific chemical compound, as demonstrated by the Ratkin Charmer.

Depending on their habitat across Solistia, ratkin adapt distinct weaponry, physical builds, and attire.

## Breeds in Solistia

### Vagrant Ratkin
Found in the dungeons of the [Crestlands](../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md), Vagrant Ratkin possess gray fur and dress in ragged clothing or salvaged armor. Lesser ratkin of this breed wield daggers, whereas the ratkings bear long fangs and carry large greataxes.

### Ancient Ratkin
Inhabiting the dungeons of the [Leaflands](../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md), Ancient Ratkin sport brown or gray fur. They adorn themselves in leafy attire, wooden armor, and feather trinkets. The lesser ratkin fight with bows from a distance, while their ratkings wield massive spears.

### City Ratkin
Native to the [Brightlands](../../locations/brightlands/brightlands.md), City Ratkin have stout bodies covered in brown fur. They arm themselves with spears and carry improvised shields assembled from scraps of wood, stone, and metal.

### Mad Ratkin
Mad Ratkin are the rarest and most savage variety of terrestrial ratkin, identifiable by their burning red eyes. In Solistia, they are contaminated mutations of city ratkin that are covered in clinging moss, wield decaying weapons, and constantly drip with virulent poison.
