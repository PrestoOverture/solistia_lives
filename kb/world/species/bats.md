# Bats

**Bats** are winged [mammalian](mammalians.md) beasts that inhabit caves, caverns, abandoned ruins, and subterranean hollows across Solistia. Known for roosting and hunting in predatory swarms, they attack targets by draining blood and inducing magical slumber.

## Varieties

### Colored Bats
Relatively small bats recognizable by thick fur ruffs around their necks. They appear in two primary environmental morphs:
- **White Bats**: Pale-furred varieties adapted to freezing northern climates and glacial caverns.
- **Black Bats**: Dark-furred cavern dwellers adapted to shadows and subterranean labyrinths. Older and more formidable specimens develop pointed tails and can weave offensive dark magic.

### Clawed Bats
Larger and more predatory than colored bats, clawed bats sport elongated rat-like tails and sharp talons. They hunt by diving headfirst at prey from cavern ceilings, latching on with their claws to gorge on blood while releasing soporific venom.

## Related Creatures
The dread fiend known as the Devourer of Dreams—while primarily possessing a caprine form—exhibits pronounced bat-like anatomy, notably large leathery wings, nocturnal hunting patterns, and the power to invoke deep slumber and dark sorcery.
