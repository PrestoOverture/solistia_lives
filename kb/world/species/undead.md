# Undead

The **Undead** are reanimated beings formed from the bones, armor, or remains of the deceased. Wandering desolate subterranean hollows, ancient crypts, and shadowed wastes across Solistia, they are driven by lingering malice or dark forces.

## Skeletons

Most skeletons arise from the bones of fallen warriors, bandits, or buccaneers, suggesting that individuals who lived violent lives are especially prone to rising again in death. They attack intruders relentlessly and can inflict supernatural terror on those they encounter.

In Solistia, Marionette Skeletons and Undead Soldiers are unique to the arid expanses of [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md), where they wander the open desert sands under cover of night rather than remaining confined to deep tombs. Skeletons such as Mysterious Bones have also been observed fighting alongside adversaries like [Garnet](../../characters/partitio/garnet.md).

## Animated Armor

Animated armors are suits of martial panoply inhabited by the spirits and decayed remnants of the dead, wielding bladed weapons to haunt the living. These hollow sentinels exude a sinister presence capable of terrifying mortals. In Solistia, certain suits have fallen under the influence of the [Shadow](../lore/shadow.md), manifesting as Shadowy Animated Armor.

## Souls

Souls, or the fallen, are anguished spectral beings lingering beyond the mortal sphere. Under infernal influence, these tormented entities appear as raw, fleshly visages that channel dark sorcery. Certain souls—such as the Raging Soul, Screaming Soul, and Wailing Soul—are summoned directly by the Omniscient Eye, tethering to the monstrous entity to project its malevolent power.
