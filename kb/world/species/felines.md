# Felines

**Felines** are a diverse family of mammalian catlike creatures found throughout Solistia. While many roam as wild beasts and predators, some can be tamed, and rare individuals possess the power of speech.

Several intelligent species share connections with felines. The elusive [caits](caits.md) are catlike demihumans renowned for carrying riches, while the sentient [beastlings](beastling.md) exhibit distinct feline features such as pointed ears and claws. In civilized settlements across Solistia—such as [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md), [Canalbrine](../../locations/harborlands/towns/canalbrine.md), and especially [Tropu'hopu](../../locations/totohaha/towns/tropuhopu.md)—ordinary domestic cats are a frequent sight.

## Species and Breeds

### Wardenbeasts
The only known living wardenbeast is [Juvah](../../characters/ochette/juvah.md), who resembles a massive white lion with blue markings across his back. Native to the island of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md), wardenbeasts possess human speech, exceptional longevity—Juvah has lived for over two centuries—and a deep spiritual connection to the beastlings. Juvah refers to the beastlings as his own kind and shares their ancestral knowledge, including the prophecy of the [Night of the Scarlet Moon](../lore/night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md). Deceased wardenbeasts are interred within the sacred [Tombs of the Wardenbeasts](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/tombs_of_the_wardenbeasts.md), where non-feline remains suggest that the form of a wardenbeast may vary across generations.

### Panthers
- **G'rohan Panthers**: Sleek, powerful dark-furred cats native to the western desert region of G'roha. Highly valued as battle mounts by the G'rohan military, they are typically jet-black, though individuals such as Maa'fa display lighter gray markings.
- **Solistian Wild Panthers**: Predatory felines native to the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md) that crackle with electrical energy and channel lightning in combat. While juveniles are modest in size, fully grown specimens—such as the Black Panther and Lightning Panther—are formidable apex predators.

### Tigers
Large, striped apex predators known as venom tigers stalk various wildernesses. Breeds such as the Venomtooth Tiger and Toxictooth Tiger feature distinctive purple or gray pelts and vivid pink eyes, delivering debilitating poisons through elongated fangs and claws.

### Snow Leopards
Adapted to frigid climates such as the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md), snow leopards are large felines with thick white coats and dark spots. Wild varieties sport long black tassels on their ears, utilize ice magic, and tend to their packmates through grooming.

### Manticores
Nimble, leonine beasts possessing horns and thick gray or greenish manes that dwell deep within forested regions. Fiercer varieties boast heavily armored forelegs, additional horns, and elongated fangs capable of tearing grievous wounds.

## Cultural Significance

- **Emblems and Statues**: Lions are widely used throughout Solistia as emblems of authority and prestige. The crest of [Timberain](../../locations/leaflands/towns/timberain.md) displays a lion's head set against a green field, while lion statuary adorns [Timberain Castle](../../locations/leaflands/dungeons/timberain_castle.md), the entrance of [Clockbank](../../locations/brightlands/towns/clockbank.md), the [Guard Outpost](../../locations/harborlands/dungeons/guard_outpost.md) in [Conning Creek](../../locations/harborlands/towns/conning_creek.md), and the [New Delsta: Game Parlor](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta_game_parlor.md).
- **Weaponry**: Arms such as the Jaguar Lance draw inspiration and naming from fierce predatory cats.
