# Ungulates

**Ungulates** are a diverse family of hoofed [mammals](mammalians.md) found across Solistia. The family encompasses both formidable wild horned monsters and domesticated livestock central to agriculture, transport, and warfare.

## Wild Varieties

### Aurochs and Yaks
Aurochs are massive, shaggy beasts resembling yaks that sport sweeping racks of horns. Primarily native to mountainous uplands, they charge intruders with concussive force. Cold-climate variants possess thick gray coats and red eyes. Known varieties include the Shaggy Aurochs (which can manifest as the Shadowy Shaggy Aurochs), Snow Yak, and Demon Yak.

### Behemoths
Behemoths are colossal, cyclopean quadrupeds reminiscent of mammoths, possessing curving tusks, elephantine trunks, and a single eye embedded in an armored stony brow. Solistian behemoths are capable of inflicting petrification upon foes and restoring their vitality by hydrating themselves. Notable specimens include the [Behemoth](../beasts/behemoth.md), Theatremoth, Dread Behemoth, and Sanct Oliphant.

### Bulls and Rhinoceroses
- **Bulls:** Fierce horned quadrupeds native to grasslands and arid expanses such as the [Wildlands](../../locations/wildlands/wildlands.md). They assault intruders with violent charges. Known varieties include the Bloody Bull and Blue Bull.
- **Rhinoceroses:** Heavily armored ungulates with thick plated hides and prominent horns found across regions such as the fertile [Brightlands](../../locations/brightlands/brightlands.md). Varieties include the Bloody Rhino, Raging Rhino, and Blackhorn.

### Deer
Forest-dwelling mammals characterized by dark brown coats and large branching antlers. They charge and ram rivals to assert dominance. Varieties include the Demon Deer (and its corrupted form, Shadowy Demon Deer) and Devil Deer. Peaceful, dappled deer also graze wild throughout the [Leaflands](../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md).

### Goats and Mythic Beasts
Mountain goats inhabit rocky heights and defend themselves with spiraling horns. The family also includes the dread Devourer of Dreams, a chimeric beast possessing a goat's body, leathery bat-like wings, and a serpentine tail. Found lurking in secluded ruins, this legendary monster weaves dark sorcery and strikes terror into intruders.

### Swine and Boars
- **Duorduors:** Massive boars unique to the [Leaflands](../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md). Alongside branching tusks, they possess small antlers on their foreheads. They can summon forest marmots to their aid, harness wind currents, and stun prey with heavy concussive charges. Known types include the common [Duorduor](../../characters/agnea/duorduor.md) and the ferocious [Dire Duorduor](../../characters/others/dire_duorduor.md).
- **Meeps:** Small, furry wild swine found throughout grasslands and woodlands. Preferring defensive habits, they frequently root through undergrowth for mushrooms. Variants include the Meep, Mossy Meep, Shaggy Meep, and the yellow-coated Buttermeep, which is endemic to the [Wildlands](../../locations/wildlands/wildlands.md).

## Domesticated Livestock

Ungulates form the bedrock of pastoral and commercial life across Solistia:
- **Horses:** Widely bred as beasts of burden, carriage draft animals, and cavalry mounts. Horse ranches operate in [Oresrush](../../locations/wildlands/towns/oresrush.md), [Stormhail: Bridge](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail_bridge.md), and [Ku: Castle Town](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/ku_castle_town.md). Mounted warriors include [King Mugen](../../characters/hikari/mugen.md) and the Southern General. [Hikari Ku](../../characters/hikari/hikari.md) fled Ku on horseback, while [Castti Florenz](../../characters/castti/castti.md) and [Malaya](../../characters/castti/malaya.md) escaped the devastation of [Healeaks](../../locations/brightlands/towns/abandoned_village.md) aboard [Jeyah](../../characters/castti/jeyah.md), the trusted horse of [Eir's Apothecaries](../factions/eirs_apothecaries.md). Elaborate statues of mounted warriors adorn [Stormhail](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail.md) and the [Stormhail: Sacred Guard Headquarters](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail_sacred_guard_headquarters.md).
- **Sheep:** Domesticated flocks are reared across pastoral villages, though they are favored prey for predatory [direwolves](direwolves.md). Wild varieties such as the Giant Sheep and Mammoth Sheep also roam the wilderness.
- **Pigs:** Herds of swine are kept by rural villagers in settlements such as [Cropdale](../../locations/leaflands/towns/cropdale.md) and formerly Healeaks.
- **Camels:** Outfitted with saddles and travel blankets, camels serve as working pack and riding mounts across arid desert settlements such as [Sai](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/sai.md) and [Ryu](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/ryu.md).
