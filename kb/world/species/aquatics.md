# Aquatics

**Aquatics** are a broad classification of water-dwelling monsters that inhabit Solistia. Found along coastlines, rivers, lakes, wetlands, and across the open expanse of the [Sundering Sea](../../locations/sundering_sea/the_sundering_sea.md), they encompass amphibians, cetaceans, cnidarians, echinoderms, fish, mollusks, and legendary sea beasts. Other aquatic or amphibious families include [arthropod](arthropods.md) crustaceans, [Froggens](froggens.md), and [Octopuffs](octopuff_travelers.md).

## Amphibians

- **Salamanders:** Large, lizardlike amphibians with slimy brown hides and wide, gaping maws. Inhabiting wetlands and tropical regions, they bellow loudly to disorient prey before ramming or exhaling blinding breath.

## Cetaceans

- **Devious Dolphins:** Despite their name, these creatures resemble beluga whales. Encountered exclusively on the Sundering Sea at night, they are portly, white-skinned cetaceans with bulging foreheads lined with distinctive pink veins.
- **Cetuses:** Rare, four-legged whales with gray skin, stubby tails, and tusked jaws. Capable of swallowing prey whole, they channel potent ice magic in the open waters.

## Cnidarians

- **Colored Jellyfish:** Small medusozoans populating rivers and inland waters. Gold varieties inhabit the waterways of [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md), while blue varieties dwell around the shores of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md).
- **Eyed Jellyfish:** Nocturnal jellyfish native to Toto'haha, distinguished by lurid pink bodies and translucent bells resembling dramatic eyes. As they mature into Demon Jellyfish, their tentacles become spiny and their bells develop clusters of small blue eyes.

## Echinoderms

- **Sea Urchins:** Spherical monsters covered in dense arrays of sharp spikes centered around a single eye. Found in both azure and crimson varieties across tidal caverns, grottoes, and coastal waters, they strike with toxic spines.

## Fish

- **Ancients:** Small, dark-scaled fish with hooked fangs that swim in the rivers of the [Leaflands](../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md). Preserved, prehistoric varieties known as Fossilized Fish dwell in the cold waterways of the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md).
- **Catfish:** Massive gray fish with heavy whiskers found in the waterways of the [Brightlands](../../locations/brightlands/brightlands.md) and Hinoeuma, capable of generating powerful electrical charges.
- **Eels:** Elongated, snakelike parasitic fish with fanged, circular mouths. Both ivory and ebony varieties inhabit Solistian waters, using their needle-sharp teeth to latch onto prey and siphon vitality.
- **Flying Fish:** Small blue-scaled saltwater fish with expansive pectoral fins found in the [Harborlands](../../locations/harborlands/harborlands.md). Larger sailfish and soarfish varieties bear formidable head spines.
- **Landwalkers:** Bipedal terrestrial fish with dark gray-blue scales and tall brown head crests. Prowling the shores after sundown, they walk on land, channel ice magic, and swallow smaller prey whole.
- **Mantas:** Large, flat rays marked with intricate pale patterns and long whiplike tails, active at night across the Sundering Sea.
- **Pufferfish:** Bulbous, spherical fish with white-and-gray patterning found throughout the Sundering Sea. Barbed subspecies bristle with sharp defensive spikes.
- **Seahorses:** Slender, orange marine fish with leafy fin protrusions found in deep sea waters, whipping their rigid tails in defense.
- **Sharks:**
  - **Common Sharks:** Voracious saltwater and freshwater predators with scarred hides and hooked dorsal fins.
  - **Greater Sharks:** Massive, battle-scarred apex predators ringed with serrated teeth. Known specimens include the Battle-Worn Shark of the open Sundering Sea and the Delsta Devil hidden within the [Sunken Maw](../../locations/brightlands/dungeons/sunken_maw.md).
- **Swordfish:** Swift blue fish with elongated, bladelike rostrums and tall crests, haunting the open sea and the decaying ruins of the [Shipwreck of the Empress](../../locations/sundering_sea/dungeons/shipwreck_of_the_empress.md).

## Mollusks

- **Ammonites:** Shelled cephalopods with coiled carapaces, fanged circular maws, and barbed tentacles. Crimson varieties inhabit the [Crestlands](../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md), while ivory varieties dwell in Hinoeuma.
- **Deep Ones:** Colossal octopuses of the ocean abyss. According to observations recorded by the Cait scholar Isla, sailors of old believed these leviathans ascended from underwater chasms, bringing catastrophe to passing ships. They hatch from eggs and protect their nests with thick ink and crushing tentacles.
- **Gastropods:**
  - **Land Slugs:** Large, slimy terrestrial mollusks with elongated eyestalks and mucosal coats that weaken anything they touch.
  - **Sea Slugs:** Delicate, translucent creatures drifting across the nocturnal Sundering Sea, displaying pink internal organs beneath gelatinous white skin.
  - **Snails:** Shelled gastropods with vibrant spiral or conical carapaces. Snail populations at the Cyphlo Banks are known to gnaw stone cliffs to extract minerals for enlarging their shells. Rare fireproof Lava Slugs dwell within volcanic caverns.

## Legendary and Unique Aquatics

- **Scourge of the Sea:** A monstrous entity dwelling within an abyssal vortex in the eastern Sundering Sea. Shrouded in crashing water, it reveals only massive, clawed gray limbs and glowing yellow eyes, dragging entire vessels into the deep.
- **Sea Serpents:** Great reptilian water dragons, epitomized by the [Creature of Legend](../lore/creatures_of_legend.md) [Cateracta](../beasts/cateracta.md) and her child [Acta](../../characters/ochette/acta.md). Revered as the Sea God, Cateracta commanded power over ocean tides and storms, possessing an elongated teal-scaled body, orange fins, and majestic whiskers.
