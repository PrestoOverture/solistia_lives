# Mammalians

**Mammalians** are a diverse family of furred quadrupeds and beasts indigenous to the wilds of Solistia. While many mammalian species fall under specialized classifications—such as [ratkin](ratkin.md), [moles](moles.md), [apes](apes.md), [bats](bats.md), [canines](canines.md), [felines](felines.md), and [ungulates](ungulates.md)—numerous other varieties inhabit the continent's diverse ecosystems.

## Notable Varieties

### Anteaters
Large, long-tongued mammals with distinctive gray-and-white coats. Native to the mountainous plateaus of the [Crestlands](../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md), they feed upon [ants](arthropods.md) and other insects, sustaining their stamina through opportunistic snacking.

### Armadillos
Small, resilient beasts protected by thick, rocky carapace plates. Inhabiting environments ranging from mountain foothills to scrub forests and arid wastes, they curl into dense armored balls and roll aggressively at threats.

### Armor Eaters
Resembling large pangolins clad in metallic, iridescent scales, armor eaters are native to rugged mountains and arid badlands. Possessing immense natural defenses, they chew through solid metals and minerals. They are capable of being tamed, as demonstrated by the beast tamer [Alpione](../../characters/ochette/alpione.md).

### Bears
Massive, apex ursines indigenous to the frigid climes of the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md). Covered in dense white fur—often marked with dark patterns on larger specimens—bears possess lethal fangs and claws capable of tearing through armor and striking terror into prey.

### Ghisarma
Enormous, agile predatory beasts possessing pale purple skin, thick black fur coats, and prominent rabbit-like ears. Known as hyper-aggressive alpha predators, ghisarma invade established forest ecosystems to slaughter native wildlife in displays of territorial dominance, viciously retaliating against any attackers.

### Llorises
Small, nimble mammals with brown coats and ringed, striped tails native to regions including the tropical islands of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md). Hunting in packs by leaping and raking with their claws, their pack leaders possess a magpie-like penchant for hoarding shiny jewelry and trinkets.

### Marmots
Small, bushy-tailed rodents marked with chipmunk-like stripes. Standard brown marmots dwell in temperate forests, while pale snow marmots thrive in frozen wastes. Defensive specimens pelt intruders with hardened nuts. Marmots can also be domesticated as companion animals.

### Porcupines and Spiny Shrews
Small rodents found in the forests of the [Leaflands](../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md) and subterranean hollows of the [Crestlands](../../locations/crestlands/crestlands.md). Their backs bristle with sharp, needle-like quills that they loose like arrows when cornered.

### Weasels
Large, sinuous mountain predators sporting tan pelts, black-tipped tails, and razor-sharp paws. They are notorious in folk tradition as "grape thieves" due to their habit of snatching provisions from travelers.
