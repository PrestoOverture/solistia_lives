# Beastling

> *"Humans fighting. [Shadow](../lore/shadow.md) swirling. Humans greedy. [D'arqest](../../characters/others/darqest.md) cursing. Humans changing. Half-human. Half-beast."*
> — [Shirlutto](../../characters/temenos/shirlutto.md), on the origin of the Beastlings

The **Beastlings** are a race of humanoid animal hybrids inhabiting the island of [Toto'haha](../../locations/totohaha/totohaha.md), primarily residing in [Beasting Village](../../locations/totohaha/towns/beasting_village.md) in the west and the secretive [Nameless Village](../../locations/totohaha/towns/nameless_village.md). Simple, carefree, and closely attuned to nature, their lives center around communal bonds, hunting, and sustenance.

## Origin

The Beastling race was created during the ancient era by the Archmage [D'arqest](../../characters/others/darqest.md). Harnessing the dark power of the [Shadow](../lore/shadow.md), D'arqest cast a curse upon warring human armies clashing near the [Rifted Rock](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/rifted_rock.md). His curse stripped the soldiers of their mortal greed, warping their physical forms into half-human, half-beast hybrids. The Beastlings who dwell in the [Nameless Village](../../locations/totohaha/towns/nameless_village.md) continue to serve as guardians of the Rifted Rock, guiding travelers through the perilous [Wandering Wood](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/wandering_wood.md) to keep watch over the sealed site.

## Physical Characteristics

- **Anatomy**: Adult Beastlings stand around 130 cm (4'3") tall. They have brown skin, cat-like ears set apart from their hair, canine-like tails (often pale gray with darker tips), claws on each digit, rounded snouts, and moist noses.
- **Senses**: Beastlings possess an acute, near-supernatural sense of smell that allows them to track quarry across great distances and instinctively discern whether a person harbors malicious intent. They also share a natural communicative affinity with wild beasts.
- **Lifespan**: Beastlings mature faster and have shorter lifespans than humans. Individuals in their late thirties are considered elders, and average life expectancy is approximately fifty years.
- **Interbreeding**: Beastlings and humans can produce viable offspring. Descendants of mixed unions (such as [Alpione](../../characters/ochette/alpione.md)) physically resemble ordinary humans, losing bestial features such as animal ears and tails while retaining an unusually sharp sense of smell.

## Culture and Society

- **Language**: Beastlings possess their own spoken and written tongue. An ancient dialect known as "Ancient Beastling" is preserved by a few village elders, though community life relies as much on shared emotion and instinct as on formal vocabulary. While most Beastlings can comprehend common human speech, the anatomy of their snouts makes pronunciation difficult, leading many to converse in concise sentences. However, select individuals—such as the traveler [Ochette](../../characters/ochette/ochette.md)—attain full fluency.
- **Diet & Economy**: Pure carnivores, Beastlings subsist almost exclusively on raw meat and cured jerky, lacking an indigenous culture of cooking or agriculture. In [Beasting Village](../../locations/totohaha/towns/beasting_village.md), dried jerky functions as the primary medium of exchange, and food is prioritized above virtually all else.
- **Customs**: According to ancient custom, Beastlings present hand-crafted "amulets of affection" to those they wish to befriend. Primitive geometric symbols—such as sun and moon markings—serve as an early form of script, inscribed with plant sap and mud.

## Settlements and Governance

### Beasting Village
The largest Beastling settlement, located in western Toto'haha, is governed by a spiritual protector known as a warden. The current warden is [Master Juvah](../../characters/ochette/juvah.md), a long-lived wardenbeast over two centuries old. Despite the Beastlings' peaceful intentions, the settlement has long experienced territorial friction with encroaching human settlers, leading to uneasy relations between the two races.

### Nameless Village
Tucked away beyond the [Wandering Wood](../../locations/totohaha/dungeons/wandering_wood.md), the Nameless Village is led by an elected mayor and dedicated to watching over the Rifted Rock. The village observes the traditional "ritual of the heir," a grueling rite of succession that subjects the designated future leader to severe physical tests and ascetic restrictions.

## Notable Beastlings

- [Ochette](../../characters/ochette/ochette.md): One of the eight travelers of Solistia, chosen by Master Juvah to protect Toto'haha from the Night of the Scarlet Moon.
- [Shirlutto](../../characters/temenos/shirlutto.md): An elder guide of the Wandering Wood who preserves the historical memory of D'arqest and the birth of the Beastlings.
