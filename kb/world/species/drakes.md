# Drakes

**Drakes** are lizardlike, winged [reptilians](reptilians.md) found throughout Solistia. Although they bear a resemblance to dragons, drakes tend to be smaller and do not breathe fire, instead channeling various elemental powers such as wind, ice, or dark magic.

Some drakes walk on two legs, possessing fully developed wings that enable flight, while others are quadrupedal with stubbier wings.

## Varieties

### Plains and Snow Drakes
- **Plains Drakes**: Feature larger wings and a bipedal posture. They inhabit grassy regions such as the [Brightlands](../../locations/brightlands/brightlands.md) and harness wind magic.
- **Snow Drakes**: Quadrupedal with stubbier wings, adapted to frigid climates like the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md) and channeling ice.

### Tyrannodrakes and Wyrms
- **[Tyrannodrake](../beasts/tyrannodrake.md)**: A massive, quadrupedal beast that closely resembles a dragon due to its stout build, horns, and multiple rows of gray spikes along its neck. It dens in moss-draped, wet grottoes, demonstrates remarkable adaptability, and can wield dark magic.
- **Giant Wyrm**: A desert-dwelling counterpart adapted to arid environments.

### Winged Serpents
**Winged Serpents** are slender, serpentine reptilians featuring large jaws, venomous claws, and wings. They inhabit mountainous terrain, cold climates, and arid deserts. Solistian varieties include:
- Winged Serpent
- Frostwing Serpent
- Silversand Winged Serpent

### Azure Armordrake
The Divine Beast Lutiya is designated as an Azure Armordrake, though unlike typical drakes, it resembles a turtle and lacks both wings and claws.
