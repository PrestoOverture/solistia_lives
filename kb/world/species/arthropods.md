# Arthropods

**Arthropods** are a diverse family of many-legged creatures inhabiting the wilds, caverns, and deserts of Solistia, spanning insects, arachnids, and crustaceans.

## Insects

### Sand Lions
**Sand lions** are massive, predatory antlion-like insects encased in heavy black carapaces with gigantic serrated mandibles. Endemic to the arid wastes of [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md), they excavate vast subterranean dens and ambush prey from below using shifting sand traps. While typically remaining underground, surface tremors and disruptions can provoke them into violent surface rampages capable of devastating caravans and settlements.

### Moths
Native to the lush canopies of the [Leaflands](../../locations/leaflands/leaflands.md), moths are large, winged insects covered in dense, insulating fuzz with feathery antennae. Emerging from sack-like silk cocoons, they produce durable threads to bind threats and navigate the forest undergrowth.

### Ants
Ants are prevalent across Solistia's diverse biomes, ranging from common tunnelers to aggressive desert army ants capable of spraying caustic acid. Certain ant species exhibit intricate symbiotic behaviors, tending and cultivating subterranean [mushroom monsters](plants.md) that rely entirely on the ants for survival while fortifying the colony's nest.

### Bugs
- **Clawed Bugs**: Primarily desert-dwelling quadrupeds sporting heavy front pincers, segmented abdomens, and bright carapaces ranging from green to violet. Notable varieties include assassin bugs and heavily mineralized stone bugs.
- **Spud Bugs**: Woodland, caterpillar-like insects covered in elongated spines that secrete sticky, slowing fluids to deter predators.

### Flies
Four-winged insects with segmented bodies and biting mouthparts native to damp and verdant environments. They range in size from slender swarming flies to bulkier, beetle-shaped varieties.

### Butterflies
Immense winged insects possessing vibrant patterns, wide multifaceted eyes, and long proboscises. Many, including the giant Monarch, disperse clouds of disorienting, toxic dust from their wings to incapacitate adversaries.

### Rollers
Dung beetles protected by thick shells that inhabit arid regions. They use their hind legs to maneuver large spherical objects across dunes, commonly rolling balls of cactus or discarded human skulls.

## Arachnids

### Spiders
Multi-eyed predators equipped with eight bristled legs and hooked grasping appendages. They spin dense webbing throughout caverns and dense foliage to snare intruders and inject numbing toxins.

### Scorpions
Armored arachnids bearing heavy crushing pincers and venomous barbed tails. While most varieties populate Solistia's desert wastes, specialized coastal species also dwell along shorelines.

## Crustaceans

- **Conchs**: Hermit crab-like crustaceans found along coastlines that seek shelter within heavy spiral shells.
- **Scissor Crabs & Reaper Crabs**: Aggressive aquatic crustaceans armed with large, jagged claws and thick carapaces, prowling coastlines and subterranean waterways.
