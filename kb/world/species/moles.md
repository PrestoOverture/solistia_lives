# Moles

**Moles** are subterranean [mammalian](mammalians.md) [demihumans](demihumans.md) native to the cavern networks of Solistia. Highly adapted to darkness and underground excavation, they live in organized tribal societies primarily throughout the [Wildlands](../../locations/wildlands/wildlands.md) and the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md). Their leaders and chieftains are recognized as molekings.

## Regional Breeds

### Wild Moles
Native to the arid badlands and underground mines of the [Wildlands](../../locations/wildlands/wildlands.md), wild moles possess thick brown fur and stocky, muscular builds. They equip themselves with heavy metallic plate armor and fight utilizing their oversized front digging claws. In combat, they are known to burrow beneath the earth to ambush adversaries from below.

### Frost Moles
Inhabiting glacial caverns and snowy peaks throughout the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md), frost moles possess gray pelts and are taller and leaner than their southern counterparts. Rather than relying on brute force and armor, frost moles dress in ceremonial cloth robes and carry wooden staffs, specializing in the casting of ice magic.
