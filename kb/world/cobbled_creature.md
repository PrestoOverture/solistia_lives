# Cobbled Creature

The **Cobbled Creature** is a chimera created by [Harvey](../characters/harvey.md) and confined within his [Underground Laboratory](../locations/underground_laboratory.md). During the events of Chapter 4 of [Osvald](../characters/osvald.md)'s story, it burst free alongside the [Deformed Creature](deformed_creature.md) and the [Synthesized Creature](synthesized_creature.md) to confront Osvald and his companions.

## Overview

In appearance, the Cobbled Creature bears a strong resemblance to regular Kingfisher enemies. In combat, the monstrous creation scavenges its opponents, draining their vitality to sustain itself.
