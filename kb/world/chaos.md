# Chaos

**Chaos**, or Primordial Chaos, is the formless state of existence that preceded creation.

## Cosmology

According to Solistian myth, the world was shaped when Solis, the Primordial God, created Solistia, the eight patron [gods](gods.md), and humanity. Solis raised the sun above the realm, establishing dawn and the flow of time, thereby driving back the formless chaos and establishing cosmic order.

## Manifestation

During the events of [The Journey for the Dawn](../stories/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md), the resurrected wicked god [Vide](vide.md) proclaimed himself to be the embodiment of "Chaos," intent on devouring all life and plunging the realm back into eternal darkness and formlessness before clashing with the eight travelers.
