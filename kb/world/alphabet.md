# Alphabet

The people of Solistia write using their own runic alphabets. Characters of Solistia's alphabet can be seen on the world map, on in-game signage, and in newspaper segments, such as those printed during [Partitio](../characters/partitio.md)'s travels.

## Linguistic Diversity

Solistia is home to a wide variety of spoken and written tongues. According to a scholar residing in [Montwise](../locations/montwise.md), at least 57 distinct languages are spoken across the realm.

- **[Ku](ku.md):** The people of Ku are bilingual, speaking Common Solistian while also maintaining their own distinct regional language and alphabet.
- **[Beastlings](beastling.md):** While younger generations of Beastlings primarily speak fragments of Common Solistian—requiring elders such as [Juvah](../characters/juvah.md) to instruct youth like [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md) in speech—the Beastlings possess their own ancient alphabet. This script corresponds to their ancestral tongue, known as "Old Beastling," which is preserved only by a few elder Beastlings.
