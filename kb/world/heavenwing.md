# Heavenwing

The **Heavenwing** is a formidable, large blue-and-purple avian beast encountered in Solistia.

## Overview and Lore

The Heavenwing is a massive predatory bird capable of commanding intense heat and fire. In *Octopath Traveler II*, the creature makes its roost within the heights of the [Old Clock Tower](../locations/old_clock_tower.md) in the town of [Clockbank](../locations/clockbank.md). Its menacing presence and territorial nesting prevented engineers and townsfolk from accessing the machinery to repair the long-silent town timepiece.

During the side story [For Whom the Clock Tower Tolls](../stories/for_whom_the_clock_tower_tolls.md), travelers scale the heights of the abandoned tower and slay the fiery raptor, allowing the clock tower's bells to ring across the town once more. Following its initial defeat, rare specimens of the beast may occasionally be spotted in the upper reaches of the tower.
