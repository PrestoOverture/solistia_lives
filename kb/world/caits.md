# Caits

**Caits** are a type of [feline](felines.md) [demihuman](demihumans.md) resembling cats that carry large sacks of treasure and flee quickly upon encountering travelers.

## Lore

In Solistia, caits are regarded as the most timid of demihumans. According to myth, they were the only demihuman race to side with humans during the ancient great war between humans and demihumans. Following the war, they settled within human society; however, their cunning earned them animosity from both sides. Consequently, the caits fled into the wild to live in secrecy.
