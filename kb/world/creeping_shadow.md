# Creeping Shadow

The **Creeping Shadow** is an unnatural manifestation of darkness encountered along the [Animal Trail](../locations/animal_trail.md) during Part II of the apothecary and hunter Crossed Paths story. It is a more powerful form of the [Wriggling Shadow](wriggling_shadow.md).

## Appearance and Nature

The Creeping Shadow takes the form of a nightmarish composite of tentacles associated with [Vide](vide.md), incorporating elements of the Wriggling Tentacle, Lithe Tentacle, Creeping Tentacle, and Thwarting Tentacle. In combat, it harnesses dark forces through destructive shadowy abilities such as Oppressive Shadow, Invitation to Darkness, and Dark Wave.

## Story

During their journey along the [Animal Trail](../locations/animal_trail.md), [Castti Florenz](../characters/castti.md) and [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md) drove off a Wriggling Shadow, only to become separated within the dark woods. Isolated in the gloom, Castti began hearing the despairing voices of patients she had been unable to save, pushing her toward the brink of despair. Ochette's voice called Castti back to her senses, and the two companions reunited to confront the Creeping Shadow. Together, they defeated the entity, silencing its insidious voices and restoring peace to the trail.
