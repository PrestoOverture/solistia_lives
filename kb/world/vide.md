# Vide

> "*Now, I am become chaos... devourer of all life in this world! Even you... Foolish gods... All shall be mine!*"  
> —*Vide*

> "*Long ago... Darkness and confusion reigned. The hearts of the people ran over with wickedness. The world seemed eager to greet its end. Then, eight [gods](gods.md) assembled. And with their [sacred flame](sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md), brought light to the world.*"  
> —*Legend of the [Order of the Sacred Flame](order_of_the_sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md)*

**Vide**, known as the **Wicked God** or the **God of Shadow**, is the primordial malevolent deity worshiped by the nihilistic [Moonshade Order](moonshade_order.md). Desiring the total annihilation of all creation, Vide is the ancient progenitor and ultimate fountainhead of the [Shadow](shadow.md). Manifesting as a colossal, burning red eye surrounded by writhing violet flames and dark mist, Vide embodies chaos and oblivion.

## Mythological History

Vide's primordial origins remain shrouded in mystery. In ancient times, wickedness consumed the hearts of humanity, and Vide sought to extinguish all life. To avert the world's destruction, the eight patron [gods](gods.md) of Solistia assembled and sacrificed their divine forms. Combining the gods' celestial strength, [Aelfric](aelfric.md) brought forth the [Sacred Flame](sacred_flame_octopath_traveler_ii.md), defeating Vide and sealing the dark deity alongside its sacred domain of [Vidania](../locations/vidania.md) deep beneath the [Sundering Sea](../locations/the_sundering_sea.md). The gods then passed into a deep slumber. Over the ensuing centuries, Vide's power gradually leaked through the weakening seal, corrupting mortals and beasts in the form of the Shadow.

## Resurrection and Fall

During [The Journey for the Dawn](../stories/the_journey_for_the_dawn.md), key leaders of the Moonshade Order systematically extinguished the four Sacred Flames across Solistia, shattering the primordial seal. Vidania breached the ocean surface once again, and the strategist [Oboro](../characters/oboro.md) journeyed to the island's heart to perform the rite of revival. 

When the eight travelers pursued him to Vidania after rekindling the four flames, Oboro cast himself backward into the dark sacrificial flame, offering his own soul and mortal life to awaken Vide. Reborn as the embodiment of chaos, Vide sought to obliterate the travelers and devour the dawn. After a desperate struggle, the eight travelers vanquished the Wicked God, permanently banishing the eternal night and watching the dawn break over Solistia as Vidania sank back beneath the waves.
