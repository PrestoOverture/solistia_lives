# Demons

**Demons** are powerful, monstrous abominations warped by dark divine power—most notably the dark magic of [Galdera](galdera.md) or the [Shadow](shadow.md). All demons were once human, but underwent drastic transformations into unnatural forms, gaining fearsome strength and infernal abilities in the process.

Most demons lose their sense of identity, resulting in the loss of reason and their descent into bloodlust. When demons speak, they do so in a resonant, echoing voice, and their deaths result in their disintegration into a cloud of dark smoke.

## Traits and Physiology

Demons take various shapes; however, most of their bodies possess unnatural proportions, such as elongated limbs, along with brightly glowing or burning red eyes, gray skin, and skeletal protrusions. Many demons remain generally humanoid, allowing them to wield weapons, while others resemble beasts with fur or scales, sprout extra appendages, or have bodies wreathed in smoke.

The appearance and nature of a demon often depend on how the transformation occurred:
- **Shadow Transformation**: Warriors who carelessly channel too much of the Shadow's power in battle—such as the [Enshrouded King](../characters/mugen.md) and Kaldena of Night—warp into heavily distorted humanoid demonic forms.
- **Darkblood Bow Transformation**: Those transformed through sacrificial rites involving the cursed Darkblood Bow—such as the [Dark Entity](../characters/dark_entity.md) and the [Grotesque Monster](grotesque_monster.md)—become fully bestial in appearance and unrecognizable as humanoid.

## Methods of Transformation

In Solistia, transformation into a demon typically cannot be reversed once complete. Known and theorized methods include:

- **The Shadow**: Although the Shadow can be harnessed without triggering a demonic transformation (as demonstrated by [Hikari](../characters/hikari.md) and [Harvey](../characters/harvey.md)), drawing excessively upon its power without proper precautions warps mortals into demons.
- **Darkblood Bow Sacrifice**: The cursed Darkblood Bow is capable of causing grotesque demonic mutations. The [Dark Entity](../characters/dark_entity.md) repeats [Roi](../characters/roi.md)'s final words before death and wears shreds of his robe, suggesting that the Dark Hunter [Petrichor](../characters/petrichor.md) mutated him after his disappearance with the bow, potentially in combination with Harvey's Shadow-based experiments. Later, following Petrichor's death with the bow in her possession, the [Grotesque Monster](grotesque_monster.md) appeared at the site of her death.
- **Arcane Experimentation**: Sorcerer [Harvey](../characters/harvey.md) crafted a grimoire known as the *Book of Demons* using the corpse of dark sorcerer [D'arqest](../characters/darqest.md), allowing him to harness the Shadow. The infernal tome *[From the Far Reaches of Hell](../stories/from_the_far_reaches_of_hell_book.md)* also outlines formulas for synthesizing dark blood-crystals.

## Lore and Expressions

- **["Hikari"](../characters/hikari_shadow.md)**: Represents a psychological manifestation of the Shadow within [Hikari Ku](../characters/hikari.md). Hikari avoids undergoing a demonic transformation by mastering Shadow's Hold rather than allowing the darkness to consume him.
- **Transformed Beasts**: Non-human creatures can also be warped by these dark forces; the [Darkling of the Sorrowful Moon](darkling_of_the_sorrowful_moon.md) is presumed to have been warped by the Darkblood Bow combined with Harvey's Shadow magic.
- **Metaphorical Use**: Ferocious warriors are sometimes referred to hyperbolically as "demons" despite remaining human; during his arena tournament, [Hikari](../characters/hikari_shadow.md) is noted to "fight like a demon."
- **Nomenclature**: Several natural beasts bear the descriptive title of "demon," including the Demon Deer, Demon Goat, Demon Yak, and Demon Jellyfish.
