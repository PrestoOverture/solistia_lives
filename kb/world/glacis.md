# Glacis

> *"Glacis... Those beautiful wings, so wide they all but cover the continent!"*  
> —*Master [Juvah](../characters/juvah.md)*

> *"It is said that [Stormhail](../locations/stormhail.md) owes its existence to its guardian, Glacis. Residing on the [Sacred Peak Altahe](../locations/sacred_peak_altahe.md), it is thought that Glacis put an end to the cold snap that nearly spelled doom for all who lived here."*  
> —*The [Mercantile Manuscript](../stories/masoud_script.md)*

**Glacis** is a divine winged beast and one of the three revered [Creatures of Legend](creatures_of_legend.md) in *Octopath Traveler II*. She serves as the ancient guardian of [Stormhail](../locations/stormhail.md), roosting at the apex of [Sacred Peak Altahe](../locations/sacred_peak_altahe.md).

## Legend & History

According to Solistian lore recorded in the Mercantile Manuscript, the city of Stormhail owes its continued existence to Glacis. Long ago, a catastrophic cold snap nearly wiped out the inhabitants of the region. Glacis struck down the raging blizzard, scattering snow and ice that accumulated into [Sacred Peak Altahe](../locations/sacred_peak_altahe.md). Glacis subsequently took up residence on the mountain as the city's protector, shielding the settlement from outsiders with perimeter blizzards while being revered by the townsfolk below.

Ten years prior to the events of the journey, the [Dark Hunter](../characters/petrichor.md) led an expedition to slay Glacis. The guardian killed nearly all of the hunters, with only the Dark Hunter and [Heig](../characters/heig.md) surviving; however, before retreating, the intruders destroyed her clutch of eggs out of malice. Consumed by grief and sorrow, Glacis grew bitter and unleashed an unceasing blizzard across the mountain, chilling Stormhail in perpetual frost.

## Story

During [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md)'s quest to rally the legendary beasts, Heig ascends Sacred Peak Altahe to bury his deceased comrades and seek retribution. Glacis attacks and wounds Heig, prompting Ochette to step forward and calm the creature. Though Glacis initially strikes out in an effort to drive the travelers away, Ochette bests her in combat and reassures her that she still has a vital purpose and land worth defending: the island of [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md). Moved by Ochette's words, Glacis willingly accompanies her to help halt the impending [Night of the Scarlet Moon](night_of_the_scarlet_moon.md).

During the calamity on Toto'haha, Glacis fights side-by-side with [Tera](tera.md), creating an expansive bridge of ice across the [Stormy Cape](../locations/stormy_cape.md) to grant the travelers safe passage.

## Characteristics & Powers

Glacis commands extreme cold, freezing gusts, and silver winds. In battle, she can freeze opponents solid, summon Blue Elementals by breathing life into the ice, and blanket the battlefield in biting sub-zero temperatures. Upon defeat, she yields the Guardian's Iceblade.
