# Avians

**Avians** are a broad family of feathered, winged beasts inhabiting the skies, forests, cliffs, and waters of Solistia. Ranging from common raptors and shorebirds to eerie monsters and sentient beings, avians play a prominent role in Solistian wildlife, mythology, and heraldry. Among their most distinctive offshoots are the [Birdians](birdians.md), a civilized [demihuman](demihumans.md) species of bipedal, weapon-bearing avians.

## Common Avians

- **Condors**: Large, scavenging vulturine birds with brown or black plumage and bald necks that frequent the arid expanses of the [Wildlands](../locations/wildlands.md). Alpha variants known as King Condors bear prominent fleshy wattles.
- **Dreadwings**: An unsettling breed of avian scavengers characterized by elongated beaks, emaciated frames, and glaring red eyes. Dreadwings undergo distinct developmental stages: beginning as mottled gray-and-green egglings, emerging as single-clawed hatchlings, developing into scuttling fledglings, and finally maturing into adult winged terrors capable of channeling wind.
- **Falcons & Eagles**: Sharp-eyed birds of prey native to rugged mountainous heights and crags across the Wildlands, commanding air currents in flight.
- **Flamingos**: Wading waterbirds encountered along the waterways and inland seas of Solistia, recognized by their sinuous necks, distinct white or pink plumage, and habit of resting on a single leg.
- **Howlers**: Large woodland owls with sweeping ocular crests and decorative eye spots across their wings. Variants dwell in dense forests and northern permafrost regions.
- **Kartikeya**: Rare, peacock-like tropical birds sporting fan-like tails of pink, blue, and green plumes, tan bodies, and ribboned plumage.
- **Kingfishers**: Streamlined marine avians with bright plumage and yellow crests that hunt along ocean shores and coastal inlets.
- **Penguins**: Rotund, flightless birds with dense coats and stubby wings that dwell across polar waters and rocky coastlines.
- **Ravens**: Flocking corvids with glossy black or ash plumage, traditionally feared as omens of misfortune.

## Unique Avians & Creatures of Legend

### Malamaowl
Endemic to the southern island of [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md), the **Malamaowl** is a rare breed of sentient owl distinguished by soft barn-owl plumage and a crescent moon marking upon its chest. The only known specimen, [Mahina](../characters/mahina.md), possesses speech, profound ancient wisdom, and the ability to channel multiple elemental magics.

### Glacis
One of the ancient [Creatures of Legend](creatures_of_legend.md), **Glacis** is a majestic, colossal avian that commands blizzards from her roost atop [Sacred Peak Altahe](../locations/sacred_peak_altahe.md). She possesses silver-blue plumage, snowflake-shaped wing structures, and a trailing tail of crystalline icicles. Though solitary, she lays eggs to ensure her lineage survives.

## Wildlife and Cultural Lore

- **Islebirds**: Bright red parrots inhabiting the jungles of [Toto'haha](../locations/totohaha.md). [Ochette](../characters/ochette.md) notes that they are exceedingly sensitive to environmental danger and startle before other animals detect a threat.
- **Domestic and Wild Fowl**: Roosters and chickens are commonly kept in settlements such as [Sai](../locations/sai.md) and [Flamechurch](../locations/flamechurch.md), while graceful wild swans frequent the lakes of the [Leaflands](../locations/leaflands.md).
- **Regional Heraldry**: Solistian vexillology incorporates native avians; a desert vulture serves as the regional symbol of the [Wildlands](../locations/wildlands.md), while a soaring gull represents the [Sundering Sea](../locations/the_sundering_sea.md).
- **Griffin Iconography**: Griffin motifs frequently recur across Solistian architecture and sculpture. Carvings of griffins adorn the banners and masonry of the scholastic city of [Montwise](../locations/montwise.md), flank the royal throne within [Castle Ku](../locations/castle_ku.md), and decorate prestigious estates in [New Delsta](../locations/new_delsta.md) (including [Diamante's Estate](../locations/diamantes_estate.md)), where they symbolize majesty and affluence.
