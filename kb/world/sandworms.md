# Sandworms

**Sandworms** are desert-dwelling monsters that burrow through dunes. Featuring thickly plated red or purple bodies, they bare gaping mouths bristling with fangs.

## Common Sandworms

Common sandworms are sizable invertebrates with dark purple skin, pale underbellies, and spiked heads. The whiteworm is an exception, featuring gray skin and red spikes. Certain species of sandworms are capable of blinding their prey.

Varieties of common sandworms include:
- Sandworm
- Desert Worm
- Whiteworm / Tombwarden Worm
- Infernal Beast

## Giant Sandworms

Giant sandworms, typified by the fearsome Lord of the Sands, possess a bulky body, rows of spines, and a many-layered maw. They dwell in ruins or caves—often guarding treasure—and can summon lesser worms to their aid, in addition to harnessing wind magic and inflicting blindness.

Varieties of giant sandworms include:
- Lord of the Sands
- Ruler of the Sands
- Master of the Sands
