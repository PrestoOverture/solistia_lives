# Plants

**Plants** are a family of botanical organisms and creatures resembling trees, mushrooms, weeds, and other vegetation. Found in forests, deserts, and field locations throughout Solistia, these wild plants harbor an affinity for the natural world and frequently exhibit photosynthetic regeneration or defensive stings. Due to their combustible nature, many species of plants are vulnerable to fire.

## Trees

### Treants
**Treants** are bipedal, ent-like arboreal creatures. Some species feature leafy branches, while others possess sharp wooden claws. They are capable of defending themselves with stings that induce blindness or silence.

### Peek-a-Boos
**Peek-a-Boos** are leafless, four-legged logs with nine pairs of eerie red eyes looking out from holes in their bark. Rather than a single tree organism, they are hollow, dead logs inhabited and mobilized by a colony of nine smaller creatures nesting within.

## Fungi

### Mushrooms
**Mushrooms** are vaguely humanoid fungi with woody stems and colorful, mossy caps. They produce soporific spores that lull targets to sleep and are capable of regenerating through photosynthesis.

### Fungoids
**Fungoids** are insectile, six-legged spores equipped with three pinching arms.

## Weeds

### Walking Weeds
Walking weeds are small bundles of mobile vines that travel on tentacle-like legs. Shambling Weeds commonly roam the wilderness and can also be proliferated or summoned by larger vegetation to aid them. Rampant Weeds are a larger, purple-hued variant with pincers.

### Wanderweeds
Wanderweeds are dense, spherical shrubs resembling tumbleweeds that thrive across diverse environments, including forests, arid deserts, and frigid climates.

## Carnivores

### Carnivorous Plants
Carnivorous Plants are immense, predatory flora with pulsing flowers and thrashing vines. Unlike ordinary vegetation, they are mobile and capable of crawling across distances rather than remaining rooted in place, consuming prey to nourish themselves.

### Manymaws
Manymaws are colossal subterranean plants featuring tubular bodies, tooth-lined blossoms, and dozens of blue eyes. They make their habitats in underground grottos and caverns, utilizing potent toxins to incapacitate their prey.

## Connection to the Dark
Plant life is occasionally corrupted or manifested by servants of darkness. Notably, the dark agent [Arcanette](../characters/arcanette.md) manifests atop a gigantic skeletal vine glowing violet and colored gray-green.
