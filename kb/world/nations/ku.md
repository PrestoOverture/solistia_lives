# Ku

> *"For centuries has the sword of our clan struck down our foes. Their blood has stained the desert red, and our nation has become great in turn."*
> —King [Jigo Ku](../../characters/hikari/jigo_ku.md)

The **Kingdom of Ku** is a martial nation located in the arid region of [Hinoeuma](../../locations/hinoeuma/hinoeuma.md). Built upon centuries of warfare and territorial conquest, Ku expanded from a modest desert settlement into the dominant military power of the region. Fifty vassal houses, including [Clan Mei](../factions/clan_mei.md), historically pledged fealty to Ku's crown.

Ku is the homeland and starting point of Prince [Hikari Ku](../../characters/travelers/hikari.md).

## Overview

The royal bloodline of Ku bears an ancient curse stemming from intermingling with the lineage of the archmage [D'arqest](../../characters/others/darqest.md). In moments of emotional upheaval or combat, this curse manifests as an overwhelming bloodlust—a ruthless shadow state that drives its host to massacre without mercy.

Prior to the reign of King Hikari, society within Ku was strictly stratified and oppressive. The highborn and military elite held absolute power over commoners, frequently cutting them down without consequence, as occurred to the father of [Ritsu Mishuyo](../../characters/hikari/ritsu_mishuyo.md) and [Mikka Mishuyo](../../characters/hikari/mikka_mishuyo.md). Foreign merchants were largely barred from trading within Ku unless they maintained longstanding connections to the royal court.

## History

### Early Foundations
The kingdom's first sovereign was King Sharak. In antiquity, when an immense and seemingly invincible empire dominated Solistia, Sharak boldly waged war against it, teaching that "friendship is the power by which any evil can be defeated." Historical records from this foundational era were written in the ancient Ku [alphabet](../lore/alphabet.md); these texts were long forbidden to the public until Hikari later lifted the ban, commissioning scholars to decipher them.

Across subsequent centuries, Ku waged near-constant war against neighboring desert states, including the fallen kingdom of [U](u.md). Ku celebrated each triumph with lavish festivities.

### Mugen's Coup
Two years prior to Hikari's journey, Ku went to war against rival desert forces under General [Mugen](../../characters/hikari/mugen.md). Prince Hikari served as a captain alongside his allies Ritsu and [Rai Mei](../../characters/hikari/rai_mei.md). After the campaign, King [Jigo Ku](../../characters/hikari/jigo_ku.md) appointed Hikari protector of [Ku: Castle Town](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/ku_castle_town.md) as a test of his compassion and leadership. Pleased by his conduct, Jigo secretly chose Hikari as heir to the throne.

Mugen staged a diversion, feigning an illicit weapons deal outside the city walls to draw Hikari away. While the prince was absent, Mugen's forces sacked the town, murdered King Jigo, and seized the throne. King Jigo urged the returning Hikari to escape and find trusted allies before dying. Retainer [Benkei](../../characters/hikari/benkei.md) remained behind to hold off pursuers, allowing Hikari to flee the country.

Taking the crown, King Mugen declared Hikari a traitor and tore up Ku's non-aggression pact with neighboring [Sa](sa.md), slaughtering its monarch and populace. Ritsu assassinated [General Rou](../../characters/hikari/rou.md) to win Mugen's favor and become supreme general. Mugen subsequently extended Ku's military aggression across the sea to [Stormhail](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail.md), subjugating [Castle Mei](../../locations/winterlands/towns/stormhail_castle_mei.md).

### Restoration of Ku
Hikari gathered allies across Solistia and returned to Hinoeuma. Utilizing a sandstorm coordinated by strategist [Kazan](../../characters/hikari/oboro.md) and backed by Clan Mei, Hikari's forces breached Castle Town. Within the castle, Hikari fought General Ritsu, who was mortally wounded and implored Hikari to build a better Ku before dying.

In the throne room of [Castle Ku](../../locations/hinoeuma/towns/castle_ku.md), Mugen revealed his longstanding goal to exterminate the lineage of Hikari's mother, [Kura](../../characters/hikari/kura.md). Mugen attempted to awaken Hikari's cursed shadow self, but Hikari confronted and conquered his internal darkness. Driven to madness, Mugen unsheathed the Darkblood Blade—an ancient Ku family heirloom soaked in the blood of countless souls—and was consumed by its dark sorcery, transforming into the [Enshrouded King](../../characters/hikari/mugen.md). Hikari defeated Mugen and reclaimed the throne.

### Postwar Reform
Upon his coronation, King Hikari dismantled Ku's aggressive military apparatus, established an 11-captain defensive force, liberated subjugated neighboring territories, and opened Ku's borders to free commerce. In place of a triumphal celebration, Hikari and the dancer [Agnea Bristarni](../../characters/travelers/agnea.md) organized a solemn memorial festival to honor those who perished during the conflict, featuring traditional lute music by [Yomi](../../characters/others/yomi.md).

## Culture and Traditions

- **Instruments and Music**: The lyre originated in Ku and remains a beloved instrument. The city is also renowned for skilled lute musicians. A revered local folk melody is titled "By the Light of the Heart."
- **Cuisine**: A traditional staple is [azuki porridge](../lore/items_octopath_traveler_ii.md), cooked from indigenous red beans. Soldiers consume it before going to battle, with its crimson color serving as a reminder of blood shed and comrades lost.
- **Folklore and Oaths**: Desert dwellers swear oaths "by the tempest." Local legend humorously posits that desert kingdoms possess short names (Ku, U, Sa) to prevent speakers from swallowing blowing sand.
- **Treasures and Heraldry**: Golden dragons feature prominently upon Ku's banners, architecture, and throne room tapestries. Ku possesses four celebrated historic treasures: the Phoenix Fan, the Dragon Vase, and two relics modeled after the qilin and the tortoise.
