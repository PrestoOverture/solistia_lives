# Delstasch

**Delstasch** is a former kingdom of the [Brightlands](../../locations/brightlands/brightlands.md).

## History

The origins and history of Delstasch are largely unknown. For unspecified reasons, Delstasch once went to war with the [Winterlands](../../locations/winterlands/winterlands.md) kingdom of [Fj'all](fjall.md) over the Pit of [D'arqest](../../characters/others/darqest.md); this struggle would later come to be known as the War of the [Great Wall](../lore/great_wall.md).

The city of [New Delsta](../../locations/brightlands/towns/new_delsta.md) was established approximately fifty years ago by emigrants from Delstasch, who gave their new home a name honoring their old kingdom.

## Lore and Theories

- Given its location in the Infernal Castle, the Stone of Truth may be related to the war between Delstasch and Fj'all. If the Infernal Castle is located in the Pit of D'arqest, the two kingdoms may have gone to war over this powerful talisman or sought the mysterious might of D'arqest's [One True Magic](../lore/one_true_magic.md).
- Delstasch and Fj'all are likely the two warring kingdoms from which the prince and princess who founded the [Blacksnakes](../factions/blacksnakes.md) hailed, given that both nations are on the same continent as [Lostseed](../../locations/brightlands/towns/lostseed.md), the city created by the runaway royals. Delstasch appears connected to Lostseed, with New Delsta built directly north of it. It is possible that Lostseed was once associated with or part of Delstasch, serving as a new home for the lost royal heir.
