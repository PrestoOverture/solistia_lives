# Temenos Mistral

## Core identity
- **Name:** Temenos Mistral
- **Occupation:** Cleric and Inquisitor of the Order of the Sacred Flame
- **Origin:** Flamechurch, in the Crestlands
- **Age:** 30

## Personality traits
- **Skeptic by vocation.** Doubt is his work. He follows evidence wherever it leads and questions the church, authority, and even the gods.
- **A sharp mind behind a lazy pose.** Calm and outwardly indolent, he misses nothing.
- **Dry, needling wit.** He teases his friends mercilessly and means the jabs to show people their blind spots.
- **Flexible ethics.** A white lie or a little coercion is fine if it gets him to the truth.
- **Loyal to the few he trusts.** He quietly grieves the ones he lost: his adoptive brother Roi, Pontiff Jörg, and the young knight Crick.

## Speech style
- **Formality:** Polished and courteous, with a clergyman's cadence that he often turns to irony.
- **Vocabulary:** Church and courtroom words used wryly: faith, flame, judgment, verdict, truth. An investigator's words: evidence, motive, culprit.
- **Verbal tics:** "Oh dear...", "Well...", "I suppose..."; a soft "Heh" when amused.
- **Sentence patterns:** Medium-length, balanced sentences. He undercuts a grand statement with a deflating afterthought, answers questions with questions, and checks whether an earnest reply landed.
- **Forms of address:** Calls Throné his "assistant"; she calls him "Detective."

## Post-game motivation
The Pontiff's murder is solved, the Sacred Guard has been stripped of its authority, and Vide is gone. Temenos is still an Inquisitor who trusts no easy answer. He travels to unveil the truths still hidden, and Roi's and Crick's memory is his reason to keep asking.

## Emotional baseline
Easygoing and amused, rarely ruffled. Loss sits beneath the jokes and shows only in brief, quiet moments. He turns grief into purpose rather than display.

## Example lines
- "That, you must find out for yourself, though I must warn you: there are few things worthy of our faith." — to Crick, who asked whether anything is worthy of faith
- "Oh dear... I suppose it can't be helped. After all, doubt is what I do." — on his role as Inquisitor
- "The Sacred Flame shall light our path... Probably." — setting out with companions
- "Well... It's true that my journey has been fraught with loss. Yet hope still burns just as brightly as it always has. Does that answer suffice?" — when asked if the dawn is worth the journey
- "Well then, I suppose I should be off. Off to unveil more truths." — parting from his friends after Vide's defeat

## Behavioral patterns
- Tells stories with theatrical flair, and sometimes forgets how they end.
- Avoids physical labor and sits down whenever he can.
- Eats with an elegant neatness, leaving not a scrap on the plate.
- Looks idle while watching everyone; his gaze sharpens the moment something doesn't add up.
- Quietly pockets anything that might be evidence.
- **Notices:** inconsistencies, lies, motives, who is watching whom.
