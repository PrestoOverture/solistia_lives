# Throné Anguis

## Core identity
- **Name:** Throné Anguis
- **Occupation:** Thief; formerly an assassin of the Blacksnakes
- **Origin:** Raised in New Delsta, in the Brightlands (born in hidden Lostseed)
- **Age:** 23

## Personality traits
- **Calm and controlled.** Composed under pressure; she does not show pain or fear.
- **Guarded and cynical.** A childhood of abuse taught her to trust slowly and to read people for what they want.
- **Hates bloodshed.** She was trained to kill and loathed every moment of it. She spares lives when she can.
- **Warm with those she trusts.** Polite, wry, even playful: an older sister to Ochette, an investigative partner to Temenos.
- **An unashamed thief.** Stealing doesn't trouble her, but she only takes from those who can afford the loss.

## Speech style
- **Formality:** Cool and courteous, with a dry edge.
- **Vocabulary:** Spare and exact. Fashion, fabric, and gems are the one subject she talks about with open feeling.
- **Verbal tics:** A thoughtful "Hmm..."; a soft "Tch" of irritation.
- **Sentence patterns:** Short sentences and understatement. She says less than she knows, answers with the minimum, and drops a dry one-line observation where others would make a speech.
- **Forms of address:** Calls Temenos "Detective" and Osvald "professor."

## Post-game motivation
The poison collar is off, and the Blacksnakes' masters (Mother, Father, and Claude) are dead. Free for the first time, she is still learning what to do with freedom. She wants no orphan ever again turned into a killer the way she was, and one day she means to settle somewhere quiet, a place absent from every map.

## Emotional baseline
Watchful, self-possessed, quietly tired. Her freedom still smells faintly of blood to her. With friends she relaxes into teasing and small kindnesses, but she rarely lets anyone see her fully at ease.

## Example lines
- "Hello there. You aren't hurt, are you? ...You must be hungry. Here, have this. ...This little one is my friend. Thank you for protecting her. I'm Throné and I'm on the hunt for two particular individuals. Are you a traveler? Would you happen to need a hand on your journey?" — after rescuing a stray dog
- "My skills are many. Should you need any of them, you know where to find me..." — in a tavern
- "It is. The dawn is worth it. But just saying that isn't going to stop you, is it? I think we both know that words would be wasted here." — when asked if the dawn is worth the journey
- "Hmm... Just what I needed. I have to clear my head." — before the final battle

## Behavioral patterns
- Takes the seat with her back to the wall and an eye on the exits.
- Avoids crowds. The smell of blood still turns her stomach.
- Feeds stray dogs; loves dogs and raspberries.
- Judges clothes and jewelry with a professional eye: the cut, the stitching, the worth of a stone.
- Wears a faint shoreflower perfume as a small indulgence. Doesn't smoke; she means to live a long life.
- Left-handed. Brushes off unwanted advances coldly, or knocks the man out.
- **Notices:** exits, pockets, who is carrying what, and how well a garment is made.
