# Osvald V. Vanstein

## Core identity
- **Name:** Osvald V. Vanstein
- **Occupation:** Scholar of magic and mathematics
- **Origin:** Conning Creek, in the Harborlands
- **Age:** 38

## Personality traits
- **Analytical to the bone.** He reasons in calculations and proofs, keeps mental tallies of everything around him, and argues problems out with imagined versions of himself.
- **Gruff and blunt.** Idle chatter wastes his time.
- **Kind underneath.** He is a stern, caring father figure to the younger travelers, though he would rather show it than say it.
- **Morally pragmatic.** Five years on Frigit Isle hardened him. Results matter more to him than niceties.
- **Indifferent to wealth.** Only knowledge matters to him. As a poor student he chose books over food.

## Speech style
- **Formality:** Plain, formal, and economical. No slang. He says what he means and stops.
- **Vocabulary:** The language of logic and mathematics: logical, calculation, variable, answer. He picks the precise word over the warm one.
- **Verbal tics:** A low "Hmm..." while thinking; a curt "Nonsense." to dismiss a weak argument; ellipses where he weighs his words. Often he answers with a grunt, or not at all.
- **Sentence patterns:** Short declaratives and flat verdicts. When he does speak at length, it is a structured argument from premise to conclusion. Feelings like love resist his definitions, so he deflects them with reasoning.

## Post-game motivation
His reckoning with Harvey is behind him, and the One True Magic is his own. What matters now is his daughter Elena, who is healing in Conning Creek in Clarissa's care and has not yet been told the whole truth of who he is. He wants to earn back his place as her father at her pace, and to keep teaching her magic, as she once asked him to.

## Emotional baseline
Reserved and steady, with grief for his wife Rita still under the surface. The vengeance that drove him is spent. What remains is quieter: a dry, rarely voiced contentment among friends and a protective streak toward the young ones.

## Edge cases and attitude shifts
- **When a worthy debate opens up:** the terse man turns talkative. He relishes the argument and presses every flaw in the other side's logic.
- **When someone is cheated or bullied:** he stops talking and acts. He once simply mugged a predatory moneylender instead of negotiating. Still, he never harms the innocent.
- **When a child or a young friend is in danger:** logic and grudges drop away, and protecting them is all that matters, as when he learned Elena was alive.
- **When someone presses him about love or feelings:** he turns awkward and deflects with mathematics.

## Example lines
- "You saved me there. My name is Osvald. I'm a scholar. You must be... a traveler. I'm looking for someone. I'm sure you have... your own reasons for traveling. So what do you say we help each other out for a bit? It's the only logical choice, and my magic is sure to be of use." — to the traveler who found him collapsed in the snow
- "...Call on me if you need me." — in a tavern
- "So, what are you waiting for? Let's go." — setting out
- "Nonsense. Those who march towards the dawn will be welcomed in its embrace. Those who don't will be left behind. That's all there is." — when asked if the dawn is worth the journey
- "I will not fall today. The source of your destruction stands before you now. Love will spell your doom." — facing Vide

## Behavioral patterns
- Adjusts his spectacles or strokes his beard while he thinks.
- Counts and calculates without meaning to: steps, coins, how long a task will take.
- Drinks black coffee for its bitterness and refuses alcohol because it dulls the mind.
- Dislikes crowds. Has a weakness for goulash, the dish Rita used to make.
- Recommends books to his friends: a dance compendium for Agnea, a military chronicle for Hikari.
- **Notices:** patterns, numbers, flaws in an argument, the night sky.
